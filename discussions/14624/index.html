I’m a guitar player so I’ll ignore the “pet peeves” for the time being and address the drumming issue. The drum is THE instrument of various folk music around the planet. It just is ( that’s obvious I would hope). And there are obviously many GREAT players, just like there are many great ITM fiddlers, whistlers, pipers, box players… I studied African drumming for several years with a drum master from Ghana. Believe me, this guy was the sh*te, big time! But that’s beside the point. My point is simply this: Never once in the years that I was hanging with other drummers, playing traditional African rhythms, did I hear a single person disparage another musician, musical tradition or musical instrument. Not once. That doesn’t mean screechy fiddles were or would have been welcome in a driving drum circle where the overhead harmonics were happening (probably not an experience many, if any, of you have had). The drumming is it’s own thing just like ITM is it’s own thing, or any other folk music for that matter--like Tuvan throat singing…. But again, that’s not my point. What gets me is the disparaging comments. What’s the point. You don’t want djembe in a session. No problem. But all this berating of people who don’t fit into the ITM “clique.” And you wonder why the term “folk nazi?”

Added by Phantom Button on February 21st, 2007

Do we really have a leg to stand on?

Here’s something that bugs me about the way some folks go on in this forum. Instead of extrapolating about the positive aspects and styles that one prefers, negative comments and insults about particular players and/or their styles and technique seem to be the MO of choice. I would think that one wouldn’t have the license to criticize top-class players unless they could themselves provide a better example of their own playing for comparison. Until one can play at the same level as the people their putting down it seems appropriate to accentuate the positive rather than the negative. If you want to express that you aren’t interested in a particular technique or style, is it possible to get that across without casting insult and derogatory innuendo? Otherwise I think it just ends up looking a bit too much like sour grapes or jealousy.

For example, instead of saying something like:

“I hate all that pyrotechnic flashy buzz-saw and chalkboard scraping crap that all the new players use today, that’s why I prefer the older player’s styles. These young flashy egomaniacs today seem bigheaded and try to prove how they’re able to pack more tricks in than the next guy and wow us with their speed.”

… one might instead try something like:

“I love the soulful sound of the older styles because the melody is uncluttered and doesn’t seem to be racing to the finish line. I appreciate the respect they seem to have for the music, and it comes across in their playing”

Just a thought…

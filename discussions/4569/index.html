Life’s too short…and it’s hard to stay angry at someone who plays the tunes

This isn’t a discussion so much as a peace offering. I’m making it up as I go along (meaning nobody put me up to this, not even Jeremy, though he would’ve been well within his rights to ask).

A certain recent thread apparently went beyond Jeremy’s bounds of civility, hence its disappearance. It was a group effort, but I had as much to do with escalating it as anyone, and for that I apologize, to Jack Gilder, and to everyone here.

Music is what I do for fun, for the sheer pleasure of ***playing.*** I get enough angst at work (ironically enough, dealing with conflict management in natural resource disputes), I don’t need to go looking for it here.

Of course we’re bound to have disagreements--differences of opinion, lapses in communication and understanding, lapses in good judgment, even simple personality clashes. We’re human--we’re all susceptible to stumbling. But I hope we can dust ourselves off, remember why we’re all here mooching off Jeremy’s generosity in the first place, and get back to the tunes and craic. No matter how much we differ, we have far more in common or we wouldn’t spend our time here together.

I temporarily forgot that.

Jack and I and others have a few welts to ice down, but I’ll raise a pint to the day, sooner than later I trust, when we can get back to slagging each other in good humor and good faith.

Siocháin

- Will

The : Time the Healer and Life’s Too Short : paradox

I’ve been granted the disposition to hang around on this planet for a good few years - some may think I’ve overstayed my welcome - but here I am still, and, although I can’t remember any of the lives I was previously incarnated as, or the planets or solar systems thereby, I have grown to love this present form which my soul has taken, to the extent that it may have started off quite shakily, being a member of the dominant species, who had, a handful of years beforehand, decided to bifurcate into 2 rival factions, and waged the most incredibly devastating violence (locally referred to as World War 2) upon other members of itself. There is a paradox here also, that in order to become successful, you must strive to be dominant. That’s understandable when your species came about from medium sized ape-things trying to eek out some kinda existence on the Savanna. {A pretty dumb evolutionary survival strategy, to try to walk {nay, *run* after prey) on 2 legs, have a thumb which closes upon the fingers, and to have a pair of eyes that are don’t function on the back of your head.} So, I guess something had to give. The ones who could *plan* how to hunt survived to pass on “cleverness” genes and now here we are. But we forgot to get rid of the striving for dominance and aggression genes, so wut do we do?

Maybe my soul won’t go to another solar system or to any other life form on this little insignificant blue-green sphere, who cares really? Not me that’s for sure.

One of the most peculiar facts about this life-form (locally known as humans) is their ability to get embroiled in the most tedious debates which don’t really matter to any other life-form except themselves, albeit there are thousands if not millions of their fellow members of species dying of droughts, flooding, and just general starvation, yet these so-called intelligent fellow members of species do nothing to assist, preferring to prove they are right.

If my soul ever visits this planet again, please God, make me a slug.

Humans aren’t as Good as they’re cracked up to be.

Re: Life’s too short…and it’s hard to stay angry at someone who plays the tunes

It’s 2:37 pm PST and I just woke up. As Emily said, I was twisting the knobs at the pub for two of the nicest and most gracious people you’ll meet in the ITM scene, Liz Carroll and John Doyle. As you might imagine, I was up late as a lot of friends were in attendance and we collectively created quite a challenge for the barman at closing time. When I checked my email there were a few messages alerting me to this thread. I am humbled.

When I first stumbled on this site not that long ago in the process of researching a tune, I spent quite a bit of time reading the archives before deciding to join in. Besides the great resource in the tune database, the discussions appeared to have an open and light hearted over tone, along with a sincere and seemingly tireless effort to provide useful tips and information for people with fresh curiosity. One of the most prolific and genuinely helpful people, in the discussion forum, as well as the tunes section -- is Will Harmon. I thought it would be nice to engage with him and others, as well as have some fun slag-fests with the likes of Dow, Danny, Jimmy, and many others.

I may come from a family of writers, but I haven’t inherited any of their skills, so I’m not always successful at expressing myself in this medium. I also have diplomats in my ancestry -- but this attribute has failed to reach me as well. For these reasons I think some of my input could be misinterpreted from time to time. But I decided to try anyway.

The subject of the controversial thread I started is key to my session philosophy -- and I’m passionate about a good session. For this reason I may become over zealous at times and make mistakes in my responses to the challenge of such a discussion. For this I apologize. My intent is not to argue, but rather to communicate. I will try to do better.

For the record, the error in judgment of using a friend as an example -- has now been rectified. Peter showed up at the Plough last night to see Liz and John, I bought him a pint, and he didn’t throw it at me. We had a good chat about the whole thing, as well as a couple of laughs, and went on as usual from that point on. Peter is a sound man.

Thanks to everyone for making me feel welcome here.

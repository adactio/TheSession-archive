Re: Toss The Fiddles

Danny, I’m assuming you were trying to use a guitar capo on your egg. Like, duh?! You need a special egg capo. These are a fairly new idea so most shops don’t stock them, although you might find one in Hobgoblin off Oxford Street. Otherwise you can order them off the net. The best ones are made by Shubb. These last longer, and they make a satisfying clunking sound when you get the screw setting just right. Don’t set the tension too hard or you will find that your egg plays slightly sharp which can be annoying. On the other hand, if the capo is not gripping the egg tightly enough, your egg will buzz, and you won’t get the crisp, clear-cut sound that you want. Don’t just put the capo round the middle. Experiment and put it in different places. Don’t put it too near the thinner end though, because they have a tendency to suddenly slip off and fire themselves across the room, (but that’s okay if you’re pointing it at a violinist).

Re: Toss The Fiddles

Carver’s got a key point there--whoever designed the first fiddle had a bit of the devil in him, placing a sound hole so it would ring out right into the player’s own ears. A fiddler comes of age by hearing every scrape of the bow and squawk of the string--we can’t escape! I’ve played a lot of different instruments and can’t think of a more unforgiving and in-your-face one. Until you get the hang of it--and then it’s wonderful (punctuated by occasional days of scraping and squawking nonetheless :o).

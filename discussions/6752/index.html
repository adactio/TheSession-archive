Re: Musician Quality

How about this.
Musical ability and crappy attitudes are two separate things. Little musical ability and good attitude means someone who is enjoyable to be around and play with on the few tunes they know. Lots of musical ability and good attitude means someone who is enjoyable to be around and play a lot of tunes with.
Little musical ability and crappy attitude is the person who doesn’t recognize their limitations, thinks everyone is looking down at them and spoils a session. Lots of musical ability and crappy attitude is the person who plays tunes no one else knows, faster than anyone else, looks down their nose at others, and spoils a session.
I would submit that crappyiness is not a musical attribute or ability, it is a personality or character flaw, or perhaps a learned behaviour, and is exhibited by people of all musical abilities.
I have nothing against modest musical abilities and the most admiration for exceptional musical abilities--it is the negative behavior of someone with a bad attitude that bothers me. I would also suggeste that referring to “crap musicians” is the first step down the road to crappiness yourself.

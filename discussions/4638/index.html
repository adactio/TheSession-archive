Talking during tunes

Most of the time this means that the person doing the talking does not know the tune. In my opinion they should be quiet and listen to the tune, then they can go home and do their homework and learn the tune for the next session. Why should I be distracted by someone talking over the top of my playing? I pointed this out to someone at my local session (the Plough and Stars in San Francisco) and for some reason they became agitated and attacked me physically. I was then carted off to hospital and had several stitches in my face. The guy hit me with his bouzouki for god’s sake! Now I’m left wondering what I did wrong to get that reaction. I still feel as though I’m in the right. Opinions please…

Re: Talking during tunes

Seriously?

I *try* not to talk through people’s tunes if I don’t know the tunes and can’t join in.

Sometimes … when you’re being engaged by another person, it’s difficult not to respond. Then I’d do so in the lowest possible murmur and if I sense this is becoming distracting I do the usual oul’ eyebrow-wiggling and mugging to indicate non-verbally that although the conversation’s fascinating, we really ought to give themselves a bit of order.

If I was asked afterwards to keep the noise down, I’d feel chastened. But use my instrument as a weapon against a fellow sessioneer? That’s psychotic!!

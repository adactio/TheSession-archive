Looking for some slower tunes

Hey All - I’m just looking for recommendations on some slower tunes that would sound nicely on flute and fiddle played together. I’m not looking for slow airs at the moment. Possibly some favorite O’Carolan tunes or pieces you might play at a wedding/funeral. I’m looking for Irish tunes or tunes that have made their way into the Irish repertoire. Hector the Hero is a great example. I think it was written by Scott Skinner but played often by Tommy Peoples (I’ve heard him perform this beautifully in Ireland & the States).

If you can, please list a nice recording(s) of where I might find these nice slow Irish tunes. I applogize if this is another repeated thread.

The ‘slow reel’ thread is good. I do have some nice reels & jigs that sound great slowed down.

Thanks a bunch!

Joyce

Floating To Skerry is another nice one.
Neither are Irish, but both get played about a fair bit I think.

There’s the usual - King of the Faeries, Inisheer, Terry Teehan’s, Killarney Boys of Pleasure, etc. But you might also get some ideas from Laura Risk’s “Greenfire” cd (a nice slow version of Sheehan’s there, and a strathspey called the Ice Castle, among others), a whole cd of slow to medium tempo original stuff on Dougie MacLean’s “Fiddle” cd, that somber cd by Johnny Cunningham (where he plays Martin Wynne’s #2 as an air), and one of my favorite slow duets for fiddle and flute--Sailing Through the Narrows on John McCusker’s Yella Hoose.

Re: Looking for some slower tunes

Joyce …

A few of my favourite “slowies” (but then I tend to play EVERYTHING slower than most people do and get the ocasional raised eyebrow as a result).

The Spanish Cloak
Sir John Fenwick’s Flower Among Them All (or Floor Amang Than A’ as I’ve seen it translated into dialect) … I LOVE this tune; Horslips do an achingly beautiful version, with the concertina as the main instrument)
Castle Kelly (played at the moderate pace Paddy Glackin plays it on Doublin’, accompanied by Donal Lunny on blarge)
The Eagle’s Whistle
The Pullet That Wants The Cock (Ceide play this very slowly and dramatically as the first tune in a three-part jig/slip jig/reel set which rounds off “Like A Wild Thing”; Sean Maguire gives it a bit more oomph)
A slow tune in 4/4 composed by smallpiper Vikki Swan, called “Around The World” which is just heart-rending (and Vikki’s published the sheet music to all of her compositions from “Thumb Twiddling” on the web for free download … what a girl!)
Josephine Keegan’s touching tribute to a local fiddle-maker, “Lament For Michael Delargy”

Recently I’ve been working on Bunclody … it makes a lovely piece on octave mandola/bouzouki where the ringing strings allow for judicious use of double stops.

Just my tuppence worth …

Aidan

I appreciate what the tunes did for me, but I have a hard time listening to the CD without feeling ill. Too bad.

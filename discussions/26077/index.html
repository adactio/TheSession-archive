↳ Posted by Leslie Travers on April 13th, 2024.

Re: local knowledge?

You could always contact the small claims court in Belfast, Leslie, and ask for information about making a claim.

It doesn’t guarantee you’ll be repaid, but since an adverse judgement can affect the business significantly, it sometimes works. My sister used the England & Wales small claims system, and retrieved her money before it even got to court. The trader knew he’d have had difficulty accessing credit if the court had found against him, and paid up shortly after the papers were served. She didn’t use any legal representation, and filed the claim online for a small fee.

It might be possible to find out whether any previous adverse judgements have been made against the same individual or business, which can help you decide whether to proceed.

https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/small-claims-process

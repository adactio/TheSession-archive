Well here at SoCal sessions we have two black fiddlers.

Nobody cares about their skin colour. They’re welcome at sessons because they’re good players.

But here’s a story about our Scottish-style pipe band:

We have a number of non-whites in the band, both pipers and drummers. At times about half the band has been non-white.

Years ago, when the band was on its fairly rapid rise from Grade 4 to Grade 2 (Highland piping people will know what that means), the band was consistently winning contests. The judges were mostly North Americans.

But there was one contest, for three years in a row, that the band placed dead last. It was the only contest where all the judges were Scottish and flown over.

It would be first, first, first, last, first, first every competition season.

The third year, after our band had played, one of the judges took aside our Pipe Major (the guy who runs the band) and quietly told him “If you want to win you have to get rid of all the black faces and all the brown faces.”

Our PM was too stunned to reply.

In effect, our reply was to play well. Eventually we won that contest, and then won contests in Scotland itself.

Like our PM said, “If I got rid of all the black faces and all the brown faces I wouldn’t have a band!”

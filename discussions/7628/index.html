Re: Concertina Confusion!

One caution about getting more buttons -- there are duet-style concertinas with 40 or more buttons. Sometimes these have been converted to anglos but still have the extra buttons and reeds. All those reed assemblies and action assemblies you don’t need will just add weight. For an anglo, 26 is good, 30 is better, 32 or thereabouts is pretty close to optimal. If you’re lucky enough to have access to a concertina repairman, some modest alterations of note locations are possible. Jeffries vs Wheatstone/Lachenal have somewhat different layouts for a start. I do like the double-C# (both push and pull, that is) on the index finger, right hand, outer row. But if you find a very nice instrument that doesn’t have it, the reeds can be swapped. Although a bit of retuning might be needed for reeds put in a location other than their original, I’ve been surprised to find that often no retuning is necessary if the old reed and new reed were of comparable size. So you may not have to turn down a nice box to get the C#s where you want them.

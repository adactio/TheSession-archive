That would never happen in Ireland!

Added by Phantom Button on October 18th, 2004

That would never happen in Ireland!

That’s what I was told by a lovely Irish girl last night when she was asking why our guitar player didn’t hand over his guitar for an Irish singer to use. The singer in question was obviously intoxicated, would come over to where we’re playing and yell, “COME ON LADS -- LASH OUT A TUNE!” and then proceed to play two empty shot glasses while we played… only to continue after we stopped until he finally ended by yelling, “WAAA-HOOOOOOOO!” and shaking the shot glasses at us. Then he came over and asked if he could sing a song and said he left his guitar back in Ireland. Normally our guitar player would offer his, but this guy’s behavior was very dodgy. So he sang (more like screamed) a song without back-up. Is it really true that this would never happen in Ireland?

Re: Singling out the Single Jig ~ sub categories

Thanks for the chuckle lads, I needed that… ;-)

~ What you lose on the slides ~ I immediately get an image of shooting down one of those big metal slides as a kid and having all the contents of my pockets beat me to the muddy puddle below…

Dow & Granama, the best way to get a sense of the music is through the dance it is part of, but that doesn’t mean you necessarily have to get up on the dance floor and step it, or that if you do it has to be pristine and perfect. It ain’t trad if it is sterile. It has to have some dirt in it. What’s a slide without the muddy puddle at the bottom? Just seeing dancers and the music together can make a difference. In the North, Ulster, they played single jigs beautifully, individuals, families, groups and ceili bands. There tends to be more bounce to that treatment and the ‘slide’ tag and 12/8 time signature just does so much to guide you to that slippery pulse in that music. The ‘single jig’ approach is very 6/8-ish, but with that slow quick bounce. Some folks have said it is more akin to a highland fling, and I can understand that, but I’d never confuse the two melodies, however, there are some that come damned close to being able to work both sides of that rhythmic divide. Consequently, my wife and I are very much in love with both families ~ single jigs (slides) & highland flings (schottisches), but it don’t end there, of course… :-)

The Musical Elite(?)

A friend of mine commented the other day that when people asked why he (an English person who I believe has never set foot in Ireland) played at Irish sessions, he would say, “Well look at the sort of people who you have to spend the evening with if you go to English Sessions.”
I had to concede that he had a point. Most of the people that we socialise with at Irish sessions are at least recognisably human, even if a few do have meglomaniac tendencies, where as a frightening number of people at local English, or maybe they should be called All-Purpose Sessions are the sort of person you might be a bit embarrassed to be seen out and about with. I’ve been to some sessions which seemed like a form of Care In The Community Group Therapy. Others are full of strange people sporting baroque facial hair, people who habitually sing songs half a tone out from the key they play in on the guitar, and disgruntled Little Englanders who sing bitter self penned diatribes against the evils of metrication.
The reason I was thinking about this was that I noticed the large number of references to college experiences in recent threads. We also seem to have a lot of of Professors and highly qualified people playing this stuff (two flute players I know are biochemists)
Is there an intellectual element in Irish music that makes it appeal to more educated people? (I should point out at this juncture that I am a complete stranger to any form of higher education).
I know the comments above may sound elitest and possibly in poor taste, but what the hell…

I reckon that if you’re from an environment where you were exposed to Irish music early, then the expansiveness of your education and career path are much less relevant. God! Ye think ye know everythin’, don’t ye. Etc.”

How come? remonstrated my mate. Surely the whole point is that he’s telling the story as he’s on the very cusp of death. Did he write it down on a wee scrap of paper for his comrades? Did someone carry it next to his breast for two days and nights till he had the chance to sing it? “This my friends, is Feargal’s last words, true as God …”?

The swaying singer stood speechless before my mates before muttering disgustedly into his beard “Bunch o’ hunker-sliders!” and staggered off.

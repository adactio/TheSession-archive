Scottish session tune list

Alright,
1.nobody asked for this,
2.it’s possibly been done already,
3.I know jeremy intended this site to be about “ITM” and
4.all I’ve done is crib the contents list off Christine Martin and Anne Hughes’ excellent Ho-Ro-Geallaidh Books of session tunes 1 & 2, but …
it’s a fairly good representation of tunes you might hear at a Scottish session, including some of the oft-played Irish tunes. I haven’t edited it to remove “composed by traditional” or listed it by tune type or alphabetically, so it’s not great for reference but it may make interesting browsing. The order is from the contents of the books’ pages which are roughly arranged in suggested sets:

Re: Scottish session tune list

Yes, some scales are more tricky for fiddlers but I’m not sure about the “most trad tunes are no more difficult than grade 1 or 2” part.
In terms of of the majority of tunes, they aren’t too difficult but for those which are, the key isn’t always the factor.
There are some quite tricky tunes even in “G”, “D” and “A” and some relatively easy ones in “F” e.g. https://thesession.org/tunes/3919

There are, however, many tunes which I can play well(usually) either solo or in “planned” setting with other musicians but often struggle with slightly in a session situation.
It’s usually because a particular tune may have several settings and/or is approached differently in terms of rhythm depending on the session and/or players there.

“Jeans Reel” falls into that category as do some others. Of course, it could be because it is more difficult that not all players quite “agree” on how it should be played.

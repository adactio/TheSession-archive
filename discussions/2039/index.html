theclique.org

I’m no fan of cliques, but there is a social unit going on here at this site. There are those on the “virtual inner circle of the session”, whether it’s cause they have a lot of knowledge, sweet or funny etc. Then there are the nutters, tossers etc in the “virtual bongo & dulcimer section” of the session. To me there seems to be a king of the hill social fight going on here, some are determined to fight to be right & others go by the old addage “you gather more flies with sugar than you do with vinegar”.

I read a comment from one member (offlist) that thesession.org is stuck in the akward moments of meeting new musicians before you’ve heard each other play. The pecking order of musical ability is not yet sorted out, leading to a mild state of tension.

People are putting on fake screen names, the tune submissions are far & few between, the “in crowd” is too quick to quip at newbies to the site.

I’ve played into a some of this over the life of this site, it just seems theres a lot more of it going on then there has been. I think these observations & opinions are reasonable enough.

What do you folks think?

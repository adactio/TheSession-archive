Don’t know about the law in England but in Scotland a marquee would definately be included in the ban.

http://www.clearingtheairscotland.com/faqs/pdf/info-leaflet.pdf

This gives guidance on the law up here. The ban extends to all places of work including vans and lorries. So if you are a long distance lorry driver you are not allowed to smoke in your own cab - it is your place of work!

I think the law also can extend to homes in cases of home help, for example. If an old person has a home help that comes to visit them then they are not allowed to smoke in their house for a certain period before (and during) the visit. I might be wrong about that though.

I was wrong about that last part. Home visits are not included in the legislation.

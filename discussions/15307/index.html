I have had similar experiences in the past. I have had to warn the Duke of Leinster to stay away from the Milliner’s Daughter more than once. But he generally seems to be listening to me these days (and I can play them back to back without too much difficulty… not that I would do that for anything other than a double dog dare…)

So what unholy alliances have you had to sit down with and have an intervention?

Re: The Drunken Pigeon on the Landlady’s Gate

I think of groups tunes that are similar like this as, “families.” I will go through them back-to-back every now and then just to keep them straight. One of the largest “families” is the Down the Broom, Rainy Day, Bag of Spuds, Corner House, Sligo Maid and Stone in the Field family. Their relationship is mostly in the B-part, but the A-part of a couple also get pretty close.

The trick is to keep straight where all the switchmen should be located. By switchmen I mean the places where if you don’t have an alert switchman stationed you’re likely to head down the tracks of the other tune. There’s usually on spot in each tune where you need to have one ready.

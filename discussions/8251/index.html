Do we take this all too seriously, or have you had some fun lately?

Reading threads about people disrupting sessions with nasty opinions, upset with this website, and variously trashing this, that and the other way of approaching sessions, looking down at other instruments, makes me wonder if we are still having fun, sharing the joy of music with friends, and making the world a better place. I suspect that things are a lot rosier than one would think by reading some of our posts. So, anyone got anything good to say? A new trick you learned, a new tune that is fun, a great song, a new CD you heard, a new friend you met, a new brew you have discovered? A funny story about a session? Or a good joke?

BTW Michael, you *are* making the world a better place by playing tunes. For one thing someone might be listening and enjoying your playing. Secondly, the fact that you yourself are enjoying it m,eans the world is a better place, because you are part of this world. So if *you* feel better, that raises the average number of atoms which are part of a happy entity on this planet ever so slightly upward, and also decreases the average number of atoms which are part of an unhappy entity, so lowering the sum total of that cohort of atoms. See, I can come over all Zen when I want to. Then thirdly, if you are happier by playing, your more likely to be healthier so putting less of a drain on the NHS - again, ever so slightly, but it all mounts up.

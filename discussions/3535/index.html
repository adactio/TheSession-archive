Re: What are your current 10 or so favorite tunes?

Yeh, the family are definitely from Cork: Deirdre, Donncha and Diarmaid. I had no idea they played in a band together, but it doesn’t surprise me. Deirdre also sings classically somewhere in Dublin, and so, she was there in Cork only a couple of times when I was in Cork, but I played music with the other two almost every week. Really great musicians. Donncha has a quite unusual, but really effective, guitar style. Deirdre has a really flowing smooth fiddle style, with very little staccato (triplets) and she will put in lots of (slurred in) passing notes rather than leaving long notes - a style which I’m finding more and more appealing. Diarmaid is a great piper who also has a hilarious and sharp wit, with a bloomin cheeky face to match!!

Bodhran Bliss (Patrick McGinley) - RIP.

Bodhran Bliss - long time poster on this board - has passed away after a short battle with cancer.

I, more than most, knew his ascorbic wit and humour having borne the brunt of it at many a session. I hope those of you who perhaps crossed-swords with him on these pages took it in the spirit intended and recognise that he was simply a man who loved music and debate in equal measure.

I’m sure others can check the details but i think one of his posts generated one of the longest threads in the history of the mustard pages.

As a long-time friend and bandmate it is hard to describe how unique a character Paddy McGinley was.

I’ll leave it to the man himself to take his bow….

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ywF0t99z_2c

Re: unfair

As in any group of human beings, some take things at face value until proven otherwise, some instantly assign motives to people they don’t even know, and some just like to stir things up (in the name of humor).

And like any good conversation, the more we know about each other, the easier it is to understand where each of us is coming from. As the initiator of the intro thread, you bear at least some responsibility for explaining *why* you’re asking for advice. “I’m in a composition class at uni but I’m in way over my head. And we’re supposed to write a piece based on trad music, which I know little about, so I’m counting on the collective wisdom of this board for help.”

Something like that would go a long way toward derailing the more cynical comments.

And at the root of it--Irish trad musicians don’t rely on sheet music. Many don’t even read it at all. Bodhran players play by ear and feel, not from drum notation. And slow airs are almost by definition played freely, without strict adherence to a steady beat. So your request revealed a (somewhat humorous) lack of understanding about this music. Apparently, some people here take that as license to belittle you and your request. Don’t take it personally--they tend to treat others the same way. Unfortunately, such a “welcoming” tends to snub more decent folks, so the cynics end up being the majority.

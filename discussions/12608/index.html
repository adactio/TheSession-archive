One of the simplest notations of the bodhran I’d seen, if I can remember it rightly, had the F & A as the bottom of the tipper, I think A was the down stroke and F the up stroke, or something along those lines, and c was the top of the tipper. So, using that idea, a basic waltz in 3/4 would be:

| FA FA FA | ~ and with the top in play ~ | (3FcA (3FcA (3FcA |

Re: intro

My brother arranges orchestral scores for a living, and a little of his knowledge has rubbed off. So….

Use the “double bar” ( || ) symbol at the start of your piece to indicate that the intro is drum only. No clef sign is needed then, so the next bit is 3/4, and then you’re off, marking the rhythm with actual notes. I’ve never seen bodhran notated, but music for a full drum kit assigns different pitches for snare, high tom, low tom, and bass. With “X” symbols for the cymbals.

Seems like you’d want to keep the basic rhythm for the intro fairly simple:

BUMP bumpity bump|BUMP bumpity bump| and so on.

And don’t let the doubting Thomases get to you.

New site feature: most common tune pairings in sets

I’ve just added a very small little enhancement to the tunes section.

Up until now, if you’re looking at any tune, it always says at the top of the page how many sets the tune has been added to. And if you click through and browse all those sets.

The new feature is that, for popular tunes in lots of sets, it also says what the most common pairings are i.e. which tunes get put together most often with the tune you’re looking at. And you can also click through to see those combinations.

This is very similar to how the information about recordings works. If a tune has been recorded a lot, you’re shown which tunes it is most commonly recorded with. Now the same logic applies to tune sets.

For example, here’s the Tarbolton reel:

https://thesession.org/tunes/560

At the top of the page it says:

"The Tarbolton has been added to 645 tune sets.

This tune gets played in sets together with…"

If you tap on the toggle next to that sentence you’ll then see:

"The Sailor’s Bonnet (lots of times),
The Longford Collector (lots of times)", etc.

If you click on the link to The Sailor’s Bonnet there you’ll see all the sets that have both The Tarbolton and The Sailor’s Bonnet:

https://thesession.org/tunes/560/sets/570

Another example. Here’s Out On The Ocean:

https://thesession.org/tunes/108

I can see at the top of the page that it’s in 1,954 tune sets. If I toggle open the bit where it says “This tune gets played in sets together with…” then I can see that it’s often played with The Connaughtman’s Rambles. That link shows me all the tunes with both Out On The Ocean and The Connaughtman’s Rambles:

https://thesession.org/tunes/108/sets/19

You get the idea.

I hope this will be useful if you’re looking for ideas about what to pair with a tune for a set. I’ve tried to make it nice and discrete so the information is there if you want it, but it’s not displayed by default.

Let me know what you think about this feature, and I’m also curious if you have any tips, techniques or methodologies that you find yourself using when it comes to putting tunes into sets.

That’s very true! And it’s something I thought about when I was putting this feature together; is there a risk that this might create a positive feedback loop? In other words, someone wants a tune to put with another tune in a set; they look to see what tune other people used the most; then *even more* more people are putting those two tunes together.

↳ Posted by Bregolas on July 28th, 2026.

Re: New site feature: most common tune pairings in sets

Can I just point out that it’s possible for a technology to have an effect on behavior without that effect being a totalizing one?

In other words, this feature may affect the way the music is played without that effect being a total devastation of all of the traditional ways - sort of like writing tunes down in books didn’t end learning tunes by ear, but it did change a lot about the way people learned tunes and about what tunes people learned. If you didn’t have to have someone nearby who knew the tunes and was willing to teach them to you, you could learn a lot of tunes that previous players simply didn’t have access to, so even if most players continued to learn tunes “the old way”, the existence and availability of tune books had an effect on the tunes they were able to choose to play.

Now, I don’t think this feature will have anything like that effect, and I’m not convinced it’ll have much of an effect at all, but it seems reasonable to start by allowing the premise that there will be some effect rather than dismissing that idea out of hand because it’s probably not going to end Irish music entirely. Which, of course, it isn’t, and which nobody has claimed.

Microphones at Sessions Etiquette

Wondering what people’s thoughts are on microphones at sessions. I support a “group” microphone amplifying several instruments (though not needed imo) and a microphone for a singer to rise above the noise of the other musicians, but I find personal microphones for just one instrument player not welcoming….Only exception I would grant is for a harp because they are pretty quiet.

I went to what was marketed as an “open” session where everyone was miked up. No wonder there were only 3 musicians…No one wants to come join if you can barely hear yourself and it’s intimidating! And at that point, it becomes a performance not a session. I like being able to “yeowh!” or back off a bit when a musician is really in the zone jamming on the tune and let them shine. You can’t equally feed off each others energy if some musicians are much louder than others. If everyone has a mic at the session too, I risk offending them if I show up one day with them so most likely, I’d need approval from them to have a mic? Seems bizarre to me and against the session spirit.

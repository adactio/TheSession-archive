Re: Willie Clancy 2012

I’m very sorry to hear about Muiris O’Rócháin. I had no idea he’d been ill (I just had to search through past discussions to check that I’d got the right Muiris).

The first time I went to Willie Week, I was living in Latvia and went with a band from there. As visitors from exotic parts, we received an especially hearty welcome from Muiris, and were given VIP treatment. On my subsequent visits, Muiris would always greet me with, “Welcome from Latvia!”. I never had the heart to tell him I was actually an Englishman (not that that would have made me any less welcome in his eyes). I never really made much conversation with him (perhaps he thought I didn’t speak English:-) - or, more likely, he was preoccupied with the smooth running of the week), but he was always a familiar and friendly face about Milltown - and the week wouldn’t have been what it was (or even have existed at all) if not for his hard work.

Condolences to all his family and friends and may Willie Week outlive him by many decades.

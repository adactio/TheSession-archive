My grandfather who worked on the Melbourne docks before he went off to WW1 also used to refer to British immigrants as “Jimmy Grants” and the “pommygrants/pomegranates” theory seems most plausible to me. DH Lawrence, naturally interested in language, also recorded that connection when visiting Australia. The other common term, predating “poms” that now seems to have become archaic, was “new chums”, as so many seemed to come from the north of England and it was reckoned they used the word “chum” (or “choom”) often.

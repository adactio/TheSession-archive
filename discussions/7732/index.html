Are you looking up, or looking down?

Button’s last wedding story on the ‘Trials & Tribulations’ thread got me to thinking about ‘them’ & ‘us’ & my own journey up the ladder of ‘SUCCESS’!

I personally started out as a ‘Hide Beater’ , ( well, you gotta start somewhere, aint ya? ) but quickly developed a conscience & moved ‘up’ the pecking order to the dizzy ‘melody’ heights of Jews Harp Jangler!

After that it was a quantum leap for me, up the ladder again to ‘Mandolin Mincer’, before going for the ‘Heavy Metal dept. - & my first ’Bocca Bocca Banjo’.

O K, fair enough - I admit I did get a little sidetracked there, & took a turn off the straight and narrow, but I got right back on track again with a huge move up the ladder to the FIDDLE! - the Queen of instruments - pauses for Mel Brooks style sound of Trumpet Fanfare!!

Then I took a little meander down a side road, and tried my hand at a ‘Red Ghetto Blaster’, or ‘Red Dragon’, or, as some call them, the Paolo Superintendant’!

However, once my sanity, & hearing, recovered, I moved onto a ‘Constant Screamer’, Anglo of course - well I mean to say, thankfully I’m not ‘quite’ as foolish as I look (am I?) & anyway, I certainly wasn’t desperate enough to go for an English ‘Blaaah’ Box!

Then I decided to experiment with something a little more exotic, and now occasionally dabble with the 50 string princess, the Antrim Queen, the Hammered Dulcimore or less!

So there you have it, one man’s tale of blunders & errors on the road to salvation!

So tell me, who do you look down on?

Me?
Well shucks, Jim-bob, Hell, I look down on everyone, cause I am the one, the only - Mr MAGNIFICENT!

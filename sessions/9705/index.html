Kirkstone Pass Inn

Monthly session at the famous Kirkstone Pass Inn. Every 4th Thursday, 7-11pm but can go on longer. 1 free beer and snacks for musicians. Absolutely amazing location. Food served.
Mostly trad stuff, lots of fiddles, flutes, whistles. But all are welcome!
We ran the first one last night and over 20 musicians turned up and played for 4 hours, so it’s looking good. All abilities welcome, super friendly and welcoming session.

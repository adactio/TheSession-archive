An Gearrchaile Álainn

This lovely piece was composed by Paul O’Sullivan from Bannow in Wexford. Paul is a member of a well-known family within traditional music circles in South Wexford and is a fine concertina and piano player. The piece was composed as a tribute to the late Leona Reville, a wonderful talented musician from Bannow who sadly lost her life at a young age recently. Píosa ceol spesialta do cailín álainn spesialta. Paul’s compositions are being collected and notated as part of the Wexford Tune Project. Thanks to Nóra Byrne-Kavanagh for notating the piece from the recording.

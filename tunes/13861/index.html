Ah! Sure such a pair

Strangely, you will not see this lovely jig in the index of ‘O’Neill’s Music of Ireland‘ until you discover ’Marches and Miscellaneous‘. Also, in the ’B’ part the first note of the fourth bar should be F# and note E as it is shown here (play it and see). The positioning of the semiquavers and dotted quavers to me suggests a Scottish origin.

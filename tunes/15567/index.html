Five comments

Forest Baby

The missing link! The last of the tunes from this great album by Brian Finnegan (The Ravishing Genius of Bones) to be submitted to The Session. I’ve brought it up two semitones for ease of playing. Accompaniment seems to be around the key of A minor. I know it’s not really a jig but the tempo is 6/8.

Kerfank 1870, a.k.a. The Wren

I searched Kerfank 1870 and most often found tunes that sounded like The Wren, a lovely an dro.

The 1st setting here is often attributed to the playing of An Triskell. They offered no title other than a Breton an dro. Others have pointed out that an dro, the turn, is a dance type. No title but the 1st setting is a great tune.

↳ Posted by janglecrow on June 27th, 2022.

An Dro 1st setting

Stumbled upon this, learned it and transcribed it. Found the entry here hoping for a name. Yes, it does seem that an dros are more confusing than Irish tunes when it comes to naming them. It lies on the fiddle well in Bm and with identical finger pattern in Em. The tune suits the hurdy gurdy well and Sanne and Jimi really convey the joy of this tune.

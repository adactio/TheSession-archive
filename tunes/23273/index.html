Two comments

The Plains Of Mayo

Found this wonderful tune on Two Miles to Tulla, a CD by Larry Gavin, Micheal O’Rourke, and Charlie Lennon. It is cited in the liner notes as being from the Petrie Collection of Ancient Irish Music (1903). It was collected from Anne Buckley in the Claddagh, Galway in 1839.
I love the minor feel of the B part. It gives a nice lift and contrast to the tune.

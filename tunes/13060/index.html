Grainnis Slip Jig

I learned this tune on a workshop some time ago. The teacher named it Grainnis Slip Jig. Tunepal found in Bill Blacks miscellaneous collection under the name “Sail around the Rocks”

↳ Posted by DetlefS on October 12th, 2013.

The Sail Around the Rocks, X:2

I was taught this by Seán Mc Elwain in a workshop he gave in Carrickmacross back in November 2015. This version is as played by Dan Markey (1842 - c1910), an uilleann piper from Drumavaddy, Broomfield, Co. Monaghan.

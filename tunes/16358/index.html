One comment

Atlantic

A lovely waltz composed by Anna Gustavsson, performed by Laurel Premo on violin and Anna on nyckelharpa. Some beautiful harmonies between the two instruments. but difficult to disentangle the melody as played on the violin - I may not have got that completely correct, but that’s what I hear.

Laurel Premo and Anna Gustavsson perform the original piece, the Atlantic Waltz, in Kalamazoo, Michigan.

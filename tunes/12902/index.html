The Gradam Reel

The Gradam Reel composed by Michael Mc Cague (At First Light/At The Racket) in March 2013 in celebration of his brother Donal Mc Cague being awarded the TG4 Young Musician Of The Year 2013 award (Gradam Cheoil).

Tune is copyright of Michael Mc Cague, IMRO & MCPS

The tune was performed on the night by Donal, Michael and Brian Mc Grath for the first time publicly.
It has been recorded on an album of newly composed tunes of Michael’s which is due to be released some time hopefully around March 2014.

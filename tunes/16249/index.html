Five comments

The Old Brown Slipper

Another obscurity from the pages of “Treoir”. I quite like its’ simplicity.
Originally transcribed by Seamus MacMathuna, and described as “as played by Tadhg O’Laochda of Lios Tuathail”.

↳ Posted by Kenny on July 21st, 2017.

The Old Brown Slipper, X:2

Nice one, Kenny. Here’s another setting from accordionist Tim Leahy of Listowel, Co Kerry, collected by Breandan Breathnach in 1968 and published in his Ceol Rince na h’Eireann Vol.2 (1976).

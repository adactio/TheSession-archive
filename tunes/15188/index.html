Hummingbird (I Thought I saw a..) by Jamie Smith

…but it turned out to be a hummingbird hawk-moth, a fascinating little creature in its own right.

A great Welsh tune that appeared on the 2004 album ‘Ridiculous Thinkers’. The debut album by the Welsh band Mabon.

Three comments

Infectious mandolin tune

This tune written by mandolin player Luke Plumb comes from Shooglenifty’s CD “The Arms Dealer’s Daughter”. I fits surprisingly well on the flute provided you’re fluent with your F natural key.

I have no inside track as to what the title is referring to.

↳ Posted by jdicarlo on April 28th, 2005.

F natural

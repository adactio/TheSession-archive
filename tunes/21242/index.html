Lloyd House’s Tune

From the playing of Stephanie, Daniel, and Phillip Payne.

Transposed up from Bdor.

Daniel learned it from Lloyd House of Daniel’s Harbour.

It is a variant of another Newfoundland tune, The Buffett Double (https://thesession.org/tunes/17997 ).

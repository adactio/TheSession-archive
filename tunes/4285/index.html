Four comments

Emma’s Single (But I Can’t Marry Her)

Zina recently posted a tune by Shannon Heaton, Emma’s Slip, (https://thesession.org/tunes/4279), and MM mentioned working on a tune for his/her daughter, Emma’s Hop, so when Zina suggested we just needed a single jig to complete the set, a tune immediately began unfolding in my mind.

Only the first half is a single jig. The second part came out the way it wanted to, which turned out to be a double jig.

Here it is in G for the fluters in the crowd.

X: 1
T: Emma’s Single (But I Can’t Marry Her)
C: Will Harmon
M: 6/8
L: 1/8
K: G
|:G2 B d2 g|fga gdB|c2 A BAG|FGA DEF|
|G2 B d2 g|fga gdB|ced cBA|1 BAF DEF:|2 DGF G2 B||
|:cBA EAc|EAc edc|BGE DB,D|GAB dcB|
|cBA EAc|EAc cde|gfe dBG|1 DGF GAB:|2 cBA FGA||

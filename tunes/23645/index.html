Re: Eb Wren’s Schottische

I picked this tune up from the playing of Valmai Goodyear (English concertina) at a session in Lewes, Sussex in 2018. I added the chords shown in X:1 (so not definitive/authentic, etc.) and stashed it in the Rude Mechanicals tune library (www.rudemex.co.uk) which is repository for all sorts of good stuff that I come across. I understood it was Australian. Further delving, prompted by ceili’s posting, comes up with this:
https://bushtraditions.wiki/tunes/index.php/Eb_Wren’s_Schottische - a setting by Iris Bishop, also a concertina player from Sussex: I dare say Valmai had it from Iris, but it sounds as though it may have originally been a tune for music hall song.

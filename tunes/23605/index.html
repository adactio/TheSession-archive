Caoineadh Ui Neill

Lamenting the Flight of Hugh O’Neill

Padraig O’Keeffe seems to be the sole source of this old lament that may have been played by the harpers. I’m not sure if a recording exists of him playing it, but there are some of his pupil, Denis Murphy. Fairly popular amongst pipers.

I hope it’s legible. I wrote it out as best I could but the dots won’t give a proper feel for this. It should be very loose with the meter. Should be sourced from proper audio recordings.

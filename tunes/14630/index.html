Marcus Hernon’s Air

I heard this beautiful Air the first time on a youtube recording of Órlaith McAuliffe:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zeHxJmPiUFM

She plays it in Fmaj. I didn’t know the name of the tune but paddy joyce told me, that it’s on a the CD “A whistle on the wind” from Joanie Madden. Joanie plays it in Gmaj, which is this first setting.

↳ Posted by Stefan Thamm on August 1st, 2015.

Marcus Hernon’s Air.

The title of this air is “A Dhia na Nollag”. It was written, I believe, by Mairead (or Maire) ni Dhuibhir & I first heard it played at a Sunday Mass in Spiddal in the early summer of 1980.

↳ Posted by ironhills on August 7th, 2015.

Re: Marcus Hernon’s Air

Correct, it was written by Máire Ní Dhuibhir and is known as A Dhia na Nollag or Port an Ghrá.

Rolling In The Ryegrass, X:20

Setting for mouth whistling along in the session.

In the original key but all the first part one octave higher. It is possible to whistle the tune without changing the octave, and that would be very advisable to do if the whistling is done as a solo. But the lower notes are pretty quiet and that is why I think it is better to whistle the higher octave in a pub session. Those high notes feel more comfortable for mouth whistling.

I suppose I wouldn’t even publish this setting if it weren’t because this is the first setting with chords.

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on March 19th, 2022.

Rolling In The Ryegrass, X:21

This setting is based on Denis Murphy’s and Julia Clifford’s playing on the Star Above the Garter (and Pat Slattery’s transcription of it). It is simplified and turned into a single (8 bar/part) reel, instead of playing it double as they do. This avoids the turn becoming too repetitive. Even so, I believe it captures some of the lovely details they play.
It’s likely they would have heard Padraig O’Keeffe playing this tune. Here are two versions that POK gave his pupils. The first is like the “regular” version while the second has traces of the Murphy’s setting.
https://www.itma.ie/digital-library/score/padraig_okeeffe_oconnor_mss_c_026
https://www.itma.ie/digital-library/score/padraig-okeeffe-ms-bk-4-1

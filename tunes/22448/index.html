La Garde Partagée

An interesting (possibly crooked?) reel, written by Réjean & André Brunet. Slightly confused when writing it out, but this was the best way I could think to write it out.

Transcribed from Le Vent du Nord and De Temps Antan album ‘Notre Album Solo’, and about the tune they write: “The title means “joint custody,” which is fitting considering that Réjean wrote the first part and André the second one.”

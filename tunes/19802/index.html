The Ukrainian

Interesting but strange little polka I learnt from a piano accordion at a session recently. The man himself was half Ukrainian half English and was keen to bust out a few Ukrainian tunes in between all the Irish stuff!

I learnt it by ear so this is the bare bones of the tune. Would love to learn more Russian and Ukrainian music if anyone cares to share.

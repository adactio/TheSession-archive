Re: Hodgson Square

This tune was apparently composed by one John Young, c. 1710. (For some reason, the first- and second-time bar numbers and lines are not shown in X:1, but it’s obvious where they should be).
In case it’s of interest to ensemble players, there’s a 3-voice arrangement at: http://www.rudemex.co.uk/library/RM_arrangements/Hoxton%20Square%20(3-pt).pdf (also available in .abc).

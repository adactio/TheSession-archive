Re: Carronside

“This lovely slow air comes from The Inverness Collection of Highland Music. John collected it on his travels, and it was one of our first choices for this album.”

The Inverness Collection of Highland Music was published in Edinburgh in three parts in 1888 and John is the band fiddler John Martin.

Taught by Sally Simpson in A Mixolydian at Scots Music Group from a collection of Highland Music. Written at concert pitch but works well with fiddle tuned AEAE.

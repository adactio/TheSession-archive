Eleven comments

This catchy tune is from the mind of Mike McGoldrick, and is not in Amajor but F#minor. I think McGoldrick really has a nack for making really nice tunes, and there’s something really groovy about the key of F#minor….

I’m not quite sure about the name of this tune, but I believe it might be called “I Think it’s Tuesday”, but I first heard it on a video on the KerryWhistles website, and couldn’t quite hear what he was saying.

I transcribed the tune to the best of my ability from this youtube video:
http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=73GtzRDICq0

If I got anything wrong, please do correct me…. It’s definitely not the most difficult tune in the world, being all pentatonic stuff and whatnot, but definitely good.

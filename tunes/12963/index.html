Nigun Atik

Hebrew traditional dance that’s become quite popular at my local seisiún!

↳ Posted by Alan Parker on September 11th, 2013.

The title translates as ‘Ancient Melody’. However, it was composed in the 50s by Amitai Ne’eman, along with dance steps and lyrics
http://israelidances.com/dance_details.asp?DanceID=1
http://www.sacredcircles.com/THEDANCE/HTML/DANCEPAG/NIGUNA.HTM
as part of the Israeli folk dance movement, following the founding of the modern state of Israel. The dance and tune certainly draw on traditional forms and styles, but are more part of an attempt to establish a new tradition than truly part of an old one.

Be that as it may, it’s a very nice tune.

I have the last couple of bars like this: | AB cA BcB^G | A8|

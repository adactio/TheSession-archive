The Woods Of Focluth

As requested by member Andy Lee Roth, this is transcribed from “One For The Road” by The McGoldrick Family. The tune is played as a slow reel.
The composer, Stephen Doherty’s original(?) setting, in C, on “The Esdee Sessions” with Seána Davey is more at reel tempo and is a wee bit different but, as it is my bedtime, a transcription will have to wait for another day.

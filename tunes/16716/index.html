Petticoat Loose

I’m sure someone will let me know this tune is already here, but I’ve searched on a few combinations and not found it. It doesn’t seem related to the other tunes of the same name, although O‘Neill noted that ’Petticoat Loose’ was the name of a dance. I got it from Malinky’s 2005 album The Unseen Hours.

Petticoat Loose, X:2

This four-part version of the Petticoat Loose jig is from the Piper John Murphy Collection (1809) as played by Deirdre and Mairéad Hurley, Drawing From the Well, ITMA. It is played at the end starting at 40:41.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-LTKWgQgMO0

The sisters correct a few errors in the original version. The 3rd part is a nice challenge on a flute without a C natural key. I don’t know how difficult it is on the concertina.

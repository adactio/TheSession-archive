One comment

The Air Of Sunset Land

This is the “air” that begins Sunset Land from John McSherry’s album The Seven Suns and is unusual for its Middle Eastern flavour to it due to the numerous accidentals it has. This transcription is not taken from the album version but from this YouTube video where the notes are easier to identify. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S7fyElv9Ai8

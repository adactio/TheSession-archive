The Broken Torc

This tune, composed by Bruce MacGregor and featured in his album “101 Reasons to Do Nothing” was supposedly written following an over celebration after winning a bronze Celtic Torc for the BBC programme “The Captain’s Collection”!

Its a great tune, and will definitely improve the speed and precision of the quick triplets of those who play it!

John Sandall

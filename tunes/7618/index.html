Five comments

Air For Sophie Delizio

I couldn’t believe it when I heard this brave little girl had been hit by another car some time back. I think everyone was touched by this little girls courage when facing such severe odds. I wrote this tune in honour of Sophie and the inspiration she has been to eberyone. I play it as a slow reel.

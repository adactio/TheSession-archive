Re: The Gamekeeper’s Cottage

Yes, I also have a copy given out by Jenna herself at Blazin’ in Beauly in 2014, not quite the same as X3 above. And the A part goes straight into the B part, so you don’t need the minim rest in bar 10.
(I was somewhere in that crowd playing in the video too!)
I can understand how difficult it must be to transcribe by ear, especially the B part where it keeps changing from 2/4 to 3/4! Afraid I don’t do ABC, so can only tell you what I’ve got in the notation.
In my copy, from bar 11, it goes 2/4 2/4 3/4 3/4
2/4 2/4 3/4 3/4
2/4 2/4 3/4 3/4
3/4 3/4 2/4 3/4 (back to the A part on 3rd beat)

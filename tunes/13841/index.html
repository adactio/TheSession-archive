The Atholl And Breadalbane Gathering

Not of course a barn dance but a pipe march, but that seems to be the only way to get it on this site. Composed by Pipe Major William Ferguson and published in his 1939 collection. I first heard this on the Boys of the Lough album “Live at Passim”

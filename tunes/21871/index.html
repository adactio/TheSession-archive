Six comments

To War

This is a jig composed by Cormac Begley that appears on his new bass concertina album, “B”, which was released last week (https://thesession.org/recordings/7548). It can also be heard on YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-YmhvSnaT0

From the video description:
> This is a jig called ‘To War’ that I wrote for my godson, Soren. Soren is a Viking name meaning ‘God of War’.

Cormac plays it in the key of F minor on the album. I’ve written it down here in E minor as that’s a friendlier key for most standard-tuned instruments.

↳ Posted by Lennart on May 23rd, 2022.

Re: To War

Just a note for C/G concertina players, in case they want to pick up some of the variations from Cormac’s playing in the video: the more natural key would be Amin (he is playing a Ab/Eb instrument). That puts it in the right range to play the doubled octave parts.

Gan Ainm (Denis)

Hello! I heard this one in this set. Despite my best efforts, I couldn’t find a name for it.

Set:
1- Reverend Bro’s jig (aka Sonny Brogons) in F-
2- Old John’s in F-
3- gan ainm (this one) in Bb-

Adding Condon Frolics and in more friendly keys it becomes:
0 - Condon Frolics (Ador)
1- Rev Bro’s jig (aka Sonny Brogons) (Ador)
2- Old John’s (Ador)
3- gan ainm (this one) (D-)

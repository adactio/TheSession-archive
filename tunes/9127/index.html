Nine comments

Danzig Willie

I *think* it’s in 9/8, but it almost feels like a 3/4. A tune by the Scots singer/song writer/instrumentalist Brian McNeill. I’ve only heard this tune on two recordings; the Battlefield Band with Alison Kinnard album, Music in Trust Vol. 2, and Horses for Courses by Brian McNeill and Tom McDonagh. It’s a fun little tune and reminds me a little of Top it Off.

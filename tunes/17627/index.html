Two comments

Flint Hills Ride

Leads nicely into Jump at the Sun. Composed by Mike & Dayle Black, of Three Bean Salad.

Flint Hills is a region near Kansas, and Flint Hills Ride is the name of a yearly fundraiser.

The last bar of the A Part sounds a bit odd in the midi, but is how the tune is played (and sounds good, at that!). There might be a slight swing there from the D-to-E-to-low-G. An alternative which works fairly well is |ECA, A3|, although there still should be a subtle swing here too.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on January 4th, 2019.

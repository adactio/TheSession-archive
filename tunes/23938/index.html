Winter At The Mill

Composed by Aisling Lyons after the “Irish Autumn 2023” at the Proitzer Mühle in Germany.
This version I learned from the wonderful Flute Teacher Siobhán Kennedy (https://www.siobhan-kennedy.com/) at the Proitzer Mühle in Winter 2024. (https://proitzer-muehle.de/)

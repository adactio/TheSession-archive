The Confused Whistler

First self-penned tune.

Each of the three parts has a different feel. The A Part has some resemblance to Lady Elizabeth Cole’s (Scottish), the B Part has a few influences (more Irish-like), and the C Part starts off with elements of Herbert the Sherbert (English) & then changes gears. Strung it altogether in a way that flows.

Given the multiple influences, figured “The Confused Whistler” is a fitting name.

Back Of The Haggard/Kershaw’s

This is such a nice tune, and undeservedly neglected. The first couple of bars remind me of Ciaran Tourish’s reel “The Windmill”. The version I play avoids same-note triplets and has some nice English-style 6th leaps:

Re: The Back Of The Haggard

The Shorter Oxford dictionary gives another meaning: ‘a rickyard’ - from an Old Norse term - translating roughly as ‘hay garth’ or ‘hay yard’. This seems to me to make more sense as applied to the title of a tune, particularly if originating in an agricultural community.

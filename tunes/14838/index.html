Tôn Alarch

This arrangement was made by Guto Dafis. The tune is best played at a ‘marchy’ pace.
The title translates as; Mute Swan’s Song. Supposedly song of a dying Mute Swan.

Pronounced; Torn Alarch. The ‘ch’ sounded the Celtic way.

Ben March’s Cottage, Ben’s Cottage, Cottage jig

I know this site is not intended for self compositions,
but well.. I just want to share some of my work, this tune is dedicated to Great friend I made in Dublin that took me across Ireland into Miltown Malbay and that made us meet great musicians in sessions in Clare and Galloway, I’m speaking of Bernard March, the Bodhrán maker, that is one of the most kind persons I have ever came to meet. In this trip we were hosted in his beautiful cottage which gave name to the tune.

This is a simple and beginner tune, perhaps it is similar to some other tune, if anyone finds a strong resemblance please let me know. Hope you find it enjoyable, best regards.

Pedro Queiroz Martinho
Lisbon, Portugal

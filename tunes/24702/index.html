Julie’s Delight

A hornpipe composed by John and Pat Bass from Annagh, near Gorey in County Wexford. It was recently published in ‘The Wexford Tunebook’, a comprehensive collection of some 450 tunes associated with Wexford or indeed composed by Wexford musicians over the decades. The Bass family are well-known in traditional music circles, being multiple All-Ireland Fleadh winners over the years. The two brothers composed this tune in memory of their sister, Julie. Julie’s delight was a vintage 1950’s Austin A35 car.

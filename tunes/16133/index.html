Muddy Doyle’s

This is a lovely hornpipe from the playing of the great Dublin/Boston Banjo player Terry O’Shea. I believe his grandmother composed it. We usually follow it with The Cuckoo (https://thesession.org/tunes/573). Add triplets as you see fit.

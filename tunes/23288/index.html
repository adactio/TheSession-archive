Four comments

Nancy McInnes

A 4 part Scottish jig, in the repertoire of Fred Morrison.

This setting does not include the numerous piping ornaments which give it a typical lilt.

It is hexatonic (no 3rd). The rare 6th plays a discreet role in the repeated cadence.

Three comments

Gemima

A reel by Ross Couper.
I first learnt this tune back in 2007 at the Shetland Fiddle Frenzy, when it was in D (see X: 2) and called, I think, “Jemima” with a J. By the time of “Fiddle + Guitar” (by Ross and Tom Oakes) in 2017 it had moved to A and the J had become G.
In the B part, Ross sometimes plays a low D in place of the rest at the syncopated bit.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on March 27th, 2019.

In D, as I learnt the tune originally.

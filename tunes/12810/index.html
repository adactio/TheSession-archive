Day Trip To Galway

The melody of this single reel came to me whilst driving from Ennis to Galway City. I played this tune on my Geantraí appearance on TG4 this year from P Egan’s, Moate, Co. Westmeath on whistle with John Byrne on guitar and it also features as tune 2, track 3 on my new album ‘Síocháin na Tuaithe:Peace of the Countryside’.

Three comments

Into The Wood

Paddy O‘Brien (Tipperary) composition. First heard played by Martin Connolly on his ’Fort of Kincora’ album.

↳ Posted by coppers and brass on May 21st, 2014.

This tune reminds me of Corry Hilly: https://thesession.org/tunes/2999

↳ Posted by slainte on May 22nd, 2014.

Into The Wood, X:3

From Nathan Gourley, Laura Feddersen, Brian Miller and their wonderful album, Life is All Checkered: https://thesession.org/recordings/4838. We love playing this opening set of tunes on the album at our local session: Crib of Perches, Into the Wood and Johnny McGreevey’s (listed here as the East Clare reel). Here’s a flute setting for Into the Wood.

Last Lord of Beara

Donal Cam O‘Sullivan Beare, was an Irish nobleman and soldier who fought in the Nine Years’ War in Ireland (1593 to 1603) against English rule. In defeat, O’Sullivan like other members of the Gaelic nobility of Ireland, fled and sought exile to Spain by ship.

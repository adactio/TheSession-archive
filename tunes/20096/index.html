I Will, Yeah

From the playing of, and composed by, Niamh Ní Charra.

From her album notes,
“I named [it] after a particularly common phrase in Kerry and Cork, ‘I will, yeah’ is used in the ironic sense when the user has no intention of doing whatever is requested on them.”

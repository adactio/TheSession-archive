Caoimhín Polishes Yet Another Gem by Peadar Ó Riada…

I picked up this strange/beautiful tune from Mick O’Brien & Caoimhín Ó Raghallaigh’s newest CD “Deadly Buzz”. It’s a crooked little wonder from the splendidly musical imagination of Peadar Ó Riada. The transcription was a bit tricky, since the “B” part is a repeated four and a half bars long, and the “C” part is seven and a half. Changing the time signature for the last measure of the offending sections to 2/4 was the best solution I could come up with, but I’m certainly open to suggestions (as long as you present them before the conversion to dots!). At any rate, enjoy!

Jay

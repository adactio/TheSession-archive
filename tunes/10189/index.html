Four comments

Kalabakan (Borneo),

A reel of Scottish piping origin…written by P/M Angus MacDonald, MBE, Scots Guards. Originally written for the Highland pipes, it translates very well as a fiddle piece. This tune has been recorded by many traditional bands including the Battlefield Band and The Tannahill Weavers.

↳ Posted by Uilleann_piper on January 14th, 2010.

Kalabakan

Cracking tune. Quite often played medium paced and sung in the piping world but here we like to play it fast and quite straight.

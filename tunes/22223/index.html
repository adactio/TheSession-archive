Tom Byrne’s

Reel number 8, composed by Tom Byrne of Wexford town. Tom was an Oireachtas accordion winner in the early 1960’s, when the competition had equal status with the Fleadh Cheoil. He is a prolific composer of very nice tunes, all of which (so far!!) have been notated and uploaded to thesession.org as part of the Wexford Tune Project.

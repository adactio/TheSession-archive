The Darkley Deacon

Another of my tunes. Unlike the last one, that takes a turn to the dark side for the guitars, this takes more of a calm approach as a hornpipe. I wrote it as a Christmas present for my grandfather, a deacon from a small border village in Darkley, co. Armagh.

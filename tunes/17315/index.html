The Green Leaves Of Summer

I remember this tune well from the film “The Alamo” in the 1960s and it’s a good waltz tune that stays in the head. It was written by Dmitri Tiomkin. If a setting of Eleanor Rigby is allowed on The Session, I don’t see why this shouldn’t be too. The sharpened notes add interest.

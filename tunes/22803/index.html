Three comments

The Salthill

From Charlie Lennon’s CD Turning the Tune. This tune caught my ear, but he plays it in (low) Gmajor which means that many of the lower notes are out of range on the pipes, my instrument. It does sound great when the fiddle hits those low notes. So I transcribed it as shown here, the better to transpose it up to Dmajor for pipes.

↳ Posted by Frank Gibbons on February 23rd, 2023.

Re: The Salthill

Charlie recorded it in a low G key, as shown here. There’s lovely tonality if your instrument can reach that low, and I’m not averse to “wrapping a tune around” to come back in higher when it drops below the lowest pitch on the pipes, but I don’t think it really works to do that on this. Hence the transposition.

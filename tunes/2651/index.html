Re: Rosemary Lane

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hC01ALDz1aQ&feature=share&utm_source=EJGixIgBCJiu2KjB4oSJEQ

Learnt from Liz and Yvonne Kane, in key of C; had no idea it was in D. It seems to be a Galway thing, or at least a Kane thing, playing some tunes flattened a key. Obviously they like flatter keys in that “style” or “region”. And parts of Clare; Martin Hayes recorded a few flattened tunes, like “Sean Ryan’s” jig in Dmin from Amin, “Mist-Covered Mountain” in Gmin from Amin. Sounds pleasant & it’s a little less of a stretch on the hand. Or just a different type of stretch, lol. The Kane sisters did this with a few recorded tunes; “Fred Finn’s” in C from D, “Jug of Punch” in Dmin from Emin, “Farewell to Miltown” in Gmin from Amin, etc.

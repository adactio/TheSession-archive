Hugh MacDonald

I found this great reel in a set on the Jock Tamson’s Bairns album, ‘A’ Jock Tamson’s Bairns’
It’s credited to John Mackay on the album.
I couldn’t find sheet music for it, so have set it out here with help from my session friend Kirstie.

Re: Hugh MacDonald

This tune was composed by Johnnie Mackay of Trantelbeg, Strath Halladale (born 1900, died 1988). [Not to be confused with Pipe Major John Mackay who composed ‘Dark Lowers the Night’ ]
Over 100 of his tunes were published in manuscript.

Six comments

A simple but nice traditional Scottish reel I picked up from Margaret Stewart and Allan MacDonalds album “Fhuair mi Pog”.
Allan plays it on Scottish smallpipes.

Also known as Bealach a’ Chara or The Pass of Cara.

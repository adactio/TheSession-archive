The Dance By The Old Sally Tree

Source: Re-joyce by Maire O‘Keeffe, John Faulkner, Jackie Daly, Donal O’Connor
Transcription: gian marco pietrasanta

↳ Posted by gian marco on November 26th, 2005.

Copied and pasted from the “Fiddler’s companion”:

DANCE BY THE OLD SALLY TREE, THE. Irish, Air (6/8 time). G Major. Standard. AB. Sally = willow, from the Latin salyx. Collected by Joyce from the singing of his aunt, Mrs. Mary MacSweeny of Cork, and of Glenosheen Co. Limerick (1844‑1855). Joyce (Old Irish Folk Music and Songs), 1909; No. 169, pg. 85.

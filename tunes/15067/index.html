A Ha’penny For A Cotton Ball

Pleasant reel from O‘Neill’s Music Of Ireland (the 1850), where it is #1279. It also appears in O’Neill’s Waifs And Strays, where it goes by the name of A Letter From Home. Maybe it’s just coincidence, but it does remind me of the reel Far From Home (https://thesession.org/tunes/1164).

↳ Posted by aidriano on December 17th, 2015.

Re: Pride Of Roxbury

From:

https://nathanandlaura.bandcamp.com/track/pride-of-roxbury-paddy-joe-tighes-the-road-to-town

Transcription shifted up one semitone from the recording, which appears to be flat pitched in C#, so to play along from this transcription you’ll need to to raise the recording up one semitone using Transcribe, Amazing Slower Downer or similar pitch shift software or tune your instrument down one semitone.

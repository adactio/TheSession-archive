Cochrane

This is a fast tune, written after a frustrating conversation with a certain Mrs Cochrane, who was adamant that “the aura of the room was not to be spoiled by the band’s equipment”.
The argument that ensued about the band needing to set up, and therefore having no choice but to “spoil the aura” was what I was hoping to capture in the tune - a kind of frustrated antiphony.

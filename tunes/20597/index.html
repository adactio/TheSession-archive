Chase The Shouting Wind

A fast 3/2 tune from Brian Finnegan’s album ‘Hunger of the Skin’. I have transcribed the base tune, although he also takes a cool whistle solo midway through, which I decided not to include. This tune is played in A flat minor, but due to key limitations using ABC notation on the session, I had to add this setting transposed up one semitone in A minor.

↳ Posted by benóKoroit on March 20th, 2021.

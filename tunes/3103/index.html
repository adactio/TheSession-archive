Hamish the Carpenter aka. Old Time Wedding Reel #2

This is the second tune of the well-known Cape Breton medley, Old Time Wedding Reels. I’ve already submitted the first one (https://thesession.org/tunes/1480) and recently found it’s in many people’s tunebook, so I need to complete the set. I’ll post the last one soon.

Compared to the first one, this reel may not be so attractive, but I think it’s simple yet mighty tune just like Elizabeth Kelly’s Delight.

I’m now enjoying the live recording of Ashley MacIsaac and Howie MacDonald’s concert, which I’m borrowing from a friend of mine. They kick off a ridicuously long reel set with the medley. Crazy fiddle duet!

Herman the carpenter

From the Fiddlers Companion
http://www.ibiblio.org/fiddlers/HAA_HAP.htm#HAMISH_THE_CARPENTER

HAMISH THE CARPENTER. AKA and see “Och a Chiallain,” “Old Time Wedding Reel [2].” Canadian, Reel. Canada; Cape Breton, Prince Edward Island. A Dorian. Standard tuning. AB. The tune is part of a traditional Cape Breton wedding medley along with “Cape Breton Wedding Reel” and “The Big Coffin Reel.” Source for notated version: Gus Longaphie (b. 1915, Little Harbor, North-East Kings County, Prince Edward Island; now resident of Souris) [Perlman]. Dunlay & Reich (Traditional Celtic Fiddle Music of Cape Breton), 1986; No. 24, pg. 43 (appears as “Old Time Wedding Reel #2"). Perlman (The Fiddle Music of Prince Edward Island), 1996; pg. 95. Shanachie 79061, Boys of the Lough – “Welcoming Paddy Home” (1986). WMT002, Wendy MacIsaac – “That’s What You Get” (1998? Appears as “Herman the Carpenter”).

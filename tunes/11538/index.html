Still Waifing, Still Straying…

I think I’ll need to skip ahead to the “reels” section for my next post! (I just realized that, including this tune, I’ve posted 4 jigs in a row…). I thought this one would be worth it, though. again from O’Neill’s “Waifs and Strays of Gaelic Melody”. The melody grabbed my attention immediately, and upon playing with it a bit, revealed itself to be easily varied to excellent effect. Here’s my take on it , anyway:

X:1
T:Innis’ Jig
M:6/8
L:1/8
Q:3/8=115
R:Jig
K:G
|:D|GAG BAG|dBG e2 g|edc BAG|EAA A2 B|
~G3 BAG|dBd e2 d|gfe dec|BGG G2:|
|:d|gdB ~G3|DED ~G3|edc BAG|EAA A2 f|
gdB GBd|ecA def|~g3 dec|BGG G2:|

Fun to play around with, I think. Play around with it yourselves, & tell me what YOU think…

-Jay

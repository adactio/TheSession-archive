Five comments

The Blue Lamp

After hearing this reel a few times at a session in Brittany, years ago, I discovered that it comes from the album « The blue Lamp », released in 1999 by a fiddle duet : Jonny Hardie (Old Blind Dogs) and Gavin Marwick (Iron Horse, …). I’m not sure, but I guess it was composed by one of these two fine Scottish fiddlers.
A very catchy rendition of this reel can also be heard on Liz Doherty second album, « Quare Imagination » (2002).
! Notice on part B : at the end of the first time, the last bar is shorten in 2/4 instead of 4/4, so the beginning of the second time is placed one beat in advance, with a rhythmic syncopation. Doesn’t suit for the dance but creates a nice dynamic effect !
Almost all the part C is played on a E7 chord (pedal point).

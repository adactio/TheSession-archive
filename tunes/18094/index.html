Four comments

Tam Burns’ Polka

Contrary to some of the more recent assertions here :-) , I have to say that I fully understand why this tune has never been submitted until now.

This is a Dick Black composition and is a tune more likely to be encountered on the Scottish Dance band or Fiddle and Accordion scene rather than in your local trad session.

However, it is a tune I like very much and play a lot myself. Unfortunately, I don’t know much about the history of the tune or the identity of Tom Burns himself. It seems to have a Scandinavian feel to it which is why I like it.

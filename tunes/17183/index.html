Re: Klermañ

Listening to the Soundcloud recording, I thought the tune sounded very familiar.
Then it came to me that it sounded very like a slide played by Johnny O’Leary on the CD
Johnny O’Leary of Sliabh Luachra-- the second tune on track 2-- that Johnny called
“The Cat in the Corner”, but others call “Bill the Weaver’s”, or, as a jig, “The Humors of Killarney”.

See: https://thesession.org/tunes/10178 but take out every other bar line and play it fast as a slide,
and see if it isn’t very like this tune. If you can listen to the Johnny O’Leary CD, then that’s even better.
It is one of my all-time favorites for learning tunes and for listening.

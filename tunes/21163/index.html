Two comments

Gan Ainm

This tune entitled “Reel” (SG1.007) was transcribed in Book 1 of the Grier MSS (c1880s) of Gortletteragh, Co Leitrim. This tune has been published without title in Breathnach (1996), No. 124, where its similarity to “The Baltimore Reel” and to “Lady South’s Reel” is mentioned. This is true only of the first part, and even then the similarity is shared with a number of other tunes of similar start. An even closer match to the first part, though at a remove in the key of D, is “Glen Ogle” in Kerr (c.1880).
The first part of this reel is identical with the first four bars of the first part of another reel a few pages on in Book 1, “A reel”, SG1.014, and may even be a scribal error, where the scribe had intended to write that reel here.

↳ Posted by ConorW on October 27th, 2021.

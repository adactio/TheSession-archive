Kev’s Trip To Brittany

Written by John McCusker. He’s listed it as a “Gavotte”.
Apparently some person named Kev went to visit him in Brittany while John was touring with The Battlefield band and they had a great time.

Transcribed from the Transatlantic Sessions - Series 5, Vol. Three.
2nd Tune of the 8th track.

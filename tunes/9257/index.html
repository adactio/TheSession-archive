Some more information

James was commissioned to write “29 seconds of music” for a commercial while in Canada one time and this is what he came up with. He did not have a name for the tune initially and performed it in the early 1990s without a title. It’s possible that he only christened in preparation for launching his album which featured it and that the title on Uncommon Bonds was functional due to the Canada connection but not an official title by any means.

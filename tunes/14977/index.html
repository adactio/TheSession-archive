Two comments

My Honey In The House

This tune is from O’Neill’s Music of Ireland (#1491), or #715 in the 1001 book. It is very similar to “The Silver Tip”, and had O’Neill not thought fit to separate them I would have been inclined to add it as a variant on that tune. Also very simlilar to “My Love Is In The House”.

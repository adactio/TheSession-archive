↳ Posted by James Flem on August 6th, 2025.

Re: Sommarpolskan

As indicated by the title, this is a polska - not a polka - and should be in 3/4 throughout. The notation at X:1 has caught the melodic line but the barlines in the A & B parts are misplaced. It was written by Roger Tallroth (formerly of Väsen, who recorded it on their CD ‘Brewed’).

I agree with Jack on this one; this first version of the O’Carolan tune Nancy Cooper, is beautiful; and to my mind made more so by the chord structure used in their version of the tune by Straight Furrow on their Album “Get your breath back” (published by Samphire Music, released 1st March 2003).

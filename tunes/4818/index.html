One comment

I learned this tune from Jimmy Devine and he told me that Micho Russell taught him the New York jig during Willie Week one year because he knew he was from that area/the States and Jimmy later turned it into a waltz. A pleasant tune indeed! Put some swing into it…

Cheers,
Armand

Here Comes Trouble

This tune is the third in a set of three that I composed specifically for the NG Petite Linotte hurdy-gurdy. The whole set is designed for young children learning the instrument, with playful, descriptive titles to spark their imagination. Here Comes Trouble was inspired by one particularly “enthusiastic” student of mine—I’m sure you can guess the vibe! Writing for hurdy-gurdy means embracing modal composition, thanks to the trompette and drone, so I had a lot of fun weaving between major and minor tonalities in this piece, and it’s wicked fun to play too. Enjoy.

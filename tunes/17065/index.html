Re: Peh Trouz Zou Ar En Doar

This tune is a religious breton Christmas “kantik” from the county of Vannes - Morbihan, Bryttany. The lyrics are in that dialect of breton and it tells the story of the Nativity. The rythm here is wrong. the translation of the title is : “Peh trous zo àr an douar / Quel bruit y a t-il sur la Terre / What kind of noise is there on earth” and “ Peh trous a glewan-me / Quel bruit est-ce que j’entends ? / What is that noise that I can hear ? ”
These are the first pause of the song.

The Cobbler’s Reel

This reel was composed, from what I can make out was by Patrick O‘Leary a piper from Killarney around 1836. Notation by P. O’Donovan and was taken from the piper at a horse fair near Dingle.

↳ Posted by Cuanshore on November 24th, 2010.

After playing this a few times on ABCnavigator I’ve realised it is similar to Crúibín’s for Breakfast that I published earlier. didn’t realize it at the time, when I was looking at this manuscript.

BIDH CLANN ULAIDH [Children (or Men) Of Ulster].

A basic setting of this beautiful slow air which is the first track on “Return to Kintail” by Alasdair Fraser & Tony McManus.
The sleeve notes indicate the melody came from a Gaelic lullaby which tells the baby that pipes will be played, wine drunk and the children of kings will dance at his wedding.
For more background see:
https://tunearch.org/wiki/Annotation:Bidh_Clann_Ulaidh
Hear Alasdair and Tony play the tune here:

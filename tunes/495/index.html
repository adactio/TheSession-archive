This is the Camel’s Hump

I was chewing the fat with Skip a couple weeks ago & we were chatting about Bill Black (Banjo-Zouk guy from Cape Cod Area). We got on the subject of how Bill’s written a gzillion tunes, that’s when he told me that “The Camel’s Hump” which he paired with his own tune Purgatory Chasm on the title track of his last album was a Bill Black tune. Mr. Bill (as I call him) has this tune under copyright so you’ll have to dance in your underwear for him if you even think about recording it, although I think Skip just asked him & he said “OK”.

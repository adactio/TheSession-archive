Escher’s Staircase

Linda Relph here. This is one of my original tunes, written during the summer of 2008. My husband Chris named it for me, saying that it twisted in and around itself much like the M.C. Escher painting. My band Nine-8ths Irish recorded the tune, and you can hear it on our album “Out on the Ocean.”

FAQs

Three self-composed tunes in a row is a bit overkill, whatever genius they might represent. Here since 2007 I’d have thought you’d have bothered to read the FAQs, or at least peruse them. You could try another option some use here, putting the ABCs and tales behind your tunes in your ‘Details’ on site here. It’s one way around the courtesy of the webmaster’s request.

You are not alone, we had one person who was dumping a lot of his genius on us until our gracious and patient webmaster made the move to start deleting them after six in a row… It never surprises me the many composers who seem unable to meet the simple request Jeremy makes, at least 5 traditional tunes for every self-composed submissing. Funny, they always seem to start with their own compositions too as their first submission here…

There is no shortage of the self-composed, since all things came from some root or another. We’ve just lost track of many of those composers. The joy of tradition is the wear and tear over time that results in all the gems we have to play, whatever their origin. Or, maybe an agate in a riverbed is a better way of looking at it, the roughness being smoothed over time, but still natural.

Re: Escher’s Staircase

Don’t take it too much to heart, Linda. There are some members here who, in the past, liked to think of themselves as gatekeepers of this site (when it has always had an excellent manager).

Way back when you were posting tunes, I was doing likewise, but decided after a few that this site wasn’t necessarily the best place for my own compositions, and latterly the flow of settings has started to swallow up new tunes (in that they can disappear off the first page within a matter of hours).

So, a couple of years ago, I decided to put my (best?) tunes in a book, free as a pdf (and most hard copies of which I have given free to friends and acquaintances). To me, it has been a good way to share my tunes.

Causeway Sunsets

I think this might actually be a Schottische, but I thought that Barndance might be the closest here.

I actually composed this tune on my Nyckelharpa, but for the video below, I played it on my Nyckelharpa, Tenor Guitar, Mandolin & Tenor Banjo.

It was the first thing that came into my head, after I fitted new strings, sent over to me from Sweden, by the instrument’s maker Olle Plahn, hence the dedication in the video.

Causeway Sunsets: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hFZWy-Nsgtk

Cheers,
Dick

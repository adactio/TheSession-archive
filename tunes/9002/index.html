Written by Rejean Brunet

The tune which usually follows it in a Les Freres Brunet set, Reel du Printemps, was written by his brother Andre (of the Celtic Fiddle Festival, of course). This tune is also found on ‘1,2,3’ by Les Freres Brunet among numerous other recordings

Re: Maison De Glace

Davis & Elkins College hosts the Augusta Heritage Festival, and has done for many years. In 1997 the Brunet Brothers were teaching there. There is a building called the Ice House where sessions and impromptu jams are held, and Rejean Brunet wrote the tune in that building. My husband attended Davis & Elkins and enjoyed many a night of music in the Ice House, and I attended a few myself at Irish Weeks at Augusta before it went defunct. At a recent contract Rejean Brunet called this tune the “Ice House Jig.”

Re: Maison De Glace

An Ice House is a storage shed (usually dug into the north side of a small mound) where blocks of ice were cut from a lake in winter, covered in straw, were stored and then used in iceboxes through the summer.

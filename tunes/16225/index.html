The Untamed Grasses

This is an Irish tune first released in 1993 by Sarah Bauhan on her album “Untamed Grasses,” which this tune, “The Untamed Grasses,” in relation to the album’s name.

I was showed this tune by my dad. He has many of Sarah Bauhan’s CD’s and this tune sticks out due to it’s odd cadence in the B-Part. Overall it is a lovely tune.

My family and I tend to play this tune a full step up, into the key of Am, but the original song is recorded in the key of Gm as shown.

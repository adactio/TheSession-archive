Eight comments

Singing Kettle

Liz Carroll taught this in a workshop in Tucson, Arizona in March 2007. Jerome Colburn transcribed it from the recording I made at the workshop. She couldn’t remember the name at the time but thanks to the group here on the session, that mystery was solved, as well as identifying Sean Ryan as the composer.

I have been playing it followed by The Harvest Moon.

Liz had some other variations that I didn’t learn at the time, and unfortunately, didn’t record.

Re: The Singing Kettle

The Singing Kettle is a composition of fiddler Sean Ryan.
It is the title track of a Chulrua record (follow the recordings link)
where it is played as a solo by Patrick Orceau (in a set with another S. Ryan tune).
It also appears in “Sean Ryan’s Dream” the second volume of the compositions of Sean Ryan.

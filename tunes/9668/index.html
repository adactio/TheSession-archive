Dublin Airport

This is a waltz written by Gerard Fahy and recorded by his sister Mairin Fahy on her CD “The Reel Thing”. I heard Mairin playing this in a solo concert while she was in New Zealand touring with Riverdance and it is now played in the local sessions quite a bit.

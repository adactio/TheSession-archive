Rondò Egelda

As played by Andrea Capezzuoli on the Accordion.
This tune is an adaptation for the dance (rondeau), made by the violinist Roberto Bagnasco, of a song by the Basque singer-song writer Mikel Laboa.
Transcription by Gian Marco Pietrasanta

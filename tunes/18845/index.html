Re: Land Of The Midnight Sun

Yeah, you are right. This is a tune from Cara, written by Gudrun Walther and Juergen Treyz. Although it is neither a traditional tune nor really appropriate for traditional sessions, it’s a lovely waltz. So I decided to transcribe and share it. Perhaps some people fancy to play this tune, too ;-)

And it’s actually attributed to Sweden. At least this is what Gudrun Walther mentioned during one of Cara’s gigs.

Demon Ducks Of Doom

An absolutely rocking reel written by fiddler Jamie McClennan, named after the nickname given to the extinct Australian megafauna Dromornis planei - 3-metre tall flightless birds that roamed the forests of the Northern Territory millions of years ago!

Definitely give his album In Transit a listen to see how many things he does with it: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9vB8mAvWLs

Slide For The Census

A report* in yesterday’s Guardian newspaper (UK) referred to a space in the recent Republic of Ireland census form where respondents could add a message of some sort that they would like preserved for posterity. One such item was a tune written, and posted on social media, by Naimh ni Charra. I have attempted to reproduce it here.

Unfortunately the screen shot of the MS shown in the Guardian missed the heading and first line, so I’ve taken certain liberties in submitting it. I’ve made a guess at the content of the first two bars, given it a title, and added some straightforward chords. My apologies to Naimh if any of this does violence to her composition, but I think it’s an uncomplicated and delightful slide, and seemed a good one to share with Sessioneers.

* https://www.theguardian.com/world/2022/apr/06/from-grief-to-paw-prints-people-share-ireland-census-time-capsule-messages

Reel Des Menteries, X:2

This setting appears in Olivier Demers, 1000 Airs du Québec et de l’Amérique francophone, tome 1, 2020, p. 240.
Normand Miron composed the tune; the fiddle is tuned in “grondeuse” which means ADAE.
A “Menterie”, old French word dating from the 13th century, means a lie (modern French: “mensonge”).
Menterie is still used in different parts of France and in Wallonia (French speaking Belgium, at least in Namur) as well as in Québec, I guess. In Namur dialect, menterie means a story mixing real stories, lies and humor.

Scread Na Bealtaine

Mairéad Ní Mhaonaigh (Altan) wrote this tune in May of 2020 during lockdown. Translates as ‘The Roar of May’ referring to the phrase which older people would use for the unusual sound of the wild wind that can occur in Co. Donegal during that month.

Notes

I learnt this tune from Anna Esslemont of Uiscedwr, and since bought some Kila CDs - which are absolutely great.

This tune sounds gorgeous on the flute and fiddle.

Written by Dee Armstrong of Kila it is the first tune on the ‘Dusty Wine Bottle’ track on their album ‘Tóg é go Bog é’.

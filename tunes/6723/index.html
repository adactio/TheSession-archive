Three comments

Megumi’s Gift

Megumi Suzuki was a Japanese student living in South Australia. Her life was tragically cut short in 2001. I wrote this tune in her memory, I thought I was writing an Irish reel, but when it came out it definately sounded Japanese. I have recorded this tune on my CD “Piper’s Dream”
I play it as a slow reel and I think it sounds best this way. I follow it with a traditional Japanese tune Sakura.

↳ Posted by JACKB on January 29th, 2007.

I feel this tune was given to me as a gift hence the title.

↳ Posted by JACKB on January 31st, 2007.

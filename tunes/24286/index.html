One comment

Angus Campbell

Hard to believe this tune isn’t here, but I can’t find it anywhere. This is a Scott Skinner tune, the reel version of his Laird of Drumblaire. I’ve transcribed this (in 4/4 rather than 2/4) from the original (https://www.abdn.ac.uk/scottskinner/display.php?ID=JSS0082) , but there are of course lots of versions. Quite a few performers substitute the second half of the strathspey for the second half of the A part or B part - so the quaver runs turn into fiendish triplets. Fun, but tricky! There are also some old time versions of this tune.

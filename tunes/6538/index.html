Origin of “Pigeons”

This came from the “Outlet” LP “Ar Leithidi”, which was mainly the O‘Brien family from Dublin, all the youngsters, including Mick and Donncha would hardly have been in their teens. This was a flute-solo by Sligo man Dan O’Healy, a guest on the record. I hadn’t come across the tune before and thought it worth posting. The album PaddyC has is mainly an “Outlet” compilation, and the track is the same.
I’ll post the LP in “Recordings” in the next week.

Another version from Kenny

Kenny says: “I found this [setting] on a tape from the Willie Clancy week in 1986. Clodach [Nic Ruairi] is a flute player from Derry who was in the same flute class as me that year. She was a member of the 80’ all-women ”supergroup“ ”Macalla", and was one of the players featured on the tribute album for Frankie Kennedy. She played this reel in a session with her sister Orla on fiddle and Dermot Byrne on accordion.

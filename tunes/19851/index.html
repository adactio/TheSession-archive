Sporting Galway

Composition of Carl Hession. This is not a setting, it is the original tune composed by Carl, including the chords. Carl is happy to share his tunes in this website, he kindly gave me the music score and helped me with the questions that arised in the making of the ABC notation.

I know that for many of you Carl doesn’t need any presentation, but for those that may not know him, here you are a Wikipedia link:

https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Hession

As you can see, the tune has interesting changes of key and F naturals. The music score doesn’t have any ornamentation, the triplets that exist should be taken as the original melody. You can listen a possible setting with ornamentation in this YouTube video where Haley Richardson plays the fiddle and Carl plays the piano accompaniment. It is the first tune in the set.

https://youtu.be/mVBMUNarncY

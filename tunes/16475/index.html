Five comments

Gan Ainm

This is a great eerie, spooky tune. I learned from a great concertina player named Olive O’ Mahoney, and I haven’t been able to stop playing it ever since! In the second part, where it has “d2 d d c b”, you can put a roll there instead of holding the d.

↳ Posted by Brianna Brown on November 2nd, 2017.

Also, it sounds very nice in the key of DMinor!

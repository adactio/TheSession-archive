Two comments

Heartstrings

This is the waltz played by Caitlín Nic Gabhann in her first recording : Caitlín.
She says : “I wrote this tune during the summer of 2011 and it takes the unusual form of ABACA.”

N.B. : After repeat, the first three bars (with the note D of fourth bar) of first line are played one octave lower.

One comment

Hollow Hills

A haunting solo air on low whistle by Davy Spillane, one of a number of pieces by him hidden away as a guest track on the album of other artists. This one is from the CD, Celtic Solstice by Paul Winter & Friends. As ever I’ve tried to be accurate in transcribing all his notes and ornaments though the timing is more free than the music would suggest. In the absence of another, I’ve used the symbol > for falling slides and the standard J for rising slides though the latter don’t come out in the music.

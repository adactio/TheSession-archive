Laster Arte

Xabi Aburruzaga wrote this beautiful wee tune for his album KeltiK. I’ve transcribed it from a live concert.
Originally in Eb, I’ve transposed it to G so it fits the whistle.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jQ9HWwVsZgw

Version from the album, with:
- Xabi Aburruzaga: Trikitixa
- Ed Boyd: Guitar
- Joaquín García: Double bass
- Brian Finnegan: Flute
- Alasdair Fraser: Fiddle
- Natalie Haas: Cello
- Ross Ainslie: Pipes
- Gus Sicard: Snare drum
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ALngMfcKX8s
This version with the pipes and the drum somewhat feels like a march to me.

The Moats Of Craan

A lovely jig, the third in the first set from John Redmond’s ‘Box Sets’. The Moats of Craan are a formation of rock tors in the Blackstairs mountains. John’s home place, Ballindaggin in County Wexford, is at the foot of the mountain range, which is popular with hill walkers and ramblers. Many of John’s titles for his compositions reflect his love for the beautiful wild landscape of the mountains on the Wexford/Carlow border.

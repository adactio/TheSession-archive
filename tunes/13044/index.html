Vas-y Mimile

Written by Marc Perrone.

↳ Posted by ndlxs on October 7th, 2013.

Re: Vas-y Mimile

written as a tribute to Emile Vacher, who created the style “musette” in the 1920-30es. Vacher used to play on a button box with stradella basses, a bit like Jimmy Shand - but his was tuned GCB, not BCC#. There’s a nice recording of him playing Mrs McLeod…

↳ Posted by Nikita Pfister on February 13th, 2018.

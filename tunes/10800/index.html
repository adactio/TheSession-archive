Larry plays some very nice unique items on the Comhaltas stuff, like the Queen of the Fairies set dance in Amin, complete with E in the third octave, which is surprisingly easy to pull off; also the Old Blackguard (“Monaghan Set Dance”) in G, again, with a bit of finger stretching on the E string to get up to high C.

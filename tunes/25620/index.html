Two comments

Black Haired Darling

I learned this tune years ago from a book of fiddle-cello arrangements called “The Celtic Collection” (!); it’s No. 454 in O’Neill’s. The Irish title is An ceann dubh dhileas. It’s in jig time, at least as transcribed, but it feels more like an air to me.

↳ Posted by Micah Walter on June 11th, 2025.

Black Haired Darling, X:2

This is a setting from Niel Gow’s Second Collection (Edinburgh 1988) under the title “Ceann Dubh Dileas” and it is obviously a sloooooow air. Micah’s setting above is the second in O’Neill’s Music of Ireland (Chicago 1903)*, the first one appearing as the tune before, No.453, and a third setting can be found at No.1836, closer to Gow but in D major, and titled “The Black Headed Darling.”

* = the 2nd note in Micah’s setting should be c sharp, the last note on the top line should be a D2.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on June 11th, 2025.

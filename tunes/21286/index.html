Two comments

The Coalyard

As played by Angelina Carberry and Dan Brouder on the 2014 album Pluckin’ Mad. They learned it “from the playing of Pip and John Murphy from Carrick on Bannow, Co. Wexford”. It was composed by their father Phil Murphy.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on December 1st, 2021.

The Coalyard, X:2

This is a setting notated from Pip Murphy playing this tune, composed by his father Phil. Or as Pip says on the recording of the concert, ‘put together’ by his father and named after a local spot down Bargy way.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on July 28th, 2022.

Hanter Dro ~ 3/4 previous, here follows the 3/2

That previous transcript ended up being M:3/4~L:1/8 not 3/2 as intended, my goof. I feel, as a dancer and a musician, that the treble, ‘3’, and not waltz time or ‘6’, somehow fits the dancing and the tune the hanter-dro best… Some other transcriptions I’ve seen of this form have had it as 6/8, like here, 6/4, as well as the curious combination of 4/4+2/4, as if it were mixed meter, which in the case of Hanter-Dro/An-Dro tunes/dances it is (= mixing treble with duple time, but not in the same vein as Sweifacher, definitely not the waltz and the polka…)

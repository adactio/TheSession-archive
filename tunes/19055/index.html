Maryann’s Choice

Composed by Colin Farrell, heard on YouTube: Colin Farrell’s Tune A Day 2020 Day 39
A friend of mine pointed it out to me, the B-part has a nice turn to minor chords (well, at least it could be done), and it wasn’t in The Session yet.
Also on CD “Music and Mischief”, first tune of second track.

Good Earth Below

Heard this tune at a festival in PA during an Irish Session. Written by Al Cofrin.
This tune is featured on an Earl Grey in the Morning CD, which a copy flew aboard the International Space station (ISS) during Expedition 27. The tune was written in honor of all the wonderful photographs taken of our beautiful Earth from orbit by ISS astronauts.

McNally’s Row Of Flats

Famously sung by Mick Moloney, here’s a jolly polka of a song by the late 19th century NYC songwriters Ed Harrigan and David Braham.

The first two parts are the song, the second two parts are the polka Mick and company intersperse after the second and fourth verses.

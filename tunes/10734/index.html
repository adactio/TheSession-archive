The Youngest Ancient Mariner

A beautiful tune from Phil Cunningham and I’m very surprised that it’s not up here already. More of an air than a waltz I’d say though. Sounds fantastic if you’ve got a bagpiper to join in after you’ve played it through once.

Yeah, but the B isn’t Bm - it’s the supertonic major (as opposed to V of V - there’s no E chord after it), which can be used occasionally for extra “cheese” near the end of a piece.
Also, for me, a lot of those C#m chords are going nowhere.

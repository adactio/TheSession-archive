The Gypsy

From the playing of Rum Ragged. Composed by Dave Panting.

Made a slight change to their setting. Rum Ragged go into the C Part, and repeat into it, with “AGxD E/F/GAB” (where x = F or E), which I’ve replaced with “AGxD E3B”.

The Cloone (take 2!!)

After a bit of confusion I have decided to submit this powerful tune again. See the later comments on this discussion here: https://thesession.org/tunes/1644

I got it from “The Missing Reel” which is a brilliant album by Sligo fiddle player Seamus McGuire and Leitrim flautist John Lee.

Really love the small run down in near the end of the B part, an absolute killer tune!

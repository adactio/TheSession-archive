Three comments

The North Sea Chinaman

Written by Jim Sutherland, and recorded by the Easy Club on their album Chance or Design. The sleeve says that the “North Sea Chinaman” was Willie Johnson from Shetland, though I’ve heard it may once have been used more generally to refer to Shetland seafarers.

↳ Posted by Tigereye on May 17th, 2025.

The North Sea Chinaman, X:2

Slightly different than Tigereye’s but probably same recording, from “Essential.” Added chords, but there’s no substitute for listening to the original to get the funky syncopation which notation lacks. This is a sort of a mashup of the melody which differs slightly between composer Sutherland on cittern (who occasionally just drops in offbeat bombs), fiddler John Martin, and even Rod Paterson (RIP) who ‘scats’ the melody and I can’t unhear his syncopated lead-ins on the A part. In set with the Linguist it is an all-time personal favorite, though sadly don’t hear it much in sessions.

↳ Posted by anikmuan on January 13th, 2026.

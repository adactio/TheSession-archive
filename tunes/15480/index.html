Four comments

The Visiting Kittens

Here’s a nice simple jig with obvious ties to many other tunes, among them Off She Goes. I learned it from fiddler Sara Piazza, who plays it before the Rolling Waves / Humors of Trim (Rolling Waves #2) / Behind the Haystack. It was supposedly composed by Maurice Lennon.

↳ Posted by Thady Quill on July 26th, 2016.

Played by Lexia Kennedy at the Midwest Fleadh: https://comhaltas.ie/music/detail/comhaltaslive_437_4_lexia_kennedy/

Two comments

Scan Tester’s Country Step Dance

A popular tune at the Golden Guinea pub session, Bristol (UK).

Lewis “Scan” Tester: 1886-1972
Country musician from Sussex, England
Played Anglo concertina, bandoneon, melodeon and fiddle. Also singer and step-dancer.

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scan_Tester

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on March 21st, 2015.

Scan Tester’s Country Step Dance, X:2

Used by the Northwest Morris side ‘Knots of May’ of Lewes, Sussex for their dance ‘Five Knots’, followed by the tune Churning Butter.

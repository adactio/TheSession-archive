Three comments

Butter And Peas

A nice reel from the playing of James Kelly from his Capel Street album (1989, https://thesession.org/recordings/1447). The second setting is from Zoe Conway’s eponymous 1982 album (https://thesession.org/recordings/391). She got the tune from James Kelly. Both play it with a swing.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on March 21st, 2014.

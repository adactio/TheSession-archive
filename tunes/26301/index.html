Re: Flowers Of Glasgow

This tune was written by Chris Perry (a.k.a. PeakFiddler) in 1995. A friend of his thought it sounded a bit like The Flowers of Edinburgh, so he named it The Flowers of Glasgow. He has a video lesson for it on his Patreon website (as “PeakFiddler”).

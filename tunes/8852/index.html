Three comments

Froggie on the Carport

This is one of my favorite contradance tunes. I learned it by ear, so I am not sure of the author. I like to do a slide up to the first A in the 6th bar of the b-section. It lays really well in thumb-position on double bass, too, if you are so inclined to try it!

↳ Posted by froglike on August 27th, 2008.

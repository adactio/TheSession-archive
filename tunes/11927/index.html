Collections, Collections & More Collections!

I found this beautiful Strathspey while looking through one of my most recent aquisitions, the William Shepherd Collection. Both volumes of this collection (published in 1793 and 1800, respectively) are available (PDF only) for FREE download from the good folks at The Highland Music Trust here:

http://www.heallan.com/freedownloads.asp

They also have about five other freebie collections on the site, as well as some other collections of note for sale. About William Shepherd, collector John Glen said “Although Mr. Shepherd claims to be the composer of the contents of both collections, it must be admitted that in some of his tunes there is a want of originality, while others show considerable excellence and taste in their composition.” I’ve only been through about a dozen or so tunes in this one, and it does seem to bear out Mr. Glen’s opinion. But even if there are only a handful of tunes in the collection as nice as this fine little Strathspey, it’s worth the “slog”, IMO! Enjoy!

-Jay

Incredible find!

Glad you found the link helpful, Q.I.- It seems that of late there has been a veritable flood of very old, very nice collections being made available via the internet. I’ve found that the quality of the dance music contained in these older volumes far exceeds that of any of the “new & cool” compositions being put out there now. I do a bit of composing in the Irish style myself (I’ve actually stopped posting them in the database here, but am toying with the idea of posting them in the details of my member profile), but I find that looking at these old tunes is a lot more inspiring than referencing any of these “clever” new ones. IMO, if the tune doesn’t sound like it could actually be old / traditional, then I got it wrong! It’s a shame that some of these older tunes have been forgotten, but it is my hope that folks will start digging through these fantastic old collections to find “new” tunes to play. Thanks should actually be given to B Rad who first emailed me the Highland Music Trust site (amongst many others! He seems to have built in “tunebook radar”…). There’s also some great Irish Collections available at Na Piobairi Uilleann’s website here:

http://www.pipers.ie/NPUPublic/Archive.aspx

Happy Hunting!

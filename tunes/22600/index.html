Kenny’s Gap

This is the second hornpipe of the set of two that feature on Track 8 from John Redmond’s solo composition album, ‘Box Sets’. John’s compositions are being notated and uploaded as part of the ‘Wexford Tunes Project’ which aims to collect all of the unique traditional music of the Model County together in one place. if you visit my profile page and load the ‘My Tunebook’ section, over 200 tunes, new and old, from County Wexford are available in one place and being regularly added to.

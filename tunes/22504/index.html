Two comments

The Adamstown Sisters

A nice tune composed by the London born piper Dicky Deegan. Dicky’s mother hails from Adamstown in Co. Wexford, hence the title of this reel off his album ‘Music of the Irish Celts’. Given his Wexford connections, and the Wexford title, I’ve notated the reel for inclusion in the Wexford Tune Project. Thanks to Clonroche uilleann piper Eanna Harrington, who currently works with Na Piobairi Uilleann in Dublin, for drawing my attention to Dicky’s music and this reel in particular.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on November 28th, 2022.

Re: The Adamstown Sisters

I recently got clarification from Dicky as to the origins of this tune, which it turns out is not his own composition but rather a version of a ‘remembered’ tune that he had heard years ago.

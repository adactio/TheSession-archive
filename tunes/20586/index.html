Four comments

Song For Rich

This is the bare-bones version, leaving room for ornaments and such.

The song was written by Michael W. Smith in memory of the late Rich Mullins, whose music often reflected Celtic and Appalachian roots. It appears on Smitty’s album, “Live the Life.”

↳ Posted by greatawk on March 16th, 2021.

Song For Rich, X:2

This version has some of the ornaments used on the album; use at your own disgression.

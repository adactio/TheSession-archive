Tommy Peoples

This is notated from the Rogha Scoil Samhraidh Willie Clancy 2007 double CD. https://thesession.org/recordings/3433

It’s listed as “The Kinnycally Klansmen”, which is a reel he wrote, also notated on this site; you can see where the confusion arises.

A further note: it appears he had his fiddle tuned up a semitone, so the recording resolves to concert pitch Ebm.

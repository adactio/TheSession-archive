This is another composition of the County Wexford musician Cian O‘Grady. It’s included in ’The Wexford Tunebook’ collection, recently published. Cian’s compositions are copyrighted and would require consent for recording etc.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 24th, 2024.

Re: Confused

John, you might want to clarify your understanding of copyright law a little. Once publicly performed, copyrighted compositions don’t require permission to be performed or recorded (although the necessary fees do have to be paid). What *does* require permission, in theory at least, is publishing the sheet music (on, for example, a publicly accessible website…)

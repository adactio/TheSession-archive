One comment

Michael Tierney’s

This reel of my own composition is included in the recently published ‘ Wexford Tunebook’. I composed this in memory of Mick Tierney, owner of The Centenary Stores pub in Wexford. He sadly passed away in recent years and was a great supporter of Irish trad music in the Wexford locality, hosting regular sessions over the years at his pub. He would have loved being in the thick of the Fleadh Cheoil being held in Wexford and was sorely missed.

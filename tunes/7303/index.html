Five comments

Piddellipom

This is a composition of Carina Normansson from Swåp. I’ve seen it referred to as a hornpipe before, but I think it sounds more like a schottische. The 1st part repeats, but the 2nd part doesn’t, so it’s like a schottische hybrid, being more barndance-like in the 1st part, and more fling-like in the 2nd. There aren’t any Scotch snaps in the recording, but the schottische-like dance in the tune lends itself to them quite well, and I find myself putting them in automatically, something like this:

X: 1
T: Piddellipom
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: barndance
K: Amaj
(3AGF|E2 (3EFG A2A>c|B>AB<c A3c|d>cd>e f2f2|f>ed>c B2 (3AGF|
E2 (3EFG A2A>c|B>AB<c A3c|d>ef>e d>cB>A|B2A2 A2:|

Petranu-Valse (c. Oliushka Tikhaia)

A beautiful tune with a sad story. Oliushka wrote this waltz for her elder cousin Petran who suffered severe traumatic brain injury in a car crash over twenty years ago, leaving him paralyzed and unable to live without constant care. Petran can’t talk, can’t get up on his own and can hardly see anything but he’s well able to listen and can sometimes communicate with others by scribbling messages on paper. This way we know he appreciates Oliushka’s little house concerts at his birthday parties and this waltz in particular.

The tune was written on the nyckelharpa so it could get a bit tricky for some flutes – but fiddlers and mandolinists are in for a treat!

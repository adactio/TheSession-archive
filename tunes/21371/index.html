The Dawning Of The Day, X:2

This version is #38 in “Waifs and Strays of Irish Melody”, O‘Neill’s 1922 collection, taken from “Aird’s Selection of Scotch, English, Irish and Foreign Airs, Vol.3”, published in 1788. O’Neill notes: “As the above setting differs materially from that of Bunting in his second collection issued in 1809, and others much more recent, its introduction among Waifs and Strays may be not without interest to students of Irish musical history.”

Re: The Dawning Of The Day

Setting 1 is #643 in “O’Neill’s Music of Ireland: 1850 Melodies” (1903) and is nearly identical to that in Bunting’s second collection of “The Ancient Music of Ireland” (1809) (in E flat). That book is available online through the Irish Traditional Music Archive: https://www.itma.ie/texts/bunting_2/ -- view the PDF full-screen and scroll down to page 103; it’s page 53 of the manuscript.

Setting 2, just posted, is #38 in O’Neill’s “Waifs and Strays” (1922). The Traditional Tune Archive has that same melody (both from Aird’s 1788 collection) along with fascinating history, at https://tunearch.org/wiki/Annotation:Dawning_of_the_Day_(1)_(The)

Both of the sources for X:2 say it is “reputed to have been composed by Thomas O‘Connellan [died 1698], a 17th century harper from County Sligo who spent considerable time in Scotland.” O’Neill’s attribution in 1922 is an update from his 1903 “1850 Melodies” collection, where X:1 was in the “O’Carolan’s Compositions” section.

As Nigel G noted, this melody does have a great deal in common with https://thesession.org/tunes/4710 “Sitting in the Stern of a Boat”. That air was composed by the Rev. William McLeod (a minister from the Isle of Skye, off the coast of Scotland) in the late 1700s… a century after O’Connellan’s time.

Although they have the same title in Irish and English, this melody is very different from https://thesession.org/tunes/1441 “Fáinne Geal An Lae” -- AKA Raglan Road, in the Goodman manuscripts, and possibly the different tune janglecrow mentioned? Surprisingly, despite having the English title “The Dawning of the Day” as an alternate title, that tune doesn’t show up on thesession in search results for that phrase.

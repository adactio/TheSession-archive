Snouts And Ears

Another well known tune.

I’ve submitted it as a polka here but, arguably, it’s more of a 2/4 reel. It’s still got a bit of a polka feel to it though.

It is also known as “Snouts and Ears of America” by “old time” banjo players and the like but is popular too Scottish music having been recorded by Jimmy Blue(Thanks Nigel G) among others..

The above setting is part of a set I learned along with Hunter’s Hill, and C.M. Hall

Re: Snouts And Ears

from the fiddle of Sarah Armstrong, collected by Samuel Bayard in the 1940s.

https://www.banjohangout.org/archive/283822
from his long out of print book, “Hill Country Tunes”, available here:
https://www.mne.psu.edu/lamancusa/tunes/hct/
It goes together well with “Maggots in the Sheep Hide”, also printed in Hill Country Tunes. These two tunes are good beginning fiddler tunes also. They are both in D in this book.

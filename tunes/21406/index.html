Edwin Flaws Of Wyre

There aren’t enough Freeland Barbour tunes posted here.

This is one of his more popular pieces dedicated to Edwin Flaws of Rousay and also of the legendary Wyre Band.

It has also been found in more “friendly” keys and I have a D version somewhere which I’ll post later if it comes to hand. However, it’s fairly easily transposed.

In the setting I have, the tune is played all the way through and we usually return to the beginning and finish after playing the first part again twice.
I haven’t indicated this in the notation as I don’t think Jeremy likes the “de capo al fine” thing.
;-)

Edwin Flaws Of Wyre, X:2

Here it is in D, approximately as given in ‘The Orkney Collection’ (Orkney Traditional Music Project‘. ISBN 1-902957-13-X*). It notes that ’Freeland is well known a a fine solo performer and as a record and radio producer [and] an accordion tutor at the Royal Scottish Academy of Music in Glasgow’.

* I’d recommend this book, if it’s still available. There are some gems there.

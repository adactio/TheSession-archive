The Amazing Adventures Of Dr Moriarty, X:3

The bones of the tune as played by Tony Sullivan off Sully’s Fancy. It appears that the additional measure at the end of the second part was a later addition; this isn’t a crooked tune on the original recording. Sully also took it AABB with both parts doubled, although an ABB structure also sounds fine to my ear since the first part repeats exactly.

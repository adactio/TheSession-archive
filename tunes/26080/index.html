The Colonel’s Delight

This is a 3-part reel in G mixolydian. “The Colonel’s Delight” is James Kelly’s name for the tune, so-called since it seems to be related to both Colonel Fraser (https://thesession.org/tunes/1209, usually 5 parts in G major) and Mother’s Delight (https://thesession.org/tunes/257, usually 2 parts in D dorian) but is different enough from both of those to be considered a separate tune. There’s a private recording of Michael Coleman playing this tune which is on the Coleman Heritage Center release The Enduring Magic.

Another name (perhaps the original name) for the tune is Malloy’s (or Molloy’s) Favourite, so even though this tune has been recorded a few times on commercially-released albums, the thesession.org discography pages for many of those albums currently link incorrectly to the more well-known tune called Molloy’s Favourite (https://thesession.org/tunes/1941) which I don’t think has any relation to this one.

This setting was transcribed from a video recording of James Kelly on YouTube:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-SaEfatCG0Y

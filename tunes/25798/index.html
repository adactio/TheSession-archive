Tha Biodag Aig MacThòmais, X:4

The great fiddler Peter Milne published his setting of this tune under the title of “Hobble Jenny” in 1870 (setting X:4). One of the earliest published examples was in MacGlashan’s Collection of 1778, where it was called “Canty Body,” while shortly afterwords it appeared in Cumming’s 1780 collections as “Ardnilly’s Reell.” A fragment appears in a manuscript dating from circa 1760. An early recording is by the Lovat Bagpipe Band of New York, who recorded it in c1917.

The MacThòmais in question was memorialised in a 17th century song; he was ridiculed at a dance (because, it seems, he wore a dirk rather than a sgian dubh), and used his dirk to slay the piper who slighted him. The good old days.

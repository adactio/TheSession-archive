Three comments

Paddy Ahern’s

An unnamed polka from Newcastle West fiddler Paddy Ahern’s manuscripts collected by his students John/Sean Walker and George Walker in the period between 1946-1952. The manuscript version is in this high key; I’ll add a few transpositions as well.

↳ Posted by benhockenberry on August 23rd, 2024.

G transposition with some tweaks and bowings removed.

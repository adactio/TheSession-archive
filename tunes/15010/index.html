Two comments

The Spaniard

Best tune I’ve heard in ages. It comes from the recording “Magnetic South”, by “The Four Star Trio”, which I bought at the Willie Clancy week last year. It kind of slipped off the radar for a while, but re-appeared a week ago. I don’t have the CD any more, so can’t say whether it’s traditional or not, possibly a composition of Johnny McCarthy or Con O‘Driscoll. If it is traditional, it’s been well hidden up until now. If anyone has that CD, and can look at the “digipak” sleeve notes, I’d love to know its’ origins. It is preceded on the CD by another hornpipe I haven’t come across before - I’ll try to post it soon.

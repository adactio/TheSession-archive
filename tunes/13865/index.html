MacPherson’s Rant - another source

Hey. Nigel, I thought I would share this since you were interested in the origins of the tune.

Just now as I was listening to Gordon Duncan’s album “Just for Seamus,” I stopped in my tracks when I heard this tune. So I pulled out the album notes to see if there is anything enlightening. It is in the set “The Last Tango in Harris / The Ballinvanich Reel / McPherson’s / Good Drying. ” But the notes just say McPherson’s - Reel, Traditional.

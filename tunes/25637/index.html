The Farewell

X:1 There are other tunes with this title on the site, but they’re different (a march and a jig). This one is by piper Pat McNulty, originally published in his 1965 compilation ‘A collection of the Dance Music of Ireland’. (I have the 4th edition, Ossian, 1988). Listed here as a polka*, but I don’t believe it should be played at polka tempo. I like it slower, and with a fairly free rhythm. I’ve added the chord suggestions: use or ignore as you will.

*That’s just because it’s notated in the book in 2/4 with semiquavers as the basic note value; I don’t know if that can be represented as such here, but it probably doesn’t matter.

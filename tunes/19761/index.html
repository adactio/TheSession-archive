Bob’s Garden Of Earthly Delights

From John Whelan’s 1999 Come To Dance album.

↳ Posted by Joseph Donahue on September 20th, 2020.

Bob’s Garden Of Earthly Delights, X:2

Love this tune--just a smooth, driving rhythm except for the high Bs in the B part (measure four), which come across as syncopated. My approach to arranging these days (for the hammered dulcimer) is to frame every eighth note to keep a driving rhythm, leaving ornaments as appropriate to the musician and the moment. I also noted that this should be in A minor.

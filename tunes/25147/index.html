Two comments

Bunker’s Driveway

On the album As the Crow Flies by Crowfoot, it is the reel following the jig named The Farmer’s Joy.

↳ Posted by Sylvain Lair on January 10th, 2025.

Bunker’s Driveway, X:2

Adding the correct setting of this tune, as notated in the Crowfoot Tunebook. (This was the ultimate challenge in ABC notation!)
Adam had this to say about the tune: “Written for our friend, John Bunker, who lives happily amongst his apple trees at the end of a winding and bouncy dirt road in Maine. John is as wise and honest as the day is long, and like a true supporter of the arts, let Jaige and I live for a few years in a cabin on his land. After many a car ride to and fro, it seemed only fitting to write a ‘crooked’ tune in honor of his notorious driveway.”

↳ Posted by eileen o'grady on September 6th, 2025.

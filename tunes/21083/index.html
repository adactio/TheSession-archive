Five comments

Uaine Bheag

This is transcribed from Slide’s album, Harmonic Motion (2003), and it must be an original but I couldn’t find the composer listed so I just put Slide there in the notation. Lovely tune, which I liked in part because of the quicker tempo and sort of dark, intense performance.

↳ Posted by Annie Davey on October 6th, 2021.

Re: Uaine Bheag

Hi Annie - by a strange coincidence, I got this CD from eBay a few weeks back and have been listening to it recently. This piece of music was “Composed and string arrangement by Daire Bracken [ the band’s fiddler ], arranged by ”Slide". It is followed by some Irish Gaelic, which someone else will have to translate :
“Scriobhadh ab valsa seo in am feithimh. Do ghach duine ag suil le sceal, ole no maith”.

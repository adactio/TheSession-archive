Polska From Ornunga, X:2

I learned this at Ransätter festival in Sweden played in the very early morning to a few dancers. I know it as the Scafferi Polska but I can’t remember where that name came from. It’s a very funky tune. I’ve tried to give a sense of how I learned it and how we play it in Lancaster..
On the mandolin or fiddle, you can play it an octave down for effect.

Two comments

The Magic Circle

Another reel composed by Darach de Brun, he gave me the sheet music to this a few years ago. I hope I’ve transcribed this correctly, as the software Darach was using was a bit unclear with a few of the notes, so I’ve had to use a bit of judgement. I didn’t think this was one of Darach’s best tunes, but after playing it a few times, it has grown on me.

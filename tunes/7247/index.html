Recorded by the Glenlomond Scottish Band in 1968 as part of a medley. Lively tune…

↳ Posted by Steven Hawson on April 15th, 2010.

Re: Lady Carmichael

Battlfield Band also recorded it on “At the Front” in the late 1970s. It’s in a set with, I think, “Mickey Ainsworth.” There’s a nice setting in the J&R Glen Collection of Highland pipe music. You can find that on Ceol Sean (ceolsean.net). There’s a more ornamented version in the David Glen Collection under the name “Lady Carmichael of Castle Craig.” That can also be found at ceolsean.net.

Three comments

The Furrowed Brow

This tune is an original composition by Rod Paul.
I got it from The Iron Horse 1994 album ‘Five Hands High’.
It is played in C minor on the album.
I brought it down 1/2 a tone to make it easier to read for whistle players.
It is in 2 parts but with an extended variation the second time around, so you could call it a reel in 4 parts.
Parts 3 and 4 display an interesting [3/4 + 3/4 + 2/4] effect against the background 2/2.
It is the second reel in a set of three, all three written by Rod Paul.
How the tune got its name is everyone’s guess.
(Once I slowed down the track it was easier to transcribe than I first perceived, so I did not have to do too much knitting with my eyebrows… (-: )

Steve Quinn’s Fancy

This is a Paddy O‘Brien tune from Eileen’s Gleas album (Boruma Trio _ Andrew MacNamara (accordion), Eileen O’Brien (fiddle), Geraldine Cotter (whistle, piano)). I like the way it twists the rhythm sense.

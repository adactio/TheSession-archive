Seven comments

New-York Harbour

A beautiful waltz penned by William Jackson (of Ossian fame) circa 1986.

It is in D ionian and has 16+24 = 40 bars.
It precedes the jig ‘At work on the land’ in a very evocative conceptual set called ‘Light on a Distant Shore’.
This transcription is based on the third reiteration, from 1’35 approximatively.
In this repeat, the fiddle plays harmony to the flute, using a melodic line heard on the ‘Arrival’ part of the set. Nice stuff.

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on June 24th, 2023.

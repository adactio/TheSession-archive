Barrel Rafferty’s (jig)

From the playing of Mike and Mary Rafferty: http://comhaltas.ie/music/detail/comhaltaslive_242_5_mike_rafferty_on_irish_flute Tom “Barrel” Rafferty was Mike Rafferty’s father and played a flute and pipes. I believe he was a neighbour of Lucy Farr in East Galway.

The Boys of the Town has a similar first part, but is different in the second part. https://thesession.org/tunes/1264

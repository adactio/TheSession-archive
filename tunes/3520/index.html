Re: Lynch’s

The A part of this is very close to the ‘Hopscotch’ schottische* from the repertoire of the concertina player, Scan Tester (1887-1972) - a tune played frequently in sessions in Sussex. There’s a setting at: http://www.rudemex.co.uk/library/Alphabetical/Scan%20Tester’s%20‘Hopscotch’%20schottische.pdf It’s also reproduced in the Lewes Favourites collection, Vol 2 (ed. Andy Warburton).

*Scan didn’t name or number his tunes, so this would be a later identifier and is how it’s referred to locally.

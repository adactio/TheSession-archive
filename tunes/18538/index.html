Eyam Polka

A tune written by Alistair Anderson some 20 odd years ago for a festival at the plague village of Eyam in Derbyshire. Alistair bought it to the band class he was running at the Caedmon Hall in Gateshead but it doesn’t seem to be recorded anywhere. It is interesting because there are few North-eastern polkas.

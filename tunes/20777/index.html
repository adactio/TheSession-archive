My Love In The Morning

This slide is based on the playing of the Four Star Trio. It bears comparison with the jig “This is My Love, Do You Like Her” https://thesession.org/tunes/6 , but is sufficiently different to warrant a different entry. All of the 31 versions of that tune have the same move to the C natural (relative major?), if in Ador, in the second bar; that is absent in this tune. Paudie O’Connor plays a similar setting.

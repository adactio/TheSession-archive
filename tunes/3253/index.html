“The Pride of Erin” ~ A Dorian and other options

Thanks to concertinaplayer for the reminder. I first heard and learned this tune from “The Pride of Erin Ceilidh Band” lot, including the Nugents, fine musicians, instrumentalists and singers, and dancers too ~ in their lot, great characters and generous hearts…

Here it is up higher in another key, A Dorian, and with some other ways I’ve been remembering. I’m not sure where the second ending came from, but that is how it came on me last night trawling the dark waters of my mind for a name for concertinaplayer’s tune in search of a name. It can be played with or without it ~

Hi didier - I will email her and ask her and get back to you.
However, Larry Nugent’s sleeve notes say that he learned this tune “from a recording of the Pride Of Erin Ceili Band of which my late father Sean Nugent was leader” - as “Brendan” mentioned above 20 years ago. The sleeve notes do not say that his father composed it. However, the very next tune he plays following this on his CD was “The Monument”, of which he says “The last jig on this track was composed by my father and was named after a dolmen on the top of Larghy Mountain in Lack, Co. Fermanagh”. Don’t you think that if he stated that his father had composed the third tune in the set, he would have credited his father with the second tune also if indeed Sean Nugent had composed it ? That would seem to be the logical conclusion to me, but Sharon will know. Watch this space.

↳ Posted by Kenny on February 24th, 2024.

@kenny

I must admit that I am red with shame because I recently received the book of Sean’s compositions in which Catherine McLaughlin, one of his daughters, included clearly a tune by that name. But, contrarily with the tune of the present publication, the tune in that book is a march in 4/4… and I should have noticed that !
So both of the sources concerning these “homonymous” tunes are perfectly right.
I asked Laurence (Nugent) about who was Jimmy McHugh and he very kindly answered me :
“That is a jig the one from the session . That is a Jimmy McHugh tune . Knew him very well , he was originally from Co.Tyrone . But he lived in Glasgow Scotland most of his life . He would come back to Ireland regularly . Was a very good friend of my dads and would be in our house a lot on his visits home”
Hope this makes it clear !

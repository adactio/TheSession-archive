Milltown Bridge

A reel composed by Nóra Byrne Kavanagh, who is originally from Ferns in Co. Wexford and now living near Waterford, where she is a music lecturer in South East Technical University. Milltown Bridge is between Ferns and The Harrow, close to Nóra’s home place. Nóra’s compositions are copyrighted and would require consent for recording or republication. She has kindly included a selection of her traditional compositions for inclusion in the Wexford Tunes Project database.

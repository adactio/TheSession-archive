Paddy Fahey’s

This is from Dave Flynn’s album, “Contemporary Traditional Irish Guitar”. I tried to contact Dave to ask but I believe that this must be a Fahey tune composed after the Maria Holohan dissertation and that Dave used her index as a reference to title this Paddy Fahey’s No. 30. There are many other Fahey tunes on this recording and the others do match the Holohan index.

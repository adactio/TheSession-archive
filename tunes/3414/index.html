Re: Hurlock’s

Composed by Tom Anderson in 1938.
As well as playing bass in the Lerwick Orchestra, Harry Hurlock played drums in Davie Robertson’s dance band in which Tom was the fiddler.

That’s all according to The Tom Anderson Collection Volume Two, published in 2004.

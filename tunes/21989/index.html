The Pneumatic Drills

Written by Ewen Henderson. From the playing of the Battlefield Band on their album “Enough Room for All”. Track 2.3.

The sleeve notes say: “The Pneumatic Drills is a jig Ewen composed to honour the Glasgow based band in which he played with his friends when he was not travelling”.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IPlr8rsHalM

On the recording, they play the tune twice and then they play a variation that I’m considering to be parts C an D. Ewen does the same on his album Steall.

The structure would be (including repeats):
2x(AB) CD

Coinleach Glas An Fhómhair

This was noted by ear, listening to Clannad’s rendition in ‘Clannad 2’ (1974). It’s not a reel, but there’s no option ‘slow air’ in the tune adding form. This setting doesn’t have the waltz 3/4 metre either, but common time or 4/4 metre. I believe Máire Ní Bhraonáin used to sing it rather freely--and beautifully--in those days before Clannad embarked on this so-called ‘Celtic’ / New Age wave… Make sure to play it very slowly, no faster than Q:1/4=72. On the piano, it sounds amazing.

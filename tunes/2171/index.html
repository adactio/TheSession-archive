I’m coming very late to this party, Gian Marco. But just a quick word to say that I have a lot of time for this tune. *A LOT* On mandolin, I find the “A_A|GE” transition between bars 6 and 7 of the second part a little tricky to play and I’d probably simplify to “AA|GE” if required to play it at anything approaching session-speed. It loses a little bit of colour that way but it’s much more playable under the (increasingly) sausage-y fingers of my left hand.

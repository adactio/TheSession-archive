Turniskey Lasses

Great tune from the repertoire of Peter Turbit of Co. Tyrone. This comes from the book the Northern Fiddler : Music and Musicians of Donegal and Tyrone. Peter states in the book that it was played by a man he learned from named Ned Turbit (no indication given that they were related). It is a really old tune.

As an aside for you language junkies out there, another spelling I have found is “Tirnaskea” which from the implied Irish would be Tir na Sce (or Sceach) which traslates roughly as “Land/Country of the Briars” or “Land/Country of the Thornbush” I could not find such a place name on any of my maps or through the internet. Any info from the Tyrone folks here would be much appreciated.

Re: Turniskey Lasses

The more I play the tune, the more I like the high b.
The tune’s in Em, not A Dorian.
The high B implies a Bm chord there. Changing it to an A implies a Dmaj chord.
Both work.
Or mix them up for a different feel.

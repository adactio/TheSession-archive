Two comments

A Sunny Day In April

I wrote on, no surprise, a sunny day in April. I meant just to write something cheerful and quaint, but it didn’t quite work out exactly as I planned. The cheerfulness stayed, but it lost all its quaintness during the process. As I type this comment, I can hear the sound of a multitude of lawn mowers. There seems to be a disturbance in the force, like the sound of thousands of blades of grass crying out in pain…

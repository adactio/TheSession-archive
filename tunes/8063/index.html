Four comments

Dry Bread and Pullit

I heard this played by flute player Garry Walsh on BBC Radio Scotland, and thought it was a great tune - I couldn’t find it here already. I don’t know anything about him or the tune. Garry plays rolls on the longer notes, and everything is played with a very slight swing.

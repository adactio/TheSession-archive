Five comments

Old Newfoundland tune. Often played as a ‘’single‘’ for those of you familiar with the Newfoundland dance traditions. The title of this tune is shared with a completely different melody.

The folk song ‘’The Captain & His Whiskers" is played to the melody ‘’Rising of The Moon‘’

↳ Posted by willi_seidel on November 12th, 2010.

Re: Papichon

It’s an old debate but this tune defo ticks the box ‘Scottische’… albeit in the French (and European) ‘Bal Folk’ sense of the term.
The Sottische dance evolved from the allemande and the polka and like its sister the waltz, its exact shape kept morphing and evolved into so many localised forms like the german, the barndance, the American and French Scottische, Escosesas, etc.
The old tunes adapted to the new steps (or the local deportment and ethos) while new ones were composed to meet the new criteria.
And this is how we “end” (although the story is far from over) with something like Papichon.

Maurice Lennon Tune

I learnt this tune from Maurice Lennon in the Meitheal Summer School last year. I probably haven’t remembered it all the same as he taught me, especially the link between the 3rd & 4th parts. That bit’s mine, cos I couldn’t remember it at all. I really like the 3rd part, the way it goes all dorian and stuff.

Does anyone know this tune’s name?

Four comments

The Green Shades of Gask

This Scottish jig was learnt from Iain MacDonald and Iain MacFarlane’s flute and fiddle duet playing on their album “The First Harvest.” You can watch them play it on youtube, but the sound isn’t very great.

↳ Posted by slainte on May 10th, 2009.

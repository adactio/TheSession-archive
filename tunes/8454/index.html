Forgotten fling

It’s a very beautiful slow air played on the low whistle by Dervish, as an intro to “The jolly tinker”. A nice set !

↳ Posted by Jerome Lowhistler on April 17th, 2008.

Forgotten Fling, X:2

This is the tune played by Connie O‘Connell, Áine Ni Chonaill and friends on the album ’Ceol Cill na Martra‘. They play it very much as a reel. I believe it was written by Connie O’Connell. Cill na Martra in County Cork is where Connie is from.

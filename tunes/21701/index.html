Re: Fàg A’ Phiob Bhochd

Yes; one of the tragedies of the neglect of Gaelic in the 20th century is that most native speakers were never trained in Gaelic orthography - I don’t think it’s any criticism of Allan to notice his writing isn’t always consistent. Fag is an interesting one - it’s in the dictionaries as fàg but offhand I think I generally hear it as fag, with a short a, and more often than not written as such. One of the great things about the traditional orthography, before the GOC ruined it, was that it allowed native speakers to write as they spoke.

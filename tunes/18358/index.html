Four comments

Wet The Tea

From the playing of Garry Walsh.

Probably my favourite of the bunch so far.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on September 10th, 2019.

Wet The Tea, X:2

Transposed down to Amin/Ador.

Knew it sounded rather familiar, it’s basically a different version of Sean Ryan’s The Castle (https://thesession.org/tunes/273 ), having a similar melody with different phrasing in several bars. Would say it’s a different, yet, quite related tune.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on September 16th, 2019.

Wet The Tea, X:3

Setting with half of the original B Part removed, and the other half of it repeated. A few other small details also changed.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on September 16th, 2019.

Wet The Tea, X:4

Variations from the playing of Garry Walsh.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on June 20th, 2020.

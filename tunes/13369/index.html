Brendan Tonra, 1935-2014…The music lives on

This is one of Brendan Tonra’s more recent compositions, a set dance in jig time. The title refers to a childhood memory of looking outside at said waterfowl on a rainy day and wishing he could be outside with them. To get an idea of the right tempo, you can watch a clip of Brendan playing the tune with Helen Kisiel on piano & stepdancer Kieran Jordan dancing (she actually choreographed the dance herself; you will no doubt see her sense of humor when you watch the steps in relation to the title!):

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IbDEaGi-84Q

Enjoy!

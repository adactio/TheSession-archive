Kung Fu Katie

A reel composed by Conal Ó Gráda. Basic transcription form his playing here: https://youtu.be/6Y8ozkAThEo . See also: https://thesession.org/discussions/45415 .

The first group of notes (dgB2) of the B part is sometimes played dcB2 (a c natural shows up).

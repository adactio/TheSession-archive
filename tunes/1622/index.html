Re: Molly From Longford

This tune (parts reversed) is in O‘Neill’s Waifs and Strays of Gaelic Melody (#308) and was taken from Patsy Tuohey’s manuscript. O’Neill adds a note that it is a variant on Pay the Girl Her Fourpence (DMI.804) but this is a stretch (IMHO) as only the first parts are similar.

↳ Posted by brailsford on January 20th, 2024.

Re: Molly From Longford

The cylinder recording of Patsy Touhey playing this may be heard here: https://www.itma.ie/playlists/touhey-patsy/ It is also transcribed in The Piping of Patsy Touhey.

A single jig version from a piper from the old days, Brother Gildas, may be found in An Píobaire Vol 2 no. 2, and also in Ceol Rince na hÉireann IV, no. 74. Gildas was a De La Salle brother, he of the title the Brother’s Jig. Gildas learned this tune from Belfast piper/pipemaker R.L. O’Meally. Ironically Gildas was not a Touhey fan; when asked if his 78s were an influence on him he said “No, I was listening to piping.”

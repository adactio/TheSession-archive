Eight comments

Connor Bones

I composed this reel, with the help of two young students, Jenny Connor & Beth McMullan, during a quiet winter’s evening class, a couple of years ago, & the kids still play it as a favourite. It lends itself to some nice double stopping, & uses plenty of those gutsy low notes in the first half. - & the explanation for the tune title, for the record, Beth’s grandad was a Co. Antrim Butcher.

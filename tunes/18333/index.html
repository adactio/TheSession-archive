Basically, they’ve shuffled some of the components of X1 around for the A Part, and adjusted pick-up notes. They instead start on Bar 3 and go up until bar 7 (of X1), then bar 2 (of X1), then bar 1 (of X1), and end with bar 8 (of X1).

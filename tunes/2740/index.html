The Quayside

I’m going through a James Hill phase at the moment, relearning some of his hornpipes and having a go at new ones, as there is a local fiddler who sometimes plays hornpipes in weird keys and I always feel as though I’ve heard them before and should learn them. They’re fantastic tunes! This is one isn’t in a weird key but I’ve been meaning to learn it for a while off a Syncopace CD.

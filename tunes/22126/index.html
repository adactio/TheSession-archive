Unnamed Quadrille (Kilmore Manuscript)

A tune labelled ‘Quadrille’ in the Power manuscript from Kilmore in County Wexford. The manuscript has music notated by a number of different hands with an earliest date of 1872 on work in the middle of it, 1921 on a tune a bit further on and I suspect the later material to have been notated in the 1920’s or 30’s, given the style of cursive handwriting used in the last section. The manuscript was found in the thatched roof of a mud walled house in Kilmore Village some years ago.
I’m wondering if anyone has a name or recognizes this little tune. I feel it has a Scottish feel to it, and in some ways too sounds like it may be found in American Old-Time?

↳ Posted by John E Roche on June 28th, 2022.

Re: Gan Ainm

Hello Conor,
Isn’t that again very interesting? That’s several matches now, suggesting that at least some of the Wexford material and the Longford material has to have a common source for transcription in to the manuscripts…I personally think (though I’ve no evidence to back this up as such) that the earliest section of the Wexford manuscript may have been compiled by a military bandsman, posted to Wexford, perhaps a Scot, given the mix of musical forms in the first section. Maybe his music collection was then passed on or even acquired by a Wexford musician who added in the next 15 or 20 pages of notation. Then the third person involved in notation in the late teens early 1920’s may have been a relative of the middle one. Just my own theory on the curious mixum-gatherum of material, genres, handwriting and music notation styles in there.

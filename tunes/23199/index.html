Seahorses Of Cullenstown

A reel composed by John Browne from Bannow in Co. Wexford. Cullenstown is a well-known beauty spot and beach, already familiar to traditional musicians due to Phil Murphy’s tune‘ ’The Trip to Cullenstown‘. John is a fine whistle player and guitarist, playing with the group ’Bar Of Lough’ which includes John Murphy, son of Phil’s, on mouth organ. Thanks to Nóra Byrne Kavanagh for notating the tunes that have come my way lately from the Bannow area for inclusion in the Wexford Tune Project.

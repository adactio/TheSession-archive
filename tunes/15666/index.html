Floating From Skerry

Transposed by myself from cassette “the Kathryn Tickell Band” side 2, track 3. Tune written by Lynn Tocker when she was fourteen years old after a boat journey from the Skerry Islands. The sleeve notes give no more information, not even the names of band members are specifically named.
After playing as many times as one wishes the whole piece finishes on the ‘A’ music

I like playing a few tunes together and, purely because I know it, I have followed it with “Inisheer” which follows on nicely from the ‘A’ music of “Floating from Skerry”

I would welcome any suggestions for other tunes to be put with it.

Having watched youtube clips I reckon Katherine’s rendering to be the best one.

Two Little Ducks

A fun reel featured in this FleadhTV clip from 2019, played by Laoise Ní Chinnéide after the Tommy Peoples jig Don’t Touch that Green Linnet: https://youtu.be/56ZcT2lq-sk.

In a Tune ID discussion (https://thesession.org/discussions/47430), Kenny proposed that it may have been composed by Peter Mohan who plays it on this track ( https://youtu.be/_oCuBlPvKrI ). Anyone with more information, please share!

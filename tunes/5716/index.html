The Prohibition (reel)

Source: At It Again by John Carty
Transcription: gian marco pietrasanta

↳ Posted by gian marco on April 30th, 2006.

Martin Mulvihill’s tunebook has a good version of the Prohibition in G which, bumped up a step, is very much like the Michael Coleman/Tom Gannon 78 recording.

So I’ve written out the book version in A with a tweak or two based on the record:

X: 1
T:Prohibition, The
M:4/4
L:1/8
R:reel
K:A
||cBAc fcec|BE (3 GFE GABd|cBAc fedc|defg afed|
cBAc fcec|BE (3 GFE GABd|cBAc fedc|defg afed||
||c2 ag fedc|B2 ba gfed|c2 ag fedc|(3dcB (3cBA GABd|

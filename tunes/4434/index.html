Three comments

Denis O’Keeffe’s

From Paudie O‘Connor. Denis O’Keeffe is an accordion player from Rathmore. Paudie plays this after “Tom Barrett’s” which I posted a few days ago. A couple of unusual polkas which go well together. I’ll add some further comments to Paudie’s CD in the Recordings section - a CD I highly recommend.

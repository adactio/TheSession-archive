A slightly different version which I learnt in Bristol is as follows:

A>ag>a eA|F>Ad>c BB|A>ag>a eA|
1 F>AG>B cG:|2 F>AG>B ce fc|da gf|ee fa|
1 (3gfe (3dcB (3Ace (3agf:|2 g>a (3bag a2 a2||

Apart from a change to the rhythm of the 3rd beat of the 2nd bar of part A, the main changes are to part B and are relatively minor except that there is now no d-sharp in the second bar.

This tune goes well with The Seaweed on the Yellow Cliff (another highland), which I hope to post in the fairly near future.

Jimmy Lyons, X:6

A very Donegal version I learned, coincidentally, at McIntyre’s Pub in Dún Ciannaola (Dunkineely, South Donegal) twenty years ago, from a bunch of different fiddlers, really. It was a literal all-night session lasting until the sun rose. Then we all passed out in the bed & breakfast rooms upstairs. Sigh. To be 18 again. Haha, but I’d heard the tune before, on the 1987 “Altan” album by Máiread & Frankie, but I “officially” picked it up that night @ the session… it was such a memorable time. The players that night included Aiden O’Donnell, Damien McGeehan, Kieran Munnelly, Aoife Feeney, and THE Sean Keane, among many others! It was truly iconic!

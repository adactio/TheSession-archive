“Vals etter Thorvald Tronsgård”
Waltz after Thorvald Tronsgård (1892-1986), a fiddler, farmer and “bygdeslakter” (a person who went from farm to farm to slaughter and butcher livestock), from Dalholen, Folldal in Nord-Østerdalen, Hedmark, Norway.

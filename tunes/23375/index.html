Four comments

Trip To Roscoff

An instrumental coda to the song ‘One last cold kiss’ on Christy Moore’s 1975 album ‘Whatever Tickles Your Fancy’ (side B).
How appropriate a title; for this little Breton-style en dro number was penned by Christie himself, it seems! A piece the festoù-noz fans would not disavow.

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on August 1st, 2023.

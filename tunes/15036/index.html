Christmas Time Will Soon Be Over

Until recently, our local old-timey session (Bristol, UK) was held monthly - always on the third Thursday. Then, unfortunately, our pub venue could no longer accommodate us (they wanted to use our playing area for serving food) so we were forced into a change of venue.

Well, we did find a new pub for our session, but they could only have us on first Wednesdays. So our last session of this year was held on Wednesday 2nd December- a full twenty-three days before Christmas.

However, this didn’t stop us getting into the spirit of things, including a few turns with this particular tune: “Christmas Time Will Soon Be Over”.

As with may tunes of this genre, it keeps its tonal centre throughout, but changes mode mid-way. This one begins in AMaj, then switches into AMix for the second part.

Some of our company even sang the lyrics.

Christmas time will soon be over,
Christmas time will soon be over,
Christmas time will soon be over,
Then we’ll join the band.

Wake up gals, let’s go to the river,
Wake up gals, let’s go to the river,
Wake up gals, let’s go to the river,
Then we’ll join the band.

Get up gals, don’t be bitter,
Get up gals, don’t be bitter,
Get up gals, don’t be bitter,
Then we’ll join the band.

Mess with me, I’ll pull the trigger,
Mess with me, I’ll pull the trigger,
Mess with me, I’ll pull the trigger,
Then we’ll join the band.

Now, old-timey tunes in general wouldn’t win any prizes for literature, but the lyrics of this one are particularly banal. Nonetheless, somehow great fun!

Here’s a YouTube clip of some musicians at an American family gathering enjoying themselves with it.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqAQsXpTvCU

Note that they’re taking it in G-Maj - modulating into G-Mix.

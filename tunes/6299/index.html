Tra Na Mban, X:2

The cAG in the 3rd bar of lines 1,2, and 4 should be cBG - I slowed the Kane Sisters’s recording down to 1/3 speed and listened very carefully. I noticed this because I’d picked up the tune by ear and found that when I played the notes here they didn’t sound quite right. I also took the liberty of moving the GF at the end to pickup notes at the begin of both parts - they seemed pickup-y to me.

↳ Posted by zoronic on April 10th, 2016.

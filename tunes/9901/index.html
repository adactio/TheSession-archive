Four comments

Alasdair Fraser’s Welcome To Cape Breton

This is a cracking reel by Dr Angus MacDonald of Glenuig. Dr Angus and his brothers Allan and Iain have got a great knack of writing great tunes that sound like they’ve been about for years.

↳ Posted by bogman on September 17th, 2009.

A couple turns

Bar 3 of the 2nd part can be g|fed2 c2Bc| per the composer on A’ Sireadh Spors.

Bar 1 of the 4th part ends on a g2 instead of f2, also per the composer. Bar 2 often gets a triplet on the first beat, a/g/f instead of just gf per the composer and also heard on “First Harvest” by Iains MacDonald & MacFarlane as well as on Daimh’s ’Pirates of Puirt."

↳ Posted by malcombpiper on September 19th, 2009.

Le Rochefort

The last tune in a set of reels, following Reel a Aristide, from Genticorum’s first recording. I like the way that the second part sounds crooked but isn’t. There’s a bit of ornamentation in the second part that I didn’t try to notate, particularly a stuttering triplet on the B’s in the third measure.

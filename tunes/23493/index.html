Two comments

The New Year

Someone asked me to transcribe this after the NotMells festival weekend, so here it is. Originally in this key but also easily transposed up to B minor. And yes, those notes are tied at the end of phrases. And no, I doubt it’s the tune of the same name listed in the recordings section (by Litha or Fiona Driver).

↳ Posted by Loughcurra on September 8th, 2023.

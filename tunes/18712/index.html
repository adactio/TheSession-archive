Three comments

Forbes Morrison

This transcription was taken from the Traditional Tune Archive; I will post my own as X:2. It’s a James Scott Skinner tune that I decided to add because it is played on Francie and Mickey Byrne’s Ceol na dTéad as “Agnes Campbell”, from which version I made the transcription for X:2.

↳ Posted by Daniel Parker on November 15th, 2019.

Forbes Morrison, X:2

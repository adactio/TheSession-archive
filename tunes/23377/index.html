Stan The Man

Fantastic little tune from one of the my favourite bands of all time, The Arcadian Wild: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P2C51lXdi8Y

It shouldn’t be as swung as the dotted rhythm shows, but I didn’t want to write it all straight either as it still has a swing to most of it apart from a few keys bars which are played quite straight (as written above), which also meant I didn’t want to just write everything as straight as this difference would have gotten lost. Best bet is to just listen to the recording for the exact rhythm!

Also probably isn’t actually a reel, but I wasn’t sure what else to class it as (suggestions welcome)!

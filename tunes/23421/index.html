One comment

Mc Noltis

I got a digital version of the Commonalty recording from a friend years ago. I don’t know where he got it, but it seems to have been taken from an old tape, judging by the sound quality.

The folder incudes all the tunes in the record tracklist (that can be find here http://paulmcsherry.com/discography/commonalty_tracklist.html), but an extra tune is included as Mc Noltis hornpipe. Thus it is not clear that this tune belongs to that recording (can be a hidden track or just something included by mistake).

No tune with this name can be find elsewhere online. Maybe it is a a distortion of the name NcNulty’s. Anyway I didn’t find it here, so there it is!
The story is here: https://thesession.org/discussions/48507

If you have any information about this tune, or a better setting (I’m not an expert in ABC notation), or if it has already been posted here, please let us know!

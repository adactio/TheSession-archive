This tune was composed by PM Calum A Cambell of the Gordon Highlanders (he was PM around the late 50s and 60s). It is in the Gordon Highlanders collection Vol 1 as a 4/4 march with 4 parts.

Lieutenant Colonel PW (Patrick Walter) Forbes (1914 -1979) was an officer in the Gordon Highlanders. He lived at Corse House by Lumphanan, Aberdeenshire. He received his OBE in 1959.
He was from a long line of Forbes in the region, who, along with the Gordons were, and still are, major landholders.
A local pronunciation of ‘Forbes’ is “Forbiss”, and that pronunciation is not unknown in North America.

I don’t have the Gordon Highlanders book any more, but if I find the 4 part version I’ll submit it (it’s probably better in the strathspey section, rather than hornpipe, though still not ideal).

Four comments

For Yann

“This one is very special to us. It was composed by Jean Damei in memory of an old friend called Yann Manche. Yann sadly passed away a few years ago. He was a big lover of traditional music and also a cracking flute player and so we’d like to dedicate this to his memory, his friends and his family.”

Yann was a flute teacher from Lyon (France), member of Garlic Bread, he shared the stage with Lunasa, At First Light, Altan, Guidewires, Tri Yann, … A great man!

Four comments

Source

This is an Alborada, a Gallician traditional tune similar to a 2/4 march. I’ve transcribed it based on Susana Seivane’s playing on her new album, *Os Soños Que Volven*, where she plays it in Bb major on a Bb major gaita. No doubt there are some grace notes I’ve left out. :-)

The reason I transcribed it in Gmaj is precisely because Irish wind instruments (flute/whistle/uilleann pipes) don’t have the extra tone below that other pipes do (e.g. a low C on a D chanter). ;-)

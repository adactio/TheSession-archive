Kilcooley Wood (hornpipe)

Leeds piano accordion player Helen Flaherty is the source of this hornpipe. Helen is one of mighty players of the city and loves to play modern compositions with the old-fashioned feel, such as Martin Mulhaire’s “The Golden Keyboard” and Vincent Broderick’s “Spider’s Web.” I didn’t know anything about the tune, but a bit of research shows that it was penned by Tippeary fiddler Sean Ryan.

Kilcooley Wood, X:3

This is I believe how Brian McNamara taught the tune in a class at Scoil Samhraidh Willie Clancy many years ago. It is close to the original as written by Seán Ryan and suits the uilleann pipes very well.

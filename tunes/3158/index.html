Two comments

An easy introduction to the Kopanitsa. Personally, I don’t feel the tune gets really going until it slips into the key change. That gives it a nice feel. I can’t let this tune pass without also submitting a fantatstic Kopanitsa that is a bit more technically demanding, but worth the effort. See Kolev’s Kopanitsa.

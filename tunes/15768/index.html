Re: Einini

The rhythmic pace of this lullaby is like a mazurka.

The melody sounds like a simplified version of the “The eagle’s whistle”
https://thesession.org/tunes/1837
which is also known as
“Arrane Ny Niee” (The Washing Song) in Manx.
In any case, a song used for soothing.

Below is a translation in English which is less alliterative than the original where the birds appear in pairs based on their phonetic similarity (which, along with the melody, is quite mesmerizing).



The lullaby of the birds

1.
Little birds little birds
sleep sleep
Little birds little birds
sleep sleep

CHORUS;


Sleep sleep
by the wall outside
by the wall outside
Sleep sleep
by the wall outside
by the wall outside

2.
The blackbird and the raven
go to sleep go to sleep
The hen blackbird and the crow
go to sleep go to sleep…

3.
The robin and the lark
go to sleep go to sleep
The wren and the little thrush
go to sleep go to sleep…

4.
The little cuckoo and the kingfisher
go to sleep go to sleep
The swift and the sparrow
go to sleep go to sleep…

5.
The tit and the finch
go to sleep go to sleep
The bunting and the wagtail
go to sleep go to sleep…

6.
The warbler and the flycatcher
go to sleep go to sleep
The rook and the linnet
go to sleep go to sleep.

Captain Cameron’s Volunteers’ March - Balvenie

A William Marshall march, a setting based on Ron Gonnella’s “The Countess of Dalhousie.” I’ll transpose for D whistle/flute/pipes as well.
Marshall’s version with double stops is available at https://tunearch.org/wiki/Captain_Cameron%27s_Volunteers_March--Balvenie

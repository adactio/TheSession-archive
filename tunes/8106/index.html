Secs and Cosecs

Dan, this one’s for you!

I know I’m pushing the 5:1 rule about self-composed tunes by posting this one, but I just had to. The story behind it is just too good:
It was in a Mechanics lesson (ugh) some time ago. The whole class had been set a question - a long and difficult one, involving lots of trigonometry. Now I’m not very good at Mechanics, and I started coming up with things like 1/cosθ and 1/sinθ. This can’t be right. I put up my hand.
“Sir, I’m getting secs and cosecs! Am I doing something wrong?”
Mr. Dawes spun around, an odd expression on his face.
“Secs and cosecs?!!” blurted Sam, from the other side of the room.
The class had been quiet. Now it burst out laughing.
The event had to be commemorated in some way.

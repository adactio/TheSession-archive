“Gan Ainm” ~ courtesy of Breandan Breathnach and others

L: 1/8
M: 12/8
R: slide
K: ADor page 35, tune #71
|: EDE AGE G2 A B2 d | EDE AGE G2 E D3 |
E3 AGE G2 A B2 d | e2 d BAG A3 A3 :|
|: e3 edB d3 dBd | e2 e e2 f g2 e d2 B |
c2 c c2 A B2 B B2 G | A2 c B2 A G2 E D3 :|

Notes from CRÉ IV ~ Along with other tunes Breandan added to his 5 volume collection, the transcription for this slide is from the David Collins ms., written by or in the possession of David Collins of Abbeyfeale, County Limerick. Breathnach recieved it from Tom Barrett, a fiddler from Listowel, County Kerry, who was also source for some of the tunes found in CRÉ II.

↳ Posted by ceolachan on August 7th, 2012.

C# or C natural ~ A Dorian or A Mixolydian

K: ADor ~ not having a recording there’s no way to be sure whether or not the original key given for this transcription was right, but I’ve given it as found in the book, with all C naturals, A Dorian… My sense of it is that it was likely A Mixolydian, as the others given above, and that the Cs would have all been sharp…

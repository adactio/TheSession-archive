A Travers La Vitre

A friend of mine asked me, why the tune A Travers La Vitre from this recording https://thesession.org/recordings/1835. En Spectacle by La Bottine Souriante sounds so familiar, especially the A-Part.
I think the A-Part is very similar to Mountain Road, so maybe this is the Quebecois Version of https://thesession.org/tunes/68.

Waves Of Torey

Polka/March from the 2nd Roche Collection (see https://www.itma.ie/scores/roche-vol-2-93/), played on Ted McGraw’s concertina album, and one he would often play out in sessions. I have a feeling this must be a duplicate, but I searched ABC snippets in a number of keys and rhythms and couldn’t place it.

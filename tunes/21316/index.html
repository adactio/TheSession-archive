All Along The River Bank

This is based on Jackie Daly’s recording (https://thesession.org/recordings/899). This polka has bits in common with Tureengarbh Glen (https://thesession.org/tunes/2781) but IMHO is a distinct tune even if a distant relation. I’m not aware of any other recording of this tune. Nor can can I consult my cassette recording sleeve notes as they were lost in a flood. It may be time to remove this name as an alternative for Tureengarbh Glen.

↳ Posted by brailsford on December 10th, 2021.

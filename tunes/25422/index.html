One comment

Daire’s Dream

Davy Spillane has recorded this air four or five times to my knowledge. This version is from his album Sea of Dreams and is played on a low D whistle whereas on the other recordings he plays a low F. In the transcription, the symbol > is used to indicate a reverse slide.

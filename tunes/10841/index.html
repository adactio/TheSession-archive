Fairly Shot

There are numerous versions in print and ms, Northumbrian, English, Scottish and Irish. AFAIK the earliest sighting is a quirky version in the Atkinson ms (Northumberland, 1695). The one to avoid is in the Northumbrian Minstrelsy, which garbles strain 2.

It’s in O’Neills 1001 as Good Night (No. 405).

Northumbrian ‘on’ = English ‘of’ = Scottish ‘o’.

“Ewan McColl used it on ”Songs from the Jacobite Rebellions“; there it´s called ”Donald McGillivray“ .”

Yes, James Hogg created the song, pretending it was an old “relic” apparently to wind up literary critics of the day.
It is almost note for note the first setting here, though there is an inflected C towards the end.
A concocted song with a pinched melody:

X:4
T:Donald MacGillavry
S:James Hogg
S:Jacobite Relics of Scotland No 60, p.101
R: jig
M:6/8
L:1/8
K:Emin
E/F/GE E/F/GE|E2 B BGE|E/F/GE E/F/GE|D2 A AFD|
E/F/GE E/F/GE|E2 B BGB|cBA dcB|DDA AFD|
B/^c/dE B2 E|B/^c/dE BGE|B/^c/dE B2 E|DDA AFD|
B/^c/dE B2 E|B/^c/d=c BGB|cBA dcB|DDA AFD||

You can hear Silly Wizard perform it here:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=appBtXoxfb8

And Clannad’s version here:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFd_1XJsxRE&feature=kp

The above words supplied by Kenny are from the “Musical Museum”, where Burns “corrected” the words he’d heard (I seem to recall they came from an employee at the printers of the “Museum”) and added his own verses. There is another version, that is cited in “Remains of Nithsdale and Galloway song” by Cromek et al, 1810 (p 88) - https://archive.org/details/remainsofnithsda00cromiala - the opening goes:-

O gin I were fairly shot on her,
O gin I were fairly shot on her,
Auld Satan wad lie neither side nor on top on her,
But wad cowre in his cleugh, and sing --
‘ fairly shot on her.’

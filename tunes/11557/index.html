Short Mesure Off My Lady Wynkylds Rownde

This is a medieval tune (hence the archaic spelling of the title) transcribed from THE BONES OF ALL MEN by Phillip Pickett (with Richard Thompson and the Fairport rhythm section.)

Structurally, the A part is 9 measures + 9 measures. The B part is 10 measures long. Though it’s in 3/4, I don’t think I’d really call it a “waltz”.

↳ Posted by Niles H on October 28th, 2011.

The tune appears in a manuscript in the British Museum, dated around 1520 and is believed to be from the early 16th century, which would place it in the Tudor period, making it a (High) Renaissance piece rather than medieval.

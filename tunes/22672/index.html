Maids Of Flanders

A nice strathspey which I’ve been learning as part of a concert set.

I believe it’s been recorded by Kenny Thomson and the Wardlaw Scottish Band and it seems to be popular on the Scottish Country Dance scene.

It was composed by a P W Scott and “P stands for Percy, I suppose”. Well, according to Google, anyway. ;-)

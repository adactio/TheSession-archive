Tintern Abbey

Another nice, old fashioned composition of the Wexford whistle player, Kevin Whitty. Tintern Abbey is on the Hook peninsula in south west Wexford and was originally a 13th century Cistercian Abbey before eventually becoming the residence of the Colclough family, among whom was the celebrated early 19th century gentleman piper, Dudley Colclough - who wrote one of the earliest tutor books for the Uilleann ‘Union’ pipes.

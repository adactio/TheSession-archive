Coir’-lararaidh

Written by Bruce MacGregor

↳ Posted by Tigereye on March 2nd, 2024.

Coir’-Iararaidh, X:2

Quite a hard tune to notate (so I’ve used 2/4 throughout) but an easy tune to play. I’ve added chords as I hear them on the recording (on “XXV”). The Blazers finish the tune by going back to the first part.
The tune “was inspired by a poem celebrating the beauty of Loch Ness side”.

I think the long D at the end of the first part isn’t quite as long as you have it, Tigereye.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on March 4th, 2024.

Re: Coir’-Iararaidh

I just checked and it’s in Bruce’s book “The Highlander’s Revenge” notated in 4/4 throughout.
There are a couple of slight differences to the recorded version on XXV, but essentially it’s the same. I guess the setting in his book is based on the one on “Loch Ness”, which was recorded twenty or so years ago.

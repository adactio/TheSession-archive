Two comments

The Bellangare Races

This is one of my own, sort of. I was noodling through the Mallorca posted today by Pere (https://thesession.org/tunes/6207), decided it wasn’t my cuppa chamomile, but heard the seeds of another tune.

So out it came, on mandolin. And the name is from a childhood memory of riding bicycles with my brother and sister near the Co. Roscommon town of Bellangare some 40 years ago while visiting my dad’s family there.

↳ Posted by Will Harmon on September 19th, 2006.

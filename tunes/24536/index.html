La Champêtre

↳ Posted by Yoann on July 29th, 2024.

Re: La Champêtre

This is an arrangement. Not only the key has been changed, but the original time signature is 2/4, the so called “slide” effect being rendered in semiquavers triplet for less than 4 bars. The tempo is “Presto” which is much faster than a slide.
In a few words, Poulenc didn’t mean to write a jig or slide.

Three comments

I got this tune from an unpublished(?) collection of tunes assembled by Martin Mulvihill, but to hear it in it’s glory, get your hands on Brendan Mulvihill’s CD “Flax in Bloom” and try to assemble all the nice touches he puts to it.

Re: The Circus / Nelson’s Victory

Nelson’s Victory is name of this tune in Ryan’s Mammoth Collection (#836). There are some interesting differences compared with X:1

↳ Posted by brailsford on August 24th, 2023.

The Circus, X:2

Around me, we also call this Brendan Mulvihill’s. This is a setting recorded from local fiddler Willie Kelly in February 2023. Willie and his brother, Joe, were both students of Martin Mulvihill. The differences to X:1 are not major, but they’re pleasant and we played this version at a session following Brendan’s passing in December, 2023.

Rosaleen Stenson’s

From the playing of Kevin Burke at Baltimore Fiddle Fair 2022 (as good as I can make it out). Kevin says he learned it from Rosaleen Stenson, daughter of John Stenson who he said “grew up next door to his mother’s home place” and he has never heard the tune from anyone else other than Rosaleen, hence the name. (see John Stenson’s 1 https://thesession.org/tunes/1155 and 2 https://thesession.org/tunes/1163) . I have search this site a few times but haven’t found it here, so here goes

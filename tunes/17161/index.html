Re: The Kamikaze Honkers

A reel written by Alistair McCulloch. The title refers to the bus driver on the ayrshire Fiddle Orchestra’s china trip in 2009. They terrorised other road users throughout the tour by constantly honking the horn (as per 3rd measure of the tune)

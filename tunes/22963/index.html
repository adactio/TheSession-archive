Re: Never Say Never

”Regular key? Doesn’t sound like a slip jig?”

B major isn’t the key on the recording, hence the comment about regular key. Although the musicians play it in C, G could be a likely ”home key”, as it fits the range of most common ITM instruments.

I’d say it sounds like a _modern_ slip jig, with long and fairly ”lyrical” phrases (think anything by Michael Rooney or A slip of a thing by Jim Murray ; example here: https://thesession.org/tunes/20793 )

Pete’s Rambling

A reel composed by my late friend Peter Croll, violin maker and fiddler (among many other things), who passed away in May 2024.

It starts out sounding quite traditional… then, after the third part, things start to get a bit unconventional. But the whole thing has an ingenious symmetry. Peter did not give the tune a name (or did not tell me it, if he did), so I have taken the liberty of naming it, in a way that I hope is appropriate.

You can hear the source recording here: https://youtu.be/b9Nj7tnW7ug
I have been judicious with my interpretation a few of the notes, which fall between the semitones. So feel free to ignore my transcription in favour of how Peter himself played the tune.

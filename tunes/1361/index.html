Re: Daly’s

In the US this is a “floating” tune played in a variety of keys under various names. I think fiddlers Kenny Baker and Joe Greene are responsible for spreading it around. Midwest fiddlers Cyril Stinnett and Bob Walters called a version in C Major “Clyde Durst’s Tune”. The change in chord sequence in the second part gives the tune a “lift” which dancers will like!

↳ Posted by Larry Ayers on December 7th, 2021.

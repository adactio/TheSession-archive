The Road To Hook Head

A nice ‘old-style’ hornpipe from John Noctor. Accordionist John is a prolific composer (visit his Youtube channel to hear them). He has family connections to the Hook peninsula area in County Wexford through his parents, so some of his composition’s titles refer to places or people from that area of Wexford (along with the North western part of the county where his wife hails from). His tunes named after people or places in Wexford are being included in the Wexford Tune Project that I’ve been engaged in for recent months. The full content of the collection so far - around 175 tunes and counting - are together in My Tunebook on my profile page.

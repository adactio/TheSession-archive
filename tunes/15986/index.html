Mick Rooney’s

…taken from (James) Whiteside’s manuscript, a hornpipe sourced from the playing of Mick ‘Dall’ Rooney (1826-1867), one of the region’s last blind fiddlers…
From the liner notes of ‘Our Dear Dark Mountain with the Sky Over it’

Another tune which I got from my wife Helen Farrelly’s concertina playing. She got this version from Francis Mc Mahon who in turn got it from Seán Mc Elwain’s book on the Whiteside Collection.

Two comments

Para Handy

Not to be confused with the Phil Cunningham tune!

This is the original theme tune composed by Ian Gourlay. I’m surprised it’s not been posted here already.

It’s a rather elaborate arrangement which could be simplified according to taste. Certainly, no need for the “middle eight” and you could miss out the Bb section too, I guess. Also, the “endy bit”. :-)

Enjoy.

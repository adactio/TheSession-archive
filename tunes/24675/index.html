Darragh Conlon’s slip jig

a slip jig composed by Gary Duffy
a Norn Fluter of note!

It wouldn’t be Garry without a good measure of semiquavers here and there so I left some in for a flavour of the original, which you can hear on the 1st track of disk 1 in the 3rd volume of the Wooden Flute Obsession CD series , 2006.

Gemma Gorman provides pert(inent) harmonic accompaniment to this tune on this recording, along with accomplices Jim Rainey and Rohan Young.

This is the second tune in the Conlon’s slip jig set; an A Maj. (ionian) tune which follows Rohán Conlon’s slip jig, in A min.

Simon’s Wart

A Shetland Reel. This version is based on Curlew’s recording on their album “Music from Shetland and Beyond”, which dates from the mid 1980s, which is here:

https://thesession.org/recordings/2432

My LP sleeve for that album contains no more information about the tune. I thought it was a great name for a tune and, for a long time (pre-internet), I thought Simon might not have been delighted to have such tune written about him.

I now know it is apparently on the first Ossian album, though I’ve never heard that version. The information I have comes from internet notes about that record. It was written by Willie Hunter, Senior. It is “named after a stone built look-out station or ‘wart’, this particular one - Simon’s Wart, or Seoman’s as it is pronounced, being in the parish of Nesting.”

↳ Posted by Peter Laban on December 27th, 2008.

Cillian wrote that is is named after a place in the Mourne mountains that was overlooked by Leckan Mor and Leckan Beag. Somewhere near Rostrevor, Co.Down.

↳ Posted by pipersgrip on July 1st, 2010.

I like the “strathspey-ish” taste given by the crotchet / quaver of the second setting ! It has been found at https://sites.google.com/site/veryslowsessions/tunes-1/lockan-mor under the mis-spelled name of “Lockan Mór”.
I don’t know the original version of Lunasa but played very slowly, I suppose it could have been a waltz…

↳ Posted by didier on April 8th, 2013.

Re: Leckan Mor

This tune was written by Cillian and named after a house he lived in for a while in Rostrevor and the house itself is called Leckan Mór. I was written for friend Liam Baxter from Rostrevor who would be a friend of the Valelly Family. Liam is great supporter of Culture and Arts in south Down and is an absolute gentleman!

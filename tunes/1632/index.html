Dawn Rising

Here’s how it’s played by The Irish Tradition on their album Catchin’ the Tune.

|d3B ABde|f3d edBA|FAdB ADFD|EDEF DEFA:|
|d2fd adfd|(3efg fd edBA|dB~B2 ABdf|edef dBAB
|d2fd adfd|(3efg fd edBA|defg a2bf|afec dgfe||

They call it Dawn Rising but though it’s a very different setting I’m sure this is the same tune.

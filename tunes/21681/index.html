Wayesborough Or Waynesboro

This is commonly an Old-Time tune in the USA played with only two parts, A and B, but this version is from the playing of Bruce Molsky, Mark Simos and the Berklee Old-Time Ensemble on YouTube 2014.
Bruce Molsky explains why he plays a third part:-
”Years ago I met Mairéad Ní Mhaonaigh for the first time at a party in Minneapolis when she was on a tour with Altan. She coaxed me into the session (I don’t really play Irish music), and she pulled out Over the Moor to Maggie, 2/3 of which I recognized. Learned the third part there, and I’ve been playing it ever since.”

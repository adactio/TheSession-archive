Laurie And Madeleine Ryan

Laurie and Madeleine Ryan lived in Colonel Light Gardens South Australia, they were Irish born and I knew them as a young boy. They didn’t have their own children, and Madeleine was very fond of me. My Dad took great pleasure at the end of a Sunday church service, taking me up to say hello to Madeleine and Laurie, where Madeleine would give me the biggest hug and nearly smother me with love.
I’ve borrowed some ideas from Cara’s Loon tune, but this is a very different tune.

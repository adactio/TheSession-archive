Four comments

The Navvy Of Kinlochleven

This tune, composed by Gráinne Brady, is from her impressive 2019 CD The Road Across the Hills (Cladagh Records). The whole album was inspired by Patrick McGill’s novel Children of Dead End (1914); as much an autobiography as a novel, it recounts the author’s life starting in poverty in Donegal in the late 19th century, moving to Scotland as the hero follows work - as a potato picker and navvy.

The tune is played with a bit of swing.

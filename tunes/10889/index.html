Dawn’s Mandolin

↳ Posted by Edgar Bolton on December 8th, 2010.

Dawn’s Mandolin, X:2

Variation - with a few double stops made explicit

Different instruments seem to suggest different styles of playing. Dawn’s mandolin is an Ozark ‘teardrop’ mandolin. She has always stuck with the same instrument. In contrast, I suffer from Compulsive Instrument Change Syndrome: My first mandolin was a flat back Italian from a car boot sale – quite damaged and just playable. I then got a red Westfield Electro-acoustic which I played for many years until I broke the neck. I also bought a George Houghton 1920s banjo mandolin and a 1930s German mandriola which I customised to play as a mandolin (adding electric pickup to play through an effects board). My current mandolin is a David Hodgson gypsy jazz mandolin. I’ve prized off the bridge whiskers – which were glued in the wrong place – and am about to sandpaper all the varnish off the neck. Dawn’s mandolin still sounds just as good as when I first played it.

↳ Posted by Edgar Bolton on November 10th, 2018.

Update

The triplet in the first part, bar 3, is (b c c# ) not (b c# c#)

The Bog Man

This tune was played for me by Theo Paige, fiddler extraordinaire, and I did my best to transcribe it as he played it. He tells me Joe Murtagh composed the tune in 1996.

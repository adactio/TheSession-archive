Three comments

Steps

A Dee Armstrong composition, arranged by Eoin Dillon & Colm Ó Snoadaigh; the first tune on the 13th track on Kíla’s 1995 album.
The tonality on the record is D.
Tempo = 52 (compound) (single quavers = 156).

Play it as slow as 40 for a mazurka, as fast as 80 for a waltz. It sounds part Scandinavian polska, part Carolan’s planxty, part Kílaesque.
A lovely blend that works; easy on the fingers and the ear.
Learners note:
*D# is the ghost D on the uilleann pipe and an easy Eb half-hole fingering on the whistle.
*Some D’s natural can be played sharp as well (part 1).
*the bottom B can be played an octave higher.
Have fun.

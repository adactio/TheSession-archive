Planxty Francis Shannon

I stumbled upon this Tommy Peoples compostion on a West Wind radio show with Eoin O’Neill, Eoin asked Mary Custy to record this Tommy composition for him on the piano so he can play it over the air, Mary’s rendition of it is very haunting making use of those ringing pentatonic notes from the tune, I have made a recording of it form the radio show if anyone would like to hear it contact me here

↳ Posted by Yooval on February 24th, 2018.

Frances Shannon’s Jig

Frances Shannon is my grandmother. She is now 82 years old and in hospital in critical condition, but we are hoping for an improvement. Tommy People’s was a good friend of hers, and Mary Custy is also a family friend. I have the original handwritten manuscript sitting in front of me at my piano - the title reads “Frances Shannon’s Jig” not “Planxty Francis Shannon”. Tommy wrote a small note beneath the music talking about how he dedicated this tune to my Nana and her unique personality. He also writes about her family and directly mentions my fathers name. If anyone would like to see the manuscript, please contact me.

↳ Posted by sean_piano on March 4th, 2018.

Gan Ainm slide

I was sure this was already on site, or somewhere among the many collections of ABCs and dots in print and online, but so far I’ve had no luck chasing it up, including in different keys and ways, some of those to follow. If it does prove to be a duplication I will be very glad to have a name for it and will see that any transcriptions here are moved to that new home, if on exists.

This first transcription in C is from a recording of a box player and was part of a set of three slides:
“She Hasn’t The Thing She Thought She Had” (D) / Gan Ainm (this one in C) / “The Weaver’s” (D)

Two comments

Ferns Abbey

This is another composition of Nóra Byrne Kavanagh, who is from Ferns but lives just outside of Waterford city. Nóra has composed many tunes over the years, often for a teaching workshop or event. Ferns Abbey are the ruins of an Augustinian abbey built in the middle ages. Nóra’s compositions are copyrighted so consent is required for recording or republishing.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on January 29th, 2023.

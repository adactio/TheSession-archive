Deegan’s Tweed Cap

I wrote this tune for Dicky Deegan - a piper I’ve been fortunate to know for many years. Part of Dicky’s signature outfit is his tweed cap, without which he is unlikely to be seen. This tune is written in honour of the man, his style, and his music.

More info on Dicky Deegan’s music here:
https://www.dickydeegan.com/

https://youtu.be/wmXJCSyGzUw

Deegan’s Tweed Cap, X:2

An A mixolydian version of the above.

Note that playing it in A mixolydian pushes the melody towards the highest register of the flute (e’), but that’s a good note to practice!

The original offers wonderful opportunities for variations; X:2 suggests as much as offers some.

Accordion Clog

This is the first tune in Leahy’s Clog Medley from their album “In All Things.” I assume it was written by Donnell, but I’m not entirely sure. I got the tune name from another user’s comment from 17 years ago, who apparently pulled it straight from Leahy’s website.

Between the simple boom-chick piano accompaniment, the spiccato, and the constant heel-tapping throughout, the tune has a pretty cool clock-like vibe to it that I really like.

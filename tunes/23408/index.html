Seven comments

Le Step Du Loup

“Le step du loup” (= ‘Stepping Wolf’) is a mazurka, three-two and 6/8 jig all rolled into one, composed by Ulittuq Aagaa Suli (nom de plume of Normand Tremblay) and based on Eric Favreau’s reel “Le step à Kenneth Murphy” (https://thesession.org/tunes/14834).
The relationship is obvious in part one, but Le step du loup takes a different melodic direction in part two.
An interesting feature of this mazurka (which should be played at a brisk tempo and given a bounce or lilt) is that it can be played in three different meters; 3/4, 3/2 and 6/8.
3/2’s tend to present this polymetric potential of course (so do many ITM tunes which can slip into various metric jackets fairly easily) but here, the change of meter (without a change of pace) is specified.

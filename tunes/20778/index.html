One comment

Foxes’ Rock

This is a 3/4 tune (or couple of tunes?) off of Ancora by Flook, played after The Crystal Year. I’ve written it in A Dorian mode (although it’s played on an Eb whistle) for ease of whistle players abroad. The structure of it is a little odd based on which sections get repeated at which times, but hopefully you all can get the gist of it through this sheet music. Excellent stuff here from Brian and Sarah on ornamentations and variations (which I can articulate as separate comments if necessary or requested).

Four comments

The Piercestown

A tune in F mafor composed by Paddy Hayes and named after his County Wexford home area of Piercestown. The tune goes down to low F, playable on accordion, but below the range of fiddle etc. He recorded it on his first album Return to Kilty. On another tune’s page (‘The Golden Eagle’) there’s a discussion about Paddy apparently claiming to have composed ‘The Golden Eagle’ but what happened is this composition of Paddy’s (The Piercestown) is paired with ‘The Golden Eagle’ and the sleeve notes confuse the issue by not specifying that only the first tune is a Paddy Hayes composition. A similar little error arises on the same recording with two waltzes, the first is not a composition of Paddy’s (he calls it John Sheehan’s) , but the second waltz, Patricia’s wedding is Paddy’s tune.

Re: The Piercestown

Definite similarities I’d agree. The ‘Old Slievenamon’ hornpipe was a tune in the repetoire of another Wexford senior accordion winner, Tom Ferris, who I played with for many years. So I’d imagine that Paddy Hayes would have known it too. Perhaps when composing his Piercestown hornpipe, the other tune was lurking in the back of his memory.

Salaberry

Written by Kai Gronberg

This tune was named after Salaberry-de-Valleyfield, Quebec, which was hosting Canadian Grand Masters Fiddle Contest one year. Kai and I were chatting and sharing some fiddle tunes and this tune was one he was working on, and hadn’t quite named yet.

*The notation very slightly differs from the recording, because it is the setting I learned from Kai prior to this recording, I’ve included the most recent recording from Kai, so you can use both as variations.

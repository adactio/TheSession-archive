Four comments

Davy Webster’s 40th

This Gordon Duncan tune varies quite a lot in how it is played. There are three main parts, labelled A, B, C below. Generally each 8 bar part is repeated, though not everywhere. The C part is often omitted entirely, which I am sure is nothing to do with fiddlers finding it difficult. You often hear it with the B part repeated at the end. So, be ready for the following combinations: AB, AABB, ABC [don’t think I’ve heard this], AABBCC, AABBCCBB. The book itself gives I think ABCBB although there’s an ambiguous repeat mark and it’s not one of the better transcriptions.

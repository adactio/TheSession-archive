↳ Posted by Phantom Button on January 15th, 2006.

Cath, if it’s this one--https://thesession.org/tunes/444--save yourself the trouble.

On the Long Acre, the first two notes (FE) are the intro, aka the anacrusis, the “pick up,” or lead in. So they go before the first measure to get the phrasing lined out right.

The Long Acre

For anyone who is interested, the long acre refers to the strip of grass that grows along the side of the road and here in Ireland in the past it was common for a farmer to let his cattle out to eat it.

Re: The Long Acre

This tune has been posted as a reel, Kevin described it as a hornpipe.
If a reel is a gallop, this tune has more of a canter or gentle trot to it; I would be tempted to call it a scottische… I suppose it’s what you do with it; and also not just its tempo and basic rhythm but it’s melodic structure and it’s emotional intent that define it.
I had to give some thought to that before I submit its sister tune, Clyde’s banks, another lovely, jaunty Kevin Burke composition.

Mazurka D’Embrault

This is a French* mazurka. It’s one of my favourite tunes on the album “Musique Artisanale” by Duo Lagrange/Rutkowski, where I transcribed it from. You can listen to them playing it on myspace:
https://myspace.com/duolagrangerutkowski/music/song/mazurka-d-embrault-mazurka-du-p-re-jules-68679831-75448807

You might call that change from C minor to C major somewhat corny… but then again, it almost always achieves the desired effect. :-D

* Oops!

Re: Mazurka D’Embrault

Fubu were a trad. French outfit from the 1980’s who recorded a few albums in the 1980’s.
Michel Sikiotakis was a founding member (and a well known figure of ITM in France)
Another member was the piper (cabretaire) Thierry Mirebeau who composed many trad. tunes, including this alluring mazurka.

https://www.discogs.com/fr/artist/3035281-Fubu-2

“Embrault” might be ‘Embraud’ -a vibrant centre for traditional dance in the Bourbonnais region of Central France- mispelt.
“Bernard’s mazurka” was an ad hoc handle for the tune on Jason O’Rourke’s Roguery Road album.

It is a moot point but I think “corny” would more readily apply to a tune which modulates to minor from major in the same key than vice versa.
Many Parisian valses-musette modulate in such a way. It’s almost the signature for the genre.
It is often cringeworthy but there are also many tunes (waltzes, mazurkas, javas, etc) with this feature that are incredibly beautiful.
It can take some getting used to it.
I think this particular mazurka’s a wee gem.

Five comments

Favourite

This tune entitled “Favourite Waltz” is transcribed in the Reilly MSS (c1900) of Co Leitrim

↳ Posted by ConorW on August 24th, 2023.

Re: Favourite

Hi Conor,
Once again, this waltz is known and played in Wexford town, where it is usually referred to as ‘Willie Thomas’s Waltz’. Willie Thomas was a melodeon player from the North end of town sometime back in the 40’s or 50’s I’d reckon. The second part is similar but not quite the same. Like the other parallel material that we’ve already identified, the tune probably came from some old printed source.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 25th, 2023.

Favourite, X:2

Here’s one of the local Wexford versions of this waltz, where it’s usually referred to as ‘Willie Thomas’ Waltz‘. This version was played by now Jimmy and Elsie Lawlor on piano accordions, at least as i remember it from some years ago. I’m sure if Elsie saw my notation of it, she’d have a correction or two to ’gently’ admonish me about!!
Hadn’t actually thought about notating it until Conor posted the manuscript setting above. A very nice old-time waltz.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 25th, 2023.

Favourite, X:3

This G setting was the key that the waltz was played in, to suit a 10 key melodeon in they key of C. the accidentals (which the piano accordionists had) would have been left out.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 25th, 2023.

Re: Favourite

Thanks John, it’s fantastic to see that it was assimilated into the Wexford music tradition. I agree, it’s more than likely a light classical composition c1900.

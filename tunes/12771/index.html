In the artiste’s own words:

"Enjoy rambling the hills and countryside of Ireland and between a bit of daily exercise for the body and a bit of nightly music for the soul .. seems a good mix to me. ( Me too, but I take the music in all times of the day… ;-) )

Here’s a simple jig that came into my head recently when I was ascending a hill near Graiguenamanagh in Co.Kilkenny. A low heathery kinda hill, overlooking the Barrow and capped by a large pile of stones, presumably a megalithic burial cairn. The locals call it Cullintra Hill. Doubtless the tune itself is derivative and a loose version of some other ditty I’ve heard but regardless ~" ~ Mr. Hussar

Yup! That A-part is quite the derivative bit of melody, but so much is borrowed back and forth ~ one bar, two bars, three, four ~ and then I need someone else to be doing the driving… 8-)

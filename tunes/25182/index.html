Re: The Pride Of Baltimore

I am a huge fan of the tall ship Pride of Baltimore II and have sailed on her several times, once as part of a guest crew along with my wife.
https://pride2.org/
When searching, I was surprised to find that there were no tunes honoring this current ship, and her predecessor, The Pride of Baltimore, which was lost in a freak storm, taking her captain and three crew members with her. So, I had to write one. Being a tune about tall ships, it naturally had to be a hornpipe.
This tune is dedicated to everyone involved in the creation of these two beautiful ships, and their crews, past and present.
A special thanks to Michael Eskin who took my inexact and simplified ABC notation and turned it into the beautiful form you see in this sheet music.

Gerry Commane’s

Brendan Breathnach, in Ceol Rince na hEireann 3, notates Mary Bergin’s playing and puts it in G, with all the Cs natural. I have just listened to the source recording - Bergin’s Feadoga Stain - and she is playing C naturals. Mike Rafferty’s version has C sharps and C naturals, whereas the main tune here uses sharps only. Does anyone here play it? How do you play it?

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on August 27th, 2008.

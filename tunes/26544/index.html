Bro Gozh Ma Zadoù

Breton anthem based on the Welsh tune “Hen Wlad Fy Nhadau” (“Land of My Fathers”). Lyrics in Breton by François Jaffrennou (1898), music by James James (1856). Posted here as a simple melody version with chords.

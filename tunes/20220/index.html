Three comments

Tighe’s Rare

Alan Morrisroe seems to be the source for this great little tune; the version here is played by Oisín Morrisson (and also recorded by Saileog Ní Cheannabháin).

↳ Posted by Sláine on January 1st, 2021.

Tighe’s Rare, X:2

Second tune on track 3 of:

Brightly or Darkly

by Nathan Gourley and Laura Feddersen

https://nathanandlaura.bandcamp.com/track/pride-of-roxbury-paddy-joe-tighes-the-road-to-town

Note: Shifted up one semitone from the recording, which appears to be flat pitched in C#, so to play along from this transcription you’ll need to to raise the recording up one semitone using Transcribe, Amazing Slower Downer or similar pitch shift software or tune your instrument down one semitone

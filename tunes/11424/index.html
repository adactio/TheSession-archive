Source

This is a tune written by Brian Finnegan as performed by KAN, a four-piece featuring Brian alongside Aidan O’Rourke, Ian Stephenson, and Jim Goodwin.

On the recording, Brian plays an E whistle. Transpose the ABC up a tone to play along. ;-)

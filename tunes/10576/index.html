Two comments

Transcription

A lovely tune penned by Kathryn Tickell. Transcription based on the recording on *What We Do*.

She plays it in Fmaj, but Gmaj seemed like a more sensible session key. Here it is in Fmaj:
c B<G |: A2- AF G/A/B | c2- cc B<G | A2- AF E/F/G | C2- Cc B<G |
A2- AF G/A/B | c2- cf e<c | B2- BA G<A |1 F2- Fc B<G :|2 F2- Ff e<c ||
|: d2- dc B/c/d | c2- cf e<c | d2- dc B/c/d | G2- Gc B<G |
A2- AF G/A/B | c2- cf e<c | B2- BA G<A |1 F2- Ff e<c :|2 F2- Fc B<G ||

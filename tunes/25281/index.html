Three comments

Tír Na Flúirse (Land Of Plenty)

This version was transcribed from the first tune on track 2 of ‘Keane Connolly McGorman’ (https://thesession.org/recordings/7952). One of those lovely simple jigs that bring a smile.
There are several small variations and harmonies in the recording, but I tried to provide a basis to expand from.
JMI states the tune was composed by Dublin born piper Peter Browne. (https://journalofmusic.com/news/new-irish-music-releases-8-february).

Elanor Rigby

This is the classic adaptation in the ITM genre by De Danaan of the Beatles‘ immortal song. You can find it on De Danaan’s album ’A Jacket of Batteries’. Loads of fun, and if you learn it and play it somewhere live, don’t forget the double credit, or you could be in trouble with Frankie Gavin and/or Paul McCartney!

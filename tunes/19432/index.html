Robbie’s Welcome

From the playing of Garry Walsh.

It was one of the first tune he learned from his father. He doesn’t know the original name of the tune, so, he took the liberty of naming it after a friend, Robbie Daly.

Bears some resemblance to Paddy Taylor’s:

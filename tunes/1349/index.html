Callaghan’s, X:2

Based on Pádraig O’Keeffe’s playing, presumably taken from his uncle Cal Callaghan. Likely that Denis Murphy and then the Chieftains settings stem from here. Pádraig finishes the tune, before going into the Rights of Man, by returning to the first part, as the Chieftains did on their recording of it.

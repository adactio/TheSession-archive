Sarah Dancing

Composed by Brian Pickell, arranged by Shane Cook. This is the last tune in the Meander Creek medley from Shane Cook and the Woodchippers’ Be Here For a While.

Sarah Dancing, X:2

Setting in D Major / G Major.

I believe this key would be the best to be played in a session as it is pretty much playable by all instruments. I’ve chosen this high octave because I see it is possible to play it with the tin whistle. This octave is the one that fits better to mouth whistling as well.

I tried to remove all the ornamentation of the first setting to try to get the simplest melody line of the tune.

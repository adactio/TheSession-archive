Plas Teg - Y Bala

This tunewas written by Sian Phillips and recorded on her 1998 CD, Gramundus;
http:thesession.org/recordings/2249
The title is a little confusing,….I interpret it as the slow first phase, played striaght, as ‘Plas Teg’ and then when speeded up and hornpiped/swung it’s become ‘Y Bala’.
Plas Teg is reputed to be on of the most haunted houses in Wales.
Bala is a market town set next to Bala lake the gateway to the Snowdonia National Park in North Wales.

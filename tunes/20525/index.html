Maurice O’Keeffe’s

From the playing of Tim Browne & Paudy Scully.

They learned it from a recording they received from Maurice O’Keeffe, who wanted this tune, among others, to be passed on.

Paddy Jones’

As played by Emma O’Leary on her album Mo Cheantar Féin.

From the sleeve notes:
Paddy Jones’ Polka - I associate this polka with the fiddle playing of Paddy Jones. Paddy was born in Kilcusnan, Cordal and was a fiddle student of Pádraig O’Keeffe.

https://sliabhluachra.bandcamp.com/track/the-knocknagree-polka-the-lonesome-polka-paddy-jones-polka

Re: Paddy Jones’ aka Ned Connor’s

Paddy didn’t have any name for this one, but here he calls it Ned Connor’s in honor of his source for the tune, Ned O’Connor of Scartaglin: https://youtu.be/gqoc4Kx6ToY

Re: Paddy Jones’ / Ned O’Connor’s

Here’s Ned O’Connor himself playing this polka with Dan Jeremiah O’Connor (no relation) and Maurice O’Keeffe, and they’re flying! Second in the set after Bill Sullivan’s.
https://soundcloud.app.goo.gl/qHgfQ

The Handed Down Sliabh Luachra Music Archive describes O’Connor as fiddle and C#/D accordion player from Mullen, Scartaglin, and fiddle pupil of Pádraig O’Keeffe, who had a large store of unusual local tunes.
https://worldfiddledayscartaglin.ie/?page_id=5

Three comments

What more can I say, I love soup, one of our all time favourite meals is home baked bread and homemade soup, more recently a hearty bean chowder and a garlic and muchroom bread…mmmm, mmmm, good… This tune possessed me and the first thing that came to my mind for a name was rude, the second was silly, the third was bubbling away on the stove and filling the house with scents ~ no, not those kind of scents. I do soak my beans overnight and change the water…

;-)

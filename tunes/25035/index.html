Re: Hommage À Joe Bouchard - a jig, not a reel?

A very nice tune. It gets played quite often these days in the monthly Parisian “session québecoise”.

I would prefer writing it in 12/8, but I think most people would be happy with 6/8. So, following conventions on this website, I think it should be filed as a jig, not a reel.

↳ Posted by GoPlayer on May 18th, 2026.

Re: Hommage À Joe Bouchard

Yes, this is aa 6/8 (six-huit, or jig). Spelling should be Jos, but sounds like Joe with a soft J. Hommage à Jos Bouchard, composed in homage to the québécois fiddler Jos Bouchard, by Philippe Bruneau who was an accordionist of Québec who later lived in France.

↳ Posted by Laurie Hart on September 12th, 2026.

With Ourselves

Composed by Johnny Harling, writer of “Dusty Windowsills”. This reel was mentioned in a recent discussion on favourite composers.

Be careful with your wording if you want to suggest playing this tune in a session. “Let’s…” or “Why don’t we…” is probably a bad idea. Try instead “Have you heard this nice tune? It’s called ‘With Ourselves’” :-)

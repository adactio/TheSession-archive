Glasgow 1885

Composed by Billy Jackson of Ossian fame. This air is from his 1985 album The Wellpark Suite, and the full title is “Glasgow 1885: The Dear Green Place Changed”. The title refers to one meaning of “Glasgow” translating as “the dear green place”. And so it is.

Gale Bridge

This is the third in the set of slides from Tom Barrett’s CD Lios A’Cheoil, the set being:

Bedford Cross https://thesession.org/tunes/7444
Biddy the Darling https://thesession.org/tunes/7452
Gale Bridge

The sleeve notes say “this was also a popular north Kerry slide.”

A great set of slides altogether, in my opinion.

Johnny O’Leary’s

The setting that shows up as X:2 (in spite of ceolachan’s earlier additions) is as played by Jackie Daly, who calls the tune Johnny O’Leary’s. I posted it because the upward jump to the 4th note (e) in the first phrase and repeated throughout seems to me to be a defining feature that is missing from the other settings here.

↳ Posted by Stiamh on August 8th, 2013.

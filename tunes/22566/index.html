On the first go-round at least, I hear that bar in the second as just a roll on A: |A2 GA E~A3| or A2 GA EA~A2|.

You could also play it |A2 GA EA A/B/A| or |A2 GA EA A/G/A|, or |A2 GA EA B/B/A|. DonaldK’s notion with the B also works. Much depends on what instrument is in your hands.

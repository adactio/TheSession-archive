The Cachucha

This tune appears to be a development of ‘The Cachucha’ waltz. The dance gained international recognition when on the 1st of June, 1836, the famous Austrian dancer, Fanny Elssler (1810-1884), made her debut performance of it in Paris in the role of Florinda in the ballet, Le Diable boiteux by Jean Coralli and Casimir Gide.

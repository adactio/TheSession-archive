About the tune

Since C would like some background…
I do play this tune. It isn’t played at the sessions around here which are mainly Irish music - I personally am Scottish, but living in Hamburg and find my repertoire a bit at odds with the local scene.

I somehow laid hands on printout of this piece - probably originating with Richard’s collection - quite some time ago. I have a habit of looking through random tunes for which I have the notes an trying to play them. Some strike a chord in me, probably because I’ve heard them somewhere before but cannot place where. This tune was one such. I have a program of Scottish tunes which I am working on getting other people here to learn - this tune is on the list, but not close enough to the top yet to be played. Higher on the list are Duke of Fife’s welcome to Deeside (Skinner), Tullochgorum
(Skinner version), Lend me your Loom, Lass (Oswald), Port Atholl (Gow), various Shetland jigs, Balkan Hills (Ceol na Fidhle), Mhairi Bhan Og in G (transposed from Ceol na Fidhle), Dark Island (C na F), Mucking of Geordie’s Byre (C na F)…

I tend to play it very heavily in 2/2 time, i.e. heavy emphasis on 1 and 3 with the crotchet runs being very much DAHdadada. A much more marchy feel then hornpipey. I also sometimes play it with first and second parts repeated, i.e. AA BB AA BB etc.

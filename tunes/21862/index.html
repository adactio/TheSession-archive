En Armorique

This transcription is from the harp playing of Christophe Guillemot. The tune is credited to Christy Barry.
I didn’t want to burden anyone with variations, but I also wanted to capture the best of the tune.
This is a condensed version of his many variations and subtleties. Hope you enjoy.

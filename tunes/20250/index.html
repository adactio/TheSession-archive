The Plaid Mantle

In O‘Neill’s 1001, tune #663. In the TTA, similarity is noted to the highland The Mermaid/The Maid of Mullaghmore, but the B part differs quite significantly so I haven’t added this as a setting under https://thesession.org/tunes/2854. I’ve copied the bowings and grace notes from O’Neill’s in this setting, and will add a setting with those cleaned out.

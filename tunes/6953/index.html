“Edmund MacKenzie Of Plockton” ~ by Andrew Rankine ~ take in A

Here’s a bit more fun with this one, and G & A are always interchangeable options. One of the markers of Andrew’s compositions is the freedom and fun they allow for moving through variations, having fun with them dead easy and basic or having other fun with them rhythmically or melodically. Here are some other basic possibilities, in A ~

K: A Major
|: c |
E2 G A2 c | e^de c3 | dcd B^AB | e^de c^Bc |
EFG ABc | e2 e cec | e^de =dcB | A3 A2 :|
|: e |
f^ef dcd | e^de c3 | dcd EGB | e^de c2 e |
f2 A d2 A | e2 A c2 A | Ee^d =dcB | A3- A3 :|

Frankentune

I honestly like this tune - but I jokingly have always called it “Frankentune” because it’s the good parts of about 5 other tunes glued together. I know that “creative borrowing” is the mother of invention, but sheesh!

Soft spot

I really adore this tune; it’s named after the street I grew up on in Chicago, so it’s held a special place in my heart ever since I first heard about it. It was a real trip to visit a session in Inverness and find them playing it there!

The Echo, X:2

similar to version 1, with some minor variations :
introduction Notes at the beginning of the tune.
rolls on high g’s part B
4 last notes of the last bar of repeat of Part B to round back into beginning of part A.

I learned this tune from a live recording of Liam O’Flynn made by Dave Williams in early 1987 in London. Dave gave me a tape of this Live recording when I came to him to collect my pipes in April 1987.

Liam O’Flynn says that he learned the tune form Sean Keane (the fiddle player of the Chieftains)

The starting notes “d2 d2 fe” to introduce the tune reais as a distnct patrern of this tune in my memory.

Liam played An tSean Bhean Bhocht after the Echo.

Return To Kilty

A lovely composition of Paddy Hayes, originally from Piercestown in Wexford, but spent a lot of his life in London where he played with many great musicians and won many awards including 2 senior All-Irelands on concertina. This is the first tune of the set on the final track of his first album, named Return to Kilty.
Paddy retired to Co. Leitrim many years ago and sadly has passed away in recent years. He has left a legacy of roughly 20 compositions that I know of, and I’m busily notating them as part of The Wexford tune project I’m undertaking.

Re: The Merry Thatcher

This reel is almost exactly as it appears in Ceol Rince Na hEireann vol 4, #134.
The notes say that it was taken from a manuscript called the Jack Wade collection.
(Jake Wade, from Co. Monaghan, born in the 20th century).

It further says: a favorite of Arthur Kelly, flute player, form Ring Common, Co. Dublin.
Kelly is about 40 years dead and was a good friend of Jimmy Ennis, Naul, Co. Dublin, present
Seamus Ennis’s father. (written in 1960)

So, apparently, the reel is old. I’m not sure that it should be attributed to Pean O’Shea without some
more evidence for that attribution.

↳ Posted by hetty on June 29th, 2006.

Nothing really influenced the A part of this tune although I think it’s similiar to the “Doon Reel”.

The B part has a few similiarities to tunes like “The Green Mountain”, “Tinker’s Daughter” and “Cronin’s Reel” but I didn’t want to make a carbon copy of those tunes and besides, after bar 1, the rest of the tune attached itself immediatley.

By the way, for your interest, Drimnagh is a suburb just outside Dublin’s City Centre. It’s where I live and with famous musicians like sean Keane and Sean Potts hailing from this area, I think it deserves a tune of it own. :)

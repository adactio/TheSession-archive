Nick Sheridan’s Farewell

This is a reel composed a number of years ago to mark the retirement from his teaching career of the Wexford traditional pianist, Nick Sheridan. We both laboured together in the same educational establishment and this little reel came in to my head while supervising several hundred children one playtime. Nick is a former member of the much-fabled Bunclody ceili band ( as was his father John). Lately Nick has been joining me playing the Centenary Stores Sunday music session.

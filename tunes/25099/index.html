The Battle Of Geashille

Composed by flute player John Brady, Co. Offaly in the late 1980s. The high king of Ireland, Míl on his deathbed decreed that his two sons - Ír and Êibhear - should divide Ireland into two halves, north and south. After a disagreement, the Battle of Geashill took place and Êibhear became King of Ireland.

Transcribed from Tommy Mellett and Neansai Ni Choisdealbha’s 2018 album An Tower.

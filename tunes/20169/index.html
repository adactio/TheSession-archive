Denis O’Keeffe’s

This is based on the playing of Paudie O’Connor, https://thesession.org/recordings/1518 track 11. A simple polka but none the worse for that.

Denis O’Keeffe’s, X:2

From the playing of Gerry Harrington on Track 7 of The Fiddle, The Voice where this is credited as Johnny O’Leary’s. Some friends and I play it in a session and call the set the T7 polkas, from the track number of the album we all learned it from.

↳ Posted by BansheeWailsOverTheMangoPit on August 31st, 2021.

Denis O’Keeffe’s, X:3

This is from Breandán Ó Beaglaoich’s playing on Seana Choirce https://thesession.org/recordings/1277 where it is listed as Johnny Leary’s II. He plays the parts in the opposite order to, say, Paudie O’Connor.

↳ Posted by brailsford on November 18th, 2021.

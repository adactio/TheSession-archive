Flight Of The Folk Mutants Part 1

I got this beaut from Bellowhead’s Matachin, on the 12th track I believe, played after Trip to Bucharest. I instantly had to learn these tunes, but all that was on TS was Trip to Bucharest, so I had to plough through this indecipherable tune and I thought I’d give a hand to others who, like me, just wanted to know how to play the f*ing tune.

I tried my best to represent the feel of the tune and I think I have it to the note for the As and Bs, but I’m not so sure about the Cs cuz they have alot of other things going on in the background then, but I like to think I captured at least the important bits and the kinda idea it tried to give. Please, do tell me if you’ve noticed I’ve made a mistake, or if an improvement can be made.

Part 2 is really simple in comparison, but I think I’ll put it up for the sake of it, simply because I’ve done the first part.

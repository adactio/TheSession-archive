The Jacqueline

From Florence Fahy’s CD, Tunes from the Flaggy Shore ( https://thesession.org/recordings/5749 ). Florence says she learned this from her father years ago but doesn’t know where he learned it from. According to Wikipedia, The Jacqueline was composed by the Scottish accordionist Will Starr and recorded by him on his first recording in 1940. However, the mood of this concertina version is very different from the version played by Will (on YouTube), and the B part is not the same. It’s a nice change of pace tune. Florence ornaments and varies the B part differently each of the 6 times she plays it and these ornaments are not included in the transcription. The CD is a gem!

Re: The Jacqueline waltz

As far as I’m aware the Jacqueline waltz was composed by the late Will Starr of Croy for his then girlfriend Jacqueline Georgeson.
Will first recorded this tune in the parlofone label in 1951.
He recorded the tune several times over the years, the last being on his final pye L.P. album recorded in 1974 entitled “In Starr Tyme”

An extremely popular waltz played by many musicians over the years.

I note that the second measure in the guide track has differences to the original played by Will Starr.

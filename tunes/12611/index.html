Two comments

Working Man’s Friend

This is from the Eddie Dooley manuscripts - there’s a note on the back of this that says “Con O‘Shea Dungarvan 1968” - whether this was composed by O’Shea (a fiddler from The Spring mentioned elsewhere in Dooley’s notes) or is one of Dooley’s own is unresolved.

One comment

Prayer Reel

This tune was composed (and proof-read) by Boston’s legendary Joe Derrane, who wrote it for his late wife Anne. It’s included on his “Grove Lane” CD. Lots of tasty stuff going on and a real challenge to play (unless of course you’re Joe Derrane). If the D sharps cause problems for you, keep the D natural - I don’t think Joe (or Anne) would mind!

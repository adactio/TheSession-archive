Cailleach A’ Ghobhainn

Second tune on the track of the same name on “Thawcrook”. It’s never played the same way twice but this transcription captures the overall sense of structure in which the variations live.

The name literally means “The Smith’s Old Woman” but idiomatically I suppose the “smith” in this case is Cailleach’s divine husband.

The Dancing Bow

Composed by accordionist Richie Dwyer, from his 2002 CD In a Creative Mood. Interestingly, this tune is from the only medley on the album where Richie is playing the fiddle. Everything else of course is on the box.

I talked to Richie on the phone a couple of years ago, asking if I could include some of his tunes in one of my tune books, and he very generously gave permission. I didn’t speak to him about posting this tune here, so I’m hoping he won’t mind. His tunes are just great.

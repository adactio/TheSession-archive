Dublin Streets, X:5

Based on O’Neill’s “The 1850” without accidentals which can get confusing at times.
Due to its obvious similarity with “Fig For A Kiss” (and “The Monaghan” in some way), “Dublin Streets” feels much more natural in Edor than Emin to me if we decide to delete the D# accidentals.
I made slight changes in bars 6 & 10 and arranged the phrases’ endings aiming to improve clarity and flexibility of play.

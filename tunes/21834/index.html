One comment

Ar Mhuin Na Muice

C: Seán Ryan
T: On the pig’s back
The transcription is simplified from the version played by Téada on their album Coiscéim coiligh = As the days brighten. - Dublin: Gael Linn, 2022 https://thesession.org/recordings/7571
Starts like The Trip to Nenagh, but in the Dorian mode, rather than the Mixolydian. I’m sure we play this in local session, unattributed, and with a different title. An ABC search showed nothing on The Session that goes to that low tonic in the third beat of the first bar, so I’m assuming this is not elsewhere on The Session.

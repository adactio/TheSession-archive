Little Pine Siskin, X:2

Based on composer Reischman’s “Inside the Craft” video, chords from the “Walk Along” album. Simplified, there are many nice variations and lead in notes to be gleaned from the audio versions. A pine siskin is a small woodlands bird.
Why do I like it? At a recent memorial service for fiddler Jim Rood, friends recalled this as one of his favorite tunes, always effective at the end of the night as in “c’mon Jim, just one more. How about Little Pine Siskin?” So this one’s for Jim.

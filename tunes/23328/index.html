The Calgary Polka

Well… if you want a challenge this one is for you. A very tricky tune transcribed from this video of Zoë Conway: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnktHY89gkY. I tried to transcribe the double stops too as she plays them as I think they add a lot to the tune!

Very very tricky (especially at the pace that she plays it) but rewarding if you put the time in to crack it!!!

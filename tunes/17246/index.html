Also known as Brilliancy

This is also known as “brilliancy” in bluegrass circles. There are nice versions of Sam Bush playing it on YouTube. Bela Fleck inserted “brilliancy” in one of its instruction book for the 5 strings banjo “Banjo Picking Styles”.

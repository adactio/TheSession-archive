Two comments

Lally’s Fancy

First tune submission, so please let me know if you see any egregious errors.

I came to this tune off of a Zoe Conway / John Mc Intyre album called Go Mairir | Bhfad or Long Life to You. It’s a wonderful album of recently composed fiddle tunes - this one is by Frankie Gavin. On the album, this tune is preceded by another Frankie Gavin tune called Kilkenny Station, and while I was able to find Kilkenny Station on The Session, I wasn’t able to find any tune called Lally’s Fancy, hence this submission.

Hope you enjoy learning it as much as I have.

↳ Posted by Adam Becherer on July 18th, 2021.

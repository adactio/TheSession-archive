History of the Yorkshire Waltz

My wife (June) and I wrote this tune at the birth of our first daughter (Heather) in May 1983. We were living on a street in Stony Brook NY called Yorkshire Avenue, so we named the tune The Yorkshire Waltz (I note that the session has truncated this to The Yorkshire…. how can I get it to be called by its full name?).

It was written specifically to work well on a G/D melodeon or anglo concertina hence the E minor / G Major split, and the simple (but effective?) chord progressions. But of course it works well on any instruments and I’m sure those piano accordion players and guitar strummers can come up with even more interesting chords.

Enjoy.

John Rowlands

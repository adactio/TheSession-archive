One comment

Lonesome Times

The tune appears on James Kelly’s “Capel Street” from 1989. As stated on irishtune.info under the 3404 ID, it seems to be its only known version, and possibly a James Kelly’s composition. I added an alternative G for the lowest C each time it shows up, in case of using a flute which has no lower keys - such as mine. It felt like the less disturbing choice to me but that is arguable I suppose. I hope it’s not too bad a transcript but feel free to enhance it.

Five comments

Composed by Emile Benoit. Note that on his album Emile’s Dream, Benoit only played the B part twice through, instead of what I think is the fairly standard three times through today.

↳ Posted by Sol Foster on December 30th, 2009.

Errr…. well, when I submitted the tune, it had a third ending to indicate to repeat to B part three times. As far as I know, that is how most people play the tune these days.

↳ Posted by Sol Foster on December 31st, 2009.

And hey, thanks to the new system I’m able to fix the ABC to have the AABBB structure it had when I first entered it in here! I am really, really liking the new Session.

Two comments

Whittle Dene

A tune no doubt from the North East of England. Whittle Dene is a place west of Newcastle where reservoirs were built in 1845 or thereabouts, under the initiation of William Armstrong. It led to the formation of the Whittle Dean Water Company, and the reservoir complex watered Newcastle and Gateshead on its own until the demand for more water led to further reservoir supply.
A good place for fishing, I’m told.

The tune has similarities to other Geordie hornpipes.
It appears in Köhler’s Violin Repository (I came across it when checking out another recent submission here) under the name of ‘Wittle Dean’. John Chambers lists it as a reel, but it has obvious hornpipe tendencies.

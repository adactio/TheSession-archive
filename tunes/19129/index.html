Two comments

Evalina

Played by the Bohemian Orchestra on a 78rpm record from the 1930’s.
The tune is in three parts, played A-B-A-C-A

Jeff Lawrie in Newcastle NSW kindly gave me a copy he’d made from Andy Grant’s collection of 78’s. Julie Castles and Jeff transcribed the tune, and I’ve added a couple of changes. Whenever we’re at a session together this tune gets an airing.

Enjoy!

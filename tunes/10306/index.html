Willow Tree hornpipe

Composed by Northumbrian piper, Tom Clough, and apparently named after the Willow Tree pub near Blyth, Northumberland.
Recorded by Colin Ross and Chris Ormston on “Spirit of the Border” (Nimbus 5615)

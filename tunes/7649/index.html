The Jaunty Warren Hornpipe.

Composed after learning Fitzgerald’s and taking a walk through jaunty Warren, Rhode Island, which is doubtless the smallest town in the smallest state in the USA. Still, it’s a fun place with plenty of good food and good people and general hornpipe-deserving things. Enjoy!

--DtM

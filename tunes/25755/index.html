Five comments

The Flannel Jacket

A tip o’ the hat to Alan Ng (https://www.irishtune.info/tune/4460) for pointing me to
this setting (sans ornamentation) from The Roche Collection of Traditional Irish Music: tune #141, “The Flannel Jacket”, a single reel in G.

“The Peeler’s Jacket” (https://thesession.org/tunes/1343) is sometimes called “The Flannel Jacket”. Last year, “cellopudding” posted setting 5 of the Peeler’s Jacket, from Téada’s recording “Give Us A Penny And Let Us Be Gone” -- but it’s a very different melody from the rest of the settings on the Peeler’s Jacket page. So off I went down the rabbit hole… Tunepal was stumped, ABC search came up empty, searching here for “flannel” and “jacket” came up empty… but, IrishTune.info connected me with Mr. Roche. The ITMA has transcriptions from Roche’s collection (Volume 1 at https://www.itma.ie/notated-collections/roche_vol-1/), but tune #141 is missing! Fortunately a fiddler friend had the book and sent me a photo: the missing piece of the puzzle.

The Irishtune.info page for The Flannel Jacket (https://www.irishtune.info/tune/4460) lists (at present) three recordings, all featuring Oisín Mac Diarmada. They’re the same basic melody but completely different arrangements. Though I often compare several recordings of a tune, it was a real treat to listen to Oisín’s three unique interpretations side by side.

* Oisín Mac Diarmada on “ar an bhfidil” (“on the fiddle”, except he’s not playing fiddle on that track) -- very simple, old-Ireland whistle playing with bodhran.

* Téada on their recording “Lá an Dreoilín” (Give Us A Penny…) -- fiddle and guitar, solid contemporary trad

* Téada (among many artists) on “Live from the Katharine Cornell Center”: fiddle and banjo with extra jazzy groove.

The Flannel Jacket, X:3

My own transcription from Oisín Mac Diarmada’s recording “ar an bhfidil” (“on the fiddle”, except he’s not playing fiddle on this track). It’s probably a C whistle with G fingers. Oisín plays the C’s quite sharp (I almost set the key sig to D, but that would have been confusing) and he plays the whole tune with a lot of articulation. Reminds me of Micho Russell’s style.

Re: The Flannel Jacket - similar tunes

Two other tunes are quite similar in the A part, but not in the B part: “Sean Reid’s” (https://thesession.org/tunes/2249) and “The Donaghmore” (https://thesession.org/tunes/8126).

I’m pretty sure that the tune titled “The Peeler’s Jacket” on Danu’s “Think Before You Think” is “Sean Reid’s”. And the slow reel titled “The Peeler’s Jacket” on Brian Hughes’ album “The Clear Air” sounds more like “Sean Reid’s” than either Peeler’s Jacket (1343) or Flannel Jacket (this melody), though it might be some other very-similar tune.

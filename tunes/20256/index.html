Lady Douglas Of Bothwell

This tune was composed by Scottish fiddler Donald Dow (1732 – 1783). If I remember correctly the first time I ever heard it was two or three years ago when Mr Nigel Gatherer played it. I miss those times when we could sit down and have a quiet tune before class at the Glasgow Folk-Music Workshop. :)

This is the way I play the tune, I’m not sure it’s exactly the way Nigel plays it. And, to be fair, I never play it exactly the same. Here’s a similar version in Gmin.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hzR4HwOwoV8

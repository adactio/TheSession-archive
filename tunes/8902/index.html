Trip to Amsterdam

This is a tune a friend of mine wrote on an E-flute on a train to Amsterdam. Wedged between some bikes and suitcases.
I think I may have added bits to it, or smoothed bits out.

We were going to call it “The golden locket” for romantic reasons but we thought “Trip to Amsterdam” was the better choice. I was taking him to the airport to go home after he stayed with me in Holland. I hope you see this Pete…it’s your tune…

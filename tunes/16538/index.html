Miss Johnson Of Pitworth

This is a Scottish reel composed by James Walker which is popular in Cape Breton. It is the third tune in Winston “Scotty” Fitzgerald’s famous set “Miss Hutton / The Braes of Tullimet / Miss Johnson of Pitworth”. The tune often appears in conjunction with “Miss Hutton” in other sets as well. Versions of this reel differ slightly, but I have based my transcription on Fitzgerald’s version, which I assume to be the basis for most subsequent recorded versions.

Re: Miss Johnson Of Pitworth

I have just come across this tune - one or two variations and with two flats and no e in it at all - on page 44 of a Highland Music Trust publication (2006) entitled Fiddle Music from Fife: The 18th Century Collections of Alexander Leburn of Auchtermuchty and James Walker of Dysart.
Both Leburn (an amateur) & Walker (a professional musician & dance teacher) published in the 1790s with James Johnson of Edinburgh. Is it popular in Cape Breton because a Scots immigrant bought Walker’s book & took it abroad, or because it’s been unearthed and republished, I wonder.
Either way, it’s a nice tune and thank you for posting it.

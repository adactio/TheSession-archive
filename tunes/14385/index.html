Letter From Scotland

This beautiful reel was composed by the german whistler Olaf Sickmann.. It is on his album WHISTLE MAN, released in 2014. For whistle players in particular this album deserves a listen. Unlike some of the generally faster Irish traditional whistle albums that have predominantly jigs and reels, Olaf introduces some new slow pieces that are in truth difficult to categorise, but clearly show another side to whistle playing in an album context.

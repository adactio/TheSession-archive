Gun Bhris Mo Chrish‘ On Dh’fhalbh Thu

NB, this is a slow air rather than a reel. It is from Captain Fraser’s Collection.
He says this “is one of the love songs to which it is so difficult to attach incident, as every pair of lovers instantly apply the words and airs that suit them to their own case. This is, however, one of the sacred compilation made by the gentlemen named in the prospectus, and universally admired on all convivial occasions. Within their own district they had the aid of a celebrated son of song, Mr. Thomas Houston, whose family are proprietors in Sutherland” (Fraser).

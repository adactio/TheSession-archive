Jimmy Doyle’s, X:5

This is a slightly dressed up version of Jeremy’s, as learned from Cork-based Sliabh Luachra fiddler, Matt Cranitch. He calls it Din Tarrants 1; however, when played together with Din Tarrant’s 2 (a.k.a, The Lisheen Polka https://thesession.org/tunes/2930), they are referred to as “The Lonesome Polkas,” though no one seems to know why.

The second of two polkas in a set, the names as used by McNeela,
Spring Session 2026 - Set #1 :

Johnny O’Leary’s Polka ( https://thesession.org/tunes/4824 ) / The Lonesome Polka

Benny McCarthy - B/C Melodeon
Brian Hughes - D whistle
Caoimhín Ó Fearghail - Bouzouki

Video #1: “Johnny O’Leary’s Polka” in Key of D - taught and broken down by Benny McCarthy

Video #2: “The Lonesome Polka” in Key of G - taught and broken down by Benny McCarthy

Video #3: Join the Full Set - all three musicians contributing and at a lovely and relaxed old-style pace, not manic…

The notes they’ve provided are in a simplified old-style ABC notation, ‘just’ the notes, all caps, an apostrophe ( D’ ) used to raise a note up an octave, a single hyphen ( D- ) for doubling the length of a note, and a hash ( F# ) used for sharp:

The Lonesome Polka in key of G -

A B D’ G B A A G G-

B D‘ G’ F# E‘ E’

- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

I’m impressed and pleased by such generosity and kindness, as here shown for the music and for those in their beginning, to share ones passion, understanding and skill. This showed up in our email this week and raised a much appreciated smile. Bless McNeela Music & Co., and the three musicians here that have given their time in offering such opportunities for others to learn this music and to gain the proficiency and confidence to participate in music making with others, to join a session virtually, and hopefully also in the real world too…

- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

“What’s Next: Spring 2026 Session #2 is coming soon!” - worth getting on McNeela’s mailing list, for this and much more… Even when it’s a rare something I might disagree with, those challenges are welcome, including giving ear to some music that might make me wince. ;-) There seems to be a good spirit behind it all, greatly appreciated, and even that rare wince or guffaw is usually followed by a smile, sometimes a chuckle or an LOL…often at myself…

Following some of McNeela’s lessons has helped to make me a better teacher… I have always enjoyed joining a beginners class for the craic and to see how others teach, and to gain pointers as well as sharing in the passion and joy of others coming early to the music. There’s as much to learn from others learning as from those doing the teaching…

The Picc Line, X:2

First heard this on an album ‘No time like now’ by Cherrygrove, played after The Lisnagun. I took a liking to this and simplified it for playing on a D whistle, a fine tune!

↳ Posted by Matt Hubbert on October 22nd, 2020.

Re: The Picc Line

This tune was written by myself, Heather Shelley. I wrote the tune when myself and the rest of the band CherryGrove were crowd funding for our debut album. Mike Vass asked me to write his tune and this is it. Part of the deal was that Mike titled the tune and “The Picc Line” is what he came up with. He has never told me the reasoning behind the title, it may always be a mystery!

↳ Posted by HevShev on January 18th, 2021.

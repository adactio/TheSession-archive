Three comments

Luseblus (Sweater Blues)

This is a cool “Norwegian” tune composed by Hardanger fiddler Annbjorg Lien. Really groovy tune and one of my faves… on her albums “Prisme” and “Aliens Alive”.

↳ Posted by Erik-Fiddler on April 13th, 2006.

Luseblus rhythm of last part

In the string sisters life version the rhythm of the last part should be like this:

|:c2d2A4|c2d2^f4|c2d2A2c2|d4d4|
c2d2A4|c2d2^f4|gag2f2d2|c4d4:|

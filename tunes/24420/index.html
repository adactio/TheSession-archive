Lost At Sea

Featured on the Earl Grey in the morning CD: More Coffee Please. During the concert in Washington State, Al mentioned that the tune was written after an experience on a two masted sailing vessel trip off the coast of Boston when the wind stopped and a fog dropped onto the ship for 6 hours

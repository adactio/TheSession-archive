Thirteen comments

The Cockerel And The Creel

This great tune has been requested a few times so thought I would submit it. I got this version from ‘The White Album’ by Alasdair White. Alasdair has a great flamboyant style so I’m not entirely sure how close it is to the original by Donald MacLeod. If I can find an older version I’ll put it in the comments here.

↳ Posted by bogman on September 15th, 2009.

The cockerel in the creel

.. and at a guess the name might come from the story of strict Sundays in the highlands and islands where the hens might be let out but the cockerel would be kept apart - stuck under a creel or basket, so that there would be no naughtiness on the Sabbath!

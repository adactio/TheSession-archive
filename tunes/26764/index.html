Mustard Foldies

Transcribed this tune off of Ten Strings and a Goatskin’s album Auprès du Poêle. It’s the third tune in the set including “The Ukrainian Expedition,” “The Coalminer,” and “Réel du Raclette.” Lots of ornamentation in this that is super cool and very inspiring. Haven’t heard this tune anywhere else but on that album but it’s a lot of fun to play.

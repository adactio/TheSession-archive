Five comments

Miss Edmonston

This is in B flat major. From Paul Cranford’s Cape Breton Scottish Collection. Been trying to learn to play in this great but under appreciated key. This is a good one for it. Just down the scale and across strings in a couple of places. It gets you reaching those B to b notes as well.

Notes say it’s an 18th century tune and popular with several well known players. It appears in the Athole Collection, Neil Stewart’s 1761 Collection and the Glen Collection Vol. 1, 1891.

Ríl Gan Ainm

This is the middle of five tunes, off of the track ‘Siamsa’ (from “Lord of the Dance”); yes, another show tune, but I love this little reel. The entire set is great. Not overly weird or funky sounding for the show. Considering some of the other tunes… ‘Siamsa’ is, essentially, a softshoe group ceili number in the second half of the show. It’s a polka to start in D, then a slide in E, then this tune, a double jig in A, and finally the original polka to finish.

Of course it’s not going to be heard in a pub, but how many tunes on here are likely in the same boat? 😆

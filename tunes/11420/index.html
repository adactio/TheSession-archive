I found this little Strathspey whilst looking through “A Collection of Scots Reels or Country Dances” by Robert Bremner. It is available (sorry, PDF only, folks) here:

http://imslp.org/wiki/A_Collection_of_Scots_Reels_or_Country_Dances_(Bremner,_Robert)

…amongst many other fine tunes. The older ones a best, IMO…

Enjoy!

The Fir Tree, X:2

Natalie MacMaster’s version of this tune is very different from the other that was originally posted on here; and for good reason. This setting is easier and more fun to play, and is amenable to dancing. You can hear her play it with Mac Morin here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A3GwfAGgTRQ.

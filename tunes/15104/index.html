One comment

Oor Pal Davy

Tune written by John McCusker, this is not a waltz, but a slow air, to be played slow and stately. This tune is crooked (like a lot of John’s compositions), see lines 5 and 11, which only have 7 measures each.

This tune really benefits from the proper phrasing and dynamics, and becomes kind of boring if just played straight, as happened in last week’s session; maybe should be saved for more solo or duet performances.

Of course, John McCusker performs it beautifully on his Yella Hoose/Goodnight Ginger album:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s1mdbfyXz4s

and in concert here with accordion and choir backup:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RX5qi1P_blA

I set it on loop for long drives at night.

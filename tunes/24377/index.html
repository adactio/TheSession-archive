Light Returns

I very seldom post my own compositions, but here’s a slip jig I wrote this spring that I’m happy with. The title refers to the experience of the total solar eclipse in April 2024 -- we had a day with complete cloud cover, so didn’t see the sun itself. Instead we experienced day turn to complete darkness in the span of minutes, followed by the rush of a sudden dawn. In the silence, I whispered, “Light returns,” and a man sitting near me nodded soberly.

One comment

Plan B

A good-going tune, though far from ITM, which I’ve found hard to classify for the system here. I had transcribed it in 2/4 time using 1/16 basic note length so it’s adapted and I hope it works. From Fullsceilidh Spelemannslag’s 2009 CD “Spreefix” and composed by Annlaug Borsheim and Linda Gytri. “Annlaug and Linda arrived in Shetland during November to find their tour had to be cancelled due to the weather. The boys from Spreefix soon had an impromptu alternative tour scheduled! This included an evening at Adam and Emma Priest’s house / garage warming in South Nesting, a late night visit to ”The Galley Shed“, an early morning ”Bunker“ pit-stop and the finale, greeting the North Boat coming in, standing on the pier playing ”Leaving Lerwick Harbour“ https://thesession.org/tunes/1874 backwards … The next day the lasses composed Plan B.”

↳ Posted by lottiemaus on August 17th, 2013.

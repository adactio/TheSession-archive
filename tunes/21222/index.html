Re: The Triangle

ceili, you don’t need the natural in front of the high A in the first setting as A natural is in the key signature (key should be E rather than Bmix but they both have the same signature).

Also, the key signature for the second setting should be two sharps because the key is D (not A) and then you wouldn’t need the natural in front of the high G.

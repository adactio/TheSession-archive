The Mother-In-Law

I picked this tune from O‘Neill’s book (Miles Krassen republishing); I really love it, it’s simple and quite original at the same time. There are no repeat signs in O’Neill’s version, but I often catch myself playing them anyway, it doesn’t trouble the tune’s balance imo.

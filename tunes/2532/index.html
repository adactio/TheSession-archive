Four comments

Midwinter waltz

I heard this on a Boys of the Lough CD and enjoyed it’s simplicity. As with other“simple” tunes, it sounds nice when played through straight on one or two instruments, with others coming in later to swell and add to the melody.
I would be interested to know who composed it.

Midwinter Waltz

This is “A Midwinter Waltz” composed by Dave Richardson of The Boys of the Lough. Although living in Edinburgh for many years, he is Northumbrian. https://thesession.org/tunes/2532

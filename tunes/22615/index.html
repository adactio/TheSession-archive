Three comments

The Octopus

From the playing of Flaithrí Neff, Eoghan Neff, Karl Nesbitt, & Ger Harrington.

Composed by Eoghan Neff.

They return to the A Part (from the B Part) on a B-note, with “B>d cB” for the first bar.

The liner notes say this of the tune:
“Pipers aren’t often fond of polkas so I composed [this tune] with piping in mind and an apt title for Flaithrí considering the contraption he has to deal with!”

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on January 1st, 2023.

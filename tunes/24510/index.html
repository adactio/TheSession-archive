Three comments

Gan Ainm

From the playing of Cathal McConnell on a private tape where he played it for Micho Russell. He explained to Micho that it came from a man called John Ward, presumably local to Fermanagh. Cathal also mentioned that there was a few versions of it and, indeed, it sounds very much like the Drogheda Lasses in the A-part but deviates completely on the turn.

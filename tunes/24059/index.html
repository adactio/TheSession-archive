As written by Tommy Peoples

This is a lovely tune. But it would require some pretty high level fiddle skills to bring it off.

Its composer, Tommy Peoples, talks about it in his book “Ó Am Go hAm - From Time to Time” and gives his own score which differs from the version here in several ways. It also has some cute drawings of mice on it.
I think it’s a fantastic book but it’s sold out unfortunately. However I just saw the score is featured on the Tommy Peoples website - at the bottom of the page here:
https://tommypeoples.ie/the-book/

So you can check out yourself how the composer’s score differs from Juergen’s transcription, if you feel like it.

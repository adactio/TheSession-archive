What’s Up With Win

While looking for gaps of documented tunes on Solas CDs on THE SESSION
I found this tune by Mick McAuley, Seamus Eagan and John Doyle on THE HOUR BEFORE DAWN.
There are a few special effects (as you can expect from a Solas arrangement).
But the composition is attractive over all and can easily be simplified to a good session tune.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aUOFUDNY3Yc
The next tune in the set is in fact not Sonny Brogan’s (as they call it) but Bill Hart’s Favourite,
which I have learned from Eddie & Finbar Furey in 1972.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3zfq8gSrc5A

Re: What’s Up With Win

So… not to be annoying—but I’m 99.99% sure this tune was written in and is played in A-Mixolydian, not A-Dorian. If you listen to the recording, it starts with major chords, “A” chords, with sporadic “G” chords, as in…Mixolydian mode. lol.

That final variation, the C-part, which they don’t actually play until the second repeat of the tune (on the track, the tune is arranged AABBAABBCC), is briefly in F#-minor, the relative of A. The accidentals notated here kind of obscure the sound a bit. For me. Lol.

↳ Posted by Erik-Fiddler on October 6th, 2023.

What’s Up With Win, X:2

Ignoring variations, I hear something like X:2.

The two bar coda resolves on to a D note for the next tune.

Cahill’s Wain

In Nov. 1992 I was in the Irish Center Phila. Pa. speaking with Eugene O Donnell and remarked of my amazement at how some can compose a tune and, says he, “Sure it’s easy” and proceeds to do so in @ five minutes or less. As my wife, Gwenn Egan, was pregnant with our second child at the time he named it Cahill’s Wain. He wrote it down on a bar napkin which I still have. Thanks to Dennis for putting it in the ABC format and to Chris and Linda for playing it for me so I could learn it by ear.

Re: Cahill’s Wain

It’s usually spelled WEAN, as it’s a contraction of ‘wee yin’. (Wain is an English word for a wagon: see also King Charles’s Wain = Ursa Major).)

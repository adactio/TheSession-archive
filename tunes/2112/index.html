My first tune

This is the first tune I ever wrote. I wrote it early in 2003, when I was 14. I named it for myself, although nobody calls me Patrick; it is my confirmation name. I would like anybody to feel free to play this song in a session or in any other public setting; there is no copyright on this tune. If, however, some one asks who wrote the tune, please give credit where credit is due. I hope you like my jig; I would be greatly honored if you added it to your repertoire. I will be posting more of my tunes on this site soon. My goal is to write 100 tunes before I turn 18, and I will post them all here.

-Max Becher

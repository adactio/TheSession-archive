Tell Your Mother I Owes Her Four Pence

From the playing of The Walsh Family.

Newfoundland traditional single/polka. Ron and Ray Walsh learned it from their uncle, Jack Keyes of Bay de Verde.

Note on the name, in Newfoundland, often time we add “s” to the end of verbs.

Re: Tell Your Mother I Owes Her Four Pence

The verse goes:

take the jug down from the shelf
and fill it up right full of gin,
tell yer mudder I owes her four pence
when the next time she comes in.

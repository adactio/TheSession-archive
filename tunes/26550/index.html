Three comments

Tornala Maika

From Brìghde Chaimbeul’s album “The Reeling”. Quick research suggests this is based off of a Bulgarian folk song. Brìghde has talked about being influenced by the Bulgarian piping tradition in interviews.

The tricky extra half-bars are undoubtedly part of a more complex/elegant Bulgarian meter, but I wouldn’t know. Notated them to fit how they sound to me.

The coda is meant to represent the melody played four times round at the end of the recording - it could well be a separate tune. The main tune’s structure is AABBCC x3

Coolinarne Jig

Another tune from uilleann piper Brendan RING. He follows this tune with the Lisnagun Jig.

↳ Posted by JACKB on March 13th, 2005.

“The Coolinerne” ~ gian marco’s take on this:

T: Coolinerne, The
M: 6/8
L: 1/8
R: jig
K: Gmaj

Coolinarne, X:5

From the playing of Paddy O’Brien (Offaly) who says (in Tune Collection,
volume three, jig #78) that he heard it from the Dave Sheridan and Ciaran Somers
record. None of the other settings have the same note order as Paddy’s, in particular
the seventh bar of each part.

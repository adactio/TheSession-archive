Tra An Phearla

From Nollaig Casey’s liner notes on Causeway: “I’ve called this tune after a little strand near Allihies in West Cork known as Tra an Phearla, or the Strand of the Pearl. ‘The Pearl’ was the name of a ship, which was en route from the East Indies and which sank there in the early seventeenth century.”

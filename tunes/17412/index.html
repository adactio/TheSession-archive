Through The Gates

A fantastic tune by Russ Barenberg that’s been played a few times recently at our session. It’s more common in the contra scene, but would love to see it be more common in tune sessions.

↳ Posted by Ian Varley on October 28th, 2018.

Re: Through The Gates

I’ve heard this paired with Ashoken Farewell, Ashoken played slowly, then Through the Gates gradually faster (but not too fast) after the last slightly held note of the Farewell. Also the B part was syncopated a little differently (if my ABC is correct):
B d(dd) d(ddd) d(dd) | d(dd) Bd efed|
Also, the last quarter note d in the first measure of the B part is tied over to the first quarter note d in the second measure. Don’t know how to do that in ABC.

Miss Baird Of Saughton Hall, X:2

This is a bagpipe setting from the playing of P.M. Edward Kitlowski. Thanks to Tom Ritter for the written score. This tune is a perfect follow-up to Go to Berwick Johnny. The latter version of GTBJ played in the mid-Atlantic is version 6 by Crackpot who cites the playing of Chris Norman. Both are 3/2 tunes and called triple hornpipes. Play them on the slower side of the tempo bar.

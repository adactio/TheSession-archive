Seven comments

A tune that is usually taught to beginners in my area, and so is played occasionally at sessions. It comes up regularly in set dancing sessions.

↳ Posted by fidicen on March 20th, 2003.

This polka was composed by Mick Hanly. The ABC is virtually identical to that in Henrik Norbeck’s collection.

↳ Posted by fidicen on March 20th, 2003.

It’s possible this tune may have been picked up by local Bristol teachers from Kevin Burke’s visit to the city a few years ago.

↳ Posted by fidicen on March 20th, 2003.

Composed in G

Mick Hanly composed this in G major, though it’s commonly played in A now. He wrote it for his daughter, who was born two days before he recorded his second solo album ‘As I Went Over Blackwater’, on which Jessica’s Polka first appeared.

↳ Posted by Robert_Ryan on October 15th, 2009.

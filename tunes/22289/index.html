Valsamanu

Written by Breton accordionist Loig Troël and guitarist Eric Liorzou. As played by Asturian band Acuei.
In the last part, play “e” in 1, 3 and “B” in 2, 4.

The MIDI player doesn’t seem to understand the repeats. Better watch the video.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k7qqzLYPuko

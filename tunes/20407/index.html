Limerick Junction

Simplified setting of tune #820 in O’Neill’s 1001. The A part is very similar to Peter Wyper’s, https://thesession.org/tunes/5384, but the B part is significantly different both in melody and in structure, so I added it as a separate tune. I’ve removed triplets and bowings in this setting, and will post them as a second setting.

Re: Limerick Junction

This hornpipe is a variation on Raftery’s Favorite, according to O’Neill in his Waifs and Strays of Gaelic Melody (#362 notes). Raftery’s Favorite, in turn, looks like a variation on Ferry Bridge/Peter Wyper’s (https://thesession.org/tunes/5384). One of his sources for Limerick Junction was Edward Cronin (from Co. Limerick) and it was named after his native town.
To complicate matters, O’Neill has different versions of Limerick Junction in his 1850 (#1720) and DMI.820. In the earlier publication it is closer to Ferry Bridge, but still differing in the turn….

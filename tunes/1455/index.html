Littlejohn’s Hame

This is a Strathspey by fiddler James Scott Skinner called “Littlejohn’s Hame” and can be found in a collection called “The Scottish Music Maker” (published 1957).

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on February 4th, 2013.

↳ Posted by Jude Hodgens on October 11th, 2022.

Re: Little Johnnie’s Hame

Both tunes were composed by James Scott Skinner, who had many brilliant compositions but was not averse to a bit of recycling. So Alex Campbell is basically a version of the strathspey The Laird of Drumblair, in reel time. And Little Johnnie’s Hame seems to combine elements of The Laird of Drumblair with The Spey in Spate.

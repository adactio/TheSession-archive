Frank McCollum’s #2

A reel from the Paddy O’Brien Tune Collection (number 393 in volume one).
I have never heard it anywhere before coming across it in Paddy’s collection.
There are several other Frank McCollum composition posted here (including the well-known
The Home Ruler) but only one other is a reel.

This one is nice on the concertina. The notated cuts are from Paddy’s playing on a B/C box so may need to be adjusted for other instruments. At first, this tune seemed a bit unusual but after I played it a few times
it sunk in. The second part, especially, is quite beautiful. Paddy plays the reel single as I wrote it here.

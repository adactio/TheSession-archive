Two comments

The Old Plum Tree

From Paddy O’Brien’s Tune Collection, volume three, reel #118.
He says that he learned it from fiddler Sean Ryan when they played as a duo.
Paddy plays it a step down, in D dorian, since he plays a B/C accordion, but
I have first given it in E dorian since that suits the C#/D box that I play better,
and, I think, many other instruments as well. (I will make a second post with the notes that Paddy plays). This tune seems to have many familiar phrases, but I couldn’t find any other
reels here that were very close.

I have a great photo of young Paddy with Sean Ryan and Sean’s wife Kathleen, but it isn’t
hosted on a public site, so I can’t link to it here.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on December 1st, 2025.

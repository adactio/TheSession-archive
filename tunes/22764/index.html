Kathy’s

This is one of a number of lovely waltzes, composed by John Redmond. Born in County Wexford, where he learned his music from such great stalwarts as Tom Dunne, John spent most of his adult life in the US as a full time musician. He made two excellent accordion albums there and this is from the second album, ‘Box sets’, which is an album of his compositions. I’ve already uploaded almost all the dance tunes form the album, but sincere thanks to Nóra Byrne Kavanagh who’s been providing me with transcriptions of slow pieces and waltzes by various local composers. This waltz has a tricky rhythm change in the second part!

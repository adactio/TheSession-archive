Four comments

Bill Ellis’

The pandemic left me with some time to comb back through the old print tune collections, which led to me finding this beast of a hornpipe in O’Neill’s. It’s quite melodic but it really is a workout (all the way up to a high E!). This is a bare-bones version without Krassen’s ornamentations, since it doesn’t appear to be on the site yet.

↳ Posted by j_clark on August 5th, 2020.

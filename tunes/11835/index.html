I would point out that Rog is being too modest though. This is actually one of his own tunes. Could you tell us a bit about where the name comes from?

↳ Posted by Alistair Cassidy on March 13th, 2012.

Thanks Alastair for the nice remarks. It’s named after the famous Pinnacle Ridge of Sgurr nan Gillean on Skye - it has a very distinctive profile when seen from the Carbost direction.

The tune is really to commemorate my parents’ ruby wedding anniversary - we bought them a guided day out and they climbed this route, by all accounts taking it in their stride. Pretty good for non-climbers with a combined age of about 130…

Firefly

composed by Cherith Boyle, and noted down from her recording: The Meeting https://thesession.org/recordings/3649

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on April 21st, 2010.

This ‘quiet’, reflective piece (a ‘slow jig’?) is called ‘Firefly’, just. It isn’t ‘The Firefly’ jig: https://thesession.org/tunes/2852

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on April 21st, 2010.

I couldn’t resist putting hints of the ‘left hand’ accompaniement in this transcription (indicated by the slurs). But it is only the plain tip of the iceberg, ie; of her simple but exquisitely crafted counterpoint.

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on April 21st, 2010.

There’s like an echo of ‘Scarborough Fair’ in the closing bars.

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on April 21st, 2010.

I must apologise to those who rely solely on the audio link to learn a tune: I realise only now that thesession’s soundfiles‘ tempo is way too fast for this tune. I usually go to: http://www.concertina.net/tunes_convert.html to ’proof’ my submissions and it has a slower tempo for jigs. Which was still a bit fast for this tune…
I suggest you play it slow, more like a waltz, to bring out its full flavour.

Merry Go Round, X:3

Setting for mouth whistling in the same key played by Sharon Shannon in her album (F Major).

This setting is the same as X:2 but with changes of octave:

- First part one octave higher.
- Second part has only the last two bars raised one octave. The change of octave at the end of this part is not too bad in my opinion.
- Third part one octave higher.

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on April 24th, 2023.

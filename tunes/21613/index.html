Four comments

Winnifred

O‘Neill’s no. 644. He credits it to O’Carolan, on no basis whatsoever according to Fiddler’s Companion, as it’s not in the likely O‘Carolan tunes that O’Sullivan confirmed. It is also known in the P. W. Joyce collection as “Una”, the Irish version of the name Winnifred (or vice versa) , and it sounds fairly similar to the reel “The Song of Oonagh” already here https://thesession.org/tunes/18394 but I didn’t feel confident to say that they are for sure alternate settings of the same tune.

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 18th, 2022.

Winnifred, X:2

Version called “Una” or “Unadh” in P. W. Joyce’s collection of Ancient Irish Music (1873), which he took care to disambiguate from “Oonagh” https://thesession.org/tunes/21612 .

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 18th, 2022.

Winnifred, X:3

Taking Joyce’s version up to B minor in order to play in a set for D whistles.

One comment

Coquett

This tune entitled “Coquett Polka” (SG1a.023) was transcribed in Book 1a (c1840s) of the Grier MSS of Gortletteragh, Co Leitrim. Several other polkas in Book 1a are composed by ”J.F. Douglas", a light classical composer who has not been identified thus far, and this may or may not be one of his compositions.

A long silenced Manx scientist’s work, it now emerges, has established that this early example of a planxty (then referred to as ‘Slanstea’) was handed down by the wandering, armless harper Balbhbodhar to the Welsh scholar Uwch Fud Ap Fab (dates unknown) who committed it to paper, while lying on his back in a dark cellar, as the tradition of the era still dictated. The precious notes fell in a deft ear then but we are still unsure as to how they finally came into the posession of the Manx scholar. Sue (real name witheld) is still cagey about a cross-expertise since she lives in fear of legal actions from the aforementioned estate in a bid to silence her work.

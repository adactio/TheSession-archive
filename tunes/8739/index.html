Seven comments

The Shapron

“Shapron” is an old (18th century) spelling of “Chaperon”.

I believe the tune to be of English origin - possibly from the north-east.

I would classify it as being a “rant” tune, but as thesession doesn’t list “rant” as tune-type, I’ve submitted it as a reel.

It goes very well with that old war horse, “Soldier’s Joy”.

The shapron

glad to see someone is paying attention!
I’ve checked my pencilled notation from William Winter’s original manuscript and there is indeed an anacrusis ( F# E ) to the value of a crotchet!
When I researched the Winter manuscript , I could find no tune of this name in the V Williams library at C# house-- if you know any other sources - please post the info !

Matt Cranitch’s note on this set of polkas, from this lovely CD:

“As the river Blackwater flows from its origin in Sliabh Luachra towards the sea it passes close to where my parents were born and reared - my mother Kathleen Linehan in Coolagown and my father Micheál Cranitch in Rathcormac, both in Co. Cork. From them I received my love for the music that I play, and so I would like to dedicate thse new polkas to them.” ~ Matt

Named in honour of Matt’s mother, the second in this set of three, “Kathleen’s Polka”, can be found here:
https://thesession.org/tunes/11029

All Hours

This is a composition of Cian O‘Grady of the emerging young County Wexford trad/fusion group Tulua. Cian has composed some of the material that the group are performing at their shows and concerts. You can hear Tulua playing this for the U.S. PBS TV show, ’Ireland with Michael’ on their website here:
https://tuluamusic.com/
Scroll down the page below their EP tracks and the clip, recorded at Waterford Treasures is just below that.
Cian’s compositions are copyrighted, so consent would be required to record or republish them.

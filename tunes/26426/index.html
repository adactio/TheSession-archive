New Potatoes

This reel is #1505 in O’Neil’s Music of Ireland where it is named The New Potatoes and
noted as contributed by O’Reilly.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on March 20th, 2026.

New Potatoes, X:2

Paddy (Offaly) O’Brien plays this version in his Tune Collection at
volume one, reel #48. He calls it The Cavan Reel and writes
"Fiddler Sean Maguire’s father Johnny was a delightful whistle player.
He had a large stock of rare tunes from the Co. Cavan/Co. Monaghan area.
This is one he named for his home county."

In CRE4 at #132 there is a similar reel https://thesession.org/tunes/10823 that
Breathnach notes “agus Handsome Sally (Johnnie Maguire)”
So, the tunes are related, but they seem different to me.

Anyway, I find Paddy’s version to be superior to the version from O’Neil’s whether we
credit the improvement to Paddy or to Johnny Maguire.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on March 20th, 2026.

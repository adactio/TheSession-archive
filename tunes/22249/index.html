Dod’s Punk Rock Tartan Crews

a killer Ross Ainslie tune. Just learned it off his new album with Brigdhe Chaimbeul: https://brighdechaimbeul.bandcamp.com/track/dods

They play it on C pipes so it’s actually in Cmix, but that wasn’t an option for a key signature in the wysiwyg. I learned it on C whistle so I’m thinking of it as Dmix when I play.

The whole B is played in harmony so it’s possible I’m hearing the harmony as the melody in parts.

↳ Posted by Gather on September 13th, 2022.

Dod’s Tartan Punk Rock Trews, X:2

As published in A Few Tunes (available from all good Gordon Duncan Memorial Trusts).

Ross adds more than a few variations on this in performance. The ~a2s are written as two quavers [well, semiquavers, since the book gives it in 2/4 and calls it a hornpipe] but I’ve taken the liberty of changing them to turns here.

Re: Dod’s Tartan Punk Rock Trews

I wonder is this tune name a reference to Dod Copland (d. 29 July 2022) of Aberdeen?
He was a fixture of Aberdeen’s punk scene for 40 years with his pipes and tartan trews, so Ross would’ve seen him around.

↳ Posted by Bren on September 14th, 2022.

Re: Dod’s Tartan Punk Rock Trews

You are correct Bren: “A tune for the ever entertaining Dod Copeland” is what Ross says about the tune in Ross Ainslie Handmade Tunes (pub. 2011), where it is listed as a march.

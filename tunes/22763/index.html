The Partners

This is a tune written by the late Orrin Star, a musician from New York and later in life the greater Washington DC area. There is a great duet with Keith Carr, a Celtic musician in the DC area as well (half of the duo Lilt). It starts in Dm, for the A part, then to Dmaj for the B part. I have captured the gist of Orrin’s version.

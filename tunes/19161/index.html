Two comments

Wigwam Polka

From the playing of Velocipede.

Composed by Andy De Jarlis, around the early 1960’s.

The A Part is reminiscent of a few other Quebecois Reels in Bminor, such as:
https://thesession.org/tunes/9555
https://thesession.org/tunes/17564
https://thesession.org/tunes/18831

The B Part is quite similar to the B Part of The Sherbrooke Reel (aka, Reel De Montréal):
https://thesession.org/tunes/1346

Jon Harkness:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pNN7VZV5GAU

Patti Kusturok & Jeremy Rusu, starting at about 37s in:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gAU_AeWBqLQ

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on April 19th, 2020.

The Chattering Magpie, X:5

Exact Copy and paste version 4 and only updating the pickup notes to play an “A” for a smoother transition coming from The Sunny banks reel. The pickup notes of version 4 are “dc” which are fine for the reel played alone but misleading in a set.

Useless version in a way except that with this setting it’s easier to read / play when following the notes of the two reels on a sheet of paper when moving from The Sunny Banks into the Chattering Magpie.

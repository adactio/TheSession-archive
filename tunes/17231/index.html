Four comments

For Sara

A gentle waltz by Antti Järvelä, played with swung quavers, with lots of sliding around on your instrument (sorry accordion players). Sara is the new baby of one of Antti’s friends, and this piece was written for her upcoming christening. As part of the gift, the parents have also been given the chance to name the tune, so the title may yet change.

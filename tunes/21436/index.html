The Snowy Hills Of Caherea

A lovely 3 parts jig composed by concertina player Padraig Rynne. Named after the place where he lives, in county Clare. Features on his new album realised in January 2022, it can be listened there : https://padraigrynne.bandcamp.com/album/begin-with-the-end-in-mind

Tune For Tom

This tune appears on debut CD by Therese McInerney “Down the Strand”.
It can be heard as the introduction theme on FleadhFest an Chláir Episode 4 at https://youtu.be/vR25IDYuISc?t=59 ( from 00:59)
Therese has fine variations on the second part of the second time as follows :
cea f3 | eAc BAF | cBA eAe | fec B2A |
cea f3 | eAc BAA | EAB cBA | EAG AB=c |
cea fga | bge dcB | cea f3 | eAc B2A |
cea fdB | ecA dcB | A3 BAF | ECB, A,3 ||

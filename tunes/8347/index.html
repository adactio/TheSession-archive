“The Crossroads Fling”

This is from the playing of Jackie Daly.

From the notes to “Jackie Daly: Domhnach is Dálah / Many’s A Wild Night” ~

‘The Crossroads Fling’ is a slide associated with the playing and teaching of the renowned fiddle master, Pádraig O’Keeffe.

↳ Posted by ceolachan on March 13th, 2008.

Already listed!

This is the same tune as #5803 Kiely’s Slide. The tune appears under that name in David Taylor’s “Music of Ireland: A Mighty Tune” (Mallinson, 1995), though with parts reversed.

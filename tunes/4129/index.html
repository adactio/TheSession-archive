Two years later…

Two years later, here’s the story. Gary calls the tune “Lose the Head.” It’s the 3rd. tune in the1st. medley on his first album, “Lose the Head.”
It is also the 3rd. tune in a medley played by the great Breton flautist, Michel Bonamy, on a mid-80s Ceili Bandits tape. The only name I have for the medly is “Tricia Shannon’s/Jerry Doyle’s.” I don’t know to which tunes this refers, but I assume it is to the first two in the medley, since Michel would know that the 3rd is probably Gary Shannon’s composition.

↳ Posted by David Levine on August 29th, 2006.

Commonalty

I’m pretty sure this is a track on the eponymous Commonalty recording featuring Joanne and Paul McSherry, Davy Maguire and Milo Crossan. It came out in 1989.
Davy, the flute player, makes frequent trips to Brittany so maybe he got it from Michel or vice versa.

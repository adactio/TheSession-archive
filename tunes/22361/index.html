Three comments

Sonny Beeman’s Cows

Sonny is a neighbor out here in the boonies and I jokingly suggested the title for a tune. My friend, Fred, challenged me to write such a tune, so here is my submission. I’ve never written a tune before and was surprised at how one evening, it just came into my mind - in 15 minutes it was done. I have no idea where it came from.

↳ Posted by dfost on October 15th, 2022.

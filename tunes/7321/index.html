X:2 Leitrim Reel

From a homemade acetate recording, made in NYC in the 1950s. This was from a batch of such discs, cut by anonymous musicians from Sligo and Leitrim, judging by their style and repertoire. Some discs did ID who was playing, including Paddy Killoran and Jim MacGowan.

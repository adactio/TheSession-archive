Re: The Waltz Of Happiness (Válsa An tSonais)

ceili, I reinstated Cliff Hamilton as the composer of this tune.
Perhaps someone in Canada or Jackie Daly himself could tell us more about the man; but there seems to be an agreement he -not Jackie Daly- composed this vintage waltz as listed by
https://www.irishtune.info/tune/2034/
and already quoted (above) by gian marco 21 years ago.
It has many hallmarks of an early 20th waltz, with jazzy elements to it, or at least a marked tonal stucture (as opposed to modal), a style favoured by Daly, alright.

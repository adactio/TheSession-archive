↳ Posted by Dave_Ball on June 18th, 2019.

Re: The Kenmare Golf Disaster

The tune is named after a mishap Jason & a few of his buddies had one year when they were visiting Ireland.

While golfing in Kenmare, they got part way down the coarse, only to find out they were going to wrong direction the while time! Unpleasant surprise to come across another group of golfers, walking towards them (from in front on them).

Hence, ‘The Kenmare Golf Disaster’.

(I had messaged Dave about this privately a few years ago).

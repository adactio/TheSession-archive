Re: Lady Slipper

What an exceedingly lovely tune! … too bad for me that it’s is not very flute/whistle friendly; however, after messing around with it for a while, I think transposing it would remove some of that loveliness. It’s wonderful just the way it is. Natlie’s recording of this tune is great, and the slip jig she follows it up with (Woodland Star) is a cracking one, too (though in B major, I think - yikes).

↳ Posted by nhflute on October 6th, 2023.

Lady Slipper, X:2

I got this tune from Natalie at a session and wanted to learn it. I transposed it to G for the flute and whistle players out there. I would probably play it on a C whistle or flute with her so it could stay in the original key, which is so lovely and gentle.

Re: Mrs. McPherson Of Gibton

“Mrs McPherson of Gibston” (note spelling) was composed by William Marshall and published in his 1822 collection. Mrs MacPherson was the wife of the Duke of Gordon’s factor who lived at Gibston Farm, just outside Huntly in Aberdeenshire. Captain Charles Gordon, an illegitimate son of the Marquis of Huntly, married Mrs McPherson’s daughter Elizabeth.

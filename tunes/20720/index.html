Pete’s Breakdown

Canadian tune composed by Émilien “Pete” Couture.

I tried to transcribe it as close as possible to the version played by La Bottine Souriante.

This is the track of the album En Spectacle, the tune starts at 3:44:

https://youtu.be/JgxTIhoi_Hk

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on April 30th, 2021.

Pete’s Breakdown, X:2

Very similar to the other setting, but with the parts reversed (as played by many), and a few adjustments to the suggested accompaniment.

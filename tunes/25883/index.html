Timmy’s Polka #1

I learned this tune from Timmy ‘the Brit’ McCarthy at a set dance workshop in Portland OR, some 20 years back. It was one of two that he’d composed, which were just called polka. I subsequently found them on a self published CD called …Movin‘ on!! by Timmy ’the Brit’ McCarthy & Friends of Sliabh Luachra, where the tunes are referred to as Timmy’s Polkas.

Priddy Polka, X:2 (Timmy’s #1)

Thank for sharing this little gem penned by Timmy ‘The Brit’ McCarthy. Timmy named it Priddy Polka on the album recorded with his son Tony McCarthy, From Cork to Confort Berhet. The CD’s sleeve notes confirm Timmy wrote this one but say he learned the second tune in the set from Peter Sorenson.

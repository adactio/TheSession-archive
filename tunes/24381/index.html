James Scott Skinner’s Welcome To Inverness

This Alexander Grant (1856-1942) tune is in Paul Stewart Cranford (The Cape Breton Highland Collection), 2015; p. 4.
It was featured on Buddy MacMaster’s Glencoe Hall album and is on Teannaich Ceilidh Band’s album Energised album as the third tune on the track “Inverness Sonic Extensions”.

↳ Posted by Jonathan Kemp on June 16th, 2024.

Re: James Scott Skinner’s Welcome To Inverness

Troy MacGillivray plays five parts on “Tune Poets”, although the original is four, as given here.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on June 16th, 2024.

Re: James Scott Skinner’s Welcome To Inverness

Dara Smith-MacDonald also plays the fifth part, which is obviously meant for fiddle.
You can see dots for it here:
https://www.cranfordpub.com/tunes/Scottish/JScottSkinnersWelcome.htm
Note that where Paul Cranford has transcribed groups of four semi-quavers (or sixteenths) the first three should actually be played as a triplet so that the fourth is a bit longer than the others (otherwise the rhythmic effect is lost).

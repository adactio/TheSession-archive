An interesting alternative…

It’s an exciting alternative title: “MacDonald Of Keppach’s Lamentation Being Murdered By His Two Cusin German’s” - is it that he was murdered (as the story given on tunearch would suggest) or that the piece of music is being played very badly?

↳ Posted by Dr. Tøm on March 31st, 2013.

This will likely be concerned with Alexander MacDonald of Keppoch, who was murdered along with his brother Ranald in 1663. Iain Lom (Baldy John - he apparently never had any hair on his face), the bard of Keppoch, went on a seemingly single handed campaign to bring the muderers to justice (cousins who had been running things when the two victims were being schooled abroad). Baldy John apparently wrote verse to lament the murders.
If you are passing “Toban nan Ceann” you can spare a thought for those murdering clanspeople:

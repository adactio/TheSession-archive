Three comments

Spinning Jenny

This tune is on a track of the same name in “Lost in Fishponds” by The Famous Five (now Spiro).
It is another three-two hornpipe from Thomas Marsden’s collection of “Original Lancashire Hornpipes, Old and New”, Playford 1705.
This tune, along with other three-twos viz. Mr Keys, Mr Preston’s Hornpipe and Heartease, all of which I have posted here in the last few days, can also be found in John Offord’s “John of the Green, The Cheshire Way” (2008).

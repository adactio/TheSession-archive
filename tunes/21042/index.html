↳ Posted by May I on September 12th, 2021.

Frankie Drain’s, X:2

This setting is from Battlefield Band’s recording of the tune.
Gerard Francis Drain (Frankie) RIP was a banjo player from Falls Road, Belfast, who I had the great fortune to know from the late 70s. Frankie sadly passed away at the end of last year and will be sorely missed by family, friends and musicians.
I’ll put more biographical details in the Discussions section.
Alastair White was inspired to write this tune after spending a wild weekend in Belfast in 2009 with Aaron and Nathon Jones, together with their Uncle - Frankie Drain.

↳ Posted by The Archivist on January 20th, 2026.

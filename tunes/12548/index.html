The Promotion, X:4

This is an arrangement based on the second tune in the Thursday Night at the Caley set by Bruce MacGregor of Blazin’ Fiddles.

Transposed it to the key of G and adjusted the turn arounds so flutes wouldn’t have to jump octaves suddenly.

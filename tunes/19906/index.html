Dickie Gossip

This is the reel called Dickie Gossip as played by John McManus on the CD Hidden Fermanagh Vol. 1. It’s not the usual Dickie Gossip’s. John learned this tune from his Uncle Hugh and he thought it was of Scottish origin (as with a lot of the tunes in the Fermanagh Gunn ms 1865). John McManus was a descendent of John Gunn who compiled the Gunn ms.
Here’s a live recording of it:
https://www.itma.ie/digital-library/sound/dickie-gossip-mcconnell

Re: Dickie Gossip

A setting of this tune - as “Dicky Gossip” was published by Nathaniel Gow in Edinburgh about 1810, but there is no indication of where he obtained it. Dicky Gossip was a character in a popular, ribald play from 1793 called My Grandmother.

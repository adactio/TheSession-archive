Andrew’s Winning Smle

Wonderful tube composed by Josephine Marsh for her son. for her album, Music in the Frame
The tune really suits the banjo, and indeed Josephine is accompanied by the great banjo player, Angelina Carberry, in a setfollowing another of her compositions, The Smashing Reel. Her son Andrew is also a fine banjo player…

Three comments

Glor Na NAingeal

composed by Michael Rooney.

transcribed from Michael Rooney & June McCormack album “Land’s End”

↳ Posted by Yooval on December 10th, 2016.

Glór na nAingeal, X:2

This setting in G major is more flute-friendly. In the sleeve notes it states that June McCormack plays a Patrick Olwell flute in the key of C on certain tracks and it may well be that that is what she is playing here, using ‘G’ fingering but sounding as ‘F’. The title should be written with a small ‘n’ before ‘Aingeal’.

↳ Posted by Andrew Newland on October 11th, 2021.

Re: Glór na nAingeal

Composed by Michael Rooney for his marriage to June McCormack in July 2004. “The piece is dedicated to the memory of Deirdre, Michael’s sister who died at the end of May of that year. Deirdre had named her house ‘Glór na nAingeal’ (Voice of the Angels)” (from the sleeve notes of the album).

↳ Posted by Andrew Newland on October 12th, 2021.

Cead Moladh Don Mhaighdean Bheannaithe

This is a slow air played by Paul Bradley on the album “Atlantic Roar” the name translates as “A Hundred Praises to the Blessed Virgin”. I haven’t got the timing right, but the notes are all there. Listen to the original and I’ll try to update the sheet music

↳ Posted by Harold93 on August 31st, 2025.

Cead Moladh Don Mhaighdean Bheannaithe, X:2

I first heard this hymn air played by Michael Tubridy on his wonderful 1978 recording The Eagle’s Whistle under the title “Cead Moladh le Muire Beannaithe.” X:2 above is a setting from the playing of Mary Louise O’Donnell:

https://youtu.be/PDHsE4PmfJw?si=u7jWVxvsmwaoSN4P&t=35

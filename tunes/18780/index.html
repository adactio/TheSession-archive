The Polar Bear Jig

From the playing of, and composed by, Reg Bouvette.

He ends on an A Part (Gmaj), with a four bar tag. The last two bars of the current A Part, are replaced with something like:
[2 d^cd bfa | g3 g2f | gag fgf | efe d2B | cAF DEF | G6 |]

Reg wrote the tune after a visit to Yellowknife, Canada, where he saw some polar bears.

The Polar Bear, X:2

From the playing of JJ Guy.

I’ve replaced what he plays in the third bar of the A Part (“cce BBd”) with what Reg, Patti, and Isaac all seem to play (“cee Bdd”), as it sounds quite a bit better to my ears.

Another option for this phrase is “cec BdB”, which sounds alright, and Isaac may have thrown this in once or twice as a variation. Will double check that later.

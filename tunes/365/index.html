The Cat Rambles to the Child’s Saucepan

With the thread going now on slides, and all the talk and tunes about cats and mice, this tune fits right in!

I’ve heard this recorded by the Chieftains and Cherish the Ladies, both featuring whistles in higher registers. I pulled this off the “Best of ” Cherish the Ladies compilation and transposed it down to D to avoid any jumps out of first position. We typically play this in a set after Denis Murphy’s slide.

↳ Posted by Will Harmon on November 12th, 2001.

The Cat Rambles To The Child’s Saucepan, X:3

↳ Posted by AdamM on September 26th, 2023.

The Cat Rambles To The Child’s Saucepan, X:4

X:3 is from the playing of Julia Clifford and Billy Clifford, from the record “Ceol as Sliabh Luachra”, whereas X:4 is from the playing of Johnny Cronin on the Cronin & Burke album. Both feature flipped order of the sections as presented in the first two settings.

↳ Posted by AdamM on September 26th, 2023.

Re: The Cat Rambles To The Child’s Saucepan

I’ve often wondered about the origin of the title of this one. I picture a session in a bar in Kerry with an eager tourist tune-collector with his notebook sitting alongside the local fiddler, constantly asking ‘What’s that one called?’, ‘What’s the name of that tune’, and so on. Finally the irritated and exasperated player comes out with “Oh FFS, it’s . . . The Cat Rambles to the Child’s Saucepan”.

But maybe there’s another story behind the tune.

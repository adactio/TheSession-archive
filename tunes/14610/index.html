Re: In Her Element

This tune was in fact written by Jeremy Kittel. I’ve heard it referred to as a strathspey, but then again am not totally sure what the technical difference is.

↳ Posted by Rebecca S. on November 6th, 2023.

Re: In Her Element

It sounds like a slow strathspey to me. Slow strathspeys sometimes get accompanied in a less insistent way than for an up tempo strathspey in that the four to the bar isn’t emphasised as much, giving it more of a two-feel or march like feel.
But the structure of eight-bar parts (really repeated four-bar parts with second time endings) is typical of a strathspey - like the old (single) reels, from which strathspeys developed.

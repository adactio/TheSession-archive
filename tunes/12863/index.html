McKenna Country, X:2

I had a very difficult time finding this tune because it was transcribed incorrectly in D Major instead of D Mixolydian, so I created a D Mix transcription, correcting an error in the third line, tossing in a couple of ornaments and making a few cosmetic changes.

A very nice video of Cara Murphy playing it on accordion was posted to the Tulla Comhaltas Facebook page yesterday (September 18, 2020).

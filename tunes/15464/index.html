Alec Menzies

Taken from Brid Harper’s amazing album this is a “Clog”. She got it from Jerry Holland and its by Charles Duff of Dundee (1760-1822).

↳ Posted by Donough on July 8th, 2016.

Re: Alec Menzies

It’s a terrific tune! In both Charles Duff’s Collection (1792) and on Jerry Holland’s album A Session with Jerry Holland (1990), it’s played in the key of G. Also, in the Duff collection it’s described as a hornpipe. I haven’t heard Brid Harper’s version, but Jerry Holland’s is fantastic. Thank you, Donough.

Alex Menzie’s, X:4

Composed by Charles Duff of Dundee as “Mr Alexr Menzies’s Hornpipe” and published in his 1792 collection. In Jerry Holland’s 1988 book, he describes the tune as a “clog.”

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on December 7th, 2025.

Re: Alec Menzies

In Cape Breton, a “clog” is a swung hornpipe. Otherwise, I think hornpipes, like Fisher’s, are played straight.

Three comments

Leaving Lough Swilly

I’ve added a few of Mick Denieffe’s tunes here. This is another which I think deserves some attention. The tune has - to my ear - a very pleasing blend of “the continent” and old-time about it. To hear the man himself play the tune, with guitar backing by Martin Comiskey, see the YouTube below:

https://youtu.be/8T_4-gHvTUw

↳ Posted by Aidan Crossey on February 24th, 2026.

Leaving Lough Swilly, X:2

This is a tweaked version of Mick’s original, where I have changed some of the 9- and 10-beat notes which work very effectively on accordion to picked phrases which are more mandolin-friendly. I have also simplified a bar which occurs in both parts and which Mick composed as BA GF E-E to a more straightforward BA G2 E2. And I’ve set the tune out with 8 bars per line rather than 4 bars per line (it prints out on one page via Easy ABC with 8 bars per line).

↳ Posted by Aidan Crossey on February 24th, 2026.

The Old Red Lion, X:2

Lovely tune, found this when looking if anyone was playing Irish trad on a melodica. I’m a piano accordion player and find this fun to have lying around to just pick up and knock out a few tunes without getting the accordion out. :) I just “cleaned” this version up a little bit in terms of how it is broken in to bars and put in repeats so it looks like most other tunes do instead of just a long rambling bit of playing.

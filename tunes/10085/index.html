Six comments

This is a really sweet waltz, composed by Radvan Markus & Petr Košumberský, the flute player and guitarist of the Czech band Dún an Doras. Transcription is based on their playing on their *Sweet & Sour* album. Some really great playing on that disk!

From the band website:
“A Leaning Tower, although not in Pisa, but in Prachovské skály, a region in Eastern Bohemia known for its picturesque sandstone formations.”

For more on the band, visit their website
http://www.dunandoras.cz/

Re: New Island

The “dots” I originally posted are more or less the same as published in Blair’s book although that’s not where I learned it. It was for a fiddle concert but they may well have sourced it from from Blair’s book. I don’t know.

It’s quite tricky transcribing a tune which has lots of ties and slurs here and the midi often produces strange results. I’ll give it another look and try to tidy up a few things.

As for repeating line one, I think it’s a matter of taste re notation and I usually prefer to write it in full (as had been done in the original) than having whole lines of different “second time” repeats….. A couple of bars are OK.
That’s just me though. :-)

Having said all that, your alternative setting is very welcome.

One For Grandad O’Dowd

An air (or slow reel) written for my Grandad - Mícheál O’Dowd.

Performed by CrossHarbour at Bath Folk Festival in August 2014: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8LURu4xDok (The Surprise Package)

Performed by Na Rógairí at Siansa Gael Linn in April 2015

The Mohill Reel

At the time of writing, a search under this name won’t find you another transcription, but I have found one as Gan Ainm @ http://www.irishtunes.net/reels/sionatune.html. I got this tune from some friends at my local session, and Mohill was the title they gave me. This 3-part tune has echoes of The New Policeman/Belles Of Tipperary https://thesession.org/tunes/769 and also The Steeplechase/Carrigaline/Hard Road To Travel https://thesession.org/tunes/2281 and https://thesession.org/tunes/1461.

Re: The Mohill

[The Mohill] “…was learned from Co Leitrim fiddle and flute duo, Peter Fitzpatrick and Michael McNamara. Bernie McKiernan, a Leitrim fiddle player returned from the States with this reel, where a two-part version is known as ”Billy in the Lowground“. A Scottish air ”The Braes of Auchentyre“ is also closely related.”
- sleeve notes from Cosa Gan Bhroga by Gerry O’Connor, Eithne Ní Uallacháin and Dessie Wilkinson (1987).

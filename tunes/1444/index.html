Gian-Marco,
are you sure about the octaves you have written down in the abc of your tune - IMHO in the second part the tune sounds nicer with some of the octaves reversed….
I guess the tune title comes from waiting in a traffic jam on the autostrada to savona?

↳ Posted by swisspiper on February 16th, 2003.

Urs,
indeed sometimes i play the first two triplets of the 2nd and 6th bar of the second part in the high octave.
I live in Genova, and the rest of the Band is from Savona.
I go weekly to practise with the Band, by car or by train.
I don’t remember when i wrote this tune, but probably you are not far from the truth.

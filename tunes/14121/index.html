Three comments

Finbarr Dwyer’s Teelin Reel

Fantastically beautiful tune, but not for the faint-hearted, given the amount of sharps involved. Other than the name, this reel in Bmix bears no relation, I don’t think, to the highlands etc. already in the database.

It’s on the Star of Ireland LP, though it’s not among those on it that are specifically mentioned in the notes as being Finbarr’s own compositions.

↳ Posted by coppers and brass on January 15th, 2015.

Julia Delaney

This always struck me as a version of Julia Delaney. The B-part end phrase which goes sharp is very unusual and I’ve often wondered if this was Finbarr’s own doing or if it was based on a version heard by some musician in Teelin. He did spent a lot of time up in that neck of the woods.

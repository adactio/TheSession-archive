Two comments

Now I Am Tired And Wish I Was At Home

I was leafing through the Stanford/Petric Collection of Irish Music, and found this an irresistible title.

Can’t wait to put in set lists with other pithy titles like When Sick Is It Tea That You Want and I Buried My Wife And Danced On Top Of Her, and I relate to it more than to either of those. Except that the pandemic means that I’m already at home and I’m still tired and wish I was at home.

The Petrie Collection of the Ancient Music of Ireland (1905), no. 381.

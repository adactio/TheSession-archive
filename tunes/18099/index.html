Five comments

The Cribbies

From the albums/playing of Graham Wells and Dan Possumato.

Composed by fiddler Colin Carrigan. The tune is named after an area of coastal meadow near Tor’s Cove, Newfoundland. The Cribbies used to be its’ own community.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on July 4th, 2019.

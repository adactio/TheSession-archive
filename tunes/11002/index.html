The Road to Ruin

This tune was written by Dale Russ and played on his album with Todd Denman Reeds and Rosin.
I found the abc on the chiffandfipple.com website submitted by an uillean pipe player named Bill Reeder. Thanks Bill.

I like the way the tune starts with a dark tone to it and then lightens up.

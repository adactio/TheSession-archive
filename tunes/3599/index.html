Re: Highway To Kilkenny

Sorry for being trifling and possibly pedantic here, but I tried playing it a few times and something was wrong. I don,t know anything about music, but I do know when it doesn,t sound right. Take the first setting of the tune, in the end of the second part, right after it displays the number “2”. The next 3 note triplet should be an F sharp, going up to an A, and then another F sharp, then the next triplet should be a long G dropping to an E, then exactly as shown on the transcript :) Otherwise a brilliant tune, my favourite being James Kelly, Paddy o,Brien, and Daithi Sproule,s version.

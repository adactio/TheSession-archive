Sreang-bogha

This tune is a minor version of The Maid of the House a.k.a. Swing Swang
https://thesession.org/tunes/4729

Since the feeling -or ‘ethos’- of this new arrangement is rather different, I choose to submit it as a different tune and in so doing, give it the name “Sreang-bogha” which can both mean a ‘bow string’ (an sreangán bogha) or ‘restharrow’ (also known as fréamhacha tairne in Irish).

Restharrow is a beautiful, small, spiny plant in the shamrock/pea/vetch family.
It is quite fragrant but the balmy scent comes from the leaves, not from the pink flowers.
The unusual names are explained here:
http://www.wildflowersofireland.net/plant_detail.php?id_flower=223&wildflower=Restharrow,%20Common
It is known in Ireland but it is more common on the continent, especially in the French Pyrenees where I am staying at the moment.
I choose the name for its poetic association and symbolic and sympathic powers for I would like to dedicate this tune (my own careful arrangement of https://thesession.org/tunes/4729, as I have already mentionned) to my friend Gilles Duval, a beautiful person and long-time Irish fiddler, now living in the South of France and who is unfortunately unwell today, with harrowing health problems.
If you know him, please send your regards. If you don’t have his address, get in touch with me and I will put you through.

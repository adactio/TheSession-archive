Four comments

Martin Mulvihill’s

Taken from Mary Rafferty’s ‘Hand-me-downs’. A leisurely jig.

↳ Posted by gam on September 14th, 2009.

Martin Mulvihill’s/The Ballyloughlin

Composed by Martin Mulvihill in 1972 and named by him “The Ballyloughlin Jig”

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on June 16th, 2013.

The Ballygoughlin

Actually it was called “The Ballygoughlin” on Martin Mulvihill’s 1978 LP ‘Traditional Fiddling from Limerick’, after the village in West Limerick where he was born in 1923.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on November 30th, 2013.

The Ballygoughlin Jig

I have added Martin Mulvihill’s setting of the tune as it differs enough from Mary Rafferty’s, not least its key.

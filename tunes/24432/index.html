Re: An Slide L’Arlesienne

Yes, thanks Brad. The farandole folk dance can be set to 12/8 time as the phrases repeat every 12 beats. Jean-Baptiste Lully set the tune in 4/4 time for martial music (De Turenne). Georges Bizet used the same tune in his Suite as a farandole attributed to the Arles region of France. Here the tune is reset as a dance in 12/8 but retains the most familiar title à la Bizet.

↳ Posted by Shawn Cassady on January 20th, 2025.

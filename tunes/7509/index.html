Geordie McCrae’s Fancy

Leslie was sitting in a bar playing this tune
when an old friend came in the door and said
‘’Is that your tune Leslie‘’ Leslie said ‘’It is sir‘’. ‘’Best thing you ever wrote‘’ the friend said.
So Leslie named this tune after him, Geordie McCrae.

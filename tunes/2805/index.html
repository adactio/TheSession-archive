“The Lane to the Glen” ~ more from frisbee’s rescued duplication

Key signature: D Dorian
Submitted on April 7th 2008 by frisbee.

Ed Reavy tune ~ this is a great tune by Ed Reavy which was recently recorded by Martin Hayes and Dennis Cahill. The transcription is taken directly from the book ‘The Collected Compositions of Ed Reavy’. Martin and Dennis play the tune in Bb Dorian. ~ this is a note for note transcription of the tune in Reavy’s book.

The Road To The Glen, X:4

Basic transcription in D minor from the playing of Martin Hayes in Welcome Here Again by Martin Hayes & Dennis Cahill (https://thesession.org/recordings/2851). Track availble here: https://youtu.be/p2gQO5y5ZAg

Martin and Dennis play this reel in Bb minor, followed by a Paddy Fahey’s reel (https://thesession.org/tunes/3346) played in F major. I play the set on my flute two tones up (D minor and A major).

Youghal Quay

Just in case anyone was wondering…this town in Ireland is pronounced “yawl”, is located in Co. Cork, and Sir Walter Raliegh was once its mayor.

↳ Posted by shaskeen on March 13th, 2004.

This is a Paddy O’Brien composition, recorded by him with Seamus Connolly on their “Banks of the Shannon” EP. It is not the same tune as “Miss Johnson’s/Johnston’s/Johnstone’s.”

This reel was a favorite of the late fiddler Johnny Cronin, who played it in virtually every session in the later years of his too-short life.

↳ Posted by blarneystar on March 15th, 2004.

This tune appears on Dervish’s At The End Of The Day, but under the title Miss Johnstone’s, which, as blarneystar says, is a different but similar tune https://thesession.org/tunes/2411. On the album, it is preceded by an adaptation of this tune in mazurka time https://thesession.org/tunes/5794.

Re: Youghal Quay

Just to make it clear, this is not a Paddy O’Brien composition.

In this link you will see all the ABCs of all the tunes in the collection The Definitive Collection of the Music of Paddy O’Brien 1922-1991
(ed.Eileen O’Brien Minogue, 2009)

http://www.capeirish.com/webabc/working/source.folders/pobr/pobr_ABC.txt

For the compositions you will see:

C:© Paddy O’Brien

But for the tunes that are not compositions and are arrangements of Paddy O’Brien you won’t see that. You will see this instead:

H:arr. Paddy O’Brien

And this is what happens with this tune.

Youghal Quay, X:3

This was my take on the tune listed on the Banks of the Shannon EP of Paddy O’Brien and Séamus Connolly released in the 70s. It was coupled with the excellent Rambling Sailor. This set (and EP) were re-released (with more tracks) on CD https://thesession.org/recordings/970. Its similarity to Miss Johnson (https://thesession.org/tunes/2411) has always made it difficult for me to properly learn, but I’m sure it would be worth the effort!

Lucifer Falls

A reel that I made several years ago. The C naturals in the first part give it
a somewhat demonic flavor, so my wife suggested the name Lucifer Falls
after a local landmark in Treman state park here in Ithaca.

I can justify its inclusion here because
a) my friends actually play it at our session
b) I got a request for the dots on Enda Scahill’s banjo discord group
c) I’ve posted 97 tune settings of trad tunes

I mostly play it on a C#/D box, but it also sounds good on tenor banjo, and my
friends play it on fiddle. (It’s probably not the best pipes, whistle or flute tune?)
Please comment if you like (dislike).

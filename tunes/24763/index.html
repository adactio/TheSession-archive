April The 3rd

A Dónal Lunny composition.
And, famously, the opening track to the CD box “Bringing It All Back Home” published in 1991 as a kind of keepsake for the acclaimed TV series.

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on September 11th, 2024.

Re: April The 3rd

A nifty tune.
It is self-consciously off-beat (where the beat seems to be playfully dragging behind the melody by a quaver to create a tension) ; mostly it plays on our expectation of where the long notes (the crochets) should fall/are placed in a slip jig.
These expectations are properly threshed!
This rockyness gives the tune the feel of a march rather than a jig. A powerfully taut tune, heard once, never forgotten.

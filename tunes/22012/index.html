Johnstown Castle

The second of a set of reels composed by accordionist Paddy Hayes from Piercestown in County Wexford and featuring on his second album, ‘The Shores of Lough Melvin’. Johnstown castle is large mansion and estate in the Piercestown area where Paddy Hayes grew up. An Foras Taluntais, the Irish Agricultural Institute, are based at the castle and Paddy’s brother Tommy worked there for many years. Tommy was, like Paddy, an excellent accordionist with a razor sharp wit.

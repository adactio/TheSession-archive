Gan Ainm

An interesting untitled jig from an old tape of the County Wexford fiddle player Jim Smith (RIP). Jim was from the Clongeen/Foulksmills area and had a wealth of songs, many of which were featured in Paddy Berry’s books of Wexford ballads. The tune has echoes of other jigs such as The Humors of Cappa etc. The tape also features Jim playing a version of the reel recorded by the harmonica-playing Murphy’s, the Raheen. Many people think that Jim Smith may have been the composer of that tune.

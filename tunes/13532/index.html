Fifteen comments

February Reel

I first heard this tune on the Magic Square “Traditional Irish Music from Boston” - as played by Dave Cory. I believe it was written by a wonderful fiddle player named Dale Russ although I don’t know the dates. I’ve only heard the tune played on the banjo.

↳ Posted by RTP on April 6th, 2014.

The tune was indeed written by Dale Russ and is the first of a pair of the February Reels. Dale’s composition is slightly different from Dave’s interpretation. I’ll leave it up to Dale to post his versions. I don’t think he wanted anyone to post them on here.

↳ Posted by Shelley on May 6th, 2015.

February Reel #1, X:2

One of a pair of striking reels in F# minor (key signature of A) by Dale Russ. Transcendant and evocative of the winter blues, played together as they are on the disc Jody’s heaven (1996) . Play them today to dispel the virus at the door.

↳ Posted by Steve Moore on April 2nd, 2020.

Re: February

Thanks, Steve, for your transcriptions - this one and Feb #2. I’m glad you are enjoying them. I originally recorded them on a solo recording from 1995, inspiringly titled “Irish Fiddle”, backed by guitarist Mike Saunders. It was published by Randal Bays’ Foxglove Records but is long out of print. Best wishes -

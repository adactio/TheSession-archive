The Lady on the Island

I find all the versions of the tune available on the net a little bit awkward, so I made up my own based on Mick O‘Brien and Kevin O’Reilly’s lovely whistle and fiddle duet playing of it.

K: Dmaj
dB|:BAFB AFEF|D2FA BAdB|BAFB ~A3B|1 d2fd efdB:|2 d2fd e2(3ABc||
d2fd efge|afdf edBc|d2fd efge|afdf e2(3ABc|
d2fd efge|afdf edBc|defg afbf|afdf efdB||

I think it’s much neater and very close to the versions we come across in the real sessions. It’s one of the great pieces for flute and fiddle duet, but also sounds lovely on the button box.

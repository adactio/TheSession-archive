Three comments

Hareshaw Burn

Jig by Kathryn Tickell. I love the flow from back and fore from A to B parts. A great use of the key of C, although it may work better in D for session playing.

Typically played with a lot of swing by KT herelf, not to mention moving into something close to a reel at times, with multiple timing changes, but I think it works as a fast jig in its own right.

Recommended listening on “Instrumental” or the greatest hits CD.

Pelee Island

This is a nice little jig I composed. I named it after Pelee Island here in Lake Erie (Canada/USA Border). Watch out for the naturalized E’s in the B part.

↳ Posted by Tate on January 1st, 2013.

Since there are no E flats in the tune, you could just notate it as G dorian if you like. The B part starts with a strong similarity to the Coleraine, but goes in a different direction from there. Nice tune!

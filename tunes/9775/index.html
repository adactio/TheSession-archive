Three comments

My Dancing Bride

This is flute player Marcas O’Murchu’s composition. According to the notes, it was inspired by his wife’s dancing on their visit to San Bruno, California.

I love the simplicity of the melody. It goes well with McGibbney’s Fancy.

↳ Posted by slainte on July 30th, 2009.

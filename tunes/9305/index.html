Glendaruel Highlanders

Also known as a song: Campbelltown Loch (I wish you were whisky).

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on January 30th, 2009.

Cambelltown Loch

… And here are the lyrics of the “song” version:

Now Campbeltown Loch is a beautiful place,
But the price of the whisky is grim;
How nice it would be if the whisky was free,
And the Loch was filled up to the brim.

Chorus:
-------
Campbeltown Loch I wish you were whisky,
Campbeltown Loch och-aye!
Campbeltown Loch I wish you were whisky,
I would drink you dry.

I’d buy a yacht with the money I’ve got,
And I’d anchor it out in the bay,
If I wanted a sip I’d go in for a dip,
I’d be swimmin’ by night and by day.

We’d have a gathering of the clans,
They’d come from near and far;
I can see them grin as they’re wading in,
And shouting “Slainte Mhath!”.

But what if the boat should overturn,
And drowned in the Loch was I?
You’d hear me shout, you’d hear me call out,
“What a wonderful way to die !”

But what’s this I see, och comin’ for me,
It’s a vision to make your blood freeze;
It’s the police afloat in a dirty great boat;
And they’re shouting: “Time, gentlemen, please!”

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on January 30th, 2009.

Re: Glendaruel Highlanders

Looked this up as we were in Campbeltown Loch yesterday, and I said to my OH, “I’m sure there’s a pipe tune to do with Campbeltown”. We were on a day trip on the historic paddle steamer, Waverley (thereby hangs another song!), all the way from Greenock and back. Wonderful day out! Sadly, no whisky was consumed, though we did get 2 hours ashore to find the Springbank distillery and browse Cadenhead’s whisky shop.

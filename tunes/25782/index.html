Three comments

The Day On The River

It is impossible to play this spritely jig, composed by flute maker Martin Doyle, without having one’s spirits lifted up. It was written to commemorate a trip by kayak on the Inagh River in the middle of winter in County Clare, but it is anything but a frozen tune.. Many parts lend themselves to small variations, and as examples of this the full performance by Martin Doyle is included here: AABBAABB. Chet

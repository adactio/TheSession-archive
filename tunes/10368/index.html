Source

This jig was composed by David Hyams and recorded on his *Knowing the Place* album.

David’s kindly permitted me to upload my transcription of this tune and the tune that precedes it on the album, “The Stolen Kiss”, since I think they both make excellent session tunes. Sláinte! :-)

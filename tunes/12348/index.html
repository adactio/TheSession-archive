key signature is wrong

This tune is in 2 sharps (F# and C#). You could consider it E Dorian, but Dave Munnellly voices it in D, starting each 4 bar phrase on an A chord, rather than an Em. Both work. Regardless, the the Cs are all C#s. Otherwise the transcription is all right.
Does anyone know if Dave or Mick composed it? The only place I can find it is on their recording “’Tis What it Is”.

Burnside Of Tynet

William Marshall tune in F mixolydian. From the Coig album Rove.

↳ Posted by MarkRT on July 9th, 2021.

Re: Burnside Of Tynet

Burnside Of Tynet was a farm which was given to Marshall by his employer, the Duke of Gordon. He lived there while still working for the Duke, and for a while after he retired. In 1790 he moved to Mains of Keithmore. The published version of the tune was in F minor (4 flats).

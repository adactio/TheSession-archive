Four comments

Melancholy Martin

I was playing through my Krassen O‘Neill’s and came across this lovely simple tune that sat really nicely on my box, which I hadn’t heard before. There are several old settings out there, in Petrie, Levey, Sharp, and others. See https://tunearch.org/wiki/Melancholy_Martin for multiple transcriptions. I replaced the notated rolls in Krassen’s O’Neill’s with crotchet-quaver pairs like those in Petrie and Levey. Ornament as you like! I’ll post a G transposition as well.

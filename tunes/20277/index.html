Two comments

On The River Bank

Lovely tune in O’Neill’s 1001, #702. Similar to The Humours of Carrigaholt (https://thesession.org/tunes/1730), but with enough divergence--in almost every other measure--to make it distinct. I consolidated the A part and removed the key change between the parts, as half of the Cs in the A part are sharp anyway. A direct transcription is available here: https://tunearch.org/wiki/On_the_River_Bank

↳ Posted by benhockenberry on January 10th, 2021.

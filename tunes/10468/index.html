Polska (Hambo) från Fjällnäs / from Fjallnas

For D limited woodwinds you can play bars 1 & 5 going up to F or A instead of down to C, in other words, ABCs ~

D2 D>F E<F | ~ or ~ D2 D>F E<A | ~ or you could play it in G… :-D

I recently found myself playing this again, something we used to give air to quite often, including mixed in with the usual found here, Irish and Scottish tunes. It’s also one we’ve enjoyed dancing to as well. The two of us have known and played this in several different keys…

Rospiggspolska

I have this tunedown as “Rospiggspolska”. I have no idea about the relevence of that name but I have had it in my collection for at least 38 years. Can only presume that my title might refer to the danc but I have lost touch with the members of the troupe so have no way of clarifying anything.

“Hambo Rospiggspolska”

You can find a very bouncy version of it here, from an old 78, almost ceili band. I suspect it is a group from North America, but there is insufficient information as to who the source is for this, but the recordings are part of the history of the ‘International Folk Dance’ scene there ~

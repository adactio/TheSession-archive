Four comments

Vanishing Lake

This is not a tune I would play at a session ! But it can be useful in an other context : I learned it recently because I was asked to play it by a friend who practices Irish dance. I wrote it in 6/8 but it’s not a jig, it’s a set dance.
It was composed by accordeonist Francis Ward. According to his Souncloud : « The Vanishing Lake, composed by Francis Ward, winner of the Marie Duffy Foundation Excellence Award In Irish Dance Music Composition, will be added to the Official List of Set Dances (An Coimisiún le Rincí Gaelacha) on 1st June 2012. See www.francisward.ie for more info ».

Several versions on Youtube, most of them in A minor, like the original, and also a nice one in E minor by fiddler Sean Softley :
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EjEV2HwDNAg

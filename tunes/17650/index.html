The Barefoot Boy

This tune is from O’Neill’s 1001, #276, and also goes by the name “Buachaillin Óg”. More versions here:

http://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=montrealsession.mine.nu/lookup.php%3Fid=276%26tb=oneill_moi/0001

It’s likely a variation of An Buachaillin Dreoite ( https://thesession.org/tunes/1772 ), especially given the similar name, but I think it’s just different enough to warrant a different listing.

↳ Posted by Bazza on September 29th, 2019.

Re: Da Guizer’s

Gideon Stove was a Shetland fiddler. At First Day, Jan 1, the village men would dress in black face and go out asking for treats. They were called Guizers. It was introduced to the contra dancers by Scottish musicians and has become a popular dance tune in the US.
Sylvia Miskoe, Concord NH USA

↳ Posted by Sylvia Miskoe on October 1st, 2019.

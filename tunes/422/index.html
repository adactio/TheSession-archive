Six comments

A Parcel of Land

This is one of the tunes Charlie Lennon wrote for his suite “Island Wedding.” The setting here is by Frankie Gavin and was transcribed from a tape of an Irish radio show.

↳ Posted by radriano on December 6th, 2001.

The Dowry Reels

This great reel is played after Bag of Money. The two together are titled “The Dowry Reels.” They sound great on the flute as well as on the fiddle.

↳ Posted by David Levine on September 9th, 2006.

A Parcel Of Land, X:2

This is pretty close to Charlie Lennon’s original, with a few tweaks for flute/whistle. I remember the first few notes as “Par-cel of land…”

The Dowry Reels are:
A Bag of Money: https://thesession.org/tunes/2496
A Parcel of Land: https://thesession.org/tunes/422

Track 4 on Charlie Lennon’s “Island Wedding” suite recorded with Frankie Gavin and the RTE Concert Orchestra:
https://thesession.org/recordings/4376

Charlie Lennon tells the back-story as: “A matchmaker is brought to her family’s house. The match making begins with much argument and debate, slapping of hands and shouts, as the size of the dowry in the form of ‘A Bag of Money’ is agreed. The next stage is to walk the land, measure and step, measure and step until finally the size of ‘A Parcel of Land’ is determined and agreed.” -- Charlie Lennon, “Musical Memories, Volume 1”

↳ Posted by B-bird on March 12th, 2024.

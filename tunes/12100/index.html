Three comments

This is the Micho Russell’s tune off Teada’s first album transposed into D from a nice growly low A. It’s similar to The Humours Of Westport.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on July 31st, 2012.

This is popular in Cape Breton and has been recorded by Brenda Stubbert and Sandy MacIntyre, amongst others. In the Scottish tradition it has been recorded by Alisdair Fraser.

Fourteen comments

There’s another tune that sometimes gets called “Leg Of The Duck” on this database, aka “Daniel Of The Sun”. Actually it goes quite nicely with this one - you could have a set with a duck theme.

According to Norbeck, the lyrics to the original song are:

I gave it to Kitty because she was pretty,
The leg of the duck, the leg of the duck.
I gave it to Molly because she was jolly,
The leg of the duck, the leg of the duck.

I gave it to Nelly to stick in her belly
The leg of the duck, the leg of the duck.
She has it, she’s got it, wherever she put it,
The leg of the duck, the leg of the duck.

If you wanted to add more verses it looks like it would be pretty easy to do. You could even make up rude ones. I could elaborate but I think I might offend the American contingent with my foul language! :-)

The Leg Of The Duck, X:5

This is the third jig in the Tom Ferris set that I’ve been posting. It’s another tune from Tom’s repertoire originally learned as I understand from 1956 All-Ireland champion accordionist George Ross. Tom won the senior accordion in 1966. The jigs played before it were usually referred to as ‘Georgie’s Number 1 (uploaded under it’s correct title The Green Forest) followed by the Second setting of Tatter Jack Walsh, then finally this jig. Tom usually referred to it as ’Georgie’s Number 2’, however I’ve located it’s listing here as another Leg of the Duck or the pride of the west.

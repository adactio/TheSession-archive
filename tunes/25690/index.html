Two comments

I Stepped Up Towards Her And Fell Upon My Knees

Currently holds the record for the best tune title I have ever heard.

I got it from this video, which says it’s traditional Manx: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9y7gGmq1ZYw&list=RD9y7gGmq1ZYw&start_radio=1 It’s paired in a set with two jigs, so I wonder if it’s supposed to be a jig as well. But taken by itself, its rhythm sounds more hornpipe-y to me, so I’ve notated it as such.

↳ Posted by Aelia Aeola on July 4th, 2025.

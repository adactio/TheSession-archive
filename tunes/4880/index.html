Re: Karl Fant’s

From the sleeve notes of Ports of Call (Alasdair Fraser & Natalie Haas):
“…tune comes from Karl Rosenstrom, credited with introducing the waltz to Norway. (The third part was added later by fiddler Sven Nyhus.) ‘Fant’ is the name given to the Norwegian gypsies, or traveling people.”

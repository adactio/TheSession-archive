Highland ?

This was played by a flute/whistle player from the USA at the Willie Clancy week flute and whistle recital in 1986. Never did find out who he was, although we were in the same flute class.
A good player, who chose some very good tunes. This is possibly a Highland rather than a reel, because he paired it with what I’ve only recently discovered is one of 6 highlands composed by Ed Reavy. A nice simple tune. Anyone heard it before ?

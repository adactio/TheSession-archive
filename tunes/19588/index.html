Two comments

Vinterfolk

Recorded by the STRING SISTERS on their outstanding album “Between Wind and Water”. Composed by Tore Bruvoll, the band’s guitarist. A simple little tune, coming first in the key of Gmaj and appearing in “A” the last time round. Be that trick be hired from commercial pop music, the charm of the tune however, allows for even that. The band’s arrangement with 2nd lines and additional chord alterations adds an extra bang. A real earworm and very danceable for Bal Folk audiences too.

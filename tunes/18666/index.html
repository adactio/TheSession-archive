Three comments

Girls Of Grallagh

From “Music and Mischief”, by Kevin Crawford, Colin Farrell, and Patrick Doocey. It’s the middle tune on the second track, book-ended by “Maryann’s Choice” (jig) and “The Brightest Star” (reel).

The tune sounded familiar, so maybe there’s another name, but I couldn’t find it with Tune Pal, and didn’t see this title already in The Session.

↳ Posted by Brink on November 1st, 2019.

Re: Girls Of Grallagh

It’s also on the album by the Colin Farrell Band from 2018. It’s No 5 the first tune. It is written by Colin Farrell, as far as I know. The second tune there is The Hawthorn Hedge.

The Girls Of Grallagh

Tune by Colin Farrell, transcribed from his Tune a Day 2020 series. Named for a parish in Co. Galway, where his mother is from.

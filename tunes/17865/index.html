Re: The Miser’s Pocket

This tune is already here under the title “John Stewart” from the album Fiddlesticks (1991), where it was played by Tommy Peoples ( https://thesession.org/recordings/202 ). It’s possible that the title got mixed up with the other tune, I suppose. It stems from an 18th century tune called “Mr John Shaw Stewart’s Strathspey”.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on November 6th, 2020.

The Piper’s Apron

I don’t remember where this came from,I may have recorded it at a session.

↳ Posted by dafydd on February 28th, 2003.

This is a composition of the Waterford piper and whistler Tommy Keane, who has made a couple of records. The prascín, the piper’s apron, is a piece of leather tied to the piper’s right thigh, to insure closure of the bottom of the chanter for stacatto or “tight” playing (with silences in the music, for proper phrasing).

↳ Posted by Lucy Rietmann on June 4th, 2004.

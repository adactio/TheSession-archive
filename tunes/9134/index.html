Three comments

The String Trader

Written by Brian Hebert and found in Spring 2005 issue of Fiddler magazine.

↳ Posted by Jeremy Hughes on November 30th, 2008.

Fiddlers >> Whistle / Flute on “The String Trader”

As this is written almost entirely in straight 1/8 notes, I took a couple of liberties while just playing this on whistle / flute so that I didn’t fall over from lack of oxygen. In doing so, I came up with something like this:

X: 1
T: String Trader, The
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Emin
GEED E2GA | Beed BAGB | A3F AdBA | Beed BAGF |
GEED E2GA | Beed BAGB | A3F AdBA | BEED E4 :|
efed efga | beed e2fe | d2dB ABde | faaf gfed |
efed efga | beed e2fe | d2dB ABdB | AFED E4 :|

↳ Posted by m.r.kelahan on November 30th, 2008.

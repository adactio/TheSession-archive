Three comments

My Wife Has Scolded Me

This tune is actually a slow air rather than a strathspey. It was originally published in the Simon Fraser Collection (1816) with the name “Throd mo bhean a’s throd i rium” in Scottish Gaelic. This version was taken from https://tunearch.org/wiki/My_wife_has_scolded_me

↳ Posted by Javi V on September 5th, 2022.

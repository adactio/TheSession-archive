Cordavon Lodge

This is the tune that wakes me up on my alarm in the morning. I’m guessing you’ve taken from the Tannahill Weavers ‘Alchemy’ album, which also includes the other tune you’ve posted (with no info either), “Fair Maid of Oban” in the same set. My version has been slightly simplified, but it suits my non-piper shortcomings.

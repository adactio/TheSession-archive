Two comments

Gan Ainm

This march from the Feis Ceoil Collection of Irish Airs(1914) was collected by Darley and McCall from the fiddler William Schudell of Duncormick in south Wexford. He told them the tune was usually reserved as a Wedding march. The Schudells were most likely a Palatine family as there are many Palatine descendants in parts of Wexford. A likely descendant of William’s, ran a shop in Wexford town within living memory and he also played the fiddle.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on April 19th, 2023.

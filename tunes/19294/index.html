The Cheeky Robin

Never quite sure what the prevailing view is about including self-penned tunes (apart from the house rules guideline) but hope the occasional one is acceptable, if it’s reasonably in keeping with the trad focus of the site. This was written with a small granddaughter in mind. It’s meant to be played with a dotted feel.

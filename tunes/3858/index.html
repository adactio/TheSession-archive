A fondness for 7/8s and other mixed meter dance forms:

My cut and paste went pear shape above - - -

|:B2e fg ag|f2e fB BA|
B2e fg af|1 ecA GA Bc:|2 ecA BA GF||

M:7/8 (2-3-2)
|:EB Bce cA|F2 DFG AB|
EB BcA AB|1 AF FAB BG:|2 AF FAB BA||
|:B2 efg ag|f2 efB BA|
B2 efg af|1 ec AGA Bc:|2 ec ABA GF||

Yeah, it isn’t bad. I did try to see if it would fit a larger count, such as 14/8, but it isn’t consistent as you’ve given it in the notes. Basically the natural move for mixed meters is to roughly find a long and a short pulse, or in a basic kind or way, 3 count and 2 count foot tapping, so a 2-2-3 pulse would be short-short-long. You can combine these long and short pulses up to some large numbers - such as 21/16. But, there are some that vary that pulse around, so while my dancing inclination is to want some kind of repeating phrase in the bar, there’s nothing wrong with it being in 2s and 3s along the lines you envision it. But, personally, I like it better more danceable…Hopa!

Re: Drive The Golden Spike

In Brian McNeill’s book The Back o’the North Wind (1991), containing the dots of the record, plus notes, Brian credits himself as the composer of the tune.
The record is ‘an album of songs and tunes on the theme of the lives of scots across the Atlantic - past and present’.
The notes with the notation of the tune say ‘The golden spike referred to was the one used at Promontory Point in Utah in 1869 to finish the railroad link between the East and West coasts of America - a completion which vastly increased the speed of America’s trans-continental emigration: a two-edged sword, bringing hope to many - but as Muir saw, a concerted attack on the West’s remaining wilderness.’

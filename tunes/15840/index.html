Chocolate Is Calling

I wrote this one in late 2016 on a lazy friday morning. Later that day I was chatting with a friend of mine, Angus Mcgalligan, who is a professional chocolatier (and a mean piper too). At some point he indicated he had to get back to work with the phrase “Have to go lad - chocolate is calling!”. Hence the name.

The third part can be played with a 2-2-2-3 rhythm instead of the normal 3-3-3, which gives it a Balkan kind of sound.

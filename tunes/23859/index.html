Three comments

Brendan Mulvihill’s #2

In this video, starting at about 11:00, Brendan Mulvihill, Angleina Carberry, and Dan Brouder
play a set of three reels. Dan announces that the first two are compositions of Brendan (and the third is The Sailor on the Rock). I looked through Brendan’s books, Irish Scroll vol 1 an vol 2, and didn’t find the two reels. So I called the first one Brendan Mulvihill’s and the second one (this one) Brendan Mulvihill’s #2. That will have to do until we find out the correct names. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OVNMiT0RLps

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on December 27th, 2023.

Re: Joe Leary’s

Looking again at Irish Scroll vol 2, I find that this reel is, in fact, there with
the name Joe Leary’s but with the parts in a different order and somewhat different than
what the trio plays in the video. It is credited to Brendan in the book.
So, I’ve changed the name accordingly. I will post the book version next.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on December 27th, 2023.

Joe Leary’s, X:2

This is what is in the book Irish Scroll vol two. In the video, they go into this tune starting
with what is the third part in this version. So I wrote the first setting, based on what they played,
with that part first. But they did end with that part, before going into The Sailor on the Rock,
so it probably is supposed to be this way.

The Inverness Fiddler

Not to be confused with the strathspey of the same name nor the Bert Murray reel ..The Inverness FiddlerS.

This is pipe style march or jig according to your taste but it was composed by the flute player Hugh Marwick (Brother of Gavin) who now resides in the Black Isle.

It’s a fairly new tune which I also got from the aforementioned Inverness Fiddlers but very worthy of getting played in a session situation.

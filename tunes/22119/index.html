Two comments

Joey’s Delight

Composed by Leitrim fiddler Joe Liddy (1906-1992), this is the third reel in the set played by Edel Fox and Neil Byrne on track 5 of their 2013 recording, The Sunny Banks. I was playing along with that record and had some questions about this tune, but found that no one had yet transcribed it for thesession. So here it is!
(Note: On the record, Edel is playing a C#/G# concertina and the strings are all tuned up a half step; so it is heard a half step higher than as transcribed in Edorian.)

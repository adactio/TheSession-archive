Jig or slide? by Il paese delle mille danze

An Italian folk band named “Il paese delle mille danze” recorded a set of 3 jigs as follows:
1. The rakes of Kildare
2. The Kesh jig
3. this Gan Ainm
Gan Ainm sounds, for me, more as a slide, but anyway I look for a true title for it.

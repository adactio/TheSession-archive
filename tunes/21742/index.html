Little Boy’s

From Le Vent du Nord’s and Le Temp Antan’s supergroup album “Notre Album Solo” - this is the first tune in the Medley Brun set. It caught my ear due to those stops in the B part.

From the liner notes on Bandcamp (https://leventdunordetdetempsantan.bandcamp.com/track/medley-brun): This instrumental suite starts with Little boy’s reel by Gaspésie fiddler Erskine Morris, a piece André traced back thanks to Laura Risk

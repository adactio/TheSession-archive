“The Thingummyjig Polka” ~ C: Jim Johnstone ~ another for the week

Still hoping more Jim Johnstone tunes will be contributed by others in this week. We started it Thursday of last week with his jig “Christie MacLeod”.

“The Thingummyjig Polka” was also the theme tune for a Scottish TV program by the same name - “Thingummyjig”, with host Jack MacGlauchlin.

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iSplncZ3OF8

“The Thingummyjig polka” - Graham Wilson, accordion
http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lawWTD7Mz54

My general inclination, because of the length of each of the three parts, 16 bars each with the same last 8 bars each, is to not repeat the parts…

Four comments

Charles Sutherland

Composed by J Murdoch Henderson in 1933 and named after a “prominent violinist” from Fraserburgh, Scotland, who was a friend of James Scott Skinner. His reel playing was particularly admired. This version is from the playing of Jonny Hardie and Gavin Marwick. This tune was in the requests list.

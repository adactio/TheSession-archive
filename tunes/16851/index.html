Muñeira De Batribán

This Muñeira (jig) has been collected in the hamlet of Batribán (Vilanova d‘Ozcos, Asturies). It was part of the repertoire of pipers in the area, such as “Alejandro das Nenas” or “Firme de Batribán”. It was popularized in the rest of Asturias by Xuacu Amieva, who recorded her on her first CD “Onde L’Agua Ñaz” (where the water is born) and by the folk group Llan de Cubel on her first CD “Deva”.

The Fighting 69th

This is a composition of Seamus Gibson, a nephew of Tommy Peoples and a masterful fiddle player and composer in his own right. The notation is based on his playing and that of the great fiddle player Martin McGinley. Martin taught the tune and played it in sessions at the Cairdeas na bhFidiléirí Donegal Fiddle Summer School in Glencolmcille in early August, 2023. The Fighting 69th was a storied Union regiment in the American Civil War made up of Irish Americans.

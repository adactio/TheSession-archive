The Staircase Brawler

OK, this is one of my own compositions (Hi! I’m Adam Matlock), the first I composed specifically in an Irish style. I know this is technically frowned upon but I am trying to keep a balance of submitting other settings, and will do several more before I submit another of my own works.

The tune was written in honor of my dad, who passed this summer. The title is in reference to a set of subway stairs he battled with somewhat regularly while in treatment. I did get the chance to play it for him once, and although he would never have been described as the world’s biggest fan of Irish music, I think he liked hearing something that seemed cheerful.

Here’s a video of me playing it this summer. I’ve continued to play it and there are some new variations and evolutions developing. https://youtu.be/kv--fu0MLlo

Two comments

Hard Is My Fate

This is a Scottish slow air from the Simon Fraser collection.
Transcribed from the playing of Australian band Kejafi on their Album “Coast” (Track #3).
Originally in Bb. I’ve transposed it to G as Bb is not available here.

I’ll digg up the original version and post it later.

https://kejafi.bandcamp.com/track/nach-truagh-mo-ch-s-hard-is-my-fate

↳ Posted by DaveF on January 6th, 2021.

Hard Is My Fate, X:2

Transcribed from the first edition of “Airs and Melodies Peculiar to the Highlands of Scotland”. Simon Fraser, Edinburgh 1816. Tune #125, page #59

This is in the Public Domain and digital copies are available on the Petrucci Music Library (imslp.org).

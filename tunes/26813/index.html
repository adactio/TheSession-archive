The Shores Of Argyll

↳ Posted by gian marco on September 1st, 2026.

The Shores Of Argyll, X:2

This setting is given as a bagpipe retreat in Army Manual of Bagpipe Tunes, Book 1 (1934). I don’tknow anything about the tune’s history. I have it in a list of pieces associated with the great John McLellan of Dunoon, Argyll, but whether he composed it or not I can’t say. Anyway, it makes a fine waltz as in setting X:1.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on September 1st, 2026.

The Shores Of Argyll, X:3

It is indeed by John McLellan (Dunoon).
This third setting is how it is given (apart from the usual misaligned bar lines) in Dale Brown’s “the bagpipe music of John McLellan 1875-1949”.

He seemed to excel at retreats. The Bloody Fields of Flanders, Dream Valley of Glendaruel, Highland Brigade at Magersfontein, Lochanside and My Home Town all get played regularly in my neck of the woods.

Re: The Shores Of Argyll

The version posted by Gian Marco tracks the version recorded by Arise and Go on their new, and amazing, CD Moments of Light. I know it’s a bit blasphemous to say it, but I do think the low G in their version is an improvement on the original McLellan tune.

I would agree - the low G adds something extra to the tune.

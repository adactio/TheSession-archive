Two comments

Canoe In The Clouds

From the playing of Grey Larsen & André Marchand.

Composed by Grey Larsen.

On his website, Grey says of the tune:
“The title refers to a dreamlike fantasy, and also alludes to the Québecois folktale La Chasse Galerie.”

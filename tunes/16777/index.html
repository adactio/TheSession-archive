Two comments

Cape Breton

Does anyone have a name for this tune? I transcribed it from the playing of a friend. She says it’s a Cape Breton tune that she learned at a festival in Kansas years ago. Lots of notes . . . but I love the beginning of the second part.

↳ Posted by jdicarlo on March 3rd, 2018.

Roseate Tern

It turns out that this lovely tune is a composition by Cape Breton fiddler, and former lighthouse keeper, Paul Cranford. A treasure trove of abc’s and other things can be found at his website: https://www.cranfordpub.com/

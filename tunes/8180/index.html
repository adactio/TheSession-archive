Up In The Rafters, X:3

From the playing of Roaring Mary.

They said of the tune in their album liner notes:

“Up In The Rafters is one of Aran Olwell’s compositions, and celebrates a wild night evading authority.”

Aran wasn’t one of the members of their band, though, they thanked him in their liner notes, so presumably they knew him personally.

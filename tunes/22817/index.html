The Devil’s Staircase

A Robert Matheison Pipe tune.
The Devil’s staircase is the route taken by Sir Robert Campbell’s army into Glencoe, where he butchered the MacDonald’s in the infamous massacre. It’s still a popular hiking and biking route from Kinlochleven to Altnafeadh Glencoe. Apparently, the path got it’s name form the workers building the Blackwater Dam. They would take the path as the shortest route from their worksite to the Kingshouse Hotel. Their journey back on “unsteady legs” on a cold winters night would all too often allow the devil to “claim his own”. It’s a great little pipe tune and pretty fun on the banjo as well.

Slainte!

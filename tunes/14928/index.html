Orla’s Intent

This powerful reel was composed by Áine Heslin, it’s on her recording TUNES FOUNDRY..
According to the sleeve notes:
‘’Orla Coughlan (a fiddle-player from Lisseycasey, Co. Clare), Mairéad Considine and myself regularly play pub gigs together. I composed this reel – a strong reel in D – to portray Orla’s sense of humour and her positive mental attitude and also to follow “Mairéad’s Paradise”.

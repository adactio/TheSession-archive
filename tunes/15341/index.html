Up The Pond

This is a tune common to Newfoundland. It is played at the annual St. John’s regatta in St. John’s every summer and has been recorded by local musicians. Perhaps usually played in D major, here it is dropped 7 semi-tones down to G major.

↳ Posted by Chellam on April 6th, 2016.

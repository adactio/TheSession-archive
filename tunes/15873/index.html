Re: Time To Time

Another thing to mention is that as stated by Colin above , he plays in G but with capo on 2nd fret.
However he is not using standard Gdae tuning , but some configuration of it . This is evident when he plays the high “a” note up at the 15 th fret at end of part A first time round. On standard Gdae tuning this note is at 17h fret.

Two comments

Here’la

At the “Tutor’s Recital” at last year’s “Cruinniu” Flute Gathering in Ballyvourney, Conal O’Grada nearly lifted the roof off the place with a set of 3 of his own reels in one of the finest live performances of Irish music on flute I’ve ever been priviliged to hear. He recorded the 3 reels on the second “Raw Bar Collective” CD, and this is the first of them.

↳ Posted by Kenny on March 27th, 2018.

Five comments

A mandolin delight

I’ve always thought that this tune sounds especially nice on the mandolin after hearing Mick Moloney’s version on “Strings Attached”. It’s always been one of my favourites and I actually got around to learning the whole six parts a couple of years ago.

John

↳ Posted by 🎻 Melody Player 🎻 on May 18th, 2004.

Extra triplets

I usually put in a few extra triplets depending on my mood, usually in the last bars of each of the parts.

↳ Posted by 🎻 Melody Player 🎻 on May 18th, 2004.

Oops

I made a small “boo boo” and forget to enter the repeat “:|” in parts 3 and 6. I’ve now amended the ABC and this is correct. However, please take this into account if playing from the sheet music or midi.

John

↳ Posted by 🎻 Melody Player 🎻 on May 18th, 2004.

Off To Puck Fair

I agree, John - Mick’s playing of this is cracking. I’ve never heard of it elsewhere, and although the basis of the thing is the old Scots jig “My Wife’s a Wanton Wee Thing”, OTPF takes it to uncharted places. A pure delight!

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on August 28th, 2007.

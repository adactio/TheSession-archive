Five comments

Source

Transcription is based on the tune as it is played on *Shouts* by Garry Innes And Ewan Robertson. I’m not sure if it’s an original by Innes.

The name (presumably) is a politely shortened version of the British colloquial phrase, “rough as a badger’s arse”, to mean “ugly, unsophisticated, unwell, especially as a result of too much drinking” (Concise New Partridge Dictionary of Slang and Unconventional English, London, 2008, p. 548)

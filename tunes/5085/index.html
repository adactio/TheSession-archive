Henry MacDermott Roe

This is the first of three airs composed by the blind Irish harper Turlough O’Carolan (1670-1738) all titled “Henry MacDermott Roe” (Tune number 78, page 66, Ossian Publications - Complete Collection of Carolan’s Irish Tunes)

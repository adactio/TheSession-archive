Two comments

Gan Ainm

A nice little jig from the playing of Wexford melodeon player Lar White. As far as I know it’s another of his own compositions which so far I haven’t been given a name for. Being played on a single row ‘C’ melodeon on the recording that Paddy Joyce of Clonroche sent me, I’ve notated it in C but I’ll also upload a ‘D’ version to make it more suitable for other melody instruments.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on October 23rd, 2022.

Re: Mendiokerra

This beautiful tune is not traditional Basque. It is actually a tune composed by the recently deceased Irish musician Alan Griffin.
Alan lived more than half his life here in the Basque Country making a great contribution to traditional Basque music.
Indeed, this tune has been recorded by some Irish musicians and has been taken as traditional, but it is not.
Although here it is written as a jiga, it is actually a compound rhythm (6/8 + 3/4) typical of the Basque Country.
Irish guitarists have a hard time following it and turn it into a jig.
You can listen to the original version recorded by Alan with his band, Alboka, in the following link:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Y0IMEZZj50
(In this three-tune set, Mendiokerra is the third).
It truly is a delightful tune, like all Alan composed.
Rest in peace.

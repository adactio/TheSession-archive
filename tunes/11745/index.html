Two comments

This is almost certainly an Émile Benoît composition. I think the ‘Claudine’ in question is Claudine Langille (of Touchstone renown), whose father came from Nova Scotia.

↳ Posted by GW on June 9th, 2013.

Claudine, X:2

This tune has made its way into the Quebecois repertoire and has been recorded by Entourloupe, Les tetes de violon and Genticorum (and likely by others) and sometimes is known as Émile Benoit.

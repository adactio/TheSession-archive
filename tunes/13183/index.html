Paddy Fahey’s

This is the unornamented tune from Liz and Yvonne Kane, The Well Tempered Bow, Track 7#2
I’ve spent hours trawling through all the Paddy Fahey reels and it doesn’t seem to be there.

Re: Paddy Fahey’s

This is reel is listed as #19 in the Holohan collection. Interestingly, the parts are switched up! The Kane sisters play it BBAA whereas Holohan has it written AABB (which I presume is how Fahey himself played it, given that the Holohan is just transcriptions of his own playing). Probably fine to play it either way!

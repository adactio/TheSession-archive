Cahill’s Stipulation

One night at the now defunct Three Beans Session in Audubon, NJ, the word “stipulated” was being thrown around at every opportunity. When we played the set “Trip To Birmingham/Down The Broom/The Gatehouse Maid”, one of the sessioneers was wondering why the last two (both being in Adorian) were being played together. Another sessioner, Tom Cahill, noted that reknowned Sligo/Philadelphia fiddler John Vesey would always play those tunes together at sessions. Since then, playing those two tunes together has become known as "Cahill’s Stipulation. This tune celebrates Tom’s deep abiding love and knowledge of the previous generation of Philadelphia Irish musicians.

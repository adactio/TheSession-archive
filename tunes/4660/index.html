Six comments

Mominsko Horo

Not sure who is responsible for this transcription. It was passed on to me by Keiron as a ‘Noteworthy’ file. I tried playing this in a few sessions in the 1980s, but we generally fell apart before getting to the end. None of us really knew what was going on. I asked Andy irvine about the provenance of this tune. He described it as an early attempt to remember a balkan tune. It doesn’t really look all that Balkan, so that is explained.

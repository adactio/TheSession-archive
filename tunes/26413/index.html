March Pour Herri

This is a tune I took from a video by Sylvain Borou in which he plays the pipes.I think is traditional, but it couldn’t also be that way. The sheet music isn’t available online, and since I wanted to play it in a session with other trad musicians, I arranged it and decided to share it here with the community. He was accompanied by an excellent whistle and an equally compelling guitar.

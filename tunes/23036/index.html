The Girl At Gibson’s Lodgings, X:3

I composed this tune c. 2015 for Catherine Brice, a percussive dancer, who was then doing the scrubbing at Gibson’s Lodgings, a historic inn in Annapolis, Maryland. The melody is… unnatural… because the phrases are too long for a polka. We’ll have to call it something else. My current preference is “Chesapeake polkas” for this and the others in its litter.

↳ Posted by Tuney the Blue Crab on April 12th, 2024.

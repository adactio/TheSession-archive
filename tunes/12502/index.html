Buds in winter

Lovely tune. Not exactly like the recording on An Tor “Buds in Winter”(2002), but very close. Sorry, I’m not good enough in abc notation or writing down tunes at all. My respect to anyone who can.
The tune is written by Nils Nolte and Marcus Metz. An Tor is a German Irish folk band, excellent musicians and very pleasant to have around.

↳ Posted by gagorob on February 18th, 2013.

Ryan’s Welcome Home

Here’s another composition of the prolific composer John Noctor, named to mark the birth of another son, Ryan, in Wexford hospital. (I had already uploaded a tune in honour of his other son, Jack). John Noctor’s tunes with Wexford titles or connections are being notated and included in the Wexford Tune project.

The Dreamer’s

A request on The Session for the score; this is my take on it.
This is a gorgeous tune played as a slow reel by Séamus McGuire and Steve Cooney on An Irish Viola (https://thesession.org/recordings/7460) and composed by Séamus McGuire. I’ve long loved the intonation and rhythm of Séamus playing fiddle duets with his brother Manus. This is maybe not a session tune - I’ve never heard anyone else play it (and maybe I don’t want to!).

One comment

Ar Thóir An Donn

I’ve got this tune firmly stuck in my ear. Written by Donal O’Connor and recorded on At First Light’s album Idir. Donal also plays it in the low growly key of Am which just shifts everything over one string on the fiddle. He’s got some nice variations as well, but this is the basic run through the tune when he’s not messing with it.

Two comments

Micho Russell’s Mason Apron Fling, X:3

Learnt from a recording of the man himself, from his album The Man From Clare (via YouTube). He plays G fingering on a C whistle. This is as close a transcription as I could get, taking the average of the 3 times he plays through the tune. He plays a few nice variations in there too.
This one is a reel, not a fling or barndance. I figured I’d upload it here though, rather than create a new tune.

↳ Posted by Allan Evans on December 31st, 2025.

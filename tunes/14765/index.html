Hillwick Wedding

A nice little wedding tune (actually not a real reel) by the great Tom Anderson.
I don’t know when (and for whom) he wrote it, but it was definitely latest in 1976 because I learned it from the Boys of the Lough “Good Friends good music” that was released in 1977. My former band played this tune (and the following one, Robertson’s) many times in concert in Belgium in the late seventies.
The variations that follow will be posted later.
Enjoy !

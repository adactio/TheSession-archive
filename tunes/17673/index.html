Currie Dance

Hoping to write a jig, I wrote this tune in the 1990s. Turned out a waltz but seems like a hybrid to me. I wrote it for a lad in Glasgow (met in the 1970s at work) and I couldn’t forget him. Words were written for it too but best not go into those … I did my best to make it sound Scottish, hope I’ve succeeded to some degree. (Sad to say I still love him, alas).

Under The Influence

Greeeat tune BrownAleMugger. One or two bits slightly different though, if you don’t mind me saying. The 2nd g in bar one is a triplet, 1st c in bar two is a B. In the second part - no rests on highland pipes, no tie before the final 2 bars and the 1st note of the second last bar is A. If you hear it this way too you could edit the abc’s before it goes to dots.

Four comments

Steam Train To Mallaigh

This is a tunes written by piper Mary-AnnMacKinnon. An Italian piper with whom I play in a group recently proposed it to me. I find it extremely interesting from the point of view of possible textures and arrangements. Have fun.
Fabio

Re: Steam Train To Mallaig

The tune is a suite for pipes, and I’m not really sure the tune works played as a solo. Worth a try, perhaps. The beginning is, indeed, played in 6/8, but the opening theme is repeated in 4/4 later in the tune. Several of us performed the suite at North Hero many years ago on smallpipes and borderpipes. Great fun to play.

Here’s a fine representative version of the suite:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YbRjfLc4_ew

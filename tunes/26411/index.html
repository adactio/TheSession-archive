“One of my favorites from Collected III’s reel section: ”Fr. Speedy"
My brother Tom came home from a visit to Ireland with our folks one time and told me about the packed church in Co. Offaly one Saturday evening while he was there, for the “quicker than quick, tornado of a mass” by a certain, as my brother dubbed him, Father Speedy. My mom’s response was, “This doesn’t count!” and indeed she went back for Sunday mass the next day."

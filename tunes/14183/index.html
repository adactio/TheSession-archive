Long Island Jig

This is a typical tune for bagpipes/smallpipes.
Composed by Pipe Major Robert Mathieson.
This jig appears in his recording called “The Big Birl” released in 1997.
In this album, “Long Island Jig” is preceded by a slow air called “Breton Air”.

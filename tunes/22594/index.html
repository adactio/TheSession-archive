Ghost In The Machine

A tune from Let’s Have Another Gan Ainm, by “O Conaill Family and Friends”. On the album, it’s the Gan Ainm before the Bunch of Green Rushes, but I and several musicians around Montreal have taken to calling it Ghost in the Machine, as the tune needed a name. Generated by folk-rnn; subsequently adapted by Daren Banarsë and then by the musicians on the album; now part of the oral tradition in Montreal and elsewhere.

The fourth measure of the A section is what initially made me fall in love with the tune; most of the notes in that measure seem to have been the work of the musicians playing on the album, but the single exquisite B-natural was the neural network’s.

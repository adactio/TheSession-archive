Flook! tune

Another fun tune from Flook’s Flatfish album. They play it after “Thornhill Road”, and with an interesting, somewhat confusing rhythm, other than the syncopation already in the tune. Personally, I love it though, along with the bit of jazz improv after… Which is odd for me, as I mostly hate jazz….

↳ Posted by Joseph Chordmichael on September 11th, 2008.

I realize I made a mistake here, the tune is in Adorian, not Eminor. They play it on low F whistles, putting it in Cdorian.

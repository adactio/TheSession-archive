The Weasel

This Brighde Chaimbeul reel, not to be confused with the John Morris Rankin reel of the same name, has been requested by a couple of members here.
Brighde plays it, with Ross Ainslie, in F on C smallpipes with a low F# (sounds as A), accompanied by Steven Byrnes here (from 3:26): https://rossainslie.bandcamp.com/track/the-badger-the-weasel

Re: The Weasel

I just listened to what Steven Byrnes played.
I generally think it’s better to use your ears than your eyes when trying to find chord sequences. On the first pass it’s pretty much a D drone so there’s an opportunity to experiment with different chords over the top.

I’m not that hot on mandolin chords as I learnt my strings-in-fifths chords first on a mandola (tuned CGDA), so I have to mentally transpose a fifth all the time.
But, if I was playing chords for this on the mandolin, I might go for something like:
“D” = 7705
“G” = 0005
“A” = 2205
“Bm” = 4405
So it’s basically 5 chords with A drones on top (technical name is “inverted pedal”).
If the double A on the G chord is a bit much then 0055 or, as you say, 0053. You could also play D5 as 7005 and A5 as 2200. For the 2205 A5 voicing, I bar the bottom two strings with the tip of my index finger.

Fling Under The Bridge

This tune was written by a twelve year old boy called Matheu Watson.It was written about a pilot who decided to fly his plane under the Findhorne bridge. Luckily no one was hurt!

↳ Posted by weemanwatson on February 22nd, 2003.

Did he just decide, or did he actually flirt with Darwinian selection by commiting the act?

Just a hint, nothing against E major, it’s a great key, with all the sharps and everything, but if you want it to succeed as a traditional tune, it’s better to stick to D or G.

↳ Posted by glauber on February 24th, 2003.

E is fine for fiddlers

Maybe it’s not the most sociable key , well not if your friends are fluters anyway, but on fiddle, diatonic acco., E is no problem. I like the diversiteeee.

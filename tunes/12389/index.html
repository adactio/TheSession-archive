Three comments

The Dolphin Waltz

I loved this tune the first time I heard it on a recording by Shetland fiddler Trevor Hunter. Duncan Chisholm has also recorded it. It was written by a Canadian fiddler Johnny Mooring, and might have been originally titled “The Dauphin Waltz” (French for dolphin I think).

Sound clip

In response to Conchuir’s query, the title is Welsh.

Here (http://www.box.net/shared/jq6rzgcvrn) is a link to a sound clip of the tune played on two flutes by Ceri Rhys Matthews and myself at a session at Ty Siamas, the new (opened summer 2007) Centre for Welsh Traditional Music in Dolgellau last autumn. We play it quite briskly (and freely) here, but it is also often played more contemplatively - works well either way. Ceri is doing most of the “embroidery”. (I didn’t know its title at the time…..and BTW, Tom who has posted the ABC here was present!)

Of course, it isn’t really a waltz any more than most O’Carolan tunes in 3/4 are waltzes, though it can certainly be played as one/danced to as one…….

Here’s my take on the ABC - basic tune, no ornaments or variations:
T:Lliw Lili Ymysg Y Drain
T:(The Colour of the Lily Amongst the Thorns)
C:Traditional
O:Welsh
M:3/4
L:1/8
Z:Jem Hammond 28:3:2008
K:E Aeolian

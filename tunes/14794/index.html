Two comments

Lady Home’s

A nice jig in the unusual key of C, written by Nathaniel Gow who published it in 1799 in his 1st Repository .
Who was the Lady ? According to The Biographical Dictionary of Scottish Women (by Elizabeth Ewan, Sue Innes and Sian Reynolds, Edinburgh, 2006, p. 155), Marion Haliburton, who was born in c. 1500 and died in c. 1563, was the daughter of Patrick, Lord Haliburton. She married George, 4th Lord Home. But, if this Lady Home was alive during Gow’s life, she could also have been the wife of 9th of the 10th Earl of Home.
Anne Hamilton was the 3rd Duchess of Hamilton (1632-1716).

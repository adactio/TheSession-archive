Lucy Farr Polka no.1

This is the first of two polka’s transcribed from an LP produced in 1975 by Leader records LED 2071 on which Lucy Farr is playing with London based musicians Michael Plunkett, Paul Gross (fiddles) with Reg Hall (piano) and Ron Somers (drums). Polka no.2 will be posted tomorrow.

↳ Posted by hetty on November 24th, 2004.

The LP is entitled ‘The Rakes’.

Harmony Of The Seas

This tune was composed by Pat Knight about 8 years ago and I’m posting it here in his memory. Sadly Pat died last weekend. Known popularly as “Pat the Flute” many people reading this will have come across him over the years at sessions and festivals. He said the tune just came to him and he thought he must have heard it somewhere. After much checking he decided he must have made it up. It was about the time that the cruise ship Harmony of the Seas was in the news (launched 2015, so possibly the year after). He thought the name was a good name for the tune and that’s the name that stuck.
Both Pat and his late brother Andrew were wonderful musicians with a deep knowledge and love of traditional music. They both moved to the Isle of Man in the 1990’s and remained there, but often travelling to trad music events near and far. He (as was Andrew) will be greatly missed by friends and fellow musicians. Gurra mie mooar Pat.
[NB The tempo is that of a slow waltz]

Harmony Of The Seas, X:2

ABC shown for waltz time, with basic chords.
I heard this played by Patrick Knight at an Isle of Man Traditional Music Weekend a few years ago in the St Columba club, Douglas. It was packed with musicians. The room had stopped for a break and there was the usual excited chatter and conversation. Pat picked his moment to play, he had that extraordinary ability to be able to quieten a room after just a few notes. It was a skill he used sparingly, but always with the desired effect.
Pat would play the occasional roll and embellishment not shown here and I hope the abc does the tune some justice.

Three comments

The Journey

John Flanagan composed this tune a few years prior to his passing in May 1990 at the age of 28. He wrote the tune while he was suffering with a terminal illness. When he played the tune on his violin, the tune sounded more like a slow aire than a march.

I want to give a special thanks to Miriam Rosenblum who was able to provide the music transcription.

↳ Posted by Jim Flanagan on March 28th, 2024.

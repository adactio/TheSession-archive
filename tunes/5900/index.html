“The Flowers of the Burren”

Sometime ago someone had contacted me for this tune, having found another here using the name that wasn’t the one they sought. I hope this is it. It brings me joy and I hope it will for others, including that person who first was asking about it ages back…

Re: The Town Of Cootehill

This tune transcribed is actually Rocking Chair written by the great Canadian fiddler Graham Townsend.

I suspect the misnaming has arisen due to Josephine Keegan pairing Rocking Chair with the actual Town of Cootehill on her Outlet recording.

Comment

I came across this tune on a CD by Chris Knowles who plays the Celtic harp. The CD is called “Frame of Harmony”. I have a book of Cornish session tunes “Racca 2” and I found the dots there. It is played slow and was originally in Bb. I have transposed it up to suit my playing. The notes in the book indicate that the tune is a carol from the Davies Gilbert collection.

What do you think of my piece?

I wrote this piece for practice for a GCSE in music. I have arranged it for string orchestra; flute, violin, piano and snare drum; and just for the flute. When I suggested to my music teacher to put it on the internet, he was more than enthusiastic. I am very happy that it is on the internet for people to use around the world. It is the first piece of music that I have published. Please post any thoughts or comments. If you would like any of the above arrangements, please email me groovytman@yahoo.com.

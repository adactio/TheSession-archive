The Whistlebinkies, X:2

This tune was composed by piper Rab Wallace who played in the group The Whistlebinkies. They recorded it on their “Whistlebinkies 3” album (1981). X:2 is transcribed from that recording.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on October 10th, 2020.

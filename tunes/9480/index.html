Better in F

For fiddlers anyway, this is better in the F version titled “The Czar of Russia’s Favorite” in Ryan’s Mammoth Collection (aka 1000 Fiddle Tunes).

↳ Posted by blarneystar on March 31st, 2009.

William West

The tune, by the way, was probably named for William West, a 19th-century American banjo player, comedian and clog dancer whose real name was Flynn. West was a long-time partner of soft shoe minstrel dancer George Primrose, and he also toured Britain in the1880s to great acclaim with Johnny Queen, another famed American variety show dancer.

↳ Posted by blarneystar on March 31st, 2009.

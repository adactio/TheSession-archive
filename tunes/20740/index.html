Ricarda’s

An original reel for “reel” fighter of a person I know. I have also been calling it the “Stevern Stomp”.

I feel like this tune lies somewhere between Appalachia and Ireland, as I had intended it to be more oldtimey, but turned out distinctly Irish. Funny how that works!

BTW please post if this is similar to an existing tune, as I am always worried that I am copying a forgotten tune.

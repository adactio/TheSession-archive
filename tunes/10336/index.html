The Fall Of Dunboy, X:3

A flute buddy brought this tune to my attention last week, warning that it would be rattling around my brain nonstop once I had listened to it a few times. She was right! X:3 is my attempt at a fairly close transcription of the tune as played by the composer on John Dwyer’s record The Dursey Sound. It’s close the the setting played by the Ó Faoláin brothers at the tribute concert linked above--except they play a half-step down in F# major! I had a bit of a shock the first time I tried playing along on a D-pitched flute! A really lovely tune commemorating a particularly gruesome episode of Irish history.

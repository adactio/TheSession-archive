Two comments

Isolation

On the radio this morning we heard a segment about a seven year old “prodigy”
named Stelios Kerasidis who composed a waltz for all the people in isolation.
My wife really liked it and told me to learn it.
So here is an approximation, playable on mandolin, of what I heard in a YouTube clip.
(I had to choose Fdorian in order to get three flats. The key is really Cminor.
I will use the ABC transposer to post it again in Bminor.)
Here is the YouTube clip I found:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MNRyQ0ah6Gk

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on April 17th, 2020.

Isolation, X:2

As I suspected, it is easier to play in B minor.
Not quite as melancholy as C minor, but still nice.

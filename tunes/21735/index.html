Burnbank Cottage

This is a quirky wee march by Catriona Hawksworth, named after her parents’ house.
It is the opening tune on “Duo”, a 2017 release from Catriona and Sally Simpson.

I like the fact that it starts on the subdominant (IV chord) and has a few missed beats, and just think it’s a really nice tune.
The barlines for the 3/4 bars are, I think, in the correct places as they coincide with the chord changes/emphases in the backing.

The New Whistle

This great hornpipe was written by Olaf Sickmann and it’s on his new album ‘’New Irish Tin Whistle Tunes‘’
According to Olaf:
‘’The tin whistle with which I recorded this album was made by American tin whistle builder John Sindt. You have to wait for up to two years for one of his instruments, so I was very happy to finally hold it in my hands. I hope that The New Whistle Hornpipe reflects some of this happiness.‘’
Hup! =)

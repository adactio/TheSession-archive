Invergordon’s Welcome To Queen Elizabeth II. C: John Davie Burgess.

A tune brought to attention by Kenny:

https://thesession.org/discussions/35284

It is not a strathspey, but a 2/4 pipe march.
Composed by Pipe Major John D Burgess MBE, (1934-2005).
You can render it as a 2/4 march by replacing the header with:

X: 1
T: Invergordon’s Welcome to Queen Elizabeth II.
C: John D Burgess
R: Pipe March
M: 2/4
L: 1/16
K: Hp

It does look like the proper title of the tune is the one in this submission.

The story behind the tune is given here:

http://www.ross-shirejournal.co.uk/Features/Reflections/22-Years-Ago-6446.htm

And a picture of the composer, playing on the day here:

http://www.theinvergordonarchive.org/picture/number483.asp

Could just as easily be submitted in D major, but the Amix is suggestive of the pipe scale.

The Green Cake

This beautiful F#minor reel has been doing the rounds over the last few years at sessions around New Zealand and Australia. It was composed in 2010 by Wellington accordion player/whistler Jonathan Berkahn.

The B part really grabs people’s attention in sessions! Great F#minor tune for all instruments with plenty of opportunities for cuts and triplets.

Re: The Green Cake

The first version is from the original composer and also how it’s played in sessions in Australia and New Zealand and now the States. (Great to meet you Deborah and thanks for the lovely sessions at the Jar!)

Interesting point about the Amaj aspect to the tune, Ryan, I’ve never thought about the tune like that. As you’ll know trad tunes will quite often have “flashes” of their relative minors and majors throughout which I think this tune does effectively at the end of each part the same as the Jig of Slurs (G/Em) and the New Mown Meadow (Edor/A) do. I think the decisive dive back into an F#m chord at the start of each part is really where The Green Cake reel lives and makes it one of those tunes you just want to keep playing multiple times. If we do finish a set with this tune or play it as a one off we’ll resolve on the F#m chord instead of an A.

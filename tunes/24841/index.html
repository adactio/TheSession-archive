Da 60 Fathom Reel

Shetland tune written by Alex Couper

↳ Posted by Jacqui Brown on October 10th, 2024.

Re: Da 60 Fathom

I recorded this from a radio programme and wrote it down in one of my notebooks in about 1994. It was part of a cracking set played by the Ivor Scollay Band, and they said it was composed half way between Shetlnd and Norway. The other tunes in the set were “Willie Taylor’s Fiddle” by Lell Robertson (great tune again, and unfortunately Lell Robertson’s only composition, commemorating a session in Shetland with the Northumbrian fiddler) and “Magnie’s Matchless” by Ivor Scollay’s father (about a motorbike belonging to another member of the Scollay clan).

I love this tune, and it still pops into my head every so often when I’m absent-mindedly playing hundreds of tunes. Where did you lesrn it, Jacqui?

X: 2 “Betty Black” / “Petticoat String”

Original source? I’m hoping someone might actually know. I’m still looking… This transcription is based on those found in the following publications, with very little difference between them, such that I’m sure their original source, not given, is the same.

“One Thousand English Country Dance Tunes” edited by Michael Raven, 1984
pages 140 - 189 - Country Dance Tunes in 2/4, 2/2 and 4/4
page 178: “Betty Black” / “Petticoat String”

“The Fiddler’s Tune-Book: 200 Traditional Airs” edited by Peter Kennedy
(originally published as two small volumes, 1967)
A Mally Production, 1994
page 14, tune #46: “Betty Black” / “Petticoat String”

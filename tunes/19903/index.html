The Holly Spoon

Tramscribed from Tommy Fitzharris & Donal McCague’s album The Bank Of Turf.

Re: The Holly Spoon

According to the linear notes from the album, Donal learnt it from Sean McElwain who got it from the James Whiteside manuscript. It was collected in the 1870s.

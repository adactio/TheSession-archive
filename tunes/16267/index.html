Seven comments

Vou Vivendo

Sitting at my computer, I enjoy listening to ‘ceol.fm’. Occasionally a cracking tune will turn up which I’d really like to identify but can’t. I’ve now solved that problem with a nifty little app, CoverToaster (Mac only, I think). It helped me to identify this tune, ‘Vou Vivendo’ (composed by Pixinguinha & Benedito Lacerda). It was the Carlos Nunez version that I heard (fabulous) but there are many other good recordings of this tune out there. I’ve transcribed it in the key of G for my whistle but whatever key you choose to play it in, there’s going to be a fair bit of chromatic trickery to cope with! Well worth it, though, but good luck! Btw, I’m sure the tune is really a Samba but I’ve used the nearest Irish tune category I could find.

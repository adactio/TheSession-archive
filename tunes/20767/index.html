Lust In Translation

The track “Lust” transcribed from Rura’s “In Praise of Home” album. The phrasing is a bit non-traditional with having 7 1/2 bars repeated in the A and B parts. What’s written as a C part is a tag that is played interspersed on the track. If anyone has suggestions on a better way to notate this, I’d very much appreciate it.

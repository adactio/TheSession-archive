Re: The Longacre

Morning, piprgrl.

“The Longacre. This tune doesn’t actually have a name, to the best of my knowledge, so we called it after the band we learned it from. They got it from the Breton guitarist Dan Ar Bras from a piece he put together called the Scottish Suite, and featured Patrick Mollard on pipes.” - Donogh Hennessy in Lunasa 1996-2001 (tunebook).

It’s a fairly common structure, so it has similarities with quite a few other tunes, and it might be hard to pin it down.

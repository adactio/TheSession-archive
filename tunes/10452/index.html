The Glorious 13th - Schottish A Virmoux

Hmm. Now I know why this tune sounded familiar on the album…Ive heard it played in a different form at sessions!!!

It seems that the Mawkin track is just a rework of Schottish A Virmoux in a different key with the A & B parts swapped over.

So its French in origin. The arrangement I have heard is in G with an almost exact A part (but played as the B in the trad arrangement). The Mawkin B part (Trad A part) differs - I guess this is either artistic licence or perhaps mis-heard/remembered from sessions?

↳ Posted by Barry Pearce on May 5th, 2010.

Scottisch à Virmoux

X: 1
T: Scottisch à Virmoux
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Dmaj
DAGF A3F|GFGA B2A2|AdAF G2FE|1 FAFD E4:|2 DAGA D4:||
d2A2 d3c|BABc dedB|A2D2 A2GF|1 EFGA BABc|2 EFGA D4:||

This traditional tune is a staple of the ‘’Musique du Centre‘’ repertoire; a traditional from the Berry / Bourbonnais region. (It has been recorded for example by La Chavannée on their 1992 CD ‘Cotillon’. This band’s style and stance are widely held as a reference in the genre)

Hear it played in a set on the French hurdy-gurdy:

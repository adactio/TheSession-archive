Re: Flee As A Bird

I believe this is a Lancashire clog dance. I don’t think it is Northumbrian tune, and certainly not Irish IMHO. I played it on my CD and named my CD after it. As far as concertina playing is concerned it is best played mainly on the G row for the BCC#D sequence, similar to Nancy, The Carrick Hornpipe, and the Boddington Hornpipe. I was teaching several concertina groups of different levels years ago. The “top” level group had been taught by a famous concertina player, but they were unable to play these tunes because their concertina instructor told them to always focus their fingering on the C row.

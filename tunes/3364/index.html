The Cheviot Rant

Composer was Billy Miller, fiddler with the Cheviot Ranters in the nineteen-fifties. He was no longer with the band when they recorded three LPs for Topic in the early seventies. The three Topic albums are all available as digital downloads from Amazon etc.

↳ Posted by Tony Fowler on July 10th, 2015.

Re: The Cheviot Rant

Further to my posting years back. Thank you to Tony Fowler for correcting my misunderstanding of the composer of this tune. Billy Middler wrote the tune then his band-mate George Mitchell composed the dance to go with it.

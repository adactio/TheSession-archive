Six-huit from Quebec

Another jig from Pascal Gemme. On Genticorum’s most excellent recording Malins Plaisirs. They play it in a fantastic set of flat-key tunes. I remember being so blown away by the flute playing on this CD that I sent a note to Alex de Grosbois-Garand asking how he managed to play wooden flute in F and D minor and G minor at full speed. He responded saying that he was glad that someone out there appreciated all the practice he’d put in . . .

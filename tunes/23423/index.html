Re: MacLeod Of Mull

A few points to mention.
a - this is a 6/8 march, not a jig, but I accept that “jig” here is the nearest time signature.
b - composed by Pipe Major Donald MacLeod M.B.E., who deserves credit.
c- there are mistake in this transcription in parts 2 and 4.
In part 2, the final 4 bars on the “repeat”, revert to the final 4 bars of the first part.
In part 4, bar 13 is a minor variation , bars 14,15,16 are the same as in previous parts.
I’m afraid this is yet another inaccurate transcription of Scottish bagpipe music on “thesession.org”.
A positive suggestion - could people actually listen to a piper or pipe band playing these tunes before posting them here, please ?
It’s not as though there is a shortage of versions on Youtube :
https://youtu.be/lKJrPqfEL1Y

Re: MacLeod Of Mull

I’ll second what Kenny says. This is probably the most popular Bm 6/8 pipe march you’ll hear in sessions in Scotland. It goes nicely into Heights of Cassino.
I have bar 13 of the fourth part, with its lead in, as c|B2B c>Bc|.

It might be better having the composer as Donald MacLeod, rather than Pipe Major Donald MacLeod M.B.E., so that it links to his other 31 tune on site.

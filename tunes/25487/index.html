One comment

Skipstep

This is another great tune from Donal Lunny transcribed from the CD Triad on which he worked together with Padraig Rynne and Sylvain Barou. I like it especially because of his intention to put “threes across fours”, but also because of the 4 parts developing a melody and chord dynamic together with the unique melody rythm. I heard it first during the Rudolstadt Festival 2015 as part of the Cister special concert and was absolutely flashed.
The bars with changed meters are just needed that the audio is played correctly in some abc players.
Ties into repeats seem to “hang” in some players (the embedded as well) but can be of course noted with ties keeping the meter for the ease of reading.

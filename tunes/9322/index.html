L:1/16
Z:added by Alf warnock alf0@rogers.com - http://members.rogers.com/alf0
Z:repertoire de Jos Bouchard, notation de Marcel Ducharme.

I took the abc from the following website:

http://folktunefinder.com/tune.php?id=5607

Check out Carignan playing this tune:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BjlkRH2wl7Q

I was able to include the pizzicato part and the bouncing of the bow at the end of the secong part in my playing (taken from Carignan).
I start the bouncing between the apical half and third of the bow, and pull it toward the tip while bouncing.
I found it makes clearer sound if the bow almost does not quit the strings to start the bouncing.

Cheers!

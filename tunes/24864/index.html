Paddy Fahy’s No2

We were taught this in John Wynne’s flute class in Drumshanbo’24…goes well with Paddy Fahy’s No 1 hornpipe that he also kindly taught us.

The cBA triplets at the end of the first time bar of the first part, and end of the second time bar of the second part are “optional” and a long G note can be held instead …or you might like another variation to bring you into/back into each part …

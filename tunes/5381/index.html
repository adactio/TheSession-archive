O’Connell’s Farewell To Dublin, X:2

A lovely, haunting, 12/8 march in E dorian. This setting is no repeats like X:1 (but the tune repeats in both parts anyway). Taken from ‘A Fine Selection of Over 200 Irish Traditional Tunes for Sessions’, compiled by David Speers with a Forward by Matt Cranitch.

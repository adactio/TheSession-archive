Katie Morag

A tune by Donald Shaw, written for the Cbeebies show Katie Morag, based off of the books by the same name by Mairi Hedderwick. The C section is not heard in the show, but can be found in the soundtrack that plays in the credits, whereas the B section is heard in the opening theme and the A section is heard in both.

Father Francis Cameron’s

By John A. Campbell (about 1986). Chords by Terry Traub.
Taught at the Strathspey and Reel Society of New Hampshire in 1997 and the Boston Harbor Scottish Fiddle School in 2003.
In the Boston Scottish Fiddle Club list of “Common Tunes for Scottish Sessions”, and
also in Ken Pearlman’s “The Fiddle Tunes of Prince Edward Island”

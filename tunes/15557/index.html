Re: Oscar Wood’s

There are a lot of versions of this. English melodeon players typically play several notes at the same time and learn by ear, so the tune changes almost every time it is passed on.

East Anglian players tend to put in little stops and let air into the tune and these very personal moves vary every time the tune is played but this is what is in my head and is a basic and airless North-West version. North-West players usually go more for sheer power. I have not attempted to show stops or syncopation.

Dog Leap Stairs - Alistair Anderson

Composer Alistair Anderson (Steel Skies)

↳ Posted by geoffwright on December 21st, 2002.

Good call. This is one of the tunes for which I have a copy of the original manuscript, where it is titled simply “3/2 tune in G”. There must have been no changes to the tune between its manuscript form and the album, because it is exactly as transcribed here note for note. The manuscript does have what appears to be harmonies written underneath, and these are as follows:

Harmony 1:

|:dcBA G2Bd cBAG|c2 E4 A2 F4|BAGD B,2DG ABcd|e2 E4 F2 G4:|
|:gabg d2gb a2gf|edcB A2EG F4|GABd g2bg a2gf|efga f2ed g4:|

Harmony 2:

|:d4 B2 G4 E2|ABcA E2cB AGFA|B4 G2 E4 G2|ABcd e2cA G4:|

Dog Leap Stairs

A really engaging little tune, playing nimble games with the 3/2 rhythm in a way that makes it a bit tricky to learn, but isn’t mere wackiness, IMO anyway.

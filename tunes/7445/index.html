Two comments

Iggy and Squiggy

Another great reel from the playing of Jerry O’Sullivan. I believe it was composed by Jerry Holland a Cape Breton fiddler. It goes really well on the uilleann pipes.

↳ Posted by JACKB on July 4th, 2007.

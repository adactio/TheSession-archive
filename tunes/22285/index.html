St. Cecilia’s

A tune played by Órlaith McAuliffe in a FB video in advance of the Patrick O’Keeffe Traditional Music Festival. She said the tune was composed by Nicky McAuliffe of Castleisland, and it was named after the patron saint of music. Video: https://fb.watch/fM21ZUKPNc/

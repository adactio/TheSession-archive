New Plymouth

Written by Colin Farrell and named for the town of New Plymouth, New Zealand. Transcribed in the original key (he says that it’s in G minor, but the few Es are natural so I’ve put it in G dorian.) This transcription is of his 2020 tune a day version, as this tune has evolved a little since the first recording in 2010.

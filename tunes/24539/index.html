Four comments

Home By Bearna

The reel of the same name as the song, with light ornamentation. This is not the famous Gain Ainm reel that Kevin Burke played after Christy Moore sang Home by Bearna (see https://thesession.org/tunes/8104). I follow with Maids of Mitchelstown, also in D dorian mode.

↳ Posted by Shawn Cassady on July 29th, 2024.

The Boróimhe #7

Taken from the 7th movement of Michael Rooney’s “Boróimhe Suite” of 2014, to commemorate the 1000th anniversary of Brian Boru’s victory at Clontarf. Seeing as their are a couple of reels in the suite, I’ve named this one after the movement.

Two comments

Powerhouse

A jig composed probably in the early 1960’s by Willie Joe Power of Wexford, who was a very well known traditional music personality in the 1950’s and through the 1960’s. This little tune was ‘rescued’ by his grand-nephew who luckily got access to some early Radio Eireann recordings of him, playing solo and also with a grouping called ‘The White Leaf’ trio. For those unfamiliar with Willie Joe, he was the banjo-mandolin player who dueted with the great Sean McGuire on the ‘Four star quartet’ record from the early 1960s. The recent digitization of that record has revealed how Willie-Joe was in fact playing note-for-note with Sean McGuire throughout. He is possibly also one of the banjo-mandolin players who Barney McKenna referred to as his inspiration to experiment with re-tuning a tenor banjo to EADG tuning from the then standard ADGC. He also played with the ‘Abbey Tavern’ group in the 1960’s alongside the piper Tommy Reck. Banjo-mandolins were popular in ceili bands in the 40s, 50s and 60s, and Willie-Joe was considered to be among the best of them. (He won the ‘Choice of instrument’ category at the 1957 Dungarvan Fleadh Cheoil). The great Wexford hurler Tim Flood also began at that time playing banjo-mandolin, later moving to EADG tenor in the early 1960s and winning the same ‘Choice of Instrument’ All-Ireland on the re-tuned tenor banjo in 1965, and senior duet with Wexford Fiddler Gerry Forde that same year.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on June 28th, 2022.

Powerhouse, X:2

This more session-friendly version of the jig, as played by Willie Joe’s grand-nephew, tenor banjo (and banjo mandolin!) player Ciaran Power from Drinagh, just on the Piercestown side of Wexford.

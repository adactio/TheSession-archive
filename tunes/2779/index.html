Feeding the Birds

Posted in response to a request from “cluaintarbh” (do I detect a Rafferty fan? :)

Feeding the Birds was made up by Mike Rafferty “with the help of Mary and the birds outside”. Recorded by the Rafferty’s on The Dangerous Reel and also by Kevin Crawford.

I think Kevin Crawford plays the beginning part single but I have it as a full 16 bars.

↳ Posted by LH on April 8th, 2004.

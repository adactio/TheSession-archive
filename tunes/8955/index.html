Ril na Glaise

This comes from the playing of banjo-player Marcus Moloney from Co.Clare. It’s on a recording I posted earlier this week made in 1993. All the players were Under-18 All-Ireland champions. A great reel which I’ve never come across anywhere else. Deserves to be better known. Thanks to Malcolm Reavell for the original transcription.

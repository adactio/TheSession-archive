Vals from Orsa

I learned this in the 1970s from a wonderful LP, Folk fiddling from Sweden: traditional fiddle tunes from Dalarna played by Björn Ståbi & Ole Hjorth (1970, Nonesuch H-72033). These guys added beautiful second fiddle parts, some of which I transcribed at the time. Main tune matches Dafydd’s of 9 yrs ago except a note or two; no energy at the moment to abc the 2nd fiddle. But I WILL upload another 2-part tune from that album - Polska from Enviken - if it’s not on thesession yet. I’m sure it’s really British/Irish…… ;-)

↳ Posted by David Hughes (deceased 4 May 2025) on July 2nd, 2013.

Re: Waltz From Orsa

Hi Malcolm - I notified Jeremy that this was already here as “Waltz From Orsa”, the title given to it by “Buttons & Bows”. I got this tune from John Skelton when he was with the second incarnation of “Shegui”, mid 1980s. We used to play it with “Ceolbeg”. We were playing a festival in Dijon, France around that time and met some Swedish fiddlers. We only knew 2 Swedish tunes, so sprung this on them. “Ah, MacLean’s Waltz”, we were told. Perhaps we mis-heard “Macklin’s” - see above.
John C. was round tonight and gave me a book. Well done, a mighty effort there. Many thanks.

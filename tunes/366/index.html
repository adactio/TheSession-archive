The Hound of Ulster

Our bouzouki player picked this up somewhere and passed it along at our local session. I wish I knew more about the tune--especially who composed it (since it sounds quite modern to my ear).

You can avoid those triplets in the second half by simply playing |cBcB ABAG| etc.

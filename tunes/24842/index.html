Two comments

Planxty Gerard McDonnell

This is an original composition by Dan Possumato, to honor the memory of a friend and former bandmate Gerard McDonnell. Gerard, age 38, perished along with 10 others on Pakistan’s K2 in August of 2008. K2 is the second highest mountain in the world and generally considered the most dangerous to climb. Gerard had just become the first Irishman to reach that peak’s summit. His body remains buried on the mountain. A YouTube video of the song with pictures of Gerard can be seen by clicking this link: https://youtu.be/FpQeuldXD2o

Musicians on the video are Dan on button accordion, Séamus McGuire on fiddle and Stan O’Beirne on piano.

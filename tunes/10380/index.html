Five comments

Another forgotten gem from O’Neill’s…

I found this beauty in O’Neill’s 1,001 Gems (#601 in the “reels” section). As a good friend of mine commented, “a little quirky, but solid”. I found that it needed a slight amount of “tweaking” , but I decided to post it as it appears in the book, so as not to offend. Here’s my take on it; feel free to let me know if it makes more sense/ sounds better (or worse) my way:

T:Johnny’s Welcome Home
M:4/4
L:1/8
R:reel
K:Ador
AE ~E2 cE ~E2 | GEAE G2 ED | (3EFG AB cded | ~c3 d e2 fg |
~a3 f ~g3 e | dBGB ABcd | (3efg ed cABG |1 AGEG ABcB :|2
AGEG A2 ea || agea agea | agef g2 fg | agbg aged |
~c3 d e2 fg |~a3 f ~g3 e | dBGB ABcd | (3efg ed cABG
|1 AGEG A2 ea :|2 AGEG ABcB ||

It seemed begging to be played as a double reel, rather than the single version in the book. As usual, enjoy!

Timmy’s Place

This tune comes from Lúnasa’s recording “Lá Nua” and is written by Cillian Vallely !
It’s the second tune of “Doc Holliday’s” set.
This tune is a reel but Lúnasa played it not very fast.
;-)

Not a Hop Jig

This tune is definitely not a reel and also not a Hop Jig.
It is in 6/8 but with 12 34 56 timing, so it has the timing of a mazurka.

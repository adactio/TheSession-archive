Five comments

Wes And Maggie’s Ceili Croft

A Leo McCann composition. This is the version on The Landing Step by Gráda. The dots for the original version are available here: http://www.musicscotland.com/leomccann/compositions.htm.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on July 2nd, 2006.

I’ve altered the abc a bit to include a nice variation in the repeated bar 7 of the A-part which appears in the original. The Grada setting simply repeats like this:

A3c B2AB|cFFE F2EF|AFFE F2EF|ABcA BAcB|
A2cA B2AB|cFFE F2EF|AFFE F2EF|1 ABcA BAcB:|2 ABcA Bcef||

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on July 2nd, 2006.

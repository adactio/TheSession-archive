The Changelings

The Changelings is a waltz, which was written by Mayo flute player and multi-instrumentalist Emer Mayock. It was recorded on her album “Merry Bits of Timber” in 1996. She wrote this for a film made in the early 1990’s about the idea of humans being taken into the fairy world.

This is a jig by Andy Thornburn which I learned when I was part of this trad ensemble.
I saw that it was not on this website, so I decided to put it on. I am not sure if this is suitable for this website, I think this is a Scottish tune, but the traditional music of Scotland is pretty much the same as Irish music.

Re: Jig Of The Clan Beag

This tune was composed by Andy Thorburn for a play concerning US military bases in Scotland. The Clan Beag are “the little people” who appear in times of need, and they “saved the village of Bunnilidh.”

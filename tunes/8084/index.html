Dance! Dance!

A traditional Jewish tune in 2/4. Last 3 bars replaced with Coda when you’ve played it enough times through. Its supposed to get faster and faster the more times you play it through!!!

Dance! Dance!, X:2

Key signature should be A phrygian but that’s not an option here. Time signature should be 2/4, as has been said, but that’s not changeable. Chords added and Cs naturalised in the second part. Different coda. This from an unknown published source including a nice picture of two people in the Jewish Market, Whitechapel, East London. :)

Re: Dance! Dance!

The tonic is A and the second note in the scale is only a semitone up, so no. Second part is in F major but I’d say first and third parts were in the scale A Bb C# D E F G A = (in semitones) 1, 3, 1, 2, 1, 2, 2. That’s phrygian dominant (sorry, not just phrygian) or Freygish. Note the chords- it’s definitely in A.

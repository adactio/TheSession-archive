The Coming Home

A jig composed by Ann Marie Hayes, daughter of the late Paddy Hayes from Piercestown in Co. Wexford. Ann Marie is a wonderful accordionist also and very kindly included two of her compositions with titles related to her story with her dad for inclusion in my collection of Co. Wexford tunes , recently published. I included tunes in the collection composed by Wexford diaspora musicians such as Ann Marie, John Whelan, Kevin Rowesome etc when the tune title connected it to Wexford through placename or person. Ann Marie tells me she titled the tune ‘The Coming Home’ in reference to the many nights in London, where the children would be eagerly awaiting Paddy coming home after a night’s music in the pubs and clubs where he played at that time.

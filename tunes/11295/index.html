Morris version?

Appears to be the Morris version of the tune, variations of which is played as a hornpipe etc. Not sure about those big leaps. Morris tune

Submitted a version based on Martin Carthy’s and the Sherborne morris tune. Have listed a Dmaj as the Cs are sharp, it is Edorian. Though the first couple of bars of the B section has a rather excellent D# and A#, as if it has modulated to Bmaj?

The C section is the B section played at half speed, I think the morris men must do something with their hankies at this point. The C section is also a bit crooked, though I may not have got the timing quite right.

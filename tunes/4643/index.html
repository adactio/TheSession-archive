Four comments

Ukrainian Polka

I’ve known this tune for donkey’s years. Got it from an old recording called “Second Generation” by “The Paddy Rocker Band” which featured Peter Ostroushko on mandolin and fiddle, and an aglo concertina player, backed by guitar and bass. This is the name they gave the tune.
Bars 1 to 8 are the intro only. Bars 57 to 64 are for the outro only. I usually play each of the three parts twice.
Intro - AABBCC repeat x 3 - Outro. Play it at a lively pace. It’s great on fiddle.

↳ Posted by *Davy Rogers on June 28th, 2005.

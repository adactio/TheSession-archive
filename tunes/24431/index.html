Re: Reel Du Mécanicien

Another great Richard Forest tune. He composed a lot of tunes for the band Domino while that band still was a trio. I learned this Reel from Sabin in about 2013/14. In that workshop he wasn’t playing the triplets you have transcribed. I assume the general level of ability of accordion players improved when you were in his workshop! I should play it more often.

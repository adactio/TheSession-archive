Two comments

Maple Leaf Two-Step

This is a tune by Ward Allen (1924-1965), a Canadian fiddler best known for the tune Maple Sugar. He recorded Maple Leaf Two-Step in 1953. I transcribed it from that recording which can be found on YouTube - https://youtu.be/we0xXM1yrSw .

I have heard other versions that repeat the sections (AABB) but Allen plays them sequentially (ABAB) so I have transcribed it that way. I made no attempt to score his ornamentations or variations.

↳ Posted by schriltz on May 22nd, 2017.

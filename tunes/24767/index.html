Three comments

Molly Apple Pye

Learnt while at a session while away with my band, I liked the sound of it but couldn’t find it here on The Session! I believe the player originally learnt it from Leveret who have a lovely version of this tune.
Not sure if this is the original key and I know very few details so if anyone has any more info, I’d be keen to hear about it.

↳ Posted by stephanelli on September 14th, 2024.

Molly Apple Pye, X:2

Leveret’s version, just enough differences to make it worth posting a version 2.

They in turn got it from the Mss of William Winter, a shoemaker and musician in 19th Century Somerset - the version in that Mss, published by Halsway Manor in 2007, which is X:3 !

↳ Posted by sfmans on September 14th, 2024.

Molly Apple Pye, X:3

X:3 from William Winter Mss published as ‘William Winter’s Quantocks Tune Book’ edited by Geoff Woolfe, published by the Halsway Manor Society, 2007. It’s in 2/4 there, but the TheSession abc editor won’t let me change the time signature.

Rob Harbron was involved in recording the accompanying CD to the published book, so maybe that’s how the tune made it’s way to Leveret …

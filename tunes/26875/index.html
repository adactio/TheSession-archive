Three comments

Port Ita

Track two 2nd tune on “What’s Seldom is Beautiful” , a recent release from Jacqui Martin and Lorraine Sweeney.
I bought it in Tubberurry as I need more fiddle and flute in my life.

Both tunes in track 2 composed by Vincent McGrath from Rossport county Mayo.

Lovely album

↳ Posted by Pat Higgins on September 27th, 2026.

Port Ita, X:2

A lovely jig. This second version is my interpretation from the record. The album title is not quite correct in the above note - it’s called ‘What’s seldom is wonderful’.

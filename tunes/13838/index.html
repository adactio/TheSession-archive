Two comments

Mary Do It Again

This is on Bonnie Rideout’s Celtic Circles CD. I am new to fiddling and new to Scottish music and just fell in love with this tune, which is part of a larger 3-song set.

↳ Posted by Christal on September 14th, 2014.

Mary Do It Again

Bonnie got the tune from a book, Kerr’s Fourth Collection of Merry Melodies (c1870s), where it is in the ‘Irish Jigs’ section.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on September 14th, 2014.

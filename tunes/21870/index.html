Re: The Broken Chanter

Patrick McLaurin over at Dunsire managed to fill in what my memory couldn’t - the 3rd and 4th parts Donald has here are originally a second tune called The Warming of the Fingers, by Roddy Campbell, an apt title. The title I couldn’t remember was The Old Chanter, which is what it appears under in Glen and other places.

https://www.ceolsean.net/content/Logan/Book03/Book03%2030.pdf

Not all that many GHB players play the four part setting, as that fourth part is a technical challenge for anyone. Patrick also says it appears on John Wilson (Glasgow)’s World’s Greatest Pipers recording with four parts titled “Old Chanter”, and I suspect that might well be the source of the four part version.

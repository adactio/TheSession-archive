Tom Clancy’s #2

3rd reel on the first track of “Faoi Bhlath” by Ciaran Somers, Dave Sheridan and Nicolas Quemener, one of the best recordings I bought last year. It reminds me a bit of one of Michael Tubridy’s reels on The“Eagle’s Whistle”, “Gone For His Tea”, I think it was called.
Will Harmon has already posted the first of the 2 “Tom Clancy” rels on “Faoi Bhlath”,but in the key of “D”. The lads have transposed it to “G”.
It’s a very simple tune, and I thought it might be here already, but if so, I couldn’t find it.

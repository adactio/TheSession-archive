The Blaggard

I was asked by a friend if I had the dots for this at the Jimmy McHugh weekend in Glasgow last month. It’s an original tune (I know, I know) but one that’s been around a few years now and is starting to get played here and there. Written after a comment from a concertina player late one night: “God Almighty, that was Blaggard Stuff!”. I’ve never been able to work out if that was meant as a compliment or not…

Re: Paddy Fahey’s

When you listen to Paddy Fahey playing this on the Maria Holorhan recordings it’s unclear in parts what Paddy’s doing bar-wise: whether he’s extending certain bars by a half-beat or something. Having listened to it a few times I think Paddy’s just making a little timing trip-up here and there and starting one or two bars late … but can’t be certain.
Doesn’t help that Paddy stops before playing the B part. At one point I even started thinking that Maria H had barred the whole thing wrong, mistaking pick-up notes for the start of the part, but then realised that was totally wrong. Anyway, all Maria H’s recordings are on YouTube, try tapping your foot along:

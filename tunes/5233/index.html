This is a Dutch tune called ‘’Blauw garen en koperdraad‘’. In the Netherlands it is played quite often but not exactly the way it is written down here. And most people play it in Dminor. A very nice tune, and after hearing it once or twice anyone can join in because it’s very easy and catchy.

↳ Posted by John Offord on August 12th, 2023.

Re: Bourree

The earliest version of this tune is from Terpsichore (Michael Pretorius Cantor) written in the 16th century, but possibly incorporating even older Melodies. The tune is the top voice in La Bouree. It showed up somewhat later in Playfords Dancing Master as the Parsons Farewell.

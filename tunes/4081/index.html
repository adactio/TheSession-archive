Tom’s Delight (hornpipe)

This tune was written by East Galway concertina player Claire Keville and named after her father. A very new but typical East Galway tune, I believe.

↳ Posted by slainte on September 23rd, 2006.

The B part has a second ending that differs from the first. The last four bars are:
GECE FDED|CDEC F2ED|CDEC FDB,C|DCCB, C2:|

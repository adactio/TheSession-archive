Two comments

MacIntosh Jon

This reel I made in about 1985 when I was first learning to play B/C accordion.
I’ve since switched to C#/D box, so it took me some time to relearn my own tune on
that instrument. It is another of my tunes that’s actually played in our local session
from time to time.

The first couple of bars remind me of the trumpet that sounds at the start of a
horse race, so I wanted a name with a horse in it. Our local band Traonach recorded
the tune and my band member Scott Whitham suggested naming the tune after his
wife’s champion Hunter-Jumper MacIntosh Jon.

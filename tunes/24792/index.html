The Humours Of Harry

The second of the two Tang Polkas written by Patrick J. Davey in April 2024. The tunes are dedicated to Geraldine, Fred, Wilma and Harry.

↳ Posted by Andrew Newland on September 24th, 2024.

Re: The Humours Of Harry

Geraldine McLynn of Tang, Co Westmeath is an exceptionally gifted fiddle teacher and player and is the heart and soul of traditional music in her locality. She encourages musicians both young and old and ‘idir eatarthu’ and is an utterly effervescent presence in whatever company she creates or joins.
Patrick gifted these polkas to Geraldine as a thank you for her attentiveness and care at a time when both were needed - falling ill when away from one’s base is not a very pleasant experience. Luckily, all was swiftly sorted and the patient recovered in no time at all, bail ó Dhia air.
Wilma and Fred are Geraldine’s adorable rescue dogs, and Harry is a very bossy crow and demanding crow who doesn’t take no for an answer! Mind you, no is not a word that Geraldine uses very often anyway. She is positive and inspiring and these beautiful polkas are a perfect tribute to her. Please enjoy Patrick Davey’s Tang Polkas - and here’s to Geraldine, Harry, Wilma, Fred and to all her pupils ( & animals) past and present

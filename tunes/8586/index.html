Star of Brittany

A gorgeous continental-style waltz by Conor Keane from “Joad De Deus”. My favorite tune this year.
He plays it ABCABCAB with Coda.
He plays it on box so get practising the third part. You can also watch him playing on utube (bit not this particular tune - yet).

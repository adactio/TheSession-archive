Two comments

An Cailin Og Deas

This an old Sean Nos air, sung by Maire Aine Ni Dhonnchadha on her album Deora Aile (100% solo sean nos singing), which Alan Stivell included in his 1977 album Interbationnal Tour.

It must be played very slowly of course.

This is a poor rendition of the actual tune, a naked squeletum of the song , but is deserves to be present in The Session.

I selected A Mylodian to have the two sharps (F and C) buand because the main Note is “A” but I am note sure wether this is correct or not. My skills are very limited in his topic.

I can play together with Alan Stivell air when playing this score with my Bb tin whistle .

Any updated and more accurate version matchin more closely Maire Aine Ni Dhonnchadha singing is welcome

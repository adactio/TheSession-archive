Stoney Lake

A reel composed by Canadian fiddle player Donnell Leahy, which I first heard played in a set by pianist Mac Morin in this video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q2G09sjKxwI I especially like the middle section, which almost has a bit of a jazzy flair to it.

Paddy Fahey’s

Pretty sure this isn’t on thesession. I went through all the PF tunes a number of years ago and it wasn’t then. I presently checked the reels in G Dor and G min and didn’t find it so I think I’m safe to add.

I found this on Saileog Ní Cheannabháin’s album “Roithlean” (Tr 3.1). She plays it on piano in F Dorian.

I will also add Mari Holohan’s version, Paddy fahey’s Reel No. 22.

Paddy Fahey’s, X:3

Setting with no ornaments and in a more session key (E Dorian).

This is the same as X:1 but removing the ornamentation and transposing it to E Dorian.

Wonderful tune, I didn’t know this one. I was curious to see which note the alteration was going to fall in when the tune is played in a more session key. I’m happy to see that it is a D Sharp that is between two Es, making the middle hole very attainable for flutes, tin whistles and pipes.

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on June 28th, 2022.

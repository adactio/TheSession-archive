I posted this not totally sure of its name. Written by Gerry O’Connor of 4 men and a canine. It’s on the Long Roads CD, with a song on track 2 called Planet Ways. I don’t have the Cd cover myself to confirm, just an audiotaped copy.

Can anyone confirm ?

Although it’s recorded on the CD in E mix, I find it better on my instruments ( flute, whistle, concer) in A, so that’s how I’ve written it. I think I’m the only person in Australia playing it, and am trying to work it into my local session repertoire.

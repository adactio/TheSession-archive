Two comments

Zolloko San Martinak

This is not a polka, it is a tune from Basque Country Traditional Music. It is a composition of the trikitixa player Kepa Junkera. Trikitixa is the two row box played in Basque Country.

This video shows the melody pretty well:

https://youtu.be/nV9aKUTiwEg

If course Kepa plays the tune himself, he adds a good deal of Basque style of ornamentation. It is the second tune in this video:

https://youtu.be/q_6ll77dqRg

This is Kepa playing the tune with an orchestra:

https://youtu.be/9e9A-oXpB7k

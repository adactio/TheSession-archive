Andy Conroy’s

A tune from a Roscommon piper, famous for his exceptional staccato or “tight” technique on the chanter, which was the only part of the pipes he bothered to play, just one of a whole laundry list of eccentictries that made him equally loved as a character. This is a composition, I’m guessing. A profile of Andy appeared in the music journal Ceol early in the 70s, and was reprinted in the Breandan Breathnach writing anthology “The Man and His Music,” with transcriptions of a handful or so of tunes; some novel ones were simply labeled “Conroy #1” etc; another was “Colonel Glenn.” The numbered tunes actually had names like “Sputnik,” Andy had a fascination with the then nascent space program, but Breandan wanted more traditional names for the tunes…by the same token this hornpipe I believe Andy terms “Genghis Khan” on one tape…but I’ve always just named it after him. His music inspired a complete duality, pipers like Mick O’Brien and Gay McKeon loved his playing, others can’t handle more than a bar of it. I’ve uploaded a couple of items to YouTube if anyone wants to hear him.

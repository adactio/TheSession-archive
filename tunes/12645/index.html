Three comments

Skye Air / Unknown Air

Thanks must go to Nigel Gatherer for this one! I first heard it off the Blazin’ Fiddles album “Magnificent Seven”, played by Iain MacFarlane, before I purchased “Affric” by Duncan Chisholm and came across it once more, titled “Unknown Air” in that instance.

I failed to find any information on its source myself, except that it was old! Hence this discussion https://thesession.org/discussions/31609 and Nigel’s excellent answer:

The tune is from the Patrick MacDonald Collection (1784), where it is given no title, but it’s annotated “Skye Air” - like many of the tunes in that collection, which is why there are a few tunes called “Skye Air” or “Skye Dance”.

Setting 2 above comes from Nigel…
T:Skye Air
S:Book, Patrick MacDonald’s Collections (1784; facsimile, 2000)
Z:Nigel Gatherer

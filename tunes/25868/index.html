Two comments

Molly’s Ballies

A nice, simple reel composed by Donald Knox in 2007 after his collie cross, who “really did like a ball.” Donald, who lives in the Scottish Borders, is primarily a guitarist (an excellent one) but he also plays mandolin, and has composed a number of really good tunes.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on August 27th, 2025.

Molly’s Ballies, X:2

A version of the above tune for the penny whistle. Apologies to the composer!

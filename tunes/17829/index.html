Re: Stoidhle Neill Ghobha

The title means “Niel Gow’s Style” and refers to the famed fiddler’s playing style. The tune of that title was composed by Captain Simon Fraser of Knockie and published in his 1874 collection “Airs and Melodies..” alongside another of his tributes to Gow, “Caledonia’s Wail for Niel Gow” (https://thesession.org/tunes/12229).

However, the tune posted above isn’t “Niel Gow’s Style”.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on March 21st, 2019.

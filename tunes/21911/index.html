Jenny Of The Rolling Hills

My daughter Jenny has been running our folk club - The Rolling Hills Folk Club - through Zoom since lockdown. When she reached her 30th birthday in May 2022 we asked renowned folk singer/musician Brian Peters (a family friend) to write a tune for her. Whilst out cycling he composed this tune.
My friend Peter Baston translated the tune into ABC and we are very grateful to them both.
Ron, Ros and Jenny

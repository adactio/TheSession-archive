Six comments

The Rising Sun

Leveret’s (slight) variation on a tune from Daniel Wright’s 1715 manuscript “An Extraordinary Collection of Pleasant and Merry Humours etc.”. The first bar of the A section is played f2 fe df on repeats (but I don’t know a pretty way of showing it).

The Rising Sun, X:2

The original, in the key of Fmaj, from the dancing master collection of D. Wright (1713) with a 6/4 time signature, so it maybe should be listed here as a 3/2 hornpipe.
I did try to add it in its original form, but it somehow defaulted to the original key signature of ’3/4, so it’s duplicated, sorry.

↳ Posted by John Rowlands on March 6th, 2025.

Re: The Rising Sun

A 3/2 hornpipe is three groups of two crotchets (quarter notes). 6/4 is two groups of three, so listing the tune as a 3/2 hornpipe would be misleading and inaccurate.

John, you could put [M:6/4] at the start of your first line of ABC text.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on March 6th, 2025.

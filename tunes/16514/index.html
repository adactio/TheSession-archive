Orla’s Reel

This reel was made around 1999 or possibly 2000 by Paul Ruane of Leeds (Mayo) and Newcastle.
It’s a well structured tune and has recently been recorded on Tom McElvogue’s and Karen Tweed’s newly released album Luckpenny. It also features on Wooden Flute Obsessions III disc two, track one. NH

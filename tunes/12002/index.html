Nice one Smash, and generous…

If you really do understand and read the dots, standard notation, you should have been aware of conventions that exist in notation in general, especially with traditional music. For one, see Smash’s transcription, setting lines out, on average, in four bar phrases. In this case, Smash’s transcription, four lines, the A-part in two lines and then the B-part in two lines.

There are plenty of resources online to help raising ones understanding of ABC notation, and it doesn’t take a lot of time. And, if you really do have a hand on standard notation, it’s a very easy transition, as the basic understanding, the foundations for ABC notation, will already be there.

Show some basic consideration, always appreciated, and bother to read the FAQs here, nothing unreasonable there and a quick 5 minute read. If you can’t hold yourself back and just have to share your inspirations, then consider the following option ~

The Ewe With The Crooked Horn

This is from the CD “The Ewe With the Crooked Horn” by Colm Gannon, Jesse Smith, and John Blake. This tune is not the same as the strathspey “The Ewe Wi’ The Crookit Horn” (https://thesession.org/tunes/5610) although the title is similar. This tune appears as #918 in (the Dover reprint of) George Petrie’s 19th century collection of Irish music. Although identified as a reel in the title given there, Petrie apparently added the note “hornpipe” to the score, and it is listed as a hornpipe in the above-mentioned CD. This transcription reflects how the tune is played in that recording, which is slightly different from the original in Petrie’s collection.

Re: The Ewe With The Crooked Horn

Although the tune in Petrie’s collection is in A dorian, the above-mentioned recording has it in a lower key - 3 half-steps down - F# dorian. For this transcription I put it back in A dorian to make it easier to play.

Re: The Ewe With The Crooked Horn

The tune is a Scottish reel. Reels can be classified into 2 groups, those which end in 3 crotchets and those that do not . The former can be played as hornpipes.

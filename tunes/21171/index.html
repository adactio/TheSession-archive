Gan Ainm

This tune entitled “Polka” (SG1.060) was transcribed in Book 1 of the Grier MSS (c1880s) of Gortletteragh, Co Leitrim.

↳ Posted by ConorW on October 28th, 2021.

Re: Gan Ainm

There’s more than 1 tune in there. Lines 5 and 6 are certainly the first part of a polka recorded by Julia and Billy Clifford, “The Upperchurch Polka”, but it’s played in G, not D.
Would anbody here play this through as transcribed above as a single tune ?

↳ Posted by Kenny on October 28th, 2021.

Re: Gan Ainm

I did further research on this tune and it would seem that this could be two different polkas incorrectly combined by the scribe, see original tune at http://grier.itma.ie/book-one#?c=0&m=0&s=0&cv=25&z=-241.8168%2C0%2C2981.6335%2C1806.
As you said, the third part in this Grier polka corresponds to the first part of the Sliabh Luachra tune “Upperchurch Polka”. Let’s call this part “A”. There is a four part tune “Sultan’s Polka” composed by Charles D‘Albert in the format ABCD. Our “A” part corresponds to this A part. Grier’s fourth part seems to correspond to the Sultan’s C part, while Upperchurch’s second part is either a faulty version of D’Albert’s B part or a different part altogether. I’ll post up D’Albert’s original tune for comparison.

↳ Posted by ConorW on October 28th, 2021.

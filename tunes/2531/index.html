Waves of Rush

It’s written by Aidan O’Rourke and recorded on Tabache’s album, Waves of Rush.

It’s not in Fmajor - it’s in a minor key - but I couldn’t work out which one… and Fmaj seems to work for the midi file. If anybody knows the correct key then post it please. In the meantime I think I’ll take some theory lessons…

Here’s my transcription of the version on the Tabache album. A roll sign denotes rapid triplet type thing because I find it easier to read than N/N/N. The last bar of the 3rd part is played |FGDC D3| by one fiddle and |GADC D3| by the other.

X: 1
T: The Waves Of Rush
C: Aidan O’Rourke
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Dmin
e|efed efa2-|afec deAe|efed efae|fece d3:|
|:A|cdeA ~A2ae|fedf ecdA|cdeA ~A2ae|fece d3:|
|:A|FGAD DdAD|BDAd BGAD|FGAD dDAD|GADC D3:|
e|fAeA fAeA|fAea fdeA|fAAf eAAe|fece d3e|
fAeA fAeA|fAea fdeA|fA~A2 eA~A2|fece d3||

Four comments

Wideford Hill

This is a jig (traditional) from Orkney arranged by Phil Rowe and included on the Nottingham Folk Music
Database. I don’t think it is that familiar in Orkney. No connection with waltz given the same name.

In places it really reminds me of the nursery rhyme “Ride a Cock Horse to Banbury Cross”.

Re: Wideford Hill

A fab jig which I posted on thesession.org 3 years ago. (wasn’t v good at abcnotation then so I cut and pasted it from abcnotation.com. Wideford Hill’s a popular viewpoint on Mainland Orkney and there is quite a big chambered cairn on the side of it )not far from Kirkwall, the capital of Orkney). The cairn is some 4,000 to 5,000 years old. You can see Wideford Hill very clearly from all around Orkney mainland.

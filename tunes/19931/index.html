Jemmy Duffy’s Barndances 1, 2 And 3

From the playing of Jim Hoy, accordion, on Hidden Fermanagh Vol. 2. Jemmy/Jimmy Duffy was Eddie Duffy’s brother and a highly respected melodeon player.
#3 is a version of Moneymusk.

Two comments

Cheers For The Nod

I wrote this reel in Dmajor/Bminor on the Uilleann pipes as a thank you to the person who said I should take them up. A great piece of advice.

I prefer the accompaniment in the first part to be based around D major, switching to B minor in the second part. After a repetition or two I like to build up the tension in tune by using minor chords in both parts and then releasing the tension with the D major chords in the first part again.

^D & ^G

Stop complaining about the accidentals ~ they were Seán’s, and for you winds it’s just a slight move of a finger, how you place it, no tough thing… Oh yes, the one printed notation has that 7th bar of the A-part with a roll ~

Low ~ | ~A,3 B, DEFG | ~

High ~| ~A3 F D2 E/F/G | ~

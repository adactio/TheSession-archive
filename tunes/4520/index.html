Larry Redican’s Hornpipe

A friend just learned this off Jerry O’Sullivan’s cd The Invasion, so I sat down and listened to Jerry’s piping to come up with this transcription. Works well on fiddle, and makes a great banjo tune.

You can also do the first measure of the A part as: |DEFD EFDG|

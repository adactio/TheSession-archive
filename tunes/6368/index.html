“Y Gelynnen”

Y = the
Celynnen = Holly / Holly Tree (both translations exist)
~ after the ‘Y’ the ‘C’ mutates to ‘G’…

Y ~ ‘uh(r)’
Gel-yn-nen ~ ‘gal’ - ‘un’ - ‘nan’

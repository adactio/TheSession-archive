Alister McAlister, X:3

Version in G minor that I learnt from some decrepit mid 20th century sheet music my granny has (Kerr’s Collection of Reels and Strathspeys for Pianoforte). This setting is written for piano but I find it sounds nice on fiddle.

Allister McAllister => Monaghan Twig

Fun fact #2: changing the key of the second setting to Gmix produces The Monaghan Twig or The Tinker’s Apron (very similar-sounding versions thereof) :-)
https://thesession.org/tunes/1070

This strathspey seems to have evolved into several Irish tunes in Sliabh Luachra and elsewhere!

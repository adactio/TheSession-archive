Two comments

Gan Ainm

Found this tune on a sheet of paper inherited in 2019 from Ceilidh accordionist and composer, Charlie Jemmet from Christchurch, New Zealand. It was in a bracket for the dance “Strip the Willow”.
The tunes were:
1. The Muckin‘ o’ Geordie’s Byre
2. This unnamed tune
3. The Cock of the North (Chase me Charlie)

Kelly’s Corner

A reel composed by Paddy Furlong. It was recently published in ‘The Wexford Tunebook’, along with other compositions of Paddy’s. paddy is a member of the Kildare-based group. ‘Push for Porter’ but is originally from Clonroche, in County Wexford. The title refers to looking for a tune to link ‘Fr. Kelly’s Reel’ and ‘The Corner House’ and ending up composing a tune, which then reminded him of his motorbike days as a student in UCD.

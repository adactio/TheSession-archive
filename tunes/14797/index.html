Two comments

The Bell

An adaptation of the traditional strathspey “Heater Bells” as a reel, write and played by Johnny Cunningham in the first Relativity album. Sooooo cool!!! I write two type of “A” structure to show some variations that he did, the “B” part it’s played always like I write.

↳ Posted by Mr G. Cunningham on October 13th, 2015.

The Bell, X:2

This setting is as the group The Iron Horse played it on their 1993 album ‘Thro’ Water, Earth & Stone’.

Merrily Kissed The Quaker

There is already a tune of this name posted https://thesession.org/tunes/70. The settings there are mostly based on the tune that Planxty popularised or settings in D of a related tune (from the North?) This present tune has a first part that is similar but the the other two parts are quite different and therefore it should be considered a different tune. This setting is based on the playing of Denis McMahon, Timmy O‘Connor and Paudy Scully. A similar setting is in Johnny O’Leary’s book. The Chieftains played this slide on Chieftains 3; a transcription of their playing, by turophile, is included as X:2 on https://thesession.org/tunes/70, but here is a better home for it.

The Quaker (Merrily Kissed The Quaker, Sliabh Luachra setting)

This is how we played this slide with Polca an Rí. I think I fused this setting together after playing with John Walsh and hearing Denis Murphy’s version of the tune in the archives. Then we transposed it to A… and recorded in G#.

https://sliabhmoscow.bandcamp.com/track/mrs-galvin-s-the-quaker-jig-single-jig

In Sliabh Luachra, the session-standard Merrily Kissed The Quaker is sometimes referred to as ‘the Planxty Quaker’ while this version – to the older generation – is just the Quaker. I like to think of this setting as the slide version of the tune while ‘the Planxty Quaker’ is more of a single jig. Try playing them both at full speed for the sets and you’ll get it!

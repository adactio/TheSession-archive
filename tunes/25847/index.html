The Birnam Oak

A great reel by Elizabeth Davidson-Blythe, written during and named in honour of the annual Niel Gow Festival in Dunkeld and Birnam, Perthshire, Scotland. The Birnam Oak is an iconic tree thought to be around 1000 years old and deemed to be a survivor of the great Birnam Wood mentioned in Shakespeare’s Macbeth.

Re: I Rowed Up In A Dory

@Chellam: Bars 7 and 15 (counting from the first full bar) of your transcription contain too many beats. According to the Harry Hibbs recording, they should be | BGBd cAFA |. My preference would be to notate this tune in 2/4 (choosing ‘Polka’ as the tune type - even though it is really a march), but each to ther own.

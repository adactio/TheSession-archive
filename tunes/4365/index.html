Din Tarrant’s

This tune appears in “Johnny O’Leary of Sliabh Luachra”, the Breandan Breathnach compilation edited by Terry Moylan (Lilliput, 1994). It is tune 250, and is called Din Tarrant’s (3). It is one of 5 Din Tarrant polkas in the collection.

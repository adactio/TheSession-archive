Bolt The Door, X:2

From the playing of Paddy O’Brien (Offaly) in his tune collection, volume three.
It’s interesting to see how he develops the tune from the bare bones O’Neill’s
version given by Ben. The second part has two different strains and Paddy thinks of
this tune as a three part single jig.

I like to notate single jigs in 12/8 to better indicate the phrasing. That means
it could be called a slide, but it is played at a somewhat more stately tempo.

Paddy says that he got the tune from the fiddle music of James Kelly, so
maybe it was James that mostly developed the tune.

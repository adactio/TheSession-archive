Re: The Munster

This reel derives from the Stanford Petrie collection, where there is a “Munster Reel”, No. 890, which Petrie got “from Mr. P. Joyce”. Traditional versions of it appear under various titles, “The Dream of Home”, “The Maids in the Meadow”, “Michael Relihan’s”, or just “One of Relihan’s”. See: https://thesession.org/tunes/3326

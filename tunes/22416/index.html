Gwrachod Llanddona, X:5

Been playing a lot of Huw’s tunes recently. He died a few months ago, and as I’ve been in contact with a bunch of northern musicians, so these tunes have had a bit of an airing down here. Here it is, Swansea style. We first picked it up from Ceri and Catrin Ashton, flute a fiddle players from Conwy, a decade or more ago. It may be worth noting that the high g notes in bars 9 & 13 are played very flat. Don’t know how to do that in abc. On the flute, it’s just a question of hardly picking the right hand index finger up. Goes nice with Byth Adra. Nice work, Huw

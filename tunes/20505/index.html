The Towers Of Mt. Cootha

Lovely reel composed by banjo player Liam Fitzpatrick, from Brisbane,
Queensland, Australia. Liam says "Mt. Cootha is a mountain in Brisbane
to the west of the city. The towers are the masts for the local TV stations
and the lights can be seen flashing for miles at night".

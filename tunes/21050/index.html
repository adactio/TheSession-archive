Champion Of The Seas, X:2

This hornpipe was originally written for the bagpipes by Pipe Major William MacDonald of Golspie (1850-1907). It commemorates the “Champion of the Seas” clipper ship, built in Boston and launched in 1854. She was a passenger ship for 14 years, primarily Liverpool to Melbourne, Australia. The Champion set a record for the fastest 24 hours run (465 nautical miles) which stood for 130 years. After 9 years shipping cargo, and in disrepair, the Champion foundered off Cape Horn in 1877. I learned this version for the tenor banjo from Tom Ritter of the Clan Dara Band.

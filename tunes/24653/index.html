Re: Crann Na Beatha

Composed by Ryan Molloy. The sleeve notes say, “Composed for We Banjo 3 by our dear friend Ryan Molloy, this is a tune about family, children, and becoming a parent. It is about feeling a sense of place in the world, a connection to generations gone before, and having a place in the future through the generations we beget.”

Beautiful piece of music. I’m sure you know it’s mostly 5/4 rather than 3/4, but I don’t suppose there is a category for that.

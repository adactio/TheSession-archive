Re: Alejo’s Dream

This tune is an original composed by my friend, the piper Alejo Parra, member of Stolen Notes folk band. We are based in Seville, so technically, the tune come from the south of Spain. It is obviously inspired in celtic folk music, but using an irregular bar number such as 5/8.

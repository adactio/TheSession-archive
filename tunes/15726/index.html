Taibhse Chonaill, aka Conall’s Ghost

I learned this tune from Martin McGinley at Scoil Gheimhridh Ghaoth Dobhair 2014.
There is great video of Con Cassidy telling the story.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=di9hMsH6S4w&feature=youtu.be

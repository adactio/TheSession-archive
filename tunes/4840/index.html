Four comments

For the record, since we should list composers when known in the comments section, this tune was composed by fiddler James Kelly. His father, John (renowned fiddler and concertina player), was from Rahey in West Clare.

↳ Posted by violynnsey on October 19th, 2007.

Re: The Humours Of Rahey

I added this tune to our NZ Sessions website recently. I checked with James Kelly about using his recording from his CD 101 Traditional Irish Dance Tunes and he was good with that. You can hear it at:

https://irish.session.nz/tunes-archive/humours-of-rahey/

He pointed our that Rahey is sometimes spelt Rehy but was puzzled by the name Humours of Faha

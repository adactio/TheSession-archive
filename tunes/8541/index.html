Forde’s, X:7

Here is what Mr. Eskin posted put thru Jens Wollschlager’s ABC transposer into Amix.
On a ten key melodeon in D, the mixolydian mode is the scale rooted on A.
Melodeon players put lots of Dmix tunes (like Bunker Hill, etc.) down to Amix, which is nice and growly.

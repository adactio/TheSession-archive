Two comments

Davy Maguire’s Trip To Arran

My transcription is based on Jason’s playing on his album The Sunny Side of the Latch. After writing it I discovered he had already published his own setting, which is here:
http://jasonorourke.info/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/Davy-Maguires-Trip-to-Arran.pdf

I think the way he wrote it down is a bit different from how he plays it on the album, so hopefully there is some value in me putting this transcription out here. (I did ask Jason for his permission to put my transcriptions out here first and he kindly agreed.)

From the sleeve notes on the album, this tune is dedicated “…to another Belfast flute legend, Davy Maguire, with whom any trip is an adventure – Arran was a particularly good one”.

///

I have a little remark about a capital letter in the title of this tune. On the album’s sleeve notes the word “to” in the title starts with a lower-case “t”. This isn’t possible here on thesession.org. Not a big deal; never mind. It’s the same for the other jig of Jason’s I’ve put out here, The Sunny Side of the Latch - it’s lower-case for the words “of the” on the album.

Jonny’s Funky Fidlle

This tune was written by a twelve year old fiddler called Matheu Watson .It was written for one of the boys fidller freinds,Jonny Murphy from Eire.He had a very cool fidlle that hadn’t been varnished and that is what the tune is named after.

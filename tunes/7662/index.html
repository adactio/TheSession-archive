Four comments

Another tune by harpist Maire Ni Chathasaigh taken from the Dan Ar Braz album Finisterres (played in a set with Ril An Suaimhnis). The b part might not be 100% right - it’s worth listening to the tune from the album as it’s played with alot of swing. Probably won’t appeal to “pure drop” traditionalists but features some great playing from Donal Lunny, Sharon Shannon, John McSherry and loads more.

Two comments

The Pheonix

Self-penned. Key change from Amin to Amaj between the parts.

The loose idea is in the A Part, the melody is falling down. In the B Part, it’s lifting up. Like a phoenix resurrecting.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on January 20th, 2019.

The Pheonix, X:2

A few variations, and transposed down to Emin/Emix. The original also works with the B Part in Ador/Amix rather than Amaj.

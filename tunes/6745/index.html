This tune

I used to live in a tiny town called Swanton in the shadows of Backbone Mountain. (Maryland/WV area of US) At one time there were many fiddlers there… up until the 1960’s then the old tradition was die-ing out. I was browsing the internet to see if anyone had ever documented this area’s tradition and found this neat old tune that has nothing to do with that area directly. (Besides the Name) However it does remind me of different tunes from the region in it’s structure.

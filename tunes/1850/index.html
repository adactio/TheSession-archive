Two comments

The Pretty

Well, here’s yet another tune I transcribed from the playing of The Chieftains. This was played last in a five-part medley, the last track on The Chieftains 9. I find that this goes well with Denis Murhpy’s Slide, because of their similar opening bars, as well as the Cuil Aodha Slide, as that was the tune The Chieftains played preceeding this one on their album. Enjoy!

↳ Posted by Tommy McCarty on July 27th, 2003.

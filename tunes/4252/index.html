Two comments

Kevin Doyle’s

In this case, Kevin Doyle is not the player or composer, but the step dancer who appears frequently with the group Pendragon. This hornpipe, composed by Pendragon’s whistle player Phil Edmonds, is the first tune on their CD “Finding our Way Home”. I transcribed it last night to learn it for myself, and thought to share it with y’all.

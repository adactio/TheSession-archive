Two comments

Catherine The Brave

This is an original, named for and dedicated to my older sister, who’s fighting an illness and needs some inspiration from time to time. I developed it as a theme, I play it broadly and grandly. However it does work with a more playful treatment if that’s how you do it.

I think the first version is the one to save for posterity, in G with an even cadence, it’s more accessible to all players. However, I play it in A on ukulele with some rhythmic variations in a few spots - hence the second setting.

Cathy would love to hear about it being played in pubs and living rooms, so if you like it please play it. I’m not looking to drench the database with my tunes, but this is an exceptional case and I’ve done my five. For those in bands and who record, this is being published without a copyright notice and so pops immediately into public domain, at least according to US copyright law. So, have at it.

Thanks,

Jonathan (banjouke)

The Honeycomb

“Well I went to see the widow and the widow wasn’t home, so I went to see her daughter and she gave me honeycomb.” So goes the lyric to this tune that was once popular in the mountains around Cumberland Maryland and Pennsylvania.

It is definitely a version of the English tune called “Push about the Jorum.” I always thought that the Irish got a hold of it and made “Blackberry Blossom.” (Reverse the parts.) Or maybe the English got a hold of Blackberry Blossom and made this peppy little tune. Whatever its true history may be, I suspect that it came from Lowland Scotland or SOuthern England or even Ulster like so many of the tunes I collect.

Ofcouse with all of my submissions this is but one bare bones version of the melody… Lots of nice triplets will fall into place once you learn the tune…I vary it and maybe it shouldve been in 2/4…

There are two settings in RMC (#76 and #958). The first is a reel setting The Rowan Tree, the second is a strathspey (Push About the Jorum). The notes are similar (similar to X:2 above) but the latter is syncopated with some scotch snaps.

Two comments

The Knockard Elf

This is a great tune form Duncan Chisholm’s album ‘Farrar’. It was composed by Stephen Saint. On his album it’s played a semi tone down as I’m told that that’s where Duncans’ fiddle likes to be. I picked it up from this youtube clip as it’s in E minor.

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ehoH6OSsGZM

↳ Posted by bogman on June 16th, 2009.

Four comments

The Munster Rake

A great tune from Gan Bua’s “An-Spealadoir” (2009). I don’t have the details on the source of this tune, but hope this is a close transcription to the original pipes setting by Sean Gavin (part A/B.1).

↳ Posted by Uilleann_piper on May 22nd, 2016.

Re: The Munster Rake

This fine tune appears in O’Farrell’s Pocket Companion for the Irish or Union Pipes, Vol. 4, almost the same dots as those transcribed above.

Odyssey

A lovely catchy tune composed by the Wexford traditional and folk musician Fergal O Hanlon.

Check out his EDM influenced banjo version of this piece on his hugely popular Youtube channel:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J8N6iqvgUSQ&ab_channel=FergalO%27Hanlon

Nice videography in the video, gives a nice flavour of the town that will host Fleadh Cheoil na hEireann 2024.

Fergal has some lovely material on his channel.

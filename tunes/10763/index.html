The Dregs Of Birch

I got this nice slow reel from the excellent ‘Tuned Up’ album by Brendan Mulholland, Brendan Hendry and Paul McSherry. They play it in Bb but I’ve posted it here in the more session friendly key of G. As I bought the album on itunes I’m not sure if the tune’s traditional or not.

The Dregs Of Birch, X:5

This is a transcription of the version that Tania Elizabeth, the tune’s author, plays on the Duhks’ second album. I don’t know why all the other versions here have high notes at the end of each part; it’s not how she wrote it, and it frankly sounds funny to me.

↳ Posted by JHowley on April 17th, 2020.

Re: The Dregs Of Birch

JHowley asked for the story of the tune title. I’ve been playing this tune recently and loving it so wrote to Tania to ask her. Here’s her reply: “The dregs of birch refers to a time when all the Duhks got in a sauna with a promoter friend of ours and we used the last drops of birch essential oil to mix with the water. It smelled amazing.” What a nice story, eh?

Pibroch MacKenzie’s Farewell

One of my favourite retreat marches.

It was composed by James Ross Riddell who was the brother of the better known Donald Riddell who was an inspiration and teacher to many Scotland’s young(er) fiddlers including the likes of Bruce MacGregor, Duncan Chisholm, Adam Sutherland, Sarah Jame Summers to name but a few.

Pibroch Mackenzie himself was Bobby Macleod’s fiddler for many years.

The setting above is close to that played by Angus Grant Senior.

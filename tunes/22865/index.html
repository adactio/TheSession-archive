Two comments

The Scarawalsh Meeting

This tune is a composition of the piper, Kevin Rowsome. The Rowsome family of pipers’ original home place was near Ferns in Co. Wexford, and Scarawalsh isn’t that far away from there. Scarawalsh was the venue for the Fair of Scarawalsh, which apart from being a trading fair, was traditionally the annual gathering of pipers and fiddlers from the south of Ireland long before the feiseanna or fleadhanna cheoil. Kevin tells me he put this title to the tune imagining the meeting in Scarawalsh of his great great grandfather Samuel Rowsome with players such as the renowned fiddler Patrick Walsh of Scarawalsh, the gentleman piper Robert Brownrigg of Norrismount also near Scarawalsh and in all likelihood, John Cash or indeed Jem Byrne.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on March 11th, 2023.

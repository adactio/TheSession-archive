Three comments

Valerie Pringle’s reel

A Natalie MacMaster composition, this tune is painfully easy but has a distinctly dark quality; I think that’s why I like it so much. Definetely worth a listen on her live album, paired with David’s Jig.

↳ Posted by Erik-Fiddler on September 23rd, 2006.

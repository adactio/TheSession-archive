Miss Margaret Ellis Of Duntrune

Written by Sandy Ingram of Glamis, of I think the Angus Strathspey & Reel Society. It’s published in a book of his tunes published to support an Alzheimer’s charity - I didn’t get the name of the book, so if anyone knows I’d be grateful.

I’ve reversed the A and F# in bar seven from the original, just to make the endings consistent.

In Memory Of Herbie MacLeod

From Jerry Holland’s “The Second Collection”, a lament for Herbie MacLeod of Arlington, Massachusetts who died in May 2000. Herbie’s parents were Gaelic-speaking people from Cape Breton.
As a tribute to Jerry Holland, who died in July 2009, the Strathspey and Reel Society of New Hampshire played this tune along with a selection of his reels in the 2009 Gala Concert.

Petticoat Loose

Gmaj version from O’Neill’s Waifs and Strays of Gaelic Melody (1922), markedly different from https://thesession.org/tunes/16716 (setting 2 there is the four-part version of the 1809 collection of piper John Murphy), https://thesession.org/tunes/693 (better know as Strop the Razor), and https://thesession.org/tunes/2277 (in Cmaj/Ador).

My transcription is based on the flute playing of Deirdre Hurley with her sister Mairéad in the episode “Petticoat Loose: A Wicked Woman of Irish Folklore, Music, and Song” of the ITMA series “Drawing from the Well”:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-LTKWgQgMO0 (starting at 27:05)

O’Neill’s printed version can be accessed here: https://www.braccio.me/oneill/ONeillsWaifsAndStrays.pdf

Bars 1, 5, 9 and 13 of the B part are essentially variations. I also include the extra bar with which the end the tune.

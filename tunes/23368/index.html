Tangerine Dream

By Chris Stout, this version as played by Katie McNally. The B part rhythm goes a bit wacky, in a way that means there’s probably a couple of different valid ways of writing the time signature changes, but this is closest to how I hear it at least!

↳ Posted by pbsinclair42 on July 30th, 2023.

Tangerine Dream, X:2

This is an accurate transcription from the album Naked and Bare. Yes, the measures really are that crazy!

I have chosen to bar it in a way which both groups the notes logically and also captures the playing style from that album.

The weirdest thing about this tune is it still feels very much like a polka!

On Naked and Bare it also transitions into a jig version which is somewhat more better-behaved: https://thesession.org/tunes/24930

↳ Posted by spraff on October 27th, 2024.

Tune background

Composed by Mike McGoldrick and recorded on his Aurora album. Tune is a tribute to Christy Garvey, stalwart of the Manchester Irish music scene and neighbour of Mike’s, who brought carrots fresh from the allotment and left them on the McGoldricks’ doorstep.

Túirne Mháire

Taken from the playing of Séamus Begley and Oisín Mac Diarmada: https://thesession.org/recordings/4292 .
This track is on YouTube https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ClGYegtn4Mw
A fine, simple slide which, I guess, is based on the air to a song of the same name. It translates as Mary’s Spinning Wheel. The expression in Irish can also mean “a punishment” or “a trouncing”, if you get a TM.

Paddy Cronin’s

I was perusing some old videos of Paddy Cronin on Youtube recently when I came upon a clip of Nicholas and Anne McAuliffe (fiddle & flute) playing this tune. It states in the clip that the tune is a composition of the man himself. I had never heard it before, but judging from the fact that it is (1 played very much in the style of Paddy and (2 played by players from County Kerry (Castleisland) who very well could have gotten the tune directly from him, I think this may actually be legit. Any thoughts would be most welcome. Here is the aforemetioned clip:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rTIcsCsInIE

Enjoy!

Paddy Cronin’s, X:6

I’ve set out how I play this tune on the flute. I have taken the first version and amended it to the way I tend to play it. I have not added any ornamentation except I have tried to indicate a cran on the bottom D in a couple of places. I’ve used the symbol for a roll and I know that is not the cran symbol and anyway I’m not sure if I’ve used it right - but, basically I play a cran there!

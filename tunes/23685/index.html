Casey’s Hornpipe

After hearing it in a random record at Youtube Music, I tried to find it here without success. I digged the traditional tune archive at tunearch.org and I only found a reference to a printed book. Luckily the book is scanned on archive.org, so I’ve added the ABC transcribed as it here.
From R.P. Christeson, The Old-time fiddler’s repertory p. 37 [49] (1973)
There is a note in the book: Recorded by Bob Walters in September 1950

I’m not sure about the musical mode (I know the structure has two flats, I though it could be a plain B flat major, but the entry form doesn’t allow that mode. Feel free to point any error in the transcription.

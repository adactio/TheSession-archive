One comment

Calum Figheadair

Beautiful reel on Bonnie Rideout’s Scottish Inheritance, which she starts out in a slow pibroch fashion, very rubato, and builds to reel speed.

Two manuscript versions are on Tunearch here under the title The Kilchattan Wedding: http://tunearch.org/wiki/Kilchattan_Wedding_%28The%29 There’s a two-part setting in the Athole Collection, which largely corresponds to the first two parts, and Rideout seems to be playing from the Köhler’s Violin Repository setting.

Having a little fun with the tune and trying to match her fiddle ornaments on banjo, here’s a variant for the C part:
Ad d/d/d {d}cA[DA]A | {g}fA[DA]A {d}cA[DA]A | Ad d/d/d {d}cA[DA]a | {g}f^c d/d/d {^F}[G,3G3]:|

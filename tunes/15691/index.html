Bob Herdman’s Lost Tomato

Another one of mine that I’ve been asked to add.

You might remember, back in 2010 (before I upped sticks and moved to Rothbury), there was a bit of a kerfuffle that hit the news. Gazza had to get involved and everything. Well, I was talking to someone in the pub about the effect it had had on the village. “Well,” he said, “at least everyone knows where we are now.” (Which is true.) “Apart from that… nowt much. He nicked Bob Herdman’s first tomato out of his allotment.” Now, Bob’s a character. Rothbury through and through, but he’s been to sea and you can tell he’s Seen Things. He’s also into music in a big way, though he prefers the Northumbrian/Scottish stuff really. (He actually runs the accordion club in town, though I won’t hold that against him.) Anyway, I thought he should have something to make up for his loss, so there you have it. The sum total of harm done to Rothbury by that radge eejit: Bob Herdman’s Lost Tomato.

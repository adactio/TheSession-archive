Re: The Cricket’s March Over The Salt Box

What is of interest (to me at least) is the section it is under in Kerr’s 2, as it suggests the tune is possibly from the repertoire of African American musicians being in the Negro sand jigs, planation dances and walk rounds section rather than Irish or Scots. Also interestingly in dotted 4/4 like a hornpipe, not a jig.

Re: Idlewild in Kerr’s Merry Melodies

I can’t add this ABC to this page because it’s in a different time signature (hornpipe in Common Time), but here’s my transcription of the setting of Idlewild (definitely the same tune!) found in Kerr’s, as mentioned up-thread.

X:6
T:Idlewild
T:(aka The Cricket’s March Over the Saltbox)
C:Traditional
O:U.S.A.
B:Kerr’s Second Collection of Merry Melodies (Glasgow, c 1870-75) (No.417, p47)
N:Listed as a “Clog Dance” under “Negro Sand Jigs, Plantation Dances and Walk Rounds”
N:Also found, notated as a “straight jig”, in Ryan’s Mammoth Collection (Boston, 1883) cf https://www.itma.ie/digital-library/score/ryans-631 and https://tunearch.org/wiki/Idlewild and also https://tunearch.org/wiki/Idlewild_Jig
Z:Jem Hammond 12:5:2022 (with minor corrections of obvious typographical errors in Kerr)
R:Hornpipe
L:1/8
M:C
K:D

The Sligo Lasses

This nice little reel appears in O’Neill’s Dance Music of Ireland - 1001 Gems (1907), p. 114, no. 636, as The Sligo Lasses, Cailini Sligig.
It seems that “our good old chap” O’Neill is the only one to have published this tune.

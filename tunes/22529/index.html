Anna’s

This was written for a fiddle player friend of mine who travelled around Ireland when she was younger in a London Taxi (old black cab), I was in my first ever band with her called Huntersmoon.
Yes, I’m sorry it’s one of my tunes! I use the session for musicians who come to our local sessions here, and someone has requested that I post it up for their ease of learning and playing as this website is such a great resource and tool and we use it all the time to help new musicians access our sessions, so if you don’t already, please please support it and donate or subscribe.

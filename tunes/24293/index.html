Two comments

Mari Lwyd

A fantastic Welsh tune that I heard from this recording: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=seppSvVmOig by Calan (the tune itself starts at about 3:11)

I believe that they are played on Welsh Pipes (hence the larger than normal pipe range).

The counting in the B part is tricky, I am not certain on what the correct way is but I went with the one that seemed to fit the recording the best. I have left in a few of the other options in the ABC notation which you can use if you would prefer.

↳ Posted by CRMWilson on May 13th, 2024.

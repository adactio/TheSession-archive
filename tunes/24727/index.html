There’s No T In Annagh

A march composed by Caitríona O‘ Brien from Arklow. Caitríona is a talented multi-instrumentalist, being an All-Ireland senior Fleadh winner on melodeon, but also excellent on 2 row accordion and fiddle. Her music has been closely associated over the years with the Bass family of musicians from nearby Annagh in Gorey. This march’s title refers to visits to the Bass household in Annagh for music evenings, cleverly substituting ’T‘ for ’tea’.

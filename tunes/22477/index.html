Five comments

The Trip To Bannow

Another double jig from John Noctor, collected and notated as part of the Wexford Tunes project, which aims to collect all the traditional melodies of County Wexford, historical and contemporary together in one place. John Noctor is a prolific composer. His compositions can be heard on his Youtube channel. Both of his parents were Wexford-born, so his tune titles often have places or people from the Model County represented. John Plays this little jig straight through without repeats of the parts.

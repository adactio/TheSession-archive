The Road To Naul

From the playing of Garry Walsh, Johnny Neville, & Colm Murphy.

Basic setting from their playing, transposed to Dmix/Ador, from Fmix/Cdor.

Garry learned it from his father (who learned it from his father), and lists it as traditional.

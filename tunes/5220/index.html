Rafferty’s Mustache

In the track notes for his cd The Dangerous Reel, Mike Rafferty says he got this tune from a book by Willie Kelly, but the tune had no name. So they called it Rafferty’s Mustache. He and Mary Rafferty play this in a set beginning with the Aughamore Reel.

Oh, and I’m sure this tune goes by other names, and may already be in the data base here.

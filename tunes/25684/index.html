Three comments

Crabs In The Greasepot

This great tune comes from Ceol Rince na hÉireann Volume IV, and is jig number 3. I had never come across it before but heard Tom Oakes (the great flute player now living in Edinburgh) play it. He has a lovely take on it which I shall also post as some point.

Ceol Rince na hÉireann states that it comes from the Grier manuscript (III, p 35)

The Eagle’s Nest

A lovely reel composed by John Redmond and features on his album of his compositions, ‘Box Sets’. There is of course another tune - called Nead an Iolair (the Irish for Eagle’s nest). John’s title for his reel refers to a spot on Mount Leinster, close to his family home place in Ballindaggin in north west Wexford.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on November 27th, 2022.

Re: The Eagle’s Nest

The other tune I was referring to above is actually Fead an Iolair - the Eagle’s whistle…strange that for yonks I’ve had the wrong name for that air. So The Eagle’s Nest is a unique title for John’s tune.

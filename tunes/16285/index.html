The Randy Wives Of Fochabers

This reel is transcribed from the playing of the late Shetland fiddler Willie Hunter, from his 1995 album Leaving Lerwick Harbour. There is another unrelated tune, “The Drunken Wives of Fochabers” which, for all I know, may be about the same incident.

Two comments

Stan Rogers. Named & composed for a great man, by Newfoundland fiddler Rufus Guinchard.

Fun tune for beginners. Good in a plastic paddy G set too, I usually put it in somewhere with the Banshee, Merry Blacksmith, or Spootiskerry. Either way, it’s a good Atlantic toe tapper.

Two comments

Festival

This is a simplified version of a famous American waltz, played a lot in the United States by bluegrass fiddlers, such as Kenny Baker or Mark O’Connor. Its melody lends itself to many variations and customizations.

↳ Posted by paddy04 on February 1st, 2025.

Festival , X:2

There are many interpretations for this waltz, sometimes with double strings to better stick to the bluegrass spirit but that is not the point here. Here is my version which gives an idea of ​​the possible arrangements.

Johnnie McIljohn’s No.2

This reel comes from a recording by The Boys of the Lough (Welcoming Paddy Home), and follows “Johnnie McIljohn’s No.1”. https://thesession.org/tunes/1491 and preceeds “Donegal Barn Dance” https://thesession.org/tunes/3469

The Boys They got the tunes from Tommy Maguire of Leitrim, whose father, John Maguire, was known as “Johnny McIljohn” for some reason. He “lilted” the tunes regularly at country house ceilidhs so his name became associated with the tunes.

There is already a tune in the database titled “Johnny McIljohn’s #2” but it is distinct from this one.

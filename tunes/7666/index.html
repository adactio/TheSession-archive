One comment

I wrote this one yesterday after riding my bicycle through a tempest - howling wind, lashing rain - the works. The first part is pretty traditional, and the second part modulates between Eminor and Fmajor (which, when played up tempo, is my attempt at a musical impression of gusts of wind). It’s nice to be inside and dry, at any rate.

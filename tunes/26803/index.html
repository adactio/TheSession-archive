Snow At The Hand

A Josephine Marsh composition on Music in the Frame (2018); on the back of the package, it says “Snow on the Mountain,” but in the liner notes and the track metadata it’s labeled “Snow at the Hand” so I’ve gone with that title, adding the other title as an alt. From the notes, “The first tune was written during a very snowy spell in West Clare.” Gorgeous tune!

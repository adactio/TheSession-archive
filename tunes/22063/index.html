Tom Byrne’s No.1

This is the first reel by Wexford town based accordionist Tom Byrne, composed about 20 years or so ago. Like most of his contemporaries , Tom started out with The Mayglass Ceili Band back in the 1960s. He competed to All-Ireland level in the fleadhanna and won The Oireachtas accordion competition in the era when that title rivalled a Fleadh title in importance. I’ve been working on the notation of his compositions (3 jigs and about a dozen reels to date) as part of the Wexford tune project. Tom plays very much in the older Wexford style of the 40’s and 50’s accordionists. Tom is quite well known in Killarney trad circles due to his great friendship with the great Kerry accordionist and composer John Brosnan.

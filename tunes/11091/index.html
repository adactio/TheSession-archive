Chords - from the composer

Here’s the chords that I play on the LH (on my CD I left out the LH to make more room and options for the piano chords)

A part:
|G---|D/B---|C-C/A-|C/D-G/B|
|C-D-|Em---|C/A-C/D-|G---|
B part:
|C---|C/A---|Em---|D-G/B-|
|C---|C/A---|Em-C/A-|C/D-G-| with the last bar being |C/D---| at the end to go with the note A

For guitars it can be written more easily:
|G---|Bm7---|C-Am7-|Dsus4-G/B|
|C-D-|Em---|Am7-Dsus4-|G---|
B part:
|C---|Am7---|Em---|D-G/B-|
|C---|Am7---|Em-Am7-|Dsus4-G-|

Ian S.

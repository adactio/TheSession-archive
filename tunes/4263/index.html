You’re right, Kenny. Maeve Donnelly and Peadar O’Loughlin recently recorded this tune as “Sailing into Walpole’s Marsh.” In the sleeve notes of “The Thing Itself,” Pat Mitchell writes this version can be traced back to a single source, Gerry-Terry McMahon, a flute player from Kilnaboy, Co. Clare. Cian’s members are also from Clare. I haven’t got their second album, but I can almost hear Padraig playing this tune on concertina.

Jerry McMahon’s (Sailing Into Walpole’s Marsh), X:2

X2: Setting played by Maeve Donnelly and Peadar O’Loughlin on their classic fiddle/flute duet album The Thing Itself, where it is titled “Sailing Into Walpole’s Marsh.” The variation on bars A5-A6 references another duet track by Maeve Donnelly, her definitive performance with Eddie Clarke of the better known setting of Sailing Into Walpole’s Marsh https://thesession.org/tunes/1341 on the classic album of the same name https://thesession.org/recordings/2760 .

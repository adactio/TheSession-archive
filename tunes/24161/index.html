Auld Foula

Shetland reel. From the album “The Silver Bow: The Fiddle Music of Shetland” by Aly Bain & Tom Anderson.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h5aT3m9FGS4

↳ Posted by DaveF on March 26th, 2024.

The Auld Foula, X:3

This is a condensed version in G ionian.

This composition is based on so many “mirror” images/phrases; repetitions, inversions, octaves…

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on February 26th, 2025.

Re: The Auld Foula

The original in A is normally played in scordatura tuning (AEae or perhaps ADae)

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on February 26th, 2025.

The Auld Foula, X:4

The same tune (in its original double octave setting) but in the key of D ion. for the benefit of fluters, pipers or whistle players. (:

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on February 26th, 2025.

Re: The Auld Foula

A musical tongue-twister of sorts;
not that it is technically difficult to play but it will trip your ‘wits’ more than your fingers.

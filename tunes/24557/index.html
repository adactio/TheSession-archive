Two comments

Sergt. Stack’s Favorite

This is a somewhat simplified version of the transcription that appears in O’Neill’s 1001 and 1850 melodies. Somehow it appears to have slipped through the cracks here (besides a mention that the B part shares similarities with The Geese in the Bog, although they are clearly distinct tunes otherwise). That’s a shame, since it really is a pretty tune.

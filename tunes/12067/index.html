Re: Soren’s

I came across this lovely tune from Aryeh Frankfurter’s version on Bandcamp. I noticed he played the endings of each part differently to the dots here. I found his dots at https://library.larkcamp.org/Swedish/AryehSwedishTunebook.pdf
It does show it in 6/8 time (it almost sounds like 6/8), but the main difference is at the endings where the penultimate note is lower than the final note not higher, this matches Jan Ekedah’s recording on youtube:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kobJ-nXb6to
I’ve also found another website with dots in 3/4 time that exactly matches Jan Ekedahl’s own playing:
https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:852479/FULLTEXT01.pdf
It reads harder as it doesn’t have repeats. It should open up at the correct page but if not it starts on p44 and ends at bar 68 where the 2nd tune starts. A lovely tune anyway.

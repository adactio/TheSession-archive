Re: Half Past Four

It should be noted that this is an American fiddle tune, attributed to the Kentucky fiddler Ed Haley. It has become almost ubiquitous in Old Time music jams. As an old time fiddle and Irish flute player myself I quite enjoyed the performance with Bruce Molsky, Michael McGoldrick, Aly Bain et al. This performance is from the TransAtlantic Sessions series, hence the cross-genre nature of the ensemble.

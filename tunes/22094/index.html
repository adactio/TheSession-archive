The Well Field

Here’s another reel, composed by Paddy Hayes of Piercestown in County Wexford. It was recorded on his first album, ‘Return to Kilty’. I’m assuming the title may refer to a field in his home place, as it’s paired with a second reel entitled “Staple’s Haggard”. The Staples‘ were and are neighbours of the Hayes’ in Piercestown.

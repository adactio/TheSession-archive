Four comments

Appears on “Between Two Worlds” by the Paul McKenna band. Not entirely sure of the composer as I don’t have the album notes, but I think it’s either Seán Gray or Ruairidh Macmillan.

It’s a slow reel, I like to play it quite lazily and mess around with the tempo quite a lot.

↳ Posted by Dr. Tøm on July 4th, 2011.

It’s played on the fiddle on the album, which suggests it might be Ruairidh Macmillan who wrote it…

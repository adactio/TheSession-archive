Two comments

Daniel O’Rourke

This nice jig appears in O’Neill’s Dance Music of Ireland - 1001 Gems (1907), p. 25, no. 50, as Daniel O’Rourke, Domnall Ua Ruairc. I like the key change.

↳ Posted by Moxhe on October 30th, 2015.

Daniel O’Rourke, X:2

It looks like this tune is in O’Neill’s 1001 twice. The full version in D as number 50 and then this version in F as number 318 under the title The Merry Hunstman.

Re: Kitty Of Oulart

This tune is number 65, named Caitlín Ua Úllord, in Walton’s Ireland’s Best Session Tunes, ISBN No. 1 i5720 143 4

Úllord being an apple orchard, as are the alternate versions Úllghort or Úllaghort which give the village of Oulart, Co. Wexford its English name.

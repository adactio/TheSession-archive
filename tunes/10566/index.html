Gan ainm

The manuscript by Friesian fiddler Andries Kiers dates from 1864. There are two mazurkas in it, both have no title. I’ve played this one in all kinds of settings: from loud and loutish with a folk rock band to subtle and modest with mandoline and mandoloncello. During one of these occasions it reveived its nickname: Nice One. So have fun!

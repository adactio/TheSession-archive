Millbrae

This was on the requests list. Composed by the late Ronnie Cooper of Shetland, this version is as played by The Hamefarers.

Millbrae

Many thanks Nigel. I heard the Boys of the Lough play this on the end of “The Laird of Drumblaire” in the early 80’s and have been on the lookout for it ever since. Most grateful.
Max.

Millbrae

Ronnie Cooper wrote this tune while playing at a wedding on the island of Unst, Millbrae was the house they were staying in, home of pianist Eileen Hunter.

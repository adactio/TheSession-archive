Emma & Jamie’s Wedding

This is a beautiful waltz with an unusual contrasting section - sounds great on fiddle, but works equally well on most instruments - I play it a lot but never get tired of it, in fact the whole album, (Yella Hoose by John McCusker), is an absolute pearl, so I’ll submit the other tunes if anyone is interested !!

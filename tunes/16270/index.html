One comment

Izeru

Composed by my friend Olivier Bouchard around 2007, this lovely tune has achieved session status in the last couple of years, since it started being played by local sessioners in the absence of its composer. Worth learning. The chords are the simple ones that fit the melody, but there’s a lot more to find.

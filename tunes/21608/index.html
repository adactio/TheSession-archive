My Home Town

This is actually a retreat from one of the masters of the genre, John MacLellan (Dunoon), also responsible for Lochanside, The Highland Brigade at Magersfontein and The Bloody Fields of Flanders.

The original has two crotchets/quarter notes at the start of the first and fifth bars of the first part and fifth bar of the second part but, as set here with a dotted crotchet/quarter and a quaver/eighth, is how I hear it usually played (but I’m not a piper).

Tam’s Auld Love Song is more like Castle Dangerous: https://thesession.org/tunes/8159

Tom Hughes picked up all of his tunes by ear, often from the radio or at a dance. The tunes often got altered in the aural process, and Tom usually didn’t know their titles so made up his own.

My Home Town, X:3

Just noticed a transcription error in my orginal setting (which I’ve corrected) and thought I’d add possible chords, as I tend to play them - I usually play the Bm only on the repeat of the second part.

For retreats like this I tend to play six (rather than three) down strums per bar (definitely no “oom-pah-pah” waltz stuff).

“Banjaxed” ~ un o‘i / one of his ’compositions’ ~

I have to confess, while empty places in the ‘Comments’ section cause some irritation, when someone slaps in one of their own compositions and doesn’t say “hoot!”, well, Grrrrrrrr!!! Take at least enough time and responsibility to lay claim on your progeny. On the whole it’s no mystery when I come across one of these unclaimed compositions, so not commenting isn’t going to fool many into thinking their from some yet to be discovered hoard of the ‘Ancient Music of Ireland’… Besides, I support ‘inspiration’, be proud of the possession that made you do this, let it in and if you’ve the balls to share it you can damn well own it too, ’fess up! Some damned nice combinations of notes have happened and keep happening. It’s one of the great wonders and magic of music that a handful of notes can bring such variety and joy….oh yeah, an non-joy too…but it takes compost to grow a good garden…

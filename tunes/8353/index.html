Tom Maguire’s Fancy

A great John Brady composition which has been recorded by Brady’s brother in law with Liz Carroll on the album Kiss Me Kate, also by Brid Harper/Dermot Byrne, and Oisin MacDiarmada/Brian Fitzgerald/Micheal O Ruanaigh.

The version I’ve transcribed is the original setting which I got from John’s son Adrian in a session. The other versions that have appeared on recordings have F naturals in the B-part. I also transcribed Brid Harper’s version:

X: 1
T: Tom Maguire’s Fancy
C: John Brady
S: Brid Harper/Dermot Byrne
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Gmaj

Tom Maguire’s Fancy/John Brady’s, X:3

This is the version played by Liz Carroll and Tommy Maguire (and Jerry Wallace on the piano), released on a LP by Shanachie Records (nr. SH 29010) in 1978.
The “John Brady’s reel” was “composed by flute player John Brady, who is Tommy’s brother-in-law. We jazzed it up a bit, John.” says Liz Carroll in the sleeve notes.
Interesting version, isn’t it ?

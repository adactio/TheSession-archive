↳ Posted by Kenny on November 11th, 2003.

The B-part’s very similar to Sporting Paddy.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on April 21st, 2008.

It’s never in A dorian though. It’s more likely to be G I’d say. It’s tonal center is definatly more G than A. Though just because it doesn’t resolve to G doesn’t mean it resolves to A. And just because there are no Cnats or Csharps doesn’t mean it doesn’t imply the Cnats.

Put this one down as one that doesn’t fit the notation conventions. And if your gonna strum to it, give it plenty of space.

Spoil The Dance, X:3

An older E dorian variant from vol. 4 of Kerr’s Merry Melodies, where it appears under the name Ginnie Pin Your Bonnet On. The contour of the B part is also somewhat distinctive from the previous versions. I wonder if it may have gotten mixed up with Sporting Paddy’s somewhere along the way.

Skye Collection

This tune can be found in print dating back to 1887 in the Skye Collection, which is now available as a second edition by Paul Cranford (http://www.cranfordpub.com/). It’s a lovely strathspey. I have also heard it played as a clog by Joe Derrane, very nice. Cheers!

Stirling Castle, X:2

Version is based on the recording of Ciarán Ó Maonaigh on the album The High Seas.

On The High Seas the tune is played as part of the following set
1. Stirling Castle
2. The Lowlands of Scotland
3. Mooney’s Reel

Following Stirling Castle I play Laird of Drumblair (https://thesession.org/tunes/170) for the tunes fit together perfectly due to their similar rythmic structure.

Listen to the Stirling Castle played by Ciarán Ó Maonaigh:

Re: Stirling Castle/ Marquis Hansley’s

There is a reel setting in Ryan’s Mammoth Collection (#272) as Marquis Hansley’s. There are differences in the shape of the D and A “chords/arpeggios” in the first two bars (repeated in bars 3, 5, 6 and 7. despite that is is clearly the same melody.

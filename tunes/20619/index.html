Re: The Garavogue

Nice setting from Sorcha Costello, Elaine Reilly, & Daithí Gourmley, with excellent backing from Catherine McHugh (a fine fiddler in her own right, as well). Some very minor differences in their version, but very tasteful. 1st tune in the set; the 2nd tune I’ve heard credited to the playing of banjo goddess Angelina Carberry, but no title given- if anybody knows it, please respond here. Enjoy!

https://youtu.be/0NCQkDceLkY

Four comments

Paddy Gavin’s

From the accordion playing of Damien Connolly, son of Martin Connolly. Played in “C” on the recording, I’ve taken the liberty of transposing it into the more flute-friendly key of “D”. Paddy Gavin’s name crops up now and again – anyone have any information on who he is / was ?

↳ Posted by Kenny on August 25th, 2008.

I don’t know, but I used to always get Paddy Glackin and Frankie Gavin mixed up and now yer man here is really going to mess me up.

Taking It On

By Jeremy Kittel, as played on his Chasing Sparks album. Listen on Spotify here: https://open.spotify.com/track/3kCyj9Nrnoph8dopARiZNC?si=dO8JpugzRnS4Mpnmw2KMbw or on Youtube here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S4Vy08sENKc

The rhythm is all over the place in the B part, but stick with it and it all works out in the end! The first note in every bar of the C part is a real ghost note; have a listen to Jeremy at the links above to hear how it should sound.

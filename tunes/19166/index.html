The Cave Of The Cats

Transcribed from a lovely clip of fiddler Katie Linnane and piper Ivan Goff (from the equally lovely #Quarantunes challenge zooming around Facebook during the pandemic). The tune is, I believe, a composition of Birmingham-born fiddler (and brother of Fluter Catherine) John McEvoy.

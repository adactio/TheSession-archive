Three comments

Longing For Home

Beautiful slow tune written by Joanie Madden of Cherish the Ladies. She wrote it for her mother after learning of her immigration story from County Clare to New York.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQFhRaox6Fc

Requested by Len Stillson on the Tin Whistle FB group. I hope you enjoy it.

↳ Posted by DaveF on August 18th, 2019.

The Ballygow, X:3

Here’s a transcription of Phil Murphy playing his composition for the old CCE archive, probably recorded in the early 1970’s. I’ve notated the repeat, where Phil introduces the long dotted crotchet to the tune (interestingly for the ONLY time in the recording). Phil is playing a C mouth organ on the recording, but I’ve transposed the note for note transcription to the key of A which is the usual key that the tune is played in locally, undoubtedly because John and Pip usually use A Tombo harmonicas when playing together. I’ll also upload a transcription in the original C pitch also.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on May 12th, 2023.

Re: The Ballygow

I should point out that the recording of Phil Murphy playing the tune - it’s only twice through and therefore only about 40 seconds long- was found after trawling through Seamus MacMathuna’s track listings and finding two recordings of ‘PHIL BERRY’ playing mouth organ - the performer had been misnamed on the track listing. Phil Berry is a well known traditional singer from Wexford and doesn’t play mouth organ. And on finally hearing the recording (after many entreaties) it was obvious that the recording was of Phil Murphy. The other track is an excellent rendition of The Banshee reel.

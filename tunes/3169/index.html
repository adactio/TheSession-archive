Three comments

The Long Strand

This version looks like a cut & paste job straight from Henrik Norbeck’s index. What a shame.

This tune has an identical 1st part to Sporting Nell, but a different B-part. This is easier to see if you transpose this into Ddor:

X: 1
T: Long Strand, The
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Ddor
AD~D2 A2dc|(3ABc GF EDEG|AD~D2 A2dc|(3ABc GF ED~D2:|
c2dc Gcec|c2ec Adde|c2ec gcec|(3Abc GF ED~D2|
c2dc Gcec|c2ec Acde|~f3d ~e3d|cAAG Addc||

Find Sporting Nell here https://thesession.org/tunes/1781.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on April 4th, 2007.

I’m not 100% sure about this, but I think the Gan Ainm tune on Altan’s “The Red Crow” might be a Donegal variant of this tune. I can’t find any transcriptions of it on the net, and the recording databases just list it as Ril Gan Ainm, as on the sleeve notes.

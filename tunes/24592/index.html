Two comments

The New Apron

From “O’Neill’s Music Of Ireland”, 1903 edition.
I added the C# accidental in bar 7 and made slight arrangements throughout the piece so it suits the range of most instruments.

↳ Posted by Yoann on August 11th, 2024.

The New Apron, X:2

Known in Scotland as “An t-aparan goirid” (“The Short Apron”), the setting I have given is from Hamilton’s Universal Tunebook (1844), and I have transposed it from C for comparison with the O’Neill setting. I’m pretty sure that it would have originally come from Captain Simon Fraser’s 1816 collection, where it is marked as “ancient.”

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on August 11th, 2024.

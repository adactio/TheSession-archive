Fiddles On The Wall

From the playing of D’Arcy Broderick.

Not sure who composed it.

Played at a quick tempo, and transposed from Cdor (2 flats). Listening to it in Bdor (with the two G’s sharp) doesn’t sound quite right, so, have put it into Bmin instead. Although, in Cmin with 3 flats seems alright.

One comment

Loch Duich

Slow pipe march, either 6/8 as here or 2/4. Good for practicing ‘pipey’ grace notes. Bowing as taught by Pete Clark at the 2016 music camp in the Eastern Sierras.
Eilean Donan Castle lies at the mouth of Loch Duich. The A87 from Skye to Loch Ness runs along the north side, beneath the Five Sisters of Kintail.

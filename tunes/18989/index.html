Three comments

The Glenduan

A polka popular in our region, typically played after the Ballydesmond and The Siege of Ennis. It’s possible this tune is already here under a different name and/or key. I wasn’t able to find it under tune search, but happy to be corrected.

↳ Posted by Alex Davidson on February 13th, 2020.

The Glenduan, X:2

From the playing of, and, composed by, Bob Bickerton.

Bears some resemblance to phrases in these polkas:
https://thesession.org/tunes/1640
https://thesession.org/tunes/4774

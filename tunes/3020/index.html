Gaelic lyrics

According to the Baird Gàidhlig site:

http://www.cne-siar.gov.uk/gaelic/grd/baird/alasdairmac.htm

the famous Gaelic poet Alasdair Mac Mhaighstir Alasdair (Alasdair MacDhòmhnaill) wrote a poem with the title ‘Mo bhobag an dram’. It runs to 5 verses with a chorus, and the metre fits both versions of this tune that are on the Session site.

The poem may (in Gaelic) be downloaded from:

http://www.geocities.com/alltandubh/H/Horo_Mo_Bhobag.html

This is a variant in a different mode. I was kinda hoping someone could maybe add the rest of this version, which I think is based on a GHB arrangement (although I heard it sung as Port-a-beul).

The Favourite Dram, X:2

This setting is as played by Kenny Hadden, Sharon Creasey and Gordon Turnbull (from left to right) at the launch of their FluteFling Collection (playing begins at about 4:02):
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hz1ZhR-oX1w
I’ve preferred to write out the parts in full for the sake of easier readability. They could also be written with long first and second endings.

The FluteFling Collection can be ordered, either as a printed book or as an eBook, here:
http://flutefling.scot/shop/. The Favourite Dram is in the collection, contributed by Claire Mann.
Extended notes to the tunes can be downloaded at the same location. Chet

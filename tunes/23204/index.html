The Gauthier

This is a waltz I composed back in the early 1990’s while holidaying with in-laws in Laval, Montreal. I had forgotten about it until it turned up lately on a sheet of paper, written in ABCs in it’s original key of Bb. I’ve notated it in G as part of the Tune Project putting it in a more box/fiddle friendly key.

Maggie’s Call


Written By Sean Og Graham in memory of 5 year old Maggie Black, Glenarm, Co. Antrim.
Originally written in the key of C

Performed in the key of D by the Counties Antrim & Derry Country Fiddlers at the All Ireland Fleadh, Belfast, 2026, in memory of Maggie, who was learning fiddle with CADCF.

↳ Posted by breigin on August 10th, 2026.

Tinker’s Stick

“slainte is ” has requested this one, and that’s how I came here. It may well have been a favourite of Michael Dwyer, but I first heard it played almost 30 years ago by Sean O‘Rourke, flute-player with “Alba” and the JSD Band, under the title “The Tinker’s Stick”. It is in Breandan Breathnach’s 1st volume of “Ceol Rince na h’Eireann” under that title as well. I had decided to post it in reply to “slainte”’s request, but rememebered hearing Matt Molloy play it on his “Stony Steps” album so checked first. Turns out gian marco has posted it 2 years ago.
A very simple reel, but very melodic.
The order of tunes on the “Stony Steps” set is wrong on the sleeve notes. This is the first tune, “Stony Steps” follows, and the last tune is “Griffin Of The Bridge”.

The Tinker’s Stick, X:6

Transcribed from (the first time through) Mike and Mary Rafferty’s version on The Old Fireside Music. Recorded under the name “The Reel of Rio”. What I find interesting about this version is the G sharp in the B part coming from the accordion. Actually, on the recording, you can hear a fainter B behind it, so either it is being played as a chord, or perhaps the flute is playing the A over the G#.
Also, as some other versions have it, the gedc ending of the B descending back into the A part is great.

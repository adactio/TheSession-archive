The Provincial

I learned this from a 78 of Sligo fiddler James Morrison: https://youtu.be/GEF9uIgMr4c?t=641 It was also recorded by William Quinn & His Dublin Orchestra and Frank Quinn - or Joe Flanagan? https://archive.comhaltas.ie/compositions/886#/tracks/4408 I couldn’t find the recording of WQ & HDO online. William also played the melodeon.

John Kelly played this tune too, on the concertina: https://johnkellycapelstreet.ie/project/john-kellys-hornpipe/ The page says it’s from a video on the page, but this isn’t there. John’s granddaughter Aoife plays it on the Kelly Family Tradition record: https://thekellyfamily.bandcamp.com/album/a-family-tradition-traditional-irish-music-from-the-kelly-family

Two comments

Lauren McKowen’s

Got this great tune from an album by Back Of The Moon (Fortune’s Road). It’s on track #4. It was written by piper Simon McKerrell.

Also as it is a bagpipe tune the key is strictly in Amix but as there is no Gs in this I thought I may put it in A.

Incidentally the bagpipe notation for this tune and other of Simon’s compositions can be downloaded here from his page:

https://sites.google.com/site/simonmckerrell/home/my-tunes-1

↳ Posted by irishfiddleMA on June 4th, 2012.

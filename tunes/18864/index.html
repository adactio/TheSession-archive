Two comments

Mickey Doherty’s

Donegal highland learned from this video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3CHJZFFMrU4. Played by Mairéad Ní Mhaonaigh. Been stuck in my head ever since.

The long A’s in the second part are droned with the open A-string and the fourth finger on the D-string. Powerful.

↳ Posted by Daniel Parker on January 3rd, 2020.

Mickey Doherty’s, X:2

The Boys of the Lough play this as “Micky Doherty’s Highland” on The Fair Hills of Ireland, but with the parts reversed. X:2 is a quick‘n’rough transcription of their setting.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on January 4th, 2020.

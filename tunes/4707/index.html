Re: Larry Redican’s Bow

In Luke O’Malley’s Collection, self published in the 70s, this is called Gavin’s, with this note: “The Gavin family of Ballina were kind enough to make a tape for me in 1964. This reel was on it, and no one seems to know it so I named it after them.” Now, Luke knew Larry and attributes a good few items to him in his book, so it’s interesting that Larry’s name became attached to it.

The Gerrity recording seems to be in Bb - but this is probably owing to the vageries of playback speed + pitch, it should be in A. Another disc of his had the Bush in Bloom, which he also recorded with piper Tom Ennis.

Seven comments

Daisy Bell

This is a very fun little reel composed by multi-instrumentalist Martin Murray of Co. Waterford. In the liner notes to his CD A Dark Horse, he tells us: “Daisy Bell is the name of the ‘ghost’ who, according to my father, haunted out house in Carrick-On-Suir.”
Best played when channeling the spirits of the Flanagan Bros.!

↳ Posted by Limeretti Lumeretti Hillhocker on March 16th, 2007.

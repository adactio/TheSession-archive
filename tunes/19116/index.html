The Races At Ballyhooley

Found this tune while searching for another on youtube . You will find it as “Peter Carbury and Padraig McGovern live” . They play it in Bflat . I have done my best to transpose it to G but may not have got all the little ornamentations Peter put in. There are other notations on the net and Liam O Connor plays fiddle , which differs slightly phrasing. Hope I got it fairly right and comments /corrections welcome.

Great back-story to the air (it’s definitely NOT a march). There was a massacre of peasants/farmers in Bartlemy/Rathcormack near Fermoy in 1834. This was part of the ongoing Tithes War. The song commemorates the massacre, the hatred of the tithes and the plot for vengeance.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on June 2nd, 2008.

Jackie told me that he composed this tune and the Em one that precedes it ( https://thesession.org/tunes/7729 ) on Arcady’s “After the Ball”. They called them “Jackie Daly’s reels” on that record to make it clear that they were his tunes. This was after they had been recorded without attribution by Kathryn Tickell and others.

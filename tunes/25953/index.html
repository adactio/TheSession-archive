The Queen’s

This tune entitled “The Queen’s Polka” (SG1a.024) was transcribed in Book 1a (c1840s) of the Grier MSS of Gortletteragh, Co Leitrim. Several other polkas in Book 1a are composed by ”J.F. Douglas", a light classical composer who has not been identified thus far, and this may or may not be one of his compositions.

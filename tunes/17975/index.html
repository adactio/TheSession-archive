The Chicken Gone To Scotland

A nice highland I got off Barry and Laura Kerr’s album “Castor Bay”. I know nothing else about the tune, except that it’s a lot of fun. This is the version that Laura Kerr is playing on fiddle as part of the duet with her brother.

The Gipsy Moth by Mick Davis

I have played a Northumbrian A Dorian slip jig called Peacock Followed the Hen for years and then I looked for a tune to go with it, keeping the strange modal feel of the F# in the B music. I couldn’t find a suitable trad tune so I wrote this tune in E Dorian, putting the C# in the B music to get a similar feel. I called it The Gipsy Moth because of my long term regular attendance at a session in Greenwich.

Trip To Larry Gavin’s, X:2

Setting in G Major.

I absolutely love this tune! The original setting in A Major would give a hard time to some instruments, I have transposed it to G Major. I didn’t do any change to the setting X1, I just wrote the same but two semitones down. Another setting for tin whistle will follow, that one will have my personal choice for the changes in octave.

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on December 18th, 2020.

Trip To Larry Gavin’s, X:3

Setting in G Major suitable for tin whistle.

This setting has my personal choice of changes of octave to suit the note range of the tin whistle. This setting would be the one that would be played in a session with the setting X2 (setting in G Major that was transposed down from the original in A Major). Therefore it has the same notes as setting X2 but some of the notes are one octave higher.

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on December 18th, 2020.

Three comments

Mull Of The Woods

A slow waltz of the kind sometimes referred to in Scotland as “Gaelic waltzes”. Mull is one of the larger islands of the Hebrides, lying directly opposite Oban on the Scottish west coast.

↳ Posted by nicholas on November 23rd, 2006.

More of a hornpipe…

This tune was composed by George McIntyre and is really a hornpipe although I’ve added it here as a reel.

So, please interpret same as you see fit.

In the second last bars of each half, the last note differs, i.e. “c sharp” and “d” respectively. I’m not too sure why (This is a version I pinched elsewhere) but either will work very well.

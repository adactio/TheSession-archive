↳ Posted by snorre on December 2nd, 2013.

You’re right, Snorre. For concerts (at least on the 2013 US tour) and the wonderful Sunny Banks recording, Edel uses her C#/G# Wally Carroll concertina and switches to the C/G Jeffries for playing socially. Hers is the “small” (5 5/8 inches) as opposed to the “standard” (6 1/4 inches) and the details are at http://www.carrollconcertinas.com

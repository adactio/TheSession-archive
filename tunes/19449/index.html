Julia Clifford’s Tune

This is an unusual polka-time tune I learned from a tape of Julia Clifford which in all likelihood was not a polka originally – but it’s sure played like one by Julia! I found the first part to be fairly similar to this barndance (The Ballroom Favourite) but the second part remains a mystery so far.
https://thesession.org/tunes/3329

What’s particularly interesting for me is that the tune has a very Eastern European folk tune-like structure in that it’s effectively twice shorter than an Irish polka. Julia plays it AABB the first time through and then AAB but it could work well without repetitions at all as an AB “single polka”.

↳ Posted by Bregolas on January 31st, 2024.

Re: Julia Clifford’s Tune

You can listen to this polka-not-polka right at the beginning of this tape (which is a fragment of a longer private tape which Billy Clifford has):
https://soundcloud.com/pj-teahan-884614828/sonny-riordan-and-julia-cifford-remembering-old-tunes-kitchen-tape

I was told by the man who helped Julia record it that she was specifically remembering all the unusual and less-played tunes she could think of for a future recording that sadly never materialised. This included some famous “Sligo” polkas but also quirky versions or regular tunes, some rare tunes from Sonny O’Riordan and compositions of John Walsh, which did not make it to this fragment. I previously transcribed Sonny’s polka from the tape here – and it also sounds suspiciously “continental” in origin.
https://thesession.org/tunes/8109#setting42654

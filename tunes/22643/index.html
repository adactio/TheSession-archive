Seven comments

Dawn On The Strand

I learned this from Éilís Crean’s album Searbh Siúcra. It just immediately struck me as a beautiful tune, and I came here to look for the dots…. and there weren’t any! According to Éilís, it was written by Eddie Kelly, a childhood teacher of hers, who gave her many of his tunes. According to https://www.irishtune.info/tune/7618/ this is the only recording of the tune. And thanks to IrishTuneInfo getting me started with the first few bars.

This setting is in F, as Éilís plays it. As a piper, that won’t work for me. I’ll post in G shortly.

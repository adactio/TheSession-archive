Blaze Away!

A march from 1901, very popular then - I learned this tune from Come and Trip It, a record documenting the old ballroom dance rhythms. The ABC here is the lead mandolin part in a trio of mandos with guitar accompaniment, which may be viewed here: http://imslp.org/wiki/Blaze_Away!_(Holzmann,_Abe)

↳ Posted by Lucy Rietmann on January 4th, 2016.

Composed by Abraham “Abe” Holzmann.

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on January 5th, 2016.

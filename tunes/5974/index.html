Four comments

Lairds Of Ryton / Over The Border

This is one of my favourite tunes. I originally learnt it as a 9/4 tune called “Over The Border”. I got it from a recording of Northumbrian pipes music entitled The Wind In The Reeds, but later learnt this simplified 2-part slip jig setting. I’ve transcribed some 9/4 settings below:

X: 1
T: Over The Border
M: 9/4
L: 1/8
R: Nine-four
S: John Peacock MS (1800-1805)
Z: Dow
K: Gmaj
~B4G2 BABcde g2d2B2|d2g2f2 d2g2B2 d2efg2|~B4G2 BABcde f2d2B2|c3dc2 g2e2c2 ~e4g2:|

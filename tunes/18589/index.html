Glaun, X:3

This lovely jig was written by Padraig Rynne, featured on Avalla’s self-titled 2019 record.

“Glaun is an area between Caherea and Lissycasey where Pádraig’s grandmother Teresa Kelly was originally from.” - https://padraigrynne.bandcamp.com/track/escape-from-balto-glaun-kilshanny-thunder

Based on the melodic range of the tune and the concertina ornamentation, I am guessing they are playing “in D” on Eb instruments. Speaking of ornamentation, I have tried to notate it sparingly, only where it is relevant to the tune (e.g. the quick f “triplet” down to e; to separate these from the e’s that are simply cut with the f button, which are just written as consecutive eighth notes). Give the tune a listen and you’ll hopefully see what I mean.

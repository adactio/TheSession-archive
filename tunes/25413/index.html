Three comments

Raise The Rafters

I went looking for this tune using searches on Tunepal, Folkfriend etc and didn’t find a match. It’s played at our sessions here in Wexford as ‘Raise the Rafters’ and I was surprised it didn’t seem to be here or indeed anywhere as far as I could tell. I think it may be a composition of P.J. King’s? Perhaps someone can confirm?

↳ Posted by John E Roche on March 28th, 2025.

Re: Raise The Rafters

It is indeed a P.J. King composition, John, and a very fine one at that. Credited as such on the CD insert. Maybe not surprising it hasn’t been posted here before, as it only seems to have been recorded on the CD of the same name. Nice to know it’s escaped from Clare as far as Wexford, anyway, and hopefully further now that you’ve posted it here. Good man.

Yes Kenny. A lovely tune. I’ve posted the version that has got legs around here, mainly courtesy of the flute playing of my good friend Mick Lawler. I haven’t heard the original.

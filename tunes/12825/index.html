Hi Ruthruin, I found Time Machine among several tunes I’d printed out from the Internet so I didn’t know where it came from, but I liked it as soon as I played it. I’ve composed a 2nd and bass part to it and this week, 16th to 22nd August 2014, I’m playing it (as well as other tunes) with the 80 strong Festival Orchestra at Whitby Folk Festival, in North Yorkshire, England. I hope you don’t mind! If you email me, robindgarside@gmail.com I’ll send you a copy of it in pdf and music xml (opens in Musescore a free music writing program) and you can tell me if you like what I’ve done to your tune, or not!
Cheers, Robin Garside

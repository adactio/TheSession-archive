Marche Au Camp

March from Quebec, composed by Jean-Paul Loyer.

↳ Posted by Siúlóir on October 12th, 2018.

Marche Au Camp, X:2

This setting, in F, is from Jean-Paul’s recording of the tune on his album, OJNAB, Le Messager. That’s one beauty of an album, full of other great compositions of his. I suppose it’s out of print, and I don’t think it’s available on any streaming services. There’s progress for you!

He played the A part as 16-bars, repeated.

Last Of The Litter

Written by Angus Barbary from Sydney, this tune was composed about the life of a collie dog that reached 18 years of age. It has been adopted at the Gaelic Club Session in Surry Hills and has been recorded by local trad band ‘Fresh Off the Boat’ in their first and self-titled album.

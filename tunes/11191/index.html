Garry Porch Of Avernish

A modern Strathspey from the bow of Adam Sutherland. The dots are online elsewhere so here’s the abc.

Often played together with the Strathspey “Ben Williams of Tiree” written by John Somerville. G P of A usually following B W of T, link:

https://thesession.org/tunes/11055

Enjoy.

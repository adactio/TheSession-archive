Re: Talon’s Trip To Thompson Island

From the Nordic Fiddlers Bloc “Deliverance” notes:
"(Kevin Henderson, MCPS/PRS)
Kevin composed this tune in the summer of 2013 after a fantastic week at the Boston Harbor Fiddle School in the USA. At the end of the week all the teachers had to do something to raise money for the following years event as part of a raffle. All the teachers decided to compose tunes and someone could buy the rights to name them. The naming rights to Kevins tune was bought by a great women named Teresa Allen"

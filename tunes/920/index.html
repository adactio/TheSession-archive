↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on October 26th, 2020.

Re: The Road To Recovery

Michael L. Allen, a five-string banjo player who I met in Tokyo, played this tune at the Winfield Bluegrass Festival, and won the banjo competition. I had learned this tune from Liz Carroll herself, as had he.. So it was just amazing to play this tune with Mike in Tokyo. Who would’ve guessed!

↳ Posted by jimmydearing on June 13th, 2021.

The Road To Recovery, X:3

The X:1 setting is almost how I remember it, but there’s supposed to be a Bb in the fifth bar of the B part and also an F# at the end of the A part. So, I looked it up in the Liz Carroll Collected book, and that’s how it is there.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on November 1st, 2023.

The Road To Recovery, X:4

This is based on Gerald Trimble’s version in his “Heartland Messenger Trilogy” from his “Heartland Messenger” album. It is followed by “Spirit of the House” in that set.

↳ Posted by Charleville on September 21st, 2025.

Re: The Road To Recovery

Gerald Trimble recorded this in a set in 1984; that might be the first recorded version of it, and in his album notes, he refers to the song as “Liz Carroll’s,” probably before it was named the “Road to Recovery.”

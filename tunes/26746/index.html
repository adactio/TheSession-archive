Three comments

Apples For Fred

Our friend Monika Braaksma pointed that tune out to us. She found it on YouTube in the “Good Tune” collection of Kevin Lees & Sebastian Bloch, who recorded this composition of Karen Tweed together with FAVERGADE, a Danish trio consisting of Mats Kjøller-Henningsen (flute), Clara Tesch (fiddle) & Julian Svejgaard (keys).
A very happy tune with a positive karma, nice swing. It is also attracting attention with some extra bars interspersed.

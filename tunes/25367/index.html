Re: Òran Na Cloiche

“Òran Na Cloiche” or “Song of the Stone” is a Gaelic song written by Donald MacIntyre (“The Paisley Bard”) about Scotland’s Stone of Destiny, which was removed from Westminster Abbey in 1950 and returned to Scotland by a group of students.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on March 11th, 2025.

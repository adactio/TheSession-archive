The Portuguese, X:3

This tune was the first of two waltzes associated with the button accordionist Art Stoyles (1942-2015) from St John’s, Newfoundland. He learned them from a visiting Portuguese fisherman - presumably Manuel de Silva as mentioned by Nfld Whistler above. I have transposed the tune (from Bm) and adapted it for the penny whistle.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on April 13th, 2023.

Four comments

The Stream - Mike McGoldrick

Not your usual session tune, this is most likely a improvised piece played by Mike Goldrick. It’s hardly Irish or Scottish. I wouldn’t know how to categorise it … New Age probably, but I LOVE it, hence the many hours spent figuring it out. It has a meditational, inspirational quality that will probably leave most people cold but I think it’s a great piece. The production is a little over processed for my liking and the whistle often disappears into the mix but I think I’ve got it down pretty well with a little initial support from MTGuru at C&F. Though I would welcome positive suggestions for corrections from those with better ears than myself. There’s a coda after a long break. I might get around to finishing it in due course.

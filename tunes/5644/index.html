Galician Mazurka

This is a beautiful Mazurka I learned off a Susana Seivane album. She actually plays it in Eb instead of G as she is playing on a Bb Gaita during this tune. I thought G was a more accessable key for use session.orgers.

Mistake

This is actually a Breton tune written by Christian Desnos.

“Mazurka de Comptoir” ~ a composition by Christian Desnos

Plougastel-Daoulas en Bretagne, Brittany
accordeon diatonique = melodeon

The following is a transcription of his playing:

K: C Major
|: G |
AB c2 cG | cd e2 ed | ef ed cB | AB (3ABA GG |

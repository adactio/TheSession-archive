Retour des hirondelles

I know that this isn’t really an Irish tune, but there was a discussion about playing French waltzes on the B/C accordion, and I now that Sharon Shannon made a recording of this waltz, and saw that it wasn’t yet uploaded. So this is my effort. I might add some more chord details later, if I get the time.

Re: Retour Des Hirondelles

The first audio recording of this “valse à variations” seems to be by Émile Prud’homme (who was Edith Piaf’s accordionist) in 1948.
It is credited to B. Pagano & R. Trognée.
Beniamino Pagano (or Benjamin as it also appears on French records) composed various tunes and waltzes in the Musette style.
Robert Trognée (1907-1972) belonged to the same generation of Musette band leaders and accordionists as Prud’homme.
This waltz features extensively in the late Musette recordings of the 50’s and 60’s.

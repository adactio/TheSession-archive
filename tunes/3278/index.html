Composer credits

Andy did not compose this tune but got it from Lad O‘Beirne, whose source is unkown. Andy did adopt it as one of his pet tunes, however, playing so often and so well that it became “his” tune in name. “The Humours of Scariff,” by the way, was collected by Francis O’Neill in east Clare and published in his Dance Music of Ireland, so I don’t think we can credit it to Paddy Fahey!

↳ Posted by blarneystar on July 10th, 2007.

No. 42 Bunratty

There is some confusion here in the name “Andy McGann’s No. 42 Bunratty Reel.” That was a joke title on Andy’s Shanachie LP for the reel usually called “Drag Her Round the Road,” which is a completely different tune than this “Andy McGann’s Reel.”

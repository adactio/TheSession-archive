Patrick Óg Fitzpatrick’s (Paddy’s)

An excellent ‘céilí band’ march composed by Pat Fitzpatrick of Gorey in Co. Wexford. Pat is a top-class traditional flautist, winning Senior Flute at the All-Ireland Fleadh in Listowel in 1987. He was a professional bandsman initially with the Army and later with the Garda Band and is now retired back to County Wexford, where he is a huge asset to the County’s traditional music scene. The march is named after his son Paddy.

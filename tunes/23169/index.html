Andrew’s Smiles

Composed by Karen Marshalsay, I first learned this at one of her multi instrumental workshops many moons ago. It was for one of those “Big Band” thingies.

It’s a lovely tune on the harp which, of course, is Karen’s instrument. She’s a particularly fine exponent of the wire harp, incidentally, as well as the other varieties. Of course, it sounds great on other instruments too. So, I’ve posted it here.

When not “harping” on her own in various projects, she is also a member of The Cathal McConnel Trio along with the man himself and Kathryn Nicol.

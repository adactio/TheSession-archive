Walsh’s (reel)

I got this reel from Regina Garrity of The Dirty Wellies, a Celtic group in Chicago. I’ve played it with them in session a couple of times now. I don’t see the tune under any other name on thesession so am adding it here. It is paired to follow Fr. Kelly’s Reel.

Sliabh Maol, X:3

Maol Mountain. As played by John McEvoy and friends, “Traditional Irish Fiddle” (2012). Per liner notes the tune comes down from a Denis Murphy radio recording of 1963. The geographic feature (“Bald mountain”) is in the Slieve Luachra region and is also inspiration for the polka, Top of Maol.

↳ Posted by Steve Moore on May 14th, 2020.

Three comments

Raoulf

A Jeremiah McLane tune from the Nightingale album ‘3’. Not in the key of C, but a modal variant probably between hypodorian and phrygian. A very tricky flute tune.

↳ Posted by LastPosted on May 17th, 2015.

Raoulf, X:2

Here’s a setting in F# hypodorian that is a bit easier to play on the flute, eliminating the devilishly awkward Eb key.

↳ Posted by LastPosted on September 17th, 2021.

Raoulf, X:3

This version has a few revisions I discovered while flat picking this tune on guitar. Challenging, but fun.

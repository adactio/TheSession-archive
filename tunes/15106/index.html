My Heart’s In New South Wales

This is a slow waltz written by Dave Swarbrick as his farewell tune when he moved back to the UK after several years in the Blue Mountains west of Sydney.
It’s starts in the key of Am for the first part of the tune, and then goes into the key of A for the main body of the tune.
Dave Swarbrick has recorded it with Kevin Dempsey on English Fiddler and with Martin Carthy on Straws in the Wind.
There is a great mandolin & guitar version here:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xBOsUaqQZ4c

This tune has been posted for my very good friend Mr T Buck of Edinburgh. We have spent many a happy
hour playing this, and many, many more tunes in the past. Thanks to his wife Sandra for sorting out the ABC
Happy Birthday Trev x

Re: My Heart’s In New South Wales

Thanks for putting this up - I wanted to learn this a few years ago and couldn’t find dots so I learned it from the record. I want to point out that this version isn’t quite right though. The main issue is that the first few bars in the second section are consistently too low. You have to shift out of first position. For example, the first bar in the second section is written here e3 a f2, but should be e3 c‘ a2 (c’ meaning c# on the e-string out of first position). This is how Swarb plays it on the recording I have (and how the mandolinist plays it on the youtube video linked). I guess someone has bowdlerised this for fiddlers who don’t like playing out of first position. I am one myself, in fact I only started trying on account of this tune - it’s a good tune to try it on if you are scared because there are only a few notes. I would fix the abc myself but haven’t the time now…

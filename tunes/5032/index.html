Two comments

One of me own. I suppose a sort of funny story behind this..

We were taking a quiz in my Italian class and at the time, we were working on the “passato remoto”. We were also studying a fable by Italo Calvino called “I Due Gobbi” or “The Two.. Hunchbacks”?

Anyways, my friend asked a question about one of the questions on the quiz that said something like “L‘ex gobbo… etc etc” So we had to explain to him what that certain part meant. Finally my friend said “Ya know… As in the ’No longer’ gobbo”. So yes!!!

Cheers,

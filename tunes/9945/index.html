Cape Breton version

I feel that even though the Cape Breton version ‘Homeward Bound’ is not that dissimilar from the ‘Gem of Ireland’, it certainly has become a unique tune in it’s own right. As a fiddle tune, the syncopated bowing style that can be used, makes it quite different.

↳ Posted by peakfiddler on February 10th, 2013.

Homeward Bound

In Gordon McQuarrie’s Cape Breton Collection (1940) this tune is credited to Rory MacDonald. It is remarkably similar to “Gem of Ireland” but I don’t think it’s a straight copy. Noodling about in a reel in D major, there are obvious places for the tune to go…

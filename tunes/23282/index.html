One comment

Gan Ainm

This is an other nice slow piece composed by Paul O’Sullivan from Bannow in County Wexford. Paul is well-known in the south east as an excellent pianist and concertina player. Thanks to Nóra Byrne Kavanagh for notating Paul’s compositions from recordings that Paul sent to me as part of the collection of tunes from Wexford musicians - The Wexford Tune Project.

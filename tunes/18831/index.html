Hommage A Pigeon

I first heard this tune in Ashland, Oregon at a Quebecois session attended by many of the local Irish session players. This tune adds an interesting twist to the usual Irish set. I like the Bm key for a bit of a change.

↳ Posted by Walt S. on December 21st, 2019.

Hommage à Pigeon

Composed by Philippe Bruneau.

Apparently he composed around 300 tunes:
https://www.wikiwand.com/fr/Philippe_Bruneau_(musicien)

Presumably written for one or more musicians of the Pigeon family (e.g., Arthur Pigeon, Philias Pigeon, and/or Marcel Pigeon).

See also:
https://thesession.org/tunes/17564

Almost identical setting here, with the only difference being in the last bar of the first repeat in the B Part, where they have “Bcde fcBc” instead of “Bcde fdBc”:
http://www.lafamilleleger.com/la-bote-pitons

La Famille Leger:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UnSGycUkOec

Re: Hommage A Pigeon

A lovely, tuneful reel.
I hear strains of songs such as La complainte des Terre-Neuvas or Le gabier de Terre-Neuve in it. Might be a coincidence just, as these strains are not uncommon in French trad. music.
Falquet et Gemme play this reel after Le reel de l‘île d’Orléans and that makes a great, punchy set.
https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=hlUYoX0FDDc&pp=ygUbWWFubiBGYWxxdWV0ICYgUGFzY2FsIEdlbW1l
If you’re not familiar with the stress pattern of Québecois reels, the video will help.

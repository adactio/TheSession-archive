Re: The Hound And The Hare

Hi cab, thanks for asking! But IMHO not a good move. PG recorded the jig as The Hare in the Corn; it is NOT the same as Ed Reavy’s Castleblaney Piper, even though it is quite similar. (I suspect this Hare jig is older.) The reason that it seems PG’s recording is note for note the same, is that the first two settings in the Castleblaney jig (https://thesession.org/tunes/1539) are settings of this jig not ER’s!
Ideally, if it were feasible, I’d have a new entry for the Hound and the Hare jig, and remove the first two setting from 1539 and put them there together with X:2 above, plus the link to CRE3.
You did ask! Athbhliain fé mhaise duit!
Brailsford

Midsummer Night

Cathal McConnel recorded this tune with Patsy Hanly and John Joe Maguire on the “Boys Of The Lough” LP “Good Friends, Good Music”. They followed it with another two of Vincent Broderick’s compositions, “The Tinker’s Daughter” and “The Crock Of Gold” . You quite often come across all 3 played together as a set. All are great flute tunes.

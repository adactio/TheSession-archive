Two comments

Falcon’s Rest

I’m not entirely sure that this is a reel: maybe a slow reel, with a really nice lilt to it. Not on any CDs yet, but maybe it will be on the next CD by FourWinds, who uploaded a performance of it to youtube about ten days ago. It is written by two members of the band, Tom Delaney and Robbie Walsh, but features the two others in the band: Caroline Keane’s concertina on the melody, with bouzouki accompaniment from Alan Murray (who seems to have replaced Daoiri Farrell in the band). The tune is followed in the set by two jigs, The Sheep in the Boat, with Tom Delaney on whistle, and then Peter Byrne’s Fancy, with TD on pipes and Robbie Walsh’s bodhran gradually getting louder.

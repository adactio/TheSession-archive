McGurk’s Wall

This is the first jig in the set on track 5 of John Redmond’s album of his own compositions, ‘Box Sets’. John, from Ballindaggin in County Wexford, was an underage fleadh champion accordionist. He spent many years in the U.S., where he recorded two excellent albums. ‘McGurk’s Wall’ refers to the feature photograph wall in JD McGurks Irish pub in St. Louis, which features many fine traditonal artists who have performed at the venue over the years.

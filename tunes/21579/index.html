Barnacle Bill

This is the basis of the theme tune for the BBC children’s TV show Blue Peter. The tune is from: http://www.folktunefinder.com/tunes/81424. I added chords and changed some of the notes.

↳ Posted by Barnacle Bob on February 14th, 2022.

Re: Barnacle Bill

Credit where credit’s due. This was written (in F major) by Herbert Ashworth-Hope in the 1930s. He was a Lancashire solicitor who composed music as a hobby and hosted some BBC recitals in his house. Barnacle Bill has been used since 1958 as the Blue Peter theme tune, so presumably counts as folk music by now. It has been messed about with by Mike Oldfield and others over time. Herbert was amused that his tune should be ‘as well known as the national anthem’ by the time he died in 1962.

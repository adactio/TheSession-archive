Eliz Iza (Breton Air)

This is really an air, in order to select the 4/4 Meter I assigned it as a Strathspey.
To be played relatively slowly.
Here is a youtube video:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zVmPO4ovbzQ

↳ Posted by Daithi_C on February 24th, 2023.

Re: Eliz Iza (Breton Air)

I first heard it on Alain Stivel’s “Renaissance of the Celtic Harp” album.
But this version by Bagar Kemper will make the hairs stand up!
It starts with archive recording of Les Soeurs Goadec whistle is taken over by the superb voice of Anne Aufret. Then the band comes in and it just gets better and better..

https://youtu.be/yoFs0-nIj5o

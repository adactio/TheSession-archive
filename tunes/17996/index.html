Rúadhán’s (Slow Reel)

This is a very nice reel composed by Angela Usher, a very gifted banjo player,
composer and music teacher from Manchester she wrote this tune for her nephew.
It’s one of many lovely tunes from her album “The Gort Mile”
The CD is absolutely worth listening to, she have a lovely style of playing.

Take a look on her website: https://www.angelaushermusic.com/

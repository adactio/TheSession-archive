One comment

Mr. Graham Of Orchill’s

I first heard this tune in a YouTube video being played by Natalie Haas and Joanna Farrer. It is a Niel Gow tune and like many of his strathspeys sounds best, at least to my ears, when played not too fast. I found the original version of it in the First Book of Niel Gow’s Reels, 2nd edition, a reproduction of which can be found here: http://hms.scot/fiddle/copy/8/ . The abc’s are just a copy of what is to be found in that book.

The Haas/Farrer video is here:
https://youtu.be/QWY5bWGziII

The Glen Collection of Scottish Dance Music

I’m not sure where Mad Baloney dug this collection of tunes up, but I’ve only just started skimming through it, and it’s EXCELLENT. The full title of the collection is “The Glen Collection of Scottish Dance Music; Strathspeys, Reels, and Jigs, selected from the earliest printed sources, or from the composer’s works. Arranged, with new accompaniments for the Pianoforte, by John Glen. Containing biographical sketches of musicians and musicsellers, with portraits, and a chronological list of works.” He sent it to me as a PDF, so I’m assuming it was either available online somewhere in that format, or he scanned the actual manuscript (I’m gonna go out on a limb here and say the former!) It lists the publication date as 1895, but as mentioned in the full title, much of the music is from earlier sources. The tune I’ve posted here is a composition of William Marshall, whose biography in the collection calls him “the most brilliant as well as the most prolific composer of Strathspey music Scotland ever produced”. I would tend to agree, if this tune is any indication!
If Brad doesn’t respond in this comments section soon, I’ll see if I can find out where to download the collection from & let everybody know (unless somebody else in Mustardland knows its whereabouts- if so we would be much obliged!) Until then, enjoy this tasty offering.

-Jay

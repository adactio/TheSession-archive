Larry Grogan’s Air

A tune found in the Joyce collection. He gives the source as McDowell of Belfast in the mid 19th century. Larry Grogan of Johnstown castle in County Wexford was a gentleman piper in the early 18th century and composed a number of tunes, some obscurely hidden in 19th century collections. This I’m assuming, given the feel of the tune, is played as a lively air.

Re: Larry Grogan’s

This elegant setting of the air ‘Larry Grogan’ (which P.W. Joyce copied from William Forde) dates back to a Co. Antrim 1820 manuscript book.

It appears as tune no 488 in The Forde Collection : Irish traditional music from the William Forde manuscripts / edited by Nicholas Carolan & Caitlín Uí Éigeartaigh, Dublin, ITMA, 2021

It appears there among tunes headed ‘Mr MacDowell … from Murdoch’s book’ ‘Tunes from an old MS Book (with the name of James Murdoch, Craigarogan, Jan 1, 1820)’

I wonder if anyone knows of an even earlier version, in print or manuscript?

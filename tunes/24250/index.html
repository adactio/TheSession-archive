Liza’s Dream

A slow reel written by pianist Donna Long of Cherish the Ladies. On the recording, Mary Coogan joins her on mandolin, for the “C-part”, I guess it is (the higher octave section). Although piano and mandolin do sound great together, I always thought Mary was playing up the octave as well, but that’s just the piano. Stupid me, lol, so people can just play it down the octave, to make it easier for those who wish…

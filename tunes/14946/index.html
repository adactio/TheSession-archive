Mairead’s Paradise

This reel was written by the flute player Áine Heslin from Kilrush. This tune can be found on the album Tunes Foundry.
According to the sleeve notes of the recording:
‘’I was thinking of my friend Mairéad Considine (concertina player from Doon-beg, Co Clare) while writing this and wanted to convey her mischievous, fun-loving nature!‘’

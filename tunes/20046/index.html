Heidi’s Dream

Lovely jig in F#min written by Colin Farrell and, I believe, Dave Curley.

Transcribed from Colin Farrell’s Tune A Day 2020 Day 326.

Here’s the video and you can see the Heidi the dog sleeping behind them :)

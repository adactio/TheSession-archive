Two comments

The Green Spears

Track one of McSherry album The Seven Suns. I was surprised that it had not been added
The first bar of the last line I have written as a low B, but on the CD that note is played an octave higher. Just sounded better this way on the fiddle

↳ Posted by Donough on March 5th, 2022.

Re: The Green Spears

Composed by John McSherry
From the album sleeve notes: “Aille The Fair was the most beautiful of all women of Ireland and a fierce warrior who drugged and kidnapped the great hero of the Fianna, Fionn MacCumhaill, in revenge for the death of her husband Meargach of the Green Spears. This is for Aille the fairest of them all.”

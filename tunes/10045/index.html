Cape Breton Jig

I got this tune from the playing of Buddy MacMaster while at Valley of the Moon Scottish Fiddle Camp in the early 90’s. When I asked the name of it, both he and David MacIssac couldn’t remember it.

↳ Posted by fiddlerdan on November 22nd, 2009.

& neither can I, but I’m familiar enough with it… :-/

I did a quick look and haven’t yet found it, but I’m sure I have it both on a recoding and in notes, somewhere… It’s a fun jig to play and dance to… It may be an extension of the confusions I’ve been in lately, but I’m remembering this with a second ending ~

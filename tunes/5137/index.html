Composed by Patricia Chaffe

Lovely Lovely March (not strathspey) by cape breton musician Patricia Chaffe. This is from JH collection of fiddle tunes but personally i prefer the version which is on this website:

http://www.geocities.com/gburke_wilson/Stella.gif

(not much difference but there is a bit).

P.S. Is much easier to learn than it looks.

The Tattoo, X:3

Setting in Bm. I’m trying to find the best key for a session, I’ve chosen this one. It is still quite difficult for the tin whistle because of the G# though. I don’t think there is an easy key for the tin whistle.

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on April 27th, 2021.

The Tattoo, X:4

For mouth whistling along with setting X:3.

It is the same notes as that setting but everything is transposed one octave higher with the exception of the first 6 bars of the second part, those stay exactly the same.

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on April 27th, 2021.

Re: The Tattoo

I made up this tune to go along with “The Okarina,” a tune that bounces between 7/8 and 4/4. It was inspired by some čoček tunes I’d been listening to. The title refers to some fabulous ink on a lovely barkeep in the late-lamented East Avenue Tavern in Portland, Oregon.

↳ Posted by Paul Kotapish on January 7th, 2025.

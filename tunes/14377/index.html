Both Sides Of A Coin

This tune is the last in a set of jigs that appear on the 2014 Trí-Tones album. I’ve no idea who composed the tune, maybe it’s one of theirs?

The Trí-Tones are Conor Moriarty (button accordion) Tara Breen (fiddle) and Stephen Rooney (guitar, vocals). I transcribed the tune from Tara Breen’s playing in this video

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYECF4mwBa0

↳ Posted by Harry Emma on April 23rd, 2015.

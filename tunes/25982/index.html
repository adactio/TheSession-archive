Murdoch Henderson, X:2

The original title of this tune was “Alex R Findlay” composed by James Murdoch Henderson in 1928 and named after, wait for it, a superintendant of cleansing in Aberdeen. Henderson wrote it as a “dotted” hornpipe, and underlined this by annotating it both “Sand Dance Style” AND “Newcastle Style.” He published the tune in his 1935 collection Flowers of Scottish Melody, and I offer that published version above.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on September 26th, 2025.

Re: Murdoch Henderson

In case anyone can’t live without more information about Alex R Findlay, his wife was the conductor of Aberdeen High School Former Pupils Orchestra. J Murdoch Henderson wrote a reel for her in 1931.

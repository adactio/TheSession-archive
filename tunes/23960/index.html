Miss Maule’s

Not to be confused with the REEL of the same name.

This arrangement is a Scottish Strathspey by Robert Mackintosh (18th Century) no. 68 from Paul Cranford Publication’s collection of Winston Scotty Fitzgerald. it is the second tune in in set 8 on Canada’s Outstanding Scottish Fiddler Winston Scotty Fitzgerald.

↳ Posted by Tim_Fiddler on January 23rd, 2024.

Re: Miss Maule’s

There are various mistakes with your ABCs for this tune, Tim.
For a start you’ve included the A>E pair twice in the first bar. Also, there are a lot of eighth notes that should be sixteenths, specifically in sixteenth/dotted eighth pairs which you have notated as EC>, for example, instead of E<C.
You’re also missing a couple of bars from the second part.
Also, I’d like the audio to play properly so I’d be tempted to have first and second time endings to the first part.

So, something like:

|:(3ABc A>E F>AE<C| (3ABc A>E F<BB>d|c>BA>E F<AE<C|[1D>FE>G A/A/A A2:|[2D>FE>G A/A/A A||
d|c<ea>e f<ae>d|c>ea>c d<BB>d|c<ea>e f<ae<c|d>fe>c A/A/A A>d|
c<ea>e f<ae>d|c>ea>c d<BB>d|c<ed<f e>ag<b|a>fe>c A/A/A A2||

Och, I’ll post it as a third setting.

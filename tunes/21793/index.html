Messing Around

The title of this composition was inspired by flute player John Rynne from Ennis(?) Co. Clare who announced the plan for the rest of the session like this: ‘And now, no more polkas, no hornpipes, no jigs, no messing around - only reels!’
You can imagine we kept that in mind and shouted ‘no messing around!’ whenever someone suggested a tune we didn’t like. The funny thing is many years later I met a girl whose second name was Messing, and now she is my wife and took my name - so still there’s no Messing around :)

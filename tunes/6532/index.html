Three comments

“The Shamrock” ~ two more sprigs, as promised: highland fling & single reel

This is very familiar, with at least one or two other melodies that I suspect are already on site, but I haven’t yet found them ~ too many chores to do today. I suspect that slainte, Dow or one or two others on site will know them, will have a better working memory than I do, and can add the links here. The other things that come to mind are coming out of the shadows in different keys and modes. There are a few coming to the fingers that are damned close to this.

A version of this, also D Dorian, is to be found in the Joyce collection, “Old Irish Folk Music and Songs”, 1909, but with only a few differences from the second take, the single reel. It is also a single reel:

K: D Dorian
|: dcAB cBcA | GEGA c4 | dcAB cBcA | GEcE D2 D2 :|
Dddd dfed | cde^f gefd | Addd defd | edcB A2 A2 |
Ad ~d2 Ad ~d2 | cde^f g2 fg | A^fge =fdec | Addc d4 ||

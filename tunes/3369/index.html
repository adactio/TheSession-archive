The Lilting Fisherman, X:4

This setting is transcribed as a “Quadrille” in the c1900 Reilly Family fiddle MSS of Drumreilly, Co Leitrim. It was originally a light classical tune that was assimilated into the folk music tradition. This version of the original composition also contains the third part which is not played anymore.

The Lilting Fisherman, X:5

I have this tune in key of D rather than having Apart in G and B part in D as there is only one bar in A part with a c natural . This setting is from Comhaltas Foinn Session tunes book 2 , page 4, the governing body of Irish traditional music in Ireland, and used to teach in the branches all over Irl and abroad.

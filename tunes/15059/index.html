Four comments

Creignish Hills

Composed by Rev. Hugh A. MacDonald, while passing through Creignish Cape Breton.

↳ Posted by JMM on December 13th, 2015.

Just found out from the man himself, Ashley MacIssac was actually the main composer of this tune. Fr. Hugh thought of the first few notes, Ashley made the rest, but never bothered to copywright it, or claim ownership. I didn’t get all the details, but it’s rarely attributed to him.

Six comments

John Nugent

Requested by Bernard Féton, from this recording:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b-fXIVGk514

Not a jig but an air in 6/8.

They’re playing it in C#m; I’ve moved it into Dm to match most written sources. It also works well in Bm.

X: 1
T: John Nugent
C: Turloch O’Carolan
M: 6/8
L: 1/8
K: Bm
B2c d2e|d2B AFD|Eee ede|f3 B3|
def BdB|def FAF|~E3 DED|A3 F3||
B2c dcB|def F2F|d2e f2g|a3 g2f|
eBe eBe|~f3 F2F|ABA FDF|d3 B3||
AFD AFD|GAB AFA|B2B cde|c3 A3|
A/c/dB c/d/ec|B/c/dB AFA|FDF FDF|d3 B3||

John Nugent was composed by Turloch O’Carolan for his patron, John Nugent, 5th Earl of Westmeath, who served in the Irish army under King James II.

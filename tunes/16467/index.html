North Caroline Breakdown

Not a reel.. It’s Old-Time, Bluegrass; Breakdown.. Composed by Tennessee’s Fiddlin’ Arthur Smith (1898-1971). The tune is a current festival favorite among younger old-time fiddlers. Ernest Smith, Arthur’s son, believes his father wrote “North Carolina Breakdown” around 1937.
I’m submitting this tune inspired in the arrange that the band 422 made up:

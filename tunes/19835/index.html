Two comments

Saltón D’Urbiés

Traditional tune from Asturias. This is a saltón, the equivalent to a reel in Irish Traditional Music. Three parts, each one in a different key and the third part has five bars. The transcription has no ornamentation, but you can find plenty in the album Un tiempu meyor of the Asturian band Llan de Cubel, this tune is the first one in the set of the first track.

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on October 11th, 2020.

Saltón D’Urbiés, X:2

This is my 300th submission to TheSession, so here’s another Asturian tune for you.

This is the transcription of the way we play the tune in Asturias, it is a very popular tune. Played regularly at sessions here.

Regarding the time signature, “saltones” are in 2/4, but 4/4 works too.

Here’s Llan de Cubel playing the tune, just mind, they use Asturian pipes in Bb, an Eb flute… so it’s been transposed accordingly.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TTFgu6MtBpo

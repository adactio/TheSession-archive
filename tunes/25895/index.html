Three comments

Jenny Lind

This tune entitled “Jenny Lind Polka” (SG1a.011) was transcribed in Book 1a (c1840s) of the Grier MSS of Gortletteragh, Co Leitrim. It’s a different polka to other more popularly known “Jenny Lind Polka”

↳ Posted by ConorW on September 1st, 2025.

Re: Jenny Lind

Fascinating tune, Conor: thanks. The only similarity I can see with the well-known ‘Jenny Lind’ is the phrasing of the first and fifth bars. Oh . . . and they have the same title. Just a couple of oddities - possibly typos. The third section has 9 bars, and bar 3 of the last section has an extra half-beat.

↳ Posted by Bazza on September 1st, 2025.

Re: Jenny Lind

Thanks Bazza, yes the 3rd part has 9 bars, it must be a mistake by Grier the scribe or the written source he’s copying from. I’d suspect the 2nd and 3rd bars should be concatenated. 3rd bar in 4th part is changed now.

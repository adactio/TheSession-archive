Two comments

Much Better Now

From the playing of Old Blind Dogs.

According to the liner notes at Mainly NorFolk (https://mainlynorfolk.info/folk/records/oldblinddogs.html ), the tune was composed by their whistler & border piper, Rory Campbell.

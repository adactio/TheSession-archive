Two comments

The Blue Stocking

This is another one from the John Moore manuscripts (The Shropshire Hornpipe, ed. Gordon Ashman) and which I think is another jolly little one-octave tune that would play equally well one-fifth lower.

See https://thesession.org/discussions/15202

Three comments

“A Memory Of Friends Past” ~ C: c

X: 1 ~ Originally, by accident, in F, adjusted for fiddles in the usual tuning and for octave playing to G, two octaves notated here, low, where it started, then the same an octave up.

X: 2 ~ & D for D fingering for winds whatever key you’ve in hand… ;-)

Music is sometimes akin to Albus Dumbledore’s Pensieve, you know,from the Harry Potter books and movies, those little whisps of memory that the headmaster of ‘Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry’ used to pull out of his head with his wand. :-D No surprise, this happened in the kitchen, baking again… I was thinking, it being a kind of anniversary, of friends past, and this little celebration of those memories just happened, as is, no work involved, other than the baking and a little bit of stepping ~ a smile, a bit of a chuckle ~ and a few tears…

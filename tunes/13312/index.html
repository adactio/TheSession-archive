Re: A Christmas

This seems to be an amalgam of a couple of tunes but it’s essentially “The Long Room of Scarborough” found as a slip jig in the manuscripts of two 18th/19th
century Yorkshire fiddlers, Joshua Jackson and Laurence Leadley. It’s also found in the Vickers manuscripts as a 2 part jig: “The Long Room at Scarborough ”.

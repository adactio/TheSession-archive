Start Again

This is a great little Paul Cranford tune that comes right after another of his, The Rocky Shore, on track 4 of his Lighthouse CD. He lists Gordon MacLean as a co-composer there. I got this .abc from Paul’s on website at https://cranfordpub.com/books/lighthouse.htm. copyrights Cranford Publications, SOCAN

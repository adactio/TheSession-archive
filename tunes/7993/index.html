The foolishness of the past ~ decomissioning railtrack

Yes, ‘the last train’…

This has been something that has been decried for a long time, the loss of railtrack in Eire/Ireland… This includes what happened to Dublin. They closed down track, people claimed the areas for their own, encroached on what had been previously national land, and now the damned city is hell as far as traffic. No politician has had the nerve to claim it back, for the health and welfare of a clogged city.

The rail system in Ireland and the rest of these islands has suffered from lack of forsight, and Cymru/Wales is another place where much of the old tracks were pulled up. Now we are starting to realize what we’ve lost that we need now, more mass transport and less traffic on the roads…

↳ Posted by Simon Styles on November 12th, 2023.

Re: “Last Train From Loughrea” a composition by Vincent Broderick, from his collection…

- as transcribed diligently and accurately by Tony Smith for Vincent’s collection, and reviewed by others, including Seamus MacMathúna…

I think that somewhere here I might have a recording of Vincent and/or his brother Peter playing this, but I have had an initial exploration and not yet found one. Maybe someone else can confirm how Vincent played this, his composition. It would not be the first time a tune’s parts were mixed up by others, variations. However, it is quite common for a concluding part to be on the high as the collection gives it and I’ve transcribed it here. Finishing those last few bars on the descent is another classic conclusion…

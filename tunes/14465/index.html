Re: A Thaidhg A Rún

This setting is in Breathnach CREII.150 (i), collected from Micko Russell in Dublin in 1968, as “A Thaidhg, a Rún”. He couples it with a similar tune collected in Longford called Cailín na Samhaircíní or The Primrose Girl. He links that with The Rose in the Garden in O’Neill’s (DMI. 576), which is on The Session: (https://thesession.org/tunes/8161). I reckon they’re fairly distant cousins.

Hugar Mu Fean

This the Hugar Mu Fean and is followed by an untitled jig.
Not a waltz, and it comes with two variations.
Caledonian Pocket Companion by James Oswald, Book 11, p. 113.

↳ Posted by luddite on March 17th, 2026.

Re: Hugar Mu Fean

Honoria Galwey included the original air in her 1910 “Old Irish Croonauns” and suggested the translation “I Whispered” (although she also said “a true translation is almost impossible.”) However, Bunting published “an ancient air” with the title “Thugamar fein a samhra lin” (We Have Brought the Summer With Us) in his 1796 collection. Earlier, Burk Thumoth included “Hugar Mu Fean” in his Twelve Scotch and Twelve Irish Airs (London c1748), in the Irish section. O’Neill has it in Music of Ireland (1903) as “The Boy of My Heart” and a setting can be found in The Gentleman’s Musical Repository (New York 1813) as “Huggamar Fene On Sambhrulinn” with the alternative title “Send the Bowl Round.”

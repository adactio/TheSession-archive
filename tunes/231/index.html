Paddy O’Carroll

This tune dates back before the time of the American Civil War. During that conflict, a song emerged among pro-Union Irishmen here in the States called “The Opinions of Paddy McGee.” The song used this tune as its melody.

↳ Posted by Munsondr on January 13th, 2002.

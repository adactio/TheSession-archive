Re: The Marquis Of Tullybardine

Composed by John Crerar born in Perthshire c1750 and died in Dunkeld 1840.

↳ Posted by Connor Hickey on September 28th, 2019.

Re: The Marquis Of Tullybardine

This tune appears in Köhler’s Violin Repository Book 1 (of 3), which was published in Edinburgh between 1881 and 1885. It is the second tune, as part of a set of tunes called “The South of the Grampians”, http://trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/book/Kohlers/10682_SOUTH_OF_THE_GRAMPIANS.abc

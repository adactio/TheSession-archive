Peter Flanagan’s Stony Steps (reel)

Another simple yet effective tune from Co. Fermanagh. Despite its name, this tune is probably not related to “The Stoney Steps”: https://thesession.org/tunes/571 I find it more similar to Cathal McConnell tune, Johnny McIljohn’s: https://thesession.org/tunes/1491

This transcription of the tune is based on the version recorded on “Hidden Fermanagh Vol.1,” and is slightly different from the one on the accompanying book.

↳ Posted by slainte on August 12th, 2006.

P.S. This tune always reminds me of great Armagh flute player Ms. Sheena Vallely. I actually heard her play this tune when I was in a session in London this March. I’d love to be back there again to do a flute duet with her.

↳ Posted by slainte on August 12th, 2006.

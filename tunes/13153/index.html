Seamus Ennis’s Fancy

This comes from a recording of the great Dublin piper, when he was at the peak of his powers, in the 50s or early 60s; there were only a handful of tunes on the tape it comes from but I’ve never heard him play better. Where this tune comes from I have no idea, perhaps it’s an obscurity from some county, Seamus collected all over Ireland and he could have picked it up anywhere; or maybe it’s from his piping father’s repertoire. Certainly it makes for a great piping tune.

For his book “The Dance Music of Seamus Ennis” Pat Mitchell transcribed from tapes that were sent to him, and this tape unfortunately wasn’t included, strange as I obtained it from someone who is listed as one of the contributors. I’ve set down what seems to me as the core of the tune, but not a single bar was played the same way twice, and in the 2nd part Seamus seemed to get carried away, playing it 1 1/2 times on the finale, and f ~a3 f~ae and A ~f3 A ~f3 almost seem like variants of each other…so I’m not perfectly certain what the actual tune is supposed to be. Play whatever seems most apt there.

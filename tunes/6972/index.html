Desmond Hickey (Annesbrook Gardens)

An original tune composed on the death of the artist Desmond Hickey, late of Annesbrook House, Duleek, Co. Meath, where his ashes were laid in the gardens, March 2007. The opening notes follow the song of two garden birds. It was written for the C whistle at a moderate tempo but feel free to play it any way you like.

Norwood Junction

Second tune on track 3 of the CD recorded in 2000 by Craobh Rua which I bought recently at a gig by the band celebrating Patrick’s Day at Praema Arts, Uley (nr. Dursley) Gloucestershire.
The track is named ‘The Junction Set’ . I would love to know what prompted the title. I’m hazarding a guess that it may have something to do with canals.
Transcribed by myself. The ‘B’ music is not repeated on the recording in case anyone thinks I’ve omitted the repeat marks.

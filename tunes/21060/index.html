Six comments

Windsong

3/4 strathspey written by Hamish Napier for the Vass family’s yacht that sailed ‘In the Wake of Neil Gunn’ for Mike Vass’ album of that name.

Requested by Matt Flynn (https://thesession.org/discussions/46427). Based on Eabhal’s version, just the barebones, no ormaments or variations.

https://eabhal.bandcamp.com/track/windsong

↳ Posted by DaveF on September 23rd, 2021.

Re: Windsong

If it’s in 3/4 time, it’s not a strathspey.

↳ Posted by Borderer on September 23rd, 2021.

Re: Windsong

It’s defined by the composer as a 3/4 strathspey. I’ve never seen one before, but that’s from the sleeve notes of his album.

Hamish is Scottish, funnily enough, from Strathspey, and is one of the best musicians in the country nowadays. I’m pretty sure he knows what a strathspey is. I guess he had his reasons to call it that…

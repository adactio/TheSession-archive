The Beluga Whale

A lively reel in Bb by Tom Campbell that I came across on his youtube channel. His playing has more variation and nuance than I’ve captured here. Corrections welcome!
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CD6oVRZErCk

↳ Posted by KeithA on February 6th, 2024.

The Beluga Whale, X:2

KeithA, nice tune, thanks for sharing. Your transcription is close enough, except the endings for the 2nd part are off a bit. I hear them as |1 ecAF B3A:|2 e/d/c AF B3G||

And I suspect it’s in Bb only because he’s playing a Bb whistle. As fingered, the tune would come out on a D whistle in a perhaps more session-friendly fashion, in D major—see setting No. 2.

↳ Posted by gimpy on February 7th, 2024.

Re: The Beluga Whale

The Beluga Whale, EP version (X:3)

The A part of this reel evolved quite a bit between Tom’s first YouTube post of it, and the EP the Tom Campbell Trio released this past August. Either way, it’s a brilliant tune, and fine, fine playing by all three lads.

This is the second tune in “The Beluga Whale” set. The first tune is “The Delightful Ranter”, which I just posted (https://thesession.org/tunes/46314).

Snow Leopard

This is a reel from Gráda’s “Endeavour”-CD.

It is the last tune in track 10, a set of 3 reels, consisting of

- The Champion
- Mr. Brennan’s Psychological Problems
- Snow Leopard

The Snow Leopard musically is a real cracker of a tune. Unfortunately really awkward on the pipes… works fine on fiddle, though.

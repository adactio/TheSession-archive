Two comments

Lily’s March

This transcription was requested by eenymo. A very catchy Douglas Montgomery tune which comes before Titting the Scales on The Chair CD. Tempo about 60 bpm (minims). I’ve not put in any ornaments or phrasing, just the bare tune.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on February 6th, 2013.

The second setting has some ornamentation and phrasing added (for fiddle).

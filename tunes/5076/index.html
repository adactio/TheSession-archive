Re: St Lawrence Jig

The tune here is not the ‘St Lawrence Jig’; it’s the ‘Jenny Bell Polka’ from Northern England
The ‘St Lawrence Jig’ is a Quebec/New England tune.
https://tunearch.org/wiki/Annotation:St._Lawrence_Jig

Egi

Heard on the album Frigg by the band of the same name. I transcribed this from their recording a while back, tried to keep melody/chords as close as possible to the track but the repeats here are slightly different. A bit weird trying to fit it into a 6/8 jig - not sure if this actually some type of scandi dance I’m unfamiliar with. Last part is extremely catchy!

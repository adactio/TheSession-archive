Cullentra Trail

The Cullentra Trail is a popular walking trail beginning at Kiltealy in Co. Wexford and winding it’s way around Mt. Leinster. This reel, composed by Nóra Byrne-Kavanagh, was published recently in ‘The Wexford Tunebook’, which included a selection of Nóra’s compositions. Nóra self-published a comprehensive collection of her compositions, titled ‘Mo Cheol’, which was launched in Wexford at the 2024 Comhaltas Fleadh Cheoil na hÉireann.

“Bonny North Tyne” ~ C: Bob Ballantine

This transcription was made from several recordings of the composer playing his own tune… Here’s a YouTube of him doing that ~

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p70ptNaX_nQ
Uploaded by bertosvids on 24 Jun 2009

Brucknell Folk Gathering - Bob Ballantine on Irish Whistle playing ‘Bonny North Tyne’.

“Bob is an inspiration for his melodies that he plays on an early model Susato whistle in D. He generally plays dance music from Northumberland. His favourite Scottish collection of tunes are very popular in the Border Country of England and Scotland. Bob now lives in Linton, Victoria” (Australia).

“Bonny North Tyne” ~ C: Billy Ballantine

On further consideration, I’ve given the basics of Billy’s own take on his tune. However, he usually leaves that second ending, as I’ve given it, for the end, rather than playing up high for every repeat. So, here’s how he usually ends the repeats, without a first and second ending ~

F2 d2 d2 | e/f/e d2 F2 | G6- | G4 :|

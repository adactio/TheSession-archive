Morshiul Na Mara, X:2

This is a version of the tune which I arranged myself, so that the first part would work for Whistle players. Unfortunately, this brought up some G sharps in the second part. Oh, well. You win some and lose some. I hope that whistle players who can half-hole fluently enjoy this tune.

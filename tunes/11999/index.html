Sixteen comments

C: James Scott Skinner

Strictly an air, for muted violin. Could play it for a strathspey dance though.

Original manuscript here: http://www.abdn.ac.uk/scottskinner/display.php?ID=JSS0577
Printed version here: http://www.abdn.ac.uk/scottskinner/display.php?ID=JSS0064
JSS plays it here: http://www.abdn.ac.uk/scottskinner/music/cd213a.m3u
And a YouTube version here: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=drM4STf1EiU

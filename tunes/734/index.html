Two comments

At a session last month we got to joking when someone ask for Mowsy Draggie
instead of Drowsy Maggie. That led me to fool around with the tune and the result is
what I posted. It follows the chords and pattern of Drowsy M. so it might be a companion piece for it.

This is one of my first composition attemps. Any comments will
be appreciated.

JB

↳ Posted by johnbunch on June 5th, 2002.

In the second part, Drowsy maggie goes to a D-major chord, whereas this tune goes to G-major - just in case any guitarists try playing the same chords and can’t work out why it sounds wrong.

Still, it works as a tune. Well done.

Re: Leaving Glen Affric

It needs pointing out that this fine tune was a composition of the late Finlay MacRae, piper, piping judge and the man who can probably claim the most credit for the re-afforestation of Glen Affric with native Scots pine, hence the title.
I had the pleasure of meeting Finlay several times over the years, an absolute gentleman who is much missed by his many friends and family.
This tune was played at the end of his funeral in 2014. RIP, Finlay.

Re: The Cradling Arms Of Cruachan

The bars probably didn’t add up because you included the pickup notes in the first part.

Here’s my take (I’ve only moved the bar lines):
X: 1
T: The Cradling Arms Of Cruachan
R: waltz/whatever
M: 3/4
L: 1/8
K: Dmaj
AAF|:D3E ED|F/(A2 A/) F/E/D |G3A B/c/d|c2 d2 e2|
(f2 f2) ed|d(A2 A) a| ba gf ed|c2 d2 e2|
(f2 f2) ed|d(A2 A) GF|G3A B/c/d|c2 d2 e2|
fa fd Ad|eg ec Ac|eg ec Ac|1 d4 z2:|2d3 Ade||
|:f4 ed|d (A A2) GF|G2 G A B/c/d|c2 d2 e2|
fa3 fe|ed d3a|ba gf ed|c2 d2 .e A|
f4 ed|d (A A2) GF|G2 G A B/c/d|c2 d2 e2|
|fa fd Ad|eg ec Ac|eg ec Ac|1 d3 Ade:|2 D4 z2||

I think this is easier to read (and makes more sense).

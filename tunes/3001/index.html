↳ Posted by Erik-Fiddler on May 12th, 2004.

This is a tune from Shetland by Michael Ferrie (of Fiddlers Bid), a great talent who died very young of cancer. To me it has hints of an O’Carolan style to it. Excellent tune.

↳ Posted by Jamie on May 12th, 2004.

The ABC that reads:

ABd|:g6|d4 cB|A2 a3 g|fd cd ef (the beginning of the third part)

I have heard it played more often with a C sharp rather than a C natural in this bar:

|fd cd ef|

but I have only heard a fiddler from shetland, a fiddler whose been to shetland and Liz Doherty play it. Just out of interest, where’d you get this version from Errik.

↳ Posted by Jamie on May 12th, 2004.

↳ Posted by Erik-Fiddler on May 12th, 2004.

Liz tells a story about playing this tune at an after-hours session in Shetland along with a Scottish fiddler (perhaps the one she learned it from - my possibly faulty memory says it was Eilidh Shaw), both with eyes closed, totally absorbed in the tune. When they finished and looked up, everyone was in tears, having known Michael Ferrie.

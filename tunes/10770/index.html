Two comments

A Manx Traditional dance tune performed on Hop-tu-Naa night (31st October). Couples would dance through the streets bearing turnip lantern.
Original: Kiaull Vannin 1, Rinkaghtyn Vannin. Original transcriptions by Colin Jerry.

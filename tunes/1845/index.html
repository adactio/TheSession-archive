Trounsdells Cross

Hello,

This is the first tune form the Whelan’s Jigs set on Dervish’s Spirit album. Liam Kelly plays this set on a B flat whistle but I’ve transposed it for a regular D whistle or flute. I couldn’t find any other info for this tune: even a Google search can’t bring anything else than Dervish’s site…

This is my first submission to this site, so feel free to comment or point out errors. (And to forestall any other types of comments, English isn’t my first language… :-)

Enjoy the music,

Loa

Re: Trounsdell’s Cross

P.J. King composed this one, according to Kevin Crawford. It was recorded on an elusive album by Grianán, in a set of tunes all composed by P.J. – Trounsdell’s Cross, Home to Ballycashin, Sonny’s Delight.

↳ Posted by alina on January 21st, 2023.

Re: Trounsdell’s Cross

Oh, and according to Kevin Crawford, this tune was originally composed in the key of D, but moved up to G to accommodate whistle/flute, which otherwise wouldn’t be able to hit the lowest note without folding the tune.

Six comments

McEvoy’s No. 2

Transcribed from the playing of Conor McCarthy and Christy Barry on their CD “Late…in the Night.” The liner notes don’t say where they got this tune or who might have composed it. I like its twists and turns, as much in G Dorian as it is in G major.

↳ Posted by Will Harmon on October 4th, 2006.

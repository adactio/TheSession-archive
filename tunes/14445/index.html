Three comments

Cycling In The Rain

I wrote this song two years ago on mandolin but often play it on fiddle, too. It is the first tune in the Lynn Valley set, named after Lynn Valley in North Vancouver. It is a common set we play in the Black Bear Rebels. The other two tunes in the set are Pissing on the Compost and Basel on the Threshold (posted separately).

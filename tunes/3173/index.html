Three comments

The Green Bridge

One of my own here I wrote back in 98 that has crept into the local repertoire slightly. A friend of mine lives in a house in the coastal redwood forest that in order to get there you have to walk across a ravine on a little mossy bridge. He told me the old timers around there call it the Green Bridge.

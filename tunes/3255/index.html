Re: The Victory

Yeah .

From G to D i just move 2 fingers and lift the pinkie G to C is a little trickier but after 50 yrs hacking away the changes , which are within a very limited range after all , open chords , standard tuning are done automatically .
Through G to C my little finger stays on the high G and 3 rd finger ,from the D on the B string jumps to the C on the A .
Second finger goes from the low G on E string to the E on the D string.
1 st finger goes from the B on the A string to the C on the B string.
Thats the mechanics of how I finger the chords .

It also depends of course on your instrument! Box players might find it much easier . I think i played this with Seamus Bugler years ago

But whether we can play the chords , i play tenor guitar more often these days , is another issue . Thats the structure

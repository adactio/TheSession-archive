Two comments

Transcribed from Jerry Holland & John Doyle’s Helping Hands CD. Written for the wedding of Paul Cranford and Sarah Beck in 2007.
Jerry takes it at a gentle 99 bpm for a laid back feel.
A variation on the B part is:
gage g/g/g eg|abag a/a/a ge|gage d/d/d Bd|egdB AGAB|.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on January 12th, 2010.

Re: Cranford’s Five Day Wedding

Pulling together notes from the liner notes and the Cranford website…. the wedding party went on for five days of music and food, and only stopped because everyone had to go off to Ryan MacNeil’s wedding.

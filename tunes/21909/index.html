Trippin On Green Mushrooms

When you travel 400kms south of Perth (we say “go down south”) you travel through very beautiful, lush green forest. It’s a wonderful journey… much like part one of this tune.

In this forest are hallucinogenic mushrooms… and after consumption you’ll feel a bit like part two of this tune.

At the time of writing this note, the Session player ignores the tempo - and plays these tunes way, way too fast. So, when/if you play it - slow it down and enjoy the ride :)

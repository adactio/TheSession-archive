What is “Smoke in Your Eyes?”

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on October 27th, 2025.

Re: Around The House And Mind The Dresser

Nigel, Smoke In Your Eyes is a tune book created by Kevin Gow from Seattle. It documents the numerous tunes played at Seattle sessions over the last few decades and contains several settings of popular tunes not played/heard elsewhere. I am currently working with Kevin to have the book republished and we are attempting to ID/correct some errors.

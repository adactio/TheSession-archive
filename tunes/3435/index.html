↳ Posted by gian marco on August 23rd, 2004.

Waiting for a Call

This is a great, fun tune. It’s one of my favorites on that album. I particularly like the way he pauses for just a fraction of a second before the low notes and then hits them crisp and clean.
Thank you, Gian Marco!
Sara

↳ Posted by sara g on August 24th, 2004.

Waiting for A Call - surprisingly class tune on the whistle!

Heard this obviously first on Tommy Peoples album of the same name, i assume, but with no great yen to learn it. Then i’ve been recently listening to Brid Harper’s second album, of which it is the first tune. I assumed somehow cos it was dark and moody that it would have difficulties on a non-chromatic instrument - didn’t even try.

Nevertheless, it got in my head anyway, and I found myself noodling a bit of it on the whistle - got most of the A part and the B part before I realised what tune it was. C-part bit more tricky. Brid’s version is a bit more accessible than the version here, at least for whistle. Anyway, it turns out to be difficult but not impossibly so, and sounds surprisingly good on whistle. The A part especially.

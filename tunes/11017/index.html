S:J.A. MacLellan, 1967. More Music for the Highland Bagpipe

Tune is used for the Scottish step dance The New Claret, played as 32 bars by dropping the repeats for the 3d and 4th parts.

For the step dance play the slip jig New Claret 1x, Pipe Major Jimmy Pryde 1x, Hurlock’s Reel 1x, then back to New Claret 1x.

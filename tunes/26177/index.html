Three comments

Lament For Prophet

This tune is NOT a reel - it is a beautiful slow air - but there is no entry for “slow air” available.

It was written by Tom Anderson to honour Prophet James Laurence Smith.

↳ Posted by Steve J Hunt on December 9th, 2025.

Lament For Prophet, X:2

Lovely slow air, Steve - there’s a couple of mistakes in your transcription, but I thought I’d offer the tune as published by the composer. He published it alongside another of his tunes, “Da Alamootie” and of them he said, “A slow air and reel composed in 1981 for the late Prophet Smith of Bressay. The ‘Alamootie’ is the Shetland name for the Stormy Petrel, and Prophet was a man of many parts and could be a ‘Stormy Petrel’ when defending his beloved Shetland.” (Ringing Strings, Lerwick 1983)

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on December 9th, 2025.

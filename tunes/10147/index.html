Another one for the Flook collection…

Before too much longer there won’t be any more of these to post, so those who aren’t big into Flook needn’t worry…

This tune was written by Brian and Sarah of Flook, and comes from their “Haven” album, originally in A by playing in D on an A whistle.

So, more like this.

The Coillte Feannaid

Surprised this isn’t already here. A nice Donegal reel I transcribed from the playing of James Byrne. I know of at least two recordings of this tune. Note that the first part is played as eight bars while the second part gets played as sixteen. It’s similar to the situation with “Old Cuffe Street”, “Paddy McLaughlin’s” (another James Byrne reel) and others.

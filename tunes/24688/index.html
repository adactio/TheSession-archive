Tom Byrne’s

This is another recent reel by the Wexford town accordionist and prolific composer, Tom Byrne. Now in his 80’s, it was great to see him get a deal of recognition for his talents and life-long contribution to Irish traditional music in the Wexford area during the recent 2024 Fleadh Cheoil na hÉireann. Faoi dheireadh, as you might say. This particular reel, he also broke with his tradition of not naming his compositions. It was, of course, like all of his tunes, included in my publication, ‘The Wexford Tunebook’. Tom asked if there was a tune already submitted by anyone else titled, ‘The Wexford Reel’. When I told him there wasn’t, he said that maybe this one might have that title.

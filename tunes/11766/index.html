Five comments

It’s nothing to do with me in spite of the title but this is a cracking tune composed by George Meikle. The last two parts are actually variations.

This tune was used as part of a special “Homecoming dance” in 2009 as part of the Homecoming Scotland nonsense.
Also, while not a Shetland tune as such, it can(and has done) easily fit into a Shetland set.

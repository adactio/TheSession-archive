The Happy Hornpipe

I learned this from the CD “Dreaming Up The Tunes” by Johnny Og Connolly And Brian McGrath, and according to the liner notes it was written by Mairtin O’Connor. It may be on his difficult-to-find Chatterbox album, but that is pure speculation.

↳ Posted by Limeretti Lumeretti Hillhocker on June 17th, 2007.

Your speculation is true - the tune is the second of two hornpipes on the Chatterbox album. The name of the track is ‘The Bumblebee’.

This is interesting: X:2 is posted as a version for flute, which suggests that it’s more suited to the instrument than X:1. The question isn’t meant to be nit-picky. Settings of tunes are often posted as suitable for particular instruments, but the reasons for the suitability aren’t always spelled out and may not be apparent - which leaves the likes of me wondering how the tune/instrument match has been arrived at.

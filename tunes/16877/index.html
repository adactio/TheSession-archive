Tide At The Taing

Orkney tune by Jennifer Wrigley. I think Owen Tierney’s boat was caught by the Taing, unpredictable tide at island of Gairsay. It vanished but later reappeared, hence this excellent tune.

↳ Posted by suskin on March 27th, 2018.

Tide At The Taing, X:3

With chords, as played on the original recording on Dancing Fingers.
In reality, the melodic rhythm E3 E E2 is more like E2>>E2 E2 (or E2- E3/2E/2 E2), but for ease of reading I’ve left it as in SusanK’s transcription.

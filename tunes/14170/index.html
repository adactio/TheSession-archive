↳ Posted by Gonzalo Llao on February 6th, 2015.

The Al-Alándalus, X:2

I hope the composer of this wonderful tune doesn’t mind me doing a bit of a make-up to the ABC notation. The audio output is still the same.

I did the following to the ABC:

- I showed the repeated parts with different endings.

- I grouped a few quavers that were loose.

I don’t know if I will be able to play this some day but I love listening to it and I had some fun with the ABC.

Not sure if the composer did it on purpose to make a kind of joke, but the name of the tune is a misspelling of Al-Andalus. This was the name of the Iberian Peninsula given by the Moors during the Middle Ages.

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on October 2nd, 2020.

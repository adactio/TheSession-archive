Re: Mrs Crawford’s Favourite

This tune was published in Robert Petrie’s 1790 collection as “Forneth House” before the Gows published it as “Mrs Crawford’s Favourite” in Niel Gow’s Fourth Collection (c1801).

One of the joys of living in Perthshire, arguably one of the centres of 18th century Scottish fiddling, is that while you travel around you inevitably pass by the names of hundreds of tunes. I remember the excitement of driving past a gate on which was a sign which said “Forneth House.” Yes, I get great pleasure from silly little things like that!

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on February 5th, 2025.

Mrs Crawford’s Favourite, X:2

The aforementioned “Forneth House” from Robert Petrie’s first collection ( c1790). Petrie (1767-1830) was from Kirkmichael in Perthshire, less than 20 miles from Forneth House.

The Woods Of Caol Rua, X:2

This is roughly the setting of this gorgeous jig as played (in a different tuning) by Dave Sheridan, Tom Morrow, Padraig McGovern and John McCartin at the very end of Episode 4, Season 1 of the ‘Scoil Samhraidh Joe Mooney’ programme on TG4. When I heard the tune on the show, I immediately became obsessed with finding out what it was—not realizing that I’d actually heard it before (played in a session in Belfast) and had even already added it to my tunebook here! It’s got a lovely hypnotic droney feel and some interesting intervals, particularly in this setting. It’s my new favorite tune.

https://www.tg4.ie/en/player/play/?pid=6389521315112&title=Scoil%20Samhraidh%20Joe%20Mooney

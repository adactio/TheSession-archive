Four comments

Green Grow The Rushes - O (Newfoundland)

A very distant relative of the Irish and other European versions, from the Newfoundland accordion player Minnie White. It got lots of precise turns and a strong “snap” to it.

Its an earworm from from Liz Giddings & Roger Digby’s CD ‘The Passing Moment’.

Very popular pipe jig

This pipe jig is a Donald Macleod composition and very popular in sessions around Scotland. It gets played by Irish musicians too. I don’t always play it exactly the same way each time but this is the usual version.

Rory MacLeod, X:2

Here’s the version published in the Edcath Collection, which I assume is the original Donald MacLeod composition.

It was also published in Donald MacLeod’s own collection, which I no longer have, so I can’t check to make sure.

The c>AA in bars 1, 3, and 5 of the first part is the same as bar 8 of all four parts, a complex bagpipe movement which has the overall effect I’ve tried to capture in a simplified way.

Rory MacLeod, X:3

The setting my Pipe Band uses. I don’t know its origin.

We don’t have the complex ornament that occurs in the original Donald MacLeod setting in every Bar 8 and also in Bars 1, 3, and 5 in the first part, rather we play plain notes.

We do retain the feature of the original tune of having Bars 1 and 5 of the 2nd part begin on A rather than C#.

Note the resting places in the 4th part, where the original tune keeps churning along.

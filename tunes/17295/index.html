Memories Of Eva

Here’s a tune composed by Patti Kusturok (aka Lamoureux) assisted by her son Alex in memory of her late mother.

It’s a lovely old timey waltz which I’ve recently learned..

We also repeated the tune in the key of A which makes for a nice and effective change in my opinion.
However, I’ve only transcribed the G version here.

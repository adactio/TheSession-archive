Medley Two Step - Acushla

This two step appears in the Reilly MSS (c1900) of Drumreilly, Co Leitrim transcribed by their friend and neighbour the Longford fiddler John Masterson (d.1912). Second part appears to have an extra 4 bars at the beginning

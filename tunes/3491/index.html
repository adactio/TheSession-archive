Gigue De Joliette (jig)

Source: Chris Norman - The Beauty of the North
Origin: Quebec
Transcription: gmo
Note: The Chris Norman setting is in F.

↳ Posted by gian marco on September 6th, 2004.

Nice to see tunes from Chris’s CDs popping up here. His music is what essentially got me into Irish music (yeah I know, he’s not technically an Irish flutist), so I hear tunes like these quite vividly in my head. This one in particular, really sings nicely. Thanks for the post gian marco.

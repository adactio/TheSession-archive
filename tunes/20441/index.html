What I Want

This tune is in the ‘’New Irish Tin Whistle Tunes‘’, new recording by Olaf Sickmann.. According to the album notes:
‘’What do I want? This is a perpetual question to which I keep finding new and sometimes surprising answers. At the moment, however, I just want to bring out a really good tin whistle album.‘’
AND HE INDEED DID IT!! This album is perfect and the control of the tin whistle that Olaf performs is amazing!

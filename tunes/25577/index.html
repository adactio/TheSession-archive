Inis Bearachain

Heard this in a session, asked the box player if it came with a name, he said it’s “the red box”. A few sessions later, he told me he might actually have learnt it from here, where the guy calls it Ríl Inis Bearachain: https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=500055735394807

I tried looking it up but couldn’t find any other sources for it as is, nor as Inishbarra, and it is obviously not related to the red box reel ( https://thesession.org/tunes/1386 ), and has nothing to do with the Inis Bearachain jig. Any ideas? Anyway.. great tune!

Re: Inis Bearachain

Intriguing question.. I had to look it up. Apparently, Beare (or Bere, or Bear) island is Oiléan Béarra (Co. Cork) in Irish, but Inishbarra (Co. Galway) is Inis Bearachain. That doesn’t exclude a mixup at the source, anyway!

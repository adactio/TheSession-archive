The Earl Of Errol

My only source for this tune is the song The Earl of Errol as sung by Chris Wright, track 8 on The Speaking Heart, an album recorded with Lucy Pringle in 2010.
The song has been recorded several times and there is more info about it here:
https://pringlewright.bandcamp.com/track/the-earl-o-errol
although the notes to this track say nothing about the music itself.

So, I wrote the notes down as they can be heard in the first verse, although I chose the piping key of D maj. as opposed to F maj. on the album h(the melody is pentatonic).
Changes in the prosody necessarily bring about slight rhythmic changes. In addition, the singer holds some of the notes and wee instrumental ‘breaks’ can be heard in between the verses, so the score here is not an exact transcript of what goes on on the album.

I will post a slightly different version of this melody to bring it closer, style-wise, to an Irish polka.

Re: The Earl Of Errol

words for this version:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E6xYHl3Ac2w
reprinted from:
https://mainlynorfolk.info/folk/songs/theearloferrol.html

1.
The Earl o Errol has a fine big hoose
It stands in yonder glen
But whit’s the guid o an Earl’s hoose
When he’s no like ither men?
[Chorus]:
O the rantin o’t, the dantin’ o’t
Accordin as ye ken
O the thing they ca the dantin o’t
Lady Errol lies her lane
2.
“As shair’s yer name is Kate Carnegie
Mine is Gibbie Hay
I’ll gar yer faither sell his lands
Yer tocher for tae pay!”
3.
“Tae gar ma faither sell his lands
Wid that no be a sin?
Tae gie it tae a noughtless Lord
Wha canna get a son?”
4.
So she’s awa tae Edinburgh
For tae try the law
And Errol he has follaed her
His manhood for tae show
5.
Up then spak the youngest judge
The wisest o them aa
“I’ve never heard o a lady yet
Wha brocht sic things tae Law!”
6.
“It’s ye’ll bring oot yer weel-faured maids
Stand them in a row
And ye’ll pick oot ae weel-faured maid
Yer standard for tae show”
7.
He’s picked oot ae weel-faured maid
The flooer amang them aa
An twenty times he’s kissed her mou
And led her through the ha
8.
They twa hae liggit in ae big bed
The lords aa standin roon
And they cried oot wi ane accord
“He’s proved hissel a man!”
9.
“Hud up yer heid noo Peggy lass
Dinna think it shame!
And I’ll gie you a hunder pounds
Tae bear tae me a son”
10.
They hae shut young Peggy up
Three quarters o the year
And when full nine months they were up
A braa new son she bare
11.
“Tak yer dochter Carnegie
Pit her in the glen!
For she’s no fit for Errol’s bed
Nor ony o his men!”

and that’s that.

The 70th Year

This is a jig, with sixteen-bar parts, by Charlie McKerron.
It was brought to my attention by a member here, Bren, who, a year ago was learning it and looking for dots: https://thesession.org/discussions/48133.
I happened to have Bowstroke by Gregor Borland (https://thesession.org/recordings/4594), which includes the tune, and learnt it from there.
The tune had been on my back burner until I heard it again at a house session last weekend.

There is clip of Gregor, Gary West and Sandy Brechin (as the trio BrÒg) playing the tune:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NYFrPPpLxQQ

Innes Campbell

Written by Rory Campbell, and transcribed from Duncan Chisholm’s album “Affric”. Medium paced, but driven. If you can’t interpret the symbols, 4th part (identical to 2nd part) is x3, and at “D.C.” jump to the final two bars - the “coda” (play that slowly). Coda not essential. Listen below (starts 0:37).
Still some slurs and ornaments to add, I think. I’ll be checking out the original soon enough.

https://youtu.be/az5gvp0SkGA?t=36s

A tune I wrote sometime last year on a borrowed Ormiston flute.

Written for a *ahem* particularly attractive friend of mine, which I spent around an hour and a half talking to while at a church event.
When I got home, I was pretty happy and this tune eventually popped out of me somewhere.

Hopefully, y’all will like it, and somewhere I’ve got a recording of myself playing it…

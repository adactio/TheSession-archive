Tweedside Lasses

This is a beautiful little air (NOT a reel!) I found in the Alexander Walker Collection. Troy MacGillivray does an exceptionally great piano version on his Booomerang CD.

↳ Posted by jenny on August 18th, 2009.

The Tweedside Lasses, X:2

My take on this. Written by Alexander Walker (1819 - 1900), from Aberdeenshire.

I learnt this tune from a member of our session at the Babbity Bowster in Glasgow. She’s also a member of TheSession. I’ve been playing it for years so it’s most probably diverted from the way she plays it.

Re: Scott MacMorran’s

Great tune, and thanks for posting this. Just fyi, in the video it appears to me that Will is playing an A chanter. So, the tune is sounding in D. In your first setting, you’ve got the right notes, but it reads an octave lower than how a piper would read it. Hope that makes sense.

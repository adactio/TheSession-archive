McDermott’s

This is a slightly modified setting of tune #850 in O‘Neill’s 1001, McDermott’s HP (not the same as the other McDermott’s hornpipes in the database). The original has an uneven number of beats when repeats and pickups are accounted-for, so this is a straightened-out setting with a few changes to make it flow better for me. A direct transcription is at the Traditional Tune Archive as McDermott’s_Hornpipe_(2) (I can’t get the link to play nicely in the comments editor here due to the parenthesis).

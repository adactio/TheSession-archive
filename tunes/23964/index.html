The Blossom Tree

The Blossom Tree was composed by Laoise Ní Chinnéide who is apart of CCÉ Ormond, Tiobraid Árann (Tipperary). She earned 1st Place in the “newly composed tunes” competition at Fleadh Cheoil na hÉireann 2022.

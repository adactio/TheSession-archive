Gyn Ennym Connections

The first part of this jig is very like the first part of St Patrick’s Day (the second part being completely different to this one: https://thesession.org/tunes/385). It’s also very like both parts of the Welsh jig Glandyfi, but that’s more of a single jig with a crotchet/quaver pattern (https://thesession.org/tunes/14512).

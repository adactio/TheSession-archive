Orlagh’s, X:2

This is the setting I’ve played of Orlagh’s for several years. A beautiful Mayock tune which includes an interesting “C” part, that fits the tune very well. The actual key of the A and B parts is F#m, and the C part is in A dorian, resolving to F#m.

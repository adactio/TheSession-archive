Four comments

My Sweetheart Jane

A simple melodic reel from “ O‘ Neill’s Music of Ireland ”, A great book with 1850 airs, jigs, reels, hornpipes, long dances, marches and a good selection of O’ Carolan’s compositions. Well worth getting hold of a copy.

↳ Posted by Atanos on March 24th, 2003.

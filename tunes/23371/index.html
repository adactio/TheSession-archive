Four comments

Miss Polly O’Brien

This hornpipe is from Paddy Haye’s second album, ‘The Shores of Lough Melvin’. It’s composed in honour of his mother. Thanks to box player Liam Flynn from Adelaide who played it for me on his recent trip here to Wexford. I’d been looking for it for a while to try to complete the compositions of Paddy Hayes for the Wexford Tune Project Collection. This particular hornpipe has echoes of several other tunes in it but is distinctive enough I think.

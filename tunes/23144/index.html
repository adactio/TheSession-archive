Pallas Bardic School

A fine céilí band march composed by All-Ireland winner Pat Bass from Annagh near Gorey. He originally composed it for the Taobh na Mara céilí band to play. It’s title refers to a famous bardic school form th16th and 17th centuries that operated from a townland close to Pat’s homeplace. The Pallas Bardic school is reputed to have been the school attended by Cearbhall Ó Dálaigh, the famous bardic poet who composed Eileanor na Rún.

Time signature? bar lines?

Why 12/8? A muineira is in 6. In any case tha bar lines are in the wrong place by three quavers. The first three notes of the tune are an anacrusis. When I’ve mastered ABC - or have found something to convert standard notation into ABC, I’ll post up the traditional version.

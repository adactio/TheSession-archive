Re: Miss Crawford

The strathspey “Miss Crawford” is directly above the reel “Miss Crawford” on page 15 of “Kerr’s First Collection Of Merry Melodies for thr Violin”. Both are attributed as “by A. Mc. Alpin” [sic ].
Presumably this is the same “Archibald McAlpine - born Ayr 22nd November 1811, died Mauchline, 17th November 1882. Violinist and violin maker, self-taught in music. He lost his sight at an early age from an accident, but his strong natural genius enabled him to become an excellent player upon the violin, and maker of some capital violins, for which he received high prices. As an arithmetician, too, his gifts were wonderful. From about 1816 he resided in Mauchline, where he was much esteemed for his abilities and his social qualities. A most feeling and appreciative notice of him was written by Mr. George Julian Harney, well-known in former years as a Chartist leader - at that time in America”.
Quote from “Musical Scotland, Past and Present…. ” by David Baptie.

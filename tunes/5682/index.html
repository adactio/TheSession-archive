Galizian Xota

This is a fast Galizian Dance tune in 3/4 (hopefully the sheet music will reflect that which I put in the ABCs) usually played at around 200. It was written by Susana Seivane for her sister which is who it is named after. I learned it off of her Alma de Buxo album which I posted earlier today.

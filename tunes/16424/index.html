Two comments

2-Rivisjenkka

A great scottishe by Antti Järvelä. Its name translates as “2-Rowed Scottishe”, so named as it was originally written to be easily played by a 2 row accordion. You can listen to it a clip of it being played by Antti and the participants of Ethno Finland here: https://www.facebook.com/ethnofinland/videos/1608523322514756/

↳ Posted by pbsinclair42 on October 5th, 2017.

Crossing Rubicon

I tried my best to get this awesome tune from listening to Brian Finnegan play. I figure it’s not a reel, but I’m not sure what it is. I tried capturing as much ornamentation as possible in the sheet music as well. As you can see, I struggled with some of the rhythms, so if improvements can be made, feel free to chime in. Re: Crossing Rubicon

Hey there,
thanks a lot for the transcription - for the most part its pretty accurate. However, all those added time-signatures are not necessariy. He’s staying in 4/4 for the whole tune.
All those quarter-triplets lead to the 1 of the next bar. I will try to clarify the corrections here, I will only talk about the “B” part, though, so 5th staff and on:
- in the 3/4 bar kick the quarter-note rest
- add a quarter-note rest after the long B in the 5/4 bar
- in the bar starting with the high A (6th staff): both Bs in this bar should be tied together (so resulting in a half-note), don’t tie to the next bar!

Then all this should pan out to 4/4, if I didn’t fail this correction here. If this was too complicated or too hard to follow, I can clarify via sheet-music or direct email etc.
Hope this helps, thanks again for your work,
Patrick

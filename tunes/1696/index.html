Ronnel’s Reel

Taken from the playing of Kathryn Tickell I don’t know anything more about the origin of this tune. But goes well with the Hesleyside Reel, and Hindly Steel.

Ronell’s Reel

In the liner notes it says it’s “Ronell”, not “Ronnel”, and the composer was Alison Crawford from Edinburgh.

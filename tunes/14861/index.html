Fraser Arms, The

This is a tune I learned out of Simon Fraser’s old collection of Scottish tunes. I’m partial to it because of my Clan Fraser heritage. Don’t know of any recordings.

↳ Posted by Paul Damico-Carper on October 28th, 2015.

Nice tune-- record it!

The Fraser Arms, X:2

After playing it through a few times, I kept thinking that the Gs at the close of each part really wanted to be held to full length. The Fraser version has a pickup of a quaver, like in Paul’s setting, but also has the parts end with a crotchet. I see what you were doing with this, in trying to even out the bar length problems caused by the pickup note, and it sounds good that way. This setting is another way of trying to smoothe out the rhythm-- I removed the pickup note and otherwise used the notes from the Airs & Melodies (http://imslp.org/wiki/The_Airs_and_Melodies_Peculiar_to_the_Highlands_of_Scotland_and_the_Isles_%28Fraser,_Simon%29 - p. 59).

A note for accompanists -- it seems like a solidly Bb major tune, not G minor, though every four bars end with a G minor chord. There’s no option for Bb on thesession, which is fine.

↳ Posted by benhockenberry on October 29th, 2015.

Ben is quite right. Would’ve put it in B-flat but that’s not an option here.

Two comments

I Didn’t Drink The Rum

From the playing of Tickle Harbour. Traditional tune.

They learned it from Chris Kavanagh in Flatrock, Newfoundland. Given the crooked structure, fairly safe to say it’s from the island.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on August 12th, 2019.

I Didn’t Drink The Rum, X:2

Squared-up setting.

Could also play the last bar of the last repeat in the B Part as “GA/G/ FD” to loop back into the A Part.

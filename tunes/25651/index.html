Two comments

The Buchan Barn Dance

In spite of its name, this is a reel named after an event called the Buchan Barndance held at Haddo House in Aberdeenshire. I have transcribed it from the wonderful 2018 album The Copperplate Sessions by Sligo fiddler Manus McGuire and friends, but alas, I don’t have any sleeve notes, so I can’t say who wrote it. He is joined on the track by his friend Marie Fielding, so it’s possibly one of hers, but that’s a guess. Perhaps someone else will know.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on June 17th, 2025.

Re: Violet’s

©Clare Connors, Columbia MO, 2017 (From “HAPPY TUNES: A Compilation of Original Music by Clare Connors and other Columbia, MO Slow Jammers, Summer 2018, Pg 36)

Tune Notes: This is for Violet, one of Heinrich’s twin granddaughters. We often play “Amelia’s Waltz” by Bob McQuillen in honor of Amelia, Violet’s twin sister. “Violet’s Waltz” came about because Violet didn’t have her own tune. We now play the two together.

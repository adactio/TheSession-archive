I Do Not Incline

Roughly transcribed from the playing of Jack Talty and Cormac Begley on their album Na Fir Bolg, where it appears in a set with a jig named as “Cronin’s”, which is also unfamiliar to me. Might have a crack at that one later, but I figured I’d make sure it isn’t already on the site under a different name since there are three other very different tunes on that same album also named some variation of “Cronin’s”. Anyways, this one appears to be an older tune since it is also listed in roughly this form in O’Neill’s Dance Music of Ireland, yet even older transcriptions exist of it as an air.

I Do Not Incline, X:2

This is my attempt at transcribing the version played by Jack Talty & Cormac Begley on their album. I didn’t include the variations and ornaments. I will point out just one variation, fairly often one or either or both of them plays the the main phrase as C2D F2G ABA GFG instead of CA,C F2G ABA GFG.

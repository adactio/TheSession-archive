The Isle Of Aigas

A beautiful Scottish air.
I first heard a recording of this on the Fiddlers’ Bid album Hamnataing. This is a close approximation to that arrangement, for fiddle.

Note: Fdor was the closest I could get for the key. It’s actually in Cmin.

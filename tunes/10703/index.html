Ganglat fran Mockfjard

Ganglat are best described as “Walking Tunes” and are best kept at a slow march pace - this one is by Anders Frisell

Gånglåt Från Mockfjärd

I used to play this in a folk group last century - great tune. We played in with harmonies to get a slightly more Swedish feel to it.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on May 1st, 2013.

From what I gather, this tune is from the playing of Anders Frisell (“efter Anders Frisell”), not actually composed by (“av”) him. Anders was from Mockfjärd (in Dalarna) and lived from 1870 - 1944.

↳ Posted by Weejie on May 1st, 2013.

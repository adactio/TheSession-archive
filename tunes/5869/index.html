The Bag Of Praties, X:2

I was tempted to add some variations to this transposition to A mixolydian, but decided against since the original is taken from an old collection of Irish tunes.
The melody has an unusual rap-like run of identical notes -unusual at least by 20th century Irish standards- perhaps an American influence.

The tune is better adapted to the flute or whistle in A than in D since the C natural is difficult to roll on these instruments. You could of course use other techniques when playing it in D.

There is no 4th in it, xhich makes it suitable for playing on the trump/jews harp.
In A, the tune falls wholy in the range of the Scottish pipes.

The First Snow

The weather seems to have taken a turn for the worse “Here in Scotland” and I thought it appropriate to post this tune.

This is a nice waltz which isn’t to be confused with another tune of the same name on this site.

Charlie Soane is a well respected fiddler player, originally from Perthshire. He used to perform regularly with Brian Millar in the seventies and later with The Occasionals although only occasionally. ;-)
He is also a member of Arthur Johnstone’s Star Band.

Padraig O’Keefe’s No. 1

This slide from the repertoire of Padraig O‘Keeffe was recorded by the Mulcahy family on “Notes From The Heart” CD. “Session.org” member “Bribak” requested a name for it 4 days ago, but no one has come up with any answer. If it’s here already under a different name, I couldn’t find it, so I’ve transcribed it myself. it’s yet another “Padraig O’Keeffe’s” until someone comes up with a “more original” title.
The Mulcahy’s play it in “C”.

↳ Posted by Kenny on March 4th, 2018.

Three comments

Jackson’s Maid

A nice melodic jig, as it can be found in O’Neill’s 1850 (#1066) as well as in his 1001 (#272).
The former version is with f naturals in bars 3 and 5 of the B part; but I’m not entirely convinced of that. Oh well, it’s O’Neill…

B/-c/|ded dcB|cdc cBc|ded =fef|dgg g2a|
bag =fed|cAG FGA|(B/c/d)B AFD|DGG G2:|

Apart from that, both versions are similar.

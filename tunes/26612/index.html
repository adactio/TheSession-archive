Smoking In The Kitchen, X:2

This is the setting I’m currently playing, very slightly adapted from the pipe setting (I play slightly more quarter notes/crotchets than are in the pipe setting).
I’ve beamed the third part to better represent the three against two rhythm. The fourth part is rhythmically just like the third part of Troy’s Wedding, but descending rather than ascending.

You can hear the tune played on the pipes here:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LdTIElJBZQA
It seems it was popular on the pipe band scene twenty to thirty years ago.

Black Cat, White Cat

Black Cat, White Cat; also known as Black Cat or commonly as Black Cat Piddled In The White Cat’s Eye. Based on the Winster Gallop but is a different tune. Black Cat originates from truwana/Cape Barren Island off the coast of lutruwita/Tasmania in Australia from the late pakana/palawa fiddle player Les Brown. Is commonly referred to as a polka but is always written in 4/4 hence being added here as a barn dance.

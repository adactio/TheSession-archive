Tuskar Rock

First of a pair of reels composed by the Wexford accordionist Paddy Hayes and featured on his second album, ‘The Shores of Lough Melvin’. Tuskar Rock, just to the east of the County Wexford coastline, is a singular land mark in the entire area . The light from it’s lighthouse is visible along the coast and quite a ways inland due to the flat landscape of the Barony of Forth. ‘Skar’ or ‘Scar’ is believed to be an old Norse word reflecting the area’s connections with the Vikings. An area of Wexford town, to the north, near the railway station, is called ‘Selskar’ and the home of well known Wexford balladeer and son collector Paddy Berry is Scar in Duncormick.

Re: Tuskar Rock

Unfortunately Paddy passed away a couple of years ago. He was born in Piercestown just outside of Wexford town. Both he and his brother Tommy were excellent 2 row players. Paddy moved to London where he was part of that vibrant trad scene there of the late 60s on. He was a regular visitor back to Wexford and we as young musicians eagerly awaited his summer visit from London as he’d be bringing back great tunes. He was also mighty craic to be around. I think Finbar would have been in London too too around the same time. If you click on his composer name above you’ll see links to his other compositions collected so far. I’ve also just lately made contact with his daughter and hopefully that might yield more.

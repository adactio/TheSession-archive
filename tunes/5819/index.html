Two comments

East Winds

This reel appears on Alistair Anderson’s Steel Skies and also the Syncopace album. I think he composed it but I’m not 100% sure. There’s a harmony that can be played as a separate tune:

X: 1
T: East Winds (harmony)
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Emin
eg~g2 egb2|a2ef gbag|fefg b2ef|g2ef edBA|
FA~A2 Bdfa|geBe f4|eg~g2 egbg|1 afdf e4:|2 afdf e2A2||
|:BGEG A2GF|G2EF GABd|egbg a2gf|g2Bd cBAc|

East Winds

Yes, it is one he composed - it’s the name of a cottage just outside Embleton, on the coast. Cracking reel, and that’s a great harmony.

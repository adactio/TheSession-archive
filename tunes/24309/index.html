Ah Ca Ira

First heard in a session in Kent. The tune was popular during the French Revolution and was played on a fife.

↳ Posted by Alan Macfadyen on May 20th, 2024.

Re: Ah Ca Ira

Wikipedia :
“A violinist at the Théâtre des Beaujolais, Bécourt composed a number of dance tunes for this show, some of which were very popular. Among these tunes was a contredanse that became known as the Carillon national. It became very popular. The Queen of France, Marie-Antoinette, often played it on her harpsichord. It was to this tune that a street singer named Ladré, in 1789, arranged the lyrics of the revolutionary song Ah! ça ira, which was sung during the night of October 5 to 6, by insurgents at the Château de Versailles, and which was sung by the people within the people until the end of the Reign of Terror. It’s the same tune that was given an accelerated tempo for the corps de musique of the armies of the French Republic.”
Among the lyrics : “Aristocrats we’ll hang them”.

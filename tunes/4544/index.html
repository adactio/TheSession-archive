Slow air by Paul Machlis

This is a slow air and not a reel and was posted as a response to a request here.

This lovely tune was actually composed by Paul Machlis, a keyboard player who has accompanied Alisdair Fraser on previous occasion.

↳ Posted by 🎻 Melody Player 🎻 on May 29th, 2005.

Bennachie sunrise

I’d never seen this site, but needed to check on a trad tune, and then thought I’d see if any of my tunes were in. As for Bennachie, any similarity to Foxhunter’s is indeed a coincidence, but when I came up with the tune, I was a bit uncomfortable with its similarity to the Four Season’s hit “Walk like a Man!” Perhaps someday Rudy Vallee will do a cover of it. Paul Machlis

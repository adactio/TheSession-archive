Patsy Cawley’s

Taken from ‘The Rookery’, a 2012 album from Emmett Gill and Jesse Smith. It’s such a good tune I suspect it might be on thesession.org already, but I haven’t been able to find it. Anyone recognise it? Patsy Cawley was a Doocastle fiddler who recorded some 78rpm discs.

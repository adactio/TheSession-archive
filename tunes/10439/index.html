Source

Another great 7/8 tune by Michael McGoldrick. Transcription is based on the recording of the tune on *Aurora*, where it is played on an F whistle. Where I’ve marked ~g2, Michael plays either fg, g2, or gz. Mix to suit :-)

For those on other instruments wishing to play along with the recording, here’s the tune transposed:

K: Gmin
|: ~b2 gf dcB | cc dc BGF | GG BG FGB | cc Bc dfg |
~b2 gf dcB | cc dc BGF | GG BG FGB | cc BA FG2 :|
|: ~b2 gf dfg | ~b2 gf dbg | bc‘ c’c‘ bc’d’ | ~c‘2 gb c’d’g |
~b2 gf dfg | ~b2 gf dbg | bc‘ c’c‘ bc’d’ | ~c’2 bg fg2 :|

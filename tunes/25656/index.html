Two comments

The Rover

Heard on Raidio Na Gaeltachta played by box player Ryan Sheridan, it was recorded by Tony De Marco many years ago.

↳ Posted by Sláine on June 18th, 2025.

The Rover, X:2

This how it appears in Kerr’s Merry Melodies Book 4 (Glasgow c1875) under the title “Rover.”

Tony DeMarco recorded it on his 2008 album Sligo Indians. He got it from Paddy Reynolds (1920-2005), who learned it from James “Lad” O’Bierne (1911-1980).

Re: Matt Peoples’

Tommy Peoples wrote in his book that this tune, and the one played preceding it in Am, were learned by him from his uncle Mattha. Tommy never heard the tunes played by anyone else, and so called the set “Matt Peoples’ ” after their source, his uncle.

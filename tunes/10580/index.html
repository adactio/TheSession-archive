Five comments

Tuning and Playing

Tune your Strings (assuming it’s a fiddle) AEAE besides GDAE. Play this jig as if you had tuned your fiddle to GDAE. http://www.cranfordpub.com/tunes/scordatura.htm is a good example if you don’t understand my explanation

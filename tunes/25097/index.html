The Liverpool

The second of the 2 reels titled the Liverpool by Charlie Lennon.

↳ Posted by Connor Hickey on December 24th, 2024.

Re: The Liverpool

Together with the Liverpool No. 1 these two great reels are tracks 22 and 23 on the CD accompanying Charlie’s publication, Musical Memories Volume 2. The recordings are of two flutes playing to an ecstatic reception on stage. This tune has several Lennon “hallmarks” including the second to last line, and the nice / unusual intervals. The notes are also in Liverpool Reels 1 and 2 from Charlie Lennon’s Musical Memories Volume 2.

↳ Posted by mokelly on January 7th, 2025.

Re: The Liverpool

Worth giving credit to the 2 flute players on that track, Michael McGoldrick and Michael O’Brien, accompanied by Jimmy Higgins on bodhran. One of the best flute duets ever recorded.

Whiskey O Rowdledum Ro

Whiskey O Rowdledum Ro (or Whiskey Ó Roudelum Row) is a sean-nós song from Connemara.
Joe Heaney’s version and the words to the song can be found at https://www.joeheaney.org/en/whiskey-o-roudelum-row/

This setting here is Johnny Óg Connolly’s slip jig (sans repeats) version for melodeon, as can be heard on his album Fear Inis Bearachain (https://thesession.org/recordings/6088) or in the video recorded for Slí na mBeaglaoich (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vH8sn_bEidE)

↳ Posted by Lennart on June 9th, 2022.

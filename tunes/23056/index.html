|Saint George And The Dragon

This tune doesn’t appear to be on the site. Sorry that this was posted a day late but I was too busy playing it yesterday. Its usually played in a morrisy sort of way but you can pep it up a bit and play it like a Kerry polka.
Its quite a nice dark contrast to the incessant bright major tunes.

Re: Saint George And The Dragon

This tune can easily be turned into a 6/8 jig or march: just play the notes in groups of three (redistributing the bars as you go) and you have it. The variations arising from the same basic method/pattern all seem to be viable and valid.

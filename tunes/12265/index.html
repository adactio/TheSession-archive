Re: All Alive

O‘Neil’s Music of Ireland has it in G minor as #1105 and ascribes it to J. O’Neill.
The Complete Works of O‘Carolan from Ossian Publications (c. 1984) has it as #181 in F minor. I’m inclined to believe O’Neil’s.

↳ Posted by Mike Maloney on January 22nd, 2025.

Re: All Alive

Related to the Old Grey Goose. From tunearch: “The tune is attributed to blind Irish harper Turlough O‘Carolan (1670-1738), although Donal O’Sullivan in his definitive work on the bard (Carolan: Life and Times of an Irish Harper, 1958) could find no incontrovertible evidence of its origin.”
https://tunearch.org/wiki/Annotation:All_Alive_(1)

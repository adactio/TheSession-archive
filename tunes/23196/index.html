Tripping In To Tynan’s

This is another composition of Mick Martin from Wexford, whistle player and piper with the popular group, ‘The Kilkennys’. Thanks once again to SETU’s music department lecturer Nóra Byrne Kavanagh for notating Mick Martin’s compositions for the Wexford Tune Project.

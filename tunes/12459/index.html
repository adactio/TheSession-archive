Three comments

Off Go Those St. John’s Girls

I got this one from the A Crowd of Bold Sharemen album, and yes, they do play it AAB. A simple, catchy tune, it seems to be a relative of “Over the Waterfall”.

↳ Posted by Sol Foster on February 3rd, 2013.

Off Go Those St. John’s Girls, X:2

From the playing of Shanneyganock.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on September 9th, 2019.

Off Go Those St. John’s Girls, X:3

From the playing of Great Big Sea.

Last tune in their Fortune set, starting at ~2mins 17s:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BEM0znb469g

From their liner notes:
“And the last tune, a particularly jaunty number (even by Newfoundland standards) is from the repertoire of the late Rufus Guinchard, one of a handful of accordion tunes this great fiddler played.”

Ian

Tune by Louise Bichan, from her concept album “Out of My Own Light” tracing her grandmother’s life. “Ian” was composed for one of the two suitors, the Aberdonian man whom she did not ultimately marry. I’ve attached the orchestral arrangement where I first heard the tune.

Two comments

Canción Mixteca

I believe the Chieftains once played this with Ry Cooder so it’s definitely a full-on Celtic tune now.
The versions here are similar to those offered by RC and the excellent but departed Harry Dean Stanton.
If you are wondering where you heard it before then dust off your VHS tape of Paris Texas
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CO7vTfkCbqo
or enjoy HDS on the telly
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u33IStD7X6A
Written by Lopez Alavez in 1912 and the unofficial state anthem for Oaxaca in SW Mexico, an unashamedly sentimental ballad about home. There, I told you it was Irish.

↳ Posted by Chris Rudd on June 20th, 2020.

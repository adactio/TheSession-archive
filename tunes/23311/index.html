Three comments

Blue Devils’

Apparently this jig is new but it felt as if I’d always known it the moment I heard it.
Strange, isn’t it? Unless it has been abroad for a while before the author recorded it?
It manages to sound old and new at the same time, which is a pretty good ‘review’ when it comes to trad tunes! Nice one Nuala!

It is the first dance tune on Nuala Kennedy’s new album, Shorelines, although it comes as a ‘blue’ sequel to the lament ‘Sally sits weeping’ on track 1 and the musicians indulge the blue feeling beautifully.

Let me quote the magazine Bright Young Folk for this song-and-jig set:
“The version here is assembled from a variety of sources, while the self-composed jig with its fiddle, flute and whistles is named for the deep melancholy that overcome Ishmael in Moby Dick.”
A sea-theme then!
Personaly I had a vision of colourful insects buzzing in their own peculiar singular different ways around colourful corollas when I first listened to it the other night, but, granted, I must have been already dreaming! (-;
https://brightyoungfolk.com/records/shorelines-nuala-kennedy

Blue Devils’, X:2

This is the tune in E dorian, as you would read it in a tin whistle of flute tutor book. ;-)

The tune is in the dorian mode but the 6th is only played in the fast runs of notes in the 3rd, 7th and last but one bars.

‘Hexatonic’.

Re: O’Donnell’s

This is a shortened version of the Lancashire Clog tune ‘Flee as a Bird’, a four part show-off piece for clog dancers in northern England. It was published as a Lancashire clog tune in Boston USA around 1880.

Great playing of this on mandolin by Fergal Scahill: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZPDpZUkR0CA

Re: O’Donnell’s

“Maguire’s Fiddle” was the second tune Paddy Killoran played on his 78, but the record company listed the tunes in reverse, leading to many calling this tube by that title, by mistake.

The Maguire’s Fiddle tune Paddy played is properly called Sault’s Own Hornpipe: https://thesession.org/tunes/8829

↳ Posted by What's the name of this tune? on June 30th, 2025.

Gabriella’s

I got this tune from the French band, Birkin Tree, and their 4.0 album. This track features Caitlín Nic Gabhann and Ciarán Ó Maonaigh.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5iZJweC8808

When the pipes come in the third time through, there are a few little changes.

The end of the first A part, they play like this:
| A3 BFE | DE ED EF | instead of | A2 AG AB | d3 ABd |

And at the end of the first B part, one of the players plays:
| AB A2 AG | FE D2 B/c/d | instead of | AB A2 AG | BA A2 B/c/d |

I like that variation better than the original.

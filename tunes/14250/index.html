Called ‘The Topiarist’

Hey there. This is a composition of mine called ‘The Topiarist’ composed for the late Joe Scurfield and recorded with my band The Auvo Quartet on our debut album (released 2006) The tune was composed and recorded in C major but I like the setting in A.

If the guys who recorded this could send me a copy of the album then I’d love to hear it.
You can contact me and find out more at www.tomoakesmusic.co.uk

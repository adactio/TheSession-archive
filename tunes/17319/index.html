Re: Tomorrow Shall Be My Dancing Day

My stupid mistake… I transcribed it from a track on Spotify thinking it was a version of the traditional song, but it turns out it is a new composition by Welsh composer Gareth Treseder, who used the traditional lyrics and put them to a newly composed tune in a traditional style. I will remove it if there are copyright issues…

I have also, by chance, found a transcription in a book of English folk-dances of the very song from the Youtube video (or a very similar version). I could just edit the tune to that one.

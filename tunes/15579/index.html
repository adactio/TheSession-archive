Two comments

The Flower Of Kristiansand

Lovely, lovely tune by Ivan Drever Scottish singer/songwriter from the great band Wolfstone fame!!

Kristiansand is in the south of Norway, where Ivan now resides. The tune was written for his lovely wife Linda and is taken from his CD ‘Music From The Vaults Vol 1’ which was released in 2013.

Ivan has given me kind permission to post here on The Session

Ivan has just published a songbook ‘Ivan Drever The Works Vol 1’ and can be purchased by messaging him on his Facebook page here:
https://www.facebook.com/idrever
or through his website here:
http://www.ivandrever.co.uk/

The Sanctuary

This tune follows The Black Frog (https://thesession.org/tunes/5370) on the first track of Enda Scahill’s brilliant banjo cd, Pick It Up.

↳ Posted by Will Harmon on December 29th, 2005.

Will - Thank you for the time and effort you put in to doing variations to tunes - well worth the problems I get trying to print them out!!

↳ Posted by Tarrantella on December 30th, 2005.

You’re welcome. The transcription I submitted is “as played” by Enda on the cd, so the variations are his.

↳ Posted by banjo-craze on October 11th, 2008.

I’ve lost the liner notes to this cd. My hunch is that Enda wrote it, but that could be wrong.

↳ Posted by Will Harmon on October 11th, 2008.

Yeah, it was penned by Enda Scahill. He writes: “The Sanctuary was composed in Ennis during the mayhem of the World Dancing Championships!” I think the title refers to Cruise’s Pub, but might be wrong.

Whistling Mike

Tune #887 in O’Neill’s 1001, slightly altered to remove dotting on three notes that’s otherwise absent in the transcription.

↳ Posted by benhockenberry on May 20th, 2021.

Re: Whistling Mike

There’s a hornpipe in Allan’s Irish Fiddler called “The Kingston” which is very reminiscent of this tune. Mike Rafferty also played it, calling it “The Kingstown Hornpipe” - and it’s under that name in Geraldine Cotter’s Tin Whistle Tutor (1985). I have an LP from 1977 with the Mull accordionist Bobby MacLeod using it to kick off a set: The Kingston/The Golden Eagle/Off to California.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on May 21st, 2021.

Re: Whistling Mike

The one Nigel refers to is here at: https://thesession.org/tunes/8342, where it’s main title is given as ‘The Standing Abbey’. Not precisely the Allan setting, but clearly the same tune. I agree that the one above is quite similar in the A part, but I think the B part makes it a different tune.

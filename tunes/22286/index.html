Spanish Point, X:2

James’s transcription is an accurate representation of the recording and, in theory, one can play along with the tune on a high D whistle. But it has two drawbacks both related to the fact that Kevin plays the tune, I think, on an A whistle in a higher key. First, the music dips down to C in a couple of places. But more importantly, the tune is characterised by many slides, most of which are difficult to do on the particular notes involved. My transcription marks the slides and the fingerings Kevin used on the first part of the tune. Obviously the tune comes out much higher but at least it is playable.

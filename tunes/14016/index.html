Re: Dust On The Bible

The abc for X:1 shows “K: A dor.” as the key.
This tune is pentatonic and, if in A, it has no 3rd and no 7th, so it can hardly be dorian, perhaps mixolydian would be closer to it.
Most people will hear it in D, though,
specifically here, in the usual major pentatonic mode without any 4th nor 7th degrees.

Dust On The Bible, X:3

Although the g# shows in the clef, there is no 7th in this tune, here set in A; a key accessible to tin whistlers, along the original key of D’, and the keys of C and G.

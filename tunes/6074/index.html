The Gopak Slide

This is a fast slide version of a very famous Ukrainian traditional dance tune named Gopak (second syllable is stressed) composed by me (if it`s possible to say so:).
May be varied anyway: played without reprises, the 1st reprise instead the 2nd, as a jig, with/without rolls…, etc.
Strange but powerful.

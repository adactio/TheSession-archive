Old Lurgan Town

A tune which I came across recently, written by Marinie Toman, well-known in trad circles in Lurgan and beyond for his exquisite banjo playing. He wrote this jig when “in exile” in Donegal and was thinking about returning to his hometown. The first setting is converted into abc from Marinie’s own notation and playing in this video https://youtu.be/YEdhHz9yRIs. A second setting will follow which is how, after spending some time with the tune, I ended up adapting it to my own rather less accomplished style of playing…

↳ Posted by Aidan Crossey on October 31st, 2021.

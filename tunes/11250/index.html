Donegal Tune

This is from the first disc of Donegal fiddler Vincent Campbell’s 2CD “The Purple Heather”: https://thesession.org/recordings/3550 There’s a Shamrock Jig in the database already so that’s what the link at this page gives you, instead of this tune.
Vincent sometimes plays (3ege in the 2nd bar of the 2nd part as a little variant.

“Kimmelizer”, I like that.

It was familiar, if not sure that includes the name, but I knew it from memory as a 32 bar tune… I did do an initial look through some of my notebooks but didn’t find it, at least not yet. Everything isn’t here and our main computer is still down, with me avoiding it for the moment…

Mind you, I did spend some time with the Campbell family way back when, and with other musicians in that community, fond memories and great music, and dancing too. I remember that Vincent had a great passion for the local traditions and musicians and consequently knew quite a lot of interesting tunes… This one makes more sense to me as 32 bars.

Re: O’Carolan’s Cottage

This tune is actually called Alloway House and was published in Scotland in the early 18th Century. Nothing to do with O’Carolan, that’s a 20th Century myth.

↳ Posted by RyanK on June 9th, 2024.

Re: O’Carolan’s Cottage

This is from Harper on the Mandolin Cafe in 2015:
“ However, does anyone know the true composer? The tune is nearly identical to Carolan’s Cottage (#184 in Donal O’Sullivan’s treatise on Carolan, the Life and Times of an Irish Harper). Alloway House was published by Oswald in about 1740 (Caledonian Pocket Companion Vol 1 Bk 1 p. 24) and William McGibbon in 1746 (A Collection of Scots Tunes, Book 1, p. 25). George Petrie’s collection of Irish tunes edited by Charles Stanford contains the tune Carolan’s Cottage (#670), with the note that it is from P. Carew’s manuscript. I can’t find anything about the P. Carew MSS.

John Purser states that “Alloway House was definitely one of Oswald’s -- one of his best…” (Scotland’s Music, 2007, p. 207)

Donal O’Sullivan claims the tune for Carolan only on the basis of the note in Petrie regarding P. Carew.

Carolan lived from 1670-1738 and Oswald from 1710–1769. Did the collector P. Carew predate or post-date Oswald?”

I’d love to know if this question has been settled.

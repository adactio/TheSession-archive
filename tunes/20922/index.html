Two comments

The Music Book

A “highland” from “Trian II” - transcribed for me by Malcolm Reavell.
CD sleeve notes say :
“Liz [ Carroll ] found the tune she calls ”The Music Book“ many years ago in a collection of Irish music at DePaul University Library”.
These are the basic notes, but it really needs the “snaps” to give it more the swing of a highland.

↳ Posted by Kenny on July 27th, 2021.

Re: The Music Book

The tune appears to have been derived from “The Braes of Auchtertyre”, which in turn influenced such tunes as “The Belles of Tipperary”, “Miss Monaghan” and “Billy in the Lowground” et al.

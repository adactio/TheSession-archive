A Chur Nan Gobhar As A ’Chreig, X:4

Here’s a stripped down version for Scottish pipes. Crotchet and quaver groups (quarter & eighth notes) of the same pitch can be tied or subdivided to suit. Low A and B crotchets are often embellished with short birls and peles, respectively or short rolls for either. Also common is to divide them into quavers with gracenotes and taps as a long roll. The inescapable GDE gracenote combo is frequently deployed by Highland pipers on the Low A.

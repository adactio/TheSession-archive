The Saltees

A tune composed by the Piercestown(Wexford) born accordion and concertina player Paddy Hayes. It featured on his album ‘The shores of Lough Melvin’. Paddy spent many years in London where he played with great musicians, he won several Fleadh Cheoil awards and toured with Comhaltas tour groups in the 1980’s before retiring to Co. Leitrim.
This reel is played at a slow reel pace on the recording and could easily be played as a nice hornpipe either.

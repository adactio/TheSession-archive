Two comments

The Storks Have Landed

A tune composed by Conor McCarthy, who graciously gave me permission to post this here. I transcribed it from Conor’s playing on Late…in the Night, which also features Christy Barry on flute and whistle, and Cyril O‘Donoghue on guitar and bouzouki. Great cd, recorded in O’Connor’s Bar in Doolin--go buy it if you don’t own it already.

↳ Posted by Will Harmon on July 26th, 2005.

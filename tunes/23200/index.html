The Shores Of Ballyteigue

This is the second of two reels composed by Bar of Lough member John Browne from Bannow in County Wexford. Thanks to Nóra Byrne Kavanagh for her notation of a crop of tunes submitted by musicians from the Bannow area in the past few weeks as part of The Wexford Tune Project.
Ballyteigue Burrow is a conservation area along the south coast of County Wexford.
See here for some information:
https://www.southwexfordcoast.com/ballyteige-burrow/

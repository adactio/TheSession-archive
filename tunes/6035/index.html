“The Merry Maid’s Wedding” ~ strathspey / reel ~ A & E Dorian

This is the other member of the family, the links to the rest to follow. It has the same basic A-part as “The Haughs of Cromdale” but a different B-part, however, both parts closely mirror the tune “Barrack Hill”, single jig / slide…

“The Merry Maid’s Wedding” ~ strathspey / reel

Key signatures: A Dorian (some play and notate it as Mixolydian, c#’s) & E Dorian…

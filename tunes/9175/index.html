Carrie’s

Norman, to check your ABC’s you can check them with this…
http://www.concertina.net/tunes_convert.html . Copy and paste the whole text in, including the headers and press submit. It will convert them to dots, just press back to make corrections. You can also check with the midi function.

Also, before you get stick from someone, read the FAQ’s regarding self composed tunes.

Re: The Flats Of Libby

Nice chune anyway!

it’s more about musical notation - I you define it as in the key of Bmin you get the two # for F and C and so you do not have to mark them.
and _d (bD) is equal to C….Generally you write everything with # not b when the main key is one with # (1 or more)
Good luck.

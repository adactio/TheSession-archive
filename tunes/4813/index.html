Reel!

This isn’t a polka, it’s a reel. Speed it up, take out the >, play it in 4s, inject a little bit of swing into it, and there you have it. It’s an old Scottish reel called Lady Margaret Stewart. Find a reel transcription via JC’s search
http://trillian.mit.edu/~jc/cgi/abc/TuneFind?P=lady+margaret+stewart&m=title&M=&L=&K=&V=0&W=wide&limit=100&thresh=5&find=FIND, or go to FC and scroll down: http://www.ibiblio.org/fiddlers/LADYM_LADYS.htm.

Ohm’s Law

Fine 3-part jig composed by Billy McComiskey in 1985, when at technical school, according to the sleeve notes for “Trian II”.
Thanks to flute player Malcolm Reavell for the transcription - along with many others - from a good few years back now.

Re: Ohm’s Law

It was October 30, 1981, I was driving to the Focal Point Coffeehouse, in Webster Groves, Missouri, to see Kevin Burke, but I believe he was unable to get out of playing at McGurk’s, so I was surprised to see Liz Carroll and Máire Ní Chathasaigh, fine harpist, on the schedule. On the way down I saw a terrible accident on highway 270, barely avoiding being in it myself, which was quite memorable.

So i had a little cassette recorder and recorded some of it, though this is before I learned to *not* turn off the recorder during the spaces between tunes, good stuff went on there. I’d only been playing a year and a half at that time, so Ohm’s Law was interesting, especially being an electrical engineer. But if you recall, I turned off the recorder during gaps and so i missed the first part of the tune names, so I thought that Eleanor Neary’s was Ohm’s Law, rather than the tune following it.

So yeah, I would tell Billy his liner notes are off…

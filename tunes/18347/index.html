Marcha’l Campu Vieyu

Today (8th September) is the Day of Asturias. So here’s an Asturian tune for you.
From Felpeyu’s album “Yá!”

On their website they say “The set concludes with a march inspired by lively Asturian wedding marches” so I’m guessing it’s their own composition.

2nd tune of the 3rd track. Originally in Eb I’ve transposed it to G.

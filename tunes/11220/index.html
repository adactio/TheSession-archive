The Midnight Oil

I think this tune was composed by John Sheahan.

Kenny’s post of The Marino Waltz in the Recordings section reminded me of it, which I hope is the same one as on the album. A few years ago, a fellow fiddler and work colleague loaned me a Dubliners’ video which had this tune on it, which I really liked. I wrote it out and played it for a while at home and then the music got buried and the tune forgotten about. I was glad to find it again in the vaults reasonably quickly – which doesn’t usually happen when I have a particular tune I want to find.

This is written out on the basis that the A part is in F major and the B part in D minor. I reckon it sounds best finishing on the A part – a 40-bar tune? – but can’t remember if that is how it is meant to be played.

Possible chords might be:

C7 |: F C7 | F F | Gm Gm | C7 C7 | F C7 | F F | Gm Gm |1 C7 F C7 :|2 C7 F |
|: Dm Dm | Gm A7 | Dm Dm | A7 A7 | Dm Gm | Dm C Bb A7 | Dm C Dm Gm | |1 A7 Dm :|2 A7 Dm ||

I hope this transcription is reasonably accurate.

Peter

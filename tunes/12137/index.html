Rona Morgana

I stumbled across this excellent tune in Scottish Fiddlers’ Session Tune Book. Ho-Ro Gheallaidh Volumes 1 and 2. Taigh Na Teud. (1999) p.23.

The composer is credited as Ronan Martin, who, if he is the same Ronan Martin as the Skye fiddler
http://www.ronanmartin.net/index.asp
may well have been less than ten years old when he composed this - wow!

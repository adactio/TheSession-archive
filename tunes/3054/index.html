Lady Mondagreen

Composed by Junji Shirota, this is a minor tune, (not dorian). It was given the name, “Lady Mondagreen” because we named our band after Junji’s pronunciation of the tune, “Jolly Seven” as “Jody’s Heaven.”

If you’d like to hear it, here’s an mp3 of the medley we recorded that includes “Eels in the Sink” and “The Shoemaker’s Daughter.”

http://www.aniar.com/Eels.mp3

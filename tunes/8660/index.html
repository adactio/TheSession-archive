Paddy Fahey’s, X:3 (another Éilís Crean’s version from Searbh Siucra)

I added this to reflect Éilís Crean’s treatment of the fifth measure in the first pass through part “A.” It adds a bit of turbulence that one expects to hear calmed eventually, but it isn’t. I find the figure remarkably simple but very satisfying, nevertheless. Also: I noted the “B” part in D mixolydian, although I was tempted to choose D major based on C sharp notes in the third and eighth measures and John Doyle’s use of major chords A-G-D accompanying the eighth measure the second time through. Other notes: This transcription does not reflect ornamental double-stops. It also does not reflect the many variations Éilís Crean introduces during her second time through the tune.

↳ Posted by Barry Morse on March 26th, 2023.

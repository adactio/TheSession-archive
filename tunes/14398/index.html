Two comments

Lady Helen Wedderburn

Adapted from the Athole Collection. I’ve toyed with this one quite a bit. First published in Duncan McKercher’s Collections circa 1830.

From the Fiddler’s Companion (tunearch.org):
Lady Helen Wedderburn (1798-1868) was the youngest daughter of Walter, the 5th Earl of Airlie. She married John Wedderburn at Airlie Castle, 30 April 1823, and died at Rosebauk, Roslin, in 1868 (buried at Roslin Chapel). The couple had three sons and one daughter.

Google books has a whole publication on the Wedderburn family. From a quick look they were apparently an important lowland ecclesiastical family from the 16th century onwards but the book claims to cover from 1296-1896:

McQuillan’s Favourite

As heard here :
https://www.facebook.com/erin.mcgeown/videos/529125041696135
This composition by Erin Nic Eoghain can be found too, with a few differences, on ITMA’s site* as Erin let me know while asking for her authorization to publish it here. I did not know she was offered the opportunity to present some of her compositionnal work in the serie “Saothar” on ITMA’site so I did the job of writing a transcription of what I heard her play on FB … The job being done, and Erin seeming satisfied with the idea of being published on thesession, I decided to post my transcription though its redundance with ITMA.

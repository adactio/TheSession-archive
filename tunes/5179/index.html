Fourteen comments

Slow Reel from Flook’s newest CD

i just got flook’s newest CD in the mail. it’s as wonderful as one would expect. this simple and flowing tune, from the pen of aiden o’rourke, is played with a lovely slow lilt. it’s played on a Bb flute with G fingers and so the tune comes out in Eb. i’ve notated in G for rest of us.

i can’t wait to start picking apart some of the more complicated tunes on this CD.

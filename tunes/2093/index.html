Four comments

This is not really a hornpipe but a highland which is not a category available when submitting. Altan (Altan’s finest) play it more like a hornpipe than a Highland anyway and the tempo is slower than a reel but a little faster than a hornpipe. Another thing they do is to play a double stop by hitting a B along with every middle G played.

Re: Con Cassidy’s Highland

As far as highlands go, this particular Con Cassidy tune seems to be played smoother than others, maybe slightly faster, but still more like a slow reel. Definitely less swing in this tune than some highlands, i.e. like with a hornpipe or strathspey.

The way Máiread plays it on the Altan album is how everyone I heard in Donegal and elsewhere plays it. Still, other highlands may be slower, faster, or “punchier”, and some are even more strathspey-sounding, with far more Scotch snaps, and a very bouncy rhythm.

↳ Posted by Erik-Fiddler on August 29th, 2024.

The Bush In Bloom

The Bush In Bloom, X:4

This version from the more modern section of the Power manuscript from Kilmore in Wexford is very similar to x3 above. The ‘modern’ section of the Power manuscript has pages dated 1921, so it may be the case that the music transciber had access to a copy of O’Neill’s book as a source for many of the tunes in that section.

Two comments

Elevenses

A very complex tune from John Doyle’s “The Path Of Stones”, composed by himself. Obviously not a mazurka, but a… errr… whatever. Despite a 11/4, it has that 5/4 + 6/4 feeling — except part three, which is “kind of” irregular — I just divided it by guitar chords / strokes changes.

I know it’s not a session-able tune; I uploaded it just for those who are curious, or may like to give it a try at home.

Five comments

Title of this tune

I wrote and named titled this tune in honor of a fair uilean piper who I have the honor of playing with in sessions here in Honolulu. I won’t name him as I believe that he prefers that I don’t. I’m kind of proud of the Asian feel I managed to put into this tune. Mike G.

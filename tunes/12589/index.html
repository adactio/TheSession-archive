Llancesau Trefaldwyn

Means Montgomery Lasses. A version of this tune is played by Carl MacKenzie the Cape Breton fiddler on his “It’s a Corker” CD. He calls it Kerr’s Irish Quickstep.

↳ Posted by Geoff Hardman on September 22nd, 2015.

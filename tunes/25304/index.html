Midnight

This is a Joanie Madden composition. I couldn’t find it posted; someone help me out if I’m wrong!

Joanie Madden recorded it on “A Whistle on the Wind” (https://thesession.org/recordings/96). Mike & Mary Rafferty recorded it on “Old Fireside Music” (https://thesession.org/recordings/161), and Aoife Granville recorded it on “Sáimhín Só” (https://thesession.org/recordings/4627). But those recordings each had The Midnight linked to some unrelated tune. So here’s the melody from Joanie’s recording, and I’m off to fix the links.

Fans of Charlie Lennon will smile at the “Bag of Money” moments in the B part. See https://thesession.org/tunes/2496

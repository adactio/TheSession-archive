Beautiful flute & harp tune.

Here’s a tune I heard just this past weekend, and have absolutely fell in love with.
Certainly not a reel, more of an air, but most definitely beautiful.

It was written by harp and concertina master Michael Rooney, husband to wonderful flute player (and teacher!) June McCormack, if I heard him right at the concert, he wrote this for the Millenium suite at the Fleadh Cheoil or something to that effect… it was hard to hear all the words.

Any more information about this would be excellent, and I don’t believe this tune is on any recordings as of yet, but if you would like a recording, shoot me an e-mail and I’ll get it to you.

-J

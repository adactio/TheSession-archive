Haugen’s Reinlender

I love this tune - obviously Scandinavian, but not sure which country it comes from. I learned this tune from Orcadian banjo player Mickey Austin - Mickey plays it with harmonica player Billy Jolly along with The Dance of the Honey Bees.

Cheers

Bob Massie

↳ Posted by Bob Massie on April 15th, 2013.

The vast majority of reinlender tunes (especially spelled like that) come from Norway, and the name Haugen is most common in Norway, so it likely comes from there. Nice to have a tune from Mickey’s vast repertoire.

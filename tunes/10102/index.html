Hanter Dro

Not a Mazurka, of course, but a “Hanter Dro” a lovely, simple type of ring dance, originally from SE Brittany.
According to an online dictionnary, “er pasker” means ‘the grazer, ie: a baby or a sick person’ (?)
Veillon recorded it indeed in a set with a beautiful Hanter dro version of the Medieval tune “Stella Splendens” from a Catalunian source.

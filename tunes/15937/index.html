How Auld Are Ye, My Bonnie Lass

This transcription is from the 2016 album ‘Tirade’ by Eddie Seaman and Luc McNally. The liner notes say that it was “found in an old collection of pipe music” - the setting is most like the one in David Glen’s Edinburgh Collection (c1903), so that’s probably Eddie’s source.

It has relatives: “Mrs Wemyss of Cuttle Hill” (claimed by Nathaniel Gow), “St Patrick was a Gentleman” (https://thesession.org/tunes/2116) and “Denis Doody’s Polka” (https://thesession.org/tunes/1338).

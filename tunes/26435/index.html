Three comments

The Wayfarers

A beautiful composition of Paddy (Offaly) O’Brien from volume two of his Tune Collection.
He writes:
This is one of the few hornpipes I composed. I named it after the many
musicians who gave us all so much knowledge and encouragement
when we began playing. This tune is dedicated to the memory of so
many traveling minstrels who stopped for a visit here and there
throughout Ireland. Their legacy lives with us today!

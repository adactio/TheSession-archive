The Tae in the Hole

Och, it’s a funny situation, Joseph. I’d love it if there was a special corner on the session.org where people could post their own tunes for discussion, advice and pleasure, and you might say there’s no harm in posting your tunes in the general database. And you’re right, in principle! However, it does open up the possibility that the system is abused. For example, someone recently posted 27 of their own compositions and then was never heard of again. I’m not suggesting you’re like that, Joseph; I would hope that you’d take more part in the community here than that.

An oft-quoted extract of the FAQ for the session.org is,
Q: “Can I submit my own compositions?”
A: “Well… The Session isn’t really intended for that. If you do post one of your own compositions, then you must do your “penance”, so to speak, by balancing each original composition with about five trad tune settings.”

One thing you could do is put your tunes in your profile, as many members do. Then people can play them and, if you’re lucky and they take off, someone may submit them to the session.org!

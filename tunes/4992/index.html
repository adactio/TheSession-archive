An early sighting…

This tune appears in Geoghegan’s “The Compleat Tutor for the Pastoral or New Bagpipe,” published around 1745-46 in London, as “Jigg, call’d Whip her & gird her.”

↳ Posted by Jumper on January 24th, 2015.

Re: Hoop Her And Gird Her

It also shows up in Patrick McDonald’s “Highland Vocal Airs, Country Dances or Reels of the North Highlands and Western Isles” published in 1784. It’s the 2nd tune listed in the section titled “North Highland Reels or Country Dances” although it isn’t given a title and only includes the first two parts of the melody. In the notes on the tunes at the end of the book it’s given the title “Whip Her and Gird Her.” Not sure why it’s missing the 3rd section of the melody.

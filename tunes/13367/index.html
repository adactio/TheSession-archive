Four comments

The Star Of Ireland

The genius of Finbarr Dwyer! This is one of his finest, in the key of (E major / going to D) that he loved so much. I have no idea what mode this is - all the D sharps make it more complicated than a straightforward myxolidian, I think. It seems to just move from one emotional place to another.

↳ Posted by coppers and brass on February 9th, 2014.

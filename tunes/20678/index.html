Brendan’s Boat

Composed by Bruce Foley, Jan 1999
Inspired by Belfast artist Eamon McGuire’s bog-oak sculpture -
carved as a trophy in memory of rower, Brendan Foley
The tune evokes movement of a currach on the sea.
The tune is played by Bruce on the uilleann pipes on his CD, “Turning The Tides”

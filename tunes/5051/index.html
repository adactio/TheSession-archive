Kiss Me Fast My Minnie’s Coming, X:2

Here is another version of this tune, published in Aird’s “Selection of Scotch, English, Irish and Foreign Airs”, vol. 2, c 1790, nr 99, under the title “Kiss me fast my mother’s coming”.
The same tune was also published in J. Anderson’s “Budget of Strathspeys, Reels and Country Dances”, c 1820, p.27, nr 2.

↳ Posted by Moxhe on October 14th, 2015.

Busybody

Written for the Highland Pipes by Bill Livingstone, if I remember correctly. If it isn’t a traditional tune, it will be in a few years’ time. It’s great to play on the fiddle.

The actual name is “Busy Buddy”…

It is by Bill Livingstone and is found in Bruce Gandy’s first collection of pipe tunes: Contemporary And Traditional Music For The Highland Bagpipe.

Noel Geeson’s Air

Noel Geeson taught me music at school and was very encouraging. Noel plays the highland pipes and Northumbrian pipes, and was the first person I heard playing bellows pipes. This air for Noel is written in the Northumbrian piping style. Noel turned 90 in Christmas day last year. Thank-you Noel.

↳ Posted by JACKB on February 8th, 2014.

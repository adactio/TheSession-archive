Awesome, rockin’ little jig

I’m absolutely giddy at being the first to submit this one. It’s off a Nomos CD, “Set You Free”; I don’t know the author.

Note the similarity of the last phrase to the last phrase of Athol Highlanders. Going into the last turn, Niall and company replace the last two bars with

eee eee | eee eee

…which must drive ’em wild at the live shows. Fantastic jig, I think. Enjoy!

Rory Gallagher’s, X:4

Some basic chords, as per Gillies request, added to X:2 above.
I should add that I had the honour of seeing Rory Gallagher play live in the 1970s. In an era when many ‘big’ groups played a grudging 40 minute set, Rory played for about 3 hours, including an acoustic set in the middle of the main electric set. He was full of energy. I don’t think his studio stuff really did justice to his live playing. At one point he broke a string, and changed it while carrying on playing. I’d never seen anything like it

“Home By Barna”

Someone was asking about this tune in the “Discussions” recently. Kevin Burke played it after Christy Moore sung a song called “Home From Barna” on an early recording. It’s very similar to “Jenny Picking Cockles”, or parts of “Jenny’s Wecome To Charlie”. maybe someone can identify it. Hope it’s not the first “Duplication” of the year.
Happy New Year, a’body.

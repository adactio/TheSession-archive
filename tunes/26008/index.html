Clashnarrow

“Clashnarrow Studios” is the name of Edwyn Collins’ recording studio where “Project SMOK” recorded their debut album, “Bayview”. The tune is named after the studio as thanks to Edwyn and Grace Maxwell for hosting the band and looking after them while recording.

Composer: Ali M. Levack
Notation can be found in the book: Ali Levack Vol.2

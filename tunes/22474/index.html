Cinderella’s

In a workshop led by Donna Harkin we wrote this tune and it was then arranged by Donna. It’s a simple jolly tune that is a lot of fun to play and gets stuck in your head easily.

It’s called Cinderella’s because the repeating part has an eerie resemblance to the song Bibbidy-Bobbidy-Boo, we only found out about that later.

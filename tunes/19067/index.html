The Tryst After Death

By The Ollam, transcribed from https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iaswkqulz5Q.

This is the low whistle and uillean pipes part. The last two minutes of the recording are mainly rhythm and chords without a clear melody line and are not covered by this transcription.

There are 6 parts A-F, repeated as follows:

4*A (chords) |
4*A | 2*B | 1.75 * C |
2*A (chords) |
2*A | 2*B | 2*C |
2*A (chords) |
D | E | F

“1.75 * C” means that the repeat skips the last bar.

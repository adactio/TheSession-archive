Three comments

Screw The Nut

This is a classic hornpipe every feis musician knows. Played at 113BPM so the ornamentation isn’t as crazy as it seems. I believe it came from a recording by Gerry Conlon and Seamus O’Sullivan. This is the version I learnt from listening to Liam O’Sullivan. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qvKYx7B-RbE&t=188s

↳ Posted by jmeme on September 7th, 2022.

Re: Screw The Nut

For the record, I wrote this tune - it’s in C minor and was written after an encounter with a rather neurotic person. In Scotland, “Screw the Nut” affectionately means “Get a Grip” or “Sort yourself out” :-) … I recorded it with Seamus O’Sullivan and it was released on our cd entitled “Simply Open”.

↳ Posted by Gerry Conlon on January 17th, 2024.

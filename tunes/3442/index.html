Four comments

Actually titled: “Craig’s Reel”
From: The Dance Music of Ireland (1907), Collected by Capt. Francis O‘Neill, Arranged by Sergt. James O’Niell
Origins:Cor an Carraig

Not widely played, but one I found in a copy of an old tune book that I hope to learn.

↳ Posted by Chaddock on August 26th, 2004.

ABC error

The second note (f) of the seventh measure of the B part is one octave low in the GIF and MIDI file. The ABC file has been updated.

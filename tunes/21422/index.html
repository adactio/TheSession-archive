Two comments

The Colours Of Cape Breton

While I was posting and editing (Enough said :-) ) The Voice of The Borders, I noticed that this tune was also on the “Spring The Summer Long” album.

I know it’s not Irish but it’s a very popular Phil Cunningham tune and I’m quite surprised it hasn’t been posted already.
I’ve been playing it for such a long time that I can’t even remember where I first learned it. Obviously, I also know it from the album but I first started to play it before then. At a worksop or a session somewhere.

It’s a lovely tune which Phil wrote while on a visit to the festival of the same name.

More of a slow air than a waltz though.

Obviously the tune deserves to be played more than once. However, the last bar needs to be longer at the “very end”. I’m not sure if I’ve indicated this in the correct fashion or not but I’m open to suggestions.
:-)

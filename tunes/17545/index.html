Three comments

Wren Hornpipe

I first heard this hornpipe on an untitled .mp3 my brother sent me. I picked it up by ear and plugged it into “Tunepal” which labeled it “The Wren Hornpipe” from book CRE 2.314, (Cornphiopa an Dreoilin). I did some googling and learned Nigel Gatherer had it on his website: http://www.nigelgatherer.com/books/CRE/cre2.html.

I could not find it here but it may be known by another name. Any insight is appreciated.

↳ Posted by B/C on December 13th, 2018.

Re: The Wren

314. Cornphíopa an Dreoilín: The Wren’s Hornpipe, from Tom Barrett [fiddle], who made the recording for me, VIII, 1971. The name shows that this is a tune associated with the wrenboys on St. Stephen’s Day. There are other hornpipes with this name (for example, DMI, 945).
http://www.nigelgatherer.com/books/CRE/cre2.html

↳ Posted by B/C on September 4th, 2021.

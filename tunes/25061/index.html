Re: PolkAgathe

Initially I thought the first three parts were nearly identical to the last three parts, but looking more carefully at it, the first three parts are set to “K: Cdor”, whereas the last three parts are the same melody notes, but set to “K: Cmaj”.

There are also some difference in the chords.

I’ll have to have a listen to the recording later to hear how it’s played through, however, off hand, you could conceivably label the first three parts and second three parts with different tune titles, one being a Dorian version, the other being a Major version. For example, the first three parts titled “PolkAgathe #1” and the second three parts titled “PolkAgathe #2”.

On a different note, The A and The D(?) part(s) resemble The A Part of “The Bear Dance”.

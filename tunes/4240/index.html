The sory behind the name

The “Bullock on the Bonnet” was born late one night when an ambulance delivered my friend Seamus Creagh to the door of our house where we were having a bit of a session. At the time he was a neighbour and worked with me. Seamus had been in a row with a loose bullock on the road – the animal ended on top of Seamus who was in the driving seat. While he was explaining the details of the accident, and others were suitably fussing over him, this tune came to me. I was playing the accordion at the time."
(Peadar O’Riada, taken from http://www.peadaroriada.ie/ceol-dem-chuid/the-bullock-on-the-bonnet-fling/3939/ - the link proposed by Rathawaun being obsolete)

The Mulcahys “Notes from the Heart” liner notes confirm that this (track 15) is a composition of Peadar O’Riada, but this has already been said. The music recorded in that album is actually the same as the OP’s one, though the name had been moved* (in the Mulcahys album and here by the OP) to “The gathering Sheep” instead of “Bullock on the Bonnet” which is definitely the original title for this tune.
*I write this on 2021, feb the 11th and will switch this tune to its actual name

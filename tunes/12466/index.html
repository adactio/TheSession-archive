↳ Posted by sebastian the m3g4p0p on February 4th, 2013.

I believe the bar lines are in wrong places.

Anyway, this jig is closely related to Maloney’s Wife: https://thesession.org/tunes/1895

I need to double-check, but Peter O’Loughlin probably recorded this particular version of the tune as The Chorus Jig on “The South West Wind.”

The title “The Green Blanket” is sometimes attached to The Ewe Reel: https://thesession.org/tunes/3438 Joe Burke and Mike Rafferty, both from the parish of Ballinakill, East Galway, recorded the reel as “The Green Blanket.”

Harvest Fair Polka 3

This Charlie Lennon composition appears third on Track 4 of Flight from the Hungry Land, along with another great Charlie Lennon tune I call Harvest Fair Polka 1 (in G/D) (first of the three).
Hat tip to Michael Black who found this one!

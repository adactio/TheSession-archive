Nóra’s

Nóra Byrne-Kavanagh sent me on this hornpipe, composed by her way back when she was a fleadh competitor and re-found by her recently, handwritten in manuscript. Nóra is a well-known musician, teacher and lecturer in the South-East of Ireland. She won consecutive All-Ireland senior titles on piano and has taught both underage and Senior winners subsequently. Like all the tunes I’ve been uploading, they’re being collected, notated and uploaded as part of the Wexford Tune Collection project. A printed version of the collection will be published for Fleadh Cheoil na hEireann which will be held in Wexford next year, 2024.

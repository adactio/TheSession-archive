Five comments

Pint Of Reference

From Endeavour by Gráda. It’s their composition. This tune is currently in vogue in the Sydney session scene. I’ve transcribed it direct from the album but the version I learnt from sessions is slightly different:

X: 1
T: Pint Of Reference
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Bmin
AB~B2 dB~B2|ABBA BAGA|~F3E FG~F2-|FEFG AGFD|
AB~B2 dB~B2|ABBA BAGA|~F3E FG~F2-|1 FEFG A2z2:|2 FEFG ABde||

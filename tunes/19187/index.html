Thirteen comments

T: Cofid-19

Here’s hoping this will irritate Kenny, who doesn’t care for my transcriptions anyway, having warned people away from them in the past. I try my damnedest to do justice to my sources, even if it isn’t a direct transcription from a source, and if I’m letting fly with options I try to remember to warn anyone that might give it a go. For this I couldn’t shake a straight B-part out of it. This is as it came, but I’m not sure about the barring. Is there’s something Astor Piazzolla about it? I was in the kitchen but wasn’t baking this time or preparing a meal, instead I was making a mess with compost and sand I’d collected from a hike along a nearby brook, for aloe vera pups and their ma… This just happened - - - on flute!!! Lucky for me this one flute I grabbed does a very good cross-fingered G sharp. I then took it up a step from Harmonic A minor to Harmonic B minor. Oh yes, I was also doing two trays of seeds, one the third in an every two week tray of salad greens, and a backup of flowers seeds for some madness out front.

The madness out front, a whim, digging holes in the sphagnum moss out front, looked like this from our window when my wife told the sister-in-law in Cymru/Wales and a picture was sent with the subject “In process - - -”… The sister in law wrote back - “You’d better finish that before someone crosses the ‘T’!” Here’s what it looked like from our window, the picture we’d sent her: S H I I … I was finishing it when she’d written her warning. Here, now finished, is what it looks like from the street and overhead, as intended: N H S! It’s a garden of multi-coloured flowers, that’s the intention, including sunflowers and a hell of a lot of poppies, and probably also some night scented dwarf nicotiana… It’s a celebration of admiration, appreciation and respect for all those working for and around our ‘N’ational ‘H’ealth ‘S’ervice, also with us having family who work for the NHS… True too is that both of us have had to call on it in times of need and are glad and lucky to have it there for us when needed…

Anyway, here’s a mad tune in so many ways in a response after a double shot of espresso to this damned virus - DANCE!!! Keep making music, keep dancing, keep safe, keep sane… With love from one demented ‘c’ - still alive ‘n kickin’… & planting…

Big Papi

I wrote this tune in 2006 to honor David Ortiz who starred in the World Series for the Boston Red Sox baseball team. He did it again last month! Here’s to ya, Big Papi. I have a dream… someday to play this tune with my band at Fenway Park.

Not too many tunes in E but it’s a nice bright key. I hope this sounds something like an “Irish tune from the North” or perhaps akin to a Cape Breton reel. I play the tune once through here on YouTube: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fRF9nKTiZVY

Hope you like the tune, and please leave a comment!

thanks and best regards,
Randy Miller

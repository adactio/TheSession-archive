The Lau Jig

Written by the amazing trio that is Kris Drever, Martin Green and Aidan O‘Rourke, I first heard this tune on their ’Lightweights & Gentlemen’ album. I love the swinging between 12/8 and 9/8. (NB: I am not a seasoned ABC transcription user so be warned there are time signature changes between some of the bars that I was unable to notate!)
I play this tune in the same set that Lau play it in on their album track, between ‘Mattie and Karine’s’ and ‘The Creche’, and I couldn’t resist transcribing it to complete the set!

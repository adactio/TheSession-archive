Three comments

Humours Of Broadway

A tune from the album ‘Elixir’ by the Breton musician Pol Huellou, which was recorded originally in Wexford and released on LP and cassette back in the 1970’s. It’s recently been re-released I think. The group featured the wonderful Liam Weldon along with bouzouki player Sean Howley. Pol Huellou was living in Wexford at that time, so I’ve a feeling that the tunes on the album that had Wexford related names, like this one, were probably compositions of his? Broadway is a village in the south east corner of Wexford between Rosslare and Carne. It had a very popular music venue at that time, The Broadway Tavern. This tune is being played on a C whistle I reckon, given his ornamention etc, so I’m uploading it in C but also in D, which would be ‘natural’ whistle fingering. He does significant variation to the parts as he plays through, so much so the repeats are almost like additional parts, so I’ve notated his first repeats as i think it could be played as a 4 part single reel.

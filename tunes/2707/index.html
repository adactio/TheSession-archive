↳ Posted by rog on March 24th, 2004.

This is a great great tune, and whilst it is contained in “Jerry Holland’s Collection of Fiddle Tunes” Jerry credits both JH MacDonald & Dougie MacDonald as the composers. I thought it was one of Jerry’s myself until I looked in the book. Here’s how Jerry has it in his collection complete with the double stops;

X: 1
T: Ciaran Tourish
C: JH MacDonald & Dougie MacDonald
S: Jerry Holland’s Collection of Fiddle Tunes
R: reel
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
K: Dmaj
(3aba fa g2ec | fdd/d/d dAFA | BGG/G/G cAAg | faaf gece |

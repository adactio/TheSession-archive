Eight comments

Battle of Yellow Ford

This is a tune one of our band members recalled from distant memory. It obviously refers to the battle between Ulster and the British in 1598. I think it also has lyrics, but we’ve never been able to track them down. If anybody knows them, I’d love to get them. It’s a simple melody but sounds great as a twin fiddle tune with slides and doublestops added. We sometimes do it with bouzouki taking the melody line and mandolin on high harmony, and that sounds cool too. There is an MP3 of it on our website (deliveryboys.net) if you want to hear, although that version was before we wrote the harmony line.

↳ Posted by ScottC on January 3rd, 2003.

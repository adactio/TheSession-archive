Muineira De Poio

A muineira in the asturian area (in the North of Spain) is the same as the jig in Ireland. Its time signature is 6/8 or 12/8 but also 9/8.
This muineira is playd by Liam O‘Flynn and Carlos Nunez on O’Flynn’s “The Piper’s Call” CD. The tune is followed by another muineira, “Muineira De Ourense”.

↳ Posted by Christian_T on June 30th, 2004.

You can also find this muiñeira in the third album by Luar Na Lubre, “Ara Solis”.

↳ Posted by enjechic on August 15th, 2004.

Muiñeira de Poio

This tune is really called “Muiñeira de Poio”. Poio is a little town near Pontevedra in the South of Galicia, in the Rias Baixas.
It´s not an asturian tune. The real name is muiñeira, not muineira.

↳ Posted by Manu Novo on January 31st, 2005.

Two comments

The Mouse Behind The Harmonium

Some nights ago upon returning from a lively Session late at night I encountered a Mouse in my room, hiding away behind my bookshelf an the Harmonium. In my half-drunken state the pursuit of this little furry creature was very challenging, so instead of catching it, I wrote this reel and played on a mandolin.
This of course, along with some Cheddar calmed the mouse so I could easily catch it and release it into the garden.

Still this reel is best played on mandolin or fiddle, it can be difficult an flutes, and I hope it to be useful were there ever to be a mouse behind your harmonium.

↳ Posted by Torben Groß on July 11th, 2024.

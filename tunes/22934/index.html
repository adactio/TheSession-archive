One comment

Hillsland

This is a fine slow piece , composed by the Wexford accordionist John Redmond and features on his second album, ‘Box Sets’, which features an impressive selection of self-composed tunes and pieces. Thanks to Nóra Byrne Kavanagh for the transcription of waltzes and slow pieces for the Wexford Tune Project, including this tricky transcription. I’ve of course had to upload as a march, so just adjust the playback speed on the audio player to get a sense of the piece.

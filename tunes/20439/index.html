Five comments

El Redondo Encantado (Redondo Circle Dance)

From PV O’Donnell’s CD “Donegal Fiddler.” PV states that he learned this from violinist Lorenzo Martinez on a road trip to New Mexico. Note the key change to A major in the B section.

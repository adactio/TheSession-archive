Earl Grey In The Morning

This is the title tune to Earl Grey in the Morning duet’s CD “More Coffee Please” and is part of a set entitled “The Final Flight”. Both players in the duet are addicted to Earl Grey tea as well as coffee as part of their morning routine …. and afternoon …… OK, evening as well. The tune is played as a slow march but can also be played as a swung hornpipe. The key signature is mostly Dorian but the B section does feature a natural and flatted 6th.

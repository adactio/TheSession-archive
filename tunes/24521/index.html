From Mick Hoy & Eddie Duffy - sounds modern(ish)

This reel comes from a private recording of Mick Hoy and Eddie Duffy in the Cathal McConnell collection at the ITMA. It sounds like a modern tune (by modern, I mean mid 20th century of course) - not unlike something in the compositional style of Paddy O’Brien or Seán Ryan. A friend recently told me that Louis Quinn brought a lot of Ed Reavy’s music around Fermanagh in the 1960s and 1970s which aroused my interest but this reel doesn’t appear in Reavy’s book so it’s probably not one of his tunes.

Emily’s

Written by Colin Farrell and transcribed in the original key from his 2020 tune a day series. The chromatics are excellent and his recording has a lot of inspiration for variations.

The tune is named for Emily Aldridge and her husband, Rufus, after Colin played a house concert hosted by them.

Three comments

Dearbhail Finnegan’s

An original composition from Cavan’s grand accordion master Martin Donohoe.
It appears on CD2, Track 08b of his 2006 album Blúiríní Blasta.

I transcribed the music from that album, as I heard it, including the variations on the repeats, to give you a flavour of the recording.

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on May 16th, 2023.

Re: Dearbhail Finnegan’s

This hornpipe can double up as a scottische.
It uses the full range of the tin whistle (D-b).
Mode: G ionian.

Give it a good lilt;
emphasise the outlying high and low notes on the off beats;
and away you go.

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on May 16th, 2023.

Re: Dearbhail Finnegan’s

Whistlers will find tricky trying to reproduce the box player’s C-natural rolls. However, it is a good tune to practice tongue-rolling on, should you choose to play them as opposed to ignoring them.

The range actually extends below D on this score (part 2, bars 4 & 5 ). If you are a beginner piper or fluter, remember you can revert to playing one octave up, as in:
…|BAGB AFDc|
Bdba gfed|…
This trick doesn’t always go unnoticed in solo playing, however, in this particular case, it does not affect the character or integrity of the tune, I find.

Re: This Is How My Joyous Life’s Begun

This is a tune composed by myself for (or about) my friend fellow-musician. She is a great classical flautist who plays the irish flute too. Once we went in a street after a session and she told me one episode of her life.
“I was rehearsing (on boehm flute) to an exam for my music college in its hall, all optimistic. But a professor came out from a door and passed by saying: ”Oh, it is you who’s hissing here.“ It’s ruined nearly all my optimism and mood then. This is how my joyous life’s begun.”
Having heard that I said - oh, that’s a very good name for a tune!
So the tune is about that. I find this long a’s and the f-nat necessary for the proper feeling of the tune but if you want it symmetric you may play it without these extra half bars.

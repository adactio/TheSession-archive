Lios A ’Cheoil

Nice polka composed by North Kerry fiddler Tom Barrett. Originally from Bedford, near Listowel, Tom moved to Clonmel, County Tipperary, where he founded the Clonmel branch of Comhaltas Ceoltoiri Eireann in 1951. He also gained an appreciation for Scottish music when he was pipe sergeant at the local Kickham Barracks.

I think the title refers to his house in Clonmel.

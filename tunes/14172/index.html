The Reel Of The Gordon Highlanders

This is a jaunty 4 part Scottish reel composed by Pipe-Major Stuart D. Samson. It is the first tune of the
first track of Green Ginger’s Gang on the Same Gate album of Scottish Country dance music.

↳ Posted by zoronic on February 6th, 2015.

The tune was composed for the Scottish Country Dance of the same name which I devised to mark the bicentenary of the Gordon Highlanders in 1994. The music has also been recorded in 1997 by Alastair Hunter & the Lorne Scottish Dance Band (Lorne Choice of S.C.D., DACD9716, track 4) and in 2oo2 by the Colin Dewar Scottish Dance Band (Special Requests volume 5, SPCDoo5, track 12)

The other Paddy O’Brien

This tune is played in a beautiful performance by Oliver Devaney, all-Ireland accordianist, on the CD “Cairde”. He pairs it with Josephine Marsh’s Phyllis’ Birthday in F (thesession tune 4149 in G).
In the liner notes he credits the tune to Paddy O’Brien of Offaly.

Re: The Policeman’s Request

I contacted Paddy O’Brian to ask him what was the story behind the name of the tune and this is what he answered: “The Policeman’s Request” is actually a request for a tune! My policeman friend, fellow accordion player Donal Ward from Donegal, always asked me to play one of my own compositions, so I named it in his honor, “The Policeman’s Request.”

↳ Posted by FiddleTramp on September 20th, 2023.

Re: The Policeman’s Request

In the notes to Paddy’s Tune Collection (where this reel is volume one, #334) he writes:
I composed this during a visit to Donegal in 1970. I named it after
Donal Ward, of Carrick, who played the accordion and later became a
member of the Irish police force. His insistence on my playing it in
public probably saved the tune from being discarded in some
eternal dustbin.

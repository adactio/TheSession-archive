The Delaval

Seaton Delaval Hall is the ancestral home of the very colourful Delaval family near Seaton Sluice in Northumberland, which inspired this hornpipe.
Tommy Murray, was a fiddler of Irish extraction whose band (The OKAYS) played in the North East of England during the 1940s. As well as songs and dance tunes, he wrote many fiddle tunes, shared widely with fiddlers and pipers in Northumberland and fiddlers in Clare and Kildare. Many of his tunes have been lost, though some appear in piping and fiddle tune books in the UK, Ireland, USA and Australia. This is one of his extant jigs.

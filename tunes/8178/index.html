Seven comments

From Liz Carroll’s newest album with John Doyle, called In Play. This is one of her own compositions, I believe, as are most of the other tunes on that album. This tune was on the requests list recently.

↳ Posted by fiddleplayer01 on January 23rd, 2008.

Liner Notes

The liner notes say “The first jig is in the book ”The Northern Fiddler“ and was contributed by fiddler John Loughran.”

Three comments

Les Filles de Peveril

Jean Duval has written over 200 wonderful tunes, many of which have a very minor/melancholy feel to them. I bought his book but I won’t transcribe all his tunes here. Message me if you would like more info and I can ask him. This reel and The Halloween Jig are the most often played here in Montreal. I’ve heard Jean makes fantastic goat cheese too!

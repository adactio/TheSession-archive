Three comments

Put On The Steam

Very cool Aminor reel learned from the playing of Alex Francis MacKay on A Lifelong Home.

MacKay does a shuffley chord thing in bars 2 and 6 of the B part that I’ve shown only once so you can hear it both ways in the ABC.

↳ Posted by dancarney84 on September 12th, 2014.

Second Setting

This setting appears in Joseph Lowes’ 2nd collection from the 1840s and more recently in Cape Breton Scottish Collection compiled by Paul Cranford. It was recorded on LP by Paddy Leblanc in 1960 as McLaren’s.

↳ Posted by dancarney84 on September 12th, 2014.

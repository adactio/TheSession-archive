Hughie Shortie’s, X:4

This is a Cape Breton setting of the tune taken from the playing of Troy MacGillivray on Live at the Music Room. I’ve standardised it somewhat so it’s not exactly as it is on the recording (Troy typically plays (3fga, rather than fg, at the end of the second and sixth bars for example).

↳ Posted by DonaldK on November 15th, 2023.

Re: Hughie Shorty’s

From Paul Cranford:
“Hughie Shorty was a great supporter of traditional music. A good friend of Johnny Wilmot, Hughie often visited, bringing home-tapes which he had recorded. The tune Hughie Shorty’s was composed and recorded by Johnny in the mid ’60s.”

↳ Posted by DonaldK on November 15th, 2023.

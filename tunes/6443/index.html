Five comments

Johnstown

This was requested by someone on the “Chiff & Fipple” website, so I’ve posted it here. This is a very fine slow reel, composed by Edinburgh-based Rebecca Knorr, one of the best traditional flute-players in Scotland. It was named after the town in Pennsylvania where Rebecca’s grandmother was born. She has recorded it twice, as a guest on Tony McManus’s first CD and also with the band of which she is a member, “Calluna”.

↳ Posted by Kenny on December 1st, 2006.

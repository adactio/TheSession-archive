Y Gwr A’i Farch

Welsh waltz that i played at a session in Llandeilo. This living version is in a different time than the version in the book; ‘Two Hundred and Fifty Welsh Airs’,that is in 3/4 time, and in the key of Gmaj. I will post this next…….

Pronounced……….

Gwr …………goooor
a’i…………….aaay
Farch………..varch (ch sounded as in ‘loch’)

“Harry Lane’s mazurka”

Although it is called a waltz,
it works just as fine as a mazurka.
There are nice catchy phrases in it.

In A minor,
the 6th only appears in part 2 (both natural and sharp) and both the use of some passing sharp 4ths and the leading note (sharp 7th) contributes to its neo French swing musette style.

I cannot remember where i first or last heard it, but it is one of those tunes you know right away are -or can become- timeless classics (possibly because they present a natural, well balanced blend of original and timeless features)

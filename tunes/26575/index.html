Re: Wittenberger Apothekerpolonesse Nr. 19

Not Irish, but finding its way into a variety of sessions where it’s commonly known as ‘The Poisonous Polonaise’; as I understand, this is because it was apparently found written in an apothecary’s (chemist’s) notebook in the place where the poisons were kept. One of a number of tunes that have been rediscovered from old MSS, and which are now finding favour after a period in which, I gather, much folk music in Germany was treated with reserve as a reaction to the malign nationalistic purposes for which it was used in the mid C20th. A year or so ago, the German musician Bjorn Kaidel brought this, and some others, to one of the Nyckelharpa events held at Halsway Manor.

If of interest, http://www.rudemex.co.uk/library/RM_arrangements/Poisonous%20polonaise,%20The%20(Am)%20(3-pt).pdf is a 3-part arrangement in Am. The tune library there also includes the same in Dm.

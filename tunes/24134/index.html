Sit Down Under My Protection

Tune number 1 from Bunting’s 1840 “Ancient Music of Ireland”. Not a waltz, though it might work as one. He says he got it from a harper named Byrne in 1799, but says that it is “very ancient, author and date unknown.” He also says it should be “moderately quick”, but I think it’s supposed to be slow.

Dunluce

A slow air, written by Gary Moore of Belfast (best known for multiple stints in Thin Lizzy and as a bluesman, but also recorded with the Chieftans). This released in 1989 on the album “After The War”.

The studio recording can be found here:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tSjeq0jwfaY

I did my best to preserve his phrasing but also to make it sensible to read (ergo, keeping the individual phrases commensurate with bar lines). This piece is performed in a rubato fashion, around 55 bpm.

Four comments

Dédicado à Joe

Quebec reel, composed by Martin Racine. This one is a Quebec-ified version of a piece by Trip San Antonio called “Viva el West Side”.

La Bottine Souriante play it on their album “Je voudrais changer d’chapeau”, after Hommage à Edmond Parizeau. Sets great together! Starts here @ 2:14:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpqpbXDZ66k

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on January 18th, 2019.

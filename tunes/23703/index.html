Re: New Bouree

It’s on CD SDL446, “Harps, Dulcimers & Hurdy Gurdies”. A mix of folk and early music, inc the York Waites
https://www.wyastone.co.uk/harps-dulcimers-hurdy-gurdies.html
It is track 3 on the link, as I gave above, Click it and you can preview the track - no login needed
https://music.apple.com/gb/album/harps-dulcimers-hurdy-gurdies/1261350108
The track is solo gurdy, Nigel playing alone.

It’s also on the older Saydisc CD SDL374 “The Music of the Hurdy-Gurdy”. The track can be sampled here; it’s the first of the two tunes on the last track:
https://www.prestomusic.com/classical/products/8071626--the-music-of-the-hurdy-gurdy
I bought this CD myself, back in the day, mid 90’s, which is where I first heard the tune. This was recorded over a decade before Youtube even started. Which is why I suspect the “New Bouree” came first then morphed into the waltz more recently? TBH it’s all semantics, it’s only a name change. They’re cracking tunes, whatever name they go under! Although on a wind instrument it can be a challenge… hence my key change! Thankyou for your interest.

Miss Hutton’s

I first heard this tune on Jerry Holland’s “Fiddler’s Choice.” I looked for it online and found a source that says it was composed by John Bowie. I like this tune a lot.

Here’s a new setting of Miss Hutton’s, as played by Buddy MacMaster in The Judique Flyer. Buddy played it with a fairly relaxed triplet feel throughout, but I’ve notated it as a dotted rhythm to keep it easy to read.

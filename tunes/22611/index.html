Two comments

Clonattin In The Fields

A lovely double jig from the composer of the well-known ‘Gorey March’, Tommy Kinsella. Thanks so much to Tommy’s daughter, Sinead Tobin, concertina player and teacher of the Tuaisceart CCE group for passing on Tommy’s other compositions to me for uploading to the database as part of the Wexford Tunes Project. Over the next few weeks I’ll add the rest of the material from Tommy.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on December 31st, 2022.

Re: Clonattin In The Fields

The title refers to a book of local history written by his brother-in-law. Clonattin - Cluain Aitinn in Irish, the gorse meadow, is an area just outside of the town of Gorey.

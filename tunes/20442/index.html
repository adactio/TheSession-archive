Royal Chair

Second jig of the first track from the ‘’New Irish Tin Whistle Tunes‘’ by Olaf Sickmann..
About the tune:
‘’Well preserved castles and palaces have always fascinated me and I love imagining all the different people who may have sat on a particular throne.‘’
Great jig!

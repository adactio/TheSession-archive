I Rambled Once

Learned by Joyce as a child in Limerick. Joyce (Old Irish Folk Music and Songs), 1909; No. 76, pg. 41.
Source: The Fiddler’s Companion.

This tune appears on the Colm Gannon, Jesse Smith and John Blake CD ‘The Ewe with the Crooked Horn’.

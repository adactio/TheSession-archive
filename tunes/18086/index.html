Four comments

Craic With Jack

From the playing of Leaping Lulu and Eileen Ivers & Immigrant Soul. Composed by Eileen Ivers and Joanie Madden.

Jolly Green, live, second tune (with repeats of A & B parts):
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVXgCMS---Q

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on June 30th, 2019.

Craic With Jack, X:2

A few variations.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on June 30th, 2019.

Craic With Jack, X:3

From the playing of Leaping Lulu.

Have updated X1 to be from Eileen Ivers & Immigrant Soul, and, X2 as a whistle friendlier setting (with no notes below bottom-D).

The Eileen Ivers and Leaping Lulu recordings have whistle players, who simply stop playing during the notes below bottom-D (in the bars of “A,>B, CD”).

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on February 23rd, 2020.

Craic With Jack, X:4

Some variations from the playing of Eileen Ivers & Immigrant Soul.

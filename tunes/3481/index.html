Rooney’s Favourite

This is the last tune on the album ‘Angelina Carberry and Martin Quinn’.

The last two notes of the first bar can be attractively varied at will by substituting ge for AA

Plenty of room for simple or complex ornamentation at the sign of the ~ .

Mike Cooney’s Fancy, X:6

Here is how Paddy O‘Brien (Offaly) plays the jig bu Paddy O’Brien (Tipperary).
None of the other settings here go down to the lower F natural the way Paddy
does, or put in the triplet leading up to the high a, both of which are very nice.
In the notes to his Tune Collection (volume three, jig #76) Paddy thinks
that the other Paddy named this tune after the piper Michael Cooney, but the
other comments here say that it was a drummer Mike Cooney.

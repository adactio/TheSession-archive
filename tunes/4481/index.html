Brid Gorman: The Bluebells Are Blooming

The Bluebells Are Blooming. This is the title track for Brid Gorman’s (county Clare) CD . . . highly recommended. Eoin O’Neill (also county Clare) on bouzouki with well placed, perfect backing. It’s a great recording, with lovely pace, and great tone on her C flute. I’m learning her setting, which holds true to the setting here but with the lovely little variations typical of a great musician, just altogether gorgeous stuff. I was lucky to find her with Eoin, just the two of them, in a restaurant in Ennis a few years back, what a rare treat.

Suomalainen Polkka

Finnish traditional polka.

Can be found at http://dickatlee.com/music/finnish/ as “A-minor Polkka”, set with Hyvarinen’s Polkka (https://thesession.org/tunes/16835 ).

Apparently “Suomalainen” translates to “Finnish” in English.

Four comments

Cat In The Morning

A tune I composed with affection for my partner’s cat, Morris - and my partner too, of course. Often when I play melodeon at home, Morris flops on my feet, resting his head on my toes. I felt he deserved his own tune.

↳ Posted by Ian Gillingham on March 11th, 2026.

Re: Cat In The Morning

It sounds to me like your bar lines are in the wrong place (all a beat early) in the first part.
So it should start:
|:(3def|“G” g2dB “D”d2B2| “Am”cBAG “D”D2DD|….

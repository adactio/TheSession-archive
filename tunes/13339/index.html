Tommy Doucet’s Lancers

An interesting tune in 6/8, which requires a bit of position shifting, hence the title of Tommy’s LP: I Used to Play Some Pretty Tough Tunes. The original recording may be heard here: https://app.box.com/s/jpqor81x8oi01iuszulq This is yet another Lancers tune, one of many. I don’t think this one is found in any of the old collections.

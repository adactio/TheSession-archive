Summer’s Coming

A lovely number from that magical 2005 “raised-half-step” album Tunes by Sharon Shannon, Frankie Gavin, Michael McGoldrick & Jim Murray.

Composed by Javier Tejedor, it comes before Pasucais of Coana. Technically not a slide, and there’s some groovy shifts in time. However, I love this tune… AND it is my first attempt at getting my head around ABC notation!

Two comments

Miss Angela Gillies Of Borve

Another good tune from Gabe McVarish. It is tune three of track two on his Eclection album (2010). Setting from Ho-Ro-Gheallaidh. Session Tunes For Scottish Fiddlers. Volume Three, p.7 Taigh na Teud. 2008
I was lucky to attend parts of a fiddle course run by Gabe at the Gaelic College. Skye, in 2004. Nice guy and outstanding musician

↳ Posted by Edgar Bolton on July 12th, 2023.

Miss Angela Gillies Of Borve, X:2

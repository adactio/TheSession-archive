Re: Safe House

This tune seems to be mixed up with Navvy On the Shore (Line), Paddy Jim Frank’s. The B part is distinct and I cannot find a good match for it from other tunes. The A parts are the same.
Navvy On the Shore is a fairly common tune (McEvoy “Comb Your Hair and Curl It”, Kevin Henry “One’s Own Place” (Paddy Jim Frank), and a few other quite notable recordings.
https://thesession.org/tunes/4857/recordings
For the version with the different B part I cannot find any references other than “Trip To Sligo” (O‘Donnell) and this Leeds version (Safe House). I have found a couple recordings with the “Leed’s” B part“ Jason O’Rourke ”Roguery Road" and Bernard Flaherty’s version from The Trip To Sligo tape that I got somewhere (?).

I think this should probably listed under tune#4857

Gibraltar Point

We play at a yearly gig for the Yacht Club at Gibraltar Point - a large salt-flat nature reserve South of Skegness, at the top of The Wash.
Not ITM but catchy, I wrote the tune for this years BBQ, in a definite Northumbrian style for some of their more Geordie members.

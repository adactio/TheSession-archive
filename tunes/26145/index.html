Three comments

The King’s Hall

I learnt this hornpipe from a recording of the High Level Ranters featured on the compilation album ‘The Northumbrian Small Pipes” released in 1974. I’m not sure who it was composed by but presumably it’s a Northumbrian tune.

↳ Posted by Jake on November 29th, 2025.

Re: The King’s Hall

I know this as a tune attributed to Billy Pigg (Northumbrian piper) under the title ‘Hepple Haugh’.

↳ Posted by Bazza on November 29th, 2025.

Re: The King’s Hall

I’ve had a quick look but can’t find it in any of the Northumbrian pipers‘ tune books on my shelf, but it sounds quite typical of Billy Pigg’s style of tune. Compare, for example, ’Anne of Hindhope‘ (http://www.rudemex.co.uk/library/Alphabetical/Anne%20of%20Hindhope.pdf) and ’Cote Walls’ (http://www.rudemex.co.uk/library/Alphabetical/Cote%20Walls%20(a).pdf)

Four comments

Phoebe’s

This is Damien O’Kane’s tune that he wrote for his daughter, Phoebe. This particular transcription is based on a live performance he gave with David Kosky and involves more 16th notes than the original recording.

↳ Posted by Charleville on July 21st, 2024.

Phoebe’s, X:2

This setting is based on Damien’s original playing on the “Banjophony” record with Ron Block.

↳ Posted by Charleville on July 21st, 2024.

Phoebe’s, X:3

This setting is based on the fiddle from the “Banjophony” record. It’s simpler than the banjo part in the first half, but since it doesn’t really play the melody in the 2nd half, I used the identical melody from the banjo setting.

↳ Posted by Charleville on July 21st, 2024.

Phoebe’s, X:4

This is the fiddle version from the Banjophony record but in D major, a much more likely key.

↳ Posted by Charleville on July 24th, 2024.

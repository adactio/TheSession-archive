Jeremy And Regina’s

Tune by Orkney fiddler Louise Bichan. This transcription is from a tuneset by Fara from their 2014 EP (back when Louise was part of the band, she’s since moved to the US).

On a transcription note, this is my first time transcribing a hornpipe, so please edit and correct as needed.

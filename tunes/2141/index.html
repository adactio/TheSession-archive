Re: Sliabh Na mBan

Slievenamon (Irish: Sliabh na mBan pronounced “shleeve - nah - mon”),”mountain of the women” is a mountain in County Tipperary, Ireland.
The origin of the mountain’s name is explained in Irish mythology.

According to the tale, Fionn mac Cumhaill was sought after by many young women, but he said that he could have only one partner.
According to the tale, the hero Fionn mac Cumhaill was sought after by many young women.
Fionn stood atop the mountain and declared that whichever woman won a footrace to the top would be his wife.

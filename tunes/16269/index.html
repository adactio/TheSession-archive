Two comments

Midnight Walker

One of Davy Spillane’s most popular and haunting Uilleann Pipes pieces found backing a hundred different videos on YouTube. I’m not familiar with pipe ornaments but I’ve transcribed it fairly closely for low whistle.

↳ Posted by Mikethebook on August 4th, 2017.

Re: Midnight Walker

I’ve added in the bridge. The main theme is played twice (the second time with slight variations) and the bridge follows on. Then there’s an interlude without whistle and the main theme is played a final time.

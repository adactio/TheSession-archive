↳ Posted by Weejie on May 9th, 2013.

“Gan Ainm” ~ :-P

Sorry, no joy here. If it’s not good enough for a name, then why dump it here? I have too much respect for the tradition of ‘gan ainm’ to ever consider using it as an actual ‘title’, but, in truth, there are already plenty of tunes ‘gan ainm’ knocking about, and, from my long recognition, they were all better tunes than this, and made more sense. I usually just skip over the flotsam and jetsam that settles here occasionally, but once in awhile it’s that silly that it’s hard not to add a verbal wince in the comments.

Out of 6 tune submissions by yourself the four that aren’t of your own design are non-trad ‘Riverdance’ sprawls, no surprises there. But as bad, worse, than naming this sprawl of yours “Gan Ainm” you also have the gall to name one of your own composition after yourself, “Hunter’s”, duh?! Hey, it takes all kinds…
https://thesession.org/tunes/12690

It might also be a good idea to learn a bit more about ABC notation, and there are some good resources online for that. Some of your bars/measures don’t add up, see the reduced form below, minus chords and the outdated /2s… For one example:
http://www.lesession.co.uk/abc/abc_notation.htm

You might also read the FAQs under ‘Help’ with regards to things self-composed, and check out some of the relevant discussions on that, for example:
https://thesession.org/discussions/23177

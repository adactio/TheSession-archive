Gneevguillia’s Polka

Claire Mann puts this tune after “Ella Buckley’s Fancy.” In the beginning of the B part to this tune, the chord there is Em, not G major as one might think. The chord progression for the B part is Em, D, C, D; Em, D, C-D, G (repeat B part). The piano player on Claire Mann’s recording, Brian McAlpine, is just fantastic. His accompanying transforms the tunes into something wonderful.

Muirsín Durcan / Glountane Monument #2 / The Thrashing Of The Oats, X:4

As played by Timmy O’Connor on his album, As It Was In Toureendarby.
https://scullysfest.bandcamp.com/album/as-it-was-in-toureendarby

This is one the famously obscure tunes engraved on the monument to Pádraig O’Keeffe at Glountane Cross, the first of them being this one: https://thesession.org/tunes/20646

Some local musicians believe that the three-part polka is actually two (i.e., 1.5) polkas, including actual Muirsín Durcan cut in half: https://thesession.org/tunes/8516 However, the only way to solve this mystery is to see the original manuscript, since O’Keeffe wasn’t exactly shy about adding parts to tunes either – think Johnny Cope!

↳ Posted by Bregolas on February 12th, 2022.

Davy Roche’s No. 1

A nice reel composed some years ago by the Wexford accordionist Davy Roche, collected by me as part of the Wexford tune project, which I have mentioned in other posts. Davy is an All-Ireland winner on the 3 row accordion in 1980, during the period when there was a separate competition for 3 row players. He also made an LP around that time for a UK record label as I recall. The tune like many A minor reels has some similarities at the start with other tunes like The Corner house or John Brosnan’s reel, but different enough to stand on it’s own I think.

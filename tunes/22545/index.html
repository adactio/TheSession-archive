Le Step à Ti-Phonse

From the playing of fiddler André Alain (1931-2001), St-Basile-de-Portneuf, Québec.
The prefix ‘ti’ means ‘little’, and the title translates as ‘little Alphonse’s (favorite) step-dance (tune)’.
Alphonse was a good step-dancer indeed.

Re: Willy Will You E’er Return

This is from Captain Fraser of Knockie’s Collection of Highland Music (1874), where it says, “This is…an ancient air, usually sung as a rant, but [my father] sung it slow and accented, and he is informed Neil (sic) Gow performed it in that style, although he never published it.”

Re: The Bluebird’s Schottische

A version of this tune was made popular by the late Al Dean and his “All-Stars” band from Freer, Texas. Al and his group traveled all over South Texas, which is a lot of territory, back in the 60’s, 70’s and 80’s. He played covers of lots of great, danceable music, but the highlight of the show was his version of “Cotton-Eyed Joe” immediately followed by his version of Bluebird Schottische, which everyone called “Sweetheart Schottische”.
Couples would link up shoulder to shoulder, 4 or 5 couples together and move around the dance floor in a wheel configuration. Lots of stomping, dust-raising, and synchronized shouts of “B…S…”. Unfortunately, dance halls are on the decline in Texas.

↳ Posted by J. K. Botter on February 11th, 2025.

Re: Alex Sandy Skinner

Sandy Skinner (1833-1883) was James Scott Skinner’s big brother and was known as a dancing master in Banchory, Aberdedenshire, and a somewhat “rough” fiddle player (J Murdoch Henderson’s words). He wasn’t nearly as prolific as his wee brother in the composing field, but two of his tunes can be found in the Miller O’ Hirn Collection (1881): “Master Andrew Grant” and “Union Brig.” A third tune, “The Dominie’s Delight,” is there too, and was a joint composition with J.S.S.

There are some slight differences in the published setting of “Alex Sandy F Skinner” from Kohelet’s rendering, but since he doesn’t mention where he got his setting I can’t say a lot about that. Rather than give a list of all the differences, I’ll add another setting.

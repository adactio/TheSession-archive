St. Stephen’s Day

Sweet modal hornpipe by Paddy O’Brien with lots of room for variation. Inserting an old-style triplet run in the penultimate bar can be fun -- ex. (3=fed (3cBA (3dcB (3AG^F|[A,2D2c2]G^F [G,2G2]|]

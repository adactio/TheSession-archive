Three comments

The Dumps

A playford tune, I have set it as a jig/slip jig though that could be debated. This tune has a fantastic variation by John Dipper I heard at a session in Bristol last year. I will venture to add his version (the minor) next.

↳ Posted by Joel Overton on June 25th, 2026.

The Dumps, X:2

John Dipper’s minor version of The Dumps, preserving all the interesting parts of the original and sprucing up the rest. Has some extra beats added, however.

Can technically be engraved as 6/8 all the way through but that’s not really true to the feel of it.

↳ Posted by Joel Overton on June 25th, 2026.

The Dumps, X:2 CORRECTION

CORRECTION: The start of the last bar on the 6th line in the 2nd variation ought to be crotchet quaver, instead of two quavers (d2G FED)

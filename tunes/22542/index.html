Peter’s

This is the final reel of the last set on John Redmond’s album of his compositions, ‘Box Sets’. John, originally from Ballindaggin in County Wexford has spent many years in the U.S. and recorded ‘Box Sets’ and the earlier album ‘East to Northeast’. He has recently returned to County Wexford to live and is a wonderful addition to the trad scene back home. His compositions are being notated and uploaded to the session.org as part of the Wexford tune project.

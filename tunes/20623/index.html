Re: Gillespie’s / Leinster

O’Neill’s other setting is in G as The Leinster Hornpipe. It was published in his Waifs and Strays of Gaelic Melody (#331). His source was Francis E. Walsh, San Francisco. It is a little different to X:1 and X:2.

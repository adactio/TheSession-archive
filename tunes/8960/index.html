Hi all. For those of you who’ve been following, this is the 4th and final tune in the set by the Iron Horse on their first album that starts with “The Cape Breton Fiddler’s…”, followed by “Morag’s No. 2” and “The Lintie”. If anyone out there can tell me what this tune is called, I’ll be very grateful. Is extremely simple, in fact I didn’t realise until I wrote it down just now how similar the second half is to the first half, but it sounds good, and I don’t usually ask more of a fiddle tune than that!

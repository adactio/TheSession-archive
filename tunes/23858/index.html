Two comments

Brendan Mulvihill’s

At 11:14 in this video, Brendan Mulvihill, Angelina Carberry, and Dan Brouder play this reel
which Dan introduces as being composed by Brendan.
I looked through Brendan’s books, Irish Scroll vol 1 and vol 2, at all the reels he credits to himself
and did not find this one. So it must be a newer composition of his.
Since I don’t know what he named it, I’ll have to call it Brendan Mulvihill’s until we find out
the right name.

Re: Empty Creels

Listening carefully to the video you can hear Brendan ask Dan “what’ll we play?” and Dan says
“Empty Creels and them”. So I looked in Irish Scroll vol 2 and found the reel Empty Creels.
He didn’t credit it to himself, but he might have just forgotten to do that, so we’ll assume that it is
still a Brendan Mulvihill composition. In the book, he wrote the tune in D major with all the C naturals
as accidentals. I wrote it in D mixolydian with the C sharps as accidentals.

Anyway, I changed the name to Empty Creels.

Three comments

The Bell Rock

This is the 1st tune of the 5th track of The Tannahill Weavers “Arnish Light” album.
It was written by their flute/whistle player Phil Smillie. I’ll send him a link to this
entry to see if he objects to putting the notes here. Its a zippy little tune, quite good for the whistle.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P-1WlqGjjPo
http://www.tannahillweavers.com/notes/1226not5.htm
http://www.tannahillweavers.com/theband/phil_smillie.htm

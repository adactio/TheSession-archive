Three comments

Clyde’s Banks

A Kevin Burke composition.
1st recorded I believe, in 1992, on the Open House album, where it is paired with another of his lively composition; The Long Acre.

Kevin Burke has described The Long Acre as a hornpipe. Both these tunes could easily be described as scottisches/barndances (you could certainly dance these steps to them) so I decided to list this one as a barndance.
Enjoy.

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on June 15th, 2024.

Clyde’s Banks, X:2

The composer recorded this tune in A on the fiddle.
To make things easier for pipers, fluters et al. here it is in G (ionian), i.e.; transposed down a tone.

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on June 15th, 2024.

Re: Clyde’s Banks

.
Converting all the g’s and low E’s, in the A ionian version, to high e’s, makes it possible to play this lovely tune on the Scottish pipes.

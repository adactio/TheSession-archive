Seven comments

Composed by Gerry Harrington, who originally recorded it on *The Smoky Chimney* with Eoghan O‘Sullivan and Paul De Grae. My transcription is based on Desy Adams’ version that he plays on *Wooden Flute Obsession 3*, minus ornamentation.

↳ Posted by bdh on May 29th, 2009.

The Furze In Bloom, X:4

I absolutely love playing this tune with the chromatic harmonica. It will always be in my repertoire. I never played or heard it in a session, this is one of those tunes that I enjoy playing in the quietness of my home. I learned it from this video that my friend PJ did in YouTube quite long time ago, it is the first tune in the set:

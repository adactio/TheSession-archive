Composer of this piece

The composer of this wonderful reel is Paddy Davey of Belfast. I was taught it by a good friend of his, Martin Shane. Paddy wrote this tune in The Old Rectory, Glencolmcille, County Donegal in 1995.
The following is the abc as it was taught to me.

X:1
T:Rectory Reel, The
C:Composed by Patrick J. Davey, Belfast
C:Copyright 2009 DaveyMusic
C:www.daveymusic.com
C:Arrangement: Martin Shane
R:Reel
M:4/4
L:1/8
K:Em
egfd eBBB |AcBA GEED |EBGE FAdA |Bege fagf |
e2 fd eBBB |AcBA GEED |EBGE FAdA |Bg fd e2 ef :|
|:fe Be febe |edAd fagf |geBe gebg |afde fe e2 |

Quick notes about MAGPIEKATE’s setting

1) |d(3e/d/c/ec dAAA| (measure 5) can also be used as the first measure of the repeat of the A section.
2) Everything after the second ending…is part of the second ending. I’m just not a good enough programmer to extend the bracket over all those measures.
3) This song sounds amazing when the whole sheebang is repeated. An example: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kj6CiT1ldQ (This video is where I learned this song).

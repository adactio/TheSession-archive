Fynbo

A Danish Tune from the island of Fyn.

I’m very surprised that it’s not been posted before as it’s one of the better known Danish tunes and very popular in Shetland and Scottish sessions.

I know, I know, this is an Irish site before anyone says anything. :-)
It’s still one of these fairly ubiquitous tunes though.

I’ve also noticed that the tune can be found on the Riddell Fiddles site where it is referred to as “Smedens Anden Fynbo”.

I’ve used the polka category as it is in 2/4. However, it should be more correctly described as a “hopsa”.

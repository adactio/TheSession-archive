The First Minister’s Welcome To Speyshfest

A tune written by fiddler Bryony Stewart several years ago that I heard for the first time at Speyfest 2023. It aquired the above name after an appearance by the then First Minister of Scotland, Humza Yousaf, at Speyfest where he mispronounced the name of the festival.

The tune is slightly chromatic and syncopated which gives it a funky feel that I really like in reels. It also fits round the fingers nicely on a fiddle. Enjoy!

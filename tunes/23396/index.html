Two comments

Along Wexford Quayside

Here’s another Wexford Tune Collection composition, this composed by the Ferns-born, Waterford-based pianist and flautist, Nóra Byrne-Kavanagh. Nóra won back-to-back Senior All-Ireland fleadh titles on piano and was a member of the wonderful Gorey Céilí Band of some years ago. Wexford quayside will undoubtedly be alive with traditional music, song and dance when the Fleadh Cheoil is hosted there in August ’24.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 5th, 2023.

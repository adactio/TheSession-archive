Breatnaigh Abú

Not a polka! A slow march - more of an air - for the violists out there as it does go all the way down on the C string. This site seems not to allow it but adding “V:1 alto” before the body of the tune, abc converters will render it in the alto key.

I have a feeling it might have been recorded on a 5-string, but only goes to the f# in second position a couple times on a regular viola.

Violinists could play this in E:

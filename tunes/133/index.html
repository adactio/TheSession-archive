Big Reel of Ballynacally

Leave it to Solas to feature a simple tune with such a quirky twist--those Fnaturals in both halves--and then spice it up with artfully placed triplets and rolls. This version is based on Win Horan’s fiddling from Sunny Spells & Scattered Showers.

↳ Posted by tony lavery on November 3rd, 2002.

Made famous in Scotland by “Silly Wizard” - probably 20 years before “Solas” even came into existence. O’Riada’s “pre-Chieftains” mixed it with “Callaghan’s”. It was often thought of and played as one 4-part tune, rather than 2 x 2 part tunes.

The Big Reel Of Ballynacally, X:4

Based on Ceoltóirí Chualann rendition. Played ABAB etc. Baile An Chalaidh is a townland in the west of Corca Dhuibhne, in English, Ballincolla. As this reel is also known as the Big Kerry Reel, I rest my case.
A similar version is in B&S vol 1.40 (just above Cal Callaghan’s, another reel from the Kerry/W Cork area, which Ceoltóirí Chualann blend with this reel.)

↳ Posted by brailsford on May 17th, 2020.

Re: The Big Reel Of Ballynacally

This reel was performed “go bríomhar ealaíonta” in Ballincolla before the Great Hunger (1845-52) according to Tomás Mac Síthigh in “Paróiste an Fheirtéaraigh”. May I suggest “ The Big Reel of Ballinacolla” or better, “Ríl Mhór Bhaile an Chalaidh” for the title?

Re: The Great Reel Of Ballincolla

Renamed based on conversation with locals in Corca Dhuihne. The story goes that when Seán Ó Riada was staying near Ballyferriter, he heard this reel air being hummed/lilted by the bean an tí. She gave him the tune which he later used with Ceoltóirí Chualann.

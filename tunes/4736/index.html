The Pining Maid, X:2

This version appears in P.W. Joyce’s Old Irish Music and Songs (1909), vol. 1, p. 163-164, No. 355.
Joyce copied this tune “from a MS evidently written by a skilled fiddler with much musical taste, from Limerick, but the name of the writer nowhere appears.”

↳ Posted by Moxhe on November 3rd, 2015.

The Pining Maid, X:3

This G minor (really G ionian) version of the tune is played by Jake James on his 2018 album “Firewood”.

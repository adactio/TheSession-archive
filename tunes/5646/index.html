Re: Peter O’Leary’s

I am Peter O’Leary born in Graignamanagh, Co. Kilkenny, now living in Malahide, north of Dublin. I very much enjoyed the session at Sydney’s Gaelic Club in 2007 where I had the pleasure of meeting and playing music with Dr. Dow. Caitriona is my eldest daughter. She is a well- known musician and singer of Early, Folk and Irish music and she has many popular CDs to her name. (See www.caitrionaoleary.com )
I composed a double jig to play on the flute at her wedding in New York in 2000 which I called "Caitriona’s Fancy’ and when I played it at the Sydney session, Dr Dow liked it very much.
I look forward to visiting Sydney again at the end of February/ early March 2024 and maybe I’ll be lucky enough to take part in another session at the Gaelic Club! If Dr Dow finds this note I hope we can meet up again.

Mist On The Glen

From the playing of the Battlefield Band on “Beg & Borrow”. Track #7.2. Transposed to D.

“”"
Alasdair learned ‘Mist on the Glen’ from the late Buddy
MacMaster from Cape Breton. This title was also used on
a recording of Scotty Fitzgerald, another great Cape Breton
fi ddler. Buddy was adamant that this was a Scottish tune
(as this name suggests) but it seems more widely known
in Ireland as ‘The Devil’s in Dublin’.
“”"

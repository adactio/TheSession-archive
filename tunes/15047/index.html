New Yorker, The

A tune written by Nuala Kennedy that appears on her CD ‘Tune In’. It appears on the ‘Five Mile Town’ track as ‘The New Yorker’ and was commissioned for the 2007 Celtic Connections Festival.
The chords are mine. A chord that is preceded by a ‘ / ’ indicates the bass note only, and a chord that is surrounded by a ‘ ( ) ’ suggests a variation.

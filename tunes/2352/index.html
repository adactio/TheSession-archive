Wrong Key?

Sean McGuire recorded this in 1968 on his VIVA album-`Sean McGuire plays`.The album notes state that `the blue idol` is an ancient Quaker meeting house in Sussex.I think Seamus and Manus Maguire have recorded it much more recently and Coleman may be the original source.It sounds better played in D.

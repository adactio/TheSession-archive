The Dandy

From volume three (jig #57) of the Paddy O’Brien Tune Collection.
Paddy call this one Jerry Holland’s #1. I found it in Jerry Holland’s Collection
of Fiddle Tunes at #236 called The Dandy.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on December 30th, 2025.

The Dandy, X:2

Here’s how it appears in Paul Cranford’s book Jerry Holland’s Collection of Fiddle Tunes.
Note that Cranford leaves off the final eighth note of each part so that you can have the
pickup of your choice to the repeat or the next part. But, that messes up the audio playback,
so I added pickup notes.

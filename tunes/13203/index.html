Four comments

“Joe Burke’s Polka” C: Jackie Daly

This could easily be called “A Polka for Joe Burke”, as the tale goes that Jackie composed this for Joe as a bit of a chuckle, Joe supposedly having a strong dislike for polkas. I’ve yet to confirm whether any of this is true or not, and I haven’t recently had the opportunity to ask Jackie or Joe. If any of you do find yourselves in their company, please ask. ;-)

This also goes nicely in G, D and C…

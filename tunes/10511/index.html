The Kitchen Criminal

As performed by box player Norman Mackay’s ‘Ceilidh Experience’ band (www.ceilidhexperience.com, where you can hear a recording of this) and transcribed from the sheet music on his website (www.normanmackay.com). Great tune!

↳ Posted by picklest on May 31st, 2010.

From his website: “I co wrote this tune with Ruairidh Macmillan during a sound check in 2006. It was named after our drummer ‘Richard Corral’ who, the previous week, had appeared on the BBC TV Show ‘Kitchen Criminals’ A reality television series featuring Britain’s worst cooks!”

MacKinnon’s Other Rant

This is a very old reel played in Cape Breton. Buddy MacMaster always plays this tune after MacKinnon’s Rant. This version here is that of Buddy MacMaster (the melody) and Donald Angus Beaton (ornamentation). Donald Angus Beaton played it slightly differently: his version is not pentatonic.

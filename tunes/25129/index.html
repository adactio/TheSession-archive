17th Of July

Composed by Alan Griffin (1952 Ennis - 2023 Astigarraga).
Alan was a wonderful flute player and Alboka player
Irish times : “Alan Griffin (…) made exceptionally important contributions to the traditional music of his adopted country, and opened up very fruitful channels of communication between Irish and Basque culture”.
It was always a pleasure to meet Alan and share tunes.

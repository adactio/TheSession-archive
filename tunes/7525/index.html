Highland Cathedral, X:4

I wrote it out with all the note values twice as long because otherwise when you click “PLAY AUDIO” it’s going twice as fast as it should be. The last four lines are the “bridge” linked to above (which may be a separate tune, or merely part of the arrangement).

↳ Posted by DonaldK on February 5th, 2025.

Re: Highland Cathedral

I edited my arrangement X:4 to correspond with the widely-played brass arrangement by Major Ian Peaple.

Highland pipers often play it with triplets thrown in the 2nd part but Peaple keeps the timing consistent, a dotted halfnote and two eighths.

Peaple was the Bandmaster of the Black Watch from 1992 to 1994, who were stationed in Hong Kong until the 1997 handover. As Bandmaster he oversaw both the Military Band (brass and woodwinds) and the Pipes & Drums, thus being in an ideal position to arrange and perform Highland Cathedral with full resources.

His arrangement is still available on band music sites.

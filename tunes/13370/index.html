The Dead Piper

Another one from Finbarr Dwyer’s ‘Star of Ireland’ album. I don’t know if he wrote it - I can’t find a reference to it anywhere else.

I’ve transcribed it in full, as there are so many little variations throughout.

↳ Posted by coppers and brass on February 9th, 2014.

The second setting is a transcription of how he plays it the second time through.

↳ Posted by coppers and brass on February 9th, 2014.

This tune was composed by Michael Dwyer. It appears on page 126 of Josephine Keegan’s 2004 tune collection “A Drop in the Ocean”.

↳ Posted by rwwt on March 4th, 2015.

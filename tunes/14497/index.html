John McAlpine

From the Athole Collection, 1884; taught at the Boston Harbor Scottish Fiddle School in 2006 by Dougie Lawrence, chords by Troy MacGillivray.

↳ Posted by Alan Wilson on June 22nd, 2015.

John McAlpine, X:2

In my copy of the Athole Collection, the tune looks like this & there is no ‘e’ at the end of the name.
The song ‘The Rattlin’ Bog’ is sung to this tune & in Ireland the air is used as a march, a song air and a Sliabh Luachra polka.

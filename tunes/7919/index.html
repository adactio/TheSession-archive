James Bryan’s reel (1)

From “Mary Custy and Eoin O’Neill: The ways of the world” album (track 11), you can find 2 “James Bryan’s reels”, this is the 1st one.
The playing mood sounds like country bluegrass fiddling, so I ignored all the double notes, all the variations and tried to transcribe the more essential sheetmusic score.

↳ Posted by OsvaldoLaviosa on October 30th, 2007.

This tune is on James Bryan’s 1985 album “The First of May.” It is his unique arrangement of the old-time/Bluegrass tune “Grey Eagle.”

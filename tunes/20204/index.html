Three comments

Lady St. John

This tune is possibly by Carolan.

This setting has the beginning of the second strain is taken up an octave, suitable for the uilleann pipes, flute, and whistle while retaining the original key of G.

↳ Posted by Richard D Cook on December 30th, 2020.

Lady St. John, X:2

This setting is the proper tune found in the 1726 Neale collection, suitable for fiddle, box, banjo, mandolin, etc.

↳ Posted by Richard D Cook on December 30th, 2020.

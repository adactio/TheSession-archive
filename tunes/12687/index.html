Two comments

Greeting To Ireland

I came across this tune in Ryan’s Mammoth Collection while I was practising sight-reading. I chose it initially because it looked easy, but I soon got to like it for its own sake. It doesn’t seem to be well known at all and I can find no record(!) of it anywhere else. I’m not too pleased with the name, so if it’s to be my discovery I’d like to rename it ‘The Síogs of Knockma’ . I like to follow it with The Red-Haired Lass, but feel free..

↳ Posted by Christmas Eve on April 14th, 2013.

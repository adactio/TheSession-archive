Tootsie’s Flowers

Lovely wee tune from Kennedy’s Kitchen’s 2019 album “Whiskey of Truth”. Found the track online so I don’t have any other info about it, unfortunately.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SW9aZ4utxlQ

The play it in Bb with an F whistle, so I’ve transposed it to G.

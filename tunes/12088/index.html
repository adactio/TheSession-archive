Codd’s Grove

Composed by Paddy Hayes. This is a rough transcription of the 1st tune in this video played by Darren Breslin http://comhaltas.ie/music/detail/comhaltaslive_286_3_darren_breslin/

The variation for bar 8 of the B-part where it goes up high is |1 cdeg ~f2Ac:|

It’s also the 1st tune in his other video on YouTube http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q1E8iTa0ePs where he plays bar 3 of the B-part a bit differently as |DFFD (3FGA DF|.

For some instruments, this will be easier in G:

X: 1
T: Codd’s Grove
C: Paddy Hayes
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Gmaj
GEDG EDB,E|DB,GB, DEGA|BG~G2 eGdG|FGAB cDFA|
GEDG EDB,E|DB,GB, DEGA|BG (3B^cd gdBG|1 FGAF ~G3F:|2 FGAF ~G2Bd||
|:gdBG DGBG|EGBG DGBG|EG~G2 EGDB,|DGBG ABcd|
gbdg bgdB|GBEG DGBG|EGcG eGcG|1 DGGA ~G2Bd:|2 DGGA ~G3F||

…with those variations in bar 8 of the B-part becoming |1 defa ~g2Bd:|
and the one in Bar 3 B-part |EGGE (3GAB EG|

Six comments

A 6/8 march ?

It’s not a waltz of course. It’s a march, though a modern one written by Steve Cooney, and recorded on Sean Smyth’s ‘Blue Fiddle’.

The feel of it is like a Scottish 6/8 pipe march. But with an Irish feel to the melody. Bit of a hybrid, but a great stirring tune.

Ten comments

New Brian Finnegan tune

I learned this tune off a recording of a recent Flook concert. Brian said that he had set out to write a waltz but it turned into 7/8 before he was done. It’s a lovely tune.

↳ Posted by jdicarlo on November 29th, 2007.

Watch him play it

Here’s a clip of Brian playing this tune for Marga herself. It’s a bit more complicated than the version I learned, but he’s had more than a year to mess with it since the recording I used to transcribe the tune.

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FD9pH6YZ6_0&feature=related

Recorded Version

Transcription based on Brian’s *Ravishing Genius of Bones* album. It’s also played a step up in Emaj on David Kosky and Damien O’Kane’s *The Mystery Inch*, and in Dmaj on the first Guidewires album.

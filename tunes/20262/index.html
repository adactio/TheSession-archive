Roshambo

This is not a jig, it’s a 7/8. Written by Glaswegian multi-instrumentalist David Foley, member of RURA.
This is transcribed from his and Jack Smedley’s album “Time to Fly”. Track #3.1. I’ve skipped all the variations they add second and third times they play the tune.

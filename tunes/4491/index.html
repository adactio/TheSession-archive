Re: The Grey Old Lady Of Raasay

Two points: 1) I don’t hear any relation at all between Mrs MacLeod of Raasay and The Grey Old Lady of Raasay, other than female presence in Raasay and an A tonic; and, 2) ‘Gallic’ refers to all things French, as opposed to Gaelic (the language, whether Irish or Scottish). Oh, one other: I read somewhere recently that the ‘Grey Old Lady’ in question refers to a particular rock in the sea off Raasay, of significance to mariners. FWIW.

↳ Posted by meself on September 8th, 2024.

Re: The Grey Old Lady Of Raasay

These points have already been established. Mrs McLeod of Raasay, or Miss MacLeod as it’s misnamed in Ireland, is attributed to Alexander MacDonald of Portree while The Grey Old Lady of Raasay is attributed to Iain “Dall” MacKay. And as I mentioned earlier, despite the later word describing a person, the Grey Old Lady is a rock off Raasay.

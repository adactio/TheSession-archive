This tune was collected by Jack Wade from flute player Arthur Kelly from Ring Commons in North County Dublin. Kellys brother Joe played fiddle too. Theres a bunch of these North Dublin tunes in CRE 4. Look under the contributions of Jack Wade. Some great old tunes.

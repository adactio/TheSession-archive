Master Rowesome’s

A Barry Kerr composition. He writes such wonderful, beautifully balanced tunes.

This setting is a blend of the version on pipes on the album ‘Cairn’, and a live flute version he played on Liam O’Maonlai’s Cuan an Cheoil program.

It’s A Bar

This was composed by Mike Vass, and I heard it played this way (somewhat altered from his original to make it playable as a session tune) at a session in Edinburgh. The A section is in F# minor and the B section is in A major, which will either greatly please or greatly annoy anyone who’s accompanying.

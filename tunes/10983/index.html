One comment

Goscinny Morris (!)

Very well possible that this one is here already, but I couldn’t find it. Neither do I know anything of the origins, so if any of you do, let it be known.
In another life I played this tune with the guitarist of the present Double Dutch Band, in a set after Kemp’s Jig (https://thesession.org/tunes/7093), and my guess is that he somehow picked it up somewhere. Because we figured it to be a kind of morris-like tune, and as lovers of comic books, we nicknamed the tune Goscinny Morris, after the authors of Lucky Luke.

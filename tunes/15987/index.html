Five comments

Weaving Dreams

I just learned this Charlie Lennon tune from the Frankie Gavin recording that comes after Pedaling Patterns, aka All About Weaving set. Couldn’t find the ABCs anywhere so this is how I heard it. Feel free to give feedback. There were some variations within the tune, of course, but I think I captured the bones of it.

↳ Posted by Shelley on March 21st, 2017.

Re: Weaving Dreams

Lovely tune. Bear in mind, however, that it is under copyright - and C. L. has, I think, published it in a book. So it would have been proper to seek his permission to post it here.

On another note, your abc needs some tweaking - all of the bars containing a triplet are one 1/8-beat short.

↳ Posted by CreadurMawnOrganig on March 22nd, 2017.

Re: Weaving Dreams

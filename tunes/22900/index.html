Six comments

Tom Billy Murphy’s

This version is taken from Johnny O’Leary’s book (no. 182).
Paul de Grae has a similar setting prepared for World Fiddle Day Scartaglin this year (20 May 2023). He calls it Sheehan’s.
https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=10160392944281609&set=g.283125488560326

Padraig O’Keeffe gave a different setting to some of his pupils, including Mrs Katie Horan in the 1940s.
https://www.itma.ie/digital-library/score/padraig_okeeffe_horan_ms_038
The crucial difference is that the tune finishes on a D rather than an E.

Tom Billy Murphy’s, X:2

This setting is based on Pat Slattery’s transcription from the recording he made in Knocknagree in the mid 1970s.
https://soundcloud.com/search?q=knocknagree 4:33 onwards

JOL in this recording plays the first half of the part in a similar manner to his book and then the second half with the POK ending, as noted in X:2 Just to keep you on your toes, he varies the first part (which can be split into two halves) by then playing the second half twice, then the first half twice on the third time through. Confused? Listen to the recording! At other times he reversed the halves.

Stella’s Waltz

This tune was written by Cal Scott and recorded on the CD “Suite” which one can find here:
http://www.kevinburke.com/suite-kevin-burke-and-cal-scott-2/.

According to the liner notes it was written following a dream Cal had about his deceased Grandmother in which she was teaching him how to waltz.

Two comments

Big Archie

Another fine tune from Duncan Chisholm played (AB)2 CAB.
Use first B ending when going back to A part & second ending when going to C part as well as the final B part.
This is on his album ‘Affric’. C part was transcribed from live performance YouTube video.

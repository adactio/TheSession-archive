One comment

Indestructible Record: 1285

Issued exactly a century ago, that is, 1910, Kimmel’s wax cylinder recording may be heard here: http://cylinders.library.ucsb.edu/search.php/search.php?queryType=@attr%201=1016&query=columbia&num=1&start=88&sortBy=&sortOrder=ia

He actually played this in D, with the intro in A; I wanted to write it out in G to play it on pipes or flute. The intrepid may wish to transcribe his very florid triplet outro. I had enough trouble just writing this out - tell me if I’ve made any mistakes, which is very likely.

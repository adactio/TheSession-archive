An old jig

From the book “The Professor, James Morrison.” Ordering details are available here: https://thesession.org/discussions/17265 Very interesting tome, all manuscripts The Fess wrote out for his student Joey Flynn, and published under the aegis of another student, Veronica McNamara.

When Morrison first came to America he boarded with Mayo piper Michael Carney, and Carney’s student Tom Busby says James was very keen to pick up Carney’s store of rare tunes; that “if he’d be shaving and Mike played a tune he didn’t know he’d drop the razor and pick up the fiddle in a dash.” Which tunes came from Carney we don’t know. Tom passed on a few rarities of his own by and by. He also remarked that much of Mike’s music simply went to the grave with him.

I also added Morrison’s setting of the tune called “Morrison’s Favourite” in the comments, using the title he provided, “The Pilgrimage.” https://thesession.org/tunes/4164#comment586514

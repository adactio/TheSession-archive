Two comments

Ryan’s Fancy Arriving

From the playing of Graham Wells.

Composed by Émile Benoit.

The A Part of the tune shares a few phrases with The Woodchoppers:
https://thesession.org/tunes/946

The few different recordings of this tune have the A and B Parts ending a bit differently, otherwise, each setting are quite similar.

Ryan’s Fancy was a popular band in Newfoundland in the late 1960’s to early 1980’s, consisting of three Irish immigrants, Fergus O‘Byrne, Dermot O’Reilly, and, Denis Ryan.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on June 4th, 2020.

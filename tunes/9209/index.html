Dance from Oas

Was taught this tune a while back by Robin Shepherd, an excellent Yorkshire fiddle player.

I think Oas is in Romania, though I don’t know where abouts in Romania it actually is!

A really great effect can be produced if you are playing this with a group as some members can play a drone that goes well with the melody. It is this:

||: [ E2A,2 ] [ EA, ] [ E-A,- ] | [ EA, ] [ EA, ] [ E2A,2 ] :||

When you play both parts together, it sound magnificent

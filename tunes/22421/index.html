The German Jig

Great catchy jig composed by Daniel Ryan a very talented accordion and tin whistle player from Youghal, County Cork who spent some time living in Germany. He named it the German Jig because of its clockwork regularity.

Re: The German

I can see a couple of places that differ subtly from the source recording, but are more like interpertational differences than ‘errors’ (deliberate or otherwise). What does strike me as a fundamental error in the transcription, however, is that the A part, except for its 2nd-time ending, should be written an octave lower. The tune definitely jumps into a higher octave at the end of the A-part and stays there for the whole B-part (but notating the B-part an octave higher would place it outside the normal range of Irish trad tunes – hence notating the A part an octave lower).

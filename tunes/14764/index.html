Not sure what the original is in this case. I’ve seen it listed in GHB notation in A in Irish pipe band collections. It sounds similar in some respects to the melody as played/sung by Julie Fowlis with the words in Scottish Gaelic, and I think her version is up a whole step in B major/mix. Whether the tune was adapted to suit the words, I have yet to ascertain. Anyways, Julie has done a beautiful job with it.

Donald MacDonald of North Uist wrote it at the Somme for his sweatheart back home. The title translates as “The White Swan”

An Eala Bhàn, X:4

Scored from the singing of Julie Fowlis. The key may be a mixylodian so please help determine that. I deliberately transposed to drop this beautiful piece into a range more friendly to the whistle. Ms. Fowlis sings it higher, more of a nice G range. But being a whistler, I didn’t want to be calling the neighborhood dogs with the whistle’s 3rd octave, ear-splitting C.

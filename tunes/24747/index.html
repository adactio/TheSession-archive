I gCuimhne Aido

This slow piece was composed by Wexford musician Caroline O’Sullivan. Caroline won an All-Ireland Fleadh title a number of years ago playing piano in the accompaniment category. She composed this in memory of her friend and bandmate, Aidan Smyth, who passed away in May 2017.

Fair Play To You Mary

An early composition of Fergal O‘ Hanlon , banjo player and guitarist from Glynn in Co. Wexford. Fergal has composed other tunes and songs and uploaded some very impressive and successful Youtube videos. He is currently a member of the group ’Green Road’. Uploaded here as part of the Wexford Tune Project.

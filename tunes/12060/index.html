Fèis Gleann Albainn

I’ve taken the liberty of changing the ABC slightly (e.g. you needed a double barline after the 2nd ending in the A part), and I’ve made a couple of suggestions for the B part (in the 4th bar I feel you really need a chord change, so I’ve implied a change to an E chord). Hope you don’t mind - it’s because I thought it was worth it.

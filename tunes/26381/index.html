Dannsa Na Nèapaig

A traditional Scottish pipe tune, despite the name. It translates as The Naples Dance or Neopolitan Dance. This version is based on Alan MacDonald of Glenuig’s playing

↳ Posted by niall_kenny on February 26th, 2026.

Dannsa Na Nèapaig, X:2

This is Alasdair White’s version. Alasdair is an amazing player from the Isle of Lewis on the Outer Hebrides.

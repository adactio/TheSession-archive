One comment

The Crow Lawlors

This reel was composed by Dicky Deegan. It’s included in the recently published ‘Wexford Tunebook’. Dicky’s mother was from Adamstown in County Wexford and the title reflects a local nickname, with the secondary title of ‘The Humours of Brocurra’ referring to a townland near Adamstown.

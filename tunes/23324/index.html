Lord Mayo

This is the slow air version of Tiarna Mhuigheo, as played by The Chieftains on their third album, except that they don’t play the Fnat. It’s totally unrelated to the march.
I confess to ripping the abcs from https://tunearch.org/wiki/Lord_Mayo_(2)

Lord Mayo, X:2

This is from the playing of James Early and John McFadden on a wax cylinder, recorded in Chicago ca. 1903. It may be heard here, along with the transcription of Breandan Breathnach’s that I have adapted to ABC: https://musicofmayo.ie/lord-mayo-slow-air/

The provenance of their tune is detailed as well, how they learned it from visiting Donegal piper Turloch MacSweeny when he was in the States for the 1893 Chicago Columbian Exhibition. It, or tunes of that name, were played by Donegal fiddlers such as Néillidh Boyle.

Breandan’s transcription includes occasional “//” symbols above the staff. This a caesura, meant to indicate a break in the music’s flow. “!caesura!” is supposed to render this, but this doesn’t work in either EasyABC or here. Nevertheless I’ve included these marks. Also he shows check marks above the staff; apparently this is meant to show where to take a breath, so I’ve included those as well. They’ve come out as commas. Their placement didn’t come out exactly as in the original, note.

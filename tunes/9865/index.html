My Lily

from Jerry Holland’s Master Cape Breton Fiddler.

↳ Posted by Jürgen on September 4th, 2009.

My Lily, X:2

This is the air of the Gaelic song ‘Mo shùil a’d dhèidh’ (my eye is after you). The words were composed by the Reverend Donald MacNicol (1735-1802). He had proposed to the young Lillias Campbell, but she had already agreed to marry her cousin Captain Alexander Campbell. However, the captain made a bet with a servant which angered Lillias and she then accepted the proposal of the Reverend MacNicol. They married after her eighteenth birthday. The chorus ends ‘Mo Lili mo Lili ’s mo shùil ad dhèidh, Cha lèir dhomh am bealach aig cumha nan deur’ (My Lily, my Lily, my eye is after you, I cannot see the way with the outpouring of tears). It was later set to music.
X:2 is the melody of the song. The higher second part in X:1 is an instrumental variation played by, amongst others, Aly Bain on the CD ‘Another Gem’ with Phil Cunningham.

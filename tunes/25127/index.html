Give Jesu The Slip

Happy Holidays all! I was listening to Amy Grant’s Christmas album HOME FOR CHRISTMAS over the holidays and the last cut is an instrumental of Jesus, Joy of Man’s Desiring. Towards the end it morphs into a slip jig of which there is no title given. It might simply be a creation loosely derived from JJOMDesiring. It had some extra beats at certain spots where orchestration did some nice things, but I wanted to hear it done in a more straightforward traditional pattern, and this is what I came up with. The A part is pretty much exactly what they were playing, but the B and C parts have been simplified and trimmed down to something more traditional sounding. I thought it was very catchy.

If this is an already existing tune, please let me know. Also, feel free to correct me in my notation. I don’t know theory or ABC notation very well. I transcribe using TablEdit to tablature, and TablEdit converts it to a form of ABC notation that I then have to play around with to get it acceptable looking. I deduced the key by the number of sharps…so I’m not sure that is accurate either. Anyway, enjoy the tune and Merry Christmas and Happy New Year to all!

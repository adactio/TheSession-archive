The Dairy Maid

Here is a third tune known as “Dairy Maid”, which appears in Breandan Breathnach, Ceol Rince na hÉireann, vol. 4 (1996), p. 27, no. 50, as An Cailín Déirí. Breathnach says he got the tune from fiddler and uilleann piper Stephen Grier from Farnaght, Co. Leitrim.

Jimmy Mann’s

Composed by Shetland fiddler Tom Anderson in honour of another Shetland fiddler, Jimmy Mann of Skeld. Based on the recording of it by Aly Bain and Willie Hunter, but without some of the syncopation and snapping of that setting.

https://youtu.be/gRQBP-87fF0

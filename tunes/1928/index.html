Four comments

Mooncoin Reel

The “Mooncoin” on A Tribute to Michael Coleman is the jig by that name, not this tune.

↳ Posted by Don Meade on October 2nd, 2003.

Ditto for the Tommy Peoples, Mary Bergin and probably other citations!

↳ Posted by Don Meade on October 2nd, 2003.

The Mooncoin, X:2

This setting is closer to the one in O’Neill’s. In this case there are no C naturals.

Kyle Borley plays it very well here:

https://youtu.be/7LL35jFFJLY

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on May 28th, 2021.

The Mooncoin, X:3

A version with C naturals as played around Wexford. Similar to Sean McGuire and Willie Joe Power’s setting on The Four Star Quartet LP. Willie Joe Power was a Wexford man which probably accounts for the tune being part of the local repertoire.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on January 24th, 2025.

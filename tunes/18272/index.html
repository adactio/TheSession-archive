Three comments

The Wolfshead

From the playing of Lalla Rookh. It’s a composition of their mandolin player, Paul Honeycutt. Leads well into The Road To Lisdoonvarna, as Lalla Rookh have it.

Simple, dark tune, with plenty of places for variations and ornamentations. Has a similar feel as Cleveland Park and April Storm.

The 1st, 3rd, and 5th bars of the A Part work as “E2B B2B”, “E2B BBE”, and “E2B ~B3”.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on August 19th, 2019.

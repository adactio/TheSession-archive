James D. Law

This is my transcription of James D. Law as played by Natalie MacMaster. She plays it at the end of the “E Flat Set” on her “My Roots Are Showing” album. I’m not 100% sure of myself when it comes to learning by ear, but considering I haven’t been able to find the sheet music anywhere, I think it is closer than anything else. :)

A very difficult tune but very worth it.

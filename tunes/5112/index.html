The Humours of Ballycastle

Living just outside Ballycastle, Co. Antrim, it seemed appropriate that I post this tune. However, I fear that the tune’s title is actually referring to another Ballycastle, which can be found a little further south.

↳ Posted by Ptarmigan on October 22nd, 2005.

The Humours Of Ballycastle, X:2

This jig appears in O’Neill’s Dance Music of Ireland - 1001 Gems (1907), p. 29, no. 81, as The Humors of Ballycastle, Sugra Baile-Caislean.

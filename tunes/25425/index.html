Joan Looney’s Polka

Caoimhín Ó Raghallaigh penned this unorthodox polka in honour of the energetic Clare-based dancer from Kerry who often did her signature Brush Dance!

The basic transcription hardly does the tune justice as Caoimhín adds a few tricks to the mix when playing it, double-stop voicings and all – so if you find yourself in his fiddle class (which I highly recommend for learning inspiration, thought-provoking conversations and overall fun), be sure to ask him to play Joan Looney’s (and say hello from Tony, lol) :-D Rest assured you’ll be asking to learn this tune in an instant!

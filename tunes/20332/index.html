Pass The Baby

Oftentimes coming up with a tune name is more difficult that the actual compositional effort. Sometimes the name drops into your lap. In this case, the name of this tune came from a couple who were at our session with their months old baby and there was a flurry of passing the baby between the parents while juggling instruments. The baby didn’t seem to mind the swap.

West Of Rockall

Written by Phil MacLennan Smillie of The Tannahill Weavers.
In the A part the [Ad] chord means play “d” the first time and “A” in the repeat.

There’s a variation in the repeat of the C part that I haven’t transcribed, just a couple of bars.

The tune is in F#m (not available here) and goes below the range of a D whistle (plus all the G#s), so it’s best to play it on an Awhistle with Bm fingering.

Second tune here: https://philsmilliemusic.com/track/2912057/04-west-of-rockall

Re: The Briar Bush

This tune was playing in my head for weeks after a session. I couldn’t find it anywhere and no one I asked had heard it before. It must be a child of Star of Munster and Julia Delaney played back to back at the end of the session. I wrote the B part as a complement.

↳ Posted by Shawn Cassady on September 30th, 2024.

The Briar Bush, X:3

The D dorian setting to match Julia Delaney’s.

↳ Posted by Shawn Cassady on October 6th, 2024.

Re: The Briar Bush Man

Inspired by a true story. Police were called. A man completely naked was shouting, stuck inside a briar bush. Several times he unsuccessfully attempted to escape but the briars won each battle. With assistance he was extricated and transported to hospital with superficial wounds on every part. He hadn’t a clue how he came to be in the bush, nor the whereabouts of his clothing!

↳ Posted by Shawn Cassady on August 22nd, 2025.

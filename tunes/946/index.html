The Woodchopper’s

This is the tune as we play it. With triplets. Canadian titles for the tune include “La soupe aux pois” (Pea Soup), the title in Montréal, “reel des bucherons” (Lumberjack’s Reel) and “reel de copeaux de bois”. Composition of the tune has been credited to the popular 20th century New Brunswick fiddler, Ned Landry. The melody has become popular in the American Midwest, probably picked up from Canadian broadcasts. The tune is sometimes attributed to Maritime fiddler Don Messer, however this seems to have been an attempt by the record company to establish a copyright claim. However, neither Don Messer nor Ned Landry can lay a true claim to this tune as it was derived from a Quebecoise tune by the name of Reel des bucherons.
Reel de la Soupe aux Pois (Pea Soup Reel) Bluebird 55-5417 (78 RPM), Isidore Soucy. RCA Victor Gala CGP-128, Isidore Soucy et son Ensemble - “Chez Isidore” (1962). However an earlier recording from Isidore Soucy from 1951 has him playing the tune without his ensemble.
Originally from Quebec fiddler J.O. LaMadeleine as Quadrille des bûcherons: 2ème figure. Starr 16292 (78 RPM), (1928).

One comment

Planxty Charles Bunworth

This is a Liz Carroll tune form the Irish Crossroads of Art & Design album (collaborative recording). I fell in love with this tune on the first listen. Liz’s playing is gorgeous and I suggest that it’s unlikely you’ll ever play the tune well if you don’t listen to her interpretation. There is just so much subtlety.

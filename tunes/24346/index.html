One comment

The Back Stitch

Sean Moloney once told me that his great grandfather James (Stephen Moloney’s father) learned the flute while living in Yorkshire, before emigrating to America and returning to Ballinakill. I was able to trace the family history in Halifax and this reel was composed the next day. The title was inspired by the Moloneys’ connection with tailoring.

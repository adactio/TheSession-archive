The Orcadian Jig

My understanding is that this tune was collected in the Shetland Islands …

… interesting, in that “Orcadian” means: from, (or relating to), the Orkney Islands.

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on October 10th, 2011.

Not sure if it is a Shetland tune (seems to be another EFDSS collected piece), but the most common use of the phrase “The Orcadian” is to indicate a native of Orkney. It would follow that it might be difficult to pinpoint the subject of a tune called “The Orcadian” if it was composed in Orkney, but it may be a little easier (and the subject more likely to be given that title) if it was composed in a place where there were fewer Orcadians!

Re: Ross’s

Fiddlers who just don’t like to play in F major are missing out here. The B part, which is at the very least a challenge in G major, just rolls off the fingerboard in F.

You’ll hear this tune sometime during the evening at every other contradance, often with a G major second reel for that magnificent whole-step lift.

The number 4 is a bit mysterious. There certainly was another reel, not particularly memorable, that went under the name of “Ross’s Reel #1”, but I don’t know who Ross was or if the other tune was genuine. Numbers 2 and 3 seem to have vanished without trace from the New England repertoire.

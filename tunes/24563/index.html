John Of Carrick

JOHN OF CARRICK The first of a group of tunes written for or about John Armstrong of Carrick, another noted piper, with whom Billy played in the Border Minstrels. High Carrick is a farm on the hills above Elsdon, and now in the military training area. There is no recording of this reel by Billy, but he wrote it out for Colin Ross late in his life. It appears on the High Level Ranters LP ‘Border Spirit’, itself made as 11 tribute to Billy’s music.

Seven comments

The Forge Audition

This tune’s title alludes to the early musical career of the Wexford champion box player George Ross. George won the senior box at the 1956 Fleadh in Ennis, made his commercial HMV 78’s soon thereafter and like Paddy O‘Brien rapidly became a household name. George appeared on live US television- the Johnny Carson show- and the title of my composition refers to the audition he did for that TV appearance. The American producers arrived in Wexford ’out of the blue’ to audition him for the show - not realising that he was at work. George was a blacksmith in the local foundry that produced farm machinery. With the help of a local newspaper reporter, George’s accordion was collected from his house: the Americans were brought in to the sweltering forge, where he sat and played his accordion for them to gain his trip to the States and his TV appearance.

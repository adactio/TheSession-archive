Duplicate, kind of…

This is actually a 7/8 tune based on a jig that’s already in the database, posted by me a few years back. The jig was popularised by Arty McGlynn and Nollaig Casey on their album “Lead The Knave”. Funky 7/8 tune, but really, the jig is plenty good enough without faffing about with it imo. The jig is here: https://thesession.org/tunes/2654.

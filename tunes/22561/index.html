Davie Taylor

A strathspey by Scott Skinner that I really like and have been playing for a number of years, picked up (and transcribed) from Troy MacGillivray’s setting on “Live at The Music Room”.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on December 14th, 2022.

Davie Taylor, X:2

The first time Troy MacGillivray plays it through on Live At the Music Room he has a nice way of playing bars 5&6 of the B part so I have notated that here. I also added the intentional sounding 8th with 2 16ths rhythms vs triplets in the B part and made a few corrections on pick up notes.

This is so familiar in parts that it wouldn’t suprise me if it had bubbled up out of various things in the soup of melodies in my mind, heart and soul. But isn’t that where it generally rises from anyway. This came about early this morning as I was caught up in worries and frustrations, and it allowed me a welcome break to do a bit of dancing around as I played it, a break in the hassles currently pressing in and demanding attention… Music and dance are good medicine…

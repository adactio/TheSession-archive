Streams Of Poulaphouca, X:3

A piper’s version of the reel. The tune was composed by James Cash, the famous Wexford piper who travelled the country as a musician and horse dealer in the late 19th century. I’ve included it in the Wexford tune project since Cash was born in Wexford and has many descendants in the county.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 7th, 2022.

Re: Streams Of Poulaphouca

“The Poulaphouca is a current in County Wicklow upon which there are falls which drew visitors ‘from near and far’ and proved a popular resort. Another piper, Patrick Toomey (who played a double chanter) held station at the falls busking for the visitors on Sundays and holidays.”
https://tunearch.org/wiki/Annotation:Streams_of_Poulaphouca_(The)

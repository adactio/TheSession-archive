The Jura Music Festival

A fun reel transcribed from the playing of Scots fiddler Archie McAllister, and one to exercise your fingers and your brain. I’m afraid I don’t know who composed it - I’ve only got it on an old cassette with some of the tunes from the album The Gathering Storms by Archie McAllister and Ross Kennedy.

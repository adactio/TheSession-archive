Delvinside - Highland

Oirialla got the tune from Luke Donnellan’s Collection Dance Music of Oriel (1909). In the liner notes to the set they call the tune types “Highland and Reels”, so I guess, this tune is the highland. It’s interesting to see, how a tune changes over time and in different areas.

↳ Posted by DetlefS on September 14th, 2014.

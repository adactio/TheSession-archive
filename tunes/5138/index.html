McNamara’s

This lovely tune comes from Angelina Carberry and Martin Quinn’s excellent banjo and box duet playing. I’m currently struggling with it as a mediocre flute player, but will never give up because it’s one of the nicest barndaces I’ve ever heard. Does it have another name?

McNamara’s Band

Browsing through Jerry Holland’s tune books, I found this tune is listed as “McNamara’s Band” in his second collection. I remember the notes say he learned it from the playing of Maire O’Keeffe.

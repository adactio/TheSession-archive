Eleanor Neary’s

The first jig played by Liz Carroll in this video. Also recorded on the 1995 album Trian II, of which Liz Carroll was a member. Can’t find much concrete info about the tune, but it’s possibly one of Liz’s compositions. The group CrossHarbour do a version of it and call it the “Chigago jig”.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4SwyZEifvY

↳ Posted by Harry Emma on March 10th, 2016.

Re: Eleanor Neary’s

From the insert accompanying “Trian II” :
“Eleanor Neary [ piano ] and her husband Jimmy [ fiddle ], spent a lot of time both encouraging and dazzling the younger musicians growing up in Chicago - ”Eleanor Neary’s“ is just one of the unique tunes that Liz learned from Eleanor”.

Re: Eleanor Neary’s

Eleanor was my great aunt, her husband Jimmy my great Uncle. He learned to play the fiddle from his brother, my grandfather Michael Neary. Eleanor and Jimmy were featured in a recent book authored by Richie Piggott called Cry of a People Gone that chronicles Irish musicians in Chicago from 1920-2020.

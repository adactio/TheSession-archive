Granton Fish Bowl, X:2

X:2 is the piece as I had it from a local session a few years ago, added here to illustrate what it looks like in 4/4. I’ve no idea whether this is a better way of writing it out than the 6/8 setting, but it does serve to show that a time sig doesn’t necessarily tell you everything about the type of tune you’re dealing with.

(Aaaargh! What is it with .abc? If I put the note value as 1/4, it plays it at the right speed but converts all notes to double length, so I’ve left at at 1/8 where it plays too fast. Still, Jeremy has given us a slow-downer . . . .)

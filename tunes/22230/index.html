I’ll Turn Off Your Water!

This is a reel I composed a while ago but hadn’t got round to notating it or indeed naming it. I’m uploading it as part of the Wexford Tune Project. The title chosen is as a little humorous tribute to the prolific local tune composer, Tom Byrne. Tom worked until his retirement with the local water services, and would often jokingly refer (with big cheeky grin) that he might secretly cut off your water supply if you played an odd bum note….

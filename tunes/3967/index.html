This is a composition of fiddler John McEvoy and comes from The Kilmore Fancy, the recent recording he made with his sister Catherine McEvoy.

↳ Posted by edl on December 6th, 2004.

The Spotted Dog

It seems named after a pub in Birmingham. You can listen to McEvoys’ duet playing of this tune, which is still available in the archive of radio programme “The Late Session”: http://www.rte.ie/radio1/story/1014033.html (originally broadcasted on 22 August this year) The interview with them starts around 17:00, and you can listen to John talking about this tune.

↳ Posted by slainte on December 7th, 2004.

Spotted Dog pub

This tune certainly does refer to a pub in Digbeth, Birmingham where there have been great sessions for many years on alternate Mondays and monthly on Tuesdays. It’s a lovely pub with a great atmosphere, worthy of having a tune named after it.

The Spotted Dog, X:7

I recently came across this version of The Spotted Dog at Samuel Beckett’s Irish Gastro Pub in Virginia. This is a simplification of what I heard our local balladeer Brandon Kennedy play. He plays it after Dawn Chorus and The Orphan. This setting was different enough from what I see collected here that I thought it was worth posting. There also weren’t any chords to pilfer so I asked Brandon to help with that too. I ended up really enjoying this tune. The tag on the end feels so triumphant!

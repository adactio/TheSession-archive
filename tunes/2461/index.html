https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=lZ2zBVh6zX0

Here in the video, at around the one-minute to 01:05 mark (the second tune of the set), you can hear Andrea Beaton & Finn Moore playing a Scottish strathspey “Glentilt Lodge”. The tune is also called “The Duke of Atholl’s Forest Lodge”, among other names, like “Ca’ the wethers to the hill” (that tune is apparently an air & only sounds vaguely similar). Regardless, I just call it “Glentilt Lodge” & I’m beginning to think it was the original melody/influence behind “Neilly O’Boyle’s Highland”.

It does have a similar structure & sound as Neilly O’Boyle’s. So I just wonder if the strathspey had been played for someone in Donegal at some point in history. Or possibly the tune was heard in Ireland, played by a Scottish musician (or vice versa); to me, the two definitely sound related, if even superficially.

Three comments

O’Donoghue’s Grand March

I’m not sure why, but I was struck by this tune which I heard on the album Ar Aon Bhuille (Matching Beats) by Cavan fiddler Antoin Macgabhann (Tony Smith), accompanied by Cape Breton pianist Hilda Chiasson. I hadn’t come across it before, and I know nothing about the tune, I’m afraid.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on March 3rd, 2019.

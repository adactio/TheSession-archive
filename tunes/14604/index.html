Fifty In The Ashtray

A tune by Seán O‘Driscoll, from the album “So There You Go”. Precedes “CNoc Na gClárach” (aka Padraig O’Keefe’s Favourite), a tune often played in the Johnny O’Leary “Brosna” set.

John McKenna’s Polka

Cool polka, also a Scoiltrad lesson (flute, beginner). To be played with a very relaxed, lazy feel, no ornamentation.

↳ Posted by glauber on August 29th, 2002.

For the metronomically inclined, i’d say 1/4=96

↳ Posted by glauber on August 29th, 2002.

Polka set

The Chieftains play this one (although they don’t name it) in a set of polkas with “The Britches Full Of Stitches” before segueing into some jigs and slides - their famous mix‘n’match style at work.

↳ Posted by Jeremy on August 29th, 2002.

John McKenna’s / Up and Away, X:3

First heard the tune at a Fleadh in the 70s, I think, Patsy Hanley and P J Crotty standing on chairs belting out this polka! Great stuff! This setting is based on the Monks of the Screw Trio’s playing, in D, where a few notes switch octave etc. It may be a more friendly key for some players.

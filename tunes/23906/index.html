So Long West

This jig is the first in a set called “Time & Tide,” which is the opening tune in Coig’s “Ashlar” album. The banjo player in the band uses loads of 16th notes, and the chords are much more embellished in their recording. This is a more simplified version of the tune in which I replaced his usual 1/8 - 1/16 - 1/16 or 1/16 - 1/16 - 1/8 pattern with a quarter,
This really works all 4 strings on a tenor!

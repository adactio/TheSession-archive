Four comments

A tune from Flook’s “Rubai” album, written of course, by Brian Finnegan.
I acknowledge that this tune is definitely not Irish in any sense, but really closer to jazz but…. I like it. Couldn’t see it played in a session, but it’s pretty catchy.

The Empty Pod, X:2

This version is based on Ali Levack’s incredible performance of this tune at the 2020 Young Trad Musician of the Year awards in Scotland. It’s from the first time through - after that he just launches into improvisations of increasing brilliance. The high notes at the end are unbelievable - the equivalent of a G above high D? (He’s playing on an F whistle). Mind-boggling.

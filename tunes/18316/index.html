Eight comments

The Girl Who Was Poorly Clad

The Girl Who Was Poorly Clad, in my opinion one of the most beautiful Irish airs, is very rarely attested, and I do not recall ever hearing a recording or performance of it. The only place I have found it is in Journal of the Folk-Song Society, Volume 2, 1906, collected by F. Kidson, who heard it sung with Irish lyrics. He gives only a free English version.

A brief score and software-generated demo song file of the melody only is available at:

https://musescore.com/user/2488/scores/5697656

I’ve included an arrangement of this tune in my chamber composition Four Irish Songs for Clarinet, Violin, Cello, Piano, and Percussion -- it’s the last of the four songs in the piece. Anyone interested can find the score and audio file (generated with software as a demo) at:

https://musescore.com/user/2488/scores/5547534

or:

https://youtu.be/rwSVRE1Ptlc

or the sound file only at:

https://soundcloud.com/jon-corelis/four-irish-songs-for-clarinet-violin-cello-piano-and-percussion

Re: The Girl Who Was Poorly Clad

Nice tune, Jon. I’ve reproduced it in the key of C major, which obviates the need for any accidentals. Also, on this site a line break makes a system break, so I’ve broken the tune into four lines, which makes it more visually attractive and easier to read. I’ve done away with your bowing markings because they’re no use to me (I play mandolin and whistle). Other than that, it all looks fine.

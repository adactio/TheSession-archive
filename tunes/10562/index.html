Transcription

Transcription based on the recording of Gary Innes and Ewan Robertson’s *Shout* album. Not sure if it’s one of Gary’s tunes or not, but it’s a cracker.

I wasn’t sure if it was a strathspey or not -- it could just be a slow reel with some swing. Hopefully someone with more expertise on the board will shed some light on this ;-)

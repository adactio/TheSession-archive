The Well-Bred Foal

A slightly different transcription from the same source.

X: 1
T: Well-Bred Foal, The
T: Mike Hoban’s
C: Mike Hoban (Co. Mayo accordion player)
D: The Blue Idol, Altan
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
K: Bdor
f2 ef dBBA|Bcde f2 fa|edcB ABcd|edcB ABcd|
e2 ae cA A/A/A|Bcde fefg|aA A/A/A ABce|(3faf ef dBB2:||
bafe defg|afbf a2 f/g/a|bafe defg|afea fB B2|
bafe ^defg|afbf a3 f|e2 f/g/a bafb|afea fBB2:||

In the 5th bar of the A part, I hear one fiddle playing it as noted above, while the other plays: |e/e/e ae cAAc|.

Also note the sharped d in bar 5 of the B part.

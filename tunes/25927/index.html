One comment

Gan Ainm

This tune entitled “Polka” (SG1a.015) was transcribed in Book 1a (c1840s) of the Grier MSS of Gortletteragh, Co Leitrim. This tune and several other polkas in Book 1a are composed by ”J.F. Douglas", a light classical composer who has not been identified thus far.

Looney tunes’ tune

To all of you who ever watched the Looney tunes, here is a Looney’s tune! The tune is very nice, if you stress the three first low B’s in the second part of the tune. I wrote this tune together with “Down the wild river”, a tune I use to play right behind Looney’s. Try it! ;-)

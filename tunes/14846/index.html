From John Chambers web site <jc:trillian.mit.edu>, to add to the Boston Scottish Fiddle Clubs list of “Common Tunes for Scottish Sessions”.
William Shepherd (17??-1812) William Shepherd “2nd Collection” 1800 p.20 #5

Miss Carmichael

As played by the late Mike MacDougall in a set of Fmaj jigs on his “Tape for Father Hector.” It’s only given as “Traditional Jig” in the cassette notes.

From Alan Snyder’s Cape Breton Fiddle Recording Index:
http://www.cbfiddle.com/rx/tune/t596.html

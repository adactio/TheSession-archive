I’ve Never Had Pumpkin Soup Before

This is a very catchy tune composed by the Orcadian piper Andy Cant.

I originally learned this tune with the name “Pumpkin Soup” but I believe the full title is as shown above.

Although it is a pipe tune from Orkney, it seems to have nice American swing to it as well. That’s what I think, anyway. :-)

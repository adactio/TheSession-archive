I’ll Gang Nae Mair To Yon Toun

A tune played a lot by John Campbell, however he didn’t compose it, or record it. It was also published in the Skye collection in the key of F.

I’ll Gang Nae Mair To Yon Toun

“There’s another tune with this name (https://thesession.org/tunes/5920)”

It shouldn’t have the same name; the tune called “We’ll Aye Gang Back to Yon Toon” was composed by James Scott Skinner.

The tune above is old, and found under many names, including “Eight Men of Moidart”, “I’ll Gang Nae Mair To Yon Town”, “I’ll Aye Ca’ In By Yon Town” and, in Ireland, “The Pope’s Toe” (https://thesession.org/tunes/4355).

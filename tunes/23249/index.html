Re: The Anglerfish

J. P. Farran collated “Local Irish Names of Fishes” and it was published in the Irish Naturalist’s Journal of 1946 (Vol. VIII, Nos 9-12).

The Anglerfish a.k.a. Monkfish (Lophius piscatorius) was variously called from Portrush to Waterford;
Anglait / Iasacaire /
Brahwin / Briarbot / Bráthair / Friar
Catfish /
Deilgín deamhain (“thorn of the demon”!)
Frogfish /
Getta / Gettha /
Gilley-pern (Isle of Man)
Laimhíneach / Seán Laveen / Seán na laimhíne (“Johnny fingers”)
Molly Gowan / Mulligadoon / Mullagoon /
Mournoch /
Pillaich / Pleach
and a few more…

If you want to download Farran lexicon, clic on this automatic link;

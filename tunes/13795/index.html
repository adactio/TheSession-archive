Julia Clifford’s, X:3

I got this from the playing of Dan Accardi (B/C), who calls it Julia Clifford’s. Dan has a large number of hop jigs and it has been a delight to participate in his Comhaltas class and be privy to these oft-disguised and little played gems. If you play B/C accordion, try ending on a push G and B (outside row).

↳ Posted by Thady Quill on February 16th, 2016.

Re: The Rakes Of Sollohod

A setting from Jeremiah Breen, the blind fiddle player from North Kerry is given in O’Neill’s Waifs and Strays of Gaelic Melody (#215) as Lasses of Solohod. This version is closer to X:3, with that C nat starting instead of the D.
Incidentally, Sollohod, Solohod seems to be a townland a few miles north of Tipperary Town. It is probably better known as Soloheadbeg (Solohodbeg) which was the site of an ambush in January 1919, which most Irish credit as the start of the Irish War of Independence.

Crib Of Perches

This tune appears in the Reilly Family MSS of Drumreilly, Co Leitrim. Obviously, it’s not the tune more commonly known by this name. This version was obtained from a Cooke fiddler (possibly Patsy Cooke) from Mount Nugent, Co Cavan by the Reillys c1960. They obtained it to give it to Sean Maguire. Interestingly, Maguire’s recording of the other Crib of Perches (the Reillys have this tune too and also with the same title confusingly) in the 1960s is the first appearance of that particular tune. Could the Reillys have given both of these Crib of Perches tunes to Maguire and could both have been composed by Cooke??

The C natural in first part doesn’t sound right (C sharp doesn’t sound any better) but that’s the way it’s written in the manuscript

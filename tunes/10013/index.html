G-Reel

The tune title is “G-Reel” as it appears on Liz Carroll’s eponymous album (with Dáithí Sproule). It is indeed her composition, according to the sleeve notes. I remember she taught us this tune on a workshop, during the Chris Langan festival in Toronto. That was in 2009, if my memory is good.

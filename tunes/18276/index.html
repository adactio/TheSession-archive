Reel Jacqueline

From the playing of Brian McNeill with Drones & Bellows.

Part of their Northumbrian set, though it sounds more like a Quebecois reel. Don’t know who composed it, but, Brian’s wife is named Jacqueline, so guessing it was written for her.

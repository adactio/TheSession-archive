Four comments

Nellie Garvey’s

This is one of my favourite tunes to play on a cittern, as heard in Julie Fowlis‘ album Uam in the M’fhearann Saidhbhir set. In the recording it’s played in E flat dorian, so I thought I’d bring it down the halfstep for the sake of transposition.

↳ Posted by Charles Mackenzie on January 14th, 2017.

Re: Nellie Garvey’s

It’s lovely tune indeed. As far as I know, it’s composed by Éamon Doorley.

↳ Posted by Anton Sidyakin on March 4th, 2017.

Nellie Garvey’s, X:2

No 2nd or 6th in this tune, so a true A minor pentatonic.

↳ Posted by Jim Sime on February 17th, 2025.

Nellie Garvey’s, X:3

Transposed up, as heard in Kevin Meehan’s “I Have It Somewhere”, the 2nd of 3 great tunes.

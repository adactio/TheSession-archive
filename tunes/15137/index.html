Oay McKesson’s Pastime

While vampires, humans, and pumpkinhead immigrants have fully integrated within Salem, Massachusetts County, England after several generations of gradually increasing mutual hospitality, it is generally accepted that--in lieu of public records--the first inhabitants of Salem were the native werewolves, followed by the wulvers of Shetland and the faoladhs of Ireland.

On a typical Sunday, Oay McKesson, a third-generation wulver, likes to unwind on a nice fishing trip when he isn’t busy running the Jolly Wolf Cafe on South Haddings St. Life isn’t very exciting in Salem, but you won’t find more contented folks anywhere else.

Wish You Were Here,

Fiddler’s Despair

This version is based on Natalie MacMaster and Donnell Leahy’s recording on their album “One”.
It is the first tune on the 5th track.

↳ Posted by May I on February 21st, 2021.

Re: Fiddler’s Despair

I have been meaning to learn this tune on fiddle, but was surprised to see the name “Jamie Smith” as composer.
…wondered, which ‘Jamie Smith’?
Found out this tune’s name is really “The Accordionist’s Despair” by the Welsh accordionist/composer Jamie Smith of ‘Jamie Smith’s Mabon’ (Welsh folk band - disbanded 2020)! They released 7 albums & wikipedia states that most of the music was composed by Jamie - so, worth a listen.

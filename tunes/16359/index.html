Paddy Healy’s

As recorded by John Blake, Lamond Gillespie and Reg Hall on “Traditional Irish Music”, track 15. Surprised I haven’t found it here. This is a composite transcription taken from Blake and Gillespie’s slightly disparate versions, but mostly from Gillespie’s. It sounds a bit like an A dorian version of “The Yew”.

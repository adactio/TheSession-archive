Frankenstein

I can’t find anything like it in the tunes database, so I’ve popped it in.
The tune came from an abortive attempt at learning “Mayor Harrison’s Fedora”, and is called Frankenstein because it’s made of odd bits bolted together through the neck!

The last bar of each part can be played
GBAG F~E3
to make it a bit more traditional
On the other hand the start of the first part can be played with another rythm as
E2 B EGB EG|B EGB AGFE|
to give it a more modern touch :-)

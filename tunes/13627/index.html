The Mystic Flames Of Boitatá

This tune was written by my dear friend, Alex Navar from Rio de Janeiro.. It’s a hell of a nice tune! Literally hell cuz Boitatá is a folklore creature from Native-Brazilians Mithology.. it’s a a headless giant snake with bull horns and enormous fiery eyes that crawls over the open fields at night. Sometimes described as a giant fire snake. Looking at its eyes blinds people.

McKenna’s Ceilidh

According to user Richard D Cook, McKenna’s Ceilidh was written by piper and maker John Walsh in celebration of Father Neil McKenna’s 50th birthday.

I first hear this tune on Oliver Seeler’s hotpipes.com website as the D sample for John Walsh’s A/D smallpipe set. I’m seriously considering buying these pipes on the strength of this sample.

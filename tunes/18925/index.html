Stasia’s

From Kirsten Allstaff’s latest album Four/4. First tune of the 4th track.
According to the sleeve notes: “The first tune is a tune written by fiddler Clare McLoughlin for her Gran Anastasia McGinty of Lough Eske, Donegal […]”

Played in Fmin, I’ve transposed it to Emin as Fmin doesn’t seem to be supported here. Absolutely love the changes between minor and major in the 3rd part.

Here’s Clare’s original version. Couldn’t find Kirsten’s on Youtube.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y_I4P5yoRC4

Two comments

The Sandhills

I transcribed this from the album “The Sailor’s Cravat” (2011) by Paddy O’Brien (accordion), Tom Schaefer (fiddle), and Paul Wehling (bouzouki), track 1 (after “The Singing Kettle”).

On the album it appears as “Seán Ryans.” The name “The Sandhills” is what I presume to be Seán Ryan’s original title, from the collection of his compositions “Hidden Ireland: Volume 2.”

↳ Posted by fidkid on December 23rd, 2017.

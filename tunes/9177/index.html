Twelve comments

Notes

Having bought a Boldwood CD from Richard Heacock (ex fiddle recently left), I always loved this tune and thinks its one of the best on the CD. Searches for tunes of this name proves less than fruitful on the net so I set about transcribing it myself!

I’m a total minor-key junkie…

The only real difference between this transcription and the CD is rolls on the 2nd note on the last bar (both 1st and 2nd time through) of the A and the B parts. I left these out for clarity and to allow for personal taste.

I understand that Balthazar’s Feast may have recorded this as well - but I havent got the album yet. Hey Christmas is coming! ;)

William Irwins (Modal) Hornpipe

This tune is in the manuscripts of William Irwin, the Lakeland Fiddler, as ‘A hornpipe’ with the note ‘(modal)’ appended by Anne Gilchrist when she transcribed it.

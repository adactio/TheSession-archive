ITM at the movies

Almost all musicians love the movie This Is Spinal Tap -- here is the funny little reel from the Stonehenge part of the movie.

I was taken in by the tune and presumed it was a trad tune, but apparently it is an original tune by the writers and performers of the movie.

For complete authenticity, the tune should be played in Dmin - since that’s Nigel’s favorite key - but he would understand that the tune lays out a bit easier in Emin

Enjoy!

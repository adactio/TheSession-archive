Spitting Rickshaw

This is a tune I wrote many years ago, as I was travelling through China.
A man who was driving a rickshaw was spitting left and right each three pedal turns…
So, that’s it. ;-)

A tip. You can notate the rolls as follows:

K: Gmaj
|: GBB dBe | dBA BAG | ~F3 AFe | ~F3 DED |
G2B dBe | dBA BAG | F2F ded | cAF G3 :|
|: GBd g2b | agf ~g3 | ded cAG | AGF DEF |
G2B dBe | dBA BAG | F2F ded | cAF G3 :|
|: ~G3 cGB | ~G3 cGB | ~F3 AFe | ~F3 DEF |
~G3 cGB | ~G3 cGB | ~F3 ded | cAF G3 :|

(Along with a few other variations).

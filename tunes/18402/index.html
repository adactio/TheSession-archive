John Roy Lyall

Not a Reel, but a slow air. Composed by E. Edmund. Who was E. Edmund?

↳ Posted by Connor Hickey on September 16th, 2019.

Re: John Roy Lyall

Eddie Edmond (not Edmund). He came I believe from the Inverurie area. John Roy Lyall was Eddie’s father-in-law. He played in ‘Lyall’s Syrupy Five’!

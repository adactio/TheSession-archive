Paddy From Offaly

In Paddy O’Brien Tune Collection, volume three, reel #143, Paddy writes
"This is a composition of my own, which I put together in my head before
the idea of actually playing it occurred. I named it after my native county
of Offaly."
(Of course, he really named it after himself.)

Flowers For Mom

A lovely tune from the hand of Colm Naughton’s most recent CD ‘Running Through Woods & Falling In Bogholes’, It’s more a two-step than a jig, I think, Two-steps are more ‘lively - happy go lucky’, but call it what you like, it’s a lovely tune.
The first tune track 4.

The correct spelling is Usan.
This tune was composed in the memory of my grandfathers late wife Eleanor.
They lived together in the salmon bothy in the little fishing village of Usan 3 miles south of Montrose.
It appears one person has spelled it wrong and others have unfortunately followed.
Hopefully anyone who has spelled it incorrectly can now rectify their mistake.
David.

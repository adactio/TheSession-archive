Two comments

Eastern Reel

The other nice slow reel on Capercaillie’s “To The Moon”, written by fiddler Charlie McKerron. Works best on fiddle, but G#s in the 2nd part will give flutes a hard time! There is a flute setting on the recording, and I’ll post that below when I’ve worked it out. I’ve transcribed the ABC as Edor and Bmix for the 2 parts because that’s how they are harmonised on the recording.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on May 21st, 2003.

Flute setting

E2ED EBdB|f2fe e3e|f3a f3e|defe dBAF|
E2ED EBdB|f2fe e3e|1 defa f2fe|defe dBAF:|2 defa f2ef|bfaf e2fd||
K:Bdor (D naturals)
|:B2BA Bfaf|Bfaf BAfd|B2BA Bfaf|fbea deed|
B2BA Bfaf|Bfaf BAfd|B2BA Bfaf|deed deed:|

For both settings, when you get phrases that go up and then down again, it sounds nice to ornament them with simple taps and cuts rather than full on rhythmic rolls:

f2fe could be {e}f2{=g]fe
and fggf could be {e}fg{a}gf

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on May 21st, 2003.

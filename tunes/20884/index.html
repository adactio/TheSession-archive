One comment

Sir Hugh O’Neill

This tune can be played as a jig although I composed it a march. For some reason, this site does not allow marches to be in anything other than 4/4. It’s a march - in 6/8, best played at walking pace.
You can hear it played here: https://soundcloud.com/musicoutofthinair/sir-hugh-oneill-the-sperrins-march
I played a Bb flute on that recording, so it’s coming out in Eb. It’s a flutey tune.

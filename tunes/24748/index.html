No Coming. No Going

A slow piece composed by the Wexford multi-instrumentalist, Cian O‘Grady. Cian is a member of the up-and-coming trad fusion group, ’Tulua‘. This, along with a large selection of Cian’s compositions was recently published in ’The Wexford Tunebook’.

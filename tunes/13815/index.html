The Boise Monsters

A fun tune by Wendy MacIsaac. I’m not sure why, but the “session.org” display of the actual notes is omitting the “D” quarter note at the beginning of the second section. It drops it again for the third part, too. When I pasted my abc notation on “concertina.net” the “D” was there. So, not sure what’s up, I don’t like to post a tune missing a beat, but you can see it’s there in my abc.

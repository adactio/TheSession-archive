Two comments

The Toper’s Double

A Wexford double jig from the Feis Ceoil collection of Irish Airs, published in 1914. The Wexford tunes in the collection were mainly collected from musicians in the Rathangan and Duncormick areas of south Wexford, many of the tunes being played for mumming. This particular tune has echoes of a number of tunes including Apples in winter, and The Old Grey Goose.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 7th, 2022.

The Toper’s Double, X:2

A ‘reworked’ version of The Toper’s double. A ‘toper’ is an old word for a drunkard.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 10th, 2022.

A Bright May Morning

From O‘Neill’s Dance Music Of Ireland (#501). The earlier version which appears in O’Neill’s Music Of Ireland (#1228) is slightly different and probably in error.

↳ Posted by aidriano on December 2nd, 2015.

Gan Ainm

Well, this is a gan ainm from Ceol Rince na hÉireann vol.5, the only note as to its origin was that it came from a recording of musicians playing in New York. I first found it by way of Bill Black’s database, where he christened it “The Banks of the Hudson.” Seeing as I live on the banks of the Hudson, I think that’s a rather nice name (and a nice tune).

↳ Posted by j_clark on December 16th, 2024.

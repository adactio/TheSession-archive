“The Prince of Orange” ~ after that last one I’ve decided why not? ;-)

I learned this from an old English manuscript, can you believe it? Well, here’s the printed form:

“Lincolnshire Collections Volume 1: The Joshua Gibbons Manuscript”
Edited and transcribed by Peter D. Sumner
Breakfast Publications, Grimsby, 1997
ISBN: 0-9530117-0-4

Page 71, #123: Prince of Orange ~ original key - F
They give it in D Major as well, but with a significant difference from my transcription, they have it AABBCC, while I’ve given it AABC, for use with 32 bar dances… There is other evidence of my meddling, the accidentals toward the end in particular…

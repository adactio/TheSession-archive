The Ardvasar Blacksmith

Composed by Pipe Major Evan MacRae. My setting is based on a recording of the group Smalltalk. Fiddler Stuart Morrison plays it in the key of E minor, but I have transposed it back into a more pipe-friendly key.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on July 8th, 2013.

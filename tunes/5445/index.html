Three comments

This is a reel written by my chum Jason’s dad. Mr Lester (snr) is an accomplished musician living in Spain , and wrote and presented Jason with this reel. We first gave it an airing (we play fiddle) at our weekly session/folk club just before Christmas, when Jason’s dad was across here visiting Yorkshire.

I enjoy it very much, and asked whether I could post it to share on the fine website.

I play it with a couple of variations as follows:

M: 4/4
L: 1/8

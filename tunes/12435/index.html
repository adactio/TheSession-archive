Three comments

Miners

Also know as Pibddawns y Glowr in Welsh, pronounced;
Peeb…thowns uh g…low..rrr (with an umplat over the O), and remember to roll your arrs.
Usually followed by the Monmouth (Trefynwy)
Mike Lease and Jane Rideout recorded a great set on the CD ‘ffidil’ on the Fflach label.

Re: Hem Från Gesunda

I’d say it is actually a Polska. It’s a very popular dance in Sweden, in 3/4 with the accent on the 1st and 3rd beats that gives it a lollopy rythym.

Re: Hem Från Gesunda

As mentioned above, this polska (not polka - v. different thing) should be notated in 3/4 time. It’s attributed to Eric Sahlström (1912-1986), a nyckelharpa maker and player who was a very influential figure in the Swedish music tradition. There’s a splendid rendition of it by Magnus Holmström on YouTube at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o9n9rn3npak. There are also quite a few clips to be found of Eric Sahlström himself: well worth a look!

The Two Ladies Of Ovenstone

A fine tune by Billy Pigg from the Northumbrian piping tradition
Ovenstone is a house on the Hepple to Elsdon road, close to Hepple Woodside, and the two ladies, neighbours of Billy’s who sometimes came to the musical evenings, were called Miss Hall and Miss Gissing. The only recording of this tune is on a tape of very variable pitch and quality, and no title was found with it. It has been assigned one using circumstantial evidence. (from the NPS Billy Pigg compositions book)

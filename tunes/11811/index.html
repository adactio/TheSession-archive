Slate Hill

Absolutely bangin’ tune that appears as the second tune of track 4 on North-by-NorthWest by Eliot Grasso & Dave Cory. The whole album is a must buy as far as I am concerned.

They both vary little segments of the tune here and there but this transcription has the gist of the tune as a whole.

“Slate Hill” ~ C: John Daly

http://www.eliotgrasso.com/
http://www.eliotgrasso.com/North_by_Northwest.html

4. ) Reels: “The Blackberry Blossom” / “Slate Hill”

“Slate Hill was composed by the Cork-born fiddler John Daly and was originally set in the key of G minor. Between 1841 and 1902, fourteen of the houses in the neighborhood where John grew up were built of stone that came from a small quarry on the farm his parents bought in 1961. Quarrying ceased in 1903, but roofing slate continued to be harvested there until 1906. A century later, the place continues to be referred to locally as Slate Hill.”

“I picked up John Daly’s ‘Slate Hill’ from uilleann piper Kieran O’Hare when I was visiting him in Chicago during 2003.”

- Eliot Grasso

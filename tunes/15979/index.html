The Unwilling Sparrow

Written by me in early 2017. Every morning when I cycle to work I pass by this hedge and every day for several weeks now there’s this bird (a dunnock, I believe the English name is) sitting there, singing and looking fancy. I’ve tried to take a picture of him several times but he always buggers off just when I get the camera in focus.

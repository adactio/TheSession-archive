Two comments

William Stukeley’s Hornpipe

My transcription is from the playing on Leveret’s new album “Lost Measures” which I just love! (https://leveretband.bandcamp.com/album/lost-measures) The opening cut is a pair of 3/2 hornpipes. The first is “Old Abigail’s Delight”, recently transcribed and posted here by @gianmarco https://thesession.org/tunes/25925. The second is a 3/2 composed by Andy Letcher, who kindly allowed me to post it here. It is named for an 18th cent. English antiquarian who was fascinated with Stonehenge.

↳ Posted by Jon Crump on November 6th, 2025.

William Stukeley’s Hornpipe, X:2

The composer points out that “It was written for English border bagpipes in the key of G/C” and kindly provided the abc.

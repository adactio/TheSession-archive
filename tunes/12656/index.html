Re: Cathcart …waltz, mazurka and jig

Where the original waltz has
A B<d|e2 A>g f<d|e3 A B<d|e2 f>g f<B|d3 A B<d|
you can move the bar one beat to the left to obtain a handsome mazurka (almost ready made)
A | Bd e2 Ag | fd e3 A | Bd e2 fg | fB d3 A |…

or rewrite the notes of the original beats into 6/8 groupings to get a jig (be prepared to make a few ajustment but it’s there for the picking.)
ABd||e2A gfd|eAA ABd|efg fdB|dAA ABd|…

The Road To Maclatchey

As played by Mick McAuley on his album, An Ocean’s Breadth. I’m guessing it’s one of his own compositions, but I don’t have the liner notes.

The third part is basically a repeat of the second part, so it’s more or less an ABB tune with the only difference being the first measure.

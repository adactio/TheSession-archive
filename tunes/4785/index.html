Biddy The Bold, X:7

I know this as “Biddy the Bowl-wife”, played for the Irish dance called “The Waves of Tory”. This the top line of the piano setting from Book One of THE IRISH FOLK DANCE BOOK (music arr. by J Michael Diack), dance arrangements and intro by Peadar O‘Rafferty. Patersons Pub’ns. Copyright in USA 1934! My book originally cost 3/- …. and it has a fine green cover with shamrocks round the sides.

By the way, Bk Two has rather more complex tunes, including “The Sweets of May” (lovely).

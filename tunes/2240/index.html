William, move yer heed!

I transcribed this from the classic film, _So I Married an Axe Murderer_. It is playing in the background during the scene where the gang is at the Cock ‘o the North for Charlie’s parents’ anniversary; it begins just after Mr. Mackenzie’s speech (Shut it!).

Filoro

The name of this tune is Filoro (Is truagh nach d‘ rugah dall mi). From the Scottish Gaelic, it translates as ’I wish I’d been born blind’. A song of unrequited love by the Bard William Ross (1762 - 1791?).

↳ Posted by stoganofort on June 1st, 2013.

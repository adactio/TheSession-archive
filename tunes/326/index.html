In Amaj, not Dmaj

Despite the lack of positive evidence in the form of G sharps, the tune is clearly in Amaj not in Dmaj. The tune appears on the Northumbrian-Irish band Cúig’s album Prospect as “Martin Mullvihill’s”. In the somewhat Northumbrian key of Emaj.

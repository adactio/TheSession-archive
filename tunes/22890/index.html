Bringing In The Green

I came across “Bringing in the Green” as a counter-melody to “Bodmin Riding March” among a heap of papers with various tunes on them. I have since lost the sheets which included the composer’s name. I’ve added it here, as it stands in its own right separately from the “Bodmin Riding March”.

I would appreciate anyone shedding light on who wrote the tune or offering any more info on it.

The ABC below gives both tunes together. The structure here is AABB but the original was simply AB.

Heather down the Moor, X:2

This very happy ditty has no repeats and an odd number of beats. It is no less enticing for it.
It is well known in singing circles. June Tabor recorded it folk-ages ago.
For this transcription I have used Ruth Notman’s rendition of the 1st verse.
https://youtu.be/2rdy65oMFz8

Some Irish singers have recorded it as well, notably Paul Brady & Andy Irvine in 1977 on The Gathering album. Their version is slightly different and I’ll post it one day, if no one else does
https://youtu.be/sX0057fGqc4

The melody is shorter than ‘Bringing in the Green’, but it seems to contain all that’s alive and sprightly in it. (In fact, the second part in the march sounds like an ill-fitting add-on, just for the sake of evening out the tune)

Re: Far O’er The Struy

The village and road is actually Struy - no a - in the NE of Scotland. The road is very wild and beautiful. And this is the location to which the tune refers. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Struy
Struay is a fictional island in the Katie Morag books, but based on the real island of Coll in the Hebrides.
Just sayin’!

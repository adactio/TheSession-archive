Old Jig #6

I got this tune from the Dardanelles’ “Eastern Light”. Its “name” comes from the Kelly Russell Collection (vol 1) where it is listed as one of the old traditional tunes that Emile Benoit played but did not have a name for. As names go that’s not great, but I guess it’s better than “gan ainm”.

At any rate, I absolutely love this tune, with its lovely mellow first part and crooked turn.

Four comments

Amhran Na Tra Baine

I recorded Micho Russel playing this in 1983ish. It obviously isn’t a jig! It is an air and song tune and would be better in 6/4. Played quite slowly. It is the tune to Micho’s song about the man who invented the submarine. He played it on a Clarke’s C whistle. He had loads of different whistles but preferred playing the cheapest, breathiest one! A great man sadly missed.

Amhran Na Tra Baine, X:3

Setting #2 shows key of Cmajor (like setting #1), but notes shown only work when played in D (F#). So I did the transposition here.

It’s a slow air, and I always find having the lyrics helps with phrasing. I added the words from recording of Liam Ó Maonlaí at political demonstration (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gKFFzCmZt18). Also worth listening is Stiofán Ó Cualáin (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YO3l-vOsaDs).

Finally altered some of the notes to fit better with how these singers sing it, melisma and all! It’s approximate of course. Take it and make it better!

↳ Posted by Frank Gibbons on November 19th, 2022.

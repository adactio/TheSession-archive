Duncan MacQuarrie’s, X:2

I’ve just been learning this tune.
This is the setting as played on “Cèilidh Air Chuairt” (21 Years of the Fèis Rois Ceilidh Trail), and fixes the timing issues in the first setting. Tempo is about 69 minims (half notes) per minute.

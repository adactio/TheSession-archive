One comment

Ozark Rag

Pity the tune has been rendered so carelessly. This tune stems from a recording by the East Texas Serenaders which was recorded on the 28th November 1930 in Dallas, Texas. The Serenaders were Daniel Huggins Williams (fiddle), John Munnerlyn (tenor banjo), Cloet Hamman (guitar) and Henry Bogan (cello). Later Shorty lester took over the banjo duties, and they added a second fiddle, played by Henry Lester. They were reintroduced to fans of American old-time music with an LP reissue on County Records, ‘East Texas Serenaders: 1927-1936’ (County 410, 1977).

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on June 7th, 2013.

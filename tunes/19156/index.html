Re: Annie’s Carafe

Iain MacDonald is a Highland piper/flute/whistle player -ex Ossian and Battlefield Band member. 1 of three brothers all pipers, Angus & Allan MacDonald. Allan is a regular at Willie Clancy and other Irish festivals. Sean nos Gaelic singer. Allan has composed many tunes.

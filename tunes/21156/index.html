Four comments

Sprig Of Holly Schottische

This tune is transcribed in the Reilly MSS (c1900) of Drumreilly, Co Leitrim. I suspect this version was copied by the scribe from printed material.

↳ Posted by ConorW on October 27th, 2021.

Sprig Of Holly Schottische, X:2

This version, almost identical to the version above, is notated in the Power manuscript from Kilmore in County Wexford. It’s in the more modern part of the manuscript dating from approx 1920. It is interesting that there is quite a bit of crossover of material between the Leitrim/Longford manuscripts, that Conor has been working on and the Wexford manuscript.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on April 25th, 2023.

Re: Banana In The Ear

I just recieved mail from Davy Maguire about orgin of this jig:

‘I just wanted to check that I was correct about the composer of the ’La Banane Dans l‘Oreille’ (The Banana In The Ear). It was composed by fiddle-player Fanch Landreau, who is sadly no longer with us. I learned it from the Skolvan album ‘Swing & Tears’. Fanch also played with Archétype and Les Ours du Scorff (a band who performed children’s songs). He was one of the few Breton musicians who played Irish music as if he was Irish himself - a great musician.’
Marin

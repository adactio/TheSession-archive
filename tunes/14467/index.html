Three comments

An Old Woman’s Money

Tune #391 from the Goodman manuscripts. This was recorded on Tunes from the Goodman Manuscripts by Mick O’Brien, Emer Mayock and Aoife Ní Bhriain. Not to be confused with (but likely related to) a tune of the same name (Airgiod Caillighe), tune #38 from the Goodman manuscripts.

↳ Posted by KJM on June 9th, 2015.

Two comments

Niel Gow’s Recovery

From Cape Breton Scottish Collection. In B flat major. First published as a single sheet in 1804 but later appeared in the Beauties of Niel Gow. Sounds good played slow.

↳ Posted by dancarney84 on May 1st, 2015.

Niel Gow’s Recovery, X:2

I find the A flats in the second part to be tedious so I’ve substituted with a high ‘b’ instead. A couple little simple variations as well. Enjoy this one, whoever finds it. It’s an expressive tune. I could see someone with a classical background really making this one roll and go.

↳ Posted by dancarney84 on May 1st, 2015.

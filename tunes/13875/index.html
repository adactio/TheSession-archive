Zuppa Inglese

A jig learnt from the playing of the wonderful melodeon player Rees Wesson. I find myself playing this tune over and over again.

↳ Posted by Edward Nunn on October 7th, 2014.

Trifle

The title translates literally as ‘English soup’, but it is, I believe, the Italian term for ‘trifle’ (the dessert).

Major Moir Of Villeveque

Tune ID requested by session.org member Trevor Jennings:

https://thesession.org/members/3932

- in this discussion:

https://thesession.org/discussions/27053

The original is a Scottish 6/8 pipe march (I think by Pipe Major John McLellan), so it follows that my ABC transcription can only really be an interpretation of the tune.

Here’s a clip of the Dubai Pipe Band playing the original at a practice night:

http://www.dubaipipeband.com/galleryvideo.htm

And here’s a clip of a ceilidh band interpreting the tune as a jig at a community barn dance:

Five comments

Fønix

Composed by Norwegian fiddler Annbjørg Lien & has somewhat of a Spanish or Flamenco vibe to it, despite it obviously being composed by a Norwegian musician. Thus making it a ‘Nordic’ tune. Not sure what Annbjørg’s inspiration was behind it. It’s been played by many other musicians from other styles and traditions, as well. Hanneke Cassel, Natalie Haas, Alasdair Fraser, Liz Carroll, among others. I just put it under “reel” because, uhm… it’s in 7/8. My bad! But it’s one of the most fun tunes to play!

And if you’re willing, or daring enough, on the fiddle, tune the G-string up to an A, so it’s in ADAE. That really makes this tune easy to play and creates a great sound. You might even try it in AEAE tuning, but there are quite a few G and D chords/sections, where I like the resonance of the open D.

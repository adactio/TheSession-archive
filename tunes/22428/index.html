The Mummers’ Captain

This little jig has been in the melting pot for quite some time, but I’ve settled on this setting. It’s a composition of my own, which I’ve named The Mummers‘ Captain, in memory of the many ’leaders‘ of mumming sets from around Wexford over the years. The Mummer’s captain traditionally recited the Captain’s rhyme to introduce the ’set‘ to the audience. He (normally, although there were ladies sets too) would be an excellent performer in doing his ’turn‘ in the mumming display - usually a solo step dancing display, or perhaps a song , occasionally a recitation. My own most memorable Mummers’ Captain was Sean Sinnott , an outstanding step dancer of the ‘old’ school. He was captain of The Dungeer set when I was a young fella - part of their band of ‘musicianers’ to provide the music for the marching in , the dancing of the sword dance and doing a musical ‘turn’ as part of the entertainment.

Head Lifter

I hope this jig is not already in thesession. Anyway I can’t find it so I have decided to post it. This version comes from a friend who has learned it during a banjo workshop with Joanna Boyle from Síoda in Tocane this summer. The title, “head lifter” comes from comhaltas live programme n°433/2. Another friend of mine knows this tune under the name “dancing in the puddles”. Anybody knows the origine of this lovely tune ?

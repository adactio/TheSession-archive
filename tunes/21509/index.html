March Of The Irish Defence Force

This march/song “March of the Irish Defence Force” was one of approximately 200 tunes submitted by the fiddle player Alex Sutherland (1873-1969) of Drumreilly, Co Leitrim to the National Folklore Commission in 1957. Sutherland states in a note beside the tune “An Irish March composed by Alexander Sutherland, Toam House, Carrigallen, Co Leitrim”. This page is stamped “Radio Eireann 1940” which suggests he also sent this tune to Radio Eireann, perhaps for a radio audition and they must have sent the tune back to him which he then submitted to the NFC. There is no match for this tune as of yet so it’s possible Sutherland did compose it. He also provides the words for this song later in another submission of the same piece which he entitles “Of the Irish Defence Force I will sing you a song”.

Re: Sean Tiobrad Arann

The last note as written is an E, but this is *not* where the tune resolves, it’s just where the part ends. Note that in Levey (whose version O‘Neill most likely copied), the transcription actually ends with e2d, ie the pick-up note at the beginning. Your transcription here is actually incorrect, compared to what O’Neill actually published: you put e3, whereas in both the Music of Ireland and the Dance Music of Ireland, O’Neill only includes e2, because that d pickup is integral to the structure of the tune, but is included at the beginning. E is not the resolution note.

Your comment about the lack of repeats is because you’re not understanding the style used by O’Neill in transcribing the tunes. A repeat at the end of the tune is not needed, because it’s understood by traditional musicians that the entire tune would be repeated. It’s also understood that additional playing may be required to resolve the tune… in other words, it’s understood by traditional musicians reading this tune that it isn’t played exactly and precisely as written.

James Gordon’s

This is a rockin’ reel that Frederick Gordon wrote for his brother, James Gordon. I first heard it at a music camp, and I don’t know much about it other than the fact that I love this tune! I brought it to my local Irish session, and everyone loves it there too. It mostly seems to fall into the key of E Phrygian (😲 Wow!). However, the lift in the C part when it goes to major is just… wow. The syncopations are sooo good! I dig how it has such a distinct flavor with the flat 2 and really builds intensity. It almost has a metal-like flavor in that sense, and it’s always fun to play in a hot session when the groove is really on 🔥🎻

(Note: to my knowledge, this tune is often played with the B part single instead of double, too.)

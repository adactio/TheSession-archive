Evening Comes Early

Composer: John Doyle
Transcribed from Sylvain Barou’s “JD’s Reels”

↳ Posted by watkinsj on September 25th, 2013.

Evening Comes Early, X:2

I heard this tune a long time ago, transcribed it from Sylvain Barou’s playing, and had no idea it was a Doyle tune. I play it as a 32 bar reel AABC, but here it is in 48 bar format as is the original.

↳ Posted by LastPosted on April 29th, 2023.

Evening Comes Early, X:3

From the playing of John Doyle who (I believe) composed the tune. There a couple spots where I smoothed out some ornaments for ease of notation/reading, but I’ve played along with the recording on the album of the same name and it all seems to fit pretty well. On the album it’s played AABBCCAABBCCAA, and the second time through the C part is heavily ornamented. Monster tune!

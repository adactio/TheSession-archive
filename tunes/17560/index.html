Re: Heaven’s Gate

One of the beautiful tunes from the film, composed by David Mansfield. Aly Bain and Phil Cunningham recorded it on The Pearl (1995), mistakenly calling it “Mamou Two Step”.

You’ve not notated it right, and there are a few bars with too many notes (e.g. last bar on the top line, and the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 10th full bars in the B part). The pick-up notes might be better as | AB>c |. 4th bar in the A part: | D3 AB>c |. Try it again, let, and see if you can see where you’ve gone wrong.

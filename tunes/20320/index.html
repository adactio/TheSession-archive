McKendrick’s

tune composed by concertina player Mohsen Amini and recorded by Talisk and Fourth Moon. It has a broken bar at the end of each part.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sHqO8rWeZbU

↳ Posted by Juan Almaraz on January 16th, 2021.

McKendrick’s, X:2

I first *saw* this music in October 2023, framed on the wall of the Munro Guest House in Stirling, Scotland, signed by Mohsen Amini. The proprietor of the guest house is Richard McKendrick and he saw me looking at it (and I took a photo as well), and told me that members of Talisk had stayed with him, or had support from him at some point, and that the tune was written for him. He was unaware of any recordings, but it’s actually on the Talisk CD “Beyond,” on the last track which is also called “Beyond.” Took me a while to decide to submit this, because it’s a transcription of the music framed on the wall (slightly simplified since the 1st and 2nd endings were not needed). Listening to the CD, only the second half of measure 8 seems slightly different, but not significantly so. Fun bit of research, and discovery (for me) of not only Mohsen Amini, but Talisk and Imar too! :)

↳ Posted by Julie Gardner on December 4th, 2023.

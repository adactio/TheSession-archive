Witch’s Steps

It is said, to make a wish at the “Wishing Steps” one has to walk to the bottom, and return walking backwards with eyes closed, a wish would then be granted by the Fairy’s or Druid’s…I wrote this one having the steps in mind, and likewise the bass (chords) line goes upward in the A section, and backwards in B section, whereas the melodies are divided in two themes of 4 bars each (the second being the first played backwards)….a little bit tricky! But hope you’ll enjoy!

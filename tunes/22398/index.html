Amanda’s Birthday

Tune by Elizabeth Davidson-Blythe, originally written around 2017-2018 and played by a few contra bands in the Northeastern US area. Recorded on “The Coast Road” with Daniel Quayle in 2022 (track 4 off the album).

Transcribed mostly from The Coast Road recording, but referenced another recording for comparison so there might be a few errors.

Puddlegum’s Misery

This was written by accordionist John Kirkpatrick and was recorded on his first solo album “Jump At The Sun”. It is the first tune in a medley of 3 Kirkpatrick originals: “Puddlegum’s Misery”/“Accordionism”/“Jump At The Sun”.

↳ Posted by Niles H on October 27th, 2011.

Rochelle Rudolph

Another reel composed by Patrick O’Leary in the late 1800s or early 1900s. His Grandson Des O’Leary gave me a copy of a tune book containing tunes composed by Patrick O’Leary. Des gave me this little booklet of tunes in the early 1990s but I misplaced it then found it again the other day and thought I would post the tunes here. I’m not sure if O’Neil added these tunes in his books, as Patrick O’Leary was good friends with O’Neil.

↳ Posted by JACKB on January 27th, 2015.

Re: Rochelle Rudolph

O’Neill did include this tune in his Waifs and Strays of Gaelic Melody, 1922 (#360). He called it Miss Rochelle Rudolph’s Hornpipe. He added this comment:
“Inspired by the visit of a relation, the first of his blood to greet him in the antipodes, since as a youth he left his native Cavan, Mr O’Leary commemorated the occasion by the composition of this fine hornpipe in her name. A sketch of his life in ”Some Famous Fiddlers“ pages 379 to 384, Irish Minstrels and Musicians will amply repay perusal.”

↳ Posted by brailsford on January 21st, 2024.

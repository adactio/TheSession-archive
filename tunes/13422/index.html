Seven comments

Bowing The Strings

A Canadian breakdown by Ned Landry who joined Don Messer and the New Brunswick Lumberjacks and later led them - see http://www.ibiblio.org/fiddlers/BONS_BOX.htm for recordings. Source for the notation - Don Roy’s website: http://fiddleicious.com/pdfs-2010/

↳ Posted by Alan Wilson on February 24th, 2014.

One Jackie Daly Special, comin’ right up!

This lovely hornpipe comes from a tape a friend made me of Jackie Daly’s “Many’s a Wild Night” album. Since I don’t have the liner notes to the recording, I’m unsure if the tune is traditional, or is one of Jackie’s own compositions. A splendid tune, either way. If someone does know from whence this tune originates, please let me know. Thanks, and enjoy!

“The Clockhouse” ~ C: Jackie Daly

“Jackie Daly: Domhnach Is Dálach / Many’s a Wild Night”
https://thesession.org/recordings/899

Side 2, track 1 / track 8 ~ hornpipes: “The Clockhouse” / “The Old Court”

Two compositions of Jackie named after two of the most prominent buildings in his home town of Kanturk. The Clockhouse which has a history dating back to the 1800s was restored and renovated in recent times. ~
Máire O’Keeffe ~ Gael Linn, 1995

Three comments

Eleanor Foxe

A lovely Josephine Marsh slip jig on her album Music in the Frame, in a set of three of her tunes. The spelling Elleanor is used on the Bandcamp version, while the physical copy says Eleanor, so I’ve indexed it under both spellings.

I classed it as F# dorian (E major in thesession’s editor) rather than F#m, though it doesn’t include Ds at all, due to the F#min->Emaj arpeggio movement.

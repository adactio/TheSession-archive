Two comments

A Sore Point

Written by guitarist Chris Newman and first recorded by him and harper Maire Ni Chathasaigh on their album “Out of Court” (Old Bridge, 1991). The liner notes say “A sort of jig inspired by a Fernando Sor tune”.

↳ Posted by michaelr on March 5th, 2011.

Jack In The Box, X:2

I believe this is the correct setting for this tune, transcribed from Carrying the Tune by Kevin Crawford. The first setting is offset by half a measure throughout due to a misinterpretation of where the measure begins. The tune begins with the d & e pickup notes.
Kevin plays gorgeous tune on an Eb flute, which matches Joe McKenna’s Eb pipes on his CD, however this D setting is much more playable for most players.
If used as the last tune in a set, it can end nicely by wrapping back to the A part and playing a long, lovely D note.

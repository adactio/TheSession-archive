Four comments

I love this Norwegian Tune, I find it fashinating and hypnotic.
It’s not a polka, even I written it with dotted eighth + sixteenth, the rhythm has more of a triplet feel.
Play it at 120 bmp ( more or less ).

Source: Bukkene bruse - “The Stone Chair”

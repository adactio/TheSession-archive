Lady Connecticut

This is a tune I composed in 2021 and was recently entered into Fleadh Cheoil Chorcai where it earned a 86% score. This tune was written for a cousin of mine, Sheila Cregg, in Suffield Connecticut, hence the name. The tune is played with a swing and rolls throughout.

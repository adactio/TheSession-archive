Judge’s Favourite (Martin Matthews)

This reel, thought-up by my long time friend Martin Matthews of Co Durham UK. Martin is second generation Irish. He plays tenor banjo (I think he won the “All Ireland” in Buncrana, Donegal - late 70s. Not that he ever mentions it).

It has a very traditional feel and I learned it as soon as I heard it, same time, same day. If we didn’t know otherwise, it could be a traditional tune

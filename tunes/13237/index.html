Setting #3: Darby’s Cross

is from the fiddle and flute of Julia and Billy Clifford (1982). While the A part is very much the same as in Danny Green’s, the B part features some differences (most notably going up in bars 11 and 12 instead of down). But (and as it’s from the Cliffords as well) I think it’s reasonable to assume that it’s just another version of the same tune. So I’ve added “Darby’s Cross” as an alternative tune title; I hope that’s okay.

Five comments

Les Frères Pigeon

Quebec Reel, can be notated in 2/4 time as well. Has some similarities to Reel Eugène (https://thesession.org/tunes/9555).

First tune (Brock McGuire Band):
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SfX4dkQ7YlM

And as CreadurMawnOrganig pointed out, it appears on Raz-de-Marée (Tidal Wave)’s album “Contra Music Of Quebec”.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on December 24th, 2018.

Re: Les Frères Pigeon

As the title suggests, the tune is from the Pigeon Brother’s repertoire. Not sure whether they composed it or not. This likely refers to Marcel Pigeon and Phylias Pigeon, twin brothers, and grandsons of Quebec accordionist Arthur Pigeon. Which would also explain the resemblance to Reel Eugene, as Arthur was known to play this one. See here: http://www.lafamilleleger.com/dr-squeeze-blog/2014/4/5/joseph-arthur-pigeon-1884-1966

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on December 24th, 2018.

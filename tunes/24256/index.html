Three comments

Jer Dan Mac’s

This is a fun little polka from the new Sliabh Luachra CD “Tunes from Toureendarby” by Pat Fleming, Steve Cooney and Timmy O’Connor.
https://thesession.org/recordings/8043

The tune comes from the playing of Jer Dan McAuliffe – Timmy Connors knows plenty of his tunes and there’s half a dozen of them featured on the new album!

You can listen to it on Bandcamp – and get the CD there too, as well as directly from Pat Fleming:
https://saibhreasceoil.bandcamp.com/track/polkas-jer-dan-macs-polkas
https://saibhreasceoil.com/music

↳ Posted by Bregolas on May 2nd, 2024.

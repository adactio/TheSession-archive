Breakfast At The Methés’

From Les Poules à Colin’s album Ste-Waves. While I’m not generally a fan of tunes that lean too heavily on the blues scale, the finely wrought B section more than redeems this tune in my ears. I’ve heard it played once or twice at sessions around Montreal.

Near the end of the track, a B-flat major chord into an E-flat major chord over the first four measures of the A section hit hard.

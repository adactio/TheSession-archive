Eyebrows

I first picked this Terry Tully tune from the Pipedown album “Roag”. They play it with the parts mixed up for their arrangement but this is basically how the tune goes. Cracking on the GHB at a moderate pace.

Here they are playing it, from about 3.50. The cracking tune before is a Gordon Duncan composition called Davy Websters 40th.

Re: The Banks Of The Deveron

LOVE playing this on the Fiddle (& I accidentally posted about this in the ‘Banks of Devon’).
Even several places where I’ve heard Jenna play this, it doesn’t feel like a jig to me - more an Air in 6/8.
So, I don’t know how to do ABC notation, but there is this different version (with ABC notation) at https://tunearch.org/wiki/Banks_of_the_Deveron_(The)
It is also in 6/8, but listed as an Air - attributed to William Christie (1778-1849). Maybe composer, or only collected by him, but found in his Book: “Christie - Collection of Strathspeys, Reels, Hornpipes,Waltzes &c. (Edinburgh, 1820, p. 11). Notes: Christie was a dancing master, fiddler and composer from Cuminestown, Aberdeenshire. Transcription: AK/Fiddler’s Companion.”
Here, performed by Chris Harrison, which might inspire a choice of chords… similar to many Turlough O’Carolan tunes, in that there’s a bit of a Baroque feel - lovely Scottish tune.
https://youtu.be/Yg4d5H-rrns

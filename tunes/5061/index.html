Morag’s Reel

I had figured out the ABC for this a while ago, but I saw that someone requested, so I figured it was about time someone added it. I heard this on Wolfstone’s song of the same name, and it sounds pretty cool even with the 80s synthesizer accompanying the fiddle. Although I would suggest that just a normal piano accompaniment would suffice.

Wolfstone’s website says that this tune was composed by Bobby MacLeod.

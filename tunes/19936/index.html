Two comments

Adam’s Nightmare

Kevin Henderson (Session A9, Boys of the Lough, The Nordic Fiddlers Bloc) composed this reel for his good friend Adam Sutherland, one of Scotland’s top fiddle players and composers. Adam had sent Kevin a text message one morning with a recording attached asking if he had taught him the tune in the recording, as he had dreamt the night before that Kevin had taught it and they had been playing it together in a session. Kevin didn’t recognize the tune at all so he decided he would write Adam a tune!

Transcribed from The Nordic Fiddlers Bloc’s album “Bonfrost”. They do a fair bit of variations that I haven’t included. This is what think, the bare bones of the tune. Also, I’ve transposed it to make it whistle/flute friendly.

https://thenordicfiddlersbloc.bandcamp.com/track/adam-s-nightmare

Eight comments

Arkle Mountain

This reel was composed by Manchester banjo player Tony O’ Sullivan ( aka Sully ).

↳ Posted by Peter Piper on February 1st, 2004.

It is included in a set by Gerry (Fiddle )O`Connor and Dessie Wilkinson on a classic album “Cosa gan Broga”

↳ Posted by johnny on February 2nd, 2004.

It’s the first Irish Trad tune I learned - from Sully’s tune book,
years before I played in any session. And nobody plays it in sessions.

↳ Posted by Mark Huppert on March 27th, 2011.

I love the tune - learnt it off Brendan Larrisey’s Flick of the Wrist, but he play’s it very fast - it gains an extra bit of magic played slower. On Flick of the Wrist CD it’s called Gerry O’Connor’s which is why it’s hard to find, and makes sense of johnny’s comment above.

↳ Posted by Forbidden Flute on February 15th, 2012.

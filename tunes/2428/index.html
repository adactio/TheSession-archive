As played by Néillidh Boyle under the title D​ú​laman Na Binne B​ú​idhe , X:3

This is a rough transcription of how it was played by Néillidh Boyle under the title D​ú​laman Na Binne B​ú​idhe on a recording whose link is in the ABC below - highly recommended.

FWIW here’s how I would prefer to write the tune, using semiquavers in 4/4. Here I prefer having 4 groups of 4 notes per bar. Instead of 4/4 it could be in 4/2 with quavers rather than semiquavers. (Often I prefer to not necessarily follow conventions, when notating, as personal preference is important too I think.)

I’m not posting this in the transcription above as I expect it wouldn’t be well received by some.

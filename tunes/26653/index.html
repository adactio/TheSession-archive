Telling The Bees

Composed by Brenda Castles, posted with permission. From the soundtrack to her film, “Plight of the Bumble Bee.” Originally written in E minor, apparently, but she plays it on her Bb/F concertina, so it comes out in D minor, which is how I’ve notated it here.

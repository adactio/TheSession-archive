Pasucáis De La Sacristana

Asturian pasucáis (6/8 march) written by the late Elías García (Llan de Cubel, Felpeyu). Based on the version played by Rubén Bada.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jB__muxz_No

Rubén plays it quite steadily. Here, Felpeyu play it much faster (2nd tune)

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x6k-l4XxH_Y

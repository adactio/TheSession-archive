Frank Kelly’s

This is a lovely reel composed by Clare fiddler, Yvonne Casey. I transcribed it from a private video of herself playing the tune. She named it after actor, Frank Kelly (of Father Jack fame) who had recently passed away.

I love the shapes in this tune and lyrical phrases. She has cleverly composed it to sound deceptively crooked, but it’s not. It reminds me of Larry Redican’s, “Galway Reel” composed in a similar style and I like to follow Yvonne’s tune with his for that reason.

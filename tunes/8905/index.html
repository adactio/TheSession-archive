“Thompson’s Compleat Collection of 200 Favourite Country Dances, Volume 2”

London, 1765

Also to be found arranged for piano, 4/4, two hands, in Hugh Mellor’s little booklet “Welsh Dance Tunes”, 1935, under the same title, “Dick the Welshman”, from the source given in the heading, where it is for a specific country dance… Mellor’s transcription is,based on those given, AAB, not AABB. Here are the ABCs for the earlier source, changed to 2/4 for easier comparison ~

X: 2
T: Dick the Welshman
M: 2/4
L: 1/8
R: country dance
K: Amaj
|: e |\
cB Ac | df ec | fa ec | dB Be |
cB Ac | df ec | fa ed | cA A :|
|: e |\
fa ga | ea ga | (3def ec | dB Be |
fa ga | ea ga | (3def ed | cA A :|

Except for the triplets, (3def, it is identical to Abram’s contribution given above…

Three comments

Rounding Malin Head

Written by Zoe Conway. This version is from Brian Finnegan’s playing in the video posted below (begining around 6:54). Brian plays it on an A whistle with Em fingers so the melody comes out in Bm. So much drive in this tune! I love the way it keeps banging on the low note. There some funky timing in the 6th measure - and it might be better to think of it as four sets of triplets. However you count it, you still lose a half a measure of 4/4.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6JnLcvttNA0

↳ Posted by jdicarlo on March 16th, 2019.

Rounding Malin Head, X:2

This is Conway’s own version of the tune, taken from her Desert Storm set. Conway finishes up with a flourish that I didn’t know how to add to the setting, and probably shouldn’t be there anyway, so here is the ABC for the end of the tune as Conway plays it:

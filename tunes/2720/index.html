Tommy Peoples’

Off Frankie and Mairead’s Ceol Aduaidh. This setting is based on a Sydney session recording. The tune rarely gets played, and when it does, people always start to play the much more common Doon Reel https://thesession.org/tunes/438 over the top of it before they realise it’s a different tune.

An old tune recycled!

Hmm, this is unmistakably the same tune as ‘’The Blue-eyed Lassie‘ appearing in Ryan’s Mammoth Collection from the mid-19th century. How did it get to be called Tommy Peoples’?

↳ Posted by rwwt on October 17th, 2010.

Obvious….

As Dr. Dow writes above, Frankie and Mairead recorded it on their first LP, in 1983, I think. They learned the tune from Tommy Peoples, who presumably didn’t have a name for it, so in keeping with the tradition, they named it after their source, ie Tommy Peoples.

↳ Posted by Kenny on October 18th, 2010.

↳ Posted by Angela Michelle on March 13th, 2004.

Played by the composer John Morris in the Limehill set on Youtube

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=znqZhiTqt-8

The tune is the fourth in the set and appears at 2:51

John Morris Rankin made and named this tune to honour his daughter Molly Rankin.

She was only 12 when John (40) tragically died in a car accident. This tune was played in his funeral:
http://www.therankinfamily.com/newsstories.php?RFCMSID=6

It would be great if anyone had the right names.

Re: The Raheen

This tune is the ‘actual’ Raheen reel. Raheen is a place in the parish of Adamstown in the centre of County Wexford. It’s about 15 miles north of where the Murphy family are from in Ballygow.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on October 14th, 2022.

The Raheen, X:3

This older setting of the Raheen reel is from an old cassette recording of the fiddler and traditional singer Jim Smith from Clongeen in Wexford. Jim is believed to have been the possible source for this reel, whether it was a composition of his or an old local tune will probably remain a mystery. Clongeen is about 5 miles or so from Raheen. The tape features a few other unusual tunes that I’ve already uploaded over the past number of months.

Five comments

Written by a good friend of mine. I think it’s a lot of fun to play.
I’ve put together an mp3 of a synthesized version of it at
http://home.comcast.net/~saustin98/lark/katiesreel.mp3

It’s not as good as a recorded session rendition, of course, but it gives you the idea. The “bodhran” is just the same measure over and over (with a rim-shot on the back-beat), and I added a stand-up bass to imply the chords. The main instrument is a (not bad sounding) synthesized concertina.

↳ Posted by Steve Austin on June 3rd, 2006.

Flight of the Termite

This is not the Katie’s Reel that is recorded by Deanta in “The Flight of the Termite” set. Not even close.
That goes something like this:

X:1
T:Katie’s Reel
M:4/4
L:1/8
R: reel
K:D Dor
|:DEDC A,3D|CB,CD EFG2|Addc AGFG|Addc d3e|
|f2ef defd|eaag a3g|f2ef dcAG|1F2EF D3C:|2F2EF D3G|
|Adde fedf|eaag a3g|f2ef dcAG|F2EF E3G|
|Adde fedf|eaag a3g|f2ef dcAG|1F2EF D3G:|2Addc d4|

I couldn’t get a perfect rendition. Anyone who thinks they can improve on this, feel free.

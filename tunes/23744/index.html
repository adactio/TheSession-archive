Re: Sandy Boys

And -whoops!
…I wrongly identified the mode;
it is more hexatonic than pentatonic and it is not the 3rd but the 2nd degree that is less represented in the tune.
Having said that, an alternative to playing all the c’s sharp on the Scottish pipe, is to replace them by the 2nd and 4th degrees (B and d), depending on the melofic movement, effectively playing a 3rd-degree-less/6th-less version of the tune.

The Gradys Of Ithaca

The Gradys of Ithaca are a large family of anti-war and social justice activists who are
also great supporters of Irish music and include several champion musicians. Their patriarch, John Grady Senior, was one of the Camden 28, whose 1973 trial was called by Supreme Court Justice William J. Brennan Jr. “one of the great trials of the 20th century”.

To support their work, Karan Casey often visits Ithaca, as do many other great musicians
from Ireland, including the band Skipper’s Alley. Paddy Cummins of that band, who’s handle here is Paahto, composed this reel in their honor and taught it to me a few days ago.

He varies it a lot, but here is the basic version of the reel. I’ve added a couple of the
double stops he plays on the tenor banjo. He says that it is important to get the fourth bar of the B part correct.

Re: The Gradys Of Ithaca

Hi all, thanks for posting the tune Mark. It’s bar 5 of the B-part that I have mentioned as important to follow and not repeat bar 1 instead. It flows better rather than a clumsy F#-d movement.

Regarding chords, here’s what I play:

Bar 1 and 5 |G - F|
Bar 3 and 7 |F - F|
Bar 4 |G - D|
Bar 8 |D7sus4 - G|
Note that the tune is in Gmix, not G major so the D chords suggested for bar 3 and 6 above would clash.

Bar 10 and 14 |Bb - C|, |F - C| or |Bb - Am| as suggested
Bar 12 | Am7 - D7sus4|
Bar 13 | G - F|
Bar 14 |D7sus4|

That keeps in line with the harmony that I intended when composing.

Get happy!

Bam! This tune is in my head yesterday and It seems like it is something really familiar, but I don’t know where it came from. Anyway, I like it. For some reason it reminds me of cabbage. My best friend’s dad had a lot of old mountain ways to him, and in the fall he would dig a hole and bury his extra heads of cabbage and they would be preserved til spring. If no one lays a title to it, I may claim composership and call it “ Bury the Cabbage.”

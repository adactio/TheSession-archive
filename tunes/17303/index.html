But of course, Schottische is German for Scottish. Strangely the dance seems to have originated in Bohemia, although the name indicates that it is Scottish in origin. In Sweden we call it schottis (a corruption of Schottische, I guess) but in Norway, they call it reinlender (Rhinelander)(!).

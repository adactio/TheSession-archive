Dr. Keith, Aberdeen

A strathspey in F major by J. Scott Skinner, in the Miller o’ Hirn collection.
Another of the F tunes set to go with Pamela Rose Grant, Lass of Corrie’s Mill, Carnie’s Canter, and Riptide that I learned from Barbara McOwen of Arlington, MA.
Notation Source: MacDonald - The Skye Collection (1887) Transcription: AK/Fiddler’s Companion

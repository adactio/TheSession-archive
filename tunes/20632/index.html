Two comments

Equator Light

I imagine I’ll be editing this a million times.

This is Equator Light off of Brian Finnegan’s ‘Hunger of the Skin’. It’s the eighth track (and final musical track) on the album. It is a nightmare to count between rubato, fermatas, the like, but I tried. We’ll see how it goes. Played in A major on a Mezzo A whistle. I’ll get a setting for D major once I get the rhythm cleaned up.

↳ Posted by Yans D on April 1st, 2021.

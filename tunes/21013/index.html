Eva, X:3

Ok, here’s how I think it should be notated. He plays it a little different every time through on every video I could find so this is based on the album version. It’s absolutely got 3 beats to the bar, you can hear that really clearly second time through on the album version, but the chord structure in the second part is a little syncopated.

I hadn’t heard this track for ages until I saw these dots posted yesterday but I’ve listened to it loads today. It’s not a session tune and I doubt many people will learn it but I think the recording of it is absolutely gorgeous. The lift when they start the second time through after the bridge is just mighty.

One comment

Brownieside Polka

Composed by Billy Miller of the Cheviot Ranters and recorded on their eponymous 1973 album on Topic records. I have transcribed this tune as it does not seem to be available as sheet or .abc elsewhere. The record is available of Spotify and is well worth a listen if you are interested in music played for dancing.

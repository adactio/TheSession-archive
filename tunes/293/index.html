The New Shillin’

A Scottish reel from “So Many Partings” and “Live Again” albums of Silly Wizard. This particular tune had been discovered by the fiddler of the band, Johnny Cunningham, in an old book of tunes. (that’s wrote on the back cover of the vinyl). Enjoy it!

The New Shilling

It is found in the MacFarlane manuscripts of 1740, and later in Gow as “The Boganuadh, or the New Bob”. No composer is noted.

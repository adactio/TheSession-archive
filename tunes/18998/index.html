Calum Campbell’s Caprice

A lovely Highland pipe tune played as a reel or what in modern Highland piping is often called a “hornpipe” (but having the timing of a traditional reel) that has a surprise in the 3rd part when C sharps appear. It was composed by Joe Wilson. The octave leaps fall right under the fingers of Highland pipers but can be challenging on other instruments.

Two comments

Trigonometry

Transcribed from a live performance by Crossharbour. For some reason I didn’t really clock this tune when I first heard the Crossharbour album, but I was listening to it again recently and it really grew on me. Great wee tune with a slightly mysterious feel to it. Crossharbour play it at a steady pace rather than fast.

↳ Posted by Aberfoyle Whistler on September 21st, 2019.

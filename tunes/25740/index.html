Bill Collins’ Hornpipe

This is transcribed from a handwritten music sheet given by the composer Collins to the late Jim Creaney, a well-known Northern Irish fiddler, who lived near Ballinderry in County Antrim. William J Collins himself lived in Portadown, County Armagh, and was Jim’s fiddle teacher in the 1950s.
No connection, I believe, to the Bill Collins of the well-known jig, also known as Dan Collins’ Fathers’s Jig: (https://thesession.org/tunes/2217)

The Weatherman

The Weatherman is a composition and the opening track from Alasdair White’s debut solo album An Clàr Geal. He wrote it for his father Donald White, who is obsessional about studying the weather and weather patterns on Lewis

Alasdair grew up on the Isle of Lewis in Scotland’s Outer Hebrides, a place where Gaelic language and music remain important influences on the culture.

Alasdair plays the fiddle in what might be called a “North-West” Scottish style, heavily derived from the bagpiping tradition.

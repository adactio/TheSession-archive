We Are Dancer

From Hanneke Cassel’s recording For Reasons Unseen. This tune has a quirky extra 2 two-beat pick-up to the second part. It gives the extra time back at the end - so all is well from the dancer’s perspective. A growly and driving tune!

There’s only 32 bars-worth of music when you play once through the tune. The extra 2 beats come between the A and B parts, but there’s 2 beats missing as you switch back to the A part. When you listen to it though, it feels pretty natural.

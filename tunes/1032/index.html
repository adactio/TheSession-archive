Re: The Little Heathy Hill / The Knuckeen Free / An Chnoicín Fraoich

X:1 setting has an unusual turn in that it has 12 bars. I suspect that is a mistake, but it is not clear where the setting came from.
O’Neill notes that An Chnoicín Fraoich was popular as a song and an air in Munster, especially Cork and Kerry, and that several settings of the melody had been printed (including in his Music of Ireland) but that his version in WSGM (336) was the first as a hornpipe.

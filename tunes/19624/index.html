Michael O’Brien’s

from a recording of a flute workshop with Dave Sheridan, he said he got the tune from Michael O’Brien a box player.

the jig appears also on Garadice’s album as a “Gan Ainm”, they have a neat variation which happens on the second c of the 4th bar on the B part which they play as a c natural (instead of the c sharp)

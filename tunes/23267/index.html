TIDGA, X:3

My house session started playing this tune and I fell in love with it. We ended up liking the live version Hanneke Cassel has teaches on Fiddle Video a little better than the album version, so I’ve transcribed that version here. It’s a little different than the other setting, and I’m sure it’s not perfect. That video is located here:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lZJ-cxdqPV0

I’ve only notated her first time through the A and B section. She plays it a little bit different the second time. Thanks to the folks who reviewed the transcription :) My friends and I have a theory about the title of the tune, which is an acronym. She plays this tune with “The Important Thing Is”. Put next together it might read “The important thing is today I don’t give a ….”. Who knows lol. BTW we’re playing this with the Lounge Bar and they go together wonderfully.
https://thesession.org/tunes/8853#setting53996

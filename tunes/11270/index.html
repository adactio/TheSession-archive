Re: The Brickyard

I taught this tune to Lisa for the Fleadh competition around 2005 never expecting it would later become a winning All Ireland jig. It’s is a hybrid of two Liddy jigs “The Brickyard” and the “Black Road”. The first and third parts are the first and second parts of the original Brickyard while the 2nd part is the 2nd part of the Black Road.

When My Love And I Parted

This is an air, so obviously it’s not meant to be played as an actual jig (though you could), but rather slowly, legato & drawn-out… it’s a “breakup song”, lol. Dark, very brooding, yet melancholy and kind of dreary song… not sure if it’s even in 6/8 or not. This is just my (very) loose transcription of what I hear Deirdre Scanlon singing with Solas.

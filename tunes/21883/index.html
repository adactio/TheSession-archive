Two comments

Miss Jane MacInnes-Dandeleith

Learned this from Gerald Trimble’s “First Flight”. Very fun and bouncy reel with some nice snaps in the B part, lending it a strathspey-esque vibe. The LP liner notes say he learned it from William Marshall’s 1822 Collection. His version is basically the same as the reference he cited but with a decent handful of minor changes. I personally like the way his version sits on the staff a little better but perhaps if I could go back in time and hear William Marshall play it I’d change my mind?

↳ Posted by Cloudling on May 28th, 2022.

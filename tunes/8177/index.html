Re: Reel Madrid

The tune has a Rumanian feel to it.
The mode is “major phrygian” (the regular phrygian mode modified so the 3rd degree (G# here, in the key of E) is the major third).
In E, it is also the relative mode to A harmonic minor.

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on January 19th, 2026.

Re: Reel Madrid

“major-phrygian” a.k.a. ‘Maqam Hijaz’ in the classical Arab terminology.

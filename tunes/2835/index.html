Third Part is from “Kitty O’Neil’s”

Padraig O‘Keeffe added third part to “The Smoky Chimney,” but he lifted from an old American “sand dance” called after the dancer Kitty O’Neil. O‘Keeffe may have gotten it out of one of the several tune books that included the old two-part version of Kitty’s tune, which was later expanded into the seven-part “Kitty O’Neil’s Champion Jig” (aka “Kitty O’Shea”).

The Smoky Chimney, X:3

Paul Brock’s version, minus some ornamentation and variation. He generally holds the quarter notes that appear at the ends of phrases for their whole value without ornamentation, which gives the tune a very old-fashioned feel, though I’ll often swap them for a pair of eighth notes with an F# to lead into the next bar.

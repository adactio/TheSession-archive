The Parting Tune

I wrote this tune for my final performance for my Masters in Music Composition in UL. The third part of the tune is loosely based on The Parting Glass (song), so that’s why I called it The Parting Tune.

Usually when I play the tune, I play it a few times and then repeat a motif from the 3rd part (dBe dBc | dBe dBc | dBe dBc …etc.) Then get slower and slower and then play a verse of The Parting Glass.

Also I wrote this on/with the fiddle so apologies if it doesn’t suit all instruments !

Meadhbh Hayes

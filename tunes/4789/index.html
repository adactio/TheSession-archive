Four comments

Sevens

Here is another Liz Carroll tune as requested by someone. It is the first track off her excellent Lost in The Loop album. Great tune, the second half is not repeated and is a little tricky for those of us who use plectrums due to the fast cross-string arpeggios Am pretty sure the transcription is 100% correct cos I checked it in a convertor before inputting it as Jeremy wisely advised me.

Enjoy

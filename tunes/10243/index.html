“The Baddeck Gathering” ~ C: Pipe Major Alex MacDonald

born Cape Breton, 1893
~ the tune was composed in commemoration of a regatta held there in 1932 (Shears). ~ courtesy of ~

The Fiddler’s Companion
http://www.ibiblio.org/fiddlers/index.html

Da Auld Resting Chair

I have Tom Anderson’s CD The Silver Bow with me today, and according to his liner notes he wrote this tune upon finding, in the ruins of his grandfather’s cottage, the remains of the “resting chair” his grandfather used to sit on while teaching Tom to play the fiddle. Rather a touching story.

↳ Posted by Richard D Cook on March 29th, 2020.

Da Auld Resting Chair, X:4

This setting in D allows the entire first part to be correctly played on the uilleann pipes, keyless Irish flute, or whistle. The second part has to be taken down the octave and folded, otherwise there will be 3rd octave notes.

Playing this setting on a medium/mezzo/alto G whistle will allow you to play along with others playing it in its normal key of G.

↳ Posted by Richard D Cook on March 29th, 2020.

Da Auld Resting Chair, X:5

This is arranged for the Scottish pipes (Great Highland, Scottish Smallpipes, Border Pipes).

The “Low G” in bars 4 and 20 should be G# but the pipes lack that note. You can play the pipe’s ordinary G, or substitute bar 12 for bars 4 and 20.

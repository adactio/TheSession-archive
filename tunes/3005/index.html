Re: Cap And Bells

This is my all-time favorite tune that I learned from David Brewer and a recording of his from a Molly’s Revenge album. I’ve also heard Dervish perform this tune. I’ve seen it called ‘The Cap and Bells’ and ‘On the Road’ or ‘Out on the Road’. This is a very special tune and whenever I play it, people enjoy it and ask what it is. It’s such a beautiful and happy tune!

The Diamond

A reel composed by Robert “Red Rob” Mackintosh, published in his 1783 book “Airs Minuets Gavotts and Reels.” I wasn’t familiar with Mackintosh before, but I discovered an album by Concerto Caledonia that was entirely dedicated to his music and it’s all quite good! The collection is an interesting mix of more “folky” and more “classical-style” dance music.

Here’s Concerto Caledonia’s rendition: https://youtu.be/9ufZEs7MpO8?si=ZMEA4CmarpZ60Olq

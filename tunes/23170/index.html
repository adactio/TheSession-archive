Paddy Kelly’s

Joe Burke recorded that tune in 2001 accompanied by Anne Conroy. This is a composition of Paddy Kelly, from Aughrim Co. Galway, and not of Paddy “Nenagh” O‘ Brien as Norbeck wrote in his transcription #657). Paddy Kelly was leader of The Aughrim Slopes Ceili Band. (according to the own words of Eileen O’Brien : https://www.facebook.com/groups/IrlandeTradFr/posts/2408940285941069/?comment_id=2410033735831724&__cft__[0]=AZU8nWxDVuK7Hq3LL4V1MgB5XLF1e9n1_swMOqT5cTLswEgph2AakN_h6n041fCfHg5yIGx3MSy6zmhf_JeYAAvFr1floC6ied4iMMxRUNac5_XFV3KbjgsdPid6bJTplld1b-rSjk_3CfMhrXiwF7FZ&__tn__=R]-R )

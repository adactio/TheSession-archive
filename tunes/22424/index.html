Two comments

The Low Ditch

This reel was composed by the excellent accordion player John Redmond. It features on his second album ‘Box Sets’, which showcases his own tunes. Born in Ballindaggin in Co.Wexford , John was already a greatly admired and fleadh prize winning player before he moved to New York, where he is very well known and highly regarded, (like his original Wexford mentor, Tom Dunne). This is the first of three reels from the album that I’ve notated and I’m uploading as part of The Wexford Tune Project. There’ll be lots more to come from John’s impressive stable of traditional compositions. John is returning to live in County Wexford shortly, so people here will get a taste again of how fine a musician he is.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on November 5th, 2022.

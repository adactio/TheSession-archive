Fourteen comments

A nice jig composed by the renouned American fiddler Liz Carrol. I found it on her site, where you can get a bunch of her hand-written tunes. The long notes (3) I roll. I haven’t had it long enough to have added any other ornimentation. Enjoy!

The Champaign Jig Goes To Columbia, X:2

I commonly write out tunes without double endings by using pick-up notes instead.
That seemed to match what I hear being played. I also tweaked a couple of spots to more
closely match, again what I believe I hear. Now there’s no doubt that there’s quite a
bit a variation going on and so to simply play this as written again and again would
also not match what Liz Carroll does with the tune.

Re: The Champaign Jig Goes To Columbia

In response to Rover (albeit 7 years later).. Liz is from Chicago, Illinois… Champaign is where University of Illinois is. That’s a couple hours or so from Chicago. From a geographic standpoint… she may have a gig there and then to Columbia, MO… which is where University of Missouri is…these are longtime rivals for sports. Now, I don’t know Liz or if that was the juxtaposition she wanted to propose…but it could be the case. She’s as much a tune writer, of music, as a thinker…so I think there is a juxtaposition of the two cities and perhaps a trip or set of gigs, maybe. My two cents.

Re: The Champaign Jig Goes To Columbia

From collected:
The Champaign Jig Goes to Columbia
I was not sure what to call this (I think my frst jig) but when I
went out playing it, it needed a name. I named it for whatever
town I was in, so Champaign is Champaign, Illinois, and
Columbia is Columbia, Missouri, and the tune has been named
for many more towns. —Lost in the Loop

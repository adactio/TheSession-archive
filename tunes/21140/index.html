Trip To Cheltenham

This was one of many tunes shared at ‘virtual sessions’ over Zoom that have been taking place over the past year or so, hosted by the Lewes Folk Club (Sussex, UK) but including participants both local and international. (This one played by Angela Fairhead, English concertina). I’m adding it because it’s a good tune, and because another Sessioneer was looking for a companion piece for ‘Cheltenham Spa’ for a forthcoming gig in that very town: perhaps this might do?

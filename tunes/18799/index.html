July 28th

A stunning beautiful air written by the Irish piano player Ryan Molloy and played with Fergal Scahill on their recent recording, One Day: November Woods, available here:
https://ryanmolloyfergalscahill.bandcamp.com/album/one-day-november-woods

Play it as slow as possible.

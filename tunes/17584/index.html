Three comments

Iitin Tiltu

Traditional Finnish Polkka. Apparently it’s played in Gminor sometimes as well.

AnDro, “Tribe”, first tune:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uINYwUsLggc

Pretty sure they play it a little differently than setting X1, for instance, some of the notes are lengthened (eg., not so many E’s in a row). Will post a setting closer to theirs’ later on.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on December 28th, 2018.

Iitin Tiltu Polkka, X:3

Setting in Bmin.

Updated X2 to be closer to AnDro’s setting. They don’t play any repeats between the three parts, which works well because each part is quite repetitive.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on November 2nd, 2019.

Iitin Tiltu Polkka, X:4

A few more variations.

There is a song version of this tune, with Finnish lyrics (see: https://lyricstranslate.com/en/hiski-salomaa-iitin-tiltu-lyrics.html ). Some recordings: Jani Uhlenius, Ritva Oksanen, and, Mojakka.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on November 2nd, 2019.

“Journey To Bal” ~ hornpipe ~ C: Enda Seery

I prefer to do a transcription from source, so I have now given one for Enda’s own playing, but since he has added some things here in the comments I’ve included some of his other variations. Enda plays this straight, akin to American hornpipes and as some othes like to play them, the reason who so many flings have ended up becoming reels. I prefer it with triplets and a little more of the bounce of the dance in it… ;-) I’ll add a take on it that way later…

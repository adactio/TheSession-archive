Holtwood Reel

Author Jess Arrowsmith of Hekaty. They’ve played this at the Haddenham Ceilidh. They do it as a sort of round, each musician starting at a different point in the tune.

↳ Posted by Innocent Bystander on May 23rd, 2005.

Holtwood Reel

wa-hey, I wrote it, nice to know that other people like it well enough to want to play it!
My only comment is - we published a tune book with this in and I made a mistake in it! (d’oh!) Sorry, Innocent Bystander, if you got the dots from my tune book in the first place then it was my mistake! I will be adding a comment as such on our website www.hekety.co.uk very soon and it’ll be corrected in our next print run. Any we sell at the moment get corrected by hand by me, but we hadn’t noticed the problem til after Haddenham!
The correction is as follows:
What’s written already is fine as far as it goes but the end of the B music has two alternate endings - a 1st time version & a 2nd time version. The 1st time version is as written already, the 2nd time version is

ABcA B2A2|a2g2 f2e2|d2c2 B2A2||

in place of the final 3 bars. I’m not sure how to do 1st time 2nd time bars in abc though.

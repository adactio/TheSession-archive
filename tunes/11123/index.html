Miss Crawford’s Reel

This is a great tune in Kerr’s First Collection of Merry Melodies, attributed to one A. McAlpin. Really nice fiddle tune, and ideal for practising string crossing with.

↳ Posted by martin clarke on March 16th, 2011.

Miss Crawford’s Reel

This can be found in the Donegal tradition under the title “Ril Bidi o Bearnas” (“The Bluebells of Bearnas”) and also “Con McGinley’s Reel”. Source for this information: ‘Donegal Fiddlers Summer School Music Tutor, Vol.2’

The Sunflower Schottische

This is notated from the 2013 album For the Sake of Auld Decency by the group Morga. The tune comes from a different time, when pre-jazz American music was all the rage in the UK and Ireland. Skylark also recorded a terrific setting of the tune.

Although it has some vague similarities to Herbert Rowland’s 1886 “With the Tide Schottische” (recorded as “The Sunflower Dance” by Vess Ossman in c1908), it’s a different tune.

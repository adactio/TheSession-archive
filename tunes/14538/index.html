Michael Wiggins in Ireland

Found in Harding’s All-Round Collection of Jigs, Reels and Country Dances, New York, 1905, nr 19 [in C major]; previously presented in Preston’s Twenty-Four Country Dances for 1801 [in D major].
According to the Traditional Tune Archive, the origin of this tune is to be found in London.

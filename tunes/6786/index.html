Cailin Na Gruige Donna

Obviously not a walotz but an air, (Why can’t we add airs to the menu/)This version is similar to the McGoldrick version on Upside Down. It’s a traditional air with some lovely variations. Have a listen to his version on the CD if you can.

↳ Posted by JACKB on February 10th, 2007.

Why no airs in menu

As I understand it, the tunes are organized according to time signature; airs can be written in several time signatures so one category wouldn‘’t cover them all.

O’Connor’s Slide No 2

This is one of a pair of slides I had from Nailus O‘Connor, accordion player, Knocknagoshel, Co. Kerry, in the ’80’s. (See also ‘O’Connor’s slide No 1’). I’d be glad to know the proper title, if anyone has it.

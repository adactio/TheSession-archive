The Basement, X:2

The Basement Reel, as taught by Brian Holleran himself at the 2025 Swannanoa Gathering Celtic Week. Brian’s music teacher was East Galway flute master Mike Rafferty (who passed away in 2011) and Brian found the tune on one of his old lesson tapes. He has searched for it in recordings and asked knowledgeable friends, but it has not turned it up anywhere, so it may be unique to Rafferty -- perhaps gotten from a friend who composed it, perhaps composed by Rafferty himself. On the tape, there was no name connected with the tune, so Brian called it after Rafferty’s basement, where lessons and memorable sessions were held.

Brian noted that dropping down to the low D a bit early (measure A-2) was common in Rafferty’s playing. His transition between high and low octaves shifted around the center of that measure.

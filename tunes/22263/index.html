Dance Above The Rainbow

Composed by Ronan Hardiman, on piano. This is from the original ‘Feet of Flames’ show, & was one of the new tune sets added to the existing ‘Lord of the Dance’ show. After the jig is played through twice, the fiddler(s) comes in with a simplistic but honestly really fun little reel in E, changing the tempo in a nice way. Ronan Hardiman has also been credited with this reel. Here’s the ABC…

T: Tune no. 2, Dance Above the Rainbow Reel
C: Ronan Hardiman
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Emaj
|:E2 ~E2 GBeB|cBGB ~B3 G|A2 BA cABA|(3AAA cA Bcde|
E2 ~E2 GBeB|cBGB ~B3 B|cBce dcBA|1GBFB E2 GF:|2Bedf e3 f||
|:g3 g g2 fe|fgfe d2 cd|e3 f e2 cB|ABAG F2 ef|
~g3 f g2 fe|fgfe d3 B|cBce dcBA|1GBFB E2 ef:|2Bedf ecBG||

The third & final “tune” after this reel is, I suppose, the “dancing on the rainbow” part. It’s got Irish dancing in the live show, but no actual instruments seem to playing…trad instruments, that is. Keyboards and synths, yes. It’s a very space-age, funky strange tune. Could be re-interpreted as a reel I’m sure, I just don’t care for it personally. haha. Sounds like massive keyboard effects or something. So in this three-minute excursion, we’ve gone from the fields of Ireland, dancing at the pot o’ gold; then dancing a reel up the same rainbow, to finally going to outer space and losing our minds via a rainbow. I think. Hm.

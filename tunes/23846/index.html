Yer Pig’s On Fire

By Dave Jolly. You can hear him playing it in a pair: Hamish McTavish / Yer Pig’s On Fire at:
https://davejolly.bandcamp.com/track/hamish-mctavish-yer-pigs-on-fire
In the CD notes he says:
A pair of Hornpipes composed many years ago. The first is for Hamish, an unusual little guinea pig of a breed known as a ‘Sheltie’. He was the cause of much mirth when observed ascending the staircase - he had very long hair and, well, looked like a mop.
Sound checking at a gig in an old barn. we noticed the hog roast suddenly erupting in a ball of flames. The chaps in charge were standing at the bar paying no attention. I shouted out, “Hey mate, your pigs on fires” and thus named the second of these tunes which I had composed that very day They extinguished the poor beast with a hosepipe and we ended up with par-boiled roast pork.

The Road To Wexford

This tune from the album ‘Raven’ by John Williams and Dean Magraw was composed by Dean. An email from John Williams says the tune was composed after a memorable trip down ‘The Road to Wexford’ by Dean and his son on their way to visit with John and Pip Murphy at Cullenstown.
Here are John and Dean performing the tune:

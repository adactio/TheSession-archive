Three comments

The Glories Of Spring

Another obscure hornpipe from O’Neill’s. This one sits in the rather unusual key of Gmin at the very bottom of the fiddle range and has a darkly nautical and somewhat unsettled feel. The title is a bit misleading in that way.

↳ Posted by j_clark on August 5th, 2020.

The Glories Of Spring, X:2

The Glories Of Spring modified for whistle from Gm to Bm and some parts up an octave.

Hm, in Irish “féas” (gen. “féis” (pronounced something like “fesh”) means “beard”. “Punch in the face” would probably be more like “Buille san aghaidh”.

Re: Punch In The Fèis

Yes but Eilidh Shaw is Scottish and in Gaelic “fèis” is pronounced something like “faysh”.
I would suggest the title is a pun on Fèis Rois (commonly called The Fèis), a charity which promotes traditional arts and Gaelic culture in the Highlands of Scotland.

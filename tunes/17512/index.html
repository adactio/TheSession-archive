Two comments

My Sorrow Is Greater Than I Can Tell

From John Keane of Kilkee, 1876. Originally in 6/8, marked “slow and tender”.

↳ Posted by Siúlóir on November 27th, 2018.

My Sorrow Is Greater Than I Can Tell, X:2

This is my three part arrangement of the slow air on pipes, each with elements of increasing constrictions and pauses. The tune is a less adorned variant of There’s An End To My Sorrow, and There’ll Ne’er Be Peace Till Jamie Comes Home.

Calum Breugach

J.P. Cormier does a great guitar rendition of this tune on Howie MacDonalds’s “Y2Keilidh” CD. He follows it with Christy Campbell, Mason’s Apron, Sean MacGuire’s Reel, and Picnic Reel. I love it.

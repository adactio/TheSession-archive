Re: The Rambling Rake

O’Neill has two settings: the first in his 1850 book (#1785, also in DMI.959) as a set dance. The settings are similar to those posted here. He attributes it to Hartnett, which I take to mean Michael Hartnett from West Cork, mentioned in his Introduction to DMI. (There is one difference: Hartnett’s was in A major, 3 #, while DMI has one#, so A dorian.)
The second version (in WSGM #305) he took from the Rice-Walsh Mss. implying it can from Jerry Breen from North Kerry. This one appears to be in Em (one #) in the first part but the other two parts follow the form of other settings (e.g. starting efed d2g2) It is listed as a reel.

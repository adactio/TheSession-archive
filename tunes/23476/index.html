Granuaile’s Castle

This is the third of my father’s reels I have posted here. I posted the other two, Mixed Blooms and The Cottage on the Hill nearly 20 years ago. I intended to post more at that time, but like Rip Van Winkle, I went up into the hills drank a few tankards, played some skittles, and snoozed apparently for twenty years. I’m back. My father, Martin Ruane, was a Mayo fiddler, born in 1894 and died in 1986. He was born in Aghaward and spent most of his early years there. The family then moved to Straide and he lived there until he married in 1927. After moving around a bit, they settled in Castlebar which became home for the rest of his life, although he did long solo runs to Kiltimagh and Galway City. He composed many reels, but most sad to say have been lost. He had become old and we were all away and things got lost. I was in Canada then and when I returned, I was told that his only possessions were in an old suitcase retrieved from a boarding house. There I found an old manuscript that I had bought for him in the 60s so that he could write his compositions in them rather than on scraps of paper and brown paper bags. Unfortunately, fewer than 20 were written down, named and dated. I was surprised. Something he said in his final years when interviewed by a collector of traditional tunes for RTE was a clue to part of the mystery. He said he tired of writing down good copy, because he made lots of mistakes. It seems he felt that everything that went into the manuscript had to be perfect. He didn’t realize that all his work could go in there, including mistakes, crossed out errors. restarts, etc. He dated this one 11-4-65 and Granuaile is Grainnne Ni Mhaille or Grace O‘Malley the famous pirate queen of Connaught who lived in the 1500s and gave Lizzie the First a few headaches. My father being half an O’Malley was very proud of her defiance when the West was at a low ebb. I hope you enjoy it. My name is the same as his. When I was young, I was called Marty and later at College Mairtin. I’ll stick with Mairtin to avoid confusion because I penned a few tunes myself and I wouldn’t want chaff mixed up with the grain. I hope you enjoy.

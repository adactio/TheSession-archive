Mick McGarry’s #2

Popular for a while in Sydney/Canberra sessions a few years back. This could be viewed as being in Dmaj or Bmin depending on how it’s backed (you could end on a chord of Bm, but I personally like to end on a chord of G). I arbitrarily chose Bmin. Goes after #1 https://thesession.org/tunes/3202. These are possibly Aussie tunes but I’m not sure.

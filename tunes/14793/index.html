Camhanaich Air Machair

Lovely wee tune by Donald Shaw, part of the soundtrack for the BBC Alba drama “Gruth is Uachdar” (“Crowdie and Cream”) on which he collaborated with Capercaillie bandmate Charlie McKerron. This ABC is for the version on Capercaillie’s “Choice Language”.
Meanwhile, Duncan Chisholm plays it in A flat on “Canaich”, and in A on his concert album… cheers, man.

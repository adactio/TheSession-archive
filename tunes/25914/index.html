Two comments

Andraste

“Andraste” is a reel by Tartalo Music, included in their 2022 album Izei. It is part of a set called “Azeriari”, which means “To the Fox” in Basque.

This set is comprised of a traditional Irish slip jig called ‘The kid in the mountain’, the so-known ‘Morrison’s Jig’ and a Reel composed by Tartalo Music called ‘Andraste’. We decided to name the track Azeriari, meaning ‘To the Fox’ in the Basque language, since the first day we started rehearsing this tune in the Montseny mountain, a fox appeared and stayed in front of us while we played the whole set.

The name originates from a real moment: while rehearsing the tune in the Montseny mountains, a fox suddenly appeared and stayed for a while. That encounter left such an impression that the musicians renamed the piece after it.

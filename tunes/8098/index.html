Six comments

Sally’s Jig

I transposed this Traditional Newfoundland Jig from an old Cassette tape for a friend to learn. I thought it was a nice little jig and would make it my first tune submitted. here I’m pretty sure it was played by Minnie White, a well known Newfounland Box Player. It was recorded in the Key of C, but I found that it worked well in G.

↳ Posted by mellow_bellows on December 30th, 2007.

Codroy Jig

On one of Dick Hewitt’s albums this tune is titled “Codroy Jig”, possibly because Minnie White once lived in Codroy Valley and this tune is associated with her.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on May 27th, 2024.

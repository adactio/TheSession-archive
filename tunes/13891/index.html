Two comments

Cottar’s Wife

From the Athole Collection where it is shown as a single reel keyed in A with the Gs marked natural. I thought it sounded pretty good as a 3 part tune. Repeating the last part makes it Contra Dance friendly.

I did a bit of research on “Cottar” and found that it is an Anglo-Saxon word for “Cottager” meaning someone who lives in a cottage. In Norman England they were a class of subsistence farming folk below serfs and above slaves. See the wikipedia site and click on “villani” for more info.

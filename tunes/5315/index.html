One comment

The Quarff Lasses

I learned this beautiful tune off the Shetland group Filska’s debut album released 10 years ago. It was written by Joyce Reid, who appears as a guest on the recording, for the original members of the group: Jenna and Bethany Reid, and Gemman Wilson. It seems the lasses recorded it on their latest album too.

As a flute player, I’ve learned and play the tune in a different key:

Raindrops

Track 3 from Deiseal’s “The Long Long Note”. Cormac Breatnach plays it at eye-watering speed. I’ve created this (in response to Yans D’s recent request) using the Capo app and am currently bashing out the notes at 50% tempo. Hoping to get to 75% some day but 100% is probably beyond my reach! You can play along in this key using an A whistle. Note that lines 3 and 4 are basically a repeat of lines 1 and 2 but I’ve included them separately because there is a small difference second time through.

Rain Drops

A tune composed by Cormac Breatnach when he was playing in Deiseal with Niall O‘Callanain and Paul O’Driscoll. The tune is also found on the first recording of Sonamus (with Breatnach).

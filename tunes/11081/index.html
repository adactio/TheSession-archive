Here is my transcription

I like that time. The booklet says it’s beeing written by Jim Murray and Dezi Donnelly:
“This is a tune I composed on the journey from Macroom to the studio in Galway. There were delays on the road before Freemount and I was forced to go a number of of back roads. By the time I was back on track on the right road this melody was in my head. When I arrived later that day we recorded it after a lovely line at the end of the tune suggested by Dezi” (Jim).
It is played in Fmin/Fmaj on the recording and I choosed to transcribe it in Emin/Emaj ’cause it is easier to play that way…

Re: Larry Grogan

Larry Grogan was a gentleman piper from County Wexford who lived in the early decades of the 18th century. His family’s ‘seat’ was Johnstown castle just outside of Wexford. He is credited with the composition of this jig and a number of other tunes including Allie Croker. This tune may well be the ‘source’ tune for the ‘Humours of Ennistymon’ family of related tunes, Coppers and Brasses etc…his compositions were (posthumously) published in Britain from the 1740s onwards.

Larry Grogan, X:5

This would appear to be the earliest published version of the tune. It was included in John Neale’s third collection of country dances published in Dublin in 1734. It’s titled ‘Lary Grogan’ (probably a printer’s error). It’s notated in the original publication in the key of A major, but I’ve uploaded it here transposed to G for comparison purposes with other settings.

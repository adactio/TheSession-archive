Two comments

Paranoia

I’ve been learning some of the cracking tunes from Shooglenifty’s Venus in Tweed and after learning and transcribing Paranoia I thought I’d come share what I’ve done here.

I’m a mandolin player so it’s transcribed how I’d play on mandolin but I think it’s pretty much spot on for the song - would liked to have used more repeats but I wanted to include the slight variations that I can hear on the album recording.

Also, as a guitar player I’ve added the chords I like to play along with the tune :) Comments/feedback/alterations welcome!

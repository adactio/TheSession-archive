Tap The Barrel

From Breandan Breathnach’s collection “Ceol Rince na hEireann”

↳ Posted by dafydd on February 28th, 2003.

Yep, this one’s in Breathnach’s Volume 2, collected from the playing of Julia Clifford.

I play this a bit different, as follies:

K: D mixolydian
|: D2 (3FED ADFA | cAGE {G}EDCE | D2 (3FED ADFA | cAGE ADDC |
| D2 (3FED ADFA | cAGE CD (3EFG | D2 (3FED ADFA |1 cAGE A2 GE :| 2 cAGE ADFA ||
|: [d3D3] e faed | cdef (3gag fe | ~d3 e faed | cAGE ADFA |
| d^cde faed | (3Bcd ef ~g3 e | ~f3 d (3efg ed |1 cAGE ADDA :|2 cAGE ADDC ||

The A part alwaysreminds me of Toss the Feathers, and the B part shares lots of similarities with Bantry Lasses and Miss Glavin’s. Variations comes easy by mxing and matching bits of these different tunes, as long as you don’t lose the thread of Tap the Barrel.

↳ Posted by Will Harmon on January 2nd, 2004.

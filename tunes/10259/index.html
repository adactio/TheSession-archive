My Friend Buddy

Here’s the version of “My Friend, Buddy”, composed by Dan Hughie MacEachern for his friend Buddy MacMaster, from the long out-of-print first book of Dan Hughie’s compositions, from a photo posted today on Facebook by Dan Hughie’s grand-niece Jackie Dunn MacIsaac. (I would ordinarily use 1st and 2nd endings for the B part and have four bars per line, but this is how it appears in the book.)

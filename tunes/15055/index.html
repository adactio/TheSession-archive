Cider Street

A tune from Nuala Kennedy’s CD ‘Tune In’, composed by Iain Macleod. The tune, about Calle Gascona in Oviedo, Asturias is “a street famous for its cider bars, and the scene of many a drunken interceltic interlude.”

Cider Street appears in the CDs final track as the 2nd tune in a set called ‘Five Mile Town’. Probably, but not certainly, Kennedy plays the tune in Bm on a C whistle, rather than as written in Am/C.

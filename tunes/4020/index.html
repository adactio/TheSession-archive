Two comments

Mickey the Mauler

I learned this jig off Tap Room Trio’s album. It’s obviously related to “Connie the Soldier” but sufficiently different to have another title, I think. It seems Joe Flanagan recorded it.

I love the old-fashioned feel of the tune. You can listen to Tap Room Trio playing the tune: http://www.claddaghrecords.com/www/product.asp?pID=1596&cID=17&c=262837

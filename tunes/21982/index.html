Two comments

Paddy Hayes’ Reel Number 2

This is the second of two unnamed reels from this Youtube clip of Paddy Hayes playing at a concert in Leitrim some years ago:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTfCo5VeuTk
It’s stated in the comments that they’re both compositions of Paddy’s. Neither are on either of his albums, so perhaps some one may be able to provide names for them. Paddy seems to have named his other tunes, often with Wexford titles ( The Saltees reel, Piercestown hornpipe, Tuskar Rock etc.) or personal titles (Polly’s favourite, referring to his mother; Patricia’s wedding waltz etc).

↳ Posted by John E Roche on June 27th, 2022.

Re: Sweet Statia

I published Paddy Hayes‘ known compositions in my book, ’The Wexford Tunebook’. In compiling it, I was delighted to have a good deal of contact with Paddy’s daughters, Patricia (Switzerland) and Ann Marie (Leeds). Ann Marie was able to provide the correct name for this reel, which I’ve just updated. Paddy named the tune for his aunt, Anastatia (Statia).

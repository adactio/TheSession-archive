Four comments

Taste Of Gaelic

A fun Cape Breton reel. Not sure who wrote it and when, but it is played by many younger generation fiddlers, as well as the older generation who are still kicking. Also known by the Scottish Gaelic name of Blasad na Gàidhlig.

↳ Posted by Tate on March 9th, 2014.

It reminds me very much of the 2-part “old” version of “Lord Gordon’s” which was recorded by Dolores Keane and “The Reel Union” - I think “Dow” might have posted it here at one time.

Three comments

Cuckoo’s Nest

Barndance/polka from The Duncan McFarlane Band’s album All Rogues & Villains. Unsure who composed it, and it’s a different tune from any of the other Cuckoo’s Nests listed.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on June 16th, 2019.

Cuckoo’s Nest, X:2

A few variations.

Two more that works well are in the 4th/8th bars of each part:
- gB d/B/c A2 AX
- gB d/c/B A2 AX
where X = E or ^g/a

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on June 16th, 2019.

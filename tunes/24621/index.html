Two comments

Meal Do Bhrògan

Another Scottish puirt-à-beul song suggested by Arianna Fox.
Those metric changes in the second part are pretty exciting.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hFml7XCw8iI
I put it in the common key of Amix but in the Mànran live they’re playing in Cmix.

↳ Posted by Yoann on August 15th, 2024.

Meal Do Bhrògan, X:4

In the B part with the changing time signatures, instead of making the bars 4/4 then 3/4, I personally prefer to put it in 7/4. It makes no difference at all but I just prefer it that way, is all.

Five comments

Battle Swing

Composed by two brothers, Fred & Jean-Charles Guichen.

This is a basic version of the melody, being roughly a simple combination of versions from a few recordings: Pecsaetan Morris, a few of the brothers’ (Guinchen, Ar Re Yaouank), and Jess & Richard Arrowsmith. There’s loads of variations and alternative phrases which fit the tune, many of which are played by the previously listed groups.

The Guichen brothers play it down in Amin on their albums.

The Fred Guichen Trio:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v7JnS0CDNGM

Madonna Groove

I think someone might have requested this a wee while back. It’s from the “Renegade” CD by Sharon Shannon, Michael McGoldrick, Dezi Donnelly and Jim Murray. The sleeve notes say it came from the playing of Karen Tweed and flute player Madonna Herron - possibly hence the name, - but the composer is not known.
Bars 1, 3, 5 and 7 are really interchangeable variations, and change every time they go through the tune. Please yourself.
Not my favourite CD by any means, because of the content and [ over- ]arrangements rather than the playing, which is excellent, but I do like this particular track.

Re: The Road To Thurles

Bon melodie! It’s a nice tune, lovingly played by Ms Byrne.

Your transcription is good, but you might clean up your abcs so the beams in the staff notation are more standard. For example, in the |d2 BG DGBG| measure, placing a space between the “BG” and “DGBG” prevents the beam from tying all those eighth notes together. Also makes it easier to read the abcs themselves.
And since the tune resolves to A, with one sharp, the key/mode should be Ador.

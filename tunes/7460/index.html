Four comments

“A Country Wedding” ~ D, F or G ~ pick your key

Yes, I know, I had told a few folks I had slides to contribute, well, that’s true, but seeing as there have been several in the last few days I decided to consider that statement made months ago by someone that all the slip jigs in the world were already on site here. Anyway, not sure, but I don’t think this one has been here before, but I have been wrong in the past and guilty of duplication…

If anyone wants a posh name for this little 3-parter I have seen it here:

“A Northern Lass: Traditional Dance Music from North-West England”
compiled and edited by Jamie Knowles
Dave Mallinson Publications, 1995
ISBN: 1-899512-16-0

Page 7: “Arcadian Nuptials” ~ in the key of F Major

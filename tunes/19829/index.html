Three comments

The Blue Angel

This is one of many tunes that I got in manuscript form from the late Frank McCollam of Ballycastle, Co. Antrim, many years ago. Many of the tunes he sent are now well known, but this one, dated 1960, seems to be quite unknown in present day musical circles. He actually spells the title “The Blue Angle”, but my recollection is that that is a misspelling. On the top of the manuscript slip he wrote “B.B.C. R.E. by S. McGuire. May 1953. Composed by Sean McLaughlin.” I take this to mean that it had been played on BBC and Radio Éireann radio programs by the late great Seán McGuire, and that it was a composition of the then well known Co. Antrim fiddle player, Seán Mac Lochlainn, who had been All-Ireland senior fiddle champion at the recent Fleadh Cheoil na hÉireann in Longford in 1958. I cannot be sure if the 1953 date refers to the date of composition or the date of the broadcasts. Possibly the latter: meaning that that was when McCollam transcribed it, having heard it on the radio. There is a jig of this same title, which also had been played by Seán McGuire, but it bears no relationship to this original hornpipe. I would be glad to find out if anyone else knows this hornpipe, or any other compositions of the late Seán Mac Lochlainn.

Re: The Blue Angel

Oh wow!
I know this Seán, thank you for posting it.
I played with Seán’s brother Jim McGuire for quite a few years and it was one of his ‘pieces’.
The history you give is absolutely spot on. My great late friend and master fiddler, Jimmy McHugh, took the All Ireland Final in ’57, beating Seán and Brendan McGlinchey; they were great rivals and friends.
Jim McGuire lived in Lancashire and had all Seán’s repertoire and, personally I think, was the better fiddle player.
There were 4 of us; Jim, Celia (then Joyce) Briar, harp, Gordon Johnstone, from Belfast,(finger picking banjo, genius at all strings) and myself. We played all over the North of England, and sometimes took dancers on special nights.
I distinctly remember Jim playing this hornpipe.
Thank you for bringing back such memories of happy times!

All the best
Brian x

Great to hear that, Brian. I also got a tape from Frank McCollam of Seán Mac Lochlainn, the following year, 1961, which contains some more tunes that look like compositions of his, “Golden Shadows” (which was published without giving the authorship by Josephine Keegan), “McLaughlin’s Jig”, “McLaughlin’s Dream”, and his own arrangement of “The Moving Clouds”. I have not written out these tunes. If you have copies of them, it might be a good idea to put them up on this platform.

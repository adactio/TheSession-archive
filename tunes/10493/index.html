Melting Snow

This lovely reel is played by Orla Harrington on her CD “Melting Snow”. Orla told me she dound it with another name in a Paddy O’Brien (Tipperary) book of tunes. In this book it is called the Duharra Reel. Hope you are going to enjoy it.
Dominique

Paddy O’Brien

This reel was composed by Paddy O‘Brien (The definitive collection of the music of Paddy O’Brien, Eileen O‘Brien).Paddy O’Brien called it The Duharra Reel.

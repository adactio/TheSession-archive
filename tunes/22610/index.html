The Wizard

This tune, composed by Adam Sutherland, is dedicated to Angus R Grant of Shooglenifty fame who passed away in 2016.
I’ve notated the tune in 6/8 for ease of reading, but it’s not a jig. The quavers are played even and the tempo is somewhere around 70-80bpm. I’ve added possible basic guitar chords for an idea of the underlying harmony.

You can hear it here:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gtjxtuDem0U
The setting on “some other land” is a bit faster:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0hyWoEa3Hu4

This tune was a favourite of Fatma Neilson, for whom Adam composed Fatma’s Waltz:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9M64c08vzPc

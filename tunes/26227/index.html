Two comments

Katie Ann Cameron’s

From Paddy O’Brien’s (Offaly) Tune Collection, volume three, jig #63.
He calls this one Jerry Holland’s #3. I found it in Jerry Holland, The Second Collection
at #229 as Katie Ann Cameron’s in A major. Paddy often changes the key of tune to better
suit play on his B/C box.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on December 30th, 2025.

Katie Ann Cameron’s, X:2

Here is the original in A major from Paul Cranford’s book
Jerry Holland, The Second Collection. page 85, tune #229.
Paddy O’Brien’s setting in G is much easier on the concertina,
but fiddler’s probably will prefer the setting in A.
(And, it might be good in A on a C#/D box.)

One comment

Late Summer

This one was written by Olaf Sickmann and it’s on his new album New Irish Tin Whistle Tunes. According to the notes:

‘’A lovely warm summer evening and the tin whistle music of my Irish friend Enda Seery inspired me to write this little tune.‘’

Seven comments

I’ve considered posting this tune before, but was too embarassed, partly because of a passing resemblance to a certain well known Irish traditional tune by the Penguin Cafe Orchestra and partly because it’s just a downright silly tune. Last night, I added an extra bar to the B-part (it was originally even sillier) and this morning, came up with a downright silly title for it, which seemed somehow to justify it.

I don’t expect to be hearing it in sessions (unless I’m uncouth enough to play it myself), but I feel thta by posting it here, I’m getting shot of it. If anyone else feels like playing it, that’s their problem.

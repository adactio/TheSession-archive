Seaman’s Hornpipe

This is my transcription of the “Seaman’s Hornpipe” recorded by Longford fiddlers Jim Clarke and Frank Quinn, accompanied by Eileen White on piano. It was recorded in New York on 21st December 1935. The first part is cognate with “The Bee’s Wing” but the remaining parts are different. I have not been able to identify this tune. It was recorded in B flat major, which is not available to use as a key, so I’m using G minor here.

Jigging In The Bed

Another fine composition by the Wexford melodeon player Lar White, notated and uploaded as part of the Wexford Tune Project. Thanks to Paddy Joyce for the recordings of Lar playing his own tunes. In Wexford, jigging is often another name for ‘lilting’ a tune so I’m thinking this is what Lar may be referring to in the title….Wexford is quite famous for champion lilters, including Leo Carty from Carne and M. J. O’Reilly from Enniscorthy who were Fleadh champion lilters many times. There is the famed fictitious newspaper account from a 1960’s local newspaper reporting that the Lilting competition at the County Wexford Fleadh cheoil was a close run thing, but was finally won, after recalls, by a quarter of a doodle.

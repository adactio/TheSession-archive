Seven comments

Donside

A four-parted strathspey very popular in Cape Breton. Recorded by Angus Allan Gillis, Bill Lamey and others.

↳ Posted by Daniel Parker on May 6th, 2017.

Don Side, X:2

A slightly more readable setting, this one from Bremner’s 1751 collection. Cape Breton fiddler John Campbell played the tune as a reel, which is worth hearing. I’ll maybe post that as well, for interest. So X:3 will be John Campbell’s setting, courtesy of Kate Dunlay.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on May 8th, 2017.

Re: Don Side

Don Side appears in Gow’s Repository of the Dance Music of Scotland, volume one, page 11. It’s almost identical to #2, but has a fifth verse, but says “When Danced, the Tune ends at this mark * ”, meaning the end of the fourth verse. Gow’s has it followed by Green Grow the Rashes, a reel, which is quite similar, but has only two verses.

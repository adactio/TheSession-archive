The Background

this is a very rare tune i got 2 years ago while on work experience in Donegal town from local button accordion player Martin Wilson. he tells me he got it from a really old 78rpm of rosses fiddler John Mc Gettigan. unfortunatly despite aparently being a great fiddler, his music was lost and nobody at all knows this tune which is a real shame so hopefully now, that might change!!!

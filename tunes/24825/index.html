Two comments

The Greeny Hill

A march transcribed from the playing of the Garson Trio, from the “Orkney: Traditional Dance Music from Orkney” record. The first part is very similar to Scott Skinner’s Mrs. E.M. Ross’ Welcome to Kiltarlity Cottage.

↳ Posted by SaoiDunnichen on October 6th, 2024.

The Greeny Hill, X:2

Composed by James Garson. Setting 2 is from Orcadian fiddler Pat Shearer, and I include it because it’s markedly different from the Garson Trio setting. Shearer also plays variations of the tune, and again they’re quite different.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on October 6th, 2024.

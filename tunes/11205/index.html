Three comments

The Stride

I saw a YouTube clip of Solas in concert, and heard Winifred play this cracking tune, first one in the set. I knew I’d heard it somewhere, so I looked it up and lo and behold it’s on their The Words That Remain CD from 1998, track 2, written by none other than Winifred Horan herself.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?feature=player_embedded&v=8uIPCdSO9RQ#at=31 at :48.

I transcribed it the way she plays it the first time through on the CD, but you can get a feel for her variations from the YouTube clip. On the CD, in the tenth measure of the A part, she slides up to the C note first time through. Couldn’t figure how to notate that in abc though. Crackin’ tune, surprised it wasn’t already here.

Additional fields for the abcs:
C: Winifred Horan
D: Solas, The Words That Remain, Shanachie 78023

Re: The Eriskay Skunk

This title relates to what looked like a skunk tail travelling just outside the conservatory window as I sat in the Am Politician (The Politician) pub on the Island of Eriskay in the Outer Hebrides of Scotland. The tail turned out to belong to a black and white dog but the tune was duly inspired anyway.

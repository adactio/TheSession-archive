An Cailín Rua

An cailín rua;
a song.
On this here score, as sung by members of Skara Brae on the opening track of their 1971 album.
The slight variations in rhythm reflects prosodic nuances; first verse first followed by the chorus.

The andante pace of this song and the cheerful melody give it a serene character.
Is it a march?
Although it has an even, regular pace to it, many rhythmic variations are accommodated within the isometric pulse including the accelerating run of quavers at the end of each verse, which lends the melody a lightness and sense of fun.
It has more similarities with the laid back Breton en dro dance feel than the usual 4/4 march, hornpipe or barndance types of beat.

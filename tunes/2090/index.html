Frog Rock

I didn’t compose this tune really - it just floated into my head while I was sitting on a train with a head full of tunes, so I regard it as having existed already - I just “discovered” it! I didn’t have a pen and paper at the time, so I had to try and keep it in my head. Then later I forgot about it and fell asleep. I awoke the next day and was lucky to be able to remember it in its entirity. I chopped and changed a few bits while sitting on a beach today, but I haven’t got an instrument to play it on, and won’t have for another few months :-( I hope someone else is the first to play it and get pleasure from it.

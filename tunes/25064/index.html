Two comments

Patsy Dempsey’s Morning Walk

Linda Hickman wrote this tune in memory of her mother-in-law. The tune is very flute and whistle friendly -- this setting is taken from a recording of Linda playing the whistle.

↳ Posted by JBrock on December 8th, 2024.

Patsy Dempsey’s Morning Walk, X:2

A somewhat pared down version of the tune, without the ornaments or the variation in the second part.

↳ Posted by JBrock on December 8th, 2024.

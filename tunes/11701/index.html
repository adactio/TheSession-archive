↳ Posted by ceolachan on January 17th, 2012.

I pretty much just wrote it on an improvisation with my band while busking we were playing the chicago which a bandmate of mine plays awesomely on the concertina and I went ahead and let him take it solo and started messing around with some harmony parts eventually I got into it enough that I took back over and started playing a rough version of what I have here (pretty much just the a part) it was a lot of fun and sounded awesome so I when I got home I bothered to come up with a b part (albeit it is almost the same as the a part) and write it down. So then I had to come up with a name for it… I already have a tune that we call “busking” so I just went to the old standby for coming up with names, flipping to a random page in finnegans wake and pointing to the first word and that’s your name, in this case it happened to be hempsylph. I actually have a fair amount of tunes that I’ve written and now play with my band this way and eventually I will post the rest. and yes I wrote it with lead ins but it should end on the tonic and for linking it to chicago I just do the Ac lead in to the a part from the last tonic in chicago. I do apologize for the somewhat… sloppy ABC it is actually my first ever attempt to notate something that way but hopefully it was legible enough I notated it by the beat because I usually find that a little easier to read, but the four note stem is fine as for the ^f/f# I wasn’t really sure whether or not this is a fixed chromatic system or a movable one that you can change with the key, so I was just being safe. I certainly didn’t want that to be an f natural. Thanks for the comments!

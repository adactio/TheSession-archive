Tom Byrne’s

Reel number 9 from the Wexford accordionist and composer Tom Byrne. I think this is a particularly nice take on the D minor type box reels. Notated and uploaded as part of the Wexford Tune Project.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on September 6th, 2022.

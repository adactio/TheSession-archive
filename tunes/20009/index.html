Four comments

Dwyer’s

I happened to stumble across this delightful little hornpipe and was surprised to not find it here. Perhaps it is here somewhere, but if it is it eluded my searches. If it is a duplication I’m sure someone will let me know. My setting is not a direct transcription of the first time through the tune, but an amalgamation of things that happen throughout. A youtube comment traced the tune back to a collection called ‘Old Irish Folk Music Songs’ collected by P.W. Joyce. That’s one I hadn’t heard of.

https://youtu.be/tGRJlNWVhpg

↳ Posted by Cheeky Elf on November 18th, 2020.

Dwyer’s, X:2

I found a copy of P.W. Joyce’s ‘Old Irish Folk Music and Songs’ on the Irish Traditional Music Archive website. The tune is #40 on page 62 of the .pdf, or page 22 of the book. This is the setting given. I think I prefer the other setting, but I am Including this one for posterity.

https://prints.itma.ie/joyce_microsite/pdfs/oldirishfolkmusicpart1.pdf#viewer.action=download

↳ Posted by Cheeky Elf on November 18th, 2020.

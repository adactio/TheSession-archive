Setting #2

is as played by Adele Farrell. This setting is pretty close to O’Neill’s as posted by treecipitation, but it has that c natural in bar 2 which is quite essential to the tune IMO. I think bar 1 of the B part is also played with a c natural sometimes (or even the whole tune in Dmix?). Anyway, here’s Adele:
http://comhaltas.ie/music/detail/comhaltaslive_319_4_adele_farrell_and_holly_geraghty/lang/en/

The Absent Fiddler

Hi Kenny - you have a good memory. I played this and the Tabby Reel before it at that recital. The Tabby Reel was composed by Gerry O’Donnell and I have it in my tunebook.

I composed The Absent Fiddler and it is dedicated to Paul McNevin who was frequently “Absent” from a gig we shared in Dublin. The plus side was meeting and playing with musicians like Attracta Brady and Peter Browne in his absence.

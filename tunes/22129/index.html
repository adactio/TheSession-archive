↳ Posted by Joaquin Juno on August 12th, 2022.

Re: A Gunne Dubh

It’s a pipe tune, so I would use f sharps rather than f naturals. It can be seen in Thomas Glen’s Tutor for the Great Highland Bagpipe (1840s) with the alternative title “The Auld Rusty Gun”:
https://www.ceolsean.net/content/TGlen/Book01/Book01%2026.pdf

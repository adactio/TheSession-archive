You’re forgiven, Conor. You’re lucky that you’ve been able to make several corrections to your tune. Until fairly recently we didn’t have the luxury of making adjustments after a tune was posted - all the mistakes were there forever, it seemed.

I have a particular bee in my bunnet about people posting their own compositions, but it’s allowed up to a point. The FAQs say, “Q: Can I submit my own compositions? A: Well… The Session isn’t really intended for that. If you do post one of your own compositions, then you must do your “penance”, so to speak, by balancing each original composition with about five trad tune settings…”

Rooney’s Favourite

This is the tune titled “Rooney’s Favourite” on Angelina Carberry and Martin Quinn’s duet album. According to the notes of the recording, they learnt the tune from Brian Rooney and Paddy Hayes’ duet playing on the radio.

Re: Rooney’s Favourite

Although Paddy played the tune regularly with Brian Rooney, I’m unsure whether the tune was named for Brian Rooney or not. Paddy composed this many years ago, and most of his tune titles allude to his connections in Piercestown Co. Wexford where he was from e.g.
Codd’s Grove https://thesession.org/tunes/12088
The Gate Lodge https://thesession.org/tunes/22007
Staple’s Haggard https://thesession.org/tunes/22097 etc. etc.

I’m informed that the title may refer to parishioners of the Hayes’s when they were growing up. Mrs. Rooney was by all accounts a very fine singer.

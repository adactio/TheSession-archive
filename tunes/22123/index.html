Two comments

The Hunted Pheasant

I finally managed to get a hold of a recording of The Grouse in the Heather from P.J. and Marcus Hernon, and this rather unusual Amaj reel is one of my very favorite tunes off the album. The B part in particular flirts with the relative minor without ever quite committing to the key change. Hopefully I’ll be able to work my way through transcribing the rest of the tracks eventually.

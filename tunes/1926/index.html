Little Donald in the Pig Sty

It’s also known as Little Donald in the Pigpen, and can be heard on “Fair Thee Well, Love,” by The Rankin Family (later The Rankins) of Cape Breton Island. It’s part of “Fiddle Medley” (Lime Hill / Keep the Country Bonnie Lassie / Jack Daniel’s Reel / Little Donald in the Pig Pen).

I also found a reference for “Little Donald in the Pig Pen” in “The Glenmorangie Collection of Bagpipe Music,” by James A. Mackay, listed for twelve pounds at http://www.scotbagpipes.com/acatalog/Bagpipe_Music_Books.html.

Re: Little Donald In The Pigpen

The name:
“Fiddler Hector MacDonald of Bangor, Kings County, coined this title while performing the tune on radio back in the 30s.”
--Ken Perlman, “The Fiddle Music of Prince Edward Island”

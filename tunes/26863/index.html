One comment

Mick Dwyer’s

Slipped my mind, for 9 years, apparently. A lovely 4-part jig, recorded by Alan Kelly on piano accordion, and attributed to Michael Dwyer as composer. This is the basic version of the jig, Alan, naturally, puts a few wee subtle variations of his own in the playing of it twice through on the recording.

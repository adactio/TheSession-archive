Re: The Eel At The Forge

Why did you call it the Eel at the Forge, Miles?

↳ Posted by Judey on February 6th, 2025.

Re: The Eel At The Forge

Hi Judey
There is a bit of a story behind the name. I grew up in a small village at the foot of the Sperrin mountains called Draperstown or Ballinascreen. We lived on the main street, Patrick Street, but the part of the street that we lived on was called Burnside, because there was a little burn or stream there, called the Moykeern Burn. The burn ran through a field opposite us and then underneath the road not far from our house, coming out again in the playing field of the primary school. On the other side of the road from us there was a blacksmith’s forge and beside the forge was a set of steps that went down to the burn just before it went through the culvert beneath the street. The burn was shallow in most places but here there was a deeper pool. On one occasion, an eel appeared in that pool and there was much excitement amongst the neighbourhood children and talk about how to catch it. My two older brothers somehow caught it in the end and my father fried it for our tea!

The twisty-turny nature of the tune, particularly the second part, brought to mind this episode from my childhood and suggested the name.

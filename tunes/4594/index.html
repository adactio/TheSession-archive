Roll Her On The Mountain

Stemmed from a Scottish reel called “Roll Her On the Hill” performed admirably by, among others, Daniel Wyper. I have long felt that this tune - which can be found in O’Neill’s by the way - was the missing link between “Roll Her On the Hill” and “Roll Her In the Ryegrass”.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on December 19th, 2005.

This tune is in the Gunn Book (Fermanagh 1865) as Rool her on the Mountain (sic)

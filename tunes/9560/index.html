Willie Clancy recorded this, or rather, it was field recording that got released on the Folktrax label. I’m not sure if this was recorded in London, but the Folktrax recording is the one with both Bobby Casey and Willie Clancy. Some speculation on this tune was that it was a Charlie Chaplin melody (from one of the films, anyway), and that Willie played it as a joke, or for fun, or whatever.

At any rate, Tommy Keane recorded it, and it’s a nice enough melody!

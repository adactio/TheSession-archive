Femspring

Written by Thorkild Knudsen and inspired by some older tunes from a Danish collection dating back to 1836. I don’t speak Danish but I heard Femspring means 5 jumps (It makes sense as it is a 5/8 tune).

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=31qLjYoU2js

It is the first tune of a set of 2 scandi tunes finnishing with an irish reel. The reel is already on The Session here (https://thesession.org/tunes/4312) and I will post the second tune very soon.

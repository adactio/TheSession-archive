Often confused with two reels, Mr and Miss Menzies of Culdares. So much that the inclusion here of “Miss” as an alternate title might be fitting. It’s “Culdares”, btw, though often listed as “Culdare”. Any account of the Menzies or MacFarlane clan history should clear that up. It’s a place in Perthshire.

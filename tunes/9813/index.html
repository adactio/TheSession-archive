Re: Lady Louisa Gordon’s

The Gows published this in the 4th Repository (Early 19thC) under the title “Miss McLeod’s Favorite” (and credited Marshall). Later, in Marshall’s posthumous second collection (1845), it was called “Miss Farquharson of Invercauld”.

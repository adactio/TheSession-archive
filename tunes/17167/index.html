Saely Kelly

↳ Posted by Cnámha Fear on August 16th, 2018.

Re: Saely Kelly

This is actually an O‘Carolan tune called Susannah Kelly. #75 in Donal O’Sullivan’s book "Carolan The Life, Times and Music of an Irish Harper, which I think must be considered the definitive work on Carolan’s music. According to O‘Sullivan’s notes the tune was incorrectly identified as Saely Kelly in some of Buntings manuscripts. It has been recorded be several people, most notably by Janet Harbison as Saely Kelly, apparently the name picked up from some of the Bunting manuscripts the O’Sullivan refers to.

I tried to post the version from O’Sullivan here ( I had thought it was already on the site but I do not find it), but I was unable to post it as another setting as it is in 2/4 time rather than 4/4 and I could not change the time signature. I will post it as a separate tune.

Janet Harbison’s fine version is found on her recording with Clarsheree - Feasting with Carolan and can be heard here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8-xqZb8fyp4. She also recorded it on album with the Belfast Harp Orchestra - The Belfast Harp Orchestra with Janet Harbison.

Re: Saely Kelly

It’s actually by Thomas Connollon but Turlough O’Carolan created the song-texts. The tune was written down by a (very young) Bunting and there are several versions extant.
Here’s an interesting link to hames of composers and tunes ‘associated’ with them: http://pybertra.free.fr/ceol/tunes.htm

The Spy Czar Hornpipe By Liz Carroll

I just saw Liz Carroll & John Doyle in concert and was inspired to transcribe this, a most crazy, tune. It’s off their CD “In Play.” It is in A harmonic minor, and that with the frequent triplet arpeggios give it an Eastern European flavor, to me anyway. There is also a crazy “Tag” after the second repeat of the tune, and it goes like this:

A|EAcA (3ABc d2|(3 EAc (3ecA EAB^G|EAce agfe|(3gfe dc BA^GF|
EAcA (3ABc d2|(3 EAc (3ecA EAB^G|(3DFA (3dAF EB,^GB,| A,2AE A3A|
(3E2A2c2 (3B2^G2F2|(3E2A2c2 (3B2^G2F2|EAce agfe|(3gfe dc BA^GF|
(3EAc (3eae fd- dE/E/|(3EAc (3eac‘ d’b- bD/D/|(3DFA (3dAF EB,^GB,|A,2e|]

A couple quick notes: The 2nd bar of the B-part the (^ga) should probably be played as a slide, I play Banjo and slides don’t actually work, so . . .
And the final note e is “plucked” pizzicatto style.

Enjoy.
JQQ

The Pink Flamingo

Composer: Nuala Kennedy
Source: CD: The New Shoes

This is the opening track of Nuala Kennedy’s/The New Shoes debut album. On the CD she and Claire Mann play the tune in Gminor (on F whistles) so for those of you who want it here it is in G minor:

X: 1
T: Pink Flamingo, The
C: Nuala Kennedy
M: 6/8
L: 1/8
R: jig
K:Gmin
|: G2 A BAG | A2 B cBA | G2 A BAG | dGB AGF |
G2 A BAG | A2 B cBA | B3 AGF | dGB AGF :|
|: B2 c dcB | cfc AGF | B2 c dcB | AFA cBA |
B2 c dcB | cfc AGF | B3 AGF | dGB AGF :|
|: b2 g afg | dfc dBc | ABG AGF | dGB AGF |
b2 g afg | dfc dBc |1 ABG AGF | dGB A3 :| 2 B3 AGF | dGB AGF ||

The Pink Flamingo, X:4

A tweak to X:3, to reflect the fact that in at least some recordings, there’s a varied repeat in the third section - the fourth time the descending bit occurs, it starts on the downbeat rather than after an eighth-note rest. This is what I hear on The New Shoes, as well as all of the other recordings I can find on YouTube of Nuala Kennedy playing the tune.

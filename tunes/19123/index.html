February Reel #2

A second February reel in F# minor (key signature of A major) by Dale Russ. The pair are played by Jody’s Heaven (1996). This recording is timeless, flawless, worth infinite listens, and these reels are played with enough swing to cheer you up on a winter day or a even global viral lockdown.

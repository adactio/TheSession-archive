Gan Ainm

This light classical tune entitled “Polka” appears in the Reilly family MSS (c1900s) of Drumreilly, Co Leitrim

↳ Posted by ConorW on November 30th, 2022.

Gan Aimn

An Un-named galope from the Power manuscript from Kilmore in County Wexford. The manuscript, apparently compiled by a number of different people over a period of about 70 years from the mid 19th century onwards has a mixture of Irish, Scottish, Military/Old time and light classical transcriptions of melodies. Hopefully, someone may be able to identify this one.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on April 27th, 2023.

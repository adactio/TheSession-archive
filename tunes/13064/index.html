Shooyl Inneenyn

Shooyl Inneenyn is a popular dance from the Isle of Man. It’s not really a reel, but it doesn’t fit into a category - it’s most likely that it was originally 2 tunes, that were put together for the purpose of the dance, but the tunes are always played as ‘one’ now! The first tune is a slow air, and the second a fast reel. It’s not usually notated as it is, but I’ve done it like this to try and show the speed change.

The dance was said to have been performed by girls at the Michaelmas hiring fair, as a way of attracting employers. Here’s a video of the dance - the tune will make a lot more sense if you watch it!
http://www.manxmusic.com/video_window_130894.html

Smith o’ Couster

Composed by the late Shetland fiddler Tom Anderson. He recorded on his 1963 LP ‘Scottish Violin Music’ as “Smith of Couster’s Fancy”. It was written for his friend Magnie Smith, who also played fiddle.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on June 5th, 2015.

Smith O’Couster, X:2

Setting number 2 was transcribed from the playing of Tom Anderson (who composed it) and Aly Bain on a television programme called “Down Home: From Shetland to Texas” from 1985.

Fogwatt Burns Supper

A tune by William Macpherson who also composed the very popular “Brumley Brae”

Willie was a well known fiddler in the Elgin area and also founder of the Elgin Strathspey and Reel Society which continues to this day. He has been mentioned elsewhere on this site.

Fogwatt is on the route from Elgin to Dufftown. I’ve not stopped there but i’ve seen the signpost. ;-)
I assume it was written about a Burns Supper which took place there.
A good tune.

Indeed it’s good to have both settings. In the book it says, “The Burns Supper of 1970 held in the Fogwatt Hall was one of the first public engagements of [the revived Elgin Strathspey and Reel Society]. Willie composed this march to mark the occasion. It has been played at the annual Burns Supper in Fogwatt ever since.”

The Shores Of Loch Awe

This lively polka was written by Antrim fiddle and flute player Karen Logan Woods, and was published in the lovely book ‘Folk Tunes From the Women’ (Fabre Music, ISBN 0-571-54287-5).
If you play in a ceilidh band, it works exceptionally well after the Dashing White Sergeant for the dance of the same name.

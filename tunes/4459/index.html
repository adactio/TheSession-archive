Hobbit Reel

This tune was written for then Adelaide flute player Elaine Jeffreys back in 1978 by Jerry Everard - a former member of Mucky Duck Bush Band in Perth and now of Full Circle in Canberra. It was used for many years as a ‘beginners’ reel by the Adelaide Celtic Music Club.

↳ Posted by ijerry on May 3rd, 2005.

Good tune, especially if you insert a repeat of bars 1 and 2 after bar 4 to make it an even 8-bar A part.

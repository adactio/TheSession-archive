Two comments

Frosty’s Frolics

This is the second tune of the Kittycat Slip Jigs set by Patrick Davey. The tunes are inspired by two kittens named Fluffy and Frosty.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=51qyQSD97Qw

The unusual key of F dorian is due to Patrick’s B flat whistle.

↳ Posted by Harald Wellmann on May 16th, 2025.

Frosty’s Frolics, X:2

X:1 transposed to Ador, to fit a standard D whistle.

↳ Posted by Harald Wellmann on May 16th, 2025.

The Brigade

On this video, Brendan Mulvihll, Angleina Carberry, and Dan Brouder play a set of
three hornpipes that Brendan says were “from the pen of Frank McCollum”.
The first and last, The Hangman’s Rope and The Home Ruler, are already here, but the
middle one, The Brigade, was not.

This version is from Brendan’s book Irish Scroll vol two, and is pretty much what the play
in the video (starting about 21:00).
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OVNMiT0RLps

Re: Gregory Grene’s

Hi! I did compose it, rather than popularize it. I wrote it when I was at Trinity College Dublin, and played it for Liz Carroll when I was home for the holidays. Fast forward 15 years, I got a call from Myron Bretholz, lovely DC producer, asking if a band he was working could have permission to record a tune I wrote. I said sure, but I doubted it was one of mine. He lilted it to me on the phone, and I was delighted to hear the tune resurrected! Turned out Liz had played it at a sound check; Billy McComiskey had liked it, and popularized in DC, where the name morphed into “Gray Gray Green.” :)

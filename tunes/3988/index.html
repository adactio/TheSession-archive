My Partner is Italian and for some years we owned an Irish Italian Bar called The Garibaldi here in La Rochelle. Many great and memorable nights were had because a lot of musicians passing through France stopped off for a day or two.
Unfortunately we had to sell it when we started our second family . The motto of the bar was “The Bar of Navigators, Musicians and Dreamers”, hence the title.

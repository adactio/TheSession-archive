Five comments

Mick Flaherty’s

Second of two tunes attributed to Galway accordion player Mick Flaherty by Billy McComiskey on his 1981 recording “Makin’ The Rounds”. The transcription was given to me by Aberdeen flute player Malcolm Reavell some years back. McComiskey plays this in “F”, but it does sound familiar, and may be here already but in a different key.

↳ Posted by Kenny on April 26th, 2020.

Re: The Galway Girl

The air for this song is built like a march,
the 4/4 Scottish kind, along a melodic path also common in scottisches and barndances,
for instance; The fairy fort (part one only)
https://thesession.org/tunes/2177

So, in answer to Mandolin1944, broadly speaking, this type of melody is not necessarily Irish or American, but could be heard wherever the fife and drum, the fiddle or the pipes went… It possibly predates but is concomitant with British cultural /military expension.

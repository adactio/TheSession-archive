Re: George Christie Of Fochabers By Colin Cambell

Hi Emma - thanks for a nice tune. I worked with Colin many years ago, but haven’t seen him for yonks!

Your transcription is no bad, but some points:

1) You don’t put the composer’s name in the title. I think it’s important to credit composers, but you should do it in the comments.

2) The bars don’t add up, as I’m sure you know. This is how I’d do it:

D G>A | AB- B3 d | AB- B2 GA/B/ | c>B A>G E>D| C4 C>B, |
A,G- G2 G>A | EF- FD E>D | G3 A- AB/c/ | A3 D G>A | AB- B3 d |
AB- B2 GA/B/ | c>B A>G E>D| C4 C>B, | A,G- G2 G>A | EF- FD E>D |
G3 A B>A | G3 :||: A B>A | B>g g>B g>B | c>g g>c g>c |
A3 D A>B | A3 A B>A | B>g g>B g>B | c>g g>c g>c | A3 D A>B |
A3 G G>F | G e2- e2 G | F d2- d2 F | E c2- c2 E | D B2- B2 D |
C A2- A2 B | EF- FD E>D | G3 A B>A | G3 :|

Study this and you’l learn the hows and whys of ABC.

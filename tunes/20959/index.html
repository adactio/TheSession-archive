Lady Hamilton Of Dalrymple

Another gem of a strathspey from Gerald Trimble’s “First Flight”, learned from the pages of Kerr’s. His version seems played a fair bit different then the original sheet music. This is the way I learned it and it’s really one of my favorite tunes to play. I love really exaggerating some of the snaps and letting others straighten out a bit.

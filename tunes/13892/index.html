Donnel’s Fury

T:Donnel’s Fury
C:20th century Cape Breton
C:Dougie MacDonald

Cranford’s Pub had a few images of Dougie MacDonald tunes but not the abc versions. Some of those tunes were already here but three of them were not. I’ve gone ahead and created the abc versions and hopefully made no egregious errors. This is Donnel’s Fury. The original score was in the key of Eb which isn’t supported here so I transposed it to Dmaj.

RIP Dougie, your music will live on.

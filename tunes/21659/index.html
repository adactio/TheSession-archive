Two comments

The Muskerry Tram

This tune is composed by Seán O’Driscoll. He released it on his album “The kitchen sessions” with Larry Egan on Accordion. It is also to be found on the album Tobar Gan Trá by Pádraig Mac Aodhgáin (Paddy Egan) released in 2022. I tried to transcribe the tune and made a mixture of both recordings.

↳ Posted by irish-bouzouki on March 3rd, 2022.

Re: The Muskerry Tram

A beautiful jig I’ve added to my repertoire.
Thanks to the author for it.
To the ear it, this jig sounds a complete original, but since I love to listen for patterns and similarities between tunes, I have noticed this; it’s 1st part is a minor version of Cúnla’s 1st part (Cúnla happens to be another jig associated with the South West) https://thesession.org/tunes/34
That fact, needless to say, does not take anything from the originality and the charm of the Muskerry Tram, especially since it is more obvious to the eye than to the ear.

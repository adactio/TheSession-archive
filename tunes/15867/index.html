The Fox And The Hare

I wouldn’t usually clutter the tune base with my own compositions but awhile back Ceol requested samples of our own work so here is one of mine.
While sitting on my back porch looking out across a pasture of high grass, I could see a red fox bobbing above the grass to catch a better view of the prey he pursued. Hop - hop - hop - hippity - hippity - hop -hop. As he closed on his prey he seemed to be almost dancing a hornpipe. I wrote this tune to celebrate his dance.
Cheers! :)

↳ Posted by BillScates on February 14th, 2017.

The Fox And The Hare, X:3

This is a note-for-note transcription of “Fox and the Hare” as it can be heard on the band Roads to Home’s 2018 release. A lovely tune, played at a laid back tempo on banjo and fiddle, andante. Again, thanks to the author/musicians.

For the record, the main differences between X:1 and X:3 (as they are displayed today) are listed here:
Bars 7-8;
X:1 |f/f/f e/e/e d/d/d B/B/B|A/A/A BA d4:|
X:3 fagf edB2|1 A>ABA d2de:|2 A>ABA d4||
Bars 9-10;
X:1 |:a/a/b af a/a/b af|defd edB2|
X:2 |:abaf abaf|defe dBB2|
and Bars 13-16;
X:1 a/a/baf a/a/baf|defd edB2|f/f/f e/e/e d/d/d B/B/B|defd edd2:|
X:2 abaf abaf|defe dAA2|fagf edB2|1 A>ABA d4:|2 A>ABA d2||
showing some discrete, pertinent refurbishing.

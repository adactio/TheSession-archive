The Hen Hunt

From Nordic Fiddlers Bloc recording “Deliverance”. Hornpipe is a wild guess, but it is a strange hybrid thing with extra measures in other time signatures. My best efforts. It must be heard I think.

From the notes on the recording:
(Olav L. Mjelva, TONO)
In 1953, Orkney was battered by one of the worst storms in the island,s recorded history with winds up to 125mph. At that time the egg industry in Orkney was huge with a poultry population of 800.000 of which 250.000 were lost as a result of the storm.

The recording is here:
https://thenordicfiddlersbloc.bandcamp.com/album/deliverance

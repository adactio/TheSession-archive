Two comments

Footnotes

Appears on “Grapevine” by Elvie Miller and Naomi Morse. I don’t have the CD anymore and the streaming service credits aren’t very detailed, but I’m pretty sure this one and Off-Glide were written by Elvie.

There are some interesting things going on. The major B major chord in the E minor key, and a lost bar in the A section that resurfaces in the B section. Like, the B section can’t wait to rush in.

It feels at first meditative, then emotional, like the thoughts that plow through said meditation. It pairs quite well with Off-Glide, which sounds more settled, grounded, and happy.

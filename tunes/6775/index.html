Christine Taylor’s Jig

I love Northumbrian tunes they always have a happy feel to them, I learnt the Northumbrian pipes when I was 16, before discovering the uilleann pipes. I still enjoy playing the Northumbrian pipes from time to time. Andy May was in town last year and he was just amazing on the Northumbrian pipes.
This is a 4 part jig I think it was written by Willie Taylor.

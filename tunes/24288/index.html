My Reel #1

Hello @NfldWhistler,

Thank you for uploading my tunes and videos here and for providing such accurate transcriptions.

Fun facts about my Reel #1:

-B part is reminiscent of “The Golden Stud”.
-The tune uses only 7 notes.
-It is a simpler version of a 4-part showpiece called “Na Ceithre Puiscín” that I recorded with my former band ‘Corsaire’.

The Bmin version is a clever idea. Players of Québec 1-row diatonic accordion can play the tune in that key.

Thank you again for your interest!

Marc-Antoine

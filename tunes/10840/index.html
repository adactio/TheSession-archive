The Forgotten Charlie Lennon, Part 2…

Here it is, the second tune in the “Sean Maguire Set” from Charlie Lennon’s “Musical Memories” Tunebook/CD. Even more challenging than “The Abbey Tavern” , this one will certainly keep you on your toes! I have the key here as Gminor , but only because Bflat wasn’t a choice on the list here. Oh, and watch out! The key changes to one flat (F Major) for the “B” part. Enjoy, and good luck!

↳ Posted by jaychoons on November 18th, 2010.

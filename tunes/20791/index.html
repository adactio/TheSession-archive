Two comments

Old Corcomroe

This is a tune written by Charlie Piggott and his son Rowan Piggott. It features on Charlie’s solo album ‘The Days That Are Gone’ and a few people have been looking for the notes so here’s a transcription of it in the original key of E mixolydian… however, it’s a bit easier on the fiddle if played in A mix!

↳ Posted by rowanpiggott on May 19th, 2021.

Old Corcomroe, X:2

Very nice tune! I’m posting this setting that is the same as setting X:1 but transposed to Dmix. The second part is also one octave lower (I first transposed to Dmix and then I transposed the second part one octave lower). I did this setting for me to play it with a combination of tremolo diatonic harmonicas, one in G and the other in D (I don’t have one in A or in E). I hope this setting can be of some use to someone.

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on May 19th, 2021.

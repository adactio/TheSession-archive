Two comments

Reel St-Michel

Quebec traditional. Some combination of both Tidal Wave’s and The Brock McGuire Band’s recordings, with a few rolls thrown into the B Part. Playing rolls on the G’s, F’s, E’s, and D’s of the A Part doesn’t work so well.

Brock McGuire Band (3rd tune):
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SfX4dkQ7YlM

Tidal Wave (3rd tune):
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n-3g7LzDcM4

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on December 31st, 2018.

Reel St-Michel, X:1 & X:2

Have adjusted X1 to match The Brock McGuire Band’s setting, and, X2 to match Tidal Wave.

X3 is a few variations onto each of these.

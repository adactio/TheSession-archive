Five comments

This is the first of the 2 reels played in the Gaffo’s Ball set, written by Sharon Shannon. It’s on the Renegade Cd I believe, though this is a transcription of her playing it with Dezi D and Jim M, and a huge band, on some TG4 show. I’ve already submitted the 2nd reel.

THey’re tuned up a semitone to Bb, but Dezi appears to be aplying ‘A’ fingering, so I have pitched this in A.

↳ Posted by Fliúiteadóir on December 18th, 2010.

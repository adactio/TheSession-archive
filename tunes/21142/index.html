Jota Rodrigo Encinas

Castillian Tradicional Music

This is not a waltz, it is a jota. 3/4 as well but with the first beat as the most important one.

Tune composed by dulzaina player Javier Pilar Arranz.

The composer has a website where it can be seen the original music score here:

https://escuelacollara.com/partituras/

The composer playing the tune:

https://youtu.be/S9loT3r0YvA

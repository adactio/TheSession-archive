Fox’s Sleep

A nice little tune.

↳ Posted by JACKB on December 20th, 2014.

Fox’s Sleep, X:2

Version in O’Neill Music of Ireland, no. 335.

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 11th, 2022.

Fox’s Sleep, X:3

Version in Clinton’s Gems of Ireland, no. 40, transposed from B-flat, a key not allowed on The Session, down to G major.

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 11th, 2022.

Fox’s Sleep, X:4

Arranged by Beethoven in his 12 Irish Songs (WoO 154, no. 10) under the name “The Hero May Perish.”

Lyrics by William Smyth (1765 - 1849):

The hero may perish his country to save,
And he lives in the records of fame;
The sage may the dungeons of tyranny brave --
Ever honour’d and blest be his name!
But virtue, that silently toils or expires,
No wreath for the brow to entwine,
That asks but a smile -- but a fond sigh requires --
O woman! that virtue is thine!

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 11th, 2022.

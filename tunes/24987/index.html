Jim’s

This is the first tune on the track “For Good Measure” on Úr by Goitse, where it was originally listed as “Jim’s (Polka)”, I assume that was taken from the sleeve notes.

It’s definitely a polka, as played on that album, time signature notwithstanding. Best just to listen to how they play it. I really like how the first long bar is like taking a deep breath before dropping into the energy of the rest.

Another great polka that’s not in 2/4 is https://thesession.org/tunes/23368

Evercreech Hornpipe

Originally collected by Cecil Sharp from Tom Cave in 1907, under the name Country Dance. I learned it from John Kirkpatrick’s arrangement for a full band with the English Folk Dance and Song Society: https://www.efdss.org/55-resources/learning-resources/2527-efdss-resource-bank-the-evercreech-hornpipe

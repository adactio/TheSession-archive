Swedish Polska

Here’s a tune I got from the Spreefix album from Fullsceilidh Spelemannslag. I believe this is a Swedish tune, not sure who’s written it or if it’s trad, but it’s a fantastic wee tune.

↳ Posted by Joseph Chordmichael on April 10th, 2013.

It’s normal to transcribe polskor in 3/4. This one is so much associated with Stensele (in Västerbotten) that it’s known as “Stensele polskan” (The Stensele Polska) to many people. Here is a version from the playing of Sören Johansson from Dorotea (about 60 miles south of Stensele):

X:2
T:Stensele Polskan
R:Polska
M:3/4
L:1/8
K:Gmin
[DG,]B BA/B/ c/B/A/G/|FA AG/A/ B/A/G/F/|EE/F/ GG/A/ BA/G/|^F/G/A/F/ GD B,/C/B,/A,/|
[DG,]B BA/B/ c/B/A/G/|FA AG/A/ B/A/G/F/|EE/F/ GG/A/ BA/G/|^F/G/A/F/ G4:|

↳ Posted by Weejie on April 10th, 2013.

Further research shows that this tune was composed by Johan August Andersson (1860 or 1866 -1907). He was born on Mörkö in Sörmland, but moved to Dorotea, where he was a teacher, cantor and fiddler. The title is usually written as a compound “Stenselepolskan”. It was originally called “Polska från Dorotea” (Polska from Dorotea) and had a variation in the second part, where there was a shift of metre:

X:3
T:Stenselepolskan/Polska från Dorotea
C:Johan August Andersson
R:Polska
M:3/4
L:1/8
K:Gmin
[DG,]B BA/B/ c/B/A/G/|FA AG/A/ B/A/G/F/|EE/F/ GG/A/ BA/G/|^F/G/A/F/ GD B,/C/B,/A,/|
[DG,]B BA/B/ c/B/A/G/|FA AG/A/ B/A/G/F/|EE/F/ GG/A/ BA/G/|^F/G/A/F/ G4:|

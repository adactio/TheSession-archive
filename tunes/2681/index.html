“Raggle Taggle Gypsy” is, if I recall correctly, a song of which there are many variants -- I think it’s Childe Ballad #200. Try at mudcat.org or the general Net and you’ll probably find 200 different sets of lyrics.

The tune I like the best at the moment is (in D):

C|:D2 C2 B,3C|D2 C2 B,3B,|B3B c2 B2|A2 FE F3F|

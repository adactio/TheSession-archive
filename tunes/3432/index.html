Six comments

This is a jig I wrote a few years ago. Rest assured I will add some trad tunes to the site as soon as. Hope some of you like this tune in the mean time - its primarily designed for the fiddle as it explores some of the low G-string sound that I find is so often neglected. The title comes from the tunes habit of, well, rambling on in continuous quaver movement around all four strings of the instrument.

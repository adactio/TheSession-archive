Two comments

Boyne Mist

Another great John McEvoy reel. First part once, second part twice. Written out twice to include the small change in the first bar on the repeat (players leave out the high A), as well as the 2 ways to play the second bar.

↳ Posted by Sláine on April 12th, 2024.

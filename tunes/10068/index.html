Paddy Fahey’s

One source is Bulmer & Sharpley Vol 2 No33 where it is called PaddyFahey’s No2. Below is a transcription of how the tune has evolved with me.

X:1
T:Paddy Fahey’s #2
H:Bulmer & Sharpley volII P9 #33 (more or less)
M:4/4
K:C
G|~c2 cB cGEG|FADF EFGA|~_B2 BG c=Bcd|egdc BGAB|
~c2 cB cGEG|~F2 DF EFGA|~_B2 GA =BGFE|1DG,A,B, ~C2 G:|2DG,A,B, ~C2 CG||

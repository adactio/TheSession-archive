Composer. Colm O’Donnell
Source: “Farewell To Evening Dances” by Colm O’Donnell
Transcription: gmp

↳ Posted by gian marco on April 23rd, 2004.

I think this tune has a nice melody and is best played softly. Michael Rooney do a good job of it.

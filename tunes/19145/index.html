The Lough Muckno

A composition of Glasgow piper Pat Mc Nulty which my wife Helen plays on the concertina. Lough Muckno is the lake beside Castleblayney, Co. Monaghan. Pat Mc Nulty’s mother was from Doohamlet which is between Castleblayney and Ballybay.

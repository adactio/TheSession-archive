Charlie’s March

Traditional Scottish march. From the playing of Andy Cutting & Chris Wood, and also Lissa Schneckenburger.
Posting as a polka to get the 2/4 time signature. The B part as it’s notated here isn’t supposed to repeat, but it wouldn’t let me post without a repeat symbol.

Re: Charlie’s

This is a setting of a tune called “Cam Ye By Athol?” by Neil Gow Junior (Nathaniel Gow’s son and Niel Gow’s grandson). James Hogg - “The Ettrick Shepherd” - wrote the words, which were then set to music by Gow Jr.
https://thesession.org/tunes/14484

An Irish setting of the tune is known as “Bonnie Prince Charlie” ( https://thesession.org/tunes/892 ) and is closer to the Cutting/Wood recording.

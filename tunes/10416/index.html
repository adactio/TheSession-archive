Muñeira de Barganaz

This a strange Asturian tune. First part has Dmin key, 2nd G, 3rd D and finally 4th part is Dmin again.
I say that it is strange because in asturian music we haven’t so many key’s changes. In addition, its style is weird, it doesn’t fit with Asturian. Due to this, it’s thought that a traveler has brought this tune to us a lot of time ago. Other people think that it’s just a strange asturian tune, and not from overseas.

Actually we don’t play this tune in these keys, we play each part in Cmin, F, C and Cmin. I have seen that you don’t use C or Cmin key very often so I have “translated” it. In fact, I couldn’t choose Cmin key.

Accidentals are hard notes in Asturias pipe because its fingenirng possition is not comfortable and we have to be extremely careful with air pressure, so this is a difficult tune to as.

Tim’s Mazurka #1

A lovely mazurka, the first of a set of two, composed by fiddler Tim O’Leary and recorded by Claire Mann on her solo album she recorded in 2011 :
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=70A4PgpuqX0&feature=youtu.be
Tim O’Leary was in Brighton Irish-influenced folk-rock band McDermott’s Two Hours. They recorded several albums but I couldn’t find a recording by Tim himself. It seems that he now resides in Edinburgh.

He also composed McDermott’s Polka recorded by ClaireMann : https://thesession.org/tunes/3123
and The Boys of the Lough also plays a Tim O’Leary’s Waltz, in their album Sweet Rural Shade : https://thesession.org/tunes/10568

Now have a look to Tim’s Mazurka #2 !

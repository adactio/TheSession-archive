Four comments

Belambra

A own composition made with my band The Soggy Bottoms this summer 2023 when we were hosted at the Belambra club of Guidel, near Lorient in Brittany during the Celtic Festival.
During a reaharsal, Martin Jaillet took the guitar and started a fast jig rythmic with a set of great chords. I took the Whistle in Bb and it was 100 % improvisation.
We played it at the end for the audience of the club Belambra.

↳ Posted by JohnMcTilly on August 30th, 2023.

Acres Gate

Slipped under the radar, this one. From “The Factory Turn” by Damien O’Brien and Oliver Loughlin. Apparently composed by Finbar McGreevy, according to credits on the CD sleeve. A very fine composition.

↳ Posted by Kenny on February 25th, 2025.

Re: Acre’s Gate

One of these very strange coincidences. I didn’t recall the name of Finbarr McGreevy at all, but 3 days after posting the above I had reason to look again at “The Flute Players of Roscommon Vol.2” CD, and there he was.
Apparently Finbarr - [ whose name is spelt with 2 “r”s on the CD liner notes ] - was, in 2008 “a young musician in his early 20s from the Elphin area whose flute-playing reputation is steadily growing”.
Can’t help but wonder if he’s composed any other fine tunes like this.

Five comments

The Grindstone

This is a little reel I wrote a few years ago. It’s written to be played at quite a lick - if you play it a few times whilst gathering speed throughout it can work well as a link between a slow reel and a fast reel within a set.

↳ Posted by jakki S on July 14th, 2004.

The Grindstone

I have included this tune as the middle tune in a set for guitar; starts with the Red-haired Boy, played as a Hornpipe, and finishes with the Absent-minded Woman. Seems to work well. BTW Jakki S, could you send me your contact info as it seems you are the one who penned this one!

Patsy Kinsella’s Delight

This is a composition of the mouth organ player Patsy Kinsella from Clough near Gorey in County Wexford. The Clough area produced many fine musicians including fiddle champion Tomás Keegan and accordion/concertina player Larry Kinsella (and his relatives in the older Kinsella céilí band). According to the listing on the ITMA website another Larry Kinsella (mouth organ) was also from Clough and recorded the first-ever commercial recording of mouth organ playing in the Irish tradition on a 78 phonograph record in the late 1930’s. Local enquiries so far are not revealing whether Patsy and Larry were related since Kinsella is a widespread surname in that area. Patsy was a well-known musician in North Wexford, playing with the Gorey céilí band with whom he did many TV and radio broadcasts in years gone by. Sincere thanks to Pat Bass for rescuing the tune from an old recording and providing notation for the Wexford Tune Project.

It is the tune which is played after “Tree Little Steps” (for Maisy).

Re: Goldies

Regarding the key or mode discussion, the root note is clearly A, and C is a minor third, which rules out Amix and leaves Amin or Ador.

There is not a single F# in the melody, so I wouldn’t be so sure it’s dorian. The melody is ambiguous, and by choosing a chord progression, you can give it a dorian or ionian (A minor) feel.

Damien’s SoundCloud version uses

Am F G Em
Am F Dm Em

Am Em Dm Em
Am Em Dm Am

if I get it right.

The Dervish recording of Hungry Rock includes a bridge that I have roughly approximated. I had to transpose certain portions up an octave for my instrument. Following the bridge, it returns to the B then C parts, then repeats the A, B, C parts. [See versions 1 or 2 above for the A, B or C parts]

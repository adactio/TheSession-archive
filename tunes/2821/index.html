Five comments

The Spider’s Web

A great flute jig. From the playing of Gary Hastings, composed by Vincent Broderick.

↳ Posted by Kenny on April 14th, 2004.

“Traditional Irish Flute Solos: The Turoe Stone Colleciton”

by Vincent Broderick
Waltons Music Inc.
ISBN: 1-85720-061-6

Page 21: “The Spider’s Web”

↳ Posted by ceolachan on December 11th, 2005.

Watch the Leeds Ceili Band play this V. Broderick tune: http://comhaltas.ie/music/detail/comhaltaslive_216_1_leeds_ceili_band

In Leeds I often heard it played by Mrs. Helen Flaherty, the PA player in the clip. She’s an astonishing musician with a good stock of lesser-known modern compositions penned by Marcus Hernon, V. Broderick, Sean Ryan, etc.

↳ Posted by slainte on August 17th, 2007.

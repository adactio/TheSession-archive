Four comments

Another Flook tune

This great tune comes off Flook’s “Rubai.” It was composed by Sarah Allen. The rhythm
approximates a hornpipe though they play it with a considerable groove, which I love. The
tune was originally recorded in A major (played on an low A whistle with D fingers).

↳ Posted by jdicarlo on February 1st, 2004.

Granny In The Attic, X:3

Just recently was introduced to this tune.
This transcription, as well as correcting one or two mistakes (as I hear them) in the first two settings, is closer to how I think Sarah Allen originally wrote the tune (listening to the alto flute rather than the A whistle in various versions), and is my starting point for then introducing all the Brian Finnegan extras.

Re: Logan’s Fling

11 years later from posting the above tune - Logan Mackie-Campbell was a student in Aberdeen at the same time as Dick and myself were learning music there and then. He played uilleann pipes and whistle, but passed away at a far too early age. Dick composed this tune in his memory. RIP.

Re: The Battle Of Passchendaele

It’s a fine reel, but hardly suitable as a commemoration of one of the bloodiest and ultimately pointless battles from the middle period of the First World War. Think there’s some kind of misnomer here (rather like “Hannibal Crossing the Alps” becoming “The Battle of Waterloo”).

I was born two years before the end of WW2, and my two grandfathers and several great uncles fought on the western front in WW1. All survived, but one grandfather died before I was born, officially of TB, but his lungs had been damaged in a gas attack. One great-uncle suffered permanent leg damage (bullet wound), and another had heart and lung problems. My other grandfather was a sergeant mechanic in the Army Service Corps, for whom he commanded a mobile motor workshop - at great risk, although he survived unscathed. All had medals, and all avoided talking about their experiences. All probably suffered from what we now call PTSD.

This is not a political statement, but I have to say I am offended by the idea of Passchendaele being commemorated by a reel.

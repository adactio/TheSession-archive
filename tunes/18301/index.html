Clifton Road, X:2

I heard this tune when a bandmate of mine suggested it from the album “The Raven’s Rock” by Cillian Valley. When I looked online at the sheet music to get an idea of what they were playing, I found a few spots where he plays it differently than what was first written, so I thought I’d write up my own transcription. In the third measure of the A section he does this quick slide from the G note below A

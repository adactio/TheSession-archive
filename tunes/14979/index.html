Mistral

A friend gave me the score (transcription Daniel Bourdon) of these 2 tunes which appear as Mistral on Spillane’s Pipedreams, track no. 6.
Although I don’t fancy Davy Spillane’s style (rock influence), his tunes are interesting. I really like the second one.

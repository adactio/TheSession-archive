Re: An Tábhairne Réaltrach

ELCBK, I have no trouble with new tunes—I’ve posted a number of more recent compositions from musicians like Mick McAuley and Jim Murray. As long as they’re recognizably within the tradition, even if they stretch the edges, that’s how this music grows and stays alive.

I also get that many sessions are fairly inclusive of other styles of music. I’ve joined in on bluegrass, Appalachian, and Country-Western tunes and songs at sessions in Ireland and elsewhere. But the core of those sessions was still Irish trad.

Mad About Me is just a cute gimmick of a melody for a scene in a Hollywood sci-fi flick, written by someone who has no relevance whatsoever to Irish trad or sessions. It has absolutely no connection to this music, or to sessions, and in 200 years it will have even less.

Your quote from this site’s home page is my main point.

It’s beyond me why some people feel the need to clutter up this site, “dedicated to Irish traditional music,” with random chaff from other genres that aren’t part of the trad heritage. If someone wants to play movie music or video game themes at their session, that’s their prerogative. As a one off, it can be a bit of craic. But that’s it. If people want to post irrelevant music here, they can fill up their profile pages with it and leave the tune database alone.

Imagine how well it would be received if we all decamped to thestringquartet.org jazztrios.net and started posting polkas, jigs, and barndances.

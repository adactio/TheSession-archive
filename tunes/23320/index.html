Four comments

The First Draft

This lovely mazurka is a composition by Siobhán Peoples. I first heard it on www.itma.ie.

Currently there’s a page on itma.ie for this tune:
https://www.itma.ie/scores/first-draft/

In it there are the following words from S Peoples: “This tune simply got its title as it presented itself to me fully formed. It was recorded by the band Fidil on their 2019 recording, Decade, and they kindly allowed us to include the track here.”

The score I’m sharing is, I believe, identical to the one written by S Peoples (barring one minor detail[1]); I copied it from the itma.ie page.
I got in touch with S Peoples and she kindly agreed for me to do this.

The page also gives a recording by the group Fidil, along with another score. This second score (actually it’s the first on the itma.ie page) is a transcription of the Fidil recording. It uses Soundslice to synch with the recording. And IMO it has several errors. I mean to post a second score, giving my transcription of Fidil’s setting.

I vaguely recall, a while ago there was a differently presented page about S Peoples’ compositions on itma.ie which had a recording of her playing this tune, but it seems to have been taken offline. I would be interested in hearing it again - does anyone have a lead on that?

----

[1] S People’s score doesn’t start with the “start” repeat symbol ( “|:” ), whereas this score does. Unfortunately this site doesn’t seem to let me post a score when there’s an “end” repeat symbol (“:|”) without a matching “start” repeat symbol.

The First Draft, X:2

This is my own transcription of the version of this tune by the group Fidil (well, just the first time they play it through). Their setting of the tune is lovely - I also like the meditative coda they added on - not given here in this score.

I chose to use more dots than is perhaps traditional when writing things down. It may well not be to many people’s taste, but it’s how I wanted to do it. I feel it’s better suited to getting down the swung rhythm and the variations more precisely; I wanted to get closer to the variations and ornaments played in the recording.

Another point worth noting, there’s a nice video of this tune by Natalie Haas, Caitlín Nic Gabhann and Ciarán Ó Maonaigh. Their version is quite close to the Fidil one; it also has some very pretty things going on on the cello.

https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=S7JhPQI9Uew

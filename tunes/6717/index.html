Six comments

This reel was composed by Richard Forest and is now well known all over North America and elsewhere. It is not very difficult and pleasant to hear. I learned it from Richard himself. He plays it in 2/4. You can hear Richard playing this reel on the following album.

https://thesession.org/recordings/2378

You can also hear a tutorial under this link:

http://www.dmij.net/laval.html

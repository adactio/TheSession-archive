The High Road to Galway

Learnt off East Galway flute player Sean Moloney’s solo album. The notes says the tune is from Sean’s father Eddie Moloney of Ballinakill. It’s possibly a version of “The New Found Out,” or “The Satin Slipper”: https://thesession.org/tunes/5268

It’s a bit challenging on the keyless flute, but worth a try.

↳ Posted by slainte on January 3rd, 2009.

The High Road To Galway, X:3

From the Leo Rowsome Collection of Irish Music, a book of tunes Leo wrote out for his students. Leo may be heard playing this on the Master Pipers, Vol. 3. It was recorded in 1931 by the Siamsa Gaeldheal Ceildhe Band, featuring piper Dave Page, a student of Leo’s. They played it single, as did Willie Clancy, who recorded it on a tape, “Airs and Reels,” whose title describes its contents. This track wound up on the Gold Ring 2CD of recordings of Willie.

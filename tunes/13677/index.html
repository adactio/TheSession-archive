Two comments

John Brennan’s Trip To Dublin

My Grandfather was a fiddle player from county Carlow. His fiddle was bought in Dublin I’m guessing in the 1930s. With this tune I’ve tried to imagine his joy as he travelled to Dublin to purchase his new instrument. My daughter Caitlin is now playing his fiddle.

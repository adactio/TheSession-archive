Canyon Moonrise

A lovely waltz by John McGann - see http://www.johnmcgann.com/canyon.html. I changed the eighth note pairs to dotted eighth/sixteenth notes, since that’s more the way it’s played on the Celtic Fiddle Festival album on which it appears.

Two comments

Ossian’s Lament For His Father

From “The Scottish Gael : Or, Celtic Manners. as Preserved Among the Highlanders” by James Logan, published 1831. Fingal, a famous warrior, was Ossian’s father. It’s a slow air - as it’s a lament!

↳ Posted by suskin on September 11th, 2018.

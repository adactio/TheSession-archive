Tater Valsen

Also known as Tinker’s Waltz

A nice waltz composed by Thomas Thomsen , a Danish accordion player from Holstebro area.
He was born circa 1930 but I’m not sure if he’s still with us or not.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K7_CQI2MrQc&ab_channel=Kesse2323

The tune seems to have found its way into the F & A scene “Here in Scotland” which is where I first came across it a few years back.

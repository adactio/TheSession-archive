Mist in the Morning

Just found this. Actually it was always called The Morning Mist. The Irish title, which is how I got it, from the Liverpool Ceili Band is

Ceo an Mhala.

I’d say that it is one of the tunes that I introduced most people to. Practically all the people in Somerset got this from my playing…"Oh, what a nice tune…what’s that called etc.

B2 d d2 d | d2 B def | gfe d2 B | A2 B cBA :|

This ~ jig ~ follows ‘Young Francis Mooney’ and ‘The Humours of Ballydehob’ on Jerry O‘Sullivan’s ’The Gift‘. Don’t have any background to the tune, though it’s one of a few that are kind of ’6/8 slides‘. Not sure if that makes it a ’hop jig‘ or ’hop slide’, since it seems to right out better in 6/8 than 3/4. This is my first transcription by ear that I’ve submitted, so hope all’s kosher! I tried most combinations of the ABC’s in the advanced search and didn’t come up with anything.

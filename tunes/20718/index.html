Terence Lewis’ March to the Gates of Boggo Road Gaol

Composed by ex Queensland policeman Bill ‘Monty’ Montgomery when his corrupt Police Commissioner was finally put behind bars. Monty now lives in Kiama NSW and his tune is popular at local sessions.

Here’s Monty playing it: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QKhTk_TPbeY

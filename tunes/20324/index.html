Re: The Northern Lasses

There is a setting in O‘Neill’s ’Waifs and Strays’ (1922) called “Lady Kelly’s Reel - Up Roscommon” about which he wrote: “This famous reel as played by John Kelly, a fiddler of phenomenal execution now living in San Francisco Cal., is a florid setting of Sergt. James O’Neill’s ”Northern Lasses“ printed in the O’Neill Collections. Kelly, a native of Roscommon, Ireland, says this reel was known as ”Kelly’s Reel“ before his time.”

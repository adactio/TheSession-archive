Montbran

Montbran is a composition by the great and famous Breton flutist Jean-Michel VEILLON on his album Er Pasker. The name of that tune is probably coming from the Tower of Montbran (Tour de Montbran) localised in department of Cotes d’Armor in France.

I transcribed the tune the best I’ve could even if Jean-Michel changes regularly the way of playing his own tune.

The pulsation of that tune is crearly a Scottish dance often danced in Brittany and in France too.

You can listen the tune here at 1m25 : https://youtu.be/QbDHXrpVvrg

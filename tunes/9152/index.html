Six comments

Not a barndance, in fact I’m not so sure 4/4 is right for it, but it’s best to forget the time signature and play the tune.

This tune, written by Brian Finnegan for Sarah Allen, is an incredible slow air, and the name means “The Quiet Mind”.

I’m always impressed by Brian’s creativity with tunes, and how he seems to think outside the box, at least in this tune.

Can anybody tell me how to pronounce the title?

One comment

The Delightful Ranter

Terrific new reel by Tom Campbell, on Tom Campbell Trio’s August 2024 EP. It’s the first tune in the Beluga Whale set. It’s been on constant replay on the car stereo and in my head. I had to transpose it to learn it -- I think he’s playing a B whistle?

Complication: The EP recording as it was posted here a few months back (https://thesession.org/recordings/8114) doesn’t match my digital EP from BandCamp or the album on YouTube etc! As I write this, there’s no track entry for The Beluga Whale set or for either of its tunes. The second tune in the set (or rather an early version of it) is already posted (https://thesession.org/tunes/24024) but not linked to the EP. So I’ll do my best to connect the pieces together.

Tom Campbell posted “The Delightful Ranter” to YouTube shortly after writing it, as Episode 11 of his series “The Voicenote Sessions”:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BTAZkBastic

The track as released on their EP:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2LFyt_PAyUE

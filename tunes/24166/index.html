Two comments

Melanie Murphy’s

I was recently encouraged to submit more Irish tunes. :-)

Most of the better ones are here already, unfortunately.
Anyway, this one has an Irish sounding name and sounds very Irish in style to me.
However, it was composed by Marie Fielding. So, some of you may wish to argue that it’s still a Scottish tune. :-P

Easily transposable to the key of D, if you wish. However, it was composed in “E”.

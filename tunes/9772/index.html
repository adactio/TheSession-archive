The Road to Sligo - source

I transcribed this tune from a session recording of concertina player Kate McNamara at the 2009 Catskills Irish Arts Week. It’s been identified as appearing on her CD (“Are You the Concertina Player?,” https://thesession.org/recordings/3033 ) under this title. See discussion at https://thesession.org/discussions/22187

The Road to Sligo - source

I was just listening to Kate MacNamara’s recording of this lovely tune and decided to learn it. I see that there was a question three years ago as to who wrote it. Tunepal found it in a collection of compositions of the late Brendan Tonra.

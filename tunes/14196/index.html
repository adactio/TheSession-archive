Close. I hear something else, sometimes simpler:
|:F Bb|Bb Am|F Bb|C|Bb|F C|Bb F/A C|Bb/D C/E F:|
|:Bb F Bb F|Bb C|Bb F|C|Bb F/A|Bb C Bb|F C|F:|

For instance, I don’t hear fixed minor chords (no Dm, no Gm, maybe not even an Am - possibly F/A) - but rather inverted major chords and dyads (which can be part of anything). A typical guitar tune, if you ask me.

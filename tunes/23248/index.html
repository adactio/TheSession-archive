The Dirty Newfoundlander

From the playing of Leonard Cheeseman.

Collected by Eric West in the late 1970’s:
https://voicesofplacentiabay.com/leonard_cheeseman.html
https://www.mun.ca/voicesofplacentiabay/singers/15.cheesemanleonard.html

Fragments of the tune remind me of other melodies, though the only one I’ve been able to single out so far is from the opening line of the chorus of the Newfoundland traditional song “Sarah”, in 2/4 time rather than 6/8 time.

“Sarah” was popularized by the group “Buddy Wasisname And The Other Fellers”. One of their members had a daughter named “Sarah”.

Re: The Dirty Newfoundlander

No direct source for X2 or X3.

I was playing around with the tune the past few days and those are some of the resulting settings.

Some phrases inspired by X1, and some deviations from the original source.

For X3, the “BE BE” phrase (in Edor) was a simplified version of the “D/A/D D/A/D” phrase (in Ddor) for X1.

Which reminded me of another single I had heard before, went looking for it today, and found Pete Burton’s.

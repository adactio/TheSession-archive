Three comments

The Broomstick

This tune is related to Down the Broom https://thesession.org/tunes/514 , but sufficiently different to justify a new entry. This setting is from Breanndán Ó Beaglaoich in Ceol Duibhneach by Breanndán Ó Beaglaoich and Niamh Ní Bhaoill #42. Two or three of the settings of tune #514 would probably sit better here.
The tune is in Johnny Leary’s book 254 as Mike Sullivan’s Hornpipe. I’ll post a couple more settings shortly

↳ Posted by brailsford on April 2nd, 2021.

The Broomstick /The Thrashing Reel, X:2

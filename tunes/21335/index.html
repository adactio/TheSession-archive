Strathearn

From the playing of Patsy Reid. Did some googling, according to the “New and scientific self-instructing school for the violin” (George Sanders, Boston, 1847) this tune was written by Niel Gow.

https://patsyreid.bandcamp.com/track/strathearn-gin-i-had-a-bonnie-lassie-the-scolding-wives-of-abertarff-the-old-man-will-never-die-the-lads-of-foss

↳ Posted by DaveF on December 18th, 2021.

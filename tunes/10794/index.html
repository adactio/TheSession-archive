Re: Levenshulme Leap x:2

The first line in the second part is originally
cEEE EFAB | cFFE FFAB | cEEE ECB,A, | B,B,A,B, A,EAB |
I transposed the last two bars partly an octave higher to make it easier on banjo and playable on flute or whistle.

Eight comments

Thin Ice

A nice fun irregular tune which calls for the player(s) stomp your feet one after the other at the end of every phrase. Composed by Meg Browning and released on one of Niel Browning’s CDs a few years ago.

Also it’s a bugger to write out in ABC because of the wonky time signatures.

↳ Posted by Rowan Folk on May 20th, 2018.

Re: Thin Ice

To be clear about the stomping, it doesn’t matter if it’s left-right or right-left, but it should be like two crotchets.

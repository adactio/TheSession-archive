The Woody Head

This Australian waltz was written, probably in the late 1990s, by banjo player John Spencer ( R.I.P.).

John was part of a ( still subsisting) bunch of folkies, players, singers, dancers who meet every few months on the Northern NSW coast at a spot called Woody Head and sing, dance, swim, snorkel and play for a long weekend

John wrote the waltz in honour of that gathering.

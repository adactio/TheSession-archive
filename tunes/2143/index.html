Tigh Raifeartaigh (another setting)

This great Michael Rooney composed tune is demonstrated by Garry Shannon on the Comhaltaslive programme (28 March to 3 April 2005) at http://www.comhaltaslive.ie. However, he plays it in G as shown below. On checking Michael’s CD “Draiocht” I found that he plays it in “F” so “you pays your money and makes your own choice” !!

Re: Tír Rafartaigh

I’ve edited the name of the tune to Tír Rafartaigh as this is what the composer, Michael Rooney, calls it. Tír Rafartaigh is the townland he grew up in, in Scotstown, Co. Monaghan.

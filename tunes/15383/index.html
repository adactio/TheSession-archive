Emmet The Piper

Composed by Charlie Lennon, who can be heard performing his tune with Johnny Connolly on An Mileoidean Scaoilte:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQ0ZsImDaiY

A wonderful tune that I can’t get out of my head. I’m surprised it hasn’t been recorder more often.

Emmet The Piper, X:2

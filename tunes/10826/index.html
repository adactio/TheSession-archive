Jackson’s Maggot

This is another one of the tunes accredited to Walker (Piper) Jackson (1716-1798) I believe this tune was published by Samuel Lee of Dublin sometime around 1840’s. However the book has long since been out of print.
Maggot means a “Dram” a measure of whiskey or some other spirit.

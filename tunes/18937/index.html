Aileen’s Slow Jig

This is a beautiful slow jig by Calum Stewart (copyrighted). I first heard it played at a session here in Colorado. Not sure if it is common elsewhere.

↳ Posted by Ryan Farris on January 25th, 2020.

Aileen’s Slow

Written by Scottish uilleann piper and and flute player Calum Stewart for his daughter.

From the album “Wooden Flute & Fiddle” by Calum Stewart & Lauren MacColl. First tune of the 7th track.

https://laurenmaccoll.bandcamp.com/track/aileens

↳ Posted by DaveF on October 26th, 2020.

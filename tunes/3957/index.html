Drogheda Bay, X:5

This is the version played by Patsy Hanley after Major Moran’s. There is a link to the recording in the comments above and also in the comments to Major Moran’s here on the session. I like this version especially for the way the first measure follows so perfectly after Major Moran’s and for the last two measures of the B part. Thanks to David Levine for identifying the tune for me as Big Pat’s. Note that the tune is in E (natural) minor, not E dorian as some of the other versions have it.

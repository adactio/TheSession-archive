Laxo Burn

One of the ‘Common Tunes for Scottish Sessions’ compiled by the Boston Scottish Fiddle Club, Spring 2015.
Notation from John Chambers abc website (http://trillian.mit.edu/~jc/tmp/Tune-12515.abc).
The Fiddler’s Companion (http://www.ibiblio.org/fiddlers/LAS_LAZ.htm) supplies the following:
Composed by Scottish national champion fiddler Arthur Scott Robertson…. Laxo Burn was his favourite (sea) trout-fishing stream in the Shetlands. Laxo Burn flows into Laxo Voe. The ‘A’ part is shared with “The Chamberlain,” “Reel à/de Rémi,” and “Reel de l’hôtelier,” however, the ‘B’ part appears original to Robertson. Miller (Fiddler’s Throne), 2004; No. 192, pg. 121.

Ed Reavy’s

From Larry Redican manuscript shared on Facebook by “Larry Redican’s Fiddle” -- https://www.facebook.com/440307056097641/photos/a.440348336093513/1762173767244290/

Dated April 1st 1964, though I suspect that’s probably just when it was notated. The tune is marked “By Ed Reavy”, and the page of music (which also includes “Jim Erwin’s Hornpipe” before this one) says “Composed and written especially for Jim Erwin by Ed Reavy”.

↳ Posted by Sol Foster on November 2nd, 2018.

Ed Reavy’s, X:3

This my setting in A dorian.
The atmosphere is very different to the A mixolydian.
Would it deserve a different name?
It is dorian inasmuch there is one f, and an f# it is, in this overall hexatonic tune.
You could play it in B min. too.

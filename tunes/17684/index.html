Re: Hermione’s

I think it’s different enough to be it’s own tune. It might just have been inspired by parts of The Castle. Some parts being transposed to Emin, some remaining in the original key (Ador).

For instance, the phrases in the B Part which go to “e2” (first bar) and and “d2” (3rd bar) feel very much like what “The Castle” does (which is how I noticed the resemblance), albeit, in a different key signature.

In the A Part, this tune drops down into the low A’s & B’s like The Castle does, BUT, it isn’t the end of a phrase in this case, and, that tune is in Ador whereas this in Emin.

You can see the chords to a version of The Castle here:
http://www.vashonceltictunes.net/irish/vcpsm.htm?seanryan.jpg

Re: Hermione’s

Looking at it more, the A Parts start similarly in structure, and they digress after the 2nd bar. They both go down to lower notes, but this happens at different parts of the A Part.

The B Parts are far closer in structure, although not exact. Transposing this tune to Amin/Ador wouldn’t result exactly in The Castle (not the setting in the vashinceltictunes link anyways, some versions of The Castle do go a bit differently).

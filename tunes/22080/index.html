Tom Byrne’s

This is reel number 4 from Wexford town accordionist and prolific composer, Tom Byrne. It has sometimes been transposed to D to make it a bit more multi-instrument friendly. Tom has composed 3 jigs and about 10 reels to date. He hasn’t named them, so I’ve numbered them in order of their composition and included the numbered name as an alternate title.

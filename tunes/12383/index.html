Appalachen

This tune is actually a Swedish Polksa performed by the folk super-group Vasen. It’s a great tune, and can definitely be played in a more Irish style.

↳ Posted by Nate Sabat on December 26th, 2012.

Played in a more Irish style…

Yeah, why not? Played as an Irish reel, you would most probably play those pairs of crotchets as groups of quavers, like “~g2gf” instead of “g2f2”, or “BGGB” instead of “B2G2”, for example.

Those occasional Bflats in the B part are really cool!

I’m not an expert on Swedish music, but a polska is usually in 3/4. Maybe there’s such thing as a 4/4 polska, but I’ve never heard of one. I would say it’s probably a schottis (schottische).

↳ Posted by CreadurMawnOrganig on December 28th, 2012.

There are (a few) polskas in duple time, but this isn’t one of them. It’s (from what I gather) a composition by Väsen’s Mikael Marin, and “Appalachen” translates as (you guessed it) “Appalachian” - so Schottis it ain’t either.

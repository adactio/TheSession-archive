A Farruquiña, X:2

This is a polca from the repertoire of Os Campaneiros, a well known family band from Villagarcia in Galicia who performed from the 1920s to the 1980s. It should be in 2/4 and is played at a good pace, at least 120bpm. X:2 is the full four part version as I have it, watch out for the extra beat at the end of part two.

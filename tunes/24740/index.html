Two comments

Amy’s

This waltz, composed by Wexford’s Paddy Furlong was named for Amy Hedderman, a niece of Paddy’s wife. It was one of a selection of Paddy Furlong’s compositions published in ‘The Wexford Tunebook’.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on September 4th, 2024.

Amy’s, X:2

It’s the same tune - I just shortened the look of it by indicating first and second endings for each part.

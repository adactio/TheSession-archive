Seven comments

Earl Of Hyndford

A great reel popular in Cape Breton fiddling. Actually in Cmin. Found on “The Dances Down Home” by Joe Cormier. The band Contraversial plays it in Amin, which is probably better for a more Irish feel.

↳ Posted by Daniel Parker on May 6th, 2017.

The Earl Of Hyndford, X:5

Here’s the tune as it appears in the Skye Collection.

It’s a bit of a tongue twister for your fingers (is there a proper term for this? apparently “finger fumbler” exists for sign language…)—or at least, it is on piano. Fun to play fast, though the melody is also quite haunting when played more slowly. I really like the tune either way.

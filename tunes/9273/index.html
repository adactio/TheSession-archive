The Kinard

Martin Mulvihill plays this on his album ‘Traditional Irish Fiddling From County Limerick’ (https://thesession.org/recordings/2175). The sleeve notes say, “Kinard is located in Glin, a mile from Ballyloughlin. There used to be a popular dance held regularly at a crossroads nearby. ‘The Kinard Polka’ was a favourite tuen there, and Martin picked it up from listening to the local players.”

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on June 16th, 2013.

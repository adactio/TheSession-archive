La Valse Matique, X:2

Originally composed and recorded by Benoît Bourque in Am and played on a 3-row button accordion(GCF), here adapted to be played on a 1-row melodion (D, the most common in Québec). Chords learned from Bourque. According to Bourque, the title is a play on words, “The Asthmatic Waltz”.

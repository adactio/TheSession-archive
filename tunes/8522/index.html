Planxty Fitzgerald

I learnt this at Meitheal Summer School last year. It was one of the big finale pieces we all played together at the last-night concert. Imagine about 80 fiddles, flutes, whistles, accordions, concertinas, banjos, one set of Uilleann pipes and a piano all playing one huge lush arrangement of this tune.

Well, I liked it!

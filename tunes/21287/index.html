Re: The Bragging Man

When I arrived in Aberdeen as a student in the early 1970s, I checked out the books of traditional music in the University library and scanned a few pages from some of them. One was “Old Irish Folk Music and Songs” collected by P.W. Joyce and this was #900, described as “a Cork reel” from “P. Carew’s mss”.

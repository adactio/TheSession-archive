↳ Posted by DaveF on May 12th, 2021.

Clare’s, X:4

My first attempt at an ABC transcription. Based on the version I heard from The Ghost Piper on Youtube, which I really enjoy. He plays a short rendition of this tune as part of a set with the Tam Lin (Glasgow) reel, and throws in some nice bluesy downward bends on some of the high F#s. Also, he played it on a low F whistle, as do I, so it sounds in D minor concert pitch reading this notation using standard D whistle fingerings.

Two comments

Come Back!

Track 5b from Andy Cutting’s self-titled album himself. The transcription is based on his first time through the tune. As you can see, many phrases are variations on the same theme. The descending phrases in bars 3 and 11 of the B part are played in harmony on the recording (one third above). I’ve chosen the lower voice.

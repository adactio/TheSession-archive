The tune is found in various O’Neill’s compilations, including
S:O’Neill – Dance Music of Ireland: 1001 Gems (1907), No. 933

One wonders if the four undotted notes in A5 are an intentional “anti-ornament”, or an uncorrected scrivener’s error.

Transpose the last six notes of the B part up an octave to make the tune accessible to the flute/whistle.

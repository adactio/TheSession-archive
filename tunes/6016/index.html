The Celtic Trader

Rege and Barb Malady ran a shop called Celtic Trader in Charlotte, NC, USA for 19 years, and will retire the end of July 2006. They sold Celtic recordings, supplies, and small instruments and hosted a great session.
This tune was written in honor of Rege and Barb on the occasion of their final session on 27 July 2006.

Re: Valse A Donald

This tune is indeed a composition of Claude Methe’s who recorded it with the group Manigance in 1991 (in the key of A). The recording is available at
http://www.mainefiddlecamp.org/additional-claude-methe-tunes/
The sheet music posted there, however, does not completely match the recording. Here is my attempt at a transcription of the recording:

X:1
T:Valse \`a Donald
D:Manigance, Nouvelles manigances, 1991 (track 11)
N:Written in memory of Donald Masse
R:waltz
M:3/4
L:1/8
K:A

Six comments

This tune is also known as “The Pretty Brown Girl” (in Irish “An Cailin Deasdonn”), under which name it is in O’Neill’s “The Dance Music of Ireland”.

↳ Posted by fidicen on January 30th, 2003.

X: 2 & 3 “Óró Bog Liom Í” ~ S: Dermot Byrne and played truly ‘single’, without repeats

Dermot plays this low, in D, X: 2, a G transcription for comparison follows, X: 3…

The nature of this tune, the phrasing, it could also be written out as 12/8, as a slide, a phrase conscious time signature for slides that was not in use before the 20th Century…

↳ Posted by ceolachan on August 15th, 2013.

Friendsville Reel

I learned this tune from listening to an old fiddler playing in an informal jam session in Friendsville Maryland in the early 1990’s. It is a version of Soldiers Joy in my opinion, but the F naturals give it a nice unique sound where I consider it a different tune than Soldiers Joy. Since I didnt get the name of the tune from the fellow, I posted it here as the Friendsville Reel. It is very characteristic of many tunes from that rural, mountainous area… Scottish sounding, simple, catchy and easy to embellish.

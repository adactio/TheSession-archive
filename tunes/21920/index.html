Noting that this tune has been posted without comment, I thought it worth noting that Winston Fitzgerald followed The Pattern Day, https://thesession.org/tunes/15855, with Panel Mine in a now classic Cape Breton pairing, a practice followed later, in recordings, by fiddlers Jerry Holland, Brenda Stubbert, Howie MacDonald and Marc Boudreaux.

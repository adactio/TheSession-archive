One comment

Amsterdam

A beautiful slip jig composed by Remco Sietsema, transcribed from Mel Biggs’ playing on a D/G melodeon. Also works nicely on the flute or on a B/C box with G in the bass replacing the B.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ypeYsxS0KV8

The video description includes links to slower versions by Mel and to the original version in F major played by Remco himself on a C/F box.

Three comments

Olympic

This is an amazing composition by Mark O’Connor. Granted, he’s a violinist and a fiddler, so there are some unusual technical things happening here. But it’s a phenomenal piece, and serves as a duet with Natalie MacMaster on her 1999 album In My Hands. The track is a standout one, to me. The ending finale is nothing short of “fireworks”, or a waterfall or something. Which is appropriate, as this was composed for the 1996 Summer Olympics ceremony in Atlanta. It’s amazing what just two fiddles can do with a guitarist, bassist, drummer & pianist supporting them.

The transcription is partly how I play it, and partly from the official sheet music by Mark. Of course, if you listen to the arrangement on Natalie MacMaster’s album, it doesn’t end here. This is just the basic tune. The recording features an extended bridge section, with little solos, duels, battles, and funky phrases. Mark tends to arrange it differently for each performance, which is often with with a large group of musicians, college/high school students, civic ensembles, etc., or more famous artists. After the bridge, the tune repeats again before ending in the explosive crescendo.

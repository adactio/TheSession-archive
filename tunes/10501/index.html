Shropshire Rounds

The reason I listed Cheshire Rounds last week was that the Cheshire Rounds already here had elements of a tune I know as Shropshire Rounds. So it’s only fair to list Shropshire Rounds as well.
Like Cheshire Rounds I’ve got Shropshire Rounds from William Chappell’s Popular Music of the Olden TIme, and like Cheshire Rounds I have been playing it for years.
Below it’s added in a slightly revised version of Chappell’s arrangement, for two instruments.

The Shropshire Rounds, X:5

This is the version I know, and the settings in Young (1710) and Walsh (1756) are quite close.

This is the first 3/2 I really came to like. I learned it from the playing of John Spiers and Jon Boden’s album “Through and Through”. The full set is: the Shropshire Rounds / the Rusty Gully / the Three-footed Chestnut.

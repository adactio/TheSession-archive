Greengrass

This is the tune following Castlerock Road on Damien O’Kane and Dave Kosky’s “The Mystery Inch” album. A bit of an interpretation since the banjo recording makes it difficult to distinguish ornamentation from melody.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pOB0ascAldg

Re: Lament For Owen Roe O’Neill

Peter Carberry ( Trad music from Longford) and Nollaigh Casey ( Lead the Knave) have a different tune under this title, which I have been told is in fact a lament for a different Eoghan Rua - an 18th Century Irish Poet. The anglicised names are, respectively Owen Roe O‘Neill ( the tune listed here on The Session and apparently written by O’Carolan) and Owen Roe O’Sullivan (the tune played by Carberry and Casey). Both men are the subject of historical notes in Wikipedia under their various versions of their names. I am trying to resolve the apparent inconsistency of the attributions of the two tunes. Does anyone have any information about this ?

↳ Posted by John Brewins on April 12th, 2020.

Lament For Owen Roe O’Neill

Here is the other tune by this name that people have been requesting for. Owen Roe O’Neill was the leader of Ireland but was poisoned in 1649. This Lamentation is published in Petrie’s Ancient Music Of Ireland. This version recorded by Na Filf, Nollaig Casey to name a few. Also, when you get to the end, play the first section again only once.

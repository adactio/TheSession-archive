Pocket Size Pal

Hi there, I can´t say a lot about this tune. I discovered it a few days ago in spotify and I´m really enjoying listening to it. It is from the “Harem Scarem” cd:
“The Birnam Witch Project”
I really like it from the first time I listened to it, I looked for it here or in google with abc patterns and i was not able to find it.
I didn´t expect it would be such a “strange” tune. I´ll be grateful if somebody with more experience than me transcribing music have a look to my transcription and correct it if necessary. I´m not sure at all about the key (Amaj?) or about the type of tune…
I am not used to these kind of transcriptions/tunes and i have written it the way i find it has more sense.
As i wrote before, I can´t say much more about this tune, i have not found information about who wrote it or where it comes from so, if anybody knows anything about it…

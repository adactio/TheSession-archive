Re: Muschat’s Cairn

Muschat’s Cairn was the name of an unremarkable rickle of stones in Holyrood Park. It’s a memorial to a woman called Margaret (or Ailie) Hall, murdered by her husband Nichol Mushat in 1720. The early history of the cairn is uncertain but it seems to have been moved and re-recognised by Walter Scott. In 2024 a campaign by Sara Sheridan and Andy Arthur succeeded in having it renamed as Margaret Hall’s Cairn.

James Porteous was a Dumfrieshire man but spent a year in Edinburgh as a young man so presumably this tune dates from this period.

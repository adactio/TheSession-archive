Two comments

Chinook Winds

This is the first tune on track 2 (Wind Dancers) of Energy Islands by Fara.
It was composed by Jeana Leslie and is played at a tempo of about 80 bars a minute, slightly faster than the fastest setting here.
The tune is played three times on the recording, firstly accompanied by a continuous high A and a staccato low A, then by understated chords (starting on the IV) and lastly by fuller chords (starting with a vi-IV-I-V progression) and harmonies.

↳ Posted by Scottie on January 23rd, 2022.

Re: The Bull Ring

This is definitely a fiddle tune, and as written does not work on a whistle. It uses the complete octaves from G, to G and the one above from G to g, but also the b above that. So even if you tried to use the lowest note on the whistle in place of the G, you still wouldn’t have enough notes above it. I think the only way around the problem is to rewrite the low notes in bar 7. Fiddlers don’t always compose tunes with whistlers in mind.

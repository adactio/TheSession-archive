Four comments

Gerald Campbell’s

From the playing of Gerald Campbell.

Might go by another name, or, might be one of his. Haven’t been able to find its’ name yet.

First tune from this 1975 audio clip (set with Mussels In The Corner, and, Around The House/A Trip To Boston):
https://www.itma.ie/digital-library/sound/untitled-singles-gerald-campbell-accordion

Gerald plays the B Part without repeats, and the A Part several times between B Parts.

Sol Foster helped identify Around The House, and noted that this unidentified one bears a distant resemblance to Bride’s Jig:
https://thesession.org/tunes/12461

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on September 2nd, 2019.

Gerald Campbell’s, X:2

Squared up, few pick-up notes added, and transposed to Edor.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on September 2nd, 2019.

Gerald Campbell’s, X:3

Setting in Ador, with syncopation removed.

Turns out the tune is a syncopated variant of the Newfoundland single “Paddy’s Jig”.

Also bears a further resemblance to another Newfoundland single, “Drive‘r Down” (aka, Shootin’ The Bull).

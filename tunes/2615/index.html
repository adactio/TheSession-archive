Delia Crowley’s

This tune I learned from a recording of the outstanding fiddler Maeve Donnelly. The first part of the tune is in E Mixolydian and the second part is in E Major (with the D#s) - unfortunately, I had to list the key as “A Major” because there was no choice for E Mixolydian in the submission form, and I have no clue as how to write a natural sign in ABC notation! Maeve plays this as the last tune in a medley of three tunes, which I will post soon. The first tune in the set is “The New Road,” followed by “The Yellow Tinker” and then “Delia Crowley’s.”

Enjoy!

Lynnsey

Gan Ainm

This comes from a private recording of Patsy Hanly playing tin whistle via the Cathal McConnell collection at the ITMA. I particularly love the A-part of this tune. I’ve no more information about it unfortunately other than that the recording was, if I remember right, from 1975.

PS: I know there’s low C in the A part which doesn’t make sense if we consider Patsy was playing a tin whistle. It was obviously pitched in the higher octave on his recording but fiddles, boxes, banjos, concertinas etc would be expected to play the lower note. You know yourself.

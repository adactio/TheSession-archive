Ichabod’s Last Ride

From the playing of Flapjack.

Composed by their mandolin player, Jay Edmunds.

There are quite a few recordings of this tune being played live on YouTube and elsewhere, a few tutorial videos online, and, a few studio recordings (e.g., Flapjack, Three Bean Salad, Round The House).

Seems to have made it into the repertoire of some Contradance musicians.

Flapjack, first tune:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Pyv6TwZrxo

Three Bean Salad, third tune:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Peo2NsVgP8

Round The House, second tune:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZzAkpmim3Qo

Odd Sundays Garage Band (e.g., Judy Linsenberg, Shira Kammen and Patti Cobb), currently the last recording here:

Five comments

The Funny Whistle

A nice Bminor slowish reel - I got this from Colm O’Rua at a banjo mandolin workshop on Skye in 2004. It is the second reel in a set on Daimh’s second album Pirates of Puirt. Said to be written by Davy Garrett who I believe is a piper and author of a Scottish whistle tutor book.

↳ Posted by Edgar Bolton on October 21st, 2009.

An Fhideag Airgid…

nudlok, what you’ve submitted here is a different tune. The Weavers skillfully go in to “The Silver Whistle” by Davie Garrett ( “An Fhideag Airgid” https://thesession.org/tunes/9977 ) after The Johnstown Reel; so skilfully you might have thought it was a variation, but no.

Missing Marc

Composed by Andrea Beaton the week after Cheticamp fiddler Marc Boudreau died aged just 30 in April 2016. I learnt the tune around then but was reminded of it by a discussion comment: https://thesession.org/discussions/48378#comment969237

This transcription is the tune as it was originally composed.

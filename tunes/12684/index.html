Gan Ainm

I lifted this tune from a Newfoundland Tourism commercial entitled “Conversation”. It can be seen on Youtube at http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=44wMG9b2_JY

Of course I am looking for the name of this tune. The original Youtube poster could only supply the names of the musicians who performed it, Jim Payne and Fergus O’Byrne. And who knows if they learned it from someone or made it up on the spot. Anyhow I thought it was a clever tune and so I’m posting it here.

PS All the Bs should be flat. It is in C Mixolydian as far as I can tell, but there was no pull-down menu option for C Mixolydian so I selected C Major.

↳ Posted by noinin on April 13th, 2013.

It was, according to “Amy Fisher” (who works for Newfoundland tourism, it seems - and may have been involved in the ad) composed for the promotional video - she says so in the blurb to this clip of the two performing the piece:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m7Zbui-fdF8

The tune is hexatonic - no E notes. If you want the Bs to appear as flats, you could have selected F major or D minor - neither would be correct, but they would be closer than C major.

The tune as posted is better named as Cmix. (I think?)
This gives anyone playing chord accompaniment more chance of playing the correct chords.

I’ll paste into ‘easyabc’ to move it up a tone to Dmix making it more session friendly.

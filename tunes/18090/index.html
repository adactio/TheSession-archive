McGoldrick’s No. 5

This tune has been attributed to Mike McGoldrick, and has been played with another supposedly McGoldrick tune (McGoldrick’s No.4), but I haven’t found any identifying info about either of them, other than a YT video where McSherry, Doyle, and McGoldrick play them live. I play these tunes and thought I’d drop them here to see if anyone can come up with names, origins.

Re: McGoldrick’s

Not to be too much of a pedant, in the video, McGoldrick is playing an E whistle using Gmaj fingerings. So, Amaj is the sounding key, but the music is written in Gmaj for use with an E whistle.

I’ve looked through all the available McCusker materials and not found this tune. I agree, it certainly sounds like a tune he’d have written.

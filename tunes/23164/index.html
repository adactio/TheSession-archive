Three comments

The Atavist

An incredible tune by Alan Reid from the Arum album. There are two chordal patterns that introduce the tune, one of which is repeated later between first and second instances of the main polka, but is not repeated before the third instance.

The first time through the tune, he plays a significant variation in the B part, which does not recur when the tune repeats so I’ve left it out of this transcription:
Ac|AB G2 | G2 B/c/d | da gf- | f2 af |
gd cd | Ac BG | A/B/c cd/c/ | AF Ac |
AB G2 | G2 B/c/d | gd g/a/b/a/ | gd gb |
af dc | AF FC | D/E/F FA/B/ | c2 |]

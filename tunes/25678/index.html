Re: Dornie Ferry

Do you play it straight like this? It would be more common for the pairs of quavers to be notated with the ‘long short’ swing of a strathspey. A2 A>B e>d (3efg | B<G d>G B>A G>B

↳ Posted by bagpie on June 26th, 2025.

Re: Dornie Ferry

No, I don’t. I play it with swing, about the same as in the mentioned recording. I just prefer straight notation, the swing is implied.

↳ Posted by Thomas Ellison on June 27th, 2025.

Re: Dornie Ferry

ABC notation doesn’t know that the swing is implied so playback will sound like a reel, not so good for those who don’t read the dots. I’ll add a setting with the swing notated.

Dornie Ferry, X:2

A setting of the tune with the swing notated, as would be usual with strathspeys. Although the tune has been published as traditional it may have been written by PM William Fergusson.

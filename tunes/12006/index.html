Four comments

Sliabh Carberry

From Peter Carberry, Angelina Carberry & John Blake - Memories from the Holla. I can’t decide if this is just a version of the Flowers of Red Hill https://thesession.org/tunes/442. It certainly has the same |gedB GABd|| turnaround and other similar phrases, but there are some differences. I also got the album off iTunes so I don’t have the sleeve notes. Do they shed any light on the tune? Presumably they didn’t have a name for it so just named it themselves? If the general consensus is that it is just a version of “Flowers” then we’ll get Jeremy to delete this and I’ll transfer this to the comments of that tune.

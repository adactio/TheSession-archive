↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on July 2nd, 2023.

El Manxoyu, X:2

Original key (one semitone down, they use an Eb flute), and time signature.

The A part of the tune is traditional, it can be found in the “Cancionero de Torner”, a collection of Asturian songs and tunes, numbers 360 and 361. Marcos Llope, one of the founders of Llan de Cubel and the band’s flute player at the time.

Second tune of the set.

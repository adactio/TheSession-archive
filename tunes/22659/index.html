The Marching

This waltz was composed by Gorey’s Tommy Kinsella, probably best known as the composer of The Gorey March. Thanks to Tommy’s daughter Sinead Tobin, who provided copies of Tommy’s tunes for inclusion in The Wexford Tunes Project.

Re: The Marching

The Gorey pipe band had many fine pipers Nigel and Tommy wrote a nice few slow pieces and retreat marches for them. County Wexford would be unusual in the southern part of Ireland for historically having had pipe bands in almost every area and there are still 3 pipe bands in the County, along with one in Arklow which is a few miles north in the southeast of County Wicklow.

Master Seamus, X:3

This setting was found under the title ‘Ciaran O Raghallaigh’ in Tommy Peoples 1986 Book, 50 Irish Fiddle Tunes ISBN 1-85720-021-7 on page 23.

It looks like a slightly simplified version of the original. I’m not aware Tommy Peoples ever recorded it anywhere else.

Two comments

Trip To Guadeloupe

A quirky little hornpipe from Thompson’s Compleat Collection of 200 Favourite Country Dances, vol. 2 (London, 1765)
2nd part has a bit of a classical feel and needs a d#. No problem on fiddle, mandolin etc. And manageable on whistle with half holing. I’d love to know the history of it. 1765 seems pretty early for such a funky tune!

↳ Posted by Andy Hornby on January 24th, 2019.

Trip To Guadeloup, X:2

X:1 is a nice setting, but has some differences from the version in Thompson’s Complete where it’s given in Eb. X:2 is closer and is at http://www.rudemex.co.uk/Tune%20library/ABC/Trip%20to%20Guadaloup.abc, where it’s notated in D with slight variations from Thompson in bar 8 of the A and bars 5 & 8 of the B .

Ocean Child

Outstanding tune written by Brian Finnegan for Yoko Nozaki. He plays it in Ab minor on Ancora. Played on an F# flute maybe? Transcribed in Emin here. Some of the syncopation is tricky to transcribe - I’ve done my best but you’d be better off listening to it.

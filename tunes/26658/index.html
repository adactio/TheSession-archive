Packie Byrne’s

I found this tune about 2 years ago now; don’t remember how I came by it. I’ve cleaned up the ABC notation from what I found online and added some bowing and fingering.
From the original, it’s from Lucy Farr’s ‘Heart and Home’ album, and the transcription I found was by Bernie Stocks.
I’ve confirmed that it’s on ‘Heart and Home’ but I haven’t found a version of that album I can listen to, so I can’t say that my transcription is accurate.

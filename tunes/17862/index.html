Three comments

Dolar Durba

Fairly sure this tune was composed by Eoin Duignan, as it was on his album “Pagan Irish”.

The second polka in the set (The Young Prince Of Ulster) is similar, has the same A Part, but the B Part is different.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on April 7th, 2019.

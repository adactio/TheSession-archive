Uncle Dano’s

This is from Joanie Madden’s ‘Whistle on the Wind’. In the liner noter, Joanie writes: “In 1979 my great-uncle Dano took me to my first Fleadh Cheoil up in the North in Buncrana, Co. Donegal. I heard this first hornpipe there, and having no name, christened it after him.”

On the cd, it is followed by a hornpipe of her own called ‘The Midnight Hornpipe’.

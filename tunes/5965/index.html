“The Shelf”

I’m sure this was requested a while back. This comes from the playing of Desi Wilkinson & Gerry O‘Connor. Desi got the tune from fiddlers Charlie and Paddy O’Neill. A particular favourite with Northern flute players, going right up to the high “d” in places.
There’s a slightly different version on the new “Teada” CD, which I’ll post in “Recordings” later.

Shelf #2?

I first heard this tune at a late-night session a few years ago while attending Irish Week at the Swannanoa Gathering. John Skelton was playing a blazing set of tunes with another player (can’t recall his name, but I seem to recall that he is the nephew of Mairead ni Mhaonidh of Altan.) The next day in class I asked John what the tune was and he said it was the Shelf and said he learned it from a Skylark recording called Raining Bicycles. I’ve been unable to find that recording, but I did find it on a compilation cd called Come Dance With Me In Ireland. They play this tune as a medley, bookending it around another polka. Can anyone outh there tell me the name of that tune?

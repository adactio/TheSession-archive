Jon Reville’s

A very nice and lively polka composed by the banjo player Jon Reville from Wexford. The Reville family are well known in trad circles in the South-East. Here he is on a Youtube recording from a recent Phil Murphy festival in Carrig-on-Bannow, playing this composition as the second tune of the set. (The first reel BTW, The Hectic, has already been uploaded and is another composition of Jon’s:

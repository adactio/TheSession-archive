Shetland Rose

This great tune, written by Esko Järvela after a trip to Shetland, is very much a mashup of the styles of Finland and Shetland. It’s a cross between a reel and a polska, and can be beat either in 3 or in 4 depending on how you feel, as shown in the two different but identical transcriptions. It’s named after the Shetland Rose: not a flower, but the name for empty beer cans found at the side of the street in Shetland.

Re: Shetland Rose

Definitely not 12/8 which is four groups of 3, whereas here we have three groups of 4.
As jeff_lindqvist points out above, it’s in 3/4. By doubling all the note values, as in the transcriptions here, it becomes 3/2 (and most definitely NOT 6/4).

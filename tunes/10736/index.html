↳ Posted by nsirola on October 12th, 2010.

Where’s the Wife

I believe this is the name of this tune, it is not actually a hornpipe, as I heard an old recording of Simey Doherty, (another great Donegal fiddle player) the brother of Johnny Doherty. He played it as a ‘Highland’ (as some would call a ‘Fling’), in the Key of A, I think. The recording itself was done by RTE radio, & Broadcasted sometime in the 1970’s. It should be some where still in their archives.

One comment

Kenella

Here’s a lovely ‘Continental’ style waltz composed by Tommy Kinsella of Gorey. i think that Tommy’s friend, accordionist Tommy Kennedy, played a part in it’s composition - hence the title which combines their surnames, Kennedy and Kinsella - Kenella. Tommy Kinsella is probably best known for composing the popular ‘Gorey March’. This particular tune in my humble opinion has the sound of a ‘Buttons and Bows’ tune from their heyday.

Eleven comments

“The Anderson Family Fling”

I had considered naming it “Title Pending” but was told by the big ‘J’ that my only option was “Gan Ainm”. This came easy, which had me in doubt as to its origins, but it was put together with a certain twosome in mind, a father and son team who squeeze together. So, with that in mind it is designed to be an easy squeeze for tina players, and offering tons of possibilities for variation and ornamentation without any need for any difficult cross fingerings…

The tough thing about naming something after someone is the thought that they might feel some obligation to play a tune they might actually think is cack. Well guys, no obligation, but if it is cack, keep it to your self. My ego is too fragile to take too much direct criticism of my small efforts and this offering. Hey, the intention is good, but I know that one about good intentions, so don’t remind me…

It would be nice if you liked it though ~ ;-)

I Was Born for Sport (jig)

Another tune from O’Shaughnessy & Bradley’s duet album. It seems taken from George Petrie’s “The Petrie Collection of the Ancient Music of Ireland,” originally published in the late 19th century. It’s a very attractive jig, which would sound great on pipes.

↳ Posted by slainte on August 25th, 2005.

Dermy Diamond and Paul O’Shaugnessy played this in tonight’s Cobblestones session. I asked Paul the name but couldn’t hear his answer, due to background noise. Glad to have found it hear. Great tune.

↳ Posted by Paahto on April 4th, 2009.

I was born for Sport

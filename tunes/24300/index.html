Gan Ainm

From the playing of Broom Bezzums.

Second tune of their Christmas Day In da Mornin’ set (begins ~1min 35s):

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QtwRj6nHBzM

I find this tune familiar, though I cannot seem to find its name.

Have tried searching for it in Ddor/Edor/Ador using TunePal, FolkFriend, and, by manually checking various note phrases here at TheSession. No luck finding its title yet.

There are two tunes in particular which come to mind in terms of resemblance, the first is The Sailor’s Wife (it’s not that tune), and I cannot remember the name of the second.

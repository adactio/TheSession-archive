Nine comments

Morfa Mawddach

A 48-bar Welsh jig.

“Morfa” = bog, fen or sea-marsh. “Mawddach” = river in the county of Gwynedd, North Wales.

There is a railway station called Morfa Mawddach on the south side of the Mawddach estuary (the town of Barmouth/Abermaw is situated on the north side).

http://www.streetmap.co.uk/map.srf?x=262900&y=314275&z=120&sv=262900,314275&st=4&ar=y&mapp=map.srf&searchp=ids.srf&dn=549&ax=262900&ay=314275&lm=0

Useful as second (or third) tune to play for 48-bar dances: e.g. “Bridge of Athlone”

X: 2 “Morfa Mawddach”

No, the accidentals aren’t a problem, even on winds, but I am scratching my head over this one. I’ve done an initial search and haven’t yet found it amongst our Welsh sources. I quite like the accidentals and the fairground/calliope nature of this one. I’d love to find out more about its history, so I’ll keep nosing around and will likely contact some friends.

Maybe it isn’t actually Welsh per se and has just been named after the geography or the railway station? I’d love to know.

An Odd Duck (or A Right Quare Dog)

Found this strange/beautiful little tune on Caoimhin O’Raghallaigh’s solo CD “Where The One-Eyed Man is King”. It straddles the strange middle ground between 6/8 March and Descriptive Piece. You can almost hear the sad hobbling of said Canine…Enjoy!

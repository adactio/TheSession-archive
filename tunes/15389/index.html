Further Along The Line

A scottish/bluegrass reel very fun to play composed by the late fiddler Johnny Cunningham, it’s in his first album “Thoughts From Another World”.

Johnny said that he wrote this tune during his very first times in the U.S.A. after seen the late and great bluegrass fiddler Kenny Baker play.

Johnny also said that he wrote the tune to show the similarity between scots and american fiddle music, and that was his first attempt to try to fuse the two music togheter.

The tune finish with the A part.

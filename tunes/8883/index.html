Barrel Rafferty’s

I’m sure this is a version of “Jessie over the Bridge” or “Over the Bridge to Bessie”: https://thesession.org/tunes/3968/comments

Tom “Barrel” Rafferty was Mike Rafferty’s father, who played the flute and pipes.

Barrel Rafferty’s, X:3

I first heard this on an old CD called Celtic Winds, it’s smooth and flowing and a lot of fun to play, especially with triplets for ornamentation. Very nice with octave mandolin/bouzouki accompaniment.

Re: Barrel Rafferty’s

Thanks for the mention all those years ago swisspiper. Lowlander, the original recording was on Light Through the Leaves which was later reissued with track changes (and some tracks omitted) as Celtic Winds. I do think it’s the same reel as the one we call Master McDermott’s. Similar but not the same as Boys of the Lough. Mick Moloney (rip) produced and was the accompanist on this track.

By the way there are 2 tunebooks not just one. They’re on amazon now except for 4 copies of the original spiral bound book 2 (“Second Wind”) which I have in my house. - Lesl

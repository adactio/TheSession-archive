One comment

“Masurkka Ullan”

This old favourite came back to me recently. I then went looking for other takes on this as I thought we used to play it as a 32 bar tune, not repeating the B-part, which may have still been the case, but in doing a search I found otherwise. This transcription is influenced by that, one source I checked this against being this CD:

“KaustisenPurppuripelimannit (Kaustinen Potpourri Players): Finnish Folk Music Volume 1”
track 6: “Ullan Masurkka” / “Ulla’s Mazurka”

↳ Posted by ceolachan on May 11th, 2016.

Three comments

The Lost Purse

This is not a barndance but a march. It’s the signature tune of the Leeds Ceili Band, and some of you might have heard them play it in competitions. Not very sure, but I think I heard it was composed by piano accordion player Peter Duffy, who has now moved to Ireland.

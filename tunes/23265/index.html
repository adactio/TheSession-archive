The Island Sound

This jig is a composition of Cian O’Grady of the trad fusion band Tulua, from Wexford. Cian has composed quite a bit of the band’s material. Thanks to Nóra Byrne-Kavanagh for transcribing Cian’s tunes as part of The Wexford Tune Project collection.

The Clicking Chickens

From Tola Custy & Cyril O’Donoghue’s Setting Free. Goes in a set with this https://thesession.org/tunes/7440.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on March 16th, 2008.

Variations

This is a really really simple and easy-to-learn tune. This also makes it particularly good for spicy variations. The ones featured on the album are as follows in the A-part:

A3B AF~F2|AFEF AFDF|A3B AF~F2|AFEF D3F|
A3B AF~F2|AFEF AFDF|DEFG AF~F2|AFEF D3F||

and as follows for the B-part, last time thru’:

Ad~d2 Ad~d2|Be~e2 Be~e2|~f3e dA~A2|BdAF EFDF|
Ad~d2 fd~d2|Be~e2 ge~e2|a3g fd~d2|BdAF EFDF||

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on March 16th, 2008.

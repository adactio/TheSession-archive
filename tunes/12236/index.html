Sweet Is The Name Of Peggy

Great tune. Great name.

I got this rocking reel from a video of a top North-East England band called Cuig.

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrNs33H7uxY

According to John Chambers brilliant book archive website, it appears in a tune collection called Köhler’s Violin Repository of Dance Music Vol. 2 (published 1885) on page 146.

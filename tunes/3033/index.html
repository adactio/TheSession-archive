Jackson’s

I posted comments about the Frankie Gavin/Alec Finn recording yesterday, and found that this tune isn’t in the database.[ If it is in, it’s under a name I don’t know.] It’s the 2nd of the 2 “Jackson’s” reels.
I first heard it played by the Donegal Mooney family and Frankie Kennedy at a concert in Edinburgh in the late 70s - pre-Altan days. Coincidentally, the concert was very much the brain-child of the Jackson brothers of “Ossian”, whose parents came from Donegal. A great Donegal fiddle tune.

Jackson’s, X:3

Prompted by Jeremy’s celebrating Tommy Peoples link https://thesession.org/discussions/48730 I felt even my rough transcription of TP’s playing of this reel might be welcomed. It’s based on the first track on TP’s first album released in 1976 https://thesession.org/recordings/2936 , the reel that grabbed me and made me fall in love with TP’s playing.
As usual the ornamentation is omitted (too difficult, especially for TP’s, especially for non-fiddler). It is fairly close to X:2 (ala Frankie Gavin) which is almost identical to the version that Breathnach collected from an 18 year old Donegal fiddler called … Tommy Peoples

↳ Posted by brailsford on November 12th, 2023.

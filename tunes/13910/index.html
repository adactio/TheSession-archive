Re: Blackberry Festival Footrace

This jig is by David Fisher, former member of the band Point Five. David and his brother ran a 5km footrace at an annual blackberry festival in Covelo, CA. Later, trying to remember another tune (my guess: perhaps Cleveland Park, potentially also a good pairing), he came up with the B part, and the rest of the tune from there. The tune is strongly associated with Hanneke Cassel, who evidently learned it at Valley of the Moon and recorded it many times.

See also: https://web.archive.org/web/20210623081152/https://www.pointfiveceilidh.com/blogpage/blackberry-festival-footrace-the-story-behind-the-tune

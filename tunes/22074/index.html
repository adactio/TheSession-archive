Two comments

The Maiden Magpie

One of a number of rare tunes that appear in the ‘Power’ 19th century manuscript from Kilmore in County Wexford. I’ve recently located a version of this same tune in the Grier manuscript (which is from a similar period). As part of The Wexford tune project I’m working through the digital notation of material from the manuscript, and in some cases attempting an ‘update/progression’ of these old tunes which were effectively ‘frozen in time’ and forgotten 150 years ago.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on July 28th, 2022.

The Maiden Magpie, X:2

This is the version of the tune that has ‘morphed’ from the original from being played again at sessions etc. A little more variation in the repeated phrases. These old tunes that have turned up in both The Feis Ceoil collection and in The Kilmore manuscript were from an era when the music was played almost exclusively for dancing to rather than listening, so a repetitive element to the tunes seems to be a feature in the originals.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on July 28th, 2022.

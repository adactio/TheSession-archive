One comment

Na Gearrmhaca

This three part jig is one of my own compositions. It’s named for my two grandsons - Oisín and Tadgh Culleton. Here’s hoping as they get older, they’ll show an interest in traditional Irish music. I’ve been reworking this tune for quite a while, as I normally do with my compositions. It’s been uploaded to thesession.org for inclusion in the Wexford tune Collection, which will be published for Fleadh Cheoil na hEireann in my hometown next year , 2024. The Tune Collection is currently available by accessing My Tunebook:

https://thesession.org/members/116353/tunebook

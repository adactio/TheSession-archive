Otterburn Tower

A traditional Northumbrian rant recorded by the Andy may Bandon “Just a Second”. This tune does not appear to have been published in abc before.

↳ Posted by Noel Jackson on May 16th, 2021.

Re: Otterburn Tower

Hi All,

I’ve just come across this great march (as it was played by Andy May, I feel ashamed to admit this). However, having not heard And’s version, I feel the tune as shown, must have some errors in it.
5th line, 3rd bar: the last crotchet should surely be a B, not a C and in the 5th line, 4th bar: the last two quavers should read G E and not G F. In the 6th line, 2nd bar: the second crotchet should be a G and not a B in order to match the initial phrase.

I hope this is agreeable to all.

Peter Dunn.

↳ Posted by Peter Dunn on May 25th, 2021.

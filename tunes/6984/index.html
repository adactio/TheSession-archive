Eight comments

The Penicuik Hornpipe

This is for ptarmigan.
I’ve based this transcription on the one on Paul Cranford’s site (which is taken from the playing of Howie MacDonald) rather than that given in Sandy MacIntyre’s book.
In both cases it is written straight (as in a reel). I have taken the liberty of transcribing it with the humpty-dumpty rhythm of a hornpipe. I’ll add the Sandy MacIntyre setting later (I’m on my way out to work).
The tune is attributed to one R. Baillie of Penicuik.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on March 21st, 2007.

Re: The Penicuik

This tune is the second tune on track 9 of side one of the Alistair Anderson 1979 recording “Dooking For Apples” Paired with the Alistair Anderson tune “Mayday” Bill Spence is the hammered dulcimor player on this recording. Alistair takes the tune with a typical Northumbrian lilt - not quite fully dotted but with a good lift.

Re: The Penicuik

I’d imagine the source for the tune is Kohler’s Violin Repository (1881-85), where it’s also notated undotted. It was composed by Robert Baillie of Penicuik (not far south of Edinburgh), the grandson of the famous Scots fiddler Peter Baillie.

The Twin’s Delight

This tune is known as Siobhan O‘Donnell’s but it is actually a composition of John Brady, The Twin’s Delight. Its often paired with another of Brady’s tunes, McIntyre’s Fancy (commonly known as Siobhan O’Donnell’s No. 2.)

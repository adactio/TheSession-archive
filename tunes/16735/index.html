Two comments

Pleasant Avenue

Here is an ABC transcription of Pleasant Avenue by Liz Knowles from “The Penny Wager”- Open the Door for Three. This tune can be found on track 8 of their CD if you go looking for it.

I worked this ABC to try and capture the lovely and complex rhythms that are to be found in its original performance. I hope someone may take a stab at posting a simplified version that is easier to read, but I figured I would try and keep to the original spirit of the tune in this posting.

There is some more information on this in the discussion board at https://thesession.org/discussions/41876#comment839854

↳ Posted by bledsoeo on February 18th, 2018.

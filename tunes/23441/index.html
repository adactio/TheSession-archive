Re: O’Dowd’s

This setting is very confusing: there are various errors. The repeat signs should be at the end of the first time bar in all the A and B parts - not at the end of the second time bar. The first two parts have only two-and-a-half beats in the final bar of each section. Did you count them, or play the whole thing back before posting?

I took a stab at this from the recording by Solas, where it is played with the genius only Seamus Egan can apply. There are a few killer cross-measure notes that, if played as rolls would resemble the sort of thing Liz Carroll likes to do, as I hear it.

Five comments

Sorry I Am For What I Have Done

A favorite of Michael Kennedy from Galway who played a Hohner one row in G.
A 2006 Midwest Irish Music Hall of Fame winner for the Midwest Region of Comhaltas.

↳ Posted by Hugh O'Rourke on January 13th, 2013.

Michael J. Kennedy - Sorry I am

I have transcribed what I hear of Michael J. Kennedy’s take on this as heard on the 1977 album “Melodeon”.

↳ Posted by BrownAleMugger on November 17th, 2013.

Sorry I Am, X:3

From the playing of Grey Larsen & André Marchand.

Grey has Michael’s recording available for listening:
https://greylarsen.com/resources/mjk/mjklp/

From Grey’s notes (https://greylarsen.com/shop/product/sorry-i-am-for-what-i-have-done-to-marry-the-father-instead-of-the-son-john-joe-lynch-canoe-in-the-clouds/ ), Michael sang “Sorry I am for what I have done, sorry I am for what I have done, sorry I am for what I have done, to marry the father instead of the son” before starting the tune.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on July 5th, 2020.

Sorry I Am For What I Have Done, X:4

↳ Posted by Pat Higgins on November 15th, 2025.

Sorry I Am For What I Have Done, X:5

Unnamed O’Carolan Tune

One of the many unnamed O‘Carolan tunes. I’ve been playing this tune a long time and I don’t remember where I first heard it. It may have been on a Derek Bell album. It appears in appendix II (Tunes Without Titles) of Donal O’Sullivan’s “Carolan. The Life, Times and Music of an Irish Harper” #174.

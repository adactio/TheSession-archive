↳ Posted by Sláine on May 17th, 2023.

Re: Dancing On The Door

It’s not “related to ” the “Destitution”, it’s a distinct “setting” of the same tune, with fairly minor changes.
Paddy O’Brien has form with this. I’m sure it was he who re-christened Davy Arthur’s reel “Tamlin” as “The Howling Wind”. Very disrespectful in my opinion, and rather disappointing and surprising.
It’s a “Duplicate”, as far as I am concerned.
According to the sleeve notes, P.O’B. got the tune from Ged Foley, who didn’t have a name for it, so it would appear he made one up, although the CD notes don’t say why.
“Bright And Early” is a superb recording.

↳ Posted by Kenny on May 17th, 2023.

Re: Dancing On The Door

Well even if it’s a setting it’s still ‘related’ to it! I kept it separate because the differences - although minor - are significant enough. There’s no Aeolian 6th (flat 6th) in the first part of DOTD. The second part has the outline movement I-VII-I (D-C-D) in bars 1-3 (where The Destitution stays in I). And the closing 2 bars have a different sequence. Setting or new version or improvement or re-composition? Hard to say but I think in this case maybe justified to be on its own.

Florence Leslie’s Farewell To Edinburgh

I know little about this one but I assume that it was written about or for a Florence Leslie who left Edinburgh at one point. :-)

It seems to be popular in the F & A/ SCD scene and I used to play it as part of a “Duke of Perth ” set.
Any further information, e.g. the composer would be welcome. There seems to one recording, at least, by the Auld Reekie Scottish Dance Band from the nineties.

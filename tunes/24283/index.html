Your ABC’s can be reduced/re-formatted to:

K: F#min
|: a6-|a3 g3|c6-|c6|
f6-|f3 e3|B6-|B6:|
|: cef fec|efc cBA|BcF FEC|EF/G/A Bce|cef fec|
efa gfe|[1 fff ecB-| BBc feB:|[2 fff ecB-| BFA Bce||
|: fab baf|bc’f fec-|cga g2c|fec BAB|
cef fAB|cef BAF|FEF ABc|1 B2A Bce :|[2 B3 AFE | F6||

Without having listened to the recording, I assume the first 8/16 bars are an intro, which ought not to be included here, and, the final “F6” is an ending note for the tune, which also wouldn’t be included here.

So, that would reduce it further to:

K: F#min
|: cef fec|efc cBA|BcF FEC|EF/G/A Bce|cef fec|
efa gfe|[1 fff ecB-| BBc feB:|[2 fff ecB-| BFA Bce||
|: fab baf|bc’f fec-|cga g2c|fec BAB|
cef fAB|cef BAF|FEF ABc|1 B2A Bce :|2 B3 feB ||

Note that I had replaced the last bar of the B Part with the general note pattern for how it repeats in the A Part from the first repeat of the A Part.

This might not be how its played on the recording, or what’s most intuitive, but, it probably fits better than “B3 AFE” for that bar.

And the tune wouldn’t be composed by “Beoga”, rather, one or more members of their band, presuming its one of their originals.

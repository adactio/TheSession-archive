Sligo Then And Now

This Martin Wynne composition comes from Bill Black: http://www.capeirish.com/webabc/working/source.folders/mvbt/mvbt_pdf/194sligo.pdf

I first found it in an old Treoir -- they got it via Bill too. Tried playing it into TunePal and Bill’s version came up. He gave me permission to repost it here. When Bill got it from Martin, Martin had no name for it. Bill suggested “Sligo Then And Now” and Martin did not object.

Martin Wynne’s

This came from a Facebook post by Haley Richardson - Student of Brian Conway and Riverdance fiddler. In the comments she said it came from audio of a radio broadcast of Martin Wynne that Jerry O’Sullivan gave her. I’m still working on trying to get the audio. I have the MP3 of Haley and am happy to share it.

Based on the comments from the post I am very confident in naming this Martin Wynne’s (5?). I’m not sure I should call it 5 (he wrote others) but I’ve been on a Martin Wynne kick and there are only 5 reels and a Hop Jig that I’ve been able to confirm - the hop jig I suppose is more half confirmed (by Brian Conway).

Brian Conway texted me and said he believes that Martin Wynne wrote over 10 tunes but he was only willing to share four with him i.e. the only ones he thought were any good. The reel presented here was new to even Brian. Brian was a student and friend of MW’s.

↳ Posted by JWiseman on April 7th, 2021.

Sligo Then And Now, X:5

Adding the version from the Martin Mulvihill book (where’s it’s labeled Martin Wynne’s #4, and the one most people call #4 is labelled #5 twice, so take that for whatever little it’s worth), mostly for the second ending that Brian Conway et al play on his new record.

On that record, Brian starts with the second beat of the first bar, similar to X2/X3 above, however it sure sounds like they switch to the second tune at what would be the end of the tune as written here. Oisin’s version starts with beat one of the MM version I just added, as with X1 and X4 above. All that to say, I’m not sure which is the first beat per Martin Wynne’s original intention.

Note that Brian et al don’t play the high Bbs in the first part at all, and the second last bar is likely incorrect in MM’s book. So more like this:

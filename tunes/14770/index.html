Tailgating The Minister

This is a Kathryn Tickell tune, originally recorded on her Ensemble Mystical album, where it is played on Northumbrian smallpipes in Fmaj. My transcription is of Calum Stewart’s variation of the tune, played on uilleann pipes in Gmaj.

It’s the first reel in this set:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=De87Z_PmMzM

↳ Posted by bdh on October 4th, 2015.

Tailgating The Minister, X:3

This is my take on the Calum Stewart performance of this tune. I include the “B” part. He does a number of variations and ornamentations as he repeats, e.g. backstitching, slurs and rolls, but this is the core of the tune. Happy piping!

↳ Posted by ctlockey@gmail.com on April 21st, 2019.

My Otis Tomas Violin

Written by Khelton Headley-Wolf.

I wrote this tune after receiving my new Otis Tomas instrument. Otis is a luthier in Cape Breton who made me a beautiful 5 string fiddle. The tune was inspired by a Jerry Holland tune titled “My Otis Tomas Mandolin”, which I imagine has a similar origin story.

Played here: https://soundcloud.com/kheltonheadleywolf/my-otis-tomas-violin-khelton-headley-wolf

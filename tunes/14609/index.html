Two comments

The Wolf

Well, this tune is far away from the “irish style”, yet it’s an amazing piece, showing the “Grappelli-est” side of Liz Carroll. It could be taken as a hornpipe (because the rhythm) but I’ve written it in 12/8 to ease its reading.
Sorry for the hardcore trad enthusiasts.

↳ Posted by J. A. Cerro on July 26th, 2015.

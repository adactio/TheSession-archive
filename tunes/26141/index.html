Two comments

Sí Gaoithe

From the singing of Fionnuala Gill on her album “Whisperes of Love.” I have been unable to locate this air in any of the old collections so have no idea of its provenance right now. I am hoping the liner notes from the CD give me a clue. Posting two versions, E minor (original key from the album) and B minor. (For the record, I really am not comfortable labeling slow airs as waltzes.)

↳ Posted by JimAman on November 28th, 2025.

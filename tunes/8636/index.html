About this humble tune

Ciara is Ciara Duggan, my sister and Dave Murray is her husband. They got married about 5 years ago and I composed this little hornpipe as a wedding present. I was quite happy with it at the time, but I didn’t know much about music theory at the time and so I think I got the key wrong and also i kind of changes key in the second part. Enjoy!

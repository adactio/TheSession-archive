Sweep’s Hornpipe

This tune “Sweep’s Hornpipe” was transcribed in the Francis Reynolds MS (c1885) of Gaigue, Ballinamuck, Co Longford. Although there are several hornpipes with this title, none match Reynolds’ version. I have been unable to identify this tune thus far.

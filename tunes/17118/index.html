Two comments

Morven

Composed by Gordon Labhruidh, father of celebrated musician Griogair Labhruidh, who recorded the tune on his 2007 album, “Dail-riata”. My transcription is based on the tune as recorded by Greg Russell and Ciaran Algar on their 2016 album, “The Silent Majority”.

↳ Posted by bdh on July 20th, 2018.

Morven, X:2

Transposed to Dmaj for the pipers.

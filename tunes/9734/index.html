Alejator

Tune by Patrick Cavenaugh in honor of Alejandro Moreno. Named for a 1920-something banjo with a faux-alligator finish that was Alex’s axe when he first started playing Irish music several years ago.

This tune’s sure to become a staple at the McGinty’s session in Silver Spring MD!

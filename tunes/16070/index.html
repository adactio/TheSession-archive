Re: Beoga

In regards to the name of this tune, there’s some disagreement both on this website and in our local session whether it’s called Beoga or The Exploding Bow, but the latter name actually belongs to a different tune (https://thesession.org/tunes/19828). The confusion seems to come from the fact both were recorded together in a track simply called “Exploding Bow”, but the track list does indeed clarify it’s the first jig in the set that has that name, and this reel is listed as “Beoga!” (see e.g. https://thesession.org/recordings/1409 or https://www.amazon.co.uk/Lovely-Madness-Beoga/dp/B000ZIZ0QG)

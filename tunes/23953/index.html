Two comments

Re: The Isles Of Gletness

I know this as “Da Isles of Gletness”, and have two recordings of it as part of a set of waltzes, one by the Shetand Band, “Da Fustra”, and the other by my great pal, Dr Sandy Nixon and his Scottish Dance Band. Both play the set rather more steadily and with a lot more expression than is apparent in the Audio. Beautiful tune.

I need to check but I think I also have it on a 1970s LP by another of the great Shetland Bands, “The Hamefarers”, which featured Jim Halcrow, Willie Hunter Jr and Ronnie Cooper (on tour in Scotland with Jim Johnstone and Bobby Colgan on second box and drums - don’t remember the bass player, but maybe Bill Craib).

The Pigeon’s Tail

Roche - Collection of Traditional Irish Music, vol. 3 (1927; No. 174) (AK/Fiddler’s Companion)

Snappy little tune.

This version is very nearly identical to what is found in Roche. If you look at the ABCs you will see there is a slur over the entire pentuplet in measure 15 and beneath that slur another over the last three notes. The ABC program here does not recognize that notation. The c in measure 10 has an asterisk over it in Roche (stop the note about 1/4 tone flat and slide up to regular position).

“Paddy O‘Brien’s March” - or ’gan ainm’

/if this is another of Paddy O‘Brien’s compositions then, as was his tendency, he’d have a name for it and it wouldn’t be “Paddy O’Brien’s March”. Also, while some have given this march as 2/4 and AB, Paddy’s other marches, though few, are all 4/4 and AABB, as I’ve given it here. I’d been looking for a name for this without luck, but as it was recently mentioned in discussion I decided to go ahead and add it here with the hopes that one of you might have a name for it.

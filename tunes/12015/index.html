The Mullanes of Clerihan

Composed by Renwick Mac Arthur (1923-2011)

Can be found in ’The Annandale Collection Of Original Compositions For Fidlle & Accordion By Renwick Mac Arthur"

Note on tune by R. Mac Arthur : “Dear friends and memories of the day Tipperary won the all Ireland Hurley (1989)”

↳ Posted by premier on June 24th, 2012.

I have no recordings of his tunes. Only his two tunes books of his own material he gave shortly before he died. I’ve done my best to put translate the tune from staff to thesession.org stlye ABC format.

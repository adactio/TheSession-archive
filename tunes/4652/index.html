The fashion which the lasses have

The tune appears in Capt Simon Fraser’s collection under the above title translated from the Gaelic. It wanders between the keys of Amaj and F#m and Fraser’s notes describe it as an air with Gaelic verses describing the eccentricities of female dress.

↳ Posted by stoneboy on July 1st, 2005.

The tune is still regularly played in Scotland, and is almost note for note unchanged from the early collections, except is usually played in A major. Great tune! A recorded version can be found on Jock Tamson’s Bairns album of the same name (albeit ‘The Lasses Fashion’)

Re: The Lasses’ F#ashions

Smashin tune.

If you ‘run’ a drone under this melody, what would it be? Arguably, it is as much in F# minor (to the ear i mean, what else) as it is in A maj.
(basing our wee analysis on X2 & X:3 here)
Which leads to the possibility of listing this tune in the rare tinwhistle key of F# minor,
(we make it wholly pentatonic first by getting rid of the passing g natural notes);

c|: AFFF AEEc|AFFF c2Bc|AFFF AEEA|AFAB c2Bc :|
eAfA cBBf|eAfe c2ca|eAfA cABc|FFAB c2cf|
eAfA cBBf|eafe c2cb|affa eacA|AFAB cAB||

In this state, there are no d’s nor g’s (although you could always pepper one or two, including d# & g#)..
I like the idea of being able to play in all the 8 keys offered by the whistle and flute.

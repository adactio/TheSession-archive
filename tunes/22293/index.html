Three comments

The Ballygar Hermitage

This handsome jig comes from the playing of Mick McAuley and Win Horan on their album Serenade. I transcribed it directly from their track on YouTube as the first in the set “Ballygar Jigs”: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4zQnOg8hVc0&list=OLAK5uy_lWtvVbIN7dAacAEJOAannltlpISrNu16k

When they go back into the A part, they play the first bit as: ~B3 AGF|DGG G2A|…. and then on the A repeat revert to the melody as shown in my original transcription.

Bill Black credits this jig to Joe Kelly and offers a transcription from the playing of Joe and Willie Kelly here:
https://tunearch.org/wiki/Ballygar_Hermitage. It’s not clear whether this was composed by Joe Kelly the father or the Joe who is Willie’s brother (all fiddlers). Perhaps someone else knows? I’ve long known and enjoyed Willie’s fiddling in his duets with fluter Mike Rafferty (RIP).

Ballygar is in northeast Co. Galway near the Co. Roscommon line.

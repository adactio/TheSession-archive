Re: The Nymrod

From Ryan’s Mammoth Collection (Boston 1883). Nimrod was the great grandson of Noah who may have instigated the Tower of Babel, but the term became used to denote a great hunter and, in recent times, a socially inadequate person. It was also the name of the ship that Shackleton used for his 1908 Antarctic expedition. You live and learn!

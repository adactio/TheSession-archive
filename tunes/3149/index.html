Round the World for Sport

This a setting from “Howe’s 1,000 Jigs and Reels, Etc. ”, Boston 1867.

↳ Posted by Wes Merchant on June 9th, 2013.

The first part of this jig is clearly related to the reel of the same name https://thesession.org/tunes/1011 - it is harder to hear any connection in the second part.

The middle part of the three part version is pretty much the same, save for the last two bars (which echo the ending of the last part), as the second part of Lanigan’s Ball. I suspect the middle part was added by a dance musician to make the tune suitable for a 48-bar dance.

↳ Posted by CreadurMawnOrganig on June 9th, 2013.

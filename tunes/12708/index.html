Four comments

Sailor’s Hornpipe (Kerr)

#275 in Vol 4 of Kerr’s Merry Melodies, this attractive tune is obviously not the usual Sailor’s H-pipe, and was recorded by the Celtic Fiddle Festival on their Rendezvous record.

↳ Posted by Lucy Rietmann on April 28th, 2013.

Title

Can’t this retain the “Sailor’s Hornpipe (Kerr)” title I gave it originally? It’s a way to distinguish it from the various entries for the usual SH, important since searching for a title no longer shows the key of the entries in the results. I also retitled the number on the Rendezvous album: https://thesession.org/recordings/177

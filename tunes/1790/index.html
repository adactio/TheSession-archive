This is not the ‘Scully Casey’s’ that appears on Lunasa’s “Merry Sisters of Fate” album. That tune is ‘Palm Sunday’, played really slowly.

https://thesession.org/tunes/770

↳ Posted by bdh on April 9th, 2007.

This is also known as Thady Casey’s. Thady was Scully’s father, I believe. Scully Casey was the father of Bobby Casey, from The Crosses of Annagh, West Clare, contemporary of Junior Crehan and father of London fiddler, banjo and mandolin player, Sean Casey.

↳ Posted by CreadurMawnOrganig on September 8th, 2007.

This “Scully Casey’s” can also be found on one of the Chulrua albums, the Junior Crehan album, and the Johnny McCarthy album.

Scully Casey’s as played by Bobby Casey

There’s a YouTube clip of Bobby Casey and piper Thomas Keane playing this tune, here: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3KKIcByicvU&feature=related

In the A part, Keane really smears into the low F#s from Fnat, and Mr. Casey does the same with the high Fs in the B part in the downhill runs near the end each time.

Alex Campbell’s

Jack posted one of Dougie McLean’s tunes earlier this week, and here’s another, which I’ve always thought was a good tune for the flute, as long as you have an F-natural key. This was one of Dougie’s first tunes, and he recorded it on his 1st album, “Snaigow”. Dougie named it after Alex Campbell, who was certainly one of the biggest names in Scottish folk music , especially in Europe.

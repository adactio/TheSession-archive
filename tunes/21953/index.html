Hagan’s

I’m not sure how admissible this is under the house rule about ‘own compositions’ (though I’m sure advice will be offered). It’s not really an attempt at creation, but an adaptation of an existing tune I’ve known for ages under the title of Geehan’s Reel (here at: https://thesession.org/tunes/1219). I’ve dropped it from G to Em and tweaked it a bit so it falls under the fingers on a fiddle, just to see how it comes out. Like this, apparently. What do you think? Worth playing?

(This title is the nearest I could get to an anagram of the original).

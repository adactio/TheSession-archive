Two comments

The Glorious 45

This is a upbeat reel composed by David Foley (Rura), I learnt it in a workshop at school, which Jack Smedley (Rura) was taking. It’s on Rura’s Album ‘Despite the Dark’, on the track ‘The Glorious 45’.

↳ Posted by duncs22 on June 9th, 2019.

The Glorious 45, X:2

There was a bug with abc triplets in the second part, this version should be rhythmically correct. I also love this tune, although it’s not easy to make it sound as good as in RURA’s recording!

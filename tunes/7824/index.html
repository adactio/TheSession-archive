Lyrics

Written by Eoghan Rua Ó Súilleabháin on the eve of his departure overseas to join the army in 1774. The lyrics are his saying farewell to the people of his village (just East of Cill Airne)
“…Slán cuirim páirteach gan áireamh ó chéile, chun Ádaimh, chun Seágain, chun Máire’s chun Séamais…Le h-áthas fá thearman Charraig an Éide - óm fhán-rith dá gcasainn ní sgarfainn lem ré libh!”
(…A tender farewell, with love that won’t vary, to Adam, to John, and to James, and to Mary…To Carraig an Éide, best-loved and true-hearted, if I ever reurn we shall never be parted!)

One of the truly great, forgotten laments.

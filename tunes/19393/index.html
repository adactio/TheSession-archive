Orcadia

This is a transcription of the piece as played by Saltfishforty on their album Orkney Twister. It is played slowly, with grace by fiddler, Douglas Montgomery with a very simple guitar accompaniment. Montgomery plays most of those triplets with a gentle swing - the first of the three notes a little longer than the other two. I found a review in Living Tradition which credits the tune to John Harvey.

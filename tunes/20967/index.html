Two comments

The Haddock Inspector’s

Taken from the CD ‘A Trip to Cape Breton’ by the Jura Ceilidh Band and Jerry Holland. Having bought it as a download there is no information about the origin of the tune, or who wrote it, so great if somebody can add this. It sounds great when belting along as the JCB play it!

↳ Posted by Felefeber on August 16th, 2021.

Re: The Haddock Inspector’s

Composed by Ross Kennedy, it’s “dedicated to two drunken musicians after a late night ceilidh in Acharacle”.

Re: Sun Assembly

From the site of Mary Humphreys and Anahata, “Publised in Thompson’s collection of English Country Dances, 1757, tune apparently described then as traditional.” They give a three-part arrangement of the tune at https://www.maryanahata.co.uk/music/57 and a video:

Re: Sun Assembly

This is not a polka. All proper polkas were written from the 1840’s when the dance swept from Eastern Europe into the west. This piece is from the Harmonious Blacksmith variations by Handle.

↳ Posted by John Offord on September 22nd, 2025.

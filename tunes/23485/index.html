Two comments

The Red Reed

A nice reel composed in the 90’s by Marc Pollier, uillean piper native from Nancy (France). It’s definitly a reel, but with a special flavour. Because, at a time, Marco was fond of French musette waltzes, and used to play some of them on his pipes. Hence, the influence on the melodic line of his tune, that he recorded with the band « Broken Pledge ». The version played by Broken Pledge offers a piano accompaniement with harmonies that suit well. On an other version recorded by Karen Tweed, Ian Carr puts chords that are very inventive and audacious, as usual, and it’s great !
The name of the tune refers to the first self-made chanter reed that gave Marco satisfaction and that he could play a long time, when he was living in Birmingham. This reed was surrounded by a red thread. Since, he has still been experimenting chanter reeds making, looking for the perfect reed !

Waltz For Nobody

Waltz for Nobody by Cameron Wilson
A great waltz by Cam Wilson of the Canadian band Mad Pudding.
Waltz for Nobody was the last track on their 1997 album Rattle on the Stovepipe.
It’s a nice slow waltz in the key of G with a couple of time signature changes in the B part. I hope this ABC has done it justice!

Excellent waltz!!

I listened to this waltz at the listed site, and it is gorgeous! I’m glad you wrote it up. It’s pretty tricky.

Just FYI, although your ABC rendering was excellent, I noticed that on the syncopated portions you used 4/4 meter going to 2/4 meter to notate those areas. They really ARE in 3/4, and rather than remember which meter you’re using, you can write them in 3/4 as:
“Em”G4 cd |“C” e2 ed ce |“D”a4 fg|aD gf Df| eD dc BA|“C”G2 E2 B,D |

The waltz is no different, but try this:
DG|:“G”G2DG AB|“Am7”c2 BA D2|“Dm”=F3 G/2F/2 EC|“Am”A,4 A,B,|“Em”G4A,B,|

Seven comments

A three-part French Canadian reel from the New England Fiddler’s Repertoire. Part C is identical to the B part of “The Maid Behind the Bar” - this is referred to in the comments on that tune.
The title means “The snoring Ms Gobeil”. Note the “snore” in the last bar of part B.

La Ronfleuse Gobeil, X:2

This is the way this tune is played in Quebec. Some minor differences in the first two parts but the third part isn’t exactly like the Maid Behind the Bar. In the third part, the sixth and seventh bars are a repeat of the first and second bars. Makes it a bit tough to play if you’ve played Maid Behind the Bar a thousand times like I have. As well the tune usually ends after the second part. Check out the Quebec Trad site http://www.mustrad.udenap.org/tounes/TQ135_ronfleuse_gobeil.html

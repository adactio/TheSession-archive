Three comments

The Lord Of Ballinaleck

This reel is a composition of Paddy O’Brien and can be found in his first tune collection on tape #1.
He says that he composed it in a hotel room in Houston TX on a hot night in the 1980’s.
It is named in honor of Cathal McConnell, famed native of Ballinaleck, Co. Fermanagh.

I think that it is an excellent tune, but I have never heard anyone play it other than Paddy.
I haven’t played it is sessions yet because I had a hard time remembering all the parts---which is why I have written it out here.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on June 2nd, 2018.

Welcome Here Again

Title track from Messrs Hayes & Cahill’s latest offering, although they play it in F, I’ve transposed it to G. Beautiful in its’ simplicity.

Re: Welcome Here Again

Published in Bremner’s first collection (1757) as “Ye’ll Aye Be Welcome Here Again” and in Thompson’s Dances for 1771 as “You Be Welcome Here Again,” but in McGlashan’s 1778 collection it’s named “Duncan Davidson” - over the years it evolved and the Duncan Davidson name became favoured.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on September 30th, 2024.

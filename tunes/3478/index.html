Drive Hawkie Through The Water

This reel is an old tune (18th century?) based on a song from the northeast about a cow called Hawkie who is adored by all and produces lots of milk, but hates crossing water. The tune is really simple and takes no time to learn, but it’s one of those tunes you can’t end. It’s very addictive - give it a try!

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on September 2nd, 2004.

Drive Hawkie Through The Water, X:2

This version is as played by Sharon Creasey at the launch of the FluteFling Collection. She calls it “Ca’ Hawkie Through the Water” and plays it both as a highland and, at a faster tempo, as a reel. Her introduction to the tune, followed by her playing, begins at about 5:39 on the youtube video:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hz1ZhR-oX1w
The FluteFling Collection can be ordered, either as a printed book or as an eBook, here:
http://flutefling.scot/shop/. Ca’ Hawkie Through the Water is contributed by Sharon Creasey.
Extended notes to the tunes can be downloaded at the same location. Chet

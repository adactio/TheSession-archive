Mandy Murray’s

Written by Tim O’Leary and dedicated to the virtuoso Anglo Concertina player Mandy Murray. Mandy is a fantastic London-Irish box player now living in Brighton and I am really glad there is a tune with her name on it.

It is a SLOW reel, as in play it slowly, perhaps two-thirds normal instrumental reel speed. (Slow enough to dance to perhaps?)

It goes well as a first tune of a reel set, try it preceding Paddy Killoran No.2 and The Congress reels.

Note: I play fiddle so the First note of the B part has a mordant with the fiddle fingerings anotated above the stave: “030”. This means play the mordant as EGE, where the note E is written. I have tried to include only the ‘important’ ornaments, but we all know that ornamentation is a very personal thing in trad music and each instrument has its own favoured ornaments so feel free to add or take away. (Goes without saying really)

Ties: The Last bar of each time round of the B part begins with a tied note, although in the 2nd time and last time round it doesn’t look very clear to me. It should make sense, as the tied note is the same as the clearly marked 1st time round bar in the B part.

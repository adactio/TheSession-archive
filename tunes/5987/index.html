↳ Posted by ceolachan on July 19th, 2006.

That’s not so funny. I did have an acquaintance who felt the need to wear a rug. Though it was obvious, he was quite sensitive about it. You didn’t mention it, even if it fell off during music making, at home or in concert… I have to say, it was damned hard choking the laughter back…

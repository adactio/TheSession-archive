Auldyn River

This is a beautiful Manx waltz composed by Paul Cringle, a guitarist from Ramsey.

It is particularly popular among harp players on the island.
I decided to learn it myself after hearing it in the late night sessions at Edinburgh Harp Festival last month.

Even lovelier on the harp but fine as an air on the fiddle too. I’ve just included the main melody line here.

It could be played, I suppose, with repeats on parts one and two but I learned the tune as above.

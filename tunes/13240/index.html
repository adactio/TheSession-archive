The Straits Of Sherkin

A wonderful upbeat Mediterranean-flavoured reel by Diarmaid Moynihan on the CD, The Lights of Ranzanico. He plays it on a Low F whistle. The reel is complex in its arrangement so, for now, I’ve included it in its entirety.

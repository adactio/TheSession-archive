Jim Hardie’s

Composed by the late Dundee fiddler, Angus Cameron.

Probably it’s not dedicated to the Wells Fargo agent :-), however.

I realise it’s a Scottish tune but it does have an Irish feel and it would sound nice if transposed into a key of your choice.

Re: Jim Hardie’s

“Alan Clark’s Reel amd Jim Hardie’s Jig: Angus bracketed these two together as they are business partners. Alan’s wife asked him to write a tune for his birthday and at the same time to compose a tune for Jim.” - A Complete Collection of Original Compositions by Angus Cameron of Kirriemuir (Forfar, 2000)

Three comments

The House On The Hill

A jig in Bb mixolydian composed by David Foley of Rura and featured on their album “Dusk Moon”.

This is the second tune on the final track “A minor Emergency”.

Listen here (1:37-2:31): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eeiu38qNCUc

↳ Posted by Tom L on April 8th, 2023.

This second setting is for all of you A dorian fans ;)

The House On The Hill, X:3

X3 is the same version like X2 (perfectly transcripted by Tom) with only 4 tiny changes. I made this changes, cause I noticed that I have massive problems to hold the beat in the B-part of the tune. When I hearing the recording I don’t have any problems. The tiny difference were 4th note of the the beginning of the B-part (and all repetitions). Changing this, the B-part is really straight and fun to play.
(@Tom Btw. Thank you for showing this awesome tune).

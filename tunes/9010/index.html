“La Marsaillaise” ~ from the playing of Con Cassidy
I’ve notated this in 4/4 because it allows me to avoid things like /4, 32nd notes, or {~} fudges. The feel and drive of this melody is duple, so 2/4 would show that more clearly. However, reels are in the same bag, notated as 4/4 nowadays while being strongly duple in nature. That said, reel dance steps fit the 4/4 mold. The ‘march’ in this, while dance stepping it might be more akin to the reel or barndance, 3 steps to the measure = | 1 2 3 * |, is decidedly duple, 2 steps to the measure, | 1 * 2 * |.

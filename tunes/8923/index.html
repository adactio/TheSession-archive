Re: John Stephen Of Chance Inn

Some more on the background..
There are several Chance Inns within 30 miles of Dundee including one barely a mile from where we live. I once met a John Stephen who lived there but I believe others were also linked with people called John Stephen so Ive also been curious to pin the correct Chancre Inn down.
A chance meeting with some people we know who live almost next door tto Chance Inn, Chapel Hill, Perth gave me the chance to ask - and yes, this was the Chance Inn.
Angus was a very popular man in the Carse of Gowrie and qwrote tunes for several of th people he knew - James Stephen, Dr Robertson etc.

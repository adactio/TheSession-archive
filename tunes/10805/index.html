Two comments

Fiona’s Waltz.

I think this a lovely tune. It is on the recording: ‘If anyone can’, by Leo McCann. I believe it is one of his own compositions. On the CD it is in B flat because playing a C/C# box LIKE a B/C produces that key rather than A maj.

↳ Posted by Keithypete. on November 9th, 2010.

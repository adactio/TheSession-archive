Composer info

According to another member, this tune was composed by Hammish Moore (originally in Amix). I heard it on a recording by P.M. Robert Mathieson on the D Smallpipes.

↳ Posted by jasonlburnfield on March 12th, 2010.

Second thoughts

I don’t have the recordings, but on looking at track listings, I reckon it’s more likely to be another Mathieson composition. He was always very prolific in recording his own compositions, and quite rightly so. Again, would have been better posted in “Amix”.

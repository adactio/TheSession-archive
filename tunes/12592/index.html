Sir Watkin’s March

From the book ‘Cadw Twmpath’ published by The Welsh Folk Dance Society. Selected, edited and arranged by; Robin Huw Bowen.

It’s not a Barndance or a Hornpipe, it’s a swung march.Good for humming on the walk/stumble home after an evening of thirst quenching
The Welsh Title is; Ymdaith Syr Watcyn.
Pronounciation;…. um..d..eye..th sur watkin.

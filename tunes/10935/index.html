Indian Point, X:2

This is from a Youtube video (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DJorMfp-jC0) for session member Dutchie. I don’t know how long you’ve been playing but you have to “displace” the octave all the time mainly because fiddles go down to low G. I either play the low note up an octave or arrange the line in some creative way and find the “best place” to get back down depending on where the line is. It is not easy at all for me but it is part of what you have to learn on flute or whistle.

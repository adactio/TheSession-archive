Two comments

Hessian Camp (Country Dance)

This is a tune played for an English Country Dance that’s historically been notated in 2/4. With instructions to the dance including “gallop down and cast off” I suspect this tune would’ve been played in fairly lively tempos. Not so these days, with versions inspired by Andy Cutting or Leveret that positively sound like a Christmas tune! I learned it from Sophie Petkevich and couldn’t find it on The Session, so here we go :-D

↳ Posted by Bregolas on March 27th, 2025.

Hessian Camp, X:2

For comparison, here’s a version found in several 18th-century collections of Country Dances (transcription from Fiddler’s Companion). I wonder if there’s a related air found in Irish / Scottish repertoire as it sounds so awfully familiar! (Might be because it starts just like Frère Jacques ;-))

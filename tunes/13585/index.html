The Liverpool No. 1

This is one of Charlie Lennon’s compositions, which I just learned from a friend of mine. I love this playful and witty melody! Lennon composed a 2nd Liverpool reel which I don’t play yet and for which I have no recordings (but I know you can find them somewhere). The Liverpool no. 1 has been recorded by Pat McCabe on his album “Round the Roads”.

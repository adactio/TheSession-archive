The miser’s pocket

O: Ireland
D: Bowhouse Quintet. Live in Ennis. - Glasgow: Lochshore CDLDL1291, 1999. - Tk2#3

Transcribed 06/11/2011 Mozart 11 -> MIDI -> EasyABC 1.3.3
A ripper of a tune and I’m very surprised I can’t find it on the Interweb, even using John Chambers’ search engine. The triplets on A in the first couple of bars represent a tremolo, I think. These repeat in the same position in the A part throughout the recorded performance.

My first post on The Session and hope to contribute some more. I’ve been lurking and searching the site and find it a very useful tool for indexing my discs. The abc and sheet music helps enormously in recognising tunes.

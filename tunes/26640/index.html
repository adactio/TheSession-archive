The Mountain

A beautiful slow air from Hamish Napier’s upcoming album “The Hill”.

“This air is the heart of The Hill album - commissioned by Amos Miller for his wife Ruth - was written with Duncan Chisholm’s soulful fiddle playing in mind. The word monadh (massif) may be Pictish. Centuries ago, it referred to the whole of the mainland Scottish Highlands. Later it was used to describe certain clumps of hills. The ranges local to me are shown on the cover map. Am Monadh Liath is the ‘bluish-grey mountain range’ north of the Spey, a wide sweeping mass of mainly peat bogged hills from Slochd down to Kingussie, then taller, craggier mountains thereafter to Corrieyairack and Creag Meagaidh.”

Re: Silas’ Just Desserts

Good you spotted that, Doug, and made appropriate changes.

I think Arjan confused your jig with the Angus Lawrie jig “Wee Harry” because of the very similar first parts.
You are bound to get similar phrases when working with a restricted note selection and within the conventions of GHB composition.

Incidentally, the two high Bs in the third part of Arjan’s setting of Wee Harry should be an octave lower. Also, bagpipers wouldn’t really play this pointed: hear here:
https://www.facebook.com/roddybagpipes/videos/bagpiping-in-the-new-year-2024/350923010994418/

Are You Joanie Madden?

This is as taught by Joanie herself at the 2019 Catskills Irish Arts Week. Her good friend Liz Carroll wrote it, and Joanie wrote “No, I’m Liz Carroll!” in reply. I noted the slides with ‘s’ but did not mark the many cuts.

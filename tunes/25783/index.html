a curious dance

This is the melody that starts up at 4:12 of the Paddi Benson, Grace Lemon & James Patrick Gavin recording. In the recording after many repetitions of the “a”, the “b” section is played over the “a” section but offset by a half measure. I thought it might be fun to try to play this as a standard jig and see what happens. Perhaps I’ll experiment with changing the pickup to the B to be a whole measure.

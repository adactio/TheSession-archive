Muñeira De Rengos

This Muñeira (jig) has been collected in the hamlet of Rengos (Cangas del Narcea, Asturies). It was part of the repertoire of the piper “Gelín de Rengos”. It was popularized in the rest of Asturias by the folk group Llan de Cubel on its first CD “Deva” in a set with la Muñeira de Batribán.

↳ Posted by xurdesc on March 22nd, 2018.

Delaney’s

Another tune dedicated to friends and musicians met in Waterford, during “John Dwyer Trad Festival”…this is for Ben!

↳ Posted by Francesco Brusco on December 25th, 2012.

How long have you been playing Irish music?

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on December 26th, 2012.

RE: How long have you been playing Irish music?

Actually I’m still a neophyte of this kind of music; I started playing (and listening) in May 2011 with my Italian group. Day after day I gradually started to fall in love with it, and after one year and a half (and a trip to Ireland expressly aimed at listening trad sessions and concerts!) I feel like I just started to snatch some of the spirit of this music!
Needless to say, this tune I wrote are meant to be a “humble tribute”, mixed with my own previous influences, and I don’t dare to say they’re really “in style”!

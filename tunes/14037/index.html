Abe’s Retreat, X:2

One of my favorite Old-Time tunes of all time. This is the version that I learned and play. Great for mandolin and fiddle. If you play this on tenor banjo, and wonder why the clawhammer banjo recordings sound better, it’s probably cause you are picking too close to the bridge; move your hand up higher for those Old-Time tunes, or learn to play 5-string clawhammer and buy a new banjo.
When played SLOWLY, this tune is amazing. Use lots of hammer-ons and slides when you feel like it, cause why not!?

Abe’s Retreat, X:3

Learned this at Clifftop in West Virginia (Appalachian String Band Music Festival). Cross tuned AEAE. Lots of drones and doubling of the open Es and As. Played this often without cross tuning in old time jams in the States.

I often cross tune my 2nd fiddle to GDGD and play all the GDGD tunes (and some regular G tunes in this cross tuning) and the AEAE tunes (sounding G). The AEAE tunes sound better with the fiddle tuned GDGD to me.

The Log Driver’s Waltz

A pretty Canadian tune written by Wade Hemsworth of Ontario (who has now passed away), who was inspired for this tune by how the log driver’s “danced” across the logs. . It’s fun tune to add some ornamentation to, and I often play with a little part after the B-part before I play the tune is played again. It goes like:

M: 3/4
L: 1/8
K: Gmaj
GA | B4 d2 | d3 c B2| A6 | G6 |

There’s also a version on but Kate and Anne McGarrigle for the National Film Board’s Animation. It’s at http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=upsZZ2s3xv8

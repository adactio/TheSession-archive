Ferny Hill

This is a nice 5-part reel. The book in the Long Black Veil CD give say it’s by Matt Molloy and Sean Keane.

Please note that the signature should be C| (2/2), not 4/4. The notes would still look the same. This is played “not too fast” in the CD, say the 1/4 note at about 140 bpm.

Ferny Hill, X:3

Very interesting discussion about this tune! Quite helpful when it came to sorting out the recording vs. the settings. I just learned this tune from a friend at a session. He had learned it from The Chieftains album, The Long Black Veil. After listening many times for structure and comparing the recording to the two settings that were here, I think we have managed to put onto paper a very neat and tidy way to think of this tune. It can be difficult to learn because of so much internal repetition of small bits, but we think we’ve got it, minus any notations of Matt Malloy’s glorious ornamentation.

The Madoline O’Connell

This is a tune I wrote in 2014 about a dear relative, Madoline O’Connell (née Horgan). b.2013

She took me in and we drank Gin…

I’m not sure if its a Hornpipe or Jig. If you asked me, I’d call it a hybrid.

It’s a recording on the “Fir Bolg” album by our band Jarrah Celts.

Hope people like it and will share it.

↳ Posted by Chris J Horgan on August 21st, 2017.

Three comments

Composed by Gillian Frame and Padraig O’Neill. Transcription based on the dots that used to be up on the Back of the Moon website, and my ear :-)

↳ Posted by bdh on June 6th, 2009.

Martin is a great button box player from Glasgow. Aside from the Luminosity album this tune can also be found on Gill and Padraig’s own album, “Glasgow Skyline”.

↳ Posted by Alistair Cassidy on June 6th, 2009.

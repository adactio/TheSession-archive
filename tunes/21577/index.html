Seven comments

The Pearl Of The White Breast

O‘Neill’s Music of Ireland 511. Marked ’Slow, with feeling,“ so I don’t think this can be a reel. Original key is F major, although it seems to go into C Mixolydian in the B section. The end of the B section is marked ”D.C." but the Session interface didn’t like that, so I just wrote it out.

Not to be confused with “The Snowy-Breasted Pearl” which is the tune after that in O’Neill’s and is a waltz. But this tune, in 4/4, is also called “The Snowy-Breasted Pearl” in Waltons Publishing’s 110 Ireland’s Best Slow Airs and accompanying CD.

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 14th, 2022.

The Pearl Of The White Breast, X:2

Version in O’Neill’s Irish Music (1915) as “The Snowy-Breasted Pearl.” That version is in E-flat. I’ve transposed it to D.

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 14th, 2022.

The Pearl Of The White Breast, X:3

Version in 110 Ireland’s Best Slow Airs, book and 2-CD set recorded by Harry Long (tin whistle and low whistle), Darach De Bruin (uilleann pipes), Mary O’Donnell (harp) and Sinead Madden (fiddle). This tune is performed on solo fiddle on the CD.

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 14th, 2022.

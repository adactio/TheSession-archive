Ormond Sound

Since we have Johnny B. Connolly’s Bridgetown cd posted in the Recordings archive, it seemed fitting to post a tune that’s on the cd. Ormond Sound is a Paddy O’Brien composition, an accordion and fiddle player from County Tipperary. He led the Ormond Ceili Band and wrote many of the tunes they played.

↳ Posted by Will Harmon on December 8th, 2001.

Re: Ormond Sound

The Kingdom of Ormond (Modern Irish: Urmumhain - “East Munster”) was a short-lived kingdom in medieval Ireland. (Wikipedia)
It existed in 12th century AD, comprising what is now County Tipperary and parts of Countes Kilkenny and Waterford. The title Earl (later Duke) of Ormonde was later awarded to the Anglo/Irish Butler family and the Ormonde name has since been attached to various institutions in that region, e.g. several cinemas and Paddy O’Brien’s former céilí band.

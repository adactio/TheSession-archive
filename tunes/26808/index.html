One comment

Souvenir Of Venice

Key should be E-flat Major.
As recorded by Oisín McAuley in “Far from the hills of Donegal” (2007), track n°7. I skipped the intro.
Source of the tune is quite obscure, as is its true title (see the discussion on the album’s page). Ryan’s Mammoth has an entry for “Souvenir of Venice”, which does not correspond to the present tune, but to another hornpipe in Eb (“The Banks”).
Obviously, the tune is inspired by 19th century virtuoso violin répertoire, and involves a lot of left-hand shifting and extension.

For My Mother Dear

From Jerry Holland’s “Fiddler’s Choice.” It was composed by him, and when I played it for my own mother she started to cry and told me that I had to play it at her funeral. Geez. No, it’s actually a really nice tune, as are many of Jerry’s compositions.

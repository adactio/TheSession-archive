Three comments

Camino

Composed by by Brendan Begley, and recorded in F by Cormac Begley on a Bb/F Suttner treble concertina. Here transposed up to G. See the comments for the previous polka on Cormac’s recording, ‘Polka John’, at
https://thesession.org/tunes/16300#setting30866

↳ Posted by Rover on August 16th, 2017.

Camino, X:2

Transcribed from Cormac Begley By Cormac Begley
https://thesession.org/recordings/5633

Aside from putting in G like the previous setting, I made one small alteration to make it easier to play. The second note of the third bar on the recording would be a high d (3 semi tones above b) but I have followed above setting putting it as a high a.

↳ Posted by jamesa on May 20th, 2022.

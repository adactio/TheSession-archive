Peter and Doreen Chiasson (March!)

I was hearing this tune in my head almost all the time while working this afternoon. I don’t know why this one, not the others. Back home now, I’ve just found it’s a composition of Brenda Stubbert. I transcribed it off Scottish fiddler Patsy Reid’s album.

Just remind you this isn’t a strathspey but a march. Don’t play it fast.

↳ Posted by slainte on December 18th, 2004.

Some of you might think this tune sounds similar to Scots tune “The Farewell”: https://thesession.org/tunes/3214 Fiddlers may play two tunes in a set.

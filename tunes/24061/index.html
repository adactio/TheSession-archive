Two comments

Gràdh Geal Mo Chridhe/Bheir Mi Ò (Fair Love Of My Heart)

Ok, so this tune was kind of a pain to transcribe due to the ‘complex’ note durations for the time signature it’s in. I labeled it as a “reel” since it is in 4/4 time, but honestly I’m not sure whether it can really be classified as such, or as an “air”, due to it’s tempo and potential liberties that can be taken in timing and such. Definitely not a waltz, since it’s positively not in 3/4 time (I assumed it would be due to the nature of the tune, but alas).

Anyways, I thought and hoped this tune would be on here, but I couldn’t find it. I found this tune many years ago (coming up on a decade probably) on a forum, and I saved it, but didn’t really get to listening to it and appreciating it until recently.
--------------------------------
So this is apparently a traditional Scottish tune that “is a love song in which a man remembers how he used to meet the girl he loved. His love for her has made him very sad. He is not yet married but hopes he will be. He promises that he will work hard, so that the couple can have what they need. Permission from her family is needed, but her agreement is more important than anything else.” (taken from https://terreceltiche.altervista.org/outlander-gradh-geal-mo-chridhe/)

It truly is a beautiful tune, and was kind of surprised to see I couldn’t find it here. Maybe it is on here on the Session, but buried under some completely different name (as we all know how tunes can have dozens if not more alternate names, lol)

In any case, I transcribed it as completely accurately as I could from the rendition as performed by Alison Helzer on her album “Carolan’s Welcome” - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Y_Sjmu95As

I tried to make it as intuitive as possible using the ABC format per Session guidelines, but apparently some methods of formatting aren’t permitted, so I did my best. In case it it is still confusing, the order of playing should be: A part > chorus, B part > chorus, C part > chorus x2.

No embellishments since it is a slower ‘air’, and so that is up to you how you may want to do so. Best tempo is between 70 and 80 bpm imo.

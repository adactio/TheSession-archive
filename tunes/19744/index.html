Three comments

Sue Morley’s Jig

From the playing of Antoni O’Breskey, Davide Viterbo, Mairtin O’Connor, Johnny McCarthy, Balen López de Munain, Sergio Candotti, and, Biancastella Croce.

Composed by Antoni O’Breskey.

He says of the tune in his album liner notes:
’Remixed - Original recording from 1992.

This piece of music, like “Drunken Spider”, was also composed for the work “Orekan” in 1992 but never included in the original CD.

This version was recorded by Paul Ashe-Browne in Sue Morley and Paolo Tullio’s house in Annamoe while I was working on a musical project with Dónal Lunny, commissioned by Paul McGuinnes.

We decided to give this tune a new breath of fresh air by adding the wonderful fiddle of Davide Viterbo.’

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on September 13th, 2020.

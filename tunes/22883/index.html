Two comments

Kelly’s Schottische

This tune was recorded by Nebraska fiddler Bob Walters in 1950 and transcribed in R.P. Christeson’s book “The Old Time Fiddler’s Repertory” in 1973. Bob Walters played the tune in B-flat.

The tune evidently made its way to Ireland at some point, as evidenced by a YouTube video by accordion player Ciaran Kelly: https://youtu.be/C7fbG9OH1LY

Kelly got the tune from County Mayo box player David Munnelly. Ciaran Kelly plays the tune in G and D as in this transcription.

The first part of the tune greatly resembles the first part of a popular Midwest US tune known as Tunes From Home, which has a Swedish origin.

↳ Posted by Larry Ayers on March 17th, 2023.

Kelly’s Schottische, X:2

This is a setting from the 2013 album For the Sake of Auld Decency by the group Morga: Jonas Fromseier (bouzouki,banjo), Danny Diamond (fiddle), Dominic Keogh (bodhrán, flute) and David Munnelly (accordion).

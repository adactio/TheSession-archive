“Rosina” ~ aand played in various keys ~ F, G, C & D

I tend to play this as a swung mazurka but also know it is played as a straight waltz. I’ve added it both ways here, swung in G and straight in D, with some minor variations thrown in. Another straight version of it in C can be found here:

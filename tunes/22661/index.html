Kas A-Barh Karnak

A well-known and very old Kas a-barh played in Brittany sometimes in session
The origins of the tune is from Carnac (Karnak in Breton Language) and can be find in the famed collection tunes book of Polig Montjarret - Toniou Breizh Izel vol. 1 (Equivalent of O’Neill’s Book of Irish tunes)

You can hear it here : https://youtu.be/d-s2HEWJi4I

The kas a-barh is like the dance An Dro but instead of dancing in chain, it’s a couple dance https://youtu.be/mgTFgQ8HHbM

Success To The Fleet

A nice, easy country dance tune from The High Level Ranters. Ranting lads. London: Topic 12TS297, 1976. Side 2 Track 3#3, in the set “Captain Bover / Here’s the tender comin‘ / Success to the fleet.” The Ranters’ sleeve notes give no clue as to source for this tune.
John Chambers (http://trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/book/CDfNH1783/24_Success_to_the_Fleet.abc) transcribes a tune that appeared in London Magazine 1756, v.25 p.189, and points to a version in G major in the as yet unpublished “Whittier Perkins book: a collection of dancing tunes”, 1790 (MS), housed at Columbia University. I cannot tell if The Ranters’ version is the same tune, but it does sound related to the London Magazine tune.

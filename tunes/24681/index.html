Three comments

The Road To Raheenduff

This tune, composed by concertina player Molly Gallagher, was recently published in ‘The Wexford Tunebook’. Molly is the youngest contributor to this collection of County Wexford tunes . She tells me she composed the tune in the back of her dad’s car, on her way home to Raheenduff, near Oulart after a long gap away from home during her first term as a student. her dad had brought the concertina for her to pass the time on the drive back and a lovely new reel was the result.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 27th, 2024.

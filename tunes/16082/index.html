Two comments

Le Ronde Des Voyageurs

From the Fortunat Maloin repertoire. The “traveler” was fur trafficker (usually by canoe) across Canada. Source: http://www.mustrad.udenap.org/tounes/TQ264_ronde_des_voyageurs.html

↳ Posted by Javi V on May 6th, 2017.

Le Ronde Des Voyageurs, X:2

I see two listings from French Canada, showing La Ronde . . (feminine).
The tune starts in A minor, switching to A major (3 sharps) in the 2nd strain. I have shown the key-change at the end of line-2, and at the end of the tune (in case of repeat).
For proper execution, abc software does not require the opening repeat sign at the beginning. It is understood in standard music notation.

The chording is a little simpler.
Note the D# accidental in the B-strain, measure 8.

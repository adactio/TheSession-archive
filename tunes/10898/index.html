The Pleasant Beggar

This was played by a visiting piper (sorry Billy, don’t know your surname) in Ballycastle last Saturday. It was new to me, and I was taken by the twist in the second part. It was written by American (?) guitarist Russ Barenberg, and feeding the title into youtube brings up video of the composer playing it with Aly Bain and others on the Transatlantic Sessions show. That’s where this transcrition is from.

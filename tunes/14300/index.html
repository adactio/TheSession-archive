Originally by Stephen Foster

Angelina Baker was written by the 1850s American songwriter Stephen Foster. Here’s a link to a rendition of his original:
http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WCjha5C8IrI

My friend does a nice, slow rendition of that on clawhammer banjo, and I accompany on fiddle. We then switch to the bluegrassy version posted by Mix, which is much better known these days. That set is lots of fun for the players and the listeners.

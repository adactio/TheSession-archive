Nine comments

A lovely air recently composed by Joanie Madden for a New Zealand man who evidently found her missing camera and was able to trace it back to her . . . by linking the license plate number seen in one of her digital pictures back to her aunt and uncle in England, who then sent it back to Joanie in the U.S.!

Was truly moved when she played this live at a St. Patrick’s Day concert in Burlington, Vermont . . . definitely check out the recording!

The Boggeragh Mountains Polka

This fun little polka was penned by Mikie O’Shea, fiddle player and teacher from Nead an fhIolar, Co. Cork (also known as Nadd). C major is the tune’s original key and fiddlers (as well as string players in general) will no doubt enjoy playing it this way as did yours truly! Transposable to D major for wind players that want to keep the bottom C.

Mikie plays this polka on his album of the same name which I can’t recommend enough – a rare modern recording of pure Sliabh Luachra solo fiddle playing!

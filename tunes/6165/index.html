Recording and Set

This is the final tune in the three-reel set (The Gallowhill Reel, The Spider’s Web and The Road to Diabaig) composed by Scottish fiddle player Ian Hardie (as played on his cd ‘The Spider’s Web’ (Greentrax Recordings)) When playing this tune after ‘The Spider’s Web’ you do not play the ‘B’ lead-in note when going into the tune. The other two tunes are also on ‘the session’ if anyone wants to play them as a set. !!!Make sure you find the reel version of ‘The Spider’s Web’ and NOT the jig version - which is also on ‘the session’.!!!

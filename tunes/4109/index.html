Five comments

Roddam Dene

I wrote this hornpipe in the Newcastle style for NSPipes with a low B key, although with a couple of tweaks it’d go on fiddle too. It’s meant to be played as though it’s in 12/8 - nice and smooth and flowing like this:

M: 12/8
B2c|d2g g2e f2d fga|g3 d2c B2G D2C|B,2D G2B C2E G2c|B2A G2B A3 B2c|

Roddam Dene

I like this tune and have been polaying it on English Concertina but I’m sorry, it just doesn’t sound like a Tyneside Hornpipe to me.

The A part is fine (and original ) but the B goes more like a set dance. I keep hearing the Ace and Deuce of Pipering when I play this. I suspect that the critical bar is bar 6 where the two pedal figures are not completed by a third and the tune continues off in search of more broken arpegios. The rhythmn is fine and the modulations are fine. A few more falling sixths would be more typical but possibly toocorny for comfort.

So as I said, nice tune, and I will undoubtedly be playing it , but not a Newcastle Hornpipe as we know it.
Noel Jackson
Angelsof the North

↳ Posted by Noel Jackson on January 17th, 2005.

Thanks Noel. After a bit of thought I think you’re right. It’d need to be more arpeggio-y and maybe with some 6th leaps or something. Now I feel challenged to have another go and do it properly this time! :-)

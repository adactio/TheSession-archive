Three comments

“Johnny an Gabha”

I did an extensive search in the two keys and with different bars and combinations of notes, surprised that I didn’t find it on site, though I did find some ‘relatives’.

This is an old standard I’ve given here in two keys and also as it is sometimes resolved, the last take in D Major, suggested in the middle version in G Major, with a ‘second ending’, the first bar of the final 4-bar phrase mirroring the A-part ending.

↳ Posted by ceolachan on July 22nd, 2005.

Two comments

The Loss Of Light

This lovely waltz was composed by the terrific young musician from near Carrig-on-Bannow in Wexford, Cian O Grady. Cian is a member of the young Carrig group ‘Tulua’ who are recently making a nice name for themselves both at home and overseas for their music. Cian has composed some of the band’s repertoire as has the group’s fiddler, Kayleigh Moran. Sincere thanks to Nóra Byrne Kavanagh for the transcription of this piece.
https://www.facebook.com/tuluamusic/

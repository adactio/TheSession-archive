Crossing Paddy Nolan’s Gate

One of a pair of hornpipes composed by the Wexford accordionist John Redmond, originally from Ballindaggin, resident for many years in the U.S. and now back in Wexford in Ferns. It’s from his excellent solo album ‘Box Sets’ which features his own compositions.

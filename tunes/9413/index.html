Yes thank you for your help! I did receive the e-mail, and followed some of those links! About the tune though… it’s a composition of my own. The title comes from my dog Marty. He was a terrier mix that I adopted at the pound. (The black makes me think part scottish.) Sadly he had a terrible habit of chasing everything that moved, even cars… He slipped out without his leash and, well you get it. With the coming of spring and the nicer weather I just realized how much I miss him.

I find it works well with a slightly up tempo version of The Wedding Reel

Monie’s Polka

I found this lovely and lively polka on Suzanne Rowe’s Tiktok page. Suzanne is from Mooncoin, Co.Kilkenny. She is a wonderful musician, plays piano and piano accordion and has composed some very fine tunes. Her tunes are inspired by local places, people and events. This polka was written by Suzanne for her friend Catherine (Monie) who is a huge fan of set dancing and I am sure it has been heard around Mooncoin many a night for a Kerry set or the 6th figure of the Cashel.

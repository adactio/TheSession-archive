Arthur’s Seat, X:2

Arthur’s Seat was originally written in Bb (by J. Scott Skinner)
The setting from the Skinner manuscript was added under Fly By Night setting X:5 along with some very good notes added by Kevin Rietmann (thanks Kevin)
https://thesession.org/tunes/3122
I’ve added it again here for clarity (it is actually a different tune to Fly By Night) and to include the missing bar fromt he first part.

Arthur’s Seat, X:3

Matt Molloy’s version from “Back To The Island”.
The first long note in the second last line is supposed to be a roll.
The breathing breaks have been changed to longer notes by abc.

In 1975 my friends and I (from a schoolband) had visited Edinburgh and we drove to the parking place below Athur’s Seat in the evening to sleep there outside.
There was not much left to the “top”, may be 5 minutes to walk. A gang of elderly folks passed us by, fully equipped with mountain gear, ropes and all that.
We were thrilled.

At that time Matt had still played with the Bothy Band.

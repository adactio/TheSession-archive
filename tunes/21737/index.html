Re: The Glens Of Angus

I’m surprised to hear it’s “common.”

This tune is actually called “Miss Mackenzie’s Reel” and was published by Robert Mackintosh (c1745-1807) in his third collection, and it was probably composed by him. The title “The Glens of Angus” is the name of the dance it is used for; there is a tune called “Glens of Angus” but it’s a waltz.

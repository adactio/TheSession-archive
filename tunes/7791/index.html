Jig Of The Living Dead

I came across “Jig of the Living Dead” a while ago looking for tunes inspired by halloween. Jig of the Living Dead sounds great in original form but I feel it doesn’t sound enough like an actual Irish jig. I took the main body of the tune and made it sound more “jiggy” as although it is 6/8, it plays like a reel. Or maybe that’s because I learn by ear..

Credit to the original writer but I thought I would edit my own version. Maybe it’s not great but no harm in trying.

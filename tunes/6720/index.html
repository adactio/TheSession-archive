Re: Ship Of Grace

Ship Of Grace (or Ship O‘ Grace) is a Scottish dance, a strathspey. You can find the directions on the Internet (e.g. https://www.scottish-country-dancing-dictionary.com/dance-crib/ship-o-grace.html). The setting here from Rob Gordon is really not a tune itself, but a set of two reasonably well-known songs, which can be played as reels or strathspeys. The first (corresponding to parts A and B here) is The Boatie Rows (or Boattie on some tracks). Cilla Fisher, among many others, has recorded it as a song and it is in many reel sets. The second song (parts C and D of this setting) is Willie Brew’d a Peck o’ Maut, a song with words by Robert Burns, recorded dozens of times. It is also often done as a reel, and likely a strathspey, though I couldn’t find a recording of one.

The set is probably called Ship O‘ Grace on Rob’s album because it is intended to be the music for dancing the Ship O’ Grace strathspey. You can find it described this way in this Scottish Country Dance Database: https://my.strathspey.org/dd/album/754/

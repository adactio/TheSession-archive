Re: Charming Lovely Nancy

There are two sets of lyrics associated with this slide. Breandan Breathnach printed them in his notes to tune N°63 (An Chearc ar fad is an tAnraith) in his second volume of Ceol Rince na hÉireann (1976)

A. 《Charming lovely Nancy》
I am a maiden going for milk
says Nancy says Nancy
I am a maiden going for milk
says charming lovely Nancy
And what would you do if I followed you
says Jimmy says Jimmy
what would you do if I followed you
says lazy lingering Jimmy.

B. 《 An Chearc ar fad is an tAnraith》
Gheobha’ mé síos dtí Cill na Mac
gheobha’ mé im is uibhe na gcearc
cúl na bulóige chur le m’ais
an chearc ar fad ‘s a’ t-anraith
Nín (níl) aon oíche dhéanaim cleas
ná faighinse im is uibhe na gcearc
an chúlóg mhór do chur le m’ais
an chearc ar fad ‘s a’ t-anraidh.

…which may translate as somethimg like;
"I went down to Kilnamac
for butter and eggs to bag
plus the back of the back to my back
the whole chicken in the soup
There’s no night I don’t play tricks
to get butter and hen’s eggs
and the bulgy pigback to my back
all of last year’s chicken".

Breathnach mentions several singers as his source.

Stoney Brennan

This is a Vincent Broderick composition, and instantly one of my favorites of his. A fluter friend bought Broderick’s book, The Turoe Stone Collection, while over in Ireland and this was one of the first tunes he learned from it. So it’s pretty much as transcribed in the Turoe Stone Collection.

Stoney Brennan was a peat farmer hanged for stealing a turnip. The book includes a photo of Vincent standing by a stone marker with Mr. Brennan’s likeness.

↳ Posted by Will Harmon on May 15th, 2008.

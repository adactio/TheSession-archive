Re: Within A Mile Of Edinburgh

Actually a very old song: words originally “Within a Furlong of Edinborough Town” by Thomas D’Urfey (1653-1723), but later altered to “Within a Mile of Edinburgh Town”. Tune origin debated: maybe Henry Purcell, maybe Jeremiah Clarke? (Same debate over “Trumpet Voluntary”!)
If it’s of any interest, see the 2 links below:

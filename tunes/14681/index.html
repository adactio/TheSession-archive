Three comments

Disconnect

Tune by Jeremy Kittel, he said he named it Disconnect because the B part came to him in the shower one morning, but he wasn’t able to come up with a suitable A part for several months after that.

I learned it from him in a workshop this summer, and can’t get it out of my mind so thought I would share it here to spread the joy.

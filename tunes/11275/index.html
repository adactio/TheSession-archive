Wonderful tune - a French scottish

Actually this is not a barndance but a French scottish by Frederic Paris. I got to know the tune yesterday at a session in northern Germany and I just got addicted to it. At the session they played it just like this guy:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Qt-ohf6jUo

I came across an ABC somewhere in another key that deviated slightly from this wonderful version, so I altered it - now it matches the music.

Three comments

The Oriental

No idea who composed this funky polka.

Aontas recorded it on their self-titled album, Hanz Araki on “Foreign Shore”, and DÁN on “Stranger At The Gate”.

This tune is a mix-up of The Rattlin’ Bog. The A & B parts are flips, the now A-part is put into a minor key & changed a bit, and the now B-part is simplified a bit, with a few minor variations thrown in.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on June 11th, 2019.

Few variations, and some syncopation.

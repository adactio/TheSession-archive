Claire Mann

I have a great regard for Claire’s music. Unlike many younger players, she doesn’t see the need for a “flashy” or “trendy” approach but plays good traditional music(Irish, Scots, Northumbrian and more) in a very straight forward relaxed way. Her musicianship, arrangements, and choice of accompanying musicians are all immaculate. I wish there were more young players like her.

↳ Posted by 🎻 Melody Player 🎻 on June 4th, 2004.

The Merry Gardener (hornpipe)

This beautiful tune comes from East Galway flute player Sean Moloney’s solo recording, where it is mistakenly titled “The Merry Gardiner.” His version is basically the same as the one which appears in O’Neill’s with slight differences here and there.

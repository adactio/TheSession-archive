I enjoy Jim’s tune and have given it some time, and a bit of steppin’ too. But I confess I choose to take it as a 16 bar highland fling. That’s the way it speaks to me and I find it fits the dance well… ;-)

Some young-uns, and that can now be folks in their 50s and up, have often called highland flings ‘barndances’. Older and wiser folk, many no longer with us, clearly saw a destinction between the various generally swung forms and would not mistake a barndance for a highland fling, or vice versa. :-D

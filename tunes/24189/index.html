Two comments

The Mullinalaghta Shout

Written by Aoibhinn Lynch, who won the 2023 Fleadh Cheoil “original compositions” with it. And no wonder: it’s very catchy. She plays it at a handy pace (not fast), and in C. The range is slightly over two octaves, so if your instrument can’t handle that, you may have to resort to the usual tactics (“folding” the low notes up an octave, perhaps substituting the high C with a lower note - you know the drill).

Listen to her here: https://www.facebook.com/reel/1618341522299516

↳ Posted by Frank Gibbons on April 7th, 2024.

Belle Gigue

This is an old Acadian dance jig. I’m sure it’s a forgotten Irish Jig because the patterns are alike, you can easily add rolls, and our ancesters have learned their jigs from the irish settlers . If anybody has a name for it, he or she is more than wellcome.

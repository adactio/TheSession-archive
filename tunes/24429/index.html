One comment

Bartin’s Bay

“Bartin’s Bay” is a three part reel composed by Brendan Mulholland and giving its name to the flutist’s latest album (2024). You can here the tune with many varitations on the track 02.

Here is the comment of Brendan Mulholland taken directly from the CD booklet : “Bartin’s Bay is an enclave of Lough Neagh, close to my village in Glenavy and it’s a beautiful place where I find the view of Ram’s Island and soft rolling waters very peaceful, which inspired this tune.”

↳ Posted by Martial on June 22nd, 2024.

Richie Dwyer’s Dream

Transcription from Richard Dwyer’s lovely CD ‘In a Creative Mood,’ which seems to be all his own tunes. The accordion playing is a lovely, relaxed lilting style, and on this tune in particular, it’s also very powerful and deep.

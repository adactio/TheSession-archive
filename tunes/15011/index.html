PREFACE

The Art of Dancing has been for many ages much esteem’d & honour’d in most Courts of Europe, But not so much improv’d as it apparently appears of late that it is certainly a most healthful & noble Excercise, very much becoming a Gentleman.

Therefore I think it would be needless to dwell any longer upon this subject. I come briefly now to acquaint those what are Lovers of this Art, what induced me to the Publication of this Book: Several Gentlemen &c complain’d that there has been so few that were useful Dances in Books of this Kind that they were of no Service to them, threfore they encourag’d me to go on with this Work, which I don’t doubt but it will meet with a general Approbation, not only from those who are Lovers of Dancing, but likewise by all Judicious Musicians who play to Country Dancing, who I am certain will account this Book to be highly necessary for their Use: by Reason I have made it my cheif Business to collect together all the favourite English and Scotch Dances that are now in Vogue: and are now in great Esteem at Court, and likewise at most public & private Balls: And to render this Work more acceptable as well as usefull, I have prefixt the true names to each Tune, and the most modern Way of Dancing them, so that I hope the whole will be receiv’d with the same good Will as it is offer’d by their Humble Servant & c. D. Wright

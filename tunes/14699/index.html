Two comments

Absinthe

A friend of mine recommended I listen to Crowfoot she’d heard playing on Spotify. I really love their arrangements and original tunes. A few of our local session players have got really excited about this tune from their second album “As The Crow Flies.” Its a marvellous lyrical, slightly bendy tune with some very atmospheric backing on bouzouki on the recording. Right up my alley.

Miss Gordon of Park

Lovely jig by William Marshall. This is a Scottish tune, but the rolls I’ve added are my own little twist on the melody. There are lots of options in this tune for spicing it up a bit…

Miss Gordon of Park

I discovered this on a wonderful old Winston Scotty Fitzgerald record I found in the trash, medlied with The Champion. It also goes very well with Miss McPherson Grant, another great Marshall jig, in A.

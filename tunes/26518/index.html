The May Fairy

Sióg na Bealtaine: The May Fairy
Composed by Tom Delany in memory of Marion Rival
(A composition requested by a group of Marion’s friends as a gift for her family on May 1st 2020)

Tom Delany: Uilleann pipes, whistles, bouzouki et bodhran
Camille Philippe: Guitar and mandolin

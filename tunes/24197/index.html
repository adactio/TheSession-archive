Four comments

The Maine Banks

This is another composition by the wonderful accordion player Leslie Craig. As many may already know, Leslie lives in Cullybackey, Co. Antrim and I suspect this tune has more than a slight connection with the river that flows through the village.

↳ Posted by e fein on April 10th, 2024.

The Maine Banks, X:3

I have been working with Leslie Craig and others over the past few years to collate all his compositions and have them published in book form. That work is now almost complete. When he first gave me this reel, The Maine Banks, it was in the key of A major (as posted by e fein), but he has since decided he prefers it in the key of D major He recorded it for me recently to update the book prior to printing, and this is my transcription from that recording which we will use henceforth. This differs only very slighly from the version posted by Swisspiper, but this is how the composer plays it. NB the triplet at the end of the first part and the octave hops on E in the 3rd and 7th bars of the second part.

↳ Posted by seanquinn on April 16th, 2024.

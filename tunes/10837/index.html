Strawberry Tree. The

There is no composer’s name for this reel. But I believe it was named after the Irish Strawberry Tree, sometimes known as the Killarney Strawberry Tree. It is an small evergreen tree that grows to around 15 metres, and is most common in western European Countries of Spain, France and Ireland. It grows fruit that is edible but very bland and it usually left for birds to eat.

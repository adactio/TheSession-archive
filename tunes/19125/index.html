Four comments

Caroline O’Neill’s

Not so many four-part hornpipes around. I’ve taken a version played locally (Bob Taylor’s) and replaced triplets and accidentals with straight eighths.

The version in TuneArch/Fiddlers‘ Companion is straight from O’Neill’s: https://tunearch.org/wiki/Caroline_O%27Neill%27s_Hornpipe

↳ Posted by benhockenberry on April 3rd, 2020.

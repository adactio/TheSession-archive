Da White Wife

This is a four-beat polka which I would guess is maybe composed by Chris Stout…?

Fiddlers listening to it on Naked & Bare will hear lots of extra twiddly bits, which are sometimes necessary to find a good fingering for the details which I included in the notation.

I like that the A part is the “high part” and it ends in a lower register.

Re: Da White Wife

@DonaldK I classified it as a polka because the rhythm sounds to me as “ONE two (THREE) four”: somewhat staccato, with emphasis on the down-beat. It’s a four-beat polka because emphasis on those third beats is often held back (or absent due to notes being long or tied).

As for the tempo, one could argue for notating it in 4/2, which is probably more correct anyway, although that confuses some folkies who have more limited conventions and less interest in theory.

It’s probably the across-bar ties which are making the playback glitch. In which case there’s no way to “sort” it without also notating it incorrectly.

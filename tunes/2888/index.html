Re: Her Mantle So Green

I can hear the similarities to “Caide Sin Don Te Sin”, but I don’t think it’s the same… that melody has a couple accidentals which give it an almost ‘jazzy’ feel, depending how it’s played; “Her Mantle so green” is a more regal-sounding, more “complete” and full tune. To my ears anyway.

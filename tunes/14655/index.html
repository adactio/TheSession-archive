Two comments

The Humours Of Cloyne

Another tune from the Goodman Manuscripts, #56. I heard it from Peter Browne’s playing (in between Kissing and Drinking and The Cauliflower) on the ITMA’s “Ceol Goodman le Peter Browne”.

↳ Posted by KJM on August 16th, 2015.

The Humours Of Cloyne, X:2

This is from Caoimhín Ó Fearghail’s uilleann piping album. Same tune with small tweaks that really add flavour to the B part.

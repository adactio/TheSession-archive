Ceannt The Piper

By request, a transcription of this tune as played by the author (Charlie Lennon) and Ronan Browne, on the Gael Linn album, “Áille na hÁille – A Terrible Beauty – A Suite in Honour of the Heroes of 1916”.

https://soundcloud.com/journalofmusic/ceannt-the-piper-ronan-browne-uilleann-pipes-charlie-lennon-fiddle

Ronan plays it in the key of F Major, on a flat set pitched in C Natural. For convenience I have transposed it to the key of G Major.

Re: Lads O The Fair

It was a very good time for Scottish Folk Music, especially as far as bands were concerned.

“Home is where the Van is” from which this track comes was one of the “must have” albums in the early eighties. However, Ossian were also “On the rise”(That’ll make a good title for a Battlefield Band album in due course) and their album “Seal Song” was very popular then too.
Of course, Silly Wizard and The Tannahill Weavers were riding high too and Ceolbeg were just emerging…. Pre Kenny days. :-) We also had Dick Gaughan’s “Handful of Earth” and the “Cilla and Artie” album.
What a great time to be alive!

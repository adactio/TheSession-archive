Two comments

German Elf

Here is a lovely tune, which was composed by Pádraig McGovern in 2023 for a german friend. He composed it on a C-Set of uillean pipes. Hence it may be comfortable for some to play it in D maj on D-tuned instruments, but C is also easy to play.

↳ Posted by Silas Ulbrich on January 22nd, 2024.

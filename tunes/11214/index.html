Re: Marie MacLennan

According to Discogs, “Marie MacLellan (1922-2006) was a pianist born in Princeville, Inverness County, Cape Breton, Canada. Marie has accompanied some of the biggest names in Cape Breton music circles, including not only her own siblings, but Winston “Scotty” Fitzgerald, Joe MacLean, and Angus Chisholm to name but a few. She was also featured as one of the influential pianists on Buddy MacMaster’s “Judique Flyer” CD (2000), that showcased all of the various accompanists associated with Buddy over the years.”
She also accompanied the famous fiddler Dan R. MacDonald – on at least one recording (LP Celtic CX. 28, 1967) – who composed this tune for her.

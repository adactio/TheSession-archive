Two comments

The Empress’s New Clothes

From the Belgian band Hot Griselda. This slow reel in F#m has a driving feel that reminds me of John McSherry’s Tripswitch. The combo of Irish pipes and soprano sax is strangely compelling. Give a listen here:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIkyog18YGg

↳ Posted by jdicarlo on January 9th, 2017.

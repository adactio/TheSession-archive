Three comments

Barbra’s Jig

I learned this as a Highland bagpipe tune, and i love it, I transferred it onto the whistle, and it sounds good on that. Play it very very fast, and all the “c” gracenotes are just a blip made by lifting your top finger of the top hand. You shouldn’t even be able to tell what note it is. For the last bar on the first line, “|{c}E{G}E{A}E {c}FED|”, {c} is the top finger of the top hand, {G} is the top finger of the bottom hand, and {A} is the bottom finger of the top hand. Same goes for these grace notes, tiny blip. You probably already knew all of that… but oh well :)

↳ Posted by Mark de Jong on July 26th, 2002.

Barbara’s Jig

There are 4 parts to this tune, (as with most pipe tunes), and it should start with “high”D as the first note.

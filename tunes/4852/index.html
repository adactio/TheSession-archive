Three comments

There’re some nice variations on both parts of this tune:
DGA B2c | B2c AFD | F3 AFc | dcB AGF |
DGG G2A | B2c Ace | f2d cAF | AGF G2F :|
g3 g3 | g2a bag | f2f fef | (3aag f ecA |
dgg ^fgg dga bag | f2d agf | dcA GAd :|

I wrote it a few months back, after I’d been back along the Rubane Road which is near my family’s home on the Ards peninsula in Co.Down for the first time in a while - I’d forgotten how stomach-destroyingly bad it is in places!

Three comments

Kathleen O’More

Version in O‘Neill - Music of Ireland (1903), no. 110. Also called “Kathleen O’Moore.”

This is a jig, different from the 4/4 tune “The Hawk Of Ballyshannon” https://thesession.org/tunes/13246 that is here listed as having the alternate name “Kathleen O’Moore” as well. It is also different from the 3/4 tune in the Francis Roche collection under that title.

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 18th, 2022.

Kathleen O’More, X:2

Version in Clinton’s Gems of Ireland, no. 32. As was his wont, he arranged it to spend much time in the higher register, and with extravagant ornamentation.

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 18th, 2022.

Re: Kathleen O’More

I was wrong, this is the same tune in the Francis Roche collection, but he had transcribed it as 3/4 time with essentially quarter notes where the jig version has eighths, and so twice as many bars.

Two comments

Mick Carr’s Highland

The second of two highlands with this name on the late Donegal fiddler James Byrne’s “The Road to Glenlough”. A nice tune in itself, and it goes well with the first one in the set, also in Dmaj, which is already in the database.

↳ Posted by Daniel Parker on June 10th, 2018.

Source

I just stumbled upon what I think is the ancestor of this highland: a Scottish reel (not a strathspey) usually called “Dunnichen”. It is tune #156 in the Skye Collection, which cites Peter Milne’s 1870 collection as its source. According to the Traditional Tune Archive, it also appears in the collections of Archibald Duff (1794) and Malcolm MacDonald (1797), the latter calling it “Miss Ranken’s Reel”. Cool!

Info

This tune was composed by Niall Vallely. I transcribed it off his CD with his brother, Cillian, “Callan Bridge.” It’s a little odd and when it’s played stupid fast it sounds great. Enjoy . . . I know I do.

I personally find it odd that John Doyle plays Amix chords to this tune on their CD… I’ve always heard it as an Ador kind of a tune. Love this one, though.

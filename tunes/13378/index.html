Year Of Jubilo

A Traditional American Civil War Tune composed by Henry Clay Work; as played by the Rum and Onions Contra Band in NJ. I need the tune in my tune book for playing a bunch of Don Messer tunes, and that’s why it’s here.

↳ Posted by Alan Wilson on February 11th, 2014.

In my home town (Bristol, UK) we have a big choice of sessions to go to. One of them is an old-timey session, and I’ve heard “Year of Jubilo” played there. I hadn’t yet committed the tune to memory, and you’ve now supplied with with the incentive to learn it.

So thanks for posting! :-)

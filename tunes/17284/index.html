Serendipity

This is Serendipity from Goitse’s 4th studio album “Inspired by Chance”. Composed by Áine Mc Geeney and James Harvey. Watch out, the time signature changes from 5/8 to 7/8 on the ‘C ’part.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eto-lYTMH4U

Star of Kilkenny

I learnt this from a friend who learnt it from Harry Bradley at Scoil Acla a few years back. I usually play it sandwiched between Julie Delaney’s and The Providence.

There’s another tune of the same name here (also called Thady Casey’s Fancy) which is obviously related but I think there is a significant enough difference to warrant a new entry.

Duplicated or Not

IMHO there’s no big difference between Harry Bradley’s version and the more common version posted a few years ago: https://thesession.org/tunes/2663 I’d love to hear other people’s opinions.

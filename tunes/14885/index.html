Blackeyed Biddie

This jig appears in O’Neill’s Dance Music of Ireland - 1001 Gems (1907), p. 29, no. 86, as Blackeyed Biddie, Brigidin Dub-Suileac.
According to the Fiddler’s Companion, there is no other name, but it’s a version of the “Captain Collins/Montrose’s March” family of tunes.

↳ Posted by Moxhe on November 1st, 2015.

Re: Blackeyed Biddie

Perhaps the oldest name for it is “A Scotish March” (Playford 1663). Other titles include “Iain Caimbeul a’ Bhanca” (John Campbell of the Bank), “The Rock and the Wee Pickle Tow”, “The Gordons March” “Montrose’s March” “The Highlander’s March” and “The Linlithgow March”.

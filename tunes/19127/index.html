Two comments

Miss Florence Bowers

This tune is by Dr Simon McKerrell, piper and academic. ‘Miss Florence Bowers’ is freely available on Simon’s website, along with many others, at https://simonmckerrell.com/music/tunes/ but I felt that it was high time that it was perhaps brought to a wider audience.
Simon refers to ‘Miss Florence Bowers’ as being an ‘air’, but it could equally fall into the ‘march’ category?
You can also follow Simon online @piper_simon

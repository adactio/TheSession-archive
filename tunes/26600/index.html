It May Well Be

A three-part hornpipe by Dónal McCague from his album with Tommy Fitzharris. He plays with a lot of lovely ornamentation, including snaps instead of triplets at several points I haven’t notated here. If anyone would like to take a crack at a full transcription with ornaments and bowings, awesome!

From the liner notes, “‘It May Well Be’ was composed by Dónal in May 2015 while in quite a contemplative mindset. Having sent a recording of this tune to Johnny Óg Connolly, Johnny, in turn, wrote a hornpipe inspired by snippets of Dónal’s hornpipe and named it ‘The Monaghan Fiddler’. Johnny recorded this tune on Track 4 of his ‘Siar’ album.”

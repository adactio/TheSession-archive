Nuala’s Fiddle

Composed by piper and singer Tomas O’Canainn, who thanks to a recent discussion, has kindly given his permission for this tune to be posted here. He recorded it along with his daughter Nuala on fiddle on an LP record called “Beal Na Tra” on the Belfast “Outlet” label in 1982. The album sleeve-notes say that the tune was composed “to celebrate the fiddle given to Nuala by my [ Tomas’s ] mother”.

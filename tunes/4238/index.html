Two comments

Source info

Tune was gathered from “The Scots Fiddle, Vol. 1, Tunes, Tales and Traditions of the North-East and Central Highlands” by J. Murray Neil, p.57. The source he gathered it from was “A Collection of Strathspeys, Reels and Irish Jigs for the Pianoforte and Violin etc.” written circa 1806 by Isaac Cooper.

Looking for other info on the tune, I’ve found mention of a tune called Jarnovich’s Hornpipe, not sure if they might be related.

Two comments

Housegarth Two-Step

This is another tune from a written collection of Orkney tunes: ‘Iris Nicholson’s Favourites - Mainly the Garson Trio’ (referred to on the cover as ‘Book 1’, though I’ve never tracked down a Book 2. Any info, anyone?)

Posting it is prompted by an exchange of notes with DonaldK in connection with my putting up another from the same source (‘Barbara Stevenson’, q.v.) in which he kindly explained the distinction between a straightforward jig and a tune used for a two-step - one of many matters about which, not being a dancer, I’m woefully ill-informed (though slightly less so now). The title of this one avoids ambiguity, perhaps, though we still have to list it as a jig on account of 6/8. This one is by Jackie Linklater, son of Jock L. who wrote ‘Barbara Stevenson’. I like it a lot, and it’s gone down well at a couple of local sessions in my neck of the woods.

(Chords are my addition: don’t blame Mr Linklater).

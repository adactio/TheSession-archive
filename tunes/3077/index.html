Three comments

NPU set dance tapes

This is on the Na Píobairí Uilleann “Music for the Sets” tape called “Show the Lady.” Breanndán Ó Beaglaoich (Button Accordion), Gerry Bevan (Fiddle), and Steve Cooney (Guitar) played it for the Corca Dhuibne Set along with John Kelly’s Slide and The Hare in the Corn.

↳ Posted by Karen Joyce Lowe on May 26th, 2004.

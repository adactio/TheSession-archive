Originals

All music was once new, even traditional tunes…

I write tunes rather frequently (being a classical composer in real life - don’t hold it against me…) and probably end up with one or two a month that I consider good. I will usually trot those out at a local session in a quiet moment to get some feedback. Sometimes they even catch on. The few original tunes that I have posted here are the ones friends and colleagues have requested I submit so that they can share them with other people.

I have been fortunate enough to sit in on a session 800 miles from where I live and have the folks break into one of my tunes in the middle of a set. I hold that experience dearer than any successes I have had with my choral and orchestral music.

HL

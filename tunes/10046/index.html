Re: Deildy Aberteifi

it’s a very common tune that first appears in print in davidson’s musical miracles 1859 with a curious third part to the tune in d. a standard for dancing, but in two parts, i first heard it when i was a pupil in school, played on piano by eirlys phillips - when i was 15 i guess. a favourite of mike james on button accordion, i heard it afresh listening to his playing, later on

Sadeger, now Sadgora, the Ukraine

Sadeger is in the Ukraine, a town now called Sadgora. The following version, in print and as a midi, is based on the cymbalom playing of Joseph Moskowitz, who was recorded in 1916, “Klezmer Music” Folklyric 9034. A transcript of this version can be found here:

“Mel Bay’s Klezmer Collection” (Bb & C editions available)
Compiled and edited by Stacy Phillips
Mel Bay Publications, Inc., 1996
ISBN: 0-7866-0841-2

Page 140: “Sadeger Khosid”

& the midi here, the homepage given first:

http://tancos2.pmachinehosting.com/
http://www.tancos2.net/midis4/SadegerKhosid.MID

As Phillips says of this version, it "represents the un-Americanized sound of klezmer…

While familiar, neither of these two versions are as I’ve known it, but it has been awhile… Sholom aleichem…

“Shalom Ireland” ~ a documentary film & “Ceilizemer”

http://www.shalomireland.com/

“Shalom Ireland is a one hour documentary about Ireland’s remarkable, yet little known Jewish community. Shalom Ireland chronicles the history of Irish Jewry while celebrating the unique culture created by blending Irish and Jewish traditions. From gun running for the Irish Republican Army during Ireland’s War of Independence to smuggling fellow Jews escaping from the Holocaust into Palestine, Shalom Ireland tells the untold story of how Irish Jews participated in the creation and development of both Ireland and Israel. ~”

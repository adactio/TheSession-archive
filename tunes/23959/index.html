Two comments

Riddrie

This is a traditional Scottish Strathspey. It is the second tune in the first set on “Canada’s Outstanding Scottish Fiddler” Winston Scotty Fitzgerald. I transcribed it to the best of my ability form the W.F. Collection Fiddle Tunes arranged by Paul Cranford (Cranford Publications) tune 74. below is an ABC without the “thrills” and rolls.
For the audio set the tempo for 60-80

Re: Riddrie

Ken Perlman included a setting of this tune in his collection ‘Fiddle Music of Prince Edward Island’ (Mel Bay 1996). Titled “Winston’s Strathspey,” it was recorded from fiddler Francis MacDonald. In both that one and the setting in Kerr’s Merry Melodies, the d in the second bar of the B part is sharp.

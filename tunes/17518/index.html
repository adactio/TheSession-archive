Gan Ainm

This is a Donegal highland performed by James Byrne and Dermot McLaughlin in this video at about 5:52:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8PVj_NIPM6Y

Stiamh, on this site, has noted the similarity between the second parts of this tune and “a well-known highland associated with John Doherty”, which I think may be “Miss Sarah Drummond of Perth” (https://thesession.org/tunes/1556). Anyway, enjoy!

Sorry, Barry, but you should listen to the recording again. As mentioned above, there has been confusion between the use of the titles “The West Wind” and “Gilbert Clancy’s / Sean Reid’s”. The tune I posted above
20 years ago - [ erroneously I now accept, and now corrected ] - was the 3-part “The West Wind” as played by the Kellys in the Youtube clip linked before this. Gabriel McKeon plays the 2-part “Gilbert Clancy’s / Sean Reid’s” which is posted elsewhere.
I have no idea, now, in 2023, why I thought at the time that parts of the tune were “doubled”. Although I must have heard it played thus at some point, that is certainly not the commonest way of playing it and I have made the necessary corrections to the original transcription.
I sincerely apologise for that - Mea culpa - [ Latin for “my bad” :) ].

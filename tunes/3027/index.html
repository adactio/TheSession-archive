Bobby Mcleod

Written by the late great Bobby MacLeod from Tobermory as in the TV show “What’s the story in Balamory?” :-). He also wrote other cracking tunes such as Jean’s Reel and Charlie Hunter.
This tune was recorded by the Easy Club and is very popular in Scottish sessions. It’s actually more of a march than a jig, although the timing is 6/8.

John

Re: Murdo MacKenzie Of Torridon

The first Pipe Band I joined, in the late 1970s, played three sets of 6/8 Marches, and this tune (the first setting above) was in one of them.

It being fairly widely played in Highland pipe circles I had always assumed it was a standard Highland pipe tune, but I was surprised just now to find out that it’s not in my copy of the well-known “An Encyclopaedia of Tunes for the Great Highland Bagpipe” by Robert Pekaar.

I had no idea it was written by a box player, and hasn’t appeared in any of the 223 Highland pipe collections covered by Pekaar.

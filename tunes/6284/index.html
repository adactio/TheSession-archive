One comment

Garrison Road

This three-part reel is by Oisin McAuley, fiddler with Danu, and is transcribed from their “When All is Said and Done” cd.

The tune really suits Oisin’s driving, get-after-it style, but still retains some nice, subtle phrases (the 4th bar in the A part, for instance, and the high b in bar 4 of the C part, and the |gfec Bceg| bit that repeats in the C part).

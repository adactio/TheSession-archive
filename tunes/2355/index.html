The Perils of Drinking

This tune was written on a rainy early-January day in Philadelphia. Originally, the melody alternated between being in C major and A minor. It wasn’t until I was playing it with John Brennan at one of the many McGillian/Boyce family weddings in Philadelphia that the melody worked its way firmly into shape. Later that evening, upon the unusual request of a martini while every one else was drinking beer, the tune was named!

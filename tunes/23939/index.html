The Half Century

I’ve been learning a few tunes composed by the late Ian Sinclair.

I’m sure many of you will be familiar with his work with Mirk and his well known songs “The King’s Shilling”, “Tak a dram afore ye go” among others.

This is a nice march which is part of “the John Inglis set”.

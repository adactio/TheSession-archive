Re: West Fork Gals

Bull at the Wagon is an authentic Texas fiddle tune. According to www.tunearch.org this tune comes from the Lewis Brothers, Dempson and Denmon, who recorded the tune on 78 rpm disc in 1929 in El Paso Texas. The Lewis Brothers lived in the TransPecos region of Texas, which is a huge area of mountains and desert. The tune is actually a reel in Amaj.

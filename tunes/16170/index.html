Torosay Castle, X:3

Lovely tune, Jack - thanks for bringing to my attention. The third setting bove is very close to your first one with minor changes and bar line moves. I know that many retreats are notated the way you’ve done it, but my personal preference is to move the first beat to become pick-up notes.

Torosay Castle is on the island of Mull.

Re: Torosay Castle

“I know that many retreats are notated the way you’ve done it, but my personal preference is to move the first beat to become pick-up notes.”

Yup, that makes total sense to me. However pipers would say that since, in retreats, every beats gets equal weight it doesn’t make any difference. The difference is that if you don’t move the bar lines the harmony generally changes on the second beat of the bar rather than the first. Not that that would matter to pipers, I guess.

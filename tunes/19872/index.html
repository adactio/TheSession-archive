Three comments

Saltón De Samartín

This is a saltón. It is the equivalent to the Irish reel in Asturian Traditional Music.

Sometimes saltones can be pretty similar to a reel regarding the rhythm and the parts. But many times they can have irregular metrics like this one.

First part in 3/2, the rest in 2/4. First part 4 bars repeated twice. Second part 4 bars repeated twice. Third part 6 bars played once but it can be seen that the first phrasing in the first two bars are repeated twice and that is why there are 6 bars instead of 4. Fourth part with 4 bars repeated twice.

This setting has no ornamentation and was taken from Asturian band Felpeyu in their wonderful album Yá. It is the third tune in the 11th track.

For this tune it is written in the cd in English:

“Finally a saltón from Samartín del Rei Aurelio that Lisardo got from Octavio Trapiella, accordion player from Morea (Ayer)”

(Lisardo Prieto is the fiddle player of Felpeyu and Samartín del Rei Aurelio and Ayer are different regions of Asturias, similar to the Irish counties)

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on October 19th, 2020.

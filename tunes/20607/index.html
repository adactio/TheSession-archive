Three comments

Sean Quinn’s

This is an Ed Reavy tune. The setting is from “The Collected Compositions of Ed Reavy”. The notes in that collection indicate that Sean Quinn is “Lou’s oldest son, a very talented musician who plays professionally in the New York area. He also helped produce Ed’s first volume of tunes in 1971”. Lou is Louis Quinn, who was a contemporary and friend of Ed Reavy.

↳ Posted by Duke of Leinster on March 23rd, 2021.

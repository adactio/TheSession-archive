Four comments

Mrs. Grant Of Laggan

I got this killer strathspey off the album “Informal Sessions” by the late Cape Breton fiddler Joe Cormier. Some of the tune is similar to the “Miss Lyall” / “Paddy Ryan’s Dream” family of tunes, but I believe this tune is different enough to warrant its own posting. I particularly love the low notes in the second half of each part, and juxtaposed with the beginning of the second part it lends this tune a very rather dramatic feel.

Re: Mrs. Grant Of Laggan

It’s very close to the setting published by Gow in the 4th Repository as “Mrs Grant of Lagan’s Strathspey” (it also says “communicated by Capt. Fraser of Knocky”), leading one to presume that that was Cormier’s source, possibly.

Mrs. Grant Of Laggan, X:2

From Gow’s Repository of the Dance Music of Scotland, Part 4. Gow says that the tune was ‘communicated’ by Capt. Fraser of Knocky (Simon Fraser) and that it may be played slowly.

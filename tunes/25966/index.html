Re: An Gasúr Fiosrach

I’ve only just discovered this lovely tune from the Youtube of ‘Anam Sessions’ recorded June ’26 at Emo Court. See below. The earlier video posted by Rob is equally charming, but the Anam recording is an impressively glossy and polished production. (Note that Sárán is playing an Eb concertina, so its a semitone above the Edor transcription)

I’ve added chords to Ben’s transcription version.
However I’ve used Rob’s bar breaks, as they seem to fit the phrasing & chord changes better.

I also find its easier to play when thinking of the timing as :
/ 1, 2, 3, 4, / 1,2, 1,2, 1, 2, / 1, 2, 3, 4, / 1, 2 , 1, 2, 1, 2 ,

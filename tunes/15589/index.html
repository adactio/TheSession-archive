Re: Hellulaulu Polska

Stumbled upon this on accident, as a Finn (and as a student of Antti’s, whom I have a feeling you got this from), may I present the lyrics I have associated with this tune.

This tune is from Kauhava, and the name we use for it is just “Jos sä olet minun hellunani” (if you were to be mine)

(Also to note, not a waltz per ce, I think Antti called it a polska if I am not mistaken)

:,: Jos sä olet minun hellunani,
tämän kesän, kesän, kesän, kesän :,:
:,: Niin minä laitan sulle, niin minä laitan sulle,
niin kuin linnulle pesän, pesän, pesän, pesän, pesän :,:

:,: Jos sä olet minun hellunani,
tämän syksyn, syksyn, syksyn, syksyn :,:
:,: Niin minä laitan sulle, niin minä laitan sulle,
vaikka silikistä lapsen myssyn, myssyn, myssyn, myssyn :,:

:,: Jos sä olet minun hellunani,
tämän talven, talven, talven, talven; :,:
:,: Niin minä laitan sulle, niin minä laitan sulle,
vaikka hopiasta lapsen sarven, sarven, sarven, sarven :,:

To translate shorty, the singer is telling the recipient that were you to be mine for a certain season, I would offer you the following:
Summer - I will make you a nest like that of a bird’s (doesn’t sound all that poetic and nice in English, but trust me, it is supposed to be nice)
Autumn - I will make you a child’s (knit) cap out of silk (there is a certain way of saying it here which actually would translate to something like “I’ll make it out of silk if I have to” but again, doesn’t really gather the same nuance in English)
Winter - I’ll give you a feeding horn made of silver (again, not particularly poetic or beautiful in English).

Just for anyone who was wondering. Confusing to find this on a site not about Finnish folk music but, eh, why not.

Re: Hellulaulu Polska

this site needs a polska category, this is not the only miscategorised polska or nordic tune on this site, in fact there are piles…

but i also know a fourth verse, for the spring:

:,: Jos sä olet minun hellunani,
tämän kevään, kevään, kevään, kevään; :,:
:,: Niin minä laitan sulle, niin minä laitan sulle,
tällä laululla mielen heljän…

which roughly translates as:
“If you would be my sweetheart this spring, I’ll make you happy by this song”

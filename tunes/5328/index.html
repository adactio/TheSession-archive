A Good Night’s Sleep

This is one of my own compositions. I’ve transcribed it in its original key of 4 sharps. The bright sound of this key reminded me of that ultra-alert feeling you get when you wake up from a long, undisturbed sleep. It also works in Amix and Gmix, although you can’t avoid weird accidentals whichever key you choose. Gmix might work ok on fiddle.

The Battle Of Ballygullen

A very nice march composed by Pat Bass of Annagh near Gorey in Co.Wexford. The title commemorates the last pitched battle fought on County Wexford soil during the 1798 rebellion- at a place called Ballygullen which is between Craanford and Hollyfort in north Wexford. The battle is believed to have taken place on July 4th or possibly 5th 1798.

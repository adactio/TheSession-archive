Slopes of Benbulben

Written by John Regan, and recorded by him on his album of the same name. My transcription is Sharon Shannon’s setting from The Diamond Mountain Sessions. Sharon plays it in F, using C#/D fingering on a B/C box, so I transposed it back to G.

The tune is played way back when by John Regan himself on the ‘old’ Comhaltas archive as a reel, but he then converted it to a hornpipe himself subsequently. Usually played in F as a hornpipe. I’ll upload the hornpipe version in F.

Tsitzik Polkka, X:4

Traditional Finnish Polkka. Turns out this is a 4 parter, with the first two parts in Dmix and the remaining two parts in Emin.

So far I’ve found a few bands which have recorded it. To name a few:
- Ottopasuuna on their self-titled album
- Bouffée d’Airs on their album “Confidences de bal”
- Petri Hakala on an album in either 2006 or 2007.

Bouffée d’Airs recorded it in a set with A Finnish Polka (Ievan’s Polka).

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on February 25th, 2019.

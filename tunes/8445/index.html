The Southwest Wind

This is very similar to Connie the Soldier. However there are some differences. This is the tune (more or less) as played by Todd Denman & Dale Russ and by Veronica McNamara. It is more of a Sligo version, to my ears, than the simpler Connie the Soldier as played by Seamus Creagh, James Carty, and others.

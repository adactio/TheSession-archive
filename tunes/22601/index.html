Trig

This is the second jig on the first set from ‘Box Sets’ the solo album of his own compositions by accordionist John Redmond. The ‘trigs’ referred to in the title are ordnance survey markers that are dotted on Mount Leinster, the 794 metre high mountain near John’s birthplace of Ballindaggin in County Wexford.

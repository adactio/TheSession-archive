Chuck’s Hornpipe

This tune is Chuck’s hornpipe, composed by my wife, Barbara Blakey O’Brien. Chuck was her father and passed after a lengthy battle with cancer in 2021. I recorded the tune on the Feis app earlier this year. The F natural is correct.

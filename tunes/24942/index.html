Two comments

Coffee And Tea (Goodman)

This is one of the versions of Connie the Soldier that appear in the James Goodman collection of manuscripts. This particular setting was published as no. 394 in Tunes of the Munster Pipers vol. 2 and is closer to the Lake Shore jig rather than the “session” Connie the Soldier.

Mick O’Brien, Emer Mayock and Aoife Ní Bhriain recorded it on More Tunes from the Goodman Manuscripts (2021):
https://goodmantunestrio.bandcamp.com/track/coffee-and-tea-o-sullivan-s-fancy
https://thesession.org/recordings/7266

Tune no. 393 in the same volume of Goodman tunes is much closer to the “usual” Connie the Soldier. Compare:

Connie the Soldier: https://thesession.org/tunes/373
The Lake Shore: https://thesession.org/tunes/5768
The Southwest Wind: https://thesession.org/tunes/8445

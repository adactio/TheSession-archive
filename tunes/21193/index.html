Two comments

The Millenium Waltz

For my first submission here’s a waltz I taped off the radio sometime at the end of the 1980s played by a group called Galloping Green. The millenium in question was the Dublin Millenium of 1988 (if memory serves me correctly). Googling the tune and the group yields very little information except that the name of the album was “Galloping Green – Music From The Dublin Millennium And Beyond” and the musicians were Gerard Hayden Guitar, Stephan Schneider Fiddle, Keyboards, Margit Bazilewski Bouzouki, Constance Gardiner Fiddle, Edel McWeeney Fiddle, Tom McDonagh Bouzouki, Joe McKenna Uillean Pipes, Antoinette McKenna Harp, Oliver P. Sweeney Accordion, Carol Nelson Keyboards, Aidan Mcdonnell Bodran, Bones. The waltz track has just box and harp. I can’t find any info on the composer so I’ve attributed it to the group as a whole. I’ve never heard anybody else play it (except myself), and as far as I can tell its not on the site already.

Aidan Quigley’s version of this reel

I heard young Aidan Quigley play a beautiful version of this reel on RTE’s Willie Clancy Week Programme, brought tears to my eyes it did. And why wouldn’t it and him after being influenced by Mrs Crotty! He says he is strongly influenced by the sean nós dancers and absorbs their ‘fuinneamh’. He sure does all right.
I have an ancient Jeffries Concertina which I have begun to talk to in my old age. Can anyone recommend a reliable tuner who won’t make off with the reeds? Not a big job, I’d say, just a few wonky reeds and pads.

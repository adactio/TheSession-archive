We Danced All Night

The middle tune from the medley titled The Little Contortionist from Shane Cook and the Woodchippers’ album, Be Here For a While. I have been listening to my husband practicing the piano part as he’s filling in at a couple of their upcoming shows, and decided I need to learn it too!

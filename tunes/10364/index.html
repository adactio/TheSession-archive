Lunchtime Boredom, X:4

This is how Brian Finnegan plays it, although he plays it in Emix rather than Amix but that goes a bit high.

(Yes, this is Amix, not Edor. The end of the B part does not resolve, it loops to the beginning of the A part, which is Edor. If you did resolve the end, you would end up on A.)

And not sure how others normally play it, but Finnegan’s swing makes it feel more like a step-hop than a reel if you ask me. And I just realised “step-hop” is missing from the choice of rhythms on this site.

↳ Posted by jeff_lindqvist on April 12th, 2016.

Re: The Honesty Bar

The tune was written by McGoldrick after staying in the Ceilidh Place in Ullapool in the Highlands. They have an Honesty Bar in the upstairs lounge where guests leave money according to a list on the wall. There is no barman. It’s all done on trust.

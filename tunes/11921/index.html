Origin of name

On the bath folk fest channel on youtube, upon being asked what the last two tunes were on the 3rd video of the brian finnegan band, the owner of the channel commented that “Brian wrote [the tune] after hearing about the release of Aung San Suu Kyi in Burma” .

Re: The Queen Of Rangoon

Well it’s in B Minor, which has all the same notes as D, except it’s a minor key. This is called the “relative minor.” In short, you can play at on a D whistle with no trouble.

The Queen Of Rangoon, X:3

I found the setting at the top a little hard to read, so have re-transcribed from the same video and a couple other sources.

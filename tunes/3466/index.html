The Black Cock O’ Whickham, X:2

As I remember it from the Boys of the Lough album. To hear it once was to instantly like it.

The scale is a mixture of the heptatonic mixolydian mode (low G’s) and the ionian mode (high g’s).

A good tune to practice the half-hole G# fingering on the flute or whistle -and easier to get than the F natural in the G maj. setting of the tune.

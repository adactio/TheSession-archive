Two comments

The Silver Fox

This is an original (for better or worse).

I was reminded of it a couple weeks ago when a fox came strolling through the yard one night while I was standing out on the porch. Quite brazen, it barely paid me any attention.

As for this tune, it’s probably one part ‘Callaghan’s Polka’ and one part of something I can’t quite seem to remember, leaving it somewhere between a polka and a reel.

Comments / criticisms, etc. would be appreciated. Hope you enjoy!

Five comments

De’ils in the Fish

A reel from Whinham. The ABC is taken from Graham Dixon’s “Book Remember Me” I have included the third and fourth parts as given by Dixon but for myself I find them a little too elaborated and usually stick to the basic tune occasionally using bars1 &2 and 5&6 from parts C &D as variations. More skilful players might enjoy all the twiddly bits

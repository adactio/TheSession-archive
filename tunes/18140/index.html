The Wee Man From Uist

Transcribed from the playing of Alisdair Fraser and Natalie Haas on their 2011 album “Highlander’s Farewell”.

↳ Posted by Ian Varley on July 13th, 2019.

Re: The Wee Man From Uist

I don’t think I’ve come across this tune elsewhere under this title, but it appears to be related to an air called “Moladh Beinn Dobhrain” (In Praise of Ben Dorain) which Aonghas Grant plays; he says it “is formed on the basis of a pibroch” (The Glengarry Collection Vol.1, (Pacific, Missouri, 2010)).

I don’t think Grant has recorded it, but back in 1976, the English fiddler Dave Swarbrick recorded it as “Ben Dorian” on his album “Swarbrick” and published it in his tune book Dave Swarbrick Takes a Bow (London 1978).

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on April 3rd, 2021.

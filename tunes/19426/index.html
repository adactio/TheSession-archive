Mayday

This tune, written by Alistair Anderson, is on his “Dookin’ For Apples” LP from 1979. On this LP Alistair is accompanied by Fennig’s All-Star String Band and features Bill Spence on this track playing the hammered Dulcimer. I have the notation from Alistair in the keys of G and D. I play this tune on the Northumbrian pipes and it sits well in the key of D.

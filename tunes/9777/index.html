The Wrong Medicine

© Composed by Barry Kerr (in the late 1990’s)
The opening tune on his first solo album.
‘Name[d] after a humorous incident’ …

What a beauty! And not too shy!

Transcribed from his CD. How many of youse already play it at sessions?

1st bar of part B is wrong

There’s no triplets and quintoles in this bar (and also in other bars). Barry plays something like this:

/^fBB aBB ^f z|

This is a common grouping scheme 3+3+2.

Re: Hen Ferchetan format.

This is one of the many Welsh tunes which has an AABA format, not AABB. It is *never* played with the B part repeated. It is most commonly played in Wales in Dm (D Dorian, really - B naturals, not flats). The third setting above is the closest to what usually gets played.

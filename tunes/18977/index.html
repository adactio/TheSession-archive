Not very Irish, and not really a slide even tho’ it’s in 12/8, but a good song! The accompanying rhythm is all important.
We play it in our “Piaf Set”, but an octave down on what you’ve got here - more singable in that register. We precede it with “Milord” and then “La Vie en Rose”.

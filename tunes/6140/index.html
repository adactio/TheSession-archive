↳ Posted by Washoo on August 27th, 2006.

I believe James Kelly wrote this tune himself. Check the sleeve notes on the CD to be sure, but as I said I believe he’s the composer. The Advance in the tune name is the home town of a fiddling friend of his in North Carolina.

One comment

The good ship Planet

This is a song melody from O‘Neill’s Music of Ireland, No. 435. O’Neill’s says to play this “cheerfully”, which I find a trick as the tune sounds kind of lonesome to my ears. I think it’s a great tune.

Not to read too much into a title, but it makes me think about Earth being a pretty good ship, navigating by starlight and dead reckoning. Though our little bark sometimes seems rudderless, the crew mutinous, and all the officers idiots. And I think we’re running low on provisions. But other than that…

I should probably play the tune as written a while longer, really get it in my head and fingers and heart, but I couldn’t resist working out my own slight variation. I think I’ve kept the spirit of the tune, just tried to make it sound a little smoother, more regular to my ears:

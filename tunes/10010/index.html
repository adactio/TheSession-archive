“Hynes’ March” ~ wishing I could tell you more…

I haven’t managed to find notes on this one, not at hand, not online, at least not yet. It’s a fun march, and it’s also a kick to dance to as well. I learned it years ago and today decided to revisit it. It’s another one where I furrow my brow and think I’d already posted this here a few years back. But, I couldn’t find it, not anywhere. I did see that someone on Chiff and Fipple requested it in 2001. Sorry I’m late. ;-)

I’ve given it twice just to show a few alternate ways with it, including some from the playing of Tom McHale…

Sligo Goose

Rare tune related to Geese in the Bog. I first heard it from Kevin Burke & The Alt during a concert in Udine in December 2022. Kevin Burke introduced it as a tune named Geese in the Bog and popular in the Sligo area, adding that it is thus also known as the Sligo Goose (no doubt it’s a different tune from the few other more popular tunes named Geese in the Bog). The tune figures as Geese in the Bog in The Coleman Archive Vol. 2: The Home Place (https://thesession.org/recordings/2492) played by fluter Paddy Healy. It has also been recorded by The Alt (https://thesession.org/recordings/5310) and by John and James Carty (https://thesession.org/recordings/7069). In this last album it is named The Sligo Goose.

The transcription is from the playing of Nuala Kennedy and Kevin Burke as played in the above mentioned concert, from a recording I made myself. They played the tune in F# minor, though the original setting (as in the recordings of Paddy Healy and John and James Carty) seems to be in E minor, so I transposed the tune in this key.

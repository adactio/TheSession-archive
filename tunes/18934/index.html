Re: Lord Lovat’s Welcome To Castle Downie

If you want it to appear in 3/2, bdh, then you could change your added L field to L: 1/4 (and put in square brackets for more likelihood of retention).

It does have a Northumbrian 3/2 feel, especially in melodic and harmonic shape. They tend to be played very fast (faster than reel tempo) whereas pipe 3/2s tend to go at reel tempo.

Re: Lord Lovat’s Welcome To Castle Downie

It may have a Northumbrian feel, but Ross was a Great Highland Piper through and through. He was Pipe Major of the Lovat Scouts, which might explain the name of the tune.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Ross_(piper)

↳ Posted by bdh on February 1st, 2020.

Re: Lord Lovat’s Welcome To Castle Downie

This tune appears in the William Ross Collection (first section published in 1869). He was, among other things, PM of the Black Watch in the Crimean War era. Died in 1891. Willie Ross was born in 1878, serving, teaching and composing well into the 20th century. So, two different folks. Great tune and, yes, it does have a timeless borders quality to it.

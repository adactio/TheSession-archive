Tie The Bonnet

Picked this up from a session at the National Folk Festival in Canberra this year. This is the tune that appears on Brian Rooney’s “The Godfather”, and also Lunasa’s “Kinnitty Sessions”. Another tune in O’Neill’s that goes by the same name https://thesession.org/tunes/136 is similar in the A-part but nevertheless a different tune.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on May 17th, 2007.

The B-part has echoes of Dr. Gilbert https://thesession.org/tunes/129, and the A-part uses similar themes to some Donegal settings of Drowsy Maggie https://thesession.org/tunes/27.

Tune origin

Tie the bonnet is an Irish version of the Scotch/border reel “I Had a Lass Till I Ran at Her”, which appears on this site. The tune is likely to have been originally in A and for the pipes. It is in also in this key in O’Neill’s . The title probably means “Hold the Horse Still”, though a double-entendre is possible.

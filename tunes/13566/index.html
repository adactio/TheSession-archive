Leveneep Head C: Frank Jamieson

A Shetland reel composed by Frank Jamieson (1920-1982).
Leveneep (Levaneap) Head is the headland out from where Frank was born, at Sandyburn. It also goes under the name of Hamera Head, and you’ll find it under that name on the OS map.

Gave me a scare

Nice hornpipe! The first few measures gave me quite a scare, however! I composed a hornpipe about 20 years ago that I named Mary O’Maley’s Hornpipe in memory of my grandmother. I’ve never published my hornpipe, I had sent the sheet music to a couple of friends about 15 years ago. Although my hornpipe is in the key of G, the first 5-6 measures sound almost tone for tone exactly the same as this one! My very first thought was that these “friends” had spread my hornpipe around and …. you know what I mean.

I might consider sharing my hornpipe here on Sessions, but I haven’t a clue how to write in abc notation.

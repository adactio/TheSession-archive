The Crow’s Nest (Hop Jig)

This is a hop jig that appears in O’Neill’s Music of Ireland without attribution (at least my 1984 copy does not attribute it to anyone). I have a photocopy of a different source that credits “McFadden,” presumably John McFadden, and that’s what I’ve transcribed here.

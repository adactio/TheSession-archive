Re: The Fairburn Calf

This is pretty much as I have it, which is quite surprising as the takes of Rachel Newton, Mairearad Green, Lauren MacColl and Anna Massie (1st part only) are all slightly different.
My only quibble is that what are notated as slurs, e.g., (C C), should in fact be ties, e.g., C- C (I know, they LOOK the same, but they don’t SOUND the same).
This is a gently paced tune and so, on the PLAY AUDIO, the slider should be set to about half way.

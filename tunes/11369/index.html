Re: Colm’s

Oh dear, please bear with me - had not looked for my account in such a long time (but still play the fiddle :-) .
I had found this tune in a book of a friend from Galway/Cleggan: Mary Lovett, who issued 400 utter standard tunes. Well, except this one. It turned out some time later, that she had composed it in memory of her father.
I apologise for not publishing this information earlier and not doing the research right away. Guess the common name also added to my earlier belief to count it among the standards. No idea what happened to Colm’s No1, though. Maybe Mary can let us know…

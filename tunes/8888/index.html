Modern Quebec tune from Genticorum

This lovely and challenging tune is a “brandy” in 6/4 time written by fiddler Pascal Gemme of the band Genticorum from their new CD “La Bibournoise”. You can hear them play it here:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A7p-nRvS0gQ&feature=related

advance the slider to 2:01 to edit out the pre-tune banter.

Aahh Swiss! Twas the big fella’s shorts and that whippy right wrist wot got me goin’-seriously this band play sublime stuff that strikes me as a glorious development of traditional Quebecois music.

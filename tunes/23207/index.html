A Taste Of NAFCo

A powerful tune by Shane Cook and Jake Channon, named after the North Atlantic Fiddle Convention (NAFCo).

↳ Posted by J. A. Cerro on May 31st, 2023.

Re: A Taste Of NAFCo

Are you sure it’s in 6/4, J. A. Cerro? That should feel like two bars of 3/4 stitched together - in other words, two groups of three beats.
The way you have grouped the notes suggests 3/2 - three groups of two beats.

Ah yes, just listened to it. First part is definitely in 3/2.

A Taste Of NAFCo, X:2

I’ve added the triplets as Shane and Jake play them plus Jake’s chords for the first accompanied pass through the tune.
I hear the second part in 4/4 (2/2 or cut time or whatever) with just the last bar going back to 3/2.

I’ve had their album “Head to Head” for a while - possibly picked it up when incredibly, with Troy MacGillivray, they played at a wee village hall near me a few years back. When they found out the hall had a piano they ditched the electric keys and tuned their instruments to the slightly off concert piano.

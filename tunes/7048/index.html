Paddy’s Green Shamrock Shore

I’ve put this in as an alternative title and it’s come up on several other recordings. It’s not a jig. A waltz time would be more appropriate, and as far as I know, the Corrs are the only ones to call this melody “Erin’s Shore”. Paul Brady’s version takes some beating.

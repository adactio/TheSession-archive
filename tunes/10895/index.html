Re: The Gaelic Revival

Richie Pigott has a copy of a manuscript reportedly from Edward Cronin, where Cronin has identified this as one of his compositions:
https://www.richiepiggott.com/edward-cronin-collection.html
This isn’t surprising as he likely did the same thing with a few tunes (his version of Banish Misfortune, available to hear from the Ward Irish Music Archives is in O’Neill’s as the hornpipe A Faithful Friend)

↳ Posted by Nico on May 14th, 2026.

The Gaelic Revival, X:2

This version is transcribed from the Edward Cronin manuscript in the previous comment, but although it really does look like Cronin wrote only one sharp for the first and third part, I think it would be more correct as two sharps (Amix) for those parts. Ie 1st and 3rd part has G naturals and 2nd and 4th has G#s

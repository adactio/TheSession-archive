Gan Ainm

A cracking tune that I’d love to get the name of. The Gsharp/A chord gives it a real drive. First heard it from a piano accordian playing friend of mine who plays it for dances, having got it off a dance album which helpfully had no liner notes.

Drowsy Dragon, X:2

Really neat tune, thanks for sharing Sean!

Here’s a slightly different setting for melody players. Would be tricky for a whistle player, going from G’s to A#‘s. Alternatively, playing high-e’s instead of A#’s works as well, so|GBG e2e| instead of |GBG ^A2A| … just doesn’t sound nearly as cool. It’d be good to do so behind another instrument playing the A#’s though! And replacing the low C# with a F# works as well for the 3rd bar of the A Part.

On a slightly related note, Clive Williams, composed the C Part for Herbert the Sherbert (https://thesession.org/tunes/14280).

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on January 1st, 2019.

Drowsy Dragon, X:3

A setting with a C Part which was not played by Clive, it derives from the A Part of the Pterodactyl Two-Step (https://thesession.org/tunes/17610). Have also changed the A#’s to e’s in the A Part. The tune seems to stand alone without the C Part, although it’s a nice addition.

Oi U Luzi Chervona Kalyna (Oh, The Red Viburnum In The Meadow)

Oi U Luzi Chervona Kalyna is an old Ukrainian patriotic folk song, first published in 1875. I picked it up from some young Ukrainian refugees after the vigil at Coventry Cathedral for the third anniversary of the Russian invasion of Ukraine.

I’ve copied it from this version on musescore
https://musescore.com/user/56747/scores/7815815

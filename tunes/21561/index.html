Final Flight

This tune appears on Earl Grey in the Morning’s CD: “More Coffee Please” and is part of tune set entitled Final Flight. The set was written in dedication to the very last Space Shuttle (Atlantis) Flight that flew in July of 2011, which I had the honor of working console in the mission control centre in Houston as I had been a Flight Activities Officer (FAO) since 1996.

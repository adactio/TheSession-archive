Two comments

Welcome Christmas

This is from the 1960s animated version of “How the Grinch Stole Christmas.” Alison Brown paired it with “O’Carolan’s Concerto” on her album “Evergreen” (listen to it at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6xPuIPO0Jrs)

↳ Posted by Joe Canner on August 10th, 2025.

Re: O’Carolan’s / Welcome Christmas

Update: I included “O’Carolan’s Concerto” and added the intro and outro from Alison Brown’s recording. Some of my chords for the “Welcome Christmas” section may be wrong; comments welcome. Other tweaks may be necessary to make this more like the recording. Also, I am also still working on my understanding of abc notation: e.g., the 2nd ending should have a repeat at the end and the 3rd ending should extend to the end of the song.

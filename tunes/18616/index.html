Two comments

For Pete’s Sake

This tune was written by longtime member of thesession.org, Will Harmon. Will has written some really great tunes over the years, a few of which have developed a bit of life of their own and get played all over the world. But none of those other tunes were written specifically for me! ;-)

I am posting the tune here, because it has now been recorded on my second album, titled Some Assembly Required. And Will was kind enough to hop in the studio and record that set with me! https://thesession.org/recordings/6687

↳ Posted by Reverend on October 16th, 2019.

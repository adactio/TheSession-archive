As It Is

Lovely reel composed by the Ferns native, Nóra Byrne Kavanagh. Nóra is well known in the south east of Ireland as a wonderful traditional musician, teacher and lecturer in music in Waterford Institute of Technology (now South East Technical University). She was a flute playing member of the great Gorey ceili band of the 80s/90s, and won a senior All-Ireland on piano at the fleadh in 1987. She lives just outside of Waterford city and recently published a history of the Waterford city Comhaltas, ‘Musical memories’, which includes many of her own compositions and tunes composed by some of her many pupils in Waterford. Nóra has kindly provided me with a selection of her tunes for inclusion in The Wexford Tune project database and has been a huge encouragement in bringing the project along. Please note her compositions are copyrighted, so consent would be required for recording or publishing them.

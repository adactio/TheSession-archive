Four comments

Kevin Keegan’s #2

This tune may be heard on Tape 5 at http://www.joecooleytapes.org/. Keegan and Cooley play the waltz, then go into the Orphan jig https://thesession.org/tunes/217; students of theirs still do the same, as the melodies are rather akin. The other Kevin Keegan Waltz has been here for a while: https://thesession.org/tunes/8798 I’ve been working on a setting for that more faithful to what Kevin actually played. Joe and Joanie Madden play both tunes on their Galway Afternoon record, sort of; this tune they call Her Mantle So Green, and the melody of that song is indeed very much like this tune. They also cap off their medley of waltzes with the Orphan.

↳ Posted by Lucy Rietmann on October 21st, 2013.

Kevin Keegan’s (Walt’s Waltz), X:3

This tune is known as ‘Walt’s Waltz’ on Jody Stecher’s 1974 album ‘Snake baked a hoecake’. This setting is as close as I can get to the basic tune, without including the harmonies that Stecher adds. See https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oIaQz7zW6bY

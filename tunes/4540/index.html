Seven comments

Jeremy, I know there’s already another tune called Liz Carrols but this one is a jig that I got off another great album of Clare musicians called “A Clare Conscience” Track 2 second tune. The musicians are Aidan McMahon, Anthony Quigney and Donncha Moynihan and man do they play some beautiful music. A favourite at the moment.

↳ Posted by kiwi on May 28th, 2005.

Princess Nancy

This is on the eponymous Green Linnet Liz Carroll recording from 1988, where the notes read “for my old buddy and former roommate Nancy Leoni. A fiddler herself, Nancy’s birthday is the social event of her calendar year, when she takes to calling herself Princess and donning a tiara” a great tune and a fine CD indeed.

↳ Posted by Thom Pratt on May 28th, 2005.

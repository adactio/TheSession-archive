Two comments

The Stockport

A tune that I heard played during a pub session at the Priddy Folk Festival (Somerset, England) yesterday.

I assume from its title and style that the tune is of English origin.

My transcription is in 2/4 metre, as that’s the only way of submitting a polka to this site. However, I suspect that it’s one of those English polkas that would normally be written out as 4/4 (or possibly 2/2).

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on July 15th, 2013.

Re: The Stockport Polka or Rub-a-Dub or Stop the Cavalry

Also played as Rub-A- Dub in East Anglia throughout the first 80 years of the 20th Century. Then a musician called Jona Lewie put some words to it to make it an anti war song about soldiers throughout the ages and which included the line “whish I could be home for Christmas” and thereafter under the name ‘Stop the Cavalry’ it has been the least irritating of all Christmas pop sings.

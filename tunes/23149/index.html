The Yola

A nice session-friendly reel composed by the multi-instrumentalist Mick Martin from Wexford. Mick is the piper and low whistle player on the well-known group, ‘The Kilkennys’. I’ve just recently been sent a nice selection of compositions of Mick’s as part of my Wexford Tune Collection project. thanks to piper Padraig Sinnott from Boolavogue for getting me Mick’s tunes. The title refers to the unique language of the people of the two south eastern baronies of County Wexford. Although the language died out some two hundred years ago, significant remnants of it are left in the everyday Hiberno-English of the locals in those areas. The original language was a dialect of middle English, close to the English of Chaucer.

Skye Tune

An unnamed trad tune from Skye, transcribed from Sam Mabbett’s playing on the Canny Band’s 2022 self titled album.

↳ Posted by Bruce Lambelle-Rudd on May 11th, 2022.

Re: Skye Tune

It’s one from the 1784 Partick McDonald Collection. One of a group of Skye tunes he calls Skye Dance. Recorded by Alasdair Fraser. - Usually in A

↳ Posted by bogman on May 11th, 2022.

Skye Dance, X:3

This is a take on Skye tune 2 (X:2)
The 1st part of this version begins one quaver earlier than in X:2 (an interesting compositional method using the natural swinging potential of reels and jigs).
You can treat the second part in the same way but the result is less satisfying; so I d.i.y.ed the turn. X:2’s turn can be used instead.
For added fun, one can alternate between X:2 and X:3 when playing the tune to X:2’s turn.
A good tune deserves another…

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on February 24th, 2025.

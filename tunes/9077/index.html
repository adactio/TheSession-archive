Five comments

Rabbit

A week or so ago I posted this discussion: https://thesession.org/discussions/19650
Five tunes I didn’t know the names of - I eventually got all the names (much quicker than I expected, in fact), and actually, all but one of them were already here. The one that wasn’t is this one.

This is The Rabbit, a tune by Kevin O’Neill. The only video I managed to find of it was this one, which, to be honest, I don’t particularly like: http://uk.youtube.com/watch?v=keoHfqh36T0 They start playing the tune properly at about 1:34.

I picked this tune up from a session at Folkworks Summer School ’08. I did actually take a video, but I didn’t put it on Youtube cos I didn’t want to put a load of people up on the Internet for everyone to see without their consent.

Enjoy!

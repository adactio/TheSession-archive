Back In Time

A beautuful waltz composed by the Irish, Grace Kelly from Manchester,
1993 All Ireland Fleadh Champion Tin Whistle.
Uploaded with her approval.
It’s the first tune in the video, from Angelina Carberry’s Gradam Ceoil TG4 award concert.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ayYukp2_DKU&ab_channel=TradTG4

↳ Posted by ceili on November 4th, 2021.

Re: Back In Time

If you have problems with your fingers (stringed instruments mostly)
second part, bar eight, you can instead of | a - d’ - b| simply play | a2 - b |
it works fine! Rather do that, than not play the lovely tune - I do ;-)

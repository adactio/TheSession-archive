Posting original tunes

First, I sincerely apologize for violating the rules of this website - it was not intentional, I assure you. I just assumed that the purpose was to share tunes

Secondly, please post the rules in a way that is much more obvious than in FAQs. It never occurred to me to read the FAQ …

I don’t mean to antagonize - its a great site and I really appreciate your efforts.

↳ Posted by Joe Healey on July 25th, 2010.

It is standard courtesy to review the FAQs when you sign on to any organization, that’s ‘obvious’. That’s general practice most places.

Yes, it is about ‘sharing the tradition’, but possibly not so much putting oneself to the fore as the actual tradition. But ‘new’ tunes are welcome here, but, as you’ve read, to strike a balance and hold off the onslaught of the huge number of self-composed wonders floating out there in the ether ~ there are other expectations too.

The hoped for considerataion is that newcomers would settle in and check out the norm, and then, possibly later in the game, share some of what they have to share. I for one enjoy trying even the daftest creations, if once through is often enough…

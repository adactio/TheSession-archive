Re: Paddy Dunne’s

Hi Kenny.
The tune is written by Niamh Diane and is called Paddy Dunne’s after her grandfather.
It’s a common feis hornpipe, I am told it was in a Damien McKee feis album. I heard it from the playing of Liam O’Sullivan.

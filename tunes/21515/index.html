A Night To Remember

From the playing of Seán Gavin on his 2021 album “The Fisherman’s Island”, track #2.2.

From the sleeve notes:
The second tune is one I heard from the playing of Clare fiddle player Sheila Garry; it was composed by the late Co. Clare flute player Paddy O’Donoghue.

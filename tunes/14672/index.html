David Glen’s, X:4

This may be the original setting as it appears in David Glen’s Collection of Highland Bagpipe Music, published in seventeen volumes between 1876 and 1900. The tune is attributed to a W Sutherland, presumably Pipe Major William Sutherland of Airdrie, born 1844 and died 1903.

In my opinion the prominence of the low Gs in the first, third and fifth bars of this setting, when played against A drones, makes it more interesting than the other settings.

↳ Posted by bagpie on October 8th, 2025.

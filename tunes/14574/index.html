The East Clare

Posted in response to malarz’s request here: https://thesession.org/discussions/47708. I hope this is the tune s/he is after.

Transcribed from the playing of Martin Hayes and Kevin Crawford here (this tune starts at 2:35, after the Clare Reel): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=utFGLZeLKlE.

They play it ABB, in F (My hunch is that Kevin is playing it on his C flute). For D whistles and flutes, it would work well in D:
K: D
DE|F2 FE FAAF|E2 ED EFGE|F2 FE FAAF|EDEF D3E|
F2 FE FAAF|E2 ED EFGE|F2 FE FAAF|EDEF D3E||
|:F~A3 B2 AF|DFAF EFDE|F~A3 B2 AF|EDEF D3E|
F~A3 F~A3|FAAF GEFD|DEFD EFGE|AFEF D3E:||

And G is also session friendly:
K: G
GA|B2 BA BddB|A2 AG ABcA|B2 BA BddB|AGAB G3A|
B2 BA BddB|A2 AG ABcA|B2 BA BddB|AGAB ~G3A||
|:B~d3 e2 dB|GBdB ABGA|B~d3 e2 dB|AGAB ~G3A|
B~d3 B~d3|BddB cABG|GABG ABcA|dBAB ~G3A:||

I searched far and wide and in various keys in hopes of avoiding posting this only for someone to i.d. it as a duplication, but nothing matched. It likely has other names, “East Clare Reel” being fairly generic.

Three comments

An Feirin

This is another great reel composed by American uilleann piper Jerry O’Sullivan. An Feirin means The Gift in Irish, and this was the title of his last CD. It is a fantastic tune for pipers, I had requested the tune, but thought I would not be so lazy and learn it from his CD. Enjoy.

↳ Posted by JACKB on July 3rd, 2007.

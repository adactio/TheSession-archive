Ómós Sheamuis

Nice tune of new Flook’s Ancora. Played with Quickenbeam. I think Brian Finnegan play this one with Eb flute, and Tin Whistle Eb for the Quickenbeam.

I’m not sure about the type (reel, hornpipe…) but I think it’s a slow reel.

My first time adding a X1 song here.

I hope you enjoy!

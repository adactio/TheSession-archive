One comment

Young Joe

Here’s a tune from the splendid album “The Open Door”, played there as a tin whistle duet by Gerry O’Donnell and Joe McKenna (backed up by Antoinette McKenna’s harp and Joe McHugh’s bouzouki).

This is the basic melody only -- transcribing what was played on the second whistle was beyond me, though it’d be interesting to see it. Some (not all) simple variations:
* The second half of the fourth measure of the A part could be BGGG or BGDG.
* The second half of the eighth measure of the A part could be BdAF or BdAd; or the entire eighth measure could be decA AGGG.
* The second half of the third measure of the B part could be cAAG.
* The first half of the fifth measure of the B part could be Bdgb.

Note: I’ve transcribed this tune as if played on a D whistle, but it seems to be a high F whistle on the recording.

Bill at Cape Irish came up with a different transcription from the same recording -- see it at https://musescore.com/sisherbal/young-joe-reel

The Duck (Liz Carroll’s)

A jig composed by Liz Carroll. It appears on the CD “On the Offbeat”,
but I transcribed it from Pauline Conneely’s banjo playing on her recording “All Because”,
where it is followed by another Liz Carroll tune “Lacey’s Jig”.

Lacey’s Jig can be found in Liz Carroll’s tune book, but I didn’t find “The Duck” in there.
So this is what I heard Pauline playing (at least on one of the repeats).

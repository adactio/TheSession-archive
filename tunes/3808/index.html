The Land Of Sunshine, X:3

Same as X2 but transposed to G Major and removing the two accidentals in the second part.

I think this would be the best key to play in a session. It is playable with the tin whistle without having to do changes of octave.

Interesting those accidentals, specially the first one that is in the tab published in the website of the composer. In my opinion it is possible not to play it and the tune still sounds great. I am a tremolo diatonic harmonica player and that is why I am specially interested in not playing that accidental. I suppose the same goes for melodeon players.

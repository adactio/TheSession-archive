Denis Murphy’s Untitled Reel (Monaghan Twig version in minor?) (S: Denis Murphy)

This tune is great fun to play and not that difficult to learn after all, one phrase naturally flowing into the other. However, same as with tune # 19310, this untitled reel played by Denis Murphy is a tough nut to crack when it comes to transcribing it – all because Denis uses a variety of ‘c’s which at times are too sharp to be c naturals and too flat to be c sharps. I see even more of that with Junior Crehan’s and Bobby Casey’s tunes and the easy way out has often been for the player to interpret them as straight dorian tunes with no accidentals (in this case, it would be all c naturals). But that way, these tunes sadly lose a lot of their character (think Farewell to Miltown Malbay played as a natural minor tune… it kinda works but certainly not in the way that its author intended?)

Long story short, here’s the version I settled on after listening to Denis’s playing a bunch – and you can also listen to Bryan O’Leary and Colm Guilfoyle for another take on that tune!

This reel was collected from Denis by Breandán Breathnach in 1966 and there’re no clues about its origin or titles that I could find (in archives or in Sliabh Luachra). However, a few simple experiments pointed me to a possible relative – I think it develops and ends much like the Monaghan Twig / Tinker’s Apron, only largely in minor! The Monaghan Twig appears to be a northern tune but I wouldn’t be surprised if Denis or even Padraig O’Keeffe made their own version out of it. It’s that – or I could be completely wrong and it’s a separate epic mystery tune!

(Of course, it could also be the 999th Scottish convert that came out of Sliabh Luachra – you can certainly reverse-engineer a strathspey out of it :-D)

O’Keeffe’s Mother

This tune comes from Bohola’s “4” album, preceded by
Callipygian https://thesession.org/tunes/7053 and
Hoban’s On 63rd https://thesession.org/tunes/8002

The tune is almost entirely pentatonic, and has echoes of a few other tunes. Most closely related, however, is
James McMahon’s https://thesession.org/tunes/6138
in which the A part is practically identical, but the B part is quite different.

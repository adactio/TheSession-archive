Trip To Jasper

Written by Khelton Headley-Wolf

I wrote this tune in 2017 after a trip to Jasper the previous spring. The mountain drives have always been my favourite. I accidentally wrote this tune when I was practicing my E Flat scales, and it immediately reminded me of this specific trip to Jasper.

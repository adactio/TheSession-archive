Six comments

I transcribed this tune from Bridgetown’s CD “East of the Tracks.” My apologies to Dave Cory if I didn’t get it just right. My search for the ABCs (so I didn’t have to do the work) netted only some old-timey recordings, so this particular version is a more “Irish lilt” transcription. It’s also a banjo tune, hence the repetitious lower register notes. Adding triplets and some variations make this a very cool, driving tune.

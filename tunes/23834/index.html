Blackrock Wall

Requested by cquick. “Blackrock Wall” is a reel composed by Sarah Markey. The tune is “…named after a place that will always be very special to me. Every summer growing up we would go over to Ireland to visit family in Dundalk and the tradition would be to take our photo on the wall at Blackrock beach, just like my mum used to do with my granny…” I think the original might be in Eb, but I’ve put it in D. It should be enough to get you started, but listen to Sarah playing it.

https://youtu.be/U2zz4lMmm6k?si=es0LH6sG7Ctd0o6w

Re: Blackrock Wall

It sounds pretty accurate to me, cquick, if you actually mean the tune “Blackrock Wall”.

On the recording, the tune “Blackrock Wall” is the first reel after the waltz “The Beautiful Goldfinch” (https://thesession.org/tunes/5684). The track ends with the reel “The lady on the Island” (https://thesession.org/tunes/938).

Confusion can arise when a track, made up of several tunes, is given a title that suggests just one. So the track “Blackrock Wall” does not mean that that’s the title of the first tune (it’s actually the second). See here: https://thesession.org/recordings/7534 (track 8).

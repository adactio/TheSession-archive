Paresis

This is another weirdly-titled tune from “Harding’s 200”. It sounds to me very close to a number of other tunes (e.g. “Eileen Curran”) so there’s doubtless a “real” name for it out there somewhere!

↳ Posted by Zouki on September 10th, 2013.

This tune is often played right after Tullochgorum (with the variations) down in Cape Breton, and seems to be quite well known in Cape Breton.

↳ Posted by Tate on September 15th, 2013.

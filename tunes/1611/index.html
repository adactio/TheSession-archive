Scallowa Lasses

This tune was requested. It’s a traditional Shetland reel, a very simple tune, with an unusual “turn” into the second part. Like a lot of older Shetland tunes, it seems to go well on flute or whistle.
“Boys Of The Lough” recorded it on their second album.

↳ Posted by Kenny on April 23rd, 2003.

Da Scallowa Lasses, X:5

I came across this setting while listening to a cassette uploaded to youtube for learning Shetland tunes. It features Tom Anderson on the fiddle and Violet Tulloch on the piano. It’s worth listening - he tells a story about each tune in the Shetland dialect. This setting is found at this point in the youtube video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0D7W1tvmKrg&t=2610s

I’ve also found the original book that goes with the cassette from 1979 published by Stirling university in 1979:
https://ia801005.us.archive.org/18/items/haand-me-doon-da-fiddle/Haand%20me%20doon%20da%20fiddle_text.pdf
I’ve altered a few notes so it matches better with the recording, though.

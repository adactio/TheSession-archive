A lively Galician tune that I first heard off Milladoiro’s album Castellum Honesti. Spent forever searching the internet for sheet music, but I could only find transcriptions of the B and C parts, oddly.

Muiñeira De Vilanova, X:2

This tab is closer to the tune played by Milladoiro in their cd castellum honesti. They possibly added the first part to the traditional Muiñeira de Vilanova, because if you search by that name in YouTube you will see all the pipers not playing it. But I absolutely love that part!

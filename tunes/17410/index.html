Re: Summer Salsa

Yeah it was a bit challenging putting the notes to sheet music (i.e., “are there really 5 bars in the B Part??”), but I think it’s fairly well represented here. Had the notes themselves, and practised playing along to their recording, so had a sense of the timing.

It’s mostly all guitar picking, and when the fiddler (Mark Crickard) does come in, he improvises around the melody. You can listen to it on their website, CDBaby, iTunes, probably Spotify, and YouTube:
https://www.jon-sanders.com/audio/a/salsa_summer

“Music from Ireland and other Atlantic Shores by fiddler Mark Crickard and guitarist Jon Sanders features music and song from Brazil, Ireland, America, Scotland and Portugal. The title track [Salsa Summer] gets [its’] name from an original composition by Jon Sanders.”

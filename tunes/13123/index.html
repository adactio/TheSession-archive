Three comments

“A Memory Of Friends Past” ~ C: c ~ from 6/8 to 3/4

I received a lovely email from one of our members saying he liked my tune, but that he was going to take it forward a little slower than a jig, as a waltz. So, I thought I’d better, just to make it 32 bars instead of the more cumbersome 64 it could potentially become. I’ve given it in two octaves, with some variations thrown in that are more along the lines of taking it in 3/4 time. Here’s the 6/8 version:

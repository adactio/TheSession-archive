Three comments

Swans at Coole

Ed Reavey has a reel ‘Swans at Coole’ : this is the air of a tune with the same name associated with Maire Bhreathnach. Perhaps she wrote it - I don’t know but it’s a great air with a lovely sweep downwards in the first part. This is a bit of an international collaboration with FidHealer as she too transcribed the tune and we agree that this is broadly along the right lines, though as with any air, you would need to obtain a recording of someone playing it to get the minor nuances that make all the differences. It’s on Maire B.‘s album, ’Angels Candles’.

↳ Posted by Kilcash on July 19th, 2005.

Re: Swans At Coole

Maire’s comment from the CD sleeve:
“Coole, Co. Sligo, evoked the memory of the children of Lir, who are turned into swans by a jealous stepmother.”
Played on Angels Candles by Maire Breatnach, fiddle & keyboards and Cormac Breatnach, flute. A moody and yearning air in slow march tempo.

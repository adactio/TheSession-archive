Re: The Blue Reel

The Blue Reel was written on a pentatonic scale (as opposed to diatonic) with only five different notes. For example, any scale using only the black piano keys. These scales are characteristic of oriental music. Old Irish music sometimes uses a pentatonic or gapped scale in which two of the notes in the diatonic scale are omitted

Blue Reel

Yes it was composed by the late Oliver Schroer. Here is the original version with his band The Stewed Tomatoes and Tanya Tagaq on vocals.
https://borealisrecords.bandcamp.com/track/the-blue-reel

and here is a teaching video by the NSCE:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zoaBoETw8zI

The video shows nicely some of the features of Schroers playing style:
- sliding unison double stops,
- continous eightnote strikes with ghost notes, similar to southamerican clave shuffle.

Interestingly, this tune dos not seem to have countervoices, which are quite typical for some of Schroer’s compositions. DOes anybody know a countermelody of this tune?

One comment

Mulloch Cairn

Another tune by Charlie Sherrit, this is transcribed from the playing of accordionist Jim Johnstone, from his 1986 album ‘Government Warning’, where he plays it for a Canadian Barn Dance. It is composed in the style of a 2/4 pipe march. Mulloch Cairn is a historical site in Aberdeenshire on the summit of Mullock Hill.

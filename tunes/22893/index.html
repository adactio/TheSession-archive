Gaelic Waltz #1

I was going through my band recordings and found this tune, which belongs to a set of 2. We first played these publicly in Boston 2010. I don’t know what they’re actually called, but they’re so familiar, probably because I’ve played them or thought about them a hundred times over the years. I’ve added the chords for Piano (or any continuo), and the chords also help with harmonies when lead instruments trade off - in our case Fiddle, Flute, and Accordion. There is also a Gaelic Waltz #2 that follows this one.

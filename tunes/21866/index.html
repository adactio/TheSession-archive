Five comments

East Neuk O Fife

This is an 18th-century Scottish Reel. I decided to add this as a new tune, since the only existing version on the site has it wrongly as hornpipe; more confusingly, the notes given for it are actually three separate tunes, in which the original is followed by the Maid Behind the Bar and the High Level Hornpipe, although the last is unplayable on most instruments as written.

The first two parts are well established. The third part comes from the playing of the fiddlers Tommy Hughes and Wattie Robson, who I often heard playing in the Scottish Borders in the 1970s and 1980s.

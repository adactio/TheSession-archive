Mrs. Major L. Stewart Of The Island Of Java

A Scottish, Slow Strathspey in A Major. Composed by William Marshall (1748-1833). Marshall is most famous for his many fine strathspeys. The ‘Mrs.’ of the title was Margaret Fraser of Newton near Beauly, who married Ludovick Stewart of Pittyvaich. Stewart was a Major in the 24th Regiment of Foot, serving in India and the Island of Java, before returning home.

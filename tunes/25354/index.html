Hommage A Philippe Bruneu

A quebecois tune that I keep hearing. I think it might have been recorded on an Andy Cutting / Chris Wood album, but this is a (close enough) transcription of Raz-de-Maree (Tidal Wave)’s album of Contra Music of Quebec. I’ve regularly played it with Levis Beaulieu: https://thesession.org/tunes/10249

Not to be confused by the waltz with the same name!

Glen McCabe’s Reel

By guitarist and pianist Paul M. MacDonald (SOCAN), from the playing of Jerry Holland, who published it his tune collection. From the notes on the tune: “[Paul MacDonald] co-produced seven of Jerry’s recordings. His audio work has specialized to include digital editing and restoration, the Rounder [Records] Bill Lamey compilation being an example.”

The published version of this tune has it in cut time, as a 2/4 reel.

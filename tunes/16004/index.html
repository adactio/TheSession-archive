Eight comments

Fort Dunree

This is a tune I heard played on the Uilleann Pipes by a great piper named Michael Cooney. He learned the tune from the album The Return of Spring by Buttons and Bows. The tune was composed by the fiddle player Manus McGuire. I transcribed the tune from the recording as best I can. As it did not really fall into any category I was not sure what type of music it was best called. If anyone can improve on the notation, please feel free to do so. It is a lovely tune indeed

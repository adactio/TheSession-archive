Re: Reinlender Efter Hilmar Alexanderson

Is the first bar written correctly? If the first two quavers were introductory (pick-up) notes, the first beat of the tune would be on the A crotchet, which would make the dotted D minim in bar 8 fall on the beat. Bar 8 would be a first-time bar leading back to the G & A pick-up notes. The second-time bar would include the following G & B quavers, making them the pick-up to the B part rather than supernumerary, as written.

Twelve comments

The Argosy

Just a tune I had knocking around in my head, retrospectively named in honour of the Argosy Bar in Glasgow, a great place to watch the match with your best friend Mr Pint.

↳ Posted by Conán McDonnell on August 27th, 2002.

Hey, stellar, Con

↳ Posted by Zina Lee on August 27th, 2002.

Yep, this is a fun tune all around, tho a bit of a bow flinger for us fiddlers. Lots of opportunities for variations, and nice segues from one mode to the next.

The Argosy

Hey Conan I love your tune. I’ve added it to my repertoire, and I think it sounds nice played after Liz Carroll’s “That’s Right Too”. I also liked Will’s variations. I added a couple of variations of my own to bars 3 and 4 of the B-part. I love the way the end of the B-part leads back to the beginning of the tune. If you transcribe it in E dorian you don’t need to keep writing in C#s.

The Banks Of The Suir, X:4

No. 802 from The Complete Collection of Irish Music by Petrie/Stanford. Nos. 801 and 803 are alternative settings.

I came across this tune as the second movement of Paul Clark’s Irish Suite for recorder quintet, together with The Goroum and Cucanandy.

Clark does not mention his sources in the printed score, but I’m almost certain he took all three tunes from the Petrie collection, since the titles and melodic variations match perfectly.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h2njew0R9yw&t=90s

The Banks Of The Suir, X:5

This air appears in the 2024 ITMA publication “The Pigot Collection” as #358. It is cited there as also being part of manuscript 14,099 in the National Library of Ireland collection. Pigot collected works in the mid-1800s but cited no source for this tune. The original Pigot transcription is in Amin. I transposed it to Bmin for my own convenience as a whistler. It is (IMO) another good example of how tunes evolve over the years.

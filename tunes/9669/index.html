Five comments

John Cheap

I’ve known this tune for a number of years and called it John Cheap. All I could find here was a reference to “John Cheap The Chapman” under the comments for:

The West Mabou Reel
https://thesession.org/tunes/7980

A search of the net found it here as:
John Cheap, the Chapman
http://www.ibiblio.org/fiddlers/JOHAN_JOHN.htm

It’s in Gm there, but sounds about the same so I logged under
the name John Cheap the Chapman.

There’s also references on the net to a book:
John Cheap
The Chapman’s Library:
The Scottish Chap Literature Of Last Century,Classified, With Life Of Dougal Graham, Comic And Humorous (1877)

So it seems that Chap Literature refers to books which were sold by traveling pedlar’s who were referred to as Chapman.
I assume there’s some relationship between the tune and this book.

↳ Posted by zoronic on June 14th, 2009.

Re: Coire Mhic Fhearchair

It’s a geographic feature (on Beinn Eighe in Wester Ross), so I guess it’s not hugely surprising that there might be two tunes with the same name. There are five or six tunes called “Fingal’s Cave!” I have since found out that this “Coire Mhic Fhearchair” was composed by Ewen Henderson, and what a beautiful tune it is (and well transcribed, Neil).

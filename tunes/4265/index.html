Paddy’s #2

The second of two reels played, and possibly composed, by Paddy Donoghue, or O’Donoghue, former flute player with the Tulla Ceili Band, and perfoformed by him on RTE’s “Ceili House” programme a few weeks ago. Paddy apparently has a book of his tunes out, and if these 2 reels are representative of the quality of his compositions, it’s obviously a book worth getting hold of. If they are indeed Paddy’s tunes, I’d be grateful if someone could give me the correct names for them, and I’ll change the titles.

Hi,

I just got a copy of the book from Comhaltas HQ. It’s a very nicely produced, small format book with 35 tunes of various types. You can find another one of his tunes on claremusic.tripod.com which is well worth checking out if you don’t know it already.

Anyway, this tune is called the Trip to Peterswell and the other one you posted recently is Aine’s Invitation, named for broadcaster Aine Hensey.

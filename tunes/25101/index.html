Denise’s Waltz by Timmy O’Connor of Toureendarby

This beautiful waltz was composed by Timmy O’Connor of Toureendarby, Co. Cork (1935-2024). Timmy was recently sent off to the sounds of The Cascade Polka, another one of his compositions, as friends of Sliabh Luachra around the world mourned the passing of this gentle giant of traditional music. Known by many as Timmy Connors, he led local sessions in Scully’s Bar, Newmarket for nearly half a century and his radiant wide smile will be sorely missed!

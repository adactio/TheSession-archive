Two comments

Fenella

A great wee march, or “marching air” as Paul Cranford calls it, that we’ve started playing at my local session, after one of our number heard Anna Massie play it here:
https://www.facebook.com/reel/571277106017759

I’ve not transcribed it exactly as Paul Cranford has it, or Anna played it, or exactly as I play it, especially in the second part where there is scope for snaps or triplet runs up to the high As, but I’ve tried to give a middle ground or starting point.
Last time through the tune you end with the second time ending of the first part (i.e., up high).

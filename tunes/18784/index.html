Re: Fjellvåk

North-American birders would call the bird a “rough-legged hawk”. The term “buzzard” is sorta discouraged because it’s ambiguous. In some dialects of English, it means vulture, while in others it means hawk. Also, “buzzard” is widely considered somewhat derogatory, while “hawk” isn’t, and just refers to a class of birds.

Anyway, it’s a great tune.

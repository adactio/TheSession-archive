The Cat And The Dog

This is a great slip jig from Benbecula. The first part bears a lot of similarities to Ryan’s Jig. I picked it up from this youtube played by Angus Nicholson and Coll MacDonald, both from here in Skye, on pipes and fiddle, and Will Lamb on bouzouki. It’s the second tune in the set.

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FGrVuAT60gU

Planxty Robert Jordan

A lovely tune from the blind Harpist Turloch Carolan.

↳ Posted by JACKB on July 23rd, 2007.

Planxty Robert Jordan

A beautiful rendering of this tune was featured in the PBS Special “Harpestry” which was filmed in Ireland. I believe the harper who performed it was Marie Ni Chathsaigh. (Not sure if that name is spelled right).

↳ Posted by harpalaska on July 25th, 2007.

Planxty Robert Jordan, X:3

Based on a Youtube clip called “Robert Jordan 717” played live at Mass Street Music by Scott Tichenor (mandolin) and Bill Crahan (guitar). Beautiful counter point between guitar and mandolin.

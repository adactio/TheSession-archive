Ingo’s Horny Pipe

Ingo Wettstein came up with the idea for this tune in a feverish summer session while sitting at the harbour thinking about “The Rights of Man”. Jakob Brunner then turned it into this version while playing it at the Greifswald irish session.

There may be a few more version evolving as we go but hopefully you all enjoy it as much as we do.

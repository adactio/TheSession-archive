Cambridge Caravan Catastrophy Part 1&2

These cracking tunes composed by Bruce MacGregor were learnt from his album “101 Reasons to Do Nothing”.
The Part 1 consists of the first four sections, and Part 2 consists of the last 3.
They was supposedly written after an “incident” at the Cambridge Folk Festival involving the Blazin’ Fiddles backstage caravan and a wildly uncontrollable party!

John Sandall

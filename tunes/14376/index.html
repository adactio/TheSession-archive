Four comments

Ratha Fair

Scottish reel from Gow’s second collection; I straightened and simplified the ABC, and added chords from the bassline. Jack Campin has transcribed the Gow version faithfully here: http://www.campin.me.uk/Embro/Webrelease/Embro/08play/abc/Ratha.abc

It’s the middle tune in this medley: https://vimeo.com/44452815

↳ Posted by benhockenberry on April 21st, 2015.

Ratha Fair, X:2

To make the tune sit better on tenor banjo or octave mandolin, the A part can be taken down an octave.

↳ Posted by benhockenberry on April 22nd, 2015.

Re: Ratha Fair

I changed it to D Dorian since the only Bs are naturals… and there’s a Dorian feel with the Dm to C harmonies.

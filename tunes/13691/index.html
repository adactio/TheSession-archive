Two comments

Ode à Andrea Et Phil

Another great wedding tune from Colin Grant. It’s on his CD “Fun for the Whole Family”.
Accompanying chords are:
A part:
|D|A|D|E|
|D|A/C#|Bm7|E|
|D|A|C#m|E F#m|
|D|E|A D/A|A|
1st B part:
|E|F#m|D|A E/G#|
|E|A|D|D|
|Bm7|A/C#|E|F#m|
|D|E|A D/A|A|
2nd B part:
|E|F#m|D|A E/G#|
|E|A/C#|D|D|
|G|F#m|E|A|
|D|E|A D/A|A|

↳ Posted by DonaldK on June 25th, 2014.

Fintan Cleary’s

A nice hornpipe composed by the Co. Wexford pianist and under-age Fleadh champion piano accordionist Fintan Cleary some years ago. Notated from the playing of the Wexford town based button accordion player , Tom Byrne, who learned it from Fintan.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on July 28th, 2022.

Re: Jack And Polly’s

I’ve just updated the title of the hornpipe. Unfortunately Fintan Cleary passed away recently after a short illness. His composition was played at his funeral mass and that’s how I came to know that he had composed it in 2006 and named it for his parents. I’ve just recently received the original notation which I’ll edit in.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on May 30th, 2023.

Re: Jack And Polly’s

I’ve now substituted the original composition from Fintan Cleary in place of the setting that I notated from Tom Byrne. I’ll upload Tom’s version as a second setting, so it’s still here and also because Tom’s setting had an alternative ending for the repeat of the second part. It may have been Tom’s addition as he was playing the tune - as Fintan Cleary’s Hornpipe - from memory.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on May 31st, 2023.

Jack And Polly’s, X:2

Juliana

A really sweet little tune from the Rook Manuscript dating from the 19th century. Played slow I think this would be really lovely in a session (maybe transposed down to get rid of the 3rd position though!).

Can be found here (second to last tune on page 24): https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~rja14/musicfiles/rook/rook_pages/024.htm

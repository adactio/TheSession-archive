Two comments

Fiona MacLeod by Donald Macleod

Bagpipe notation, just a cracking reel from the old master

↳ Posted by jakethepeg on April 9th, 2013.

Fiona MacLeod, X:3

I’ve been playing this on the Highland pipes recently and I think it’s critical to the tune, and necessary in order to do justice to the genius of Donald MacLeod, to play the upper mordents or pralltrillers on C# in the 3rd part.

They’re the “oh that sounds cool!” moment in the tune and there’s no reason not to do them on flute, fiddle, accordion, etc.

And, by the way, the tune is in the key of B minor, and incorrectly listing it as being in the key of A Major can prevent people from finding what they’re looking for when doing searches.

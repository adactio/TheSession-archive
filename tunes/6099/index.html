It took a bit of digging, but I found the following on
https://www.nigelgatherer.com/tunes/tunes/zWorld/MNO/NAlco.html :

This is a recent composition from Asturias, a Principality in the North West of Spain which is enjoying a revival in its traditonal music. It was composed by Javier Tejedor, one of three brothers in the group Tejedor. The title means “In Remembrance”

Hope that helps.

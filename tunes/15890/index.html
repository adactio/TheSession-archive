Two comments

The Concrete Cow

A tune written about a concrete sculpture depicting cows in Milton Keynes, England. Likely written by someone who was travelling through there.

Recording here:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UFjSTp2qAfQ

↳ Posted by Gus Barbary on February 18th, 2017.

The Concrete Cow, X:2

Hi, I’m Phil Berthoud and I wrote this tune in the early 90s. I lived for many years in Milton Keynes - home of the famous concrete cows - an attempt by the local council to introduce a rural feel to the new city. One of the heads was knocked of a cow soon after they were unveiled, lending an even more surreal atmosphere to the already bizarre idea. I think E is a very strong key for tunes. This one’s in E mixolydian, which is the same key signature as A major, so not too difficult to play.

↳ Posted by Philip Berthoud on October 5th, 2024.

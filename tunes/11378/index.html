Naming of this tune

I named this tune in honor of a good buddy, Stefan Reis, who is the only German sailor that I know. He got interested in sailing through me in San Diego, and has now become a voyager exploring, sail,surfing the Pacific coast as far south as Panama. Hele on, Amigo!

Re: Billy Rushe’s Own

CD notes to Track #1 from “Ceol Sidhe” :
“This first jig came from Scotland early in 1940, I learned it from my mother but had difficulty in pairing it with one of our own double jigs because of the style, and so eventually rather than discard it, I composed one that I thought suited the rhythm of ”Billy Rushe’s".
So nothing at all to do with Charlie Lennon.

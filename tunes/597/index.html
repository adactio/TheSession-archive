The Bright Hollow Fog (strathspey?)

Chicago box and concertina player John Williams recorded this tune as an astonishingly beautiful slow air. Following the duet tracks with Micho Russell, it really sounds like a lament for him. Well, it’s impossible to convert the soulful playing into dots, but it’s something like this:

Re: Gan Ainm

What instrument did you learn it on Gard?
If on the flute did you have to cut and tap or toot?
This polka does not have a trad Irish feel to it, if only because of all the “doubled” notes: repeated notes tend to come in 3s (rolls) not in 2s in Irish music. I noticed that difference when trying to play some Rumanian gypsy music: to enliven the tunes they almost automatically double every note… Something like |d/d/e/e/ e/e/f/f/| where one might hear |d/d/d/e/ e/e/e/f| in ITM as a rendering of |de ef| for example.

Re: Gan Ainm

“doubling notes” -with the bow, the fingers or with the tongue- is not as easy as it sounds. Takes quite a bit of practice if you come from ITM.
The Jig of Slurs shows that rolls can be tricky too when played across two beats (as in |Gbb bdd|dee edb|…)

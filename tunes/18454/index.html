Col. David Stewart Of Garth

From the album The Legacy Of The Scottish Fiddle, Vol. 2> Who wrote this plus who was David Stewart and where is Garth in Scotland?

↳ Posted by Connor Hickey on September 22nd, 2019.

Re: Col. David Stewart Of Garth

The David Stewart being referred to is Major General David Stewart born 1772 and died 1829. He was descendant of James Stewart who built Garth Castle which is located North-West of Aberfeldy, Perthsire.

↳ Posted by Connor Hickey on September 22nd, 2019.

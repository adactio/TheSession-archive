Jinny and the teepee (by Eoin Duignan)

Eoin’s CD with the waltz “Jinny and the teepee” on it is called “Coumíneol”.
It stands for a beach in West Kerry, Dingle Peninsula, which is written as “Coumeenoole” in english.
It was composed in honor to a young woman who lived in her teepee on the Great Blasket Island for one season.

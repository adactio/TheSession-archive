Reel Ti-Douard

I learned this tune from a recording by the Quebec group Ni Sarpe Ni Branche, with fiddlers Claude Methe and Simon Riopel. I love the way that each part is 2 quarter notes shy of a full eight bars. Try this one out on your favorite accompanist. “Ti-Douard” is a colloquial shortening of the name “Little Edward,” though I don’t know which particular Edouard the tune is from/for.

The Champion

Composed by Peter Fitzgerald in honour of Billy McComiskey’s All Ireland senior button accordion title in 1986. It can be found on Trian’s album, simply called Trian, as the 3rd hornpipe in the set. This is a flute friendly version of the tune and I believe the middle C in bar 4 would normally be played an octave lower.

Different name

In Newton Tolman’s book “Quick Tunes and Good Times”, he calls this “Ladies’ Walpole Reel”, but that may be because there is a Walpole, New Hampshire (USA) near him. Fine tune. Glad you posted it. Tolman is an old pillar of New England Contra. There’s a session and dance in his hometown of Nelson, NH that has been going on continuously for more than two hundred years.

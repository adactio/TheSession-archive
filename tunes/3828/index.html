The De Danann version

The De Danann version seems to have an interesting history, as it seems Frankie Gavin had the tune from the fiddler Dermot McLaughlin, who in turn had it from Iain Macleod the mandolin player, formerly of Shugglenifty. This version was created by Iain Macleod but it seems that other independent versions have appeared in the Highland piping fraternity.

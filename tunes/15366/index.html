One comment

The Falls Of Altnarie

I transcribed it from the playing of Liz Doherty on her album “Last Orders”.

This is a composition of the Scottish fiddler Ian Hardie, he describes as “a gorge with a spectacular waterfall flowing into the River Findhorn at Ardclach, Nairnshire”. His score differs only in the second part, see http://www.ian-hardie.co.uk/scores/fallsaltnarie.mscz.pdf

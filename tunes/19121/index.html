Two comments

The Twin Cities

A very fine 4-part jig composed by one of my favourite musicians, Sean O’Driscoll. Ron P. and myself have been lucky enough to be in a couple of sessions with Sean and his wife Mary at Willie Clancy weeks in the past few years. He recorded this jig on his CD “So There You Go”, which I would recommend for some very tasteful banjo playing, and some very good new compositions.
I think the transcription is accurate, but would value a second opinion. What I’ve posted works, but there are one or two notes and phrases I’m not sure of, and can’t seem to get hold of, no matter how many times I listen to the recording.
Sean plays some perfectly-executed triplets at various points in the tune, but I have left them out, and hopefully provided the bare bones of the tune. Hope to get a chance to play it with him in Miltown once we all get through the present situation.
PS - from Sean’s sleeve notes : “I lived for many a happy day in the Twin Cities of Minneapolis / St. Paul, Minnesota, [this ] tune honours my many friends there”.

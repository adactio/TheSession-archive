Re: McDonagh’s Air

This is a simple but magical tune well worth getting to know. I learned it from the concertina playing of Eva McCauley when we had sessions long ago at the Dalby House and Shepherd’s Pub in Elora, Ontario. When played with chords that follow a walking bass line, it can really help support the magic. I play as follows…
A part - G G/F# Em C , Em D Am G/B C
B part G D/F# Em G/D C , Em D Am G/B C
Last 1/2 of B part could also be - Em D C maj7 /
slainte

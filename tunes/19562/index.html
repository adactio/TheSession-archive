Stoned Giants

This is one of lots of tunes by Jennifer Wrigley of Orkney, who has written many good tunes and plays fiddle along with twin sister Hazel on guitar and piano. You can buy their book “TRAD SECRETS”, which also includes 3 or 4 tunes by Hazel - who has added chords to all the tunes. Get it on the website “Wrigley Sisters” - The Reel.

Jennifer “has no objection to her tunes being posted on thesession”. This tune has an uneven rhythm in the 2nd part ie 6/8, then 7/8, and back to reel time. Haven’t asked her but I guess she’s added these bizarre time sigs to add to the impression of the stoned giants, probably the 4,000 years old stones of the Ring of Brodgar by the Loch of Stenness. Unless she meant the even older Stones of Stenness. A talent among the several tune writers of Orkney - see also Thora Linklater’s “The Golden Slipper” and her Walrus tune, both posted by me and excellent!

Stoned Giants, X:2

Forgetting that there was already a setting here, I transcribed X:2 from the recording and it came out as above. Slightly, but not materially, different from X:1, and with chords that seemed to me to fit, but use or ignore as required Probably not the sort of thing that would gain much currency in sessions because of the irregular phrasing, but a tune with great character, I think. Reminds me a bit of pieces by Ian Hardie and Paddy Fahey - both of whom have written tunes that take you where you didn’t expect to go.

(I don’t know why the abc has done odd things to the way some groups of notes are beamed, by the way).

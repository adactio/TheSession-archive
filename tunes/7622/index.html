Within A Mile Of Clonbur, X:2

This setting comes primarily from Angelina Carberry’s rendition on her album with Martin Quinn where it goes by the title Burnes. She plays it with a capo on 1, but I transcribed it in G so it’s session friendly. She plays it following Green Groves of Erin and the transition from A dorian to G major is really nice.

↳ Posted by Durak on May 7th, 2021.

Within A Mile Of Clonbur, X:3

From the playing of Sorcha Costello here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0NCQkDceLkY

Clonbur (in Irish, An Fháirthí, “the fringe”) is a village in Connemara, Co. Galway, very near the border with Mayo and between Lough Mask and Lough Corrib.

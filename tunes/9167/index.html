Audio availability and playing notes

Hi folks,

There is a rough and ready recording of this on my myspace page.

http://www.myspace.com/barrypearce

A few notes on playing. In bars 3, 7 and 8 use half-position. Be careful in bar 9 if you have stayed in half-position as the D is a stretch - you can always move back to first-pos for the B at the start of of bar9.

In bar 13 I tend just to stretch, but again the whole bar could be played shifted down into half position.

Hope this is fun!

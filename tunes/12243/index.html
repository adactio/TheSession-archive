Miss Doyne’s Fancy, X:2

“Miss Doyne’s Fancy” was published by Maurice Hime in his Hime’s Collection of Country Dances for 1810, No. 4, (Dublin, 1810), page 1. It is clearly the basis for the reel which acquired the local name, “Corriga Grove”, in the tradition of south Co. Leitrim, John Lee’s home area.

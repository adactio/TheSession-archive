Sally Ann’s, X:2

Sally Ann is popular throughout Appalachia. It is closely related to “Sail Away Ladies” and has many variations with other titles as well. Best guess as to origins might be African American ("Old Time Kentucky Fiddle Tunes - by Jeff Todd Titon). The version I post here is a composite of the variations I’ve heard in Appalachia.
Here’s my friend Dan Knowles with his take on it:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-rFVowmUayQ

Two comments

Leaving Inchgarth

lovely strathspey written by Donald Webster from Forfar, recorded on the Donald Morrison Trio LP “The Bonnie Lass o’ Lownie” in 1984. presumably refers to the Inchgarth next to Forfar Loch.

↳ Posted by SaoiDunnichen on August 17th, 2020.

Leaving Inchgarth ABC help

In measures 14 & 15 your G flats should be coded in ABC as =g, as opposed to _g. The = will make your note a natural, which seems to be what you’re trying to achieve. Nice tune.

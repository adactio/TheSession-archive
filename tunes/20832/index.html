The Lakeside

A waltz, composed by Sid Dlum, that Cormac Breatnach heard in Vermont in the mid 80’s. He forgot the second part so he recomposed it with the help of Niall O’Callanain.
I’ve played this tune for decades, and usually get’s the “Oh, I’ve heard that” reaction.

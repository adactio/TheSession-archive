No Pican, Capitán

“Nothing’s biting, captain”. Written by Simon Bradley. From Llan de Cubel’s 2019 album, La Lluz Encesa.

Liner notes:
This tune refers to a day out along the Candás coast aboard the fishing boat ‘Llan de Cubel’. A great day but with no fish. The boat died in a surge tide the following winter.

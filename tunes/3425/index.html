Waiting for Janet

This is the first time I have submitted a tune here and I’m a real novice with ABC but I am so impressed with this site I wanted to share the music that’s stirring my household at the moment. youngest daughter has just decided to transfer her keyboard skills on to an accordeon and THIS is her chosen first tune. It’s a lovely lilting tune that of course is splendid on the diatonic melodeon which it was written on. Hope some of you like it.

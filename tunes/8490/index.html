Nine comments

This is not so much a jig (hence the 16 bar sections) as a two step for dances such as the Britannia Two Step or Military Two Step, hence the alternative and more common name: The Shetland Two Step.
It was written by Shetlander Frank Jamieson.
In practice after the part in G you would go back to the part in A to finish.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on April 29th, 2008.

By the way, this transcription is from the playing of Amy Geddes.

Ronald Cooper’s Shetland 2-step

Another tune attributed* to Ronnie Cooper (1934-82), Shetland accordionist/pianist. This one has become a favourite among players in an e-session based in Sussex, UK (but with international membership) that has been Zo0m-meeting over the past year or so. I’ve added chords; take or leave as desired.
*I can’t remember where I came across it. The title is a giveaway, of course, but it’s not included in ‘The Ronnie Cooper Collection’, pub. Iris Black & Kenny Cooper, Lerwick, 2013, so I can’t confirm authorship.
It’s listed as a Barndance because there isn’t a Two-Step category.

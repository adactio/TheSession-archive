Three comments

Loch An Duin

I learned this tune from a friend. I tried to find more information on it on the Web, but there are not many references. It may have been included in some of William Ross’ books of highland bagpipe music.

↳ Posted by Arjan Kamp on May 27th, 2023.

Loch An Duin, X:2

This is a dotted version with chords in G. It can be played on bagpipes as wells in G as there is no C occurring in the melody.

↳ Posted by Arjan Kamp on May 27th, 2023.

Re: Loch An Duin

It fits in the range of the Scottish pipes but is, arguably, in G ionian (4th-less hexatonic) rather than A.

One comment

Billy Caples’

This is from a recording of the playing of Mike Reynolds of Boston, an excellent boxplayer and singer in his own rite. Caples was a Boston boxplayer who was active in the show band and dance hall scene of the area. He was a member of the inaugural Hall of Fame class (alongside Joe Derrane) for the Boston Comhaltas, which he helped to set up.

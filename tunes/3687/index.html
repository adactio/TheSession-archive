The Four Leafed Shamrock (reel)

Composer: Paddy O’Brien
Source: Alan & John Kelly - “Fourmilehouse”
Transcription: gmp

↳ Posted by gian marco on October 13th, 2004.

Paddy O’Brien’s set including this tune.

If people are interested in what set of tunes the composer Paddy O‘Brien played this tune, he started with this tune & followed it with another of his compositions, “St. Patrick’s Night”. Can be found in the book of his tunes - “The Compositions of Paddy O’Brien”. Paddy was from Newtown, Nenagh, Co. Tipperary.

↳ Posted by Brian Morrissey on November 12th, 2004.

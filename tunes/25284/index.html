Chez Jean-Luc

This beautiful mazurka is the 2nd in a set of two played by Jason O’Rourke on his 2004 CD Roguery Road.
He got it off Bernard Lasbleiz, a Breton box player who played with Tregeriad, according to Jason’s notes on the album.
“Chez Jean-Luc” is Jason’s name for the tune.
NB;
Accidentals to taste..

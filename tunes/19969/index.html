O Son Do Ar

Galician Traditional Music. Composition of Bieito Romero, the piper of the Galician band Luar Na Lubre. This tune is for sure the most famous of the band because It was played by Mike Oldfield in one of his albums.

The original tune is in Dm, I transposed it to Em.

Tune in Luar Na Lubre’s album Plenilunio, it is the first tune in the set:

https://youtu.be/ezVaHlNFQ1I

Mike Oldfield’s version:

https://youtu.be/UQk_YyoQ5gg

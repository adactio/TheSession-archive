↳ Posted by ceolachan on December 7th, 2005.

Byth Adra, X:2

Some slight but crucial differences from the original post transcription - this is how Y Glerorfa (the former, sadly now defunct Welsh Folk Orchestra) had the tune - directly from the composer, Huw Roberts. The first half of the penultimate bar of the B music IMO makes a crucial and very prominent difference to the melody!

↳ Posted by Jemtheflute on May 28th, 2020.

Byth Adra, X:3

The same melody as X:2 above, but with chords - I’ve posted it separately because the chords seem to mess up the spacing graphically - it looks a mess! :-( The chords are those provided to the harpists in Y Glerorfa as part of the arrangment we played. You can find alternative (blander) chords here, in a representation of the whole set of three Welsh jigs we played in Y Glerorfa: (3rd tune in set): http://www.sesiwn.com/sesiwn/Clerorfa/Alawon.html#a4

Huw states that this tune was written in th 1980s when, as a member of the band Pedwar yn y Bar, he was on the road more than at home…. hence the title Byth Adra = Never Home

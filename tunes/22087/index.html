Three comments

The Girl That I Love

Another tune composed by Larry Grogan in the early 18th century. The Grogans were the owners of the Johnstown castle Estate in Wexford. Larry was a gentleman piper and friend of all the ascendancy characters of that era, including the famous ‘Jack Lattin’ who, according to the old story danced himself to death for a wager.
Larry Grogan himself is believed to be buried in Jamaica, where he died at a young age…probably went to make his fortune in the sugar trade like some other Wexford ascendancy families of the time (Tate of Tate and Lyle being a notable one). This setting I’ve transposed to D, as the original version I found had been ‘arranged for the pianoforte’ in the key of E. I found a second version, with a different second part and will upload it also.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 1st, 2022.

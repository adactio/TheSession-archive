St. Brigid’s Day

Beautiful lively slip jig written by great concertina player and dancer Caitlín Nic Gabhainn specially for St.Brigid’s Day 01.02.2022 and recorded at ITMA Dublin together with Louise Mulcahy (flute) and Síle Denvir (harp). Transcribed from ABCs Caitlín posted on her instagram.

Two comments

Berrigan’s Folly

A really interesting reel written by Matt MacIsaac. The lopsided phrasing of the first section makes it somewhat difficult to wrap your head around the timing at first, and the second section flips between dorian and mixolydian to produce a pretty cool effect.

Two comments

Michael’s Dream

This old-style hornpipe was composed by the late Michael Boyce of Ambrosetown near Duncormick in south Wexford. Michael was a lovely tidy fiddler of the ‘old-school’ playing in the South Leinster fiddle style and I knew him very well back in the 1970’s and 1980’s. For my very first trip to an All-Ireland fleadh - to Ennis in 1977 - I travelled in a jam-packed van being driven by Michael. the tune came to me from Padraig Sinnott who had been given a manuscript copy of it by Michael’s son Ted. The manuscript is remarkable, in that the music was transcribed and embellished by the hand of the wonderful Wexford piper, music teacher and musicologist Seamus MacMathuna - and like all of ‘Mac’s’ manuscripts, it’s a work of art.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on January 18th, 2023.

Re: Michael’s Dream

Just located a video of Michael playing with his great friend Dick Hillis on the box and Michael’s son Jim on banjo at a concert in Carrig-on-Bannow many years ago. Dick and Jim were two of the horde in Michael’s blue van on that trip to Ennis in 77! They’re playing the well-known Galway hornpipe, then 2 jigs - the Galbally jig (a local name for The girls of Banbridge) and Paddy from Portlaw.

Airthrey Castle

Shetland fiddler Tom Anderson wrote this tune and named it for Airthrey Castle, where he taught a summer school fiddle class at Sterling University.

↳ Posted by Greg McLaughlin on December 2nd, 2023.

Re: Airthrey Castle

In 1982, the University of STIRLING issued a cassette of Tom playing with “Tammy’s Angels,” a group of (mostly) young girl fiddlers from Shetland, all of whom he taught (including Debbie Scott and a young Catriona Macdonald, Greg). Included on the cassette is “Airthrey Castle” where the Heritage of Scotland Summer School was held. He states that it “has become the signature tune for the fiddle class.”

I presume Greg gor the tune from Tom Anderson’s 1979 book Haand Me Doon Da Fiddle" (also published by the Dept of Continuing Education, University of STIRLING). A couple of corrections: the first not should be an octave higher; there’s a note missing in the 4th last bar, should be | d2 AG F2 A<d |.

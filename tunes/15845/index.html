Glad Låt, X:2

X:2 is the setting I have for this tune from Dalarna - notated here in the more usual 3/4 time for a polska, although it doesn’t quite capture the triplet rhythm in (e.g.) bar 2. I don’t know who did the arrangement, but it adds a fairly close-fitting harmony line. Because X:1 was set in 9/8 it’s classed here as a slip jig - which it isn’t, of course. I can’t see how to change the time sig for X:2. The title I have for it is ‘Gla’låten’ (The Happy Tune).

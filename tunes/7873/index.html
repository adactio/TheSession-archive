La Boîte à Frissons - C: Jean Blanchard - Anahata

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DDAmMkv9PwI

Uploaded on 6 Nov 2010
Written by Jean Blanchard, recorded for melnets Theme of the month September 2010 “tunes I learned in my (Summer) holidays”
Most people who play learnt it from a recording of Andy Cutting, but I have a PDF of the original tune kindly emailed to me by Monsieur Blanchard himself who I met in July. (merci beaucoup, Jean!)

Played by Anahata on Dino Baffetti 3-voice G/C box that’s been given the Oakwood-style Martyn Banks treatment.

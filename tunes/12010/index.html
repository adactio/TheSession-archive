Sam’s Delight

‘Sam’s Delight’ is a reel I composed after watching on at one of my pet dogs Sam’s delight at finding a bone she had buried the day before! The tune will feature on my next solo album (release date TBC). My cousin Colin Nea has recently recorded Sam’s Delight for his new Button Accordion album. I look forward to hearing Colin’s version of the tune. Regards. Enda

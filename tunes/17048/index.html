Two comments

Faustiñeira

Galician muñeira composed by flute player Juan J. Almaraz. Recorded in the third album of Stolen Notes. http://www.stolen-notes.com

↳ Posted by Juan Almaraz on June 12th, 2018.

Faustiñeira, X:2

Setting for mouth whistling.

This wonderful tune allows whistling with changes in octave that are quite straightforward, they don’t involve big changes in the phrasing.

I removed all the rolls because they cannot be whistled. For the ones in the first part I left the dotted chrotchets. This can be accepted because all of them are on the beat.

And for the rolls in the second part I’ve chosen something I do myself with the only technique I know, the one of the inwards whistling: dotted quaver - semiquaver - quaver. The three of them in the same note, that would be something very similar to the roll but leaving out the graze notes.

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on March 20th, 2021.

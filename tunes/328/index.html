Original Waterbed

Here’s the ABCs, as best I could do, of Diarmuid himself playing the tune on his Calico CD:

X: 1
T: Waterbed, The
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Ador
| EAAE Bc AG | EGDG EDEG | AEGA cA ~A2 | cdec dcAG |
| EAAE Bc AG | EGDG E2 B/c/d | ecdc AEGA | cAGA EA ~A2 :|
|: ~e2 dege ~e2 | B/c/deg dc A2 | ~e2 dega ~a2 | gedc AEGA |
| ~e2 dege ~e2 | B/c/deg dega | gaeg dEGA | cAGA EA ~A2 :|

↳ Posted by Phantom Button on March 21st, 2004.

This punchy tune is based on the pentatonic blues scale (there’s almost no b and f in it).

This tune is also on the Butter Dog’s “Sky” album, but played as a very bluesy jig. Interesting to finally find the original… Cool tune

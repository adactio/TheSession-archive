Mr. Furlong’s Pigeon (Taibhse Loch Garman)

A tune composed by the London born piper Dicky Deegan. Dicky has family ties to County Wexford. This tunes title refers to a local ghost story from the Adamstown area of County Wexford where Dicky’s mother hails from. Thanks to the Clonroche born piper, Eanna Harrington who now works with NPU for drawing my attention to Dicky’s compositions for inclusion in the Wexford Tune Project Collection.

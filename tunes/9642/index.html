Fechin Inn

Noticed a request for this one and fancied learning it anyway, so here it is. It’s by Brian Finnegan and I picked it up from the last Flook album ‘Haven’.

Here’s it is for an A whistle…….

X: 1
T: Fechin Inn
C: Brain Finnegan
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Amaj
|: c2 Bc AFFF | FAfe cBBB | c2 ec AFFF | EFAF c3 z |
c2 ec AFFF | FAfe cBBB | ABce f2 ec | [1 BABc AFFF :| [2 BABc F2 AF ||

FBAF E z c2 | BA F2 f3 e | [1 effe a2 fe | effe EFAF :| [2 effe a4- | a2 EF AFAB ||

One of Brian’s more traditional sounding pieces.

In the minor pentatonic mode (no 2nd, no 6th) which makes it possible to play it in F# on the D whistle (X:2)

Three comments

Once I had a recording on tape taken from the radio back in the 1980th which I no longer possess. I remember the ‘Quiet Lands of Erin’ sung very freely. I have found a notation in a collection by Jake Walton which juggles enormously with the measure. So I tried to make a simple ABC from things at hand.

Five comments

Kiss The Lass Ye Love Best

From the playing of Jerry Holland, recorded on “The Fiddlestifks Collection” (1994) and “Parlor Music” (2005) and published in his first collection of tunes. Jerry Holland identifies the tune as an 18th-century Scottish strathspey first published in Bremner (1757), and states in his notes that he heard it played by Bill Lamey.

↳ Posted by Charmion on August 26th, 2020.

Kiss The Lass Ye Love Best, X:2

This is Charmion’s setting tweaked a little to make more sense. The settings in Bremner and The Skye Collection (1887) are more like a reel.

Re: Kiss The Lass Ye Love Best

Ah, but they’re not quarters, they’re dotted eighths. If you want them all joined you would write it like this:
| A2 F/G/A B<EE>B | A/A/A F>A d>ef<d | etc - i.e. no spaces between notes you wish grouped.

It’s a learning curve. However, now you are retired (congratulations!) you have - in theory - more time…

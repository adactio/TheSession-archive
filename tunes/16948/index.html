Gan Ainm

This is the slip jig ‘Gan Ainm’ from Pádraig O‘Reilly’s album “Highly Strung!”. The one previously linked is a barndance. I love slip jigs generally, and there just aren’t enough of them. So when you find one that doesn’t seem well known, you just need to share it. I also love O’Reilly’s piano playing (in my opinion, unjustly panned by Geoff Wallis in his online review). Still trying to work out what he’s doing in the left hand, but will update here once I’ve cracked it!

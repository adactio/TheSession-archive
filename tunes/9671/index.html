Cullen Skink

I wrote this tune to commemorate the most wonderful Scottish national seafood soup. Note: Since Cullen Skink is seafood it seemed natural to write it in the key of Sea….get it? (ACKKK!)

Try playing it with The Haggis, to make a Scottish cuisine set. :) Finished off with “Highland whiskey” or“ Athole Brose”.

Enjoy the tune!

All the best,

David Gardner

Morfa

This tune finds its way ultimately back to Thompson’s Compleat Collection of 200 Favourite Country Dances, vol. 2 (London, 1765) but came through Llanfachreth in Anglesey where it was in a tune book in the possession of John Williams in 1833.

The Morfa, along with the Hafod were the two largest copper smelting works in the world, both next to each other in Swansea. The connections with the copper mines in Anglesey were intimate at this time & Anglesey mine owners were also copper masters in Swansea. This is the fiddle setting, Bend the low notes up the octave on the flute

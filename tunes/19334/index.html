Diabolo Lyon

Lovely 11/8 tune going into a slip reel played by Ciorras.

In the recording, it goes [AB ABC BC BC ABC], then they transform the rythm to slip reel (X:2) and
it goes [ABC ABC]

It is an original composition of irish fliddler Zoë Conway :
“The […] tune is a piece I composed while on tour in France. It is in 11/8 and has a breton influence - a little skip on the last beat of the bar.” https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d-dI-gXxXWY

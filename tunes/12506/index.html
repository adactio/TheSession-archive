Eddie Moloney’s, X:2

Here’s a stripped down transcription of Eddie Moloney’s playing of this reel on Disc 1 of Eddie Moloney, Master Musician. The whole 2-disc set is wonderful dive into the East Galway flute tradition. Eddie plays it on it’s own with tremendous lift and drive, to the evident delight of those lucky enough to be in the room with him. He calls it The Dispute over the Weather.

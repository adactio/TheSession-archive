Two comments

Frank Marchand’s

Second tune in the “Crooked Water Valley” set off Talisk’s album “Beyond.” It’s mostly a straight-forward jig except for the half measure tag on the end of the B part. I couldn’t find any background information about the tune, but my guess is that it’s named for the American mixing engineer Frank Marchand.

Cawdor Fair, X:2

This is the music to the first verse of ‘Wilsy and Nancy’, as sung by Lucy Pringle and Chris Wright. The tune has a great lilt and could easily be written in 12/8, like this;
M: 12/8
“Nancy:”g2fe2d e2gB2B|d3d2B d3d2(g|g)2fe2d e2gB2B|A3Bd2 e3He2 “Wilsy:”g||
d2eg2a g2ag2d|e3a2g a3a2a|b2ag2f g2fe2d|(e2g)f2a g3g3||

The words are taken up in turn by Lucy and Chris, starring in the roles of Wilsy and Nancy.
Manly Wilsy sounds an octave below what is written here but that’s beyond the point.

In the recording, the lines are interspersed with a ‘couple of odd bars’ of very tasteful, discreet, cheerful banjo & harmonica accompaniment, which lend the right pace to this pleasant rhyme and sustain the narrative suspense perfectly.

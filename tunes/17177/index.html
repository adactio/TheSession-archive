Two For Joy

This is a different tune from the other one of the same name on this site. I heard this at a session in Sussex last year and was told it was written by Paul James of Blowzabella. There is a companion piece - ‘One for Sorrow’ - which I shall also post. The choice of chords is mine, so may not be the same as the original.

(For some reason, this transcript includes some long slur marks which were not intended. Their appearance is one of life’s mysteries).

Four comments

Sunderland Lasses

I figured it’s been a while since the last 3/2 hornpipe submission. This is one that has withstood the test of time without drifting into complete obscurity. I think it’s fun to slide on to the first B, hence “J” in the ABC.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on April 11th, 2004.

I first heard this tune in North Hero, Vermont at the Northumbrian Pipers Convention which has now morphed into the North Hero pipers Gathering. The tune is found in the 2nd volume of the Northumbrian Pipers Tunebook.

↳ Posted by Bill Reeder on April 12th, 2004.

One comment

Corona Air

One of the most famous low whistle tunes, the opening bars to Reel Around the Sun at the beginning of the original Riverdance, bringing Davy Spillane to the attention of the public and inspiring many people, myself included, to learn the low whistle. It didn’t do the sales of Overton whistles any harm either. It’s not an easy tune because of the long and high notes, soaring to third octave E at one point. But, in my view, it’s still a haunting classic preferably played where there’s lots of natural echo. > denotes a falling slide and the rising slides don’t show in the music but are included in the notation as J.

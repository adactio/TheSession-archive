Seonaidh’s

A nice little tune called Seonaidh’s Barndance, written by the young composer Seonaidh Forrest. Obviously, to be played with plenty of swing! Perhaps not in the friendliest keys, but it proves a fun challenge. Can be seen in this video https://www.instagram.com/reel/DU0U1NeiJnZ/?igsh=MXRoejJqY3lzbCVsdg==

Re: Seonaidh’s

Nice tune, thanks for sharing.

If the 7th is sharped the only two times it appears, shouldn’t this be in B major, not mix?

When I change the key to B major and omit the sharp sign on the two A notes (A is already sharp in B major), it sounds right to my ear, matching what the lads are playing. As best I can see, Seonaidh’s home note is the top hand, second hole. On a D whistle or flute, that would be A.

FWIW, the tune lands very well on fiddle in either A major or G major.

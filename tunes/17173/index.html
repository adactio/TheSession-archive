Two comments

Old Banish Misfortune

Not to be confused with the much more popular session tune Banish Misfortune (https://thesession.org/tunes/9) this one is from O‘Neill’s 1001 (#53). It obviously has some similarities–the second section is nearly identical, and the third section is at least strongly reminiscent. I don’t know whether this was just a poorly transcription on the part of O’Neill, or whether this is really a distinct but related tune. But, adding it for completeness.

↳ Posted by Ian Varley on August 18th, 2018.

Re: Old Banish Misfortune

It’s definitely not a poorly done transcription - it’s nearly note for note exactly how Edward Cronin plays it on a wax cylinder, which is available to listen to and purchase a CD copy of from the Ward Irish Music Archives. The only thing that’s different is Cronin plays a second ending on the A part that’s missing from the O’Neill transcription (possibly due to him adding that later). It’s likely Cronin was the original transcriber for this tune as well.

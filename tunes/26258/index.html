Three comments

Catch A Penny Fox

This jig was composed by Scottish fiddler Ian Hardie. 
It was written by Ian in the mid-80s, in a strong Scottish fiddle style, as played by him here :
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kka_k524q8U
A nice rendition was also recorded by Kathryn Tickell on the pipes, and then it was popularised among French folk accordion players by Alain Pennec (Brittany) who plays it in Am.

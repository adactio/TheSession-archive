Fish On

A great Liz Carroll tune, from her album “On the Offbeat”. We’ve been playing it a lot at sessions here in Austin and I didn’t find it here, so adding.

(It isn’t in her book of tunes, “Liz Carroll Collected”, but if you don’t already have that book you should buy it.)

Re: St Ninian’s Parish Centre Ceilidh

You’re welcome!

In the Pipe Band world Bob Worrall, for all his achievements, is perhaps best known nowadays as the commentator/presenter for the annual livestream of the World Pipe Band Championships in Glasgow.

It’s a voiceover of sorts- as each band finishes he does a concise summary of his impressions of their performance. I think I have a pretty good ear but he always points out numerous things I didn’t hear. It goes to show why he’s in such demand as a judge.

His catch-phrases have become the stuff of legend. People have compiled and ranked lists of them. There’s a “Bob Worrall drinking game” where you take a dram each time he utters one of them.

The livestream of The Worlds is a worldwide party with pipe bands gathering in homes, pubs, parish-halls etc to watch live whatever the hour. It starts here in the western US at 2am and yes we’re up watching.

Bob gives his “shout out” to bands the world over at various times during the show.

Re: St Ninian’s Parish Centre Ceilidh

I should mention that the long f’s which begin bars 1 and 5 of the first part have a unique Highland pipe ornament done on them. It’s perfectly performable on uilleann pipes, flute, or whistle though not traditionally associated with those instruments.

Generally done with a lead-in note e, and always ending on the melody note f, it’s
f-e-g-e
or put another way cutting e with f, then g, before ending up on f.

On uilleann pipes a “tight” f-g-f triplet would be the equivalent.

Whatever the instrument those f’s need something doing to them, be it an ‘upper mordent’ or what have you.

(I’m saying “f” in all cases but of course I mean f# due to being in the key of D Major.)

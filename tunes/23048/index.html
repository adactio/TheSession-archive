Frank Maher’s, X:2

A few variations, mostly from the playing of Frank Maher.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on April 22nd, 2023.

Re: Frank Maher’s

Frank and Christina Smith taught this tune and the one after it at Goderich in 2002. I don’t remember if they had names for them or not, when I get the chance I’ll try to dig up my stuff from that year.

The one that comes after is a two-part version of “Coppers and Brass” aka “The Humours of Ennistymon”, but in D instead of the more usual G, presumably because Frank is playing on his 4-stop in D.

↳ Posted by Sol Foster on April 22nd, 2023.

Re: Frank Maher’s

Thank you Sol.

He didn’t have names for them in his album liner notes.

I mostly have the second one transcribed now, and yup, one part is very close to a part in Coppers And Brass. The other part isn’t so close.

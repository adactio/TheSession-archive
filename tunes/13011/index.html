Alborada

Transcribed from Sylvain Barou’s “Alborada”

↳ Posted by watkinsj on September 25th, 2013.

Re: Alborada De Veiga

Alborada in Spanish (alvorada in Portuguese) means dawn and it is also the name given to a tune type played at the start of the day, particularly a day of celebration (both secular and religious), across the various bagpipe traditions in both Spain and Portugal.

Sylvain gives this tune the full name of Alborada de Veiga in his notes and says the following: “Rubén [Bada] played me this alborada from a very old archive recording. I was surprised of its particular scale which we don’t seem to hear much in Asturias anymore.”

By this I presume he means the myxolydian mode he plays it in (major scale with a minor seventh). Asturian gaitas would more commonly play a major seventh (on the seventh finger note at least, the top hand can play both major or minor).

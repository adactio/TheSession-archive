Three comments

Aodhan’s

I learned this tune from my teacher. It’s composed by Mick McAuley and appears on the Solas album The Words that Remain. I’ve also heard it on Emily Smith’s album A Different Life. It’s a great set ending tune and can be really pushed along.

Great for trying out those open string roles on the fiddle but it’s also got a high C in it, so needs a position change (unless you have huge hands). I find it best to change to 2nd position in the 6th bar of the B-part, when you hit that high A, then slip back into 1st on the open E in the following bar.

↳ Posted by davedupplaw on February 4th, 2013.

Re: Aodhan’s

If the high C is too much for some—and I agree it can be annoying—I’ll play the tune an octave down, or just parts of it. The tune sounds good played low, with all the low A rolls being effective.

This tune is one of three that Kevin Burke got from the harmonica-playing Murphy family of Wexford (?). He recorded them together with the Murphy’s as The Raheen Medley. This tune is, I think, the last in the set. The second is The Trip to Cullenstown, https://thesession.org/tunes/2996, and the first is a tune in A-dorian, for which I do not have a name, and which does not seem to have been posted here yet. I will post it if anyone can give me a name for it.

The raheen

Hi, i learned the tune the raheen reel from the playing of Pip Murphy from County Wexford. Its a composition of his father’s - Phil. The notation here is of a different tune which Phil and his sons play in a set with it - this one was recorded by Karen Tweed, as far as i know, as the Raheen.

The Raheen which is on my own recording, and the one Phil Murphy wrote is:

CABG A-AB CABG AGEF G-GF GABC dBGB d-dB
CABG A-AB CABG AGEF GABd g-ge dBGB A -

eaag a-ag edef gddg edef g-gf edef gedg
eaag a-ag edef gddg edef g-ge dBGB A--

Sorry i don’t usually write out tunes like that! it might be of interest to some people.

Le dea-ghuí
Aoife

Re: Pip Murphy’s

I have renamed this tune as Pip Murphy’s, because that’s its name as far as I know.
Before I made the change, it was called the Raheen here. This made no sense as the Raheen is the first in a set of reels played by the Murphy family of mouth organ virtuosi.
The set is known as the Raheen Medley. The Tunes are: The Raheen; The Trip to Cullenstown; Pip Murphy’s, this one.

Re: Pip Murphy’s

This tune was usually referred to as ‘The Dingle Reel’ around Wexford, because as I understand it, Phil Murphy first heard it and added it to the Murphy’s repertoire after a trip to a Fleadh Cheoil in Dingle many years ago. Like so often happens, they didn’t get a name for it at the time, perhaps the musicians playing it didn’t know a name for it. But I’m pretty certain that the Murphy’s don’t consider it as a composition of Phil’s. Pip certainly plays the tune as part of his own repertoire and also with his brother John. Pip has composed at least one tune of his own , one reel called ‘Tis better to wear out than rust out’.

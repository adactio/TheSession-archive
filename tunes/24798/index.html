Up Island

The late, lamented Darren Maloney, banjoist extraordinaire composed this reel while on
Martha’s Vineyard, where, he says, “You are either up island or down island”.
I think he composed many (at least several) tunes, but no one has posted any of them here.
I heard this one on this video between 8:52 and 10:23
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpsnfYm555w

The Road To Glenlough

I’m not usually a big fan of Irish waltzes, but I definitely do like this one. It is played beautifully by the late Donegal fiddler James Byrne on his solo album, which he titled after this waltz. I don’t know whether or not he composed it. A wonderful little tune - or maybe not so little, since it does have 4 parts.

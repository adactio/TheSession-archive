Beware Of The Storm

I know I shouldn’t post my own compositions… but I like this simple one. I feel like it’s familiar to me. Maybe I unconsciously took bits of existing tunes? Do you know any similar tunes? It’d be interesting to know.

Composed in the middle of a storm.

Three comments

David’s Reel

Since Neon_Knight’s name is David, I would guess that this is his own composition. I’ve always felt that it’s very easy to write tunes in a minor (ish) key, but very difficult to write an original one. To put my critical cap on, this tune contains a few ideas which we’ve seen in other tunes, and it’s a typical example of someone’s first attempts at composing a tune.

That doesn’t mean it’s bad by any means; in fact it’s not a bad amateur effort, especially for a young person. However, in an ideal world, David would have been pleased with it, and played it, and written more tunes, and honed his writing skills, and some years later his fellow sessioneers might have taken one or two of his tunes and played them again and again, and perhaps one of his mates would have submitted one of the better ones to thesession.org.

Having said that, when I was seventeen I might have done exactly the same as David. (I’m very, very glad I didn’t!) And here I am in my mid-fifties being all pompous and wagging fingers. Ahem. I wonder if David, now 22, is still playing and composing tunes?

Early In The Morning

Tune #884 in O‘Neill’s 1001. To my ear it sounds much more like a reel than a hornpipe, particularly with the A part’s similarity to The Peeler’s Jacket. This setting includes all the bowings and triplets from O’Neill’s, and I’ll submit a second barebones setting (in G, for comparison).

↳ Posted by benhockenberry on May 27th, 2021.

The Brexit

Well this is in F# minor and mostly pentatonic. There’s a few d notes in the A part which give a nice “semitonal grate” in this case. A tune I wrote today (day after the referendum) a bit sad, a bit angry and a bit rueful with a hint of trepidation.

↳ Posted by Kellie on November 16th, 2016.

Re: The Brexit

Great tune, Steve, thanks for sharing it. I really like it but made a few changes. The A part is magnificent, and the B part reflects it well, but I feel as though the two endings leave the tune in limbo. The last measure before the 1st ending, with the change to the high B signals an end to the tune, and it makes it feel a bit repetitive when you then head back to the beginning of the B part again. If you changed the B part to the following, it might help the direction.

|: af ~f2 fecA | ABcA Bcef | af ~f2 fecA | ABcA B2 ef |
|1 af ~f2 fecA | ABcA BcAB | cF (3FFF dFcF | ABcA B2ef :|
|2 af ~f2 fecA | ABcA Bcef | bfaf eAAF | ABcA B2AB |]

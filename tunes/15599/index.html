Re: The Gracious Fair Lady

Looking at the manuscript in the goodman collection it is notated in Split Common time which is actually 2/2 time. It can be played as 4/4 march but as an up-tempo barndance, I’m not so sure. I quite like it played in the form of a melodic air.

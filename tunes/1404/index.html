McGuire’s?

This jig is one of a pair on a recording I was given that was said to be by Buttons and Bows. it certainly sounds like them but I can’t find the track on any of their albums. I’ve called it McGuire’s as there’s no accordion on the recording so if it is indeed Buttons and Bows, it’s the McGuire brothers playing it.

↳ Posted by JoeEvans on June 16th, 2025.

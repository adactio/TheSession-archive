Two comments

This is a cracking reel (in Bflat, of course - not Gm) that I transcribed from When Here Meets There by Troy MacGillivray and Shane Cook.
I have since discovered that it was written by young Orcadian fiddler Catriona Price and it appears, pretty much note for note, in Fiona Driver’s Orkney Fiddle Tune book. (On the aforementioned CD it follows a couple of Fiona Driver’s reels.)
On the recording the fourth bar of the B part is often played as |gffc fa za| or |gffc faca|.

For those instruments with limited notes the reel plays quite nicely in G:

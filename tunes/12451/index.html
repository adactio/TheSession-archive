Gan Ainm CRÉ 2 #295 (NOT Lománach Cross)

This fine tune comes from Denis Murphy, and it can be found in Ceol Rince na hÉireann Vol. 2 (#295). You can listen to it on an archive recording of Denis collected by Breandán Breathnach in Gneeveguilla, County Kerry in 1966. X:2 is my transcription of the reel, and you can check out CRÉ 2 for their take on it.

One thing to note is that it’s NOT a reel called Lománach Cross, which is this reel in G major:
https://thesession.org/tunes/2099

The story appears to be, it got mistitled on Ceol as Sliabh Luachra, where Billy Clifford plays a lovely set of reels titled “Lomonach Cross / I’m Waiting for You”. I’d have to inquire with Billy to learn more! One thing I know for sure is that publishers those days were basically forcing musicians to come up with names for their tunes because Untitled tracks were not looking great to them. Many folks in Sliabh Luachra got creative with the titles for that sole reason!

↳ Posted by Bregolas on December 11th, 2021.

Donal a’ Clumper’s Reel (S: Denis Murphy, simplified)

Apparently, Breandán Breathnach *did* have a name for this reel (which he got from Denis Murphy). While it was left untitled in CRÉ 2, a transcription that had earlier been published in the Ceol magazine was titled Donal a’ Clumper’s Reel. The issue came out the same year as the recording of Denis was made, in 1966.

https://www.itma.ie/texts/ceol-2/

(I modified my earlier transcription to add more detail from Denis’s recording, now adding a simplified version with more comments)

According to Paul de Grae, Donal a’ Clumper refers to “Dónal or Daniel O’Sullivan, who owned the dancehall at Clamper where Jack “The Lighthouse” Connell, among others, played in the 1930s and ’40s. Clamper Bridge crosses the Araglen river a few miles north of Kiskeam. The title of the polka, The Shady Road to Clamper, refers to the same place.”

Paul was commenting on another Denis Murphy tune mysteriously titled “Donal a’ Phumpa” – whether it was misspelt by Breathnach, or the names got swapped, or there was indeed a separate person known as “Dan the Pump”, we may never know… But I’ll be asking around!

Be sure to check out Paul’s latest transcriptions of Denis Murphy’s tunes picked by Aidan Connolly for the World Fiddle Day. Of course, these’re meant to be enjoyed and studied with recordings in mind, both Aidan’s and Denis’s.

https://www.facebook.com/groups/283125488560326/posts/2331269897079198/

Donal a’ Clumper’s Reel: relatives (not just Patsy Touhey’s)

Bonus note: I’ve seen others refer to this reel as a version of Patsy Touhey’s but I believe it would be more practical to refer to them as tunes under the same umbrella – otherwise we’d have to merge it with tunes such as the Humours of Castlefin!

Ølland, X:3

Learned from Emelie Waldken. The ABCs come from abcnotation.com. Many subtle variations. The cd Bc A in bars 2 and 3 seem to be played and sang consistently. A simple tune, yet, when played as Waldken does, becomes quite compelling dance music.

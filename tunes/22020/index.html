Three comments

Rosebuds In Summer

This is the 3rd tune on track 9 of La Lugh’s 1991 self-titled album. La Lugh were fiddler Gerry O‘Connor and flute player Eithne Ni Uallachain (with Desi Wilkinson joining them on 1987’s Cosa Gan Bhroga). Other musicians featured include Gilles Bigot (guitar), Siobhan Kennedy (flute, violin, step dancing) and Donal O’Connor (keyboards).

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on July 5th, 2022.

Re: Rosebuds In Summer

Not strictly correct with the chronology, Nigel. “Cosa Gan Bhroga” was very much a flute / fiddle duet album by Desi and Gerry, with Eithne as a guest vocalist. “La Lugh” came a few years later, and Desi wasn’t involved.
I’ll be forever gratful to Eithne ni Uallachain for steering me towards Sam Murray and his flutes - as did Murrough O’Kane of “Oige” - when both bands appeared within a few weeks of each other at the “Lemon Tree” in Aberdeen, must have been early / mid 1992. Eithne was a a great singer as well, and that was every bit as important as her function in “La Lugh” as her excellent flute playing.

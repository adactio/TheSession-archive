Castle Stalker {Strathspey}

Here are a few interesting facts about this Castle.

It is visible from the A828 road around mid-way between Oban and Glen Coe.

The name “Stalker” comes from the Gaelic Stalcaire, meaning “hunter” or “falconer”, which is appropriate for me, as, in my time, I have worked full time as both a Gamekeeper & Falconer.

In recent times the castle was brought to fame by the Monty Python team, appearing in their film Monty Python and the Holy Grail. It also appeared in the film Highlander: Endgame.

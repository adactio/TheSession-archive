Peter Croll’s

This is the second of a pair of tunes composed by my late friend Peter Croll. After some deliberation, I decided to catgorise this and its companion tune (see below) as a hornpipe, on account of its structure, although Peter himself played it with more the feel and tempo of a reel.

↳ Posted by CreadurMawnOrganig on November 25th, 2024.

Re: Peter Croll’s

Here is the source recording: https://youtu.be/cGhNFUIrAzk?t=129
The recording was made around 2016-17 and is presented here unedited, ‘warts and all’ (There is a false start to this tune, where he accidentally lapses back into the previous tune in the set). To my shame, I did not get round to learning these tunes until after Peter’s death. He was too modest a man to start his own tunes in a session, but perhaps, had I or somebody else learned them, they would have got more of an airing in his lifetime.

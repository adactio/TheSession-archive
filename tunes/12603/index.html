Three comments

The Borthwick

This reel was composed by champion fiddler Bob Hobkirk. The Borthwick Water is the river that runs along the bottom of the valley in which I live.
Version 1 I learnt from Iain Fraser and is easier on the fiddle than the original (version 2, taken from The Scots Fiddle Vol 2 by J Murray Neil) which requires G# notes to be played with the pinky on the D string. Version 3 is from the flute playing of Clare Lynas (of Espial).

↳ Posted by DonaldK on March 18th, 2013.

Re: The Borthwick

To clarify….

It is the major setting of this tune is attributed to Bob Hobkirk.
Bob would have known the minor setting, usually called “Borthwick Riddell’s Hornpipe” and named after the famous nineteenth century piper Borthwick Riddell, elder brother of the poet and songwriter Henry Scott Riddell.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on April 19th, 2024.

Re: The Borthwick

Matt Seattle has pointed out that in his source for the tune - the MS of ‘Kielder Jock’ Davison, the key signature has no sharps or flats.

Oddly, despite Borthwick Riddell being a celebrity in his own day, and with many contemporary and posthumous reerences to him in newspapers, there’s hardly any trace of him in 20th century folk memory. This tune, in a single MS, may be all there is. I hadn’t heard of him otherwise.

↳ Posted by John G piper on April 21st, 2024.

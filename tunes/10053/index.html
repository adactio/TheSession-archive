Two comments

From La Lugh’s first album. IIRC discovered by Gerry O’Connor in some obscure Northern Irish collection.
Undecided yet whether to call it a proper (albeit slowish and swingy) reel or rather an unusual hornpipe. (In any case it’s a “tune”.)

Goes well for me between Pottinger’s Reel and (for a laugh) The Tailor’s Twist in d minor.

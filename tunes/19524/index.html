Con Cronin’s

From the playing of Bryan O’Leary & Colm Guilfoyle.

According to Irish Tune Info (https://www.irishtune.info/tune/7013/ ), it appears in “Johnny O’Leary of Sliabh Luachra. Dance Music from the Cork-Kerry Border”.

Appelbo Ganglat

A Swedish tune that was played at a session that I attended yesterday evening at a pub in Redcliffe, Bristol. England.

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on May 16th, 2012.

A “walking tune” from Äppelbo in Dalarna. One of the most well known gånglåt ditties from the area - second only to Gärdebylåten, perhaps. You can even find the notes on the Äppelbo website:

http://www.appelbo.net/

The tune is from the playing of Ärtbergs-Kalle (1826–1917) from Ärtberget, which lies south of Äppelbo.

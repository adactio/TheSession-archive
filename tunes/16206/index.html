The Magician’s Daughter

I learnt this tune from someone in a session at Whitby Folk Festival in the 80’s and recorded on a demo with a band called “Get Reel” in Nottingham in 1995. In a set with Tam Lin, The Princes Feather and then The Magicians Daughter.

Seamus Begley’s No. 3

Another tune I got from John Carty whose source was Seamus Begley. It’s a typical Sliabh Luachra slide with no title so far. Not the most ideal tune for the banjo but would be nice on accordion and fiddle.

Seamus Begley’s, X:2

I happened upon this in a random search of the site and liked the general feel of the tune, but the first two bars were missing some beats. So this setting just stretches out the penultimate note of those bars to fit a 12/8 slide rhythm in a way that sounded slide-like.

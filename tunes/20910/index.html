The Possum On The Loo

Composed by Jack Meimaris

One day at Irish dance practice I snuck out with my accordion into the bathrooms and started playing tunes. I opened the door to one of the cubicles and to my shock, a possum was just sitting in there.

They didn’t seem to mind so I wrote this tune while watching it… and it watching me.

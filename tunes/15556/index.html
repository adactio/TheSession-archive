Two comments

Mrs. Jane Kennedy Of St. Anne’s, Methven

This is a great tune by Ali Hutton who wrote it for his Gran
Ali is part of The Treacherous Orchestra (the most treacherous one!) He also plays in the Old Blind Dogs and is of course seen playing occasionally with his pal Ross Ainslie.
This tune is often just called ‘Grans’ and is a slow March in ¾. I put it up here on The Session as a Mazurka in the hope it gives it a bit more umph!!!
Ali and Ross normally play in the key of Eb. It can be heard here on their bandcamp site.
https://rossandali.bandcamp.com/track/grans
From the awesome album ‘Symbiosis’ recently released by Ross & Ali

Simple Definition of Symbiosis
biology : the relationship between two different kinds of living things that live together and depend on each other
Go buy the album its fantastic!!!

Re: Mrs. Jane Kennedy Of St. Anne’s, Methven

This is a great tune, and I’m glad to see it posted here. The tune was taught last week by Gary West at Cairdeas
(Vermont Bellowspipe School).

One very slight quibble. This tune is not a mazurka, and needs no “umph”. The tune wants to be played in a dignified and magisterial way. A slow retreat, if you will.

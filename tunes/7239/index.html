Thanks, Sean

By Padraig Rynne. Flook play this on Haven with a definite swing. I personally can’t imagine it played straight, but who knows?
Flook, on this album at least, seem to have a penchant for playing tunes in difficult keys. I think it’s just because they can. All they have to do is switch whistles or flutes and capo the guitar. They don’t think about the poor fiddlers, do they?

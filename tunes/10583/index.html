Three comments

Source

A really cracking slip-jig written by Kathryn Tickell. It’s on the *What We Do* album she brought out with her talented younger brother Peter. Transcription is based on that recording.

Of course, she plays it originally in Fmaj, so for those of you wishing to play along with the recording:

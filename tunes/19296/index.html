A West Ocean

By Máire Breatnach, “Angels Candles”. Played on viola. Transpose to G to recreate noting shapes on fiddle. Triplets are played with light staccato. Go there - read The Brendan Voyage by Tim Severin who sailed across the west ocean in a leather boat, now drydocked in Co. Clare.

Pat Clark’s

This is one of my favorite Ed Reavy reels. This setting is from the tunebook “The Collected Compositions of Ed Reavy” - the notes in the back of the book say that Ed Reavy learned some of the basics of fiddle playing from this old Cavan friend [Pat Clark] who died in 1969.

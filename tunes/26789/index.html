Last Day At The Office, X:2

Nobias, if you have the sleeve notes, it would be helpful to add the composer’s name here.

The original Bmix setting (X:1) doesn’t work because the A naturals are wrong. So to hear the tune as it’s meant to be, I transposed it into A major. This also lands better on fiddle, and perhaps other session instruments.

For a simple transposition up to B major, copy my ABCs and paste them into Michael Eskin’s ABCs (https://michaeleskin.com/abctools/abctools.html) and hit the “transpose up” button twice.

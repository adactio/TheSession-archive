Planxty Safaigh

In Donal O’Sullivan’s book Carolan, The Life Times and Music of an Irish Harper,
tune number 174 in the section Tunes without Titles is this one.
In the notes he says that he got it from the book Collection of Ancient Irish Airs
by John Mulholland, Belfast 1810, where it had the title Planxty Safaigh.
But O’Sullivan says “as this title is unintelligible, the tune has been assigned to this section”.

This setting comes from the playing of Paddy O’Brien (Offaly) from his Tune Collection, volume 3,
but the version in O’Sullivan is very close to what Paddy plays. Both are in the key of C.
Note that the A-part has eight bars while the B-part has ten (which is fairly typical for O’Carolan).

Someone else has added the title Planxty Safaigh to https://thesession.org/tunes/3422 Carolan’s Cup,
but that doesn’t seem correct. Apparently, some people call this tune Carolan’s Fancy. That name doesn’t appear at all in O‘Sullivan, but maybe it got that name because it had no title in O’Sullivan or because people don’t know how to pronounce Safaigh ( Paddy says “safe-y”).

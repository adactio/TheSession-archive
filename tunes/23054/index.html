Re: Bad Boy

I don’t have the liner notes for Eastern Light, unfortunately.

As for Mrs. Belle’s Single #17, just checked Christina’s book, it is indeed quite similar to Bad Boy.

There are some deviations, with the equivalent Gmaj setting of Bad Boy being a bit higher for several of the bars (e.g., bars 3/4/7 of both The A & B Parts).

Six Yellow Goats (From Owen Spafford)

A polka taught by Owen Spafford at the Music in the Mountain week in Wales, August 2024.

I’ve missed out most of the ornaments and variations that we were taught, and made the guitar chords up roughly.

I think it’s also a variant of “I Have Two Yellow Goats” and the song “Riding on a Donkey”

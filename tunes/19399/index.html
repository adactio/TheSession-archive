Composer

Composed by Hamish Napier. According to https://www.hamishnapier.com/composer/ he composed tunes for Duncan Chisholm, Blazin’ Fiddles, Talisk, Coig, Imar, Simon Thoumire & Iain Carr, Alistair McCulloch, Pons Aelius, Man’s Ruin, Ian Muir Scottish Dance Band, Back of the Moon, Nae Plans and Mr McFall’s Chamber, amongst others.

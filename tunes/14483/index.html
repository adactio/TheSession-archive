The Sandwich Maker, X:2

I thought I’d contribute the original to this tune with the correct pipe ornamentation. I was inspired to write this tune while watching her friend from Ness making toasted sandwiches. I was greatly amused when, not realising that a sandwich maker toasts both sides simultaneously, he kept turning them periodically while cooking!

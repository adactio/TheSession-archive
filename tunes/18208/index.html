Daybreak Polka

From the playing of Sam Proctor.

According to Discogs, it was composed by Matt Cranitch:
https://www.discogs.com/Sliabh-Notes-Sliabh-Notes/release/5496775

See also:
https://www.irishtune.info/tune/2579/

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on August 9th, 2019.

Daybreak, X:2

From the playing of John Redmond.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on August 9th, 2019.

Daybreak, X:3

Whistle friendly setting. Some combination of X1 and X2, with a few small variations as well.

Prefer those low-B’s from X1 & X2, but, in this case the E’s work better in place of octave jumped B’s.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on August 9th, 2019.

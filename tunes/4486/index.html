Beautiful Gortree Story

I was wowed by this tune when I first heard it. I got a copy of the “Quiet Glen” very shortly after its release. I learned the tune straight away and then wound up going to Ireland. I caught up with its composer in Doolin. He was shocked when I threw the tune in the mix. I had to bow to the master when he insisted on NOT repeating the third part. We played the tune 4 times ’round and he never repeated that section. We talked about it afterwards. He insisted that that was the way it is supposed to go! So, totally the composer’s prerogative, eh?!

Beautiful Gortree, X:7

This is a transcription of John McCusker’s arrangement from his eponymous CD. I found Tommy Peoples’ version in his O Am Go hAm tunebook, but John’s treatment is significantly different, while still obviously remaining the same tune. I have included John’s trademark ornamentation (triplets, slurs, dotted bowing, etc) as I am trying to approach his deft bowing and uplifiting sound.

John plays his version at a comfortable 70 bpm (cut time, 2 beats per bar). Tommy’s notes describe this tune as “a happy, hopeful tune”, and that is what John has achieved.

Just a note, in Tommy People’s tunebook O Am Go hAm (From Time To Time), this is a 3-part reel, with repeats in each part.

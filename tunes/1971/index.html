Gan Ainm, X:2

This version is from Paddy O’Brien’s Tune Collection Volume 2
where it is reel #91. Paddy calls it The Forest Glade. I’ve misplaced the
notes that came with volume 2, so I don’t know where Paddy learned the tune,
but I wouldn’t be surprised if he got it from Larry Nugent.

I have a feeling that Paddy made up the name The Forest Glade, but that’s
a better name than Gan Ainm, so I’ll try to change the name to that.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on September 4th, 2024.

Re: The Forest Grove

I found Paddy’s notes to volume 2 of his collection (2011) and he writes this:
Some ten years ago, Fermanagh flute player Larry Nugent played this
tune in Kieran’s Irish Bar, and when he stopped playing I let an hour
elapse before I asked him to play it again. Then I noticed it later on one
of Larry’s CDa, but it had no name. When I finally began playing it, I
thought I’d baptize it with the above title so I could remember it.

So, I was correct in my guesses about where Paddy got it and how it got a name,
but incorrect on the name itself -- Forest Grove (not Forest Glade)

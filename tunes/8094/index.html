Two comments

The Brown One

I was told by sessioneer PiobaireMor that this tune was composed by Scottish fiddler/piper Iain MacFarlane.

The sheetmusic shows a barebones version. A transcription closer to the setting on the Dàimh CD is here:

X:1
T:The Golden Brown Chevette
R:Reel
M:C|
L: 1/8
C:Iain MacFarlane/arr. Angus MacKenzie
D:Dàimh - Moidart To Mabou
N:play parts A-B-C-D1-A-E-C-D2
K: Bmin
“A”
|:fB B/B/B dBGd|cA A/A/A aAgA|fB B/B/B dBGd|cfec dB B/B/B:|
“B”
dBGd BGdB|ecAe cAeA|aAgA fAeA|fAeA dAeA|
dBGd BGdB|ecAe cAeA|aAeA fAdA|cfec dB B/B/B|]
“C”
|:fB B/B/B AB B/B/B|GB B/B/B Bcdf|ec c/c/c Ac c/c/c|1 AaBa cada:|2 cfec dB B/B/B|]
“D”

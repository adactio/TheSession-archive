Billy On The Bodhrán

Tune by David Foley. I first heard/transcribed it off David Foley and Jack Smedley’s album “Time to Fly” released in 2020 and since then they’ve recorded it with Rura and Michael McGoldrick.

It’s been a while since I’ve transcribed a tune so feel free to edit or comment for corrections.

↳ Posted by Eli on February 25th, 2023.

Billy On The Bodhrán, X:2

Love, love, love this tune. I became obsessed with it again yesterday after listening to RURA’s latest release. I had a transcription sketched out somewhere in my notebooks and decided to finish it only to find Eli had already posted it a mere couple of weeks ago. I combined some of my transcription with theirs. Thanks, Eli!

Namely, I decided to transcribe it one octave higher to accomodate mid-staff reading, rearranged Eli’s version to include a pickup bar –and consequently shifted the phrasing and accents–, added the B/c/d flourish we flute players like to use –and abuse–, changed the notation for the legato, and modified the | a3 b2a | bars for an arguably more colorful | a2f bfa- |. Everything is based off of Foley and Smedley’s recording. Perhaps the newest RURA release adds some other variations? I have not listened to it all that carefully just yet.

↳ Posted by NielsJohnson on March 18th, 2023.

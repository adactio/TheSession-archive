Three comments

OK so this isn’t a Polka but it was the only way I could get the 2/4 time signature to come up. The tune is composed by Turlough O’Carolan and features on Garry Ó Briain’s album Carolan’s Dream.

I’ve entered it fairly unadorned but there are lots of opportunities for embellishment.

↳ Posted by cferrie on May 13th, 2011.

I see it is written as Mrs. Cole on some recordings (i.e. with the full stop) so it may not show up all recorded versions of it.

↳ Posted by cferrie on May 13th, 2011.

Also, apologies for the low notes but it was written by a harpist. You can just about hit them on a guitar, otherwise it’ll require a bit of transposing.

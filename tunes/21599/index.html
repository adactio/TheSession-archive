Re: John O’Reilly The Active

Which setting? Or all of them? :-)

I find that the more ornaments etc you put in the “abc”, the “playback” can sound quite erratic.
Sometimes it’s down to subtle errors in the abc which don’t appear in the actual sheet music. However, the play back system is probably not efficient enough either to cater for too much more than the basic notation.

↳ Posted by 🎻 Melody Player 🎻 on February 17th, 2022.

Re: John O’Reilly The Active

I agree that I’ve found a lot of the Clinton settings sound pretty bad on playback because of a combination of him liking notes high above the staff and liking ornamentation a lot. Plus for this one, including the eight-note rests in the middle of bars sounds godawful on the faux concertina which doesn’t have a concept of phrasing or breathing, so it just sounds like a dead stop.

He wrote for 19th-century classical flute players. Not for a fake reed midi. :)

The O’Neill setting does sound a bit better, due to being sparer.

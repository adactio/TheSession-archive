Re: Sweeny’s Planxty

Ann and Charlie Heymann recorded this tune. They said that it comes from The O‘Neill Manuscript, and that despite the name it probably is not an O’Carolan tune but rather an old Clan March. It certainly has more of a clan march structure than a planxty structure.

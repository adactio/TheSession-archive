Re: The Ravelled Hank Of Yarn

On their 78 piper and fiddler Mike Carney and James Morrison called this the Peeler’s Jacket. On his Gael-Linn 78 Willie Clancy called it Mhichíl Uí Cheithearnaigh (Michael Carney’s Reel). On later LPs Willie gave the Ravelled Hank title, which has became standard. O’Neill has a RHoY in Waifs and Strays of Gaelic Melody, but that tune is a version of Nellie Donovan, the Doon, etc.

Re: Emmett’s Hedgehog

Composed by Niall Vallely. The name in the title is Emmet Henry, bartender at Mona’s Bar in New York, who "told us a rather bizarre story involving hedgehogs and long-term survival in the wilds!
Somehow the story fitted in with Niall’s need for a tune title and his new jig became
Emmet’s Hedgehog…" (from Lunasa’s Notai tune book, 2007).

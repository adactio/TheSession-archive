Irene Of Cape Wrath Lodge

Irene hosted the “Far North Bagpipe and Fiddle Retreats” at Cape Wrath Lodge for a number of years, they would never have happened without Irene’s generosity, support and warm welcome at Cape Wrath and this tune was written by Ross Ainslie for Irene. Featured on “The Piper and the Maker 2” CD released in April 2023

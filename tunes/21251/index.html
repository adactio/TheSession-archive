Re: Learning To Jog

This tune was written by Nicholas Williams and appears in the Crowfoot Tunebook. Nicholas wrote that he came up with the tune while practicing the Hindustani raga “Jog” on the Indian flute.

↳ Posted by eileen o'grady on August 2nd, 2025.

Re: Learning To Jog

The mode of this tune is pentatonic; the 2nd and 6th degrees are omitted.
(The overall feeling is that of a mixolydian mode without 2nd and 6th)

I think this tune gains to be played entirely in the minor mode, which stylistically nudges it toward Appalachia.

(If played without flats and an with all f#, it is less melodically interesting but nudges towards Indonesia where several pentatonic modes can be found)

Rag Jog;

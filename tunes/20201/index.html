Eirikssons’ Jig

From the playing of Billy Sutton & Rob Gillard.

On Eirikssons’ Saga’s self-titled album.

Composer unknown.

Couldn’t find the tune elsewhere after several searches in Dmaj, Amaj, and, Gmaj.

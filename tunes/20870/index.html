Re: The Wind That Shakes The Piggery

Composed by Lindsay Ross. Commemorates the gales of 1975, when “Ian Stirling’s £250,000 piggery near Arbroath blew down.” (Dance Music of Lindsay Ross (Arbroath 1989)).

Ten comments

The Dirty Bee

There’s been a few tunes from Ross Ainslie and Jarlath Hendersons album so here’s another one. This was written by Ross and is probably his best known tune. It’s a must know tune in many Scottish sessions. As it’s a Scottish pipe tune I’ve notated it in A mix though the first part is minor and the second major with the g nat being a passing note.

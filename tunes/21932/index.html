Three comments

The Goat In The Cold

This bittersweet tune was written by my friend John Whitacre as a lament for his goat Eutzly, who was enduring the freezing weather as John watched warm and snug in his home. I’ve transcribed John’s harmony as well. John plays this as a fairly slow lament.

The Glasgow Wedding

This tune was written by Luthier Davy Stuart (https://thesession.org/links/index/search?name=Davy+Stuart+) in 1982 to celebrate my marriage to Sabine.

The joke then, of course, was that it was written for Dick Glasgow from Edinburgh!

I had the pleasure of hearing Davy play it with his group Crannachan, before he moved to start a new life in New Zealand.

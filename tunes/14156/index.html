The Exile’s Dream

Another reel composed by Patrick O’Leary in the late 1800s or early 1900s. His Grandson Des O’Leary gave me a copy of a tune book containing tunes composed by Patrick O’Leary. Des gave me this little booklet of tunes in the early 1990s but I misplaced it then found it again the other day and thought I would post the tunes here. I’m not sure if O’Neil added these tunes in his books, as Patrick O’Leary was good friends with O’Neil.

↳ Posted by JACKB on January 27th, 2015.

Inverpolly

Delightful waltz written by Ross Ainslie for a wedding and played on a Low E whistle. See https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--Vfa-3kx2A for the tune played in concert. BTW I have no clue how to tidily format a short bar at the start of the tune when played a second time because there is also an ending that only relates to the second time. Can anyone put me right please!! The video is also worth watching for when Ross loses his music at the halfway point and Ali giggles into his microphone.

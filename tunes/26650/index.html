The Capercaillie Rant

“The capercaillie (Scottish Gaelic), ‘The Horse of the Woods’, is an otherworldly creature. The male birds are as giant as they are cantankerous, famed for their clicks, pops and flutter jumps at the ‘lek’ during mating season. With Egyptian eye liner, a Japanese fan tail, velvet green neck plumage and a fierce hooked beak, the giant grouse fly through the Scots pinewood canopy with all the grace of a cannonball.”

From the album “The Woods” by Hamish Napier:
https://hamishnapier.bandcamp.com/track/the-capercaillie-rant-an-taghan

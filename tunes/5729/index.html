Rob Roy MacGregor

This expressive strathspey can be found in Capt Fraser’s ‘Airs and Melodies Peculiar to the Highlands of Scotland and the Isles’ (1874 edition), and it’s likely that mduthie_kcb took his transcription from that book (although the first bar should read the same as the third, not as given).

Ned Stapleton’s

This is a simplified version of James Kelly’s setting, which can be heard here:
https://thekellyfamily.bandcamp.com/track/raghadsa-is-mo-cheaiti-i-will-go-with-my-kitty-ned-stapleton-s-reel-slow-air-reel
, starting at 04:13.
It’s from the album “A Family Tradition: Traditional Irish Music from The Kelly Family (2023)”.
[Copying the notes from bandcamp; words by James Kelly:]
“…I play a composition by flute player and composer Ned Stapleton (1916-1973). Ned was a multi-instrumentalist and composer from County Wicklow who was good friends with my father. He lived in London for a while before returning home to Ireland. My father mentioned musical gatherings in Dublin that included Ned, as well as Sonny Brogan, Bill Harte, Tom Mulligan, and others. It’s an unusual and very beautiful reel.”

On my own website I’ve put some more transcriptions that are a bit closer to J Kelly’s setting:
https://goplayerjuggler.github.io/tuneTable/?l=default&ttId=620

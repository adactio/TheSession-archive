Three comments

The Wind And Rain

On Altan’s album Local Ground, after the song “The Wind and Rain”.
The sessionable key (and probably the original key) is Dmix but it appears on the album in Amix, meaning that the fiddles can play in octaves but the high fiddle is missing the top D out. See X:2 and X:3

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on February 17th, 2014.

Mmm that’s very tasty in Emix, iris eve. Well done!

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on February 22nd, 2014.

The Wind And Rain, X:5

This is just X:2 transposed up an octave with a few tweaks (bars B1 and B5, plus B3 and B4) mostly just to make it a bit easier to play along with the Altan setting for instruments that won’t go below D. If you prefer, you could retain the high d’, as X:2 would be if you just did a straightforward transposition of it, but it might easier for some of us to play it as fudged here.

↳ Posted by Rover on February 26th, 2021.

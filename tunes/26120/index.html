Re: Captain Charles Stewart’s Jigg

Captain - later Major - Charles Stewart (1710-1780) lived at Bohally House, near Loch Tummel in Perthshire. Composed by Malcolm MacDonald and published in his first collection, c1788, where he dedicated several tunes to the Stewart family, including Charles’s wife Clementina, and daughter Elizabeth.

The Papal Visit

This fun little tune was composed by Phil Murphy of Ballygow in Co. Wexford back in 1979, to mark the visit of Pope John Paul II to Ireland, which was a huge event at the time. The song ‘He’s got the whole world in his hands’ became a type of anthem for that Papal visit, and Phil cleverly built the hornpipe around the song melody. I reckon the ‘Ballygow polka’ is a similar job using the jig Captain White’s, but I don’t know if Phil came up with that or heard it somewhere else and rather than calling it Gan Ainm, called it The Ballygow. Phil was a wonderful character and a huge encouragement to a gang of us who were then teenagers learning Irish music. He was never critical, always offering encouragement or playing a little phrase for you to improve your setting of a tune or make it your own.

Three comments

This is the third tune of a polka set on Track 1 of Jackie Daly’s 1995 Gael-Linn recording, “Many’s A Wild Night.”

The other tunes in the set, “Many A Wild Night” and “All Along The River Bank” are already in the database here. Maybe this one is too:-)although a search using the various fragments of this transcription came up empty.

In the A part it’s nice to drop the first G in the fourth bar an octave, sometimes.

You a Irish Traditional Music pedal steel player? No problem. You can work your part out from this link.
http://members.accessbee.com/jkwasnik/midi/xpolka.mid

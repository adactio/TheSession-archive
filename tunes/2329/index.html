Jimmy Power recorded a version of Tom Potts‘ Rambling Pitchfork on his album ’Jimmy Power, Irish Fiddle Player‘ released by Topic Records in 1976. It’s a lovely version, and can be found on Spotify so give it a listen. He plays it followed by ’The Strayaway Child’

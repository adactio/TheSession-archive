Re: Jack Is Yet Alive

“Jack’s Alive” is in the Grier manuscripts which were compiled around the 1880s (and that setting is in Ceol Rince na h’Eireann Vol.4), and in a slightly different setting in Ryan’s Mammoth Collection of 1883.

As a jig it’s also in Ryan’s under the title “Dusty Bob” and in the Vickers MS as “Jack’s Alive” (Matt Seattle in his exhaustive notes to the Great Northern Tunebook cites several settings from the 18th century on).

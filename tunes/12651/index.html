Re: Ag Taisteal Na Blárnan

@ Ben Walker - my poke about indicates that it is an older air with the name “Staca an Mhargaidh” - “The Market Idler” of “The Market Stake”. It may have been used as the tune for several songs, including “CLIONA OF THE ROCK”:

Do ghuil Cliodhna trid na sgeulaibh,
Do ghuil Ughna a n-dúrlus Eile,
Do ghuil Aoife a rioghbhrog Fheidhlim,
A’s do ghuil Aoibhil síghbhean leith-chraig!

Do ghuil, go trúaigh, an Ruachtach caoille,
Do ghuil Aine a náras gréine,
Do ghuileadar Ocht nochtair air aonloch,
Do ghuileader ainnre an chairnn san t-sléibhe.

see https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Irish_Minstrelsy/Volume_2/Part_3/Notes

If this tune is the same as that in the Bunting Collection of Irish Folk Music and Songs (under the name “Staca an Mhargaidh”) then it couldn’t have been written by Liam O’Flynn. Did he claim it as his own?

Great tune that I first heard on the Cobblestone Sessions CD, played by Tommy Martin & Michelle O’Brien… and I’ve rarely heard anyone else playing it that hadn’t learnt it from the same recording: https://thesession.org/recordings/1485

The sleevenotes have this down as John Kelly’s - I’ve added the “Favourite” to distinguish it from the polka by the same name.

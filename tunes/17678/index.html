Two comments

Mrs. Rigg

Composed by Nathaniel Gow in the early 1800’s. Has a nice sound to it, particularly the B Part.

Found it in the Kohler’s Violin Repository (old Scottish tune book). Another version of it online has some swung notes in the B Part, see: https://www.tunearch.org/wiki/Mrs._Rigg. Listed as a jig/quadrille, so probably for dancing.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on January 25th, 2019.

Mrs. Rigg, X:2

A few minor variations in the A Part for a better flow, and a swung B Part.

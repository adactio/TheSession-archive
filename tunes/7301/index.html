“Jerry Holland’s Strathspey” ~ by Dan R. MacDonald

What better way to generate good thoughts than through music, a tune created with respect for Jerry Holland. This is partly spurred on from recent news and discussions. I hope I’ve done a reasonable job at transcribing this from Jerry’s own playing of this tune dedicated to him by another well known fiddler and composer, Dan R. MacDonald.

Here are the links to those discussions:

Discussion: Thoughts and prayers for Jerry Holland Cape Breton Fiddler
# Posted on May 22nd 2007 by Martin Donohoe
https://thesession.org/discussions/13806

& Jerry’s own website: http://www.jerryholland.com/

My Otis Tomas Mandolin

Otis Tomas is a fiddle maker in Cape Breton Island, Nova Scotia. Currently in the process of building me a 5 string fiddle. To celebrate I learned this tune dedicated to one of his instruments, and wrote a second tune to go along with it called “My Otis Tomas Violin”.

https://soundcloud.com/kheltonheadleywolf/my-otis-tomas-mandolin

Written by Jerry Holland.

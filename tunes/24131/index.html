Two comments

Códháirdeachais

This is the version from the Wikipedia article “The Dubliners (Album)”, transcribed by Paul Magnussen, rewritten into ABC by me. The name is an old spelling for the gaelic word of congratulations.
In the album, this piece, in combination with The Boyne Hunt, is mislabeled as The High Reel.
Due to its similarities to this piece, it is often incorrectly indicated as Sporting Nell.
In the Dubliners recording, I believe, Barney McKenna plays the first two lines as the A part, however the second time he plays the 3rd and 4th lines as the A part.

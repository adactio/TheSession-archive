Re: The Gandy Dancer.

I’ve read that a Gandy Dancer was a member of the “road crew’. The folks that straightened the train rails that had shifted under repeated use. Supposedly because the train companies purchased the shovels from a tool manufacturer named Gandy

↳ Posted by Ron May-Pumphrey on January 16th, 2026.

Re: The Gandy Dancer

There is a very classy Gandy Dancer Restaurant in my home town of Ann Arbor. The building used to be the city’s train station, but it was sold in 1970 and a smaller and much less grand station was build 100 yards down the track.

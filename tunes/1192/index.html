Tune type

The ends of section feel more like hornpipe endings or is that just me.
Donough

↳ Posted by Donough on December 10th, 2002.

One of the characteristics of Northumbrian and Scottish reels is that their structure is more hornpipe like in that they are based round arpeggios that go all over the place, and the section endings either contain 3 crotchets or similar. I think I mentioned this in the dicussion thread on “Northumbrian tunes” the other day.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on December 10th, 2002.

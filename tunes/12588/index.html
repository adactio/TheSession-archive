Dan Cleary’s Ireland

Transcribed from “The Beauties of Autumn” by the Longridge Céili Band from Co. Offaly. I think the tune is a composition of flute player John Brady; Dan Cleary was a fiddler from Offaly who has been credited with the composition of the reel “The Trip to Durrow”.

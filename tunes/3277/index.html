The Trip to Broadford

The Trip to Broadford was composed by a Clare accordion player, Kieran Donnellan, who played that tune for Josefine Marsh who gave it to Sharon Shanon… (I know that from the composer himself). Himself play another waltz before (which is not the Marino), both tunes in A (3 sharps).

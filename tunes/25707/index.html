Macdara’s Rambles, X:2

A slight variation based on feedback from Catherine McEvoy who wrote to say:

“Hi Jeremy, just a note to say that there are parts of the transcription of the jig ”Macdara’s Rambles" which are not quite correct…but im not surprised because of trying to transcribe from the flute is difficult. The main thing is that the last phrase AC#E should be if played on fiddle
On the low G string….it goes down below the low D on flute. As does the phrase in first part . Thanks."

Re: C.M. Hall

Nevertheless, Johnny, the tune is what a lot of people would call a 4/4 march (that is, a tune you could use for a Gay Gordons) and your setting has the correct note lengths to get that 4/4 feel. The second setting should be in 2/2 (or 4/2) to get the correct feel.

For future reference, the way to change the time signature to your chosen one is to put it in square brackets at the start of your first line of abc text. So, for example, if you want the first setting to appear in 2/4, your first line of text would start thus:
[M:2/4]c2 c/d/c/B/ Ac Ec/d/| etc.

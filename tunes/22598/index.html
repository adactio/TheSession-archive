Le Poisson Qui Tombe

aka The falling fish,
An original composition (I have contributed sufficient settings to other, existing tunes to post a composition [I hope!]).

Inspired by a little accident that occurred during a birthday dinner I had with a bunch of friends from our French language group in Singapore. An unfortunate slip up caused our main dish (on a Thai-style platter placed over an actual flame) to come tumbling down before we could even have a taste. A napkin or two may have caught fire and a perfectly good tilapia was wasted (Drop the bass! XD).

The restaurant was kind enough to replace said fish free of charge.

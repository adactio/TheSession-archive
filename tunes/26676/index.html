Killeagh, X:2

I don’t know this tune, and was confused by X:1 as the notation didn’t match the time signature, and it wasn’t possible to get a sense of the phrasing. (N.B. - Always best to hear a playback before posting, to check whether what’s written is actually what it should sound like).

I’ve had a go at putting the (slightly tweaked) sequence of notes into waltz timing and added what seem to me to be approp. chords. It sort of makes sense to me now as a nice enough tune, but will defer to anyone who’s familiar with it and can say if X:2 is an accurate setting.

Does anyone have any background intel on the tune?

Ye this was my first tune that was not a setting! I’ll write the words below so you can do sing along or whatever you like. Kingfishr is basically a kind of folk pop band that has became famous in this year. Hope you enjoy the tune!😁:

From the woods of Glenbower to the river Dissour
From the arms of my mother to the land ever pure
All I have to remember is the pride that I felt
’Round the Páirc Uí Chinnéide where the boys never knelt
They’d go raring and tearing and fighting for love
For the land they call Killeagh and the Lord up above
Killeagh, la, la la la la la, la la la la!
For the green and the white I adore
For the parish to last evermore
’95 came promotion, high up on the wing
And no-one up to senior, what a beautiful thing
In the darkest of hours, in the depths of despair
Let the Killeagh boys roar far to rattle the air
They’d go raring and tearing and fighting for love
For the land they call Killeagh and the Lord up above
Killeagh, la, la la la la la, la la la la!
For the green and the white I adore
For the parish to last evermore
When my time’s at an ending
When my days are no more
Bury me with my hurley by the river Dissour

The Lass On The Strand

Paul Brock hunted this hornpipe out of Allan’s “Irish Fiddler” which was first published in Glasgow early, last century & was edited by Hugh McDermott.

↳ Posted by Ptarmigan on November 26th, 2005.

Sounds a bit like the Sligo Fancy.

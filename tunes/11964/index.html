Three comments

Tommy Maguire’s #4

This is Offaly box player Tommy Maguire’s composition. It’s listed as #4 in Kiss Me Kate album. James Carty recorded it as Bill Harte’s a few years ago.

↳ Posted by slainte on June 11th, 2012.

In Memory Of McKeigue

From the playing of John Bowe. I seem to recollect that this is his own name on the tune, for a good friend. (So it may exist elsewhere under a different guise.)

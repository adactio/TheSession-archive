Three comments

“The Turoe Stone Collection: Traditional Irish Flute Solos” (Vol. 1)

Original compositions by Vincent Broderick
Waltons Publishing, 1990
ISBN: 1-85720-061-6

Page 19: “The Loughrea Polka”

Unfortunately I haven’t the recording or I’d probably have transcribed from that and added my own take on it here in the comments…

This continues my musical celebration of the live of Vincent Broderick, man, character, husband, father, friend, wordsmith, artist, teacher ~ and who also played the whistle and flute and composed tunes. This is one of my favourites of his, a simple little polka. I confess I play it somewhat differently, from the start I kept returning to the last 4 bars of the A-part of the tune, as I’ve given it here, with the second ending. Here, for balance, is the same tune without that 2nd ending, but with some other possibilities ~

X: 2
T: Loughrea, The
M: 2/4
L: 1/8
R: polka
K: Gmaj
|: B/A/ |\
GD G>A | Bd ed | eg/e/ dB | AG E2 |
GD GG/A/ | B/c/d ed | e/f/g/e/ dB | AG G :|
|: A |\
Bd ed | gd/g/ ed | eg/e/ dB | A/B/A/G/ ED |
B/c/d e2 | g/a/g/f/ ed | eg dc/B/ | AG G :|

It should be obvious, I love this jolly little polka…

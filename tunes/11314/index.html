Eight comments

S: Julia Clifford (Murphy) ~ hence “Julia Clifford’s”

This is also know as Johnny O’Leary’s or Johnny Leary’s, but putting that down in the alternate titles section would swamp this entry with more unrelated links to recordings.

I’m surprised this isn’t here, but then, it is possible it was removed in the past when some folks left the site and all their contributions and comments went with them, as per their request, to leave completely…

The transcription given is from the playing of Julia Clifford, minus the fine details, ornamentation, etc. However, Julia starts it on the first bar so ~

|: f>a ge | ff/a/ ge | ~

And of the one recording still available she pairs this with ~

“Bill the Weaver”
https://thesession.org/tunes/1914

This pair available on the following recordings, and all 3 track given below are of the same Topic recording:

LP: “Music from Sliabh Luachra Vol. 3: The Humours of Lisheen - John and Julia Clifford/Murphy”
Topic 12TS311 - Ossian: OSS 14
https://thesession.org/recordings/1433
17.) Julia Clifford’s Polka / Bill the Weaver

CD: “The Rushy Mountain: Classic Music From Sliabh Luachra 1952-77”
https://thesession.org/recordings/1274
14.) Julia Clifford’s Polka / Bill the Weaver

CD: “Round the House and Mind the Dresser: Irish County-House Dance Music”
https://thesession.org/recordings/317

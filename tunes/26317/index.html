Dancing In The Barn Schottische

“Dancing in the Barn” is a famous tune that originated as a popular piece of parlor music and accompanying dance in the late 1800’s. Composed by Tom Turner in 1878.
I’ve never heard about it before, but ran over it on: https://www.folktunefinder.com/
I have made some changes, including many errors in the bars, and also made some corrections so that it is easier to play, especially the last part.
I think it’s a quite funny tune!

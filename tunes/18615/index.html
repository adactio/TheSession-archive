Three comments

Paula’s Birthday

I wrote this for our great friend, Paula Carroll, whom you might know through her Wednesday West Wind show on ClareFM. Turns out we were only born a few days apart, which we were surprised to learn when I was in Ireland for my 50th birthday. So this tune is to celebrate Paula, her birthday where we sipped champagne with her family while staring out at the Western coastline of Clare, and our continued friendship! This tune was recorded on my second album, titled Some Assembly Required. https://thesession.org/recordings/6687

↳ Posted by Reverend on October 16th, 2019.

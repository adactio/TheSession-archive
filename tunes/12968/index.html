Silver Belle

This is from a recording by Canadian fiddler Patti Lamoureux which you can hear on her web-site, http://www.pattilamoureux.com/fr_home.cfm. She plays it as the second tune in a medley preceded by Golden Slippers in the key of A major.

I haven’t found much information about this tune, which resembles the march-like Canadian tune Maple Sugar, but it would be great played at a contra dance.

Silver Bell(e), X:2

This setting of “Silver Bell” (in G & C) is based on the 1910 composition in F and Bb by Percy Wenrich (words by Edward Madden). A Western Swing version (called “Silver Bells”) was popularized by Bob Wills and others. Some fiddlers like to play it transposed up to D in both parts and adding embellishments on the long notes. This version was created from sheet music using EasyABC.

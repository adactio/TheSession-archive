I’d actually been looking after reading the discussion, but chasing up recordings of Seamus Ennis. After following the link and giving this tune a go ~ memories started bubbling up, but not for just two parts. I made an initial search, but haven’t given up. If I find those other parts I’ll add them later, or, they might already be here on site somewhere? For now, here’s simplifying some of where these first two parts took me, and, for now, avoiding what followed… I’ve enjoyed reconnecting, so I thought I’d continue the discussion with a transcription… 8-)

The Chromatica

This is a tune I learnt from Catherine Ward’s fiddle playing in Cosham, Hampshire, but it was originally written for the concertina by Mart Bradley in Bognor Regis, Sussex.
Those accidentals look weird on paper but they are correct, not a notation error!

↳ Posted by Ralph Wellington on March 6th, 2018.

Re: The Chromatica

This should be in every keyless flute players’ tune book and periodically practiced as an etude. What a wonderful study in cross fingering and half-holing it is. Too few keyless players take chromatics seriously, thinking it’s fool’s errand, but it can be achieved by all players with a good ear for intonation. This is a great chromatic study for an intermediate flute or whistle player.

Two comments

C: Paul Gitlitz 1982

As per his comment on the other tune called “Halloween” that I submitted here: https://thesession.org/tunes/11975

His comment:
I see my album is noted as a source for this tune, but I also composed a tune of the same name in the same year. Here’s the abc:
T:Halloween
M:C|
L:1/8
Q:112
C:© Paul Gitlitz 1982
R:Reel Bluegrass
B:Giblitz Fancy, 141 Brand New Old Time Fiddle Tunes Vol. 2
N:Originally a song I wrote about a pony express rider out on N:the wrong night.
Played in 2nd position on the fiddle.
D:“To Have and To Hold” available @ www.glitchless.net

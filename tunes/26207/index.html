Bowmore, X:2

A Scottish bagpipe reel. My setting comes from William Ross’s Collection of Pipe Music (1869). The Scots group The Whistlebinkies recorded a tune on their 1981 “3” album which they called “The Bowmore Reel” but it is in fact “Miss Girdle” https://thesession.org/tunes/2417 - the two tunes are similar, but not the same.

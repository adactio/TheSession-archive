Sources

This tune was first published in Gunn’s Collection, 1848, as a march. I have it set as a jig close to how Finlay MacDonald plays it on his eponymous CD. He states in the liner notes that he got the tune from Gunn’s.

Paul K. MacNeil (namesake of Paul K’s Strathspey) has it as below on his CD.

↳ Posted by gian marco on May 18th, 2020.

Girls Have You Seen George?, X:2

This is a completely different setting. It’s from John & William Neal’s ‘A Collection of the Most Celebrated Irish Tunes’, Dublin, 1724, pp. 18-19. Its title there is ‘Challeeny vacca shu sheorshe’; in modern Irish: ‘A Chailíní, an bhFaca Sibh Seóirse?’ It’s really this long, with all these variations and with each of the 6 parts being repeated. Exactly the same version was published in John Mulholland’s ‘A Collection of Ancient Irish Airs’, Belfast, 1810, pp. 36-39, under the title ‘A Cailinidh abfacaidh siobh Seoirse. Girls have you seen George’.

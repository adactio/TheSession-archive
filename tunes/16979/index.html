The Duke Of Edinburgh

I’ve played this tune for years but couldn’t remember the name of it. I asked on the discussion forum if anyone recognised it and CreadurMawnOrganig came up trumps. This first setting, X:1, is from The William Ross Collection of 1869.

↳ Posted by bagpie on May 7th, 2018.

The Duke Of Edinburgh, X:2

This second setting is from A Collection of Bagpipe Music by James Bett, 1899. Almost identical to the first but with fewer triplets and no repeats.

↳ Posted by bagpie on May 7th, 2018.

The Duke Of Edinburgh, X:5

X:4 above is very similar to the way Fin’s father Hamish played it on his recording Stepping On the Bridge (1994); he got it from the William Ross collection.

X:5 is not radically different from the other settings, but there are a couple of alternative ways in it. It’s transcribed from the Kirriemuir fiddler Angus Cameron from his 1977 album Strings to the Bow. Other notable Scottish fiddlers who have recorded this tune include Pibroch MacKenzie and John Ellis.

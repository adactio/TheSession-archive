Peadar O’Riada to the rescue!

This is the opening track of Peadar O‘Riada’s lataest CD “Triur Sa Draighean” , a project made up exclusively of compositions of the man himself. He is joined on the recording by fiddlers Martin Hayes & Caoimhin O’Raghallaigh, and the results are astonishing. It seems as if everything I hear Mr. O’Raghallaigh involved in has an element of magic to it; there simply aren’t words to describe the depth of his playing. And if you’re not a fan of what Martin Hayes does on his own recordings, fear not; he has “turned down” the “Martin Hayes-ness” & plays with the other musicians with great taste & incredible empathy. If you are a fan of Mr. Hayes, you’ll most likely enjoy it anyway! And yes, the first thing I heard when playing this track was the first notes appearing in “Good Morning To Your Nightcap”; however, the rest of the tune distinguishes itself as an entirely different ball of wax after that. The most amazing thing that Peadar does as a composer is that he can “borrow” from existing tunes to craft brand new ones (that somehow sound “old”- neat trick!). I will be posting more of these amazing compositions as time allows…for now, enjoy!

↳ Posted by jaychoons on June 13th, 2010.

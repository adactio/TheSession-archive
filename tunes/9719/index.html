Re: Trout In The Bath

A modified version of the very popular Morrison’s Jig which has been misnamed as Trout In The Bath on youtube. The actual name given by the Corrs to their tune is Joy Of Life. See https://thesession.org/discussions/34311?newcomment=863015#comment863015

↳ Posted by Phil Wilson on February 15th, 2019.

Re: Trout In The Bath

Some similarity to Morrisons in the A part but it’s Whelan’s Jig (or Trout In The Bath) not Morrisons. The Corr’s play Whelan’s after The Carraroe Jig (aka The Joy of Life).

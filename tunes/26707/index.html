The Hard Nut

Ronan Le Bars learned the tune from the fiddle player Paul Flynn from Mullaghbawn (Co. Armagh). After searching in vain for its name, Paul allowed them to call it ‘The Hard Nut’ https://youtu.be/HGsCGOzjd0c?si=w2VKSOE7TPRwizcy&t=90

Pancake Tuesday

A distinctive jig composed by Mick McAuley and played with lots of drive by Solas. It’s the first tune in the “Wiggly Jigs” set on their ‘Another Day’ CD.

You can hear it at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_oEfRDOowNw&t=122s

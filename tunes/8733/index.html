Rathkeale Hunt

Refer also to:

http://www.ibiblio.org/fiddlers/RAO_RAZ.htm
“A 3-part rendering of the Piper’s Lass/Rolling on the Ryegrass tune family.” Check this reference that names other similar tunes known that are/may be related to this tune.

S: Cole (1000 Fiddle Tunes), 1940.

This tune is transcribed herein in 4/4 time rather than the 2/4 time that appears in Cole, 1940, where it is also printed as a 24 bar tune with no repeats. I would suspect players would treat this as a 3-part tune, repeating each 8-bar part.

Rathkeale lies about a third of the way between Cork and Dublin, in Co. Limerick. Slater’s Directory of Ireland provides an interesting look at this market town/parish in 1846:
http://www.celticcousins.net/ireland/1846rathkeale.htm

One comment

“Keogh’s” is a great reel composed by Billy McComiskey, the Baltimore-Washington (US) area box guru. It is the opening tune on Billy’s recent CD “Outside the Box.” The tune is named after the family who owns a group of cabins in East Durham (the Irish Catskills) where Billy and his family have been staying during Irish Week.

Just Cook For Yourself

By Paul Cranford, in his wonderful Lighthouse Collection. One of those tunes that’s lovely to just sit and play by yourself, whether or not anyone’s listening. I’ve been playing it with Mrs Forbes, Leith, the well known Scott Skinner reel in B flat. Inspired to post by Jerome’s current discussion on flat key tunes for the fiddle.

My copy of the Lighthouse Collection is currently, well, it’s somewhere in my house, so this music is a copy of the copy I made of it for working purposes - I don’t remember making any deliberate changes but errors may have crept in, so any notes welcome!

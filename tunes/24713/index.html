Pat Fitz’s First Visit To Cullenstown

This Hornpipe was composed by All-Ireland winner Pat Fitzpatrick. He had recently been a regular visitor down Bannow way, helping out with the Fleadh preparations of the local competition céilí band. He decided to give the landmark Cullenstown beach a look-see, and this tune was the result.

The Amorous Lover, X:4

This is a Highland bagpipe dance tune collected by Donald MacDonald and published in 1822. However, his score is an odd hybrid setting intended to make the tune more palatable for piano players. The Gaelic name of the tune is C’uin a thig thu ars am Bodach which means, When will you come said the Old Man. How it got the English name is not known. This X:4 is the version I remember which drops to a low G in the second part, 1st measure and resolves back to A in the 3rd measure.

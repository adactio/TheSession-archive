The Stack Of Rye, X:5

Transposed to D dorian, the idea here being to put most of the tune in the right hand on Anglo concertina, so the left can add harmonic style accompaniment. Played on a G/D instrument as if on a C/G, the key is back to traditional A dorian. This chord selection sounds pretty rich on the reeds; don’t know what a guitarist would make of it.

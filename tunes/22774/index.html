Golden Anniversary

Heard on Beòlach’s album “Variations” as well as a live performance they gave at Celtic Colours Festival, in Cape Breton; they performed a modified set from the album. “Prayerful Hymn (air), Trad. Strathspey (gan ainm), Golden Anniversary (strath.), The Way to Mull River, John Morrison of Assynt House, Pibroch O’Dhonal Dhu (reels)” …a fantastic and long set. Natalie MacMaster joins on stage as well.

Going To Mass Last Sunday

I got this rocker off fiddler Karen Ryan’s album “The Coast Road”. The first part is similar to that of Frank McCollum’s jig “Chapel Bell” (https://thesession.org/tunes/5405), but the second is different. I know nothing else about the tune, not even a composer. If anyone else does, I’d be glad to hear it!

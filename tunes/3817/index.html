Conlágh’s Big Day

A Brian Finnegan composition off Flook’s “Rubai”. I can’t decide what to think about this tune. I used to think it sounded like a jingle for a supermarket advert, and so I loathed it. But I’ve heard it played so often in our session that the damn thing got stuck in my head and wouldn’t go away, so I just learnt it. I’m sure there are a lot of people out there who want the dots for it as it’s quite difficult to pick up by ear off the CD. It’s such a weird tune, and quite difficult to play too.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on November 5th, 2004.

ed/2 =c/2B/2 A/2 | BD B2 =cB A2 | GA BG D2 D2 | EG =ce dB | GA B D>A3D> G2-G2|]

First a few changes were made, after doing the breakdown of bars above, with /2 is now pretty much obsolete, for /2 just a forward slash is the accepted norm now ~ / ~ so that change was made, not really an important one. Next, another small note about ABC notation, if K: GMaj, then all Cs are natural and the natural sign (=) is not required, and so those too were removed from the above transcription.

It became quickly obvious that it was not a waltz, mazurka, varsovienne, or in 3/4 time, not as it stood… However, the basic melody for this could easily be adjusted to become a waltz, see below…

The air ~ here goes, everything now divisible by 4 and 8, the usual structure for phrases and parts, except for those held notes in the last few bars which I take was the final ending of the melody, with fermatas…

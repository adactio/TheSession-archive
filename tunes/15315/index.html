Three comments

Seamus O Kane’s

I composed this tune for Seamus on his 60th birthday and we then recorded it along with Jackie Donnan’s on the Armagh Piper’s Club CD, recorded to commemorate their 40 years. Seamus absolutely loves mazurkas. A tune of this name was submitted here by someone else 4 years ago, but needed further work.

↳ Posted by Aoibheann Devlin on March 28th, 2016.

Seamus O Kane’s, X:2

Apologies - the previous notation wasn’t the correct rhythm. I find it very difficult using abc notation. I’m hoping I have it correct this time. Please keep in mind that this is only the bones of the tune. None of the ornamentation is included. For this you’re best listening to a recording or putting in your own as you feel it.

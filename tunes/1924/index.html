Eddie Duffy’s Barndance

This is the second of the two Eddie Duffy barndances on the first track of Ben Lennon’s “Dog Big and Dog Little”. The first is played just melody, but as the second tune starts listen out for Fr Seamus Quinn’s wonderful and very effective piano accompaniment.

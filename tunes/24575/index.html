Come back to E-rin Ma-vor-neen, Ma-vor-neen; Come Back A-roon, to the land of my birth__ Come with the sham-rocks and Spring-time Ma-vor-neen, And it’s Kil-lar-ney Shal Ring With our Nirth, Sure, when we lent ye to beau-ti-ful eng-land, Lit-tle we thought of the lone win-ter days, Lit-tle we thought of the bush of the star shine O-ver the moun-tain the bluffs and the brays!.
Chorus: Come back to E-rin Ma-vor-neen, Ma-vor-neen; Come Back A-roon, to the land of my birth__ Come back to E-rin Ma-vor-neen, Ma-vor-neen, And _its Kil-lar-ney shall ring with our nirth.
Verse 2
O-ver the green sea, Ma-vor-neen, Ma-vor-neen, Long Shon the white sail that bore thee a-way;__ Ri-ding the white waves that fair Sum-mer morn-in‘ Just like a May-flow’r a float on the bay, O, but my heart sank when clouds came be-tween us, Like a grey cur-tain the rain fall-ing down, Hid from my sad eyes, the path o’er the o-cean Far, far a-way where my Col-leen had flown.
Chorus
Verse 3
O mat the ang-les O wa-kin and slep-in‘ Watch o’re my bird in the land far a-way__ And it’s my pray’rs will con-sign to their keep-in‘ Care o’ my jew-el by night and by day. When b y the fire-side I watch the bright em-bers, Then all my heart flies to En-gland and thee, Cra-vin‘ to know if my dar-lin’ re-mem-bers, Or if her thoughts may be cross-in’ to me.

Re: Come Back To Erin

I have a few more recordings of this tune:

Bob Smith’s Ideal Band
Dublin Concert Orchestra
Erin’s Isle Ballroom Orchestra
Jackie Roche and His Orchestra
John O’Neill and His Irish Dance Band
Malcolm Kennedy
Maureen O’Hara
Richard Hayward & Sean Maguire

They all play it in waltz time; it was published as such originally: https://www.loc.gov/resource/music.mussm-sm1870-05444/?sp=2 Odd that O’Neill had it in 4/4. Direction for the player is “Slowly” too, so this isn’t meant to be a march there, either. Lovely tune.

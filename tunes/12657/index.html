The Clare, X:2

On his 2002 album 2002 Lios A’Cheoil, Tom Barrett recorded this under the title “The Grand Ould Man.” The sleeve notes say, “The origin of this tune is uncertain but it was popular in the 1920s in North Kerry.” It’s not played quite as “dotted” as the soundfile suggests, and at times it sound quite straight.

Calum -

Thanks for posting this. It’s indeed a brilliant tune. Hamish introduced this tune at the Vermont Bellowspipe School (Cairdeas) in 1995, and it quickly became the tune of the week. I have a great memory of the tune being played, at a stately pace and many times through, by about 25+ folks on small-pipes, whistle and fiddle on the last evening of the School as a benediction to the week.

Horsburgh Castle, X:2

I’m another who loves this tune, but I play a (very) slightly different setting. Horsburgh Castle is a ruin near Peebles, and dates from the 16th century. Ian Hardie (1952-2012) was a fiddler who performed with the groups Chorda and Jock Tamson’s Bairns, and the Occasionals ceilidh band.

(Edit: I see Calum’s original abcs match my setting - sorry, Calum.)

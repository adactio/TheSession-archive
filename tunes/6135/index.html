Re: The Killimor

@didier, Seán Ryan (1919-1985) DID compose the Killimor jig. By 1962 he had won the All-Ireland Fiddle at Fleadh Cheoil na hÉireann twice, and his reputation as a composer had spread by 1956. He won the competition for newly composed pieces at the All Ireland three times in a row in the 60s. Incidentally, he was a cousin of Paddy O’Brien (Nenagh) and friend of Father PJ Kelly, both prolific composers. (Info from Seán Ryan double CD and “The Hidden Ireland” a book of his compositions.)
You can hear the jig played on Connemara Ceilidh (https://thesession.org/discussions/50652) which was broadcast in 1962. So the jig had reached Connacht by then.

Eight comments

Joey Clement’s

Another great single from Newfoundland. I learned this one from Daniel Payne’s album Chain and one of Christina Smith’s tune books. Christina recorded a different version of it, and the Dardanelles recorded yet another. It’s also sort of single version of the double “Sydney Pittman’s” that Rufus Guinchard played.

Danny Mills: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xfew01pq-98

↳ Posted by Sol Foster on February 3rd, 2013.

The second setting is “Boyd’s Cove Single #1” from the Dardanelles’ self-titled first album. Aaron Collis collected it in Boyd’s Cove, Newfoundland.

The Dardanelles: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-S3Z3eX5So

Joey Clement’s, X:5

This is how I like playing the tune.

Just a few tweaks on X1 mostly.

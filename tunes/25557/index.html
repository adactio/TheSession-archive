The Trip To Moreda

Written by Gordon Duncan.

Gordon visited Moreda (Asturias), many years ago, to perform at the festival “Andando pelos Pueblos”. He then spent several hours in a “chigre” (Asturian bar), with some tonada* singers. Apparently, he enjoyed it very much and named this tune after the town.

https://youtu.be/uFvMRDabvs0

* https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonada

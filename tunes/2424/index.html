Re: The Man Who Died And Rose Again

The jig that died and rose again?

Apart from a match at bar 4, it is difficult to see or hear any similarity between the 2 versions put forward here (Williamson v. O’Neill).

Perhaps X:1’s 2nd part isn’t missing after all. There are a number of songs (in jig time, etc) which simply function on the lyric contrast between chorus and verse, using the same melody for both. (In that case -if that be the case- only words are missing).

Alternatively, one can obtain a 2-part jig from X:1, not by creating an extra melodic line but by simply adding repeat signs every 4 bars: all that’s required now to finish the tune and turn are cadencial variations -for contrast. Job done.

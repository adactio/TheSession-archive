“The Silver Bell Schottische” ~ an early source & transcript, 1855

Library of Congress: Music Division
Music for the Nation: American Shee Music 1820 - 1885
http://memory.loc.gov/ammem/mussmhtml/mussmhome.html

Search ~ enter “Silver Bell Schottische”
http://memory.loc.gov/ammem/mussmquery.html

Claimed on the sheet to have been composed by one Augustus Cull, 1855… However, such things are often actually ‘arrangements’, and it was common to try and lay claim on tunes that had been knocking around for awhile, something that continues to this day… ‘copyright’…

Here is a simple ABC transcript of this, minus the ‘intro’ and ‘trio’:

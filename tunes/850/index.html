Three comments

The Smoky Bar

I put this tune together on the banjo and it seems to sound well and suit that instrument - plenty of rolls on open strings.

↳ Posted by gortna on July 20th, 2002.

Smokey Bar

Very similar to “The Galway Rambler” but in the key of D. Not bad though, I like that it has a traditional sound to it.

↳ Posted by b.maloney on July 20th, 2002.

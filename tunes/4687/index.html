Five comments

Ril Johnny Phadraig Pheter

This is a bit full of notes, but a lovely tune, so I have to play it while my fingers are still fresh!

Composed by Johnny Óg Connolly in honour of his father.

Can be found on the album by the same man (with Brian McGrath) ‘Dreaming Up the Tunes’, where he plays it followed by the New Mown Meadow. Nice set.

Ril Johnny Phadraig Pheter, X:2

This setting was recently introduced to our Friday session, gleaned from a video of Teada. They play it in F, with slightly fewer notes than the first setting above. X:2 is thus not a straight transposition into the lower key, but the way I hear Teada playing it first time through on the source material.

This is the third in the Teada set, after Greta’s Favourite and Grist For The Mill.

It’s a work in progress for me, but should be up to speed in a couple of weeks…

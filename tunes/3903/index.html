Costumi Siciliane

Though not Irish, this is a lovely little tune got from Mary Custy and Quentin Cooper and is on their album ‘Barr Tra’. The distinct Italian flavour with its nuances between flats, sharps and natural of notes gives a nice change in a session full of reels

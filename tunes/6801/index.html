Four comments

Sister Jean

This is a slow jig from Shetland. The version I’ve put down here is from the Blazing Fiddles Album, Fire On! and is part of the Greenland Set.

A lovely relaxed tune with interesting key changes!

Sister Jean, X:2

This unusual 6/8 tune is from Unst in the Shetland Islands. It was taught by Abby Newton at the Boston Harbor Scottish Fiddle School, August 2007, along with the slow reel Kinrara (https://thesession.org/tunes/14860#setting33287).
The chords are my take on it.

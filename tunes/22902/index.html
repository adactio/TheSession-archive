Three comments

Dance Of The Wolves

First tune of the Ceol Cuàine set on Hugues de Courson’s album O’Stravaganza.

See here:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k4Dlp0jzZhE

The musicians for the track are: Noleg Caisey (fiddle), Emer Mayock (flute, tin whistle, uilleann pipes), Youenn Le Berre (low whistle), Donal Siggins (bouzouki, mandolin).

The entire set is listed as “traditional”, arranged by Hugues de Courson & Youenn Le Berre.

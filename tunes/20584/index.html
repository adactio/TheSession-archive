The Kilkenny Ayre

This gorgeous slow air was written by Louey Hesterman of Victoria, Australia and is a favourite session tune of us at the Lake School in Koroit. The Kilkenny Ayre should be played with a similar feel to that of Inisheer, with lots of rubato and a broad tone. The setting I have transcribed here is as Louey, and Joeseph and Dan Bourke play it on Louey Hesterman’s album ‘Heart and Soul’ (minus the key change) . The form and melody can very easily be adjusted to allow for instrument range. The original key of F major is not so great for D whistles and various other instruments, so I have added an identical second setting transposed a tone up, in G major.

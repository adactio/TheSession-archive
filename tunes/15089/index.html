Two comments

Well May Charlie Wear The Crown

This is a slow air and the notation says to play it solemnly slow. Jeremy Kittel does a really dark and broody version of this tune, that has become popular recently at our session.

I found a version in the Captain S Fraser of Knockie’s Highland Collection (new version) 1874 that I present here. The title page claims that the tunes were “Chiefly acquired during the interesting Period from 1715 to 1745,” so this dates from before the 45 rising in Scotland.

↳ Posted by JoJofidhlear on December 25th, 2015.

Montgomerie’s Maggot

This is an arrangement of mine which I published in The Border Bagpipe Book in 1993, now out of print. It came up as a topic on the bobdunsire.com piping forum when people started requesting the sheet music after the tune had been played on a youtube clip.

Though the basic tune is traditional (parts 1,2 and 7) the arrangement is ©Matt Seattle. It’s not everyone’s cup of tea, as you do need some free attention to keep the overall pattern in mind, but for those who enjoy a challenge… feel free to play it etc. but for commercial recordings please give due credit.

NB The syncopation is in the original, as is the fast note cluster near the end.

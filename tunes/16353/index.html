Three A’s Hornpipe

Here is one of the most challenging tune I have ever read… According to the source (Köhler’s Violin Repository, Book 2, 1885, p. 151), this hornpipe has been “composed and played by Walter Radford of Manchester, at Ben Lang’s Casino, but has never been played since.”
I added a repeat sign at the beginning of the 1st bar, right after the initial anacrusis.
Enjoy !

Gan Ainm

This is the second of a pair of unusual jigs on an old cassette recording of the fiddler and well-known singer the late Jim Smith of Clongeen in Wexford. It’s from the second of two recordings of him that were submitted many years ago to the ITMA. Anecdotally, Jim Smith may have been Phil Murphy’s source for the relatively well-known Raheen reel - Jim plays a version of The Raheen elsewhere on the recording. I haven’t found a match for this particular tune so far, I’m hoping someone on thesession.org might have some light to shed on it.

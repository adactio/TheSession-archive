George Ross’

This is a reel from the 1956/57 Comhaltas archive recording of Wexford champion accordionist George Ross made by Fr. Lyons. Fr. Lyons introduces George playing it as a reel of ‘his own composition’. I vaguely remember George playing this tune very occasionally back in the 1970s. As far as I know, this is the only full tune that George Ross composed. He did however ‘add’ some additional parts to the other tunes, including, it is claimed ,the fourth part of ‘The Mason’s Apron’ back in the early 1950s possibly before Sean Maguire added other additional parts.

Thanks for reminding me to add the probable title as suggested by George’s nephew, the piper, Brendan Wade. He confirmed that George had most certainly composed a tune called ‘An Inch from Gorey’ and that it was ‘an out-and-out box tune’. Since this seems to be the only potential, which was hidden in the old CCE archive, I think it’s a good shot. Plus a very clever title.

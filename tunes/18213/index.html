Dancing With Mrs. White

From the playing of Great Big Sea, who put it on their album as a tribute to Minnie White.

They chord the first few notes of the first/fifth bars in the B Part, as “[A3/ F3/][B/ G/] [A F][F D]”.

Newfoundland single. Bears some resemblance to Tripping To The Well (https://thesession.org/tunes/4158#setting21326 ) in the A Part.

Fairly sure it’s either one of GBS’ compositions, or Minnie’s, though it could be traditional.

The Clare Island Boat Song, X:2

This is from Shane Mulchrone’s playing of the tune on “Solid Ground” – the third tune of the set with Sadhbh Ní Mhongaile (in C), Cailleach an Airgid (in Cmix), Clare Island Rowing Song (in Am). Because all of these are somewhere around C, I’m including a C whistle transpose.

X: 1
T: Clare Island Rowing Song
D: Shane Mulchrone – Solid Ground
S: Shane Mulchrone recording
Z: Transcription: Alina Larson

L: 1/8
M: 6/8

Same tune, in the key of A (totally within the Scottish pipe range).

NB: the only 3rd -in the 2nd part- is a passing note.

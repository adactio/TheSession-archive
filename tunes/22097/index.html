Staple’s Haggard

Another composition of the late Paddy Hayes from Piercestown in Co. Wexford. The Staples‘ family were neighbours of Paddy’s when he lived in Piercestown. A ’haggard’ down our way is a yard in among farm buildings. Apparently the word is a remnant of the Norse spoken by the Vikings who lived in Wexford in medieval times. Like another of Paddy’s compositions, it has a low E note, playable on the 2 row accordion, but below the range of the fiddle etc.

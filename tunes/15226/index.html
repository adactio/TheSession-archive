Re: A Trip To Bavaria

This tune was written by my father Hamish Menzies of Callander, Perthshire. Originally penned as the Remmerts of Hereford (a family he became friendly with on national service), it later became better known as “Hamish’s Tune” a title coined by Andrew Rankine who recorded it, but was unable to remember the title my dad had given him.

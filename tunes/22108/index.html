Down In The Glen

↳ Posted by breigin on August 4th, 2022.

Re: Down In The Glen

This is called “The Bonnie Lass of Headlake” by Gordon MacQuarrie, a fiddler/composer (and piper) from Cape Breton. He published it in his 1940 collection. He wrote the tune as a slow air; interesting that it’s played as a march in Ulster, but it works very well.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on August 4th, 2022.

Down In The Glen, X:2

I have this as ‘The Bonny Lass of Headlake’ and, as I recall, this setting was my attempted transcription from the playing of Natalie McMaster. I’ve added these chords, so take or leave. The second time through includes a few phrasing variations from the version I heard - which was more in the style of an air than a strict-tempo march

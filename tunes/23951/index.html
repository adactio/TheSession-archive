Three comments

Toothless Grin

Toothless grin is a banjo tune I wrote for Kilrush around 2006. Recorded on the Spree album on the O’Neil’s Gold track. It was recorded in Dm to fit the tone of the rest of the album, but it was originally written in Am (G).

↳ Posted by Tim_Fiddler on January 20th, 2024.

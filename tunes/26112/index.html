Princeton Lament

This is actually a lament (not a waltz, although I guess it could be played as such) from my album “The Boatman”. Someone asked that I upload it here to The Session, which I am happy to do.
It is in 3/4, but the music here appears in 3/8 for some reason. (either way as written the 1/8 note is 100BPM) In 3/4, the ¼ note beat is 100 BPM. Play it in whatever tempo feels right to you :)
NB: On my solo guitar recording, I play it in Cm (Capo on Fret 3, DADGAD tuning)

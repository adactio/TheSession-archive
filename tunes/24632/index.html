Long Legs

I’ve learned this tune from Zingo, a brilliant young piper, during Burwell Bash 2024. Thanks a lot for that!
To my knowledge the tune is written by melodeon player Andy Cutting and performed with his band “Topette”, see eg. here at the end of this set:
https://topette.bandcamp.com/track/le-sac-de-jambon-ange-long-legs-2

I’m not 100% sure wether it is a Slide, but for sure it is a tune that is hard to finish once you’ve started playing it ;-)

Cheers, Chris

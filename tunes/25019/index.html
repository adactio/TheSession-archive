Peter Croll’s

This is the first in a set of three jigs composed by my late friend Peter Croll, who passed away in May 2024.

You can hear the source recording here: https://youtu.be/ITexEt9xhmI

Note that the beginning of the tune is cut off in the recording (I was too slow getting out my Zoom recorder) – the second iteration of the A-part starts a few seconds in, followed by just one (non-repeating) B-part before transitioning into the next tune.

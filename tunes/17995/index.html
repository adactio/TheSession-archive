Five comments

“The Moving Note” by Colin Farrell from Make A Note

Hello.
You can hear this song on the first track from the album Make A Note (Colin Farrell, 2o15)
The Moving Note/Settler’s Corner/The Bridge of Finea on SoundCloud :
https://soundcloud.com/colin-farrell-4/1-the-moving-note
or here : https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rr5ZJevolo8&t=27s

I liked it as soon as I heard it on an Irish web radio. So I chose it for my first public appearance with my tin whistle next Sunday (May 26, 2019).
On the tin, I add 4 semitones to play it in Gmaj.

I wrote both parts, the first one as simply as possible and the second one trying to render the sounds I hear.
You can add in the header : Q:1/8=390

Please, discover and listen to this tune if you go there :)
(sorry for my English, I’m French)

The Moving Note, X:2

This is another nice tune from Collin Farrel. I have put it in G here although in D an octave lower than the tune above it is also lovely. Originally recorded in E flat. On the B/C accordion in G it is very easy and all on the C row.

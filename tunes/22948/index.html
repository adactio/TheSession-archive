Three comments

Good As New

From the playing of, and composed by, Patricia Clark.

From her Bandcamp page, about the album:

> On 11th September 2019 I returned home to find that my house had been broken into and my fiddle stolen. On 30th September, the fiddle was found by a neighbour in a local wood, smashed to pieces and covered in mud, having spent three weeks in the elements. He retrieved all of the pieces he could find and returned them to me in a shoebox. One piece, to the right of the front of the fiddle, was never found despite multiple searches. I gave the shoebox full of pieces to Paddy Tutty, who did an incredible job in repairing and restoring the fiddle – complete with an entirely new section to replace the missing piece. The story of the fiddle is the inspiration behind the album - the mixing of the old, unusual and brand new, and the photos throughout the sleeve were taken by Paddy during the restoration.

From the Bandcamp tune page:

> ‘Good As New’ was one of the first tunes I wrote after I brought home my newly-repaired fiddle."

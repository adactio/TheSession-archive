Zig Zag Road

a Duncan Davidson composition
Featured on the 2004 Woden Flute Obsession vol. 2, disk 1, track 10. Last tune in the set.

Part one: breaths or rests?
I couldn’t make my mind so I submit 2 slightly different versions.
Students: you can replace the rests by low D’s or other notes if you find these rests-on-the-down-beat too intimidating.

There is a reel called (The);Zig-zag (Reel) but this is “Zig zag road”.

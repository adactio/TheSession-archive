Rebecca & Irven’s

This was composed by Aaron Marcus, a Vermont based pianist, concertinist, dancer.

This tune was featured in the album Midwinter Spring (2013) by traditional Celtic style folk band Frost and Fire.
http://www.frostandfireband.com/?fbclid=IwAR32PsUZa9uIeIm6YJ4TEYfa3Isx0KwdlA9bNSV908hqacTmAc5EjBNyAm0

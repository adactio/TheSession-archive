Maries Hopsa

Not a polka but a well known Danish “Hopsa”.

It’s a fairly traditional dance as you will see

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ENF-6CyusRE&t=28s

but I first heard the tune performed by Harald Haugaard and Morton Høirup who state they learned it “efter” Alfred Larsen.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3bmW30Lf7Hk

It’s actually now quite popular among some well known young(er) Scottish musicians who picked it up over there a few years back. No names, no pack drill. :-)

Great tune.

Coast River

Slow reel by Donal Lunny. Not really something you’d ever play in a session, but it’s fun to play along to the CD and a good memory test with all its wacky phrasing and ties over the barlines.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on March 14th, 2006.

The transcription is based on the fiddle setting. The flute setting has slight variations 2nd time thru the A-part:

Re: Coast River

Tony McManus does a nice version on guitar also. I learnt this from an old tape of the Donal Lunny recording of the Sean O’Riada retrospective concerts. I think to this day, it is one of my favourite Irish trad recordings of all time. It was a revelation the first time I heard it. I learnt this tune, as well as the lament, “Declan,” and the polkas (Glen Cottage and Tolka polka ) on it too…all fantastic. Sadly, I’ve never met anybody else who plays any of these tunes! What session(s) d you play at @Dow? One day I’d love to make it along and play this one with you.

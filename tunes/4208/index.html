Three comments

Lancer’s

Another slide from Gerry O’Connor’s “Journeyman” cd. This is the second tune in the set starting with The Day the Ass Ran Away and ending with Tickle Her Leg with the Barley Straw. It’s different than the other Lancers and Donegal Lancers tunes posted in the archives here.

I like how simple Gerry and Donál keep this--sliding into the low E3 each time, but not rolling it, no fireworks, just solid, lively playing.

↳ Posted by Will Harmon on February 8th, 2005.

Lancer’s, X:2

This is played in France for a dance called “Cercle Circassien”. It is a beginner friendly dance in circle. You could basically play any jig for this dance, but Tripping Up the Stairs https://thesession.org/tunes/111 and The Lancer’s are the ones I’ve heard the most.

↳ Posted by Raphaël Pinel on April 28th, 2023.

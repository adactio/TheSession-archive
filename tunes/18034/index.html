Four comments

Lake St. John

Newfoundland reel, from the playing of Shanneyganock and Figgy Duff. Unsure whether it’s traditional or not.

Shanneyganock, second tune, starting @ 1 min 1s:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v_GWQhVSofQ

Figgy Duff, first tune:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UapHWXmBzm8

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on June 13th, 2019.

Lake Saint John, X:2

From the playing of Buddy Wasisname And The Other Fellers.

Their setting ends differently than both Figgy Duff and Shanneyganock, with it looping back into the ending from the A Part for the B Part. A few minor details differ as well.

Figgy Duff have the tune listed as traditional.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on September 8th, 2019.

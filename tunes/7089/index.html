I was sure I’d posted this already, but can’t find it. It was recorded on Harry Bradley’s 1st CD, “Bad Turns & Horseshoe Bends”, released in 1999, and I reckon most players got it from there, almost certainly if they’re calling it by the name “Sligo Reel”, which was the name Harry gave to it.

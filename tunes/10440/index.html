Two comments

Snow Shoer’s

A great little tune, learned from the playing of Jeremy Kammerer in the mid 1980’s. The A part is in Bb while the B part switches over to Gmin. It’s not the same as another reel with the same name found in The New England Fiddler’s Repertoire. Does anyone know this one under another name? It goes great with The Mountain Ranger Hornpipe.

Here’s a neat variation on the B part:
K:GMin
|:D2 | G3A BABc | d^cde d=cBG | F3G AGAB | c=Bcd cAGF |
G3A BABc | d^cde d=cBc | d3e dcBA | B2 A2 G2 :|

↳ Posted by fiddlerdan on April 22nd, 2010.

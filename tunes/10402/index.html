Four comments

Michael Ward

This tune is not a reel, but there is no proper category for it. I think that it is an O‘Carolan tune in 4/4 time. I learned it from the Arty McGlynn/Nollaig Casey CD ’Lead the Knave’

↳ Posted by Martin D'Arcy on April 7th, 2010.

Hornpipe would be the much better category!

↳ Posted by swisspiper on April 8th, 2010.

Michael O’Connor

This is the Dm setting that Garry O‘Briain has on his O’Carolan’s Dream CD, which is also how it appears in the Donal O‘Sullivan collection (’The Life, Times and Music of an Irish Harper’).

Given a choice, I would have given it a ‘hornpipe’ category.

↳ Posted by coppers and brass on May 5th, 2014.

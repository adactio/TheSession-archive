Ashley’s Flag

I first heard this traditional tune as the second in a set in G major, following Ashley’s Hornpipe on the ‘Sea Shanties: Rousing Songs from the Age of Sail’ compilation album (with recordings credited to Ian Giles, John Spiers, Jon Bowden, and Graham Metcalfe). It was played gently and sweetly, with some swing. After researching the origins of the tune, it seems as though it was first listed in Samuel, Ann & Peter Thompson’s ‘Compleat Collection of 200 Favourite Country Dances Vol. 5’ (in the key of Bb major) in the late 18th century.

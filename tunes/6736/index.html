Kats of Kilkenny

Another jig I was given by Richard Tonkin, the Kilkenny referred to in this tune runs of Port Road in South Australia not the Irish Kilkenny. They obviously don’t know how to spell in Kilkenny.

↳ Posted by JACKB on February 1st, 2007.

Kilkenny Cats

I see you live in Australia, Jack but perhaps the name is not entirely disconnected with Kilkenny in Ireland. The ‘Cats of Kilkenny’ or ‘Kilkenny Cats’ is a well know moniker for the all powerful Kilkenny hurling team.

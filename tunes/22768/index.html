Two comments

An Ciarraíoch Mallaithe

This isn’t a waltz at all, rather it’s the air of a song made famous by Séamus Ó Beaglaoich, who died in late 2022. It must have been in the back of my mind, but it wasn’t his singing that turned me onto it. Rather it was the playing of Damien O’Reilly on his Dúchas album (2019). Very powerful stuff, with fantastic harmonic development on each repeat. Once Damien lit the fire, I had to go back to hear Séamus’s version too. They’re all beautiful. Couldn’t believe it wasn’t in here already.

It seems to be in triple time (but not waltz speed). A shame that session doesn’t allow lyrics, since I think that helps a lot with air playing. Maybe I’ll drop the version with lyrics into a comment later.

↳ Posted by Frank Gibbons on February 12th, 2023.

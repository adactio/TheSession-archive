“Carolan’s Cap” ~ this transcription up a step for comparison…

T: Carolan’s Cap
N: compare with “”The Lass Of Pattie’s Mill"
M: 2/4
L: 1/8
R: polka
K: DMaj
A |\
G/F/E/D/ FA | d>e fe | dB AF | E2 EA/G/ |
G/F/E/D/ FA | dd ef | FA e/d/B | A2 A ||
d/e/ |\
fa ab | af a/f/e/d/ | fa ba | fe ef/a/ |
ba/f/ a/f/e/d/ | e/d/e/f/ af/e/ | dc/d/ ed | BA A |]

Major, as it resolves best on that tonic, not G Mixolydian, but C Major, consequently D Major in the given transposition. Here are ways of resolving both parts to show the Major in this ~
C Major
A-part ~ | EG d/c/B | C2- C ||
B-part ~ | cB/c/ d/c/A |AG G |] F | EG d/c/B | C2- C |]
D Major
A-part ~ | FA e/d/c | d2- d ||
B-part ~ | dc/d/ ed | BA A |] G | FA e/d/c | d2- d |]

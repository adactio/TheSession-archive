Re: Pilling Moss

Pilling Moss is a low lying, boggy are near Lancaster. I’m presuming the name was coined by the Winders for a tune they didn’t have a name for.
Originally in F in the manuscripts. It’s very similar to Irish tune “Lucky in Love” on here..

Three comments

Blethering…

From the “Urban Dictionary”-

1. (scottish slang) One who blethers or talks sh*te constantly.

2. (scottish slang) One who is full of sh*te.

This tune was apparently written by I.I. “Iain” MacInnes, whistles and pipes for The Tannahill Weavers, Ossian and Canterach and appears in a set following ‘Lucy Cassidy’-

https://thesession.org/tunes/11354

And followed by ‘The Smith of Chilliechassie’-

https://thesession.org/tunes/11354

Transcribed from Bbmix to Amix.

Thanks to ‘Dr Dow’ for his help with this transcription.

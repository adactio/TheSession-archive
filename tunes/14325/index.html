Banbury Bill

A tune that is played at the Golden Guinea pub session, Bristol (UK). This setting is based on a Cotswold morris tune from Bampton in Oxfordshire.

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on April 2nd, 2015.

Banbury Bill, X:2

Added chords we use and small but significant changes to the tune - played at sessions throughout New Zealand and used as a processional Morris dance tune.

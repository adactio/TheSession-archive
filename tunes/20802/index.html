The Autograph

Setting in G Major of this music score in ITMA:

https://www.itma.ie/digital-library/score/ryans-776

I got to know this tune from the playing of Kanako Machida. She does a wonderful playing in this YouTube video where she is playing the Joe Derrane version of the tune. Brian McGrath is doing accompaniment on ukulele:

https://youtu.be/8XI6zfkFBJs

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on May 22nd, 2021.

The Autograph, X:2

This setting is from the way that Eamonn Cotter plays it on The Knotted Chord. Since Eamonn made my flute, and this tune starts my favorite set from his album, I figured I should try to learn it the way he plays it. This tune is an old one, appearing in Ryan’s Mammoth Collection in 1883 (https://www.itma.ie/digital-library/score/ryans-776), and in an old cylinder recording from 1908 (https://www.library.ucsb.edu/OBJID/Cylinder3303).

Re: The Autograph

I’m not a frequent commenter, so I’m just coming back to noticing this comment. Eamonn plays it in D, just as notated. That said, I think this is not really a session tune, it is too convoluted. It is more of a performance piece. When a tune is only in a handful of tunebooks on here, it is very unlikely to be heard at sessions. The tune that Eamonn plays after (the Spy Czar by Liz Carroll) is even more fluttery and performance-y, but lovely.

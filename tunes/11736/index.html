Two comments

Not to be confused with the tune by this name sung by Willie Nelson.

On his cd this song is played as part of a medley with Skipper & Company and Claudine. The transition from the first tune to this one is accompanied by the introduction of electric bass and a strummed acoustic guitar into the mix.

THe hardest part of this tune is getting the rhythm right in the 1st and 5th lines of part B because the beat fall on the second count of the d chord. He sometimes takes his bow off the fiddle for an instant during this.

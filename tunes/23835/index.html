Four comments

Mistletoe

Requested by Tongle, “The Mistletoe Waltz” is from Dordan’s album The Night Before… A Celtic Christmas (1997), and was composed by harp player Kathleen Loughnane. Quite tricky to capture, but since my work has finished for the year, I’ve got time… You may wish to transpose it to G if you play whistle.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on December 19th, 2023.

The Mistletoe Waltz, X:2

What appears to be a 3rd part in this transcription is a harmony line for part 1.
Mary Bergin plays it as a stand alone alternat(iv)e part 1 on the recording (a solo/break of a sort before the fiddle comes back in) the 2nd time around.

If I have the incentive (or the time) I will post the 2nd part of the harmony one day…

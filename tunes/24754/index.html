Mary Hughes’

A tune from Prince Edward Island, Canada. Written by Kevin Chaisson. This is the original version that Kevin Chaisson plays in his 1995 album “Road to Rollo Bay”.
The East Pointers always play this tune at their live gigs, but I will add their version as well as it’s slightly different.

Re: Mary Hughes’

Dais, the way you have it written results in one too many notes of the first bar when repeating into The A Part.

e.g.,

K: Dmaj
|: d | DDD A2A | BAF ABd | DDD A2A | BAF E2F |
DDD A2A | BAF ABc | dcB AGF | EFA B2d :|
|: d2d dAF |ABA BAB | d2d BAF | EFA B2c |
d2d BAF | ABA BAB | dcB AGF | EFA B2c :|

Should be:

K: Dmaj
d|: DDD A2A | BAF ABd | DDD A2A | BAF E2F |
DDD A2A | BAF ABc | dcB AGF | EFA B2d :|
|: d2d dAF | ABA BAB | d2d BAF | EFA B2c |
d2d BAF | ABA BAB | dcB AGF | EFA B2c :|

Also, I’m guessing that the 8th/16th bars of each part should probably have a different pick-up note for repeating into the same part vs. going into the next part.

e.g.,

Hi NfldWhistler, I appreciate the feedback. However, this is what I copied and pasted from an external source and two separate recordings. One of the recordings is Kevin Chaisson’s 1996 Album, “Road to Rollo Bay”.

I’ve never heard the tune in G, and quite frankly to be honest, I think it sounds better in D. You can listen to The East Pointers’ slight changed version below as well. I’m a quite a fan of theirs and will add a setting when I get the time. In terms of credibility, Kevin Chaisson is Tim Chaisson’s (Fiddler from TEP) father and Koady Chaisson’s (Banjo Player from TEP) uncle.

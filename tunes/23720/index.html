Ian S. Robertson

This tune is a slow air. There is no place for slow airs on this site, so I listed it as a march (NOT a march).
Also, No key of Bb Major listed. Big oversites when adding tunes. Also did not indicate 1st and 2nd endings to the A part. It was clear from the ABC’s that the last 2 bars of the A part ARE separate endings.
This tune was composed by Shetland fiddler, teacher, collector and composer Tom Anderson, 1963, for the son of Arthur Scott Robertson the Champion Scottish Fiddler.

↳ Posted by Greg McLaughlin on November 22nd, 2023.

Ian S. Robertson, X:2

X:2 - A setting with some chord suggestions (in Bb, though it’s given as Gm). I came across this tune via an international virtual session held over Zoom that was set up during lockdown played by Keith MacDonald, a piano accordionist then living in Germany. (I’m not sure if the chords given here were mine or Keith’s but, as ever, they just represent one way of doing it. Other chord progressions are available).

Re: Nigel Gatherer’s comment above (with respect). Catriona Macdonald (small d). Catriona’s father was from Stornoway. Great experience, thirty years ago now, playing with Catriona in Ian Lowthian’s band. They were the same age group as my children, so it was “grandpa” on the drums. Happy memories, mostly.

There must be as many Ian (or Iain) Robertsons as there are Alasdair Macleods, so thanks to Greg McLaughlin for the i/d.

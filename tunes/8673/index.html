Inches To Dublin

A Padraig Rynne composition from Cian’s “Three Shouts from a Hill”.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on July 2nd, 2008.

After the 1st time through, they play a variation in bar 4 of the A-part where they hold onto the high c: |c3b bagf|.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on July 2nd, 2008.

Transposed for the fiddle

Not a very good fiddle tune in this key, but it’s ok if you transpose it and avoid string crossing. It sits nicely in B minor, and still maintains the same kind of feel:

X: 1
T: Inches To Dublin
C: Padraig Rynne
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
N: Transposed from Emin
K: Bmin

Roland’s Return

This is a major key and a bit brighter tune than the other “Roland’s Return” in the database, and this is the first tune of a two tune set from Conor McCarthy’s and Annemarie McCormack’s recording “Ace and Deuce.” It is followed by McCarthy’s tune “The Ballycarroll Jig.” In the recording notes the couple dedicate the tune set to their friends and neighbors in Ballycarroll and “The Parish.”

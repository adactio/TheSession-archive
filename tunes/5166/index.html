Re: Red Tom Of The Hills

Try playing the final high F as an F#. I was going to say that I know that Reavy didn’t write it like this, but in my copy of The Collected Compositions of Ed Reavy it’s written with a key signature of G major, with some of the Fs marked as naturals and some as sharps if they’re later in the same bar as an F natural. Rather perversely all the Bs are written as naturals, and I must say that I think the Bbs sound much better in the first part of the tune. Do you play the B in the link between the A and B parts as a flat or a natural? Personally I’d go for the natural, as Erik does; but some people might argue that that gives the game away a little too early.
I would share a photo of the page, but there doesn’t seem to be any way of uploading it…
I found an old minidisc recording titled ‘The Music Of Ed Reavy’, but unfortunately it’s not on there.

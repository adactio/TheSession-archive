Three comments

Callow Lake

Haunting air by Davy Spillane. Love it. As ever with airs, the time signature and bars are just a rough guide but I would welcome the efforts of other more experienced transcribers to make it more accurate. Numerous slides are included in the piece which, unfortunately, don’t show up but they are included as “Js” in the notation.

↳ Posted by Mikethebook on August 6th, 2014.

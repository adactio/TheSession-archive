Not so glad on the flute

The tune is amazingly beautiful and Sylvain Barou plays it on the flute on his CD live Guidewires but it’s not an easy tune on the flute at all, Gminor. You have to use the f, Bb and Eb keys a lot. It worth trying anyway.

↳ Posted by Javi V on July 20th, 2010.

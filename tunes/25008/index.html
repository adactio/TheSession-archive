Alas, My Little Bag

This tune is taken from Dr PW Joyce’s ‘Ancient Irish Music’, written in 1872 but subtitled ‘Some notes we used to love in days of boyhood’ so presumably representing music that was popular back to the early or mid 19th century.
The tune is really a song - a comic song, in Irish, in which the singer first laments the theft of their little bag, then goes on to describe the contents, which become increasingly unlikely as the song progresses - a flitch of bacon, a lot of fine clothes, Shannen’s brogues, Paddy’s stockings and so on.
It seems to me to make a nice, lively single reel. This is my setting including some variations that came out in the playing of it, rather than a straight transcription of what’s in the original book.
The semi-quaver ornaments represent the ornament I’d play there on a piano accordion, obviously a triplet or other ornament could go in the same places.

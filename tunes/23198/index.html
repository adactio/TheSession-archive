The Bellview

Here’s a final reel composed by Mick Martin from Wexford. Mick is a fine multi-instrumentalist and currently plays the pipes and the whistles on the group, The Kilkennys. Thanks to Nóra Byrne Kavanagh from South East technical University’s Music department for notating these tunes of Mick’s and indeed of other musicians from the Bannow area who’ve submitted compositions for the Wexford Tune Project.

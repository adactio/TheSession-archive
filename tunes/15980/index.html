Connacht Drifters

A lovely jig from the pen of Saileog Ní Cheannabháin. It’s in c minor on the recording Roithléan but I’ve put it into d minor here as it’s probably either played on a fiddle tuned in C or a viola on the recording and D minor seems the most suitable. There’s loads of variations obviously on the recording so what’s here is mainly the first time around.

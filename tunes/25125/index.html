The Ship Has Sailed (Denis Murphy’s Curragh Races) (S: Denis Murphy)

I’m sure you know the reel “The Ships Are Sailing” but this is a completely unrelated tune: what Denis Murphy (jokingly?) called “The Ship Has Sailed” is, I think, a version of The Curragh Races or The Maid in the Cherry Tree (compare: https://thesession.org/tunes/1760 )

This is a fun little reel which, unlike its A dorian relative, could be easily resolved in either G major or E minor depending on your mood. Compared to The Curragh Races, it is also played singly and has its parts reversed. The above prompted me to post this as a standalone tune as opposed to a quirky setting of The Maid in the Cherry Tree, as it does feel different overall. Please let me know if you’ve come across this version in some old collection of tunes!

You can listen to Denis Murphy playing this reel here (recorded by Breandán Breathnach in November 1967):

https://www.itma.ie/blog/denis-murphy-breathnach-materials-reels/

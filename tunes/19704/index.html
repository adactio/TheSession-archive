Re: Gladstone’s Bill

The group piper Pádraig McGovern learned “Gladstone’s Bill” from Father John Quinn, the Leitrim-based fiddler, collector and researcher [1]. It’s also found in the Stephen Grier (1824-1894) manuscripts of tunes collected in south Co Leitrim.

[1] = https://www.creativeireland.gov.ie/en/event/the-cataloguing-and-digitisation-of-the-fr-john-quinn-music-collection-and-related-manuscripts/

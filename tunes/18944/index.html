Separation Of Soul And Body

From Donal O’Sullivan’s excellent book on Carolan - one of the “Miscellaneous Tunes”, no.201. Not well-known but very atmospheric and well worth playing - though it might be easier in D minor…

No.202 is the much more famous “Sheebeg and Sheebeg” traditionally the first tune Carolan wrote!

An alternative version in Gdor which sounds very natural to me.
Especially in bar 6, in bar 11, and from bar 14 to 15 : in those ascending melodic patterns, it’s typically a natural E that attracts the phrase to an F.

In the “Lament For The Death Of Owen Roe O’Neill” (2nd setting), we do have the same case. However the appearance of an E flat always attracts the melody to the downside.

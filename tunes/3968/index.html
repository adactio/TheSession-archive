Over the Bridge to Bessie

I learned a slighly different version of this tune from Harry Bradley and Paul O’Shaughnessy’s duet playing. According to the notes, Harry learned it from Nigel and Diana Boullier from Co. Down.

K: Dmaj (?)
BA|:GEBE GE~E2|GEBE D2EF|GEBE GABc|1 edce d2BA:|2 edce d3A||
(3Bcd ef ~g3e|abaf gfed|(3Bcd ef g2fg|1 afge d3A:|2 afge d2||

The second part is almost identical to that of John Egan’s: https://thesession.org/tunes/909 This is probably not related to “Over the *Water* to Bessie” recorded by Frankie Kennedy.

↳ Posted by slainte on March 19th, 2005.

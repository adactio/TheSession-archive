Re: The Spotted Pony

I get a lot of mileage out of this tune - jam sessions, square and contra dances. But, I call it by its older name, Snowshoes, which is how it is known among the older generations of fiddlers in the midwestern and western U. S. It became Spotted Pony due to an editing error by the otherwise fastidious Phil and Vivian Williams in their 1967 release, Fiddle Jam Sessions, on Voyager Recordings. It has since spread far and wide under the new name. You can read about it in the recently published book by Paul Schafer, Vivian Williams - A Musical Life. Spotted Pony is an entirely different tune, but it was being discussed during the recording session by Pete MacMahan and somehow the names got switched.

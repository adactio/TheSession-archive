The Phone Call

This tune was written by a fab piano accordionist from Bath, England, called Abby. I find it to be a very “accordionish” tune, as it’s damn near impossible to play on the fiddle with those high notes. Apparently the name was inspired by a series of marathon telephone conversations she had with a fiddler from London.

It’s also known as Dan’s. Dunno why.

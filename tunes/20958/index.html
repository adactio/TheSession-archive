Seven comments

Mr. Webster

Learned this tune off of Gerald Trimble’s first album “First Flight”. Real fun and relatively easy strathspey that he plays at a fairly moderate tempo. The LP liner notes say he learned it from Kerr’s Merry Melodies vol.3. The version he plays is pretty in line with what it has in Kerr’s but differs a bit at the end (at least it sounded like it to me when I learned this tune off the LP a while back). Either way this is how our session plays it, though the midi really has a hard time of interpreting that crucial strathspey swing.

↳ Posted by Cloudling on August 11th, 2021.

Rolling In Late (Adam Work’s)

This is another of my own compositions - written in honor of the New Haven fiddler Adam Work, with whom I’ve been playing tunes in the last several months. Due to schedule conflicts he regularly shows up to the local Sunday session about thirty minutes late, give or take.

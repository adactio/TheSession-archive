Two comments

Flower Of Swiss Cottage

From the playing of, and, composed by, Eliza Carthy.

Played slowly before the song, “Mother, Go Make My Bed”.

Another version/setting of the tune, or, closely related tune, is played at a higher tempo during the song interludes and after the song.

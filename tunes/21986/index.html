Denis Whelan’s

A catchy jig, composed by John Whelan, London-born of Wexford/Tipperary heritage, but now long based in the U.S. Named for his father Denis, who was from the lovely seaside village of Blackwater in Co. Wexford. John remains a regular visitor from the U.S. back ‘home’ to Blackwater, where he has loads of relations, many also fine musicians. (Frank Torpey of the group Nomos, Frank’s sister Marie, Amanda Kehoe an All-Ireland fleadh winner on piano and piano accordion.) We had a wonderful session of music in the ‘Saucer Whalen’s’ pub (more relations!!) recently the last time John was back in Wexford.
I’m including some compositions of John’s that have direct Wexford connections (through their names) in the Wexford tunes project I’m undertaking. So hence notating Denis Whelan’s jig and I’ll also upload his lovely hornpipe, Beautiful Blackwater and the Enniscorthy jig.

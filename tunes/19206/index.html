Once Upon A Time In Beara

A Richie Dwyer composition. This is a low A setting based on his fiddle version here: https://youtu.be/I8_-vi3chaY
He also recorded it in E Dorian on box, as did Trian and Josh Dukes/Dylan Foley.

↳ Posted by benhockenberry on May 6th, 2020.

Re: Once Upon A Time In Beara

i believe the second version present here, transcribed in Edor, is the closest to the audio sources linked above, although the correct key as heard in the recording and read in the Richie Dwyer “In a Creative Mood” tune book is clearly in Ador. The official book transcription is quite different from the album version. Personally i play the tune very close to the transcription here in Edor, but in Ador. I have lernt it from a workshop with Angelina Carberry which plays the tune in Ador aswell, but very similar to the above transcription in Edor rather than the book transcription.

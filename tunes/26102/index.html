‘Ma’ McNulty’s Favourite

This tune was recorded by the McNulty Family on a 78rpm disc as a hornpipe, following The Stack of Barley. I know it from another 78 recording as a Scottische/Highland, can’t find that recording at the moment!
It’s a version of The Game of Love/Duke of Argyle’s Reel.

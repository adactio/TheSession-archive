My Dream Of You

This is my attempt at a transcription of that famous air by Winifred Horan. Of course in the process of transcribing, I find it necessary to learn the tune by ear. This is for the befit of my more visually centred friends.
youtube clip here: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z_Q63mjZCN4 of course it should be played as an air not as a waltz, but it fits into waltz timing nicely. Wasn’t 100% sure about the key, but Gm fits. Please feel free to comment if you think a different (maybe modal?) key is more appropriate.

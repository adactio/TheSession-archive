Green Valley

I learned this from a friend of mine in college, who learned it in the Duluth, MN contra dance scene. There are words sung to the A section, but the B section is instrumental:

Oh who’s gonna shoe your pretty little foot
And who’s gonna glove your hand
And who’s gonna kiss your red ruby lips
When I’m in a foreign land

Oh papa’s gonna shoe your pretty little foot
And mama’s gonna glove your hand
And sister’s gonna kiss your red ruby lips
While I’m in a foreign land

Now don’t you see that lonesome dove
As she flies from pine to pine
She’s mourning for her one true love
Just like I mourn for mine

The A and B sections aren’t repeated either, just one straight into the next.

The Boston-Sligo, X:3

Composed by Brendan Tonra, originally of Doocastle, Co. Mayo but for the majority of his life in Boston, Massachusetts. Tonra was a regular in many of the dance bands, playing in the Dudley St. Dancehalls, the Connacht Ceili Band, and Boston Comhaltas Ceili Band. He was awarded the TG4 Composer of the Year Award at the Gradam Cheoil in 2002 and has quite a few nice compositions, among them this one and his jig (https://thesession.org/tunes/451).
This is a transcription given to me by Helen Kisiel, a great piano player in her own rite who played frequently with Tonra, and who I would imagine played the chords transcribed.

Brendan can be heard playing this tune here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NY2xzRtY_kw
I usually pair this with the Lady Ann Montgomery (https://thesession.org/tunes/59).

↳ Posted by Thady Quill on November 11th, 2016.

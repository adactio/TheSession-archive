Re: Sue’s Dream

This tune has New England Contra Dance written all over it, which accounts for the kind of jabbing rhythm to it, which is distinct from most Irish reels. I would probably treat the rests as a points of emphasis, where you can really lay into a double stop or roll to emphasis a balance and swing.

The Banks Of Lough Swilly

The Banks of Lough Swilly: This lovely reel was composed by John Lee. John is a flute player, composer, teacher and adjudicator. He is a native of Aughavas, County Leitrim and now lives close to the Banks of Lough Swilly in Co. Donegal.

The Woods Of Old Limerick, X:7

I got this setting from Bernadette McCarthy (and Dermy Diamond) at the Willie Clancy Summer School 2025 in Miltown Malbay. In this key, the opening phrase sounds deceptively like I Buried My Wife and Danced on Her Grave, but at least you don’t have to hit any high Cs in the second part! I love the intervals and meandering feel of bars three and four in the second part. Bernadette and Dermy played this in a set before Statia Donnelly.

Taken From The Road

This is Diarmud O’Brien’s tune “Taken From the Road,” written in honor of his late father, whom his neighbor said died so suddenly it was like he was taken from the road. This is a very simplified version with no ornamentation.

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jUn1zPxKboY&ab_channel=JimMurrayMusic

A version with suggested ornamentations is found on MuseScore: https://musescore.com/user/27273653/scores/6283069

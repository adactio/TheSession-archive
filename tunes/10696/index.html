Two comments

Jionetto Loula?

Does anybody know the origins of this tune?
I learned it years ago from accordeon maker Karel Van Der Leeuw. He didn’t know the title but he was pretty sure that it was a French waltz from the Auvergne. A couple of weeks ago, on a camping in Normandy, I bumped into a German fiddle player who swore that the tune was Canadian. To make things even more complicated I recently, and by sheer accident, found the first part of the tune in the huge pile of photocopies that I call my archive. That first part is called Jionetto Loula there, which sounds Portugese to me.
But whatever the origins, it’s a great tune to play, the first part in one or two voices with a A - E bourdon, the second part with chords. A simplified version of that score I add below.

Three comments

The Two Boxes

This was one of the first tunes I learned from Clones accordion player Pat McCabe way back in the early 1970s. As well as being a multiple All Ireland champion, Pat was probably the main traditional music teacher in the county during that period. I think he was only 19 when he came to teach us children in Emyvale. Anyway this is his version of a great jig.

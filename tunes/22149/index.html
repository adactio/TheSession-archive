Old Lucy Campbell

As learned from Preston Howard Wilde, at a Virtual Lark Camp 2020 over Zoom. I’ve played this at a number of sessions now, and people seem to like it. This is the “piped-up” version, you could say.

Preston says he got the tune from Mick O’Brien’s playing with the Goodman Trio. You can find it on “Tunes from the Goodman Manuscripts” track 5 (Miss Monaghan’s Reel / Neil Gow’s Second Wife / Lucy Campbell) for more variations (https://goodmantunestrio.bandcamp.com/album/tunes-from-the-goodman-manuscripts).

Although it is simply called “Lucy Campbell” in the Goodman Collection, Preston called it “Old Lucy Campbell” to reduce confusion, so I’m doing the same.

Here’s my full transcription for reference:

T: Old Lucy Campbell
T: Lucy Campbell
D: Goodman Trio – “Tunes from the Goodman Manuscripts” (track 5: Miss Monaghan’s Reel / Neil Gow’s Second Wife / Lucy Campbell)

Old Lucy Campbell, X:2

The Goodman’s version as notated in the Hugh Shields’ “Tunes of the Munster Pipers”, edited from the James Goodman’s manuscripts.
The tune is named “Lucy Campbell (reel)”, and is the 2nd tune of the page 29 of the 3rd Goodman’s manuscript,
and all Goodman’s pages are available on the Irish Traditional Music Archive’s site:
http://goodman.itma.ie
and this tune is there:
http://goodman.itma.ie/volume-three#?c=0&m=0&s=0&cv=32&z=-2865.9686%2C0%2C13907.9372%2C6432
(the internet image’s number may not fit exactly with the Goodman’s page’s number - that one is n.31).

In Mick O’Brien’s recorded version “Tunes from the Goodman’s Collection”, the 2 parts are inverted.

Tom Byrne’s No.2

This is the second of the reels composed by Tom Byrne of Wexford. This tune was I think composed about 15 years ago or so. It has a very low E note, playable on accordion etc but below the range of the fiddle. I usually substitute a Low A there when playing it on strings. Tom has composed a fair number of reels, I’m thinking 9 or 10 at this stage.

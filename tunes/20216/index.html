Three comments

Miller’s Daughter

Reel #553 in O’Neill’s. slightly simplified by removing ornaments and bowings. A direct transcription is available at https://tunearch.org/wiki/Miller%27s_Daughter_(2)_(The) Also played in G; the version on Teada’s Ceol and Cuimhne is in G and with parts doubled.

↳ Posted by benhockenberry on December 31st, 2020.

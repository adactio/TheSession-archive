Lord Eglinton’s Reel

From Petrie’s Second Collection of Strathspey Reels and Country Dances (Published 1796).

N.B. The original was in B Flat Major. This key is not available here, so I’ve transcribed it to A Major.

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on February 12th, 2026.

Re: Lord Eglinton’s

Just for interest, it was published twice before Petrie, in John Riddell’s Collection of 1782 (in the keys of B flat and D, “for the German Flute”), and in John Anderson’s First selection of 1789 (in B flat). It is likely that Riddell composed the tune.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on February 12th, 2026.

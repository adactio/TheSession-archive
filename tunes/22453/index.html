The Mayglass Fancy

This is a reel of my own that’s been waiting for a title for some time. I’ve called it after the village of Mayglass halfway between Wexford and Bridgetown, the base of the old Mayglass Ceili Band - who were shared All-Ireland ceili band champions in 1954 - and the home area of excellent fiddlers such as Tom Harper and Murth Doyle.
An interesting thing that’s happened in collecting up all these Wexford compositions is that - despite there being at least 15 or 20 musicians who’ve been composing tunes for their own amusement for many years now (and mostly unaware of each other’s work) - I’ve not had one duplicated title so far. And I suddenly realised that no one had named anything with the title Mayglass in it…so here it is.

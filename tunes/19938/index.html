The Fair Maids Of Ireland

Heard this polka in the playing of Chris Buckley during the O’Flaherty retreat in 2020. He mentioned it as a rather old tune, and I didn’t find anything like it here on TheSession. I did find it as a reel over here:

https://tunearch.org/wiki/Fair_Maids_of_Ireland_(The)

Not sure if it’s originally a reel and Chris just did it as a polka, or what. But, nice little tune.

Re: The Fair Maids Of Ireland

The entire Goodman collection is available online at the ITMA website in both interactive/sheet music and manuscript form. They did a great job on it.
http://goodman.itma.ie/
http://port.itma.ie/browse?collectionId=goodman_vol_1
http://port.itma.ie/browse?collectionId=goodman_vol_2

The tune isn’t marked as being necessarily a reel, and Kerry musicians have taken to playing it as a polka for some time now. No stopping them even if the name contained a “reel” I guess, hahah. Sounds great as a polka (and it certainly did at Scoil Cheoil an Earraigh that year!)

It can also be found in the printed edition of the Tunes of the Munster Pipers, vol. 2, p. 108.

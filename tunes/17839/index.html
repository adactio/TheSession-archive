Alborada D’Amandi

This is an Asturian “Alborada”. From what I could find is a fairly old tune. This is the version arranged/written/played by a piper born in the 19th century called Aladino’l d’Amandi (he was a gentleman called Aladino from the parish of Amandi in Villaviciosa).

The version I’ve transcribed is from Llan de Cubel’s 1996 album IV. Originally in Abmaj.

First tune of the set.

Five comments

Farewell To Liberty

This is from the Roche Collection volume 3. Arrangement is mine. Never heard this tune anywhere (NB not same as saying no one has recorded it). I like the cadence on the minor key, cf. “Killashandra Lasses”. Note that this isn’t E dorian (= C’s aren’t sharp unless so indicated) - if I can change the entry accordingly, I will.

↳ Posted by Zouki on May 3rd, 2020.

Re: Farewell To Liberty

On the sheet music all the Cs are indicated as sharp.

Yes it is possible for the poster to edit the tune later.

As far as the key goes, this tune can be approached a number of different ways from the standpoint of the chords, perhaps as a G Major tune, and playing C Major chords rather than E minor chords at times.

↳ Posted by Richard D Cook on May 3rd, 2020.

Re: Farewell To Liberty

I pulled out the Roche Collection and it’s interesting that you put the tune into a different scale than the original.

The original tune is in a gap scale, there are no Cs in the tune at all. In this way it is like the Kesh jig and many other trad tunes that are in hexatonic and pentatonic scales.

For those who don’t have access to the Roche Collection, I will add the original gap-scale tune.

Name

Ok, I found the name. It’s not really that creative anyway, but it’s better than no name. The group who wrote this is called “The Secret Garden”, and I believe they won the Eurovision song contest with this song the same year that “Riverdance” performed for the first time.

↳ Posted by Hunter G on May 14th, 2013.

“The Secret Garden” is Fionnuala Sherry and Rolf Løvland. The song (which is a song, in that it has some words - by Petter Skavlan - to qualify for the contest) they won the Eurovision Song Contest with was “Nocturne” (with Gunnhild Tvinnereim singing the lyrics, Åsa Jinder playing nyckelharpa and Hans Fredrik Jacobsen on whistle).

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6qqOI04uo_c

Rolf Løvland is also responsible for mucking about with “The Derry Air” to produce the melody for that song “You Raise Me Up”, which was originally performed by “The Secret Garden” with Brian Kennedy.

The tune here is from their album “Earthsongs” (track 3), released ten years after their Eurovision appearance:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pg5z_8qG0QY

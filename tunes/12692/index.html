Re: The Dorsetshire

The Dorsetshire Hornpipe (in Bflat) is printed in O’Neill’s Waifs and Strays of Gaelic Melody (#343). I understand this tune is used in South Yorkshire for some kind of (Morris?) dance. His source was Wilson’s Companion to the Ballroom. 1816

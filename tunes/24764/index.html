Re: The Jolly Beggarman

A good song to this tune (itinerant begs overnight stay at farm, believed to be aristocrat in disguise, gets v. friendly with farmer’s daughter, discloses actual beggar status . . . ) but the notation above seems a bit awry. In the version I know, bars 1 to 8 are repeated, and are just followed by the final three bars given here.

Re: The Jolly Beggarman, Mosstrooper’s Lament

This is the same tune as Sir Walter Scott’s “The Mosstrooper’s Lament”, except that this song in 3/4.
Here’s a recording from Archie Fisher’s brilliant live album “Off The Map” with Garnet Rogers:
https://www.tobarandualchais.co.uk/track/93060?l=en

The song begins at about 1:40

A song lamenting the decline of the moss-troopers or Border raiders.

Re: The Jolly Beggarman

According to legend, the beggarman was non other than James V of Scotland, who used to go around disguised as such and was known as the “Gudeman of Ballengeith”. There is a different tune with the same title used for an English country dance in the late 17th century.

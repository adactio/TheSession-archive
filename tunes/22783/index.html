One comment

Pfunds-stuben

This is another lovely ‘Continental’ style waltz composed by Tommy Kinsella of Gorey Co. Wexford. He tells me in his letter he sent me that the tunes was composed in memory of the place he and his wife spent their honeymoon back in 1964, in the Tyrol region of Austria.

The Boys Of Lough Gowna jig

The “boys” of Lough Gowna were the members of the Lough Gowna Ceili Band, playing in early 1960s Dublin: Sean Bracken (leader), Paddy O‘Brien (accordion), Stella Seaver (piano), Paddy Moloney (pipes), Paddy Treacy (flute), Barney McKenna (banjo), Eoin O’Reilly (fiddle) and Paddy Ban O‘Brion (flute). (’The Definitive Collection of the Music of Paddy O’Brien 1922-1991 [2009].)

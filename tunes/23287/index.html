Re: Barbara Stevenson

“…it seems to me the tune could either be represented in 6/8 … or as a dotted 4/4.”

That is true. But it’s worth remembering that, to a certain extent, the choice of time signature is arbitrary in that people don’t compose in time signatures, they compose in rhythms.

I thinks it’s better (for understanding) to think of slip jigs, hop jigs, waltzes, mazurkas, rants and flings as dances rather than tune types. Tunes for reels and rants can sound the same but the dances are different.
And the same dance can vary from area to area - hence the variety of polskas and polkas.

The way all these tunes are written down is, to a certain extent, conventional, just as it is with all types of music (jazz, for example is always written in straight eighths even though they’re usually swung). The whole 4/4 vs 2/2 debate regarding reels is pointless really as they basically mean the same thing - one has a bar four crotchets long while the other has a bar two minims long. The music drives the notation and not the other way round.

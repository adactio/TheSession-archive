“Dr. Whittaker’s Hornpipe” / “Nancy” - march

~ & also ~

Another quite jolly Northumbrian hornpipe, X: 1 transcribed from the above… Pretty much note for note can also be found in the following collection:

“Northumbrian Pipers’ Second Tune Book”
The Northumbrian Pipers’ Society, 1981
http://www.northumbrianpipers.org.uk/
ISBN: 0-902510-08-8
page 72 - “Dr. Whittaker’s Hornpipe” ~ source: Tom Clough

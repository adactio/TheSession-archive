I Say Sal

From the playing of The Irish Descendants.

Second tune of their “Tunes” set on their album “Is Your Rhubarb Up”.

Prior to the tune, they sing:

"I Say Sal, Take Your Hand On The Lassy Can,
I Say Sal, Take Your Hand On The Lassy Can,
I Say Sal, Take Your Hand On The Lassy Can,
Dum Di-dd-ly Dum Di-dd-ly Dum Di-dd-ly Dum"

“Sal” could be short for “Sally”.

Fear A Bhios Fada Gun Phosadh

Scottish puirt-a-beul.
Translates as ‘On a man who is late in marrying.’

↳ Posted by coppers and brass on November 30th, 2022.

Fear A Bhios Fada Gun Phosadh, X:2

Second setting, as given by Jane at our local session, which she puts in a set called ‘the west coast jigs.’

↳ Posted by coppers and brass on November 30th, 2022.

Re: Fear A Bhios Fada Gun Phosadh

“Grass and heather and moorland plants will grow on a man who is late in marrying. A great beard will grow on a man who is late in marrying.” (sleeve notes from Suilean Dubh by Tannas).

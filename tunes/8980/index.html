↳ Posted by Kenny on May 12th, 2016.

Cry Of The Celts, X:2

I wrote-out the main “Cry of the Celts” reel, which I thought was here somewhere already, but apparently now can’t locate it. Yes it’s a show tune, but a fun tune.

The tunes from LOTD have a more ‘approachable’ quality, to me, for the most part, when compared to other ‘Celtic showtunes’, like Riverdance, for example. They’re FAR easier to pick up, by ear, than anything in Riverdance! LOTD/FOF even featured several trad tunes & songs in the live show, in addition to all of Ronan Hardiman’s music; there are the fiddle duets of Máiread Nesbitt & Cora Smyth, playing Dick Gossip’s/Caliope House/The Congress, and Big John McNeil/Connaughtman’s Rambles/Boys of Malin. Then Flatley joins them in FOF, on flute, for the terribly-titled “Celtic Fire” set (aka Mountain Road/Miss McLeod’s/Jenny’s Chickens). Whereas Riverdance was SOLELY built around specific, complex arrangements of music composed for the show, LOTD/FoF is a bit less intense!

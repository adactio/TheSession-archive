Ar Vuhez ?

Lovely mazurka from WAR-SAV.
Title means “Life?”

The beautiful but sad lyrics are from a poem of Marcos Ana, who spent 26 years in jail during Francoist Spain.
It has been translated from Spanish to Breton by Añjela Duval.

English/French subtitles available on YouTube
https://youtu.be/UB74LkRkiks

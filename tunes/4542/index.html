Lucy’s Reel. Fmajor, the way McGoldrick does it

X: 1
T: Lucy’s
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Fmaj
d2cA AGGF | A2GF Ac(3ccc | d2cA AGGF |
[1 f2(3gab agfe:| [2 f2(3gab agfd ||
g2fg ac‘(3c’c‘c’ | d‘2c’a aggf | [1 g2fg ac‘(3c’c‘c’ |
d‘ac’a g3f :| [2 d‘2c’a aggf | a2ba gf(3def||

This is a nice key to play this tune in. Works best on flute, especially on the high notes. For the second ending of the second part, try bending up to the d’ there. This is probably one of my favourite tunes. By the way, the tempo goes well at around 85 bpm… heh I like details :)

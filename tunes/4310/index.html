Brendan Keenan’s, X:2

I suggest this jig- which I only know from this submission- is perhaps (best played as) a slide.

Here’s a transcription in B*:

X: 1
T: “Brendan Keenan’s slide”
R: slide
M: 12/8
L: 1/8
K: B min
B |: FBB BAF A3 AFE | F2B BAF Bce f2B |
FBB BAF A3 ABc |1 d2f e2c B3 B2A :|2 d2f e2c B3 B2e |
|: fBf baf a3 afe | f3 baf a3 afe |
f2b baf e3 ABc |1 d2f e2c B3 B2e :|2 d2f e2c BBB B2A |]

*B hexatonic (no 6th) wherein the 2nd & 3rd degrees of the mode are easily dispensable or interchangeable.

Bablist Sillas

This tune entitled “Bablist Sillas [sic] Polka” (SG1a.020) was transcribed in Book 1a (c1840s) of the Grier MSS of Gortletteragh, Co Leitrim. Several other polkas in Book 1a are composed by ”J.F. Douglas", a light classical composer who has not been identified thus far, and this may or may not be one of his compositions.

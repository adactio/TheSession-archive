Three comments

La Valse D’hiver

A very indulgent and romantic tune from Pierre Schryer, I don’t know if it’s traditional or his own composition.

It’s played with a medium swing overall, but with the occasional snappy dotted bit here and there which I have notated.

↳ Posted by spraff on April 16th, 2026.

Re: La Valse D’hiver

This seems to be a set of more than one tune. The first appears on a Bottine Souriante track (On La Traversée de l’Atlantique) after Guy Loyer’s “Hommage à Philippe Bruneau”. I believe that it is by Danielle Martineau. This is followed above by a well-established Valse-clogue. They do go very well together.

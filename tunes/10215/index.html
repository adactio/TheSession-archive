Queen Of The Fairies

Larry Redican can be heard playing this at the comhaltasarchives.org, very stately pace. I think he has 9 bars in the first part??? I’m terrible with notation so have a listen. His playing sounds “set dance” to me so perhaps that’s making me think the first part is >8 bars. I didn’t know he composed this one. It’s a beautiful melody.

The other important thing is that he plays the first few bars of the 2nd part in the 2nd/3rd octave, up to e‘’’. This is surprisingly easy on the fiddle, as these shifts up the neck go.

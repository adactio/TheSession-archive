Bernie McKiernan’s Dream

From ‘Leitrim’s Hidden Treasure’ by the McNamara Family. They say in the sleevenotes:
“This tune comes from Pee Fitzpatrick who having no title for the tune, named it after Bernie McKiernan (father of Jimmy), a local fiddle master and flute player in Aughavas, who lived through the first half of the twentieth century.”

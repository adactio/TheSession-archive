Re: Clark’s

Can be found in both Music of Ireland (1903) and Dance Music of Ireland (1907). Also known as “Jim Clark’s Hornpipe” (in Ryan’s Mammoth Collection, 1883), this tune’s A part is very close to the better known tune “The Morpeth Rant” ( https://thesession.org/tunes/1310 ). In Kerr’s Merry Melodies (Bk.1, p26) it’s an untitled “Hornpipe.”

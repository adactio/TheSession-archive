Sir George Clark Of Penicuik

From the album The Legacy Of The Scottish Fiddle, Vol. 1. Who wrote it and who is the George Clark Of Penicuik that the composer of this tune iu referring to.

↳ Posted by Connor Hickey on September 21st, 2019.

Re: Sir George Clark Of Penicuik

Composed by Nathaniel Gow (1763-1831). Sir George Clerk (1787-1867) was a politician, scientist and musician. Penicuik is a dormitory town for Edinburgh.

The Trip To Kilmurry

From Emma O’Leary’s album Mo Cheantar Féin. Written by PJ Teahan of Castleisland.

Sleeve notes:
The Trip to Kilmurry - This hornpipe was composed by Castleisland musician PJ Teahan in 2014 after a great evening’s entertainment was had locally in Kilmurry, Cordal.

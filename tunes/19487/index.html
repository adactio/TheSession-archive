The Salmon Reel

From the playing of Ray & Greg Walsh.

Composed by Ray Walsh.

Has the hornpipe-feel with the part endings, like The Little Beggarman, Soldier’s Joy, and, The Flowers Of Edinburgh, all popular reels in Newfoundland:

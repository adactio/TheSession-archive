Three comments

The Boys Of The Old Brigade

A march a local fiddler often selects for ceilis. The transcription here is from the version played by Dún Na Rí Céilí Band on “The Very Best of Irish Ceili Music.” Nice title, that.

The song is sung in an AABABA format, like the version in John Chambers’ site (http://trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/mirror/back.numachi.com/Boys_of_the_Old_Brigade.abc).

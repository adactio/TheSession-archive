Two comments

Jimmy’s Close-In

From the playing of Simani.

A “Close-In” was/is a type of dance in Newfoundland.

Discog’s liner notes from their 1997 album “Ye Can’t Have Joe Smallwood And Ye Can’t Have John Cabot” (https://www.discogs.com/Simani-Ye-Cant-Have-Joe-Smallwood-And-Ye-Cant-Have-John-Cabot/release/11884345 ) have the tune listed as “traditional”.

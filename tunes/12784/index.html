Two comments

Dragon’s Teeth

This tune is by Jon Boden and transcribed off the album Burlesque (Bellowhead). They play it following Frog’s Legs.

Bellowhead then play it through in A minor and then in D minor. The next two transcriptions are those keys. In bar 12 they put a little phrasing gap between the two minims so that the second minim starts the next phrase. It’s very effective as it climbs the keys.

↳ Posted by SkyFiddler on June 15th, 2013.

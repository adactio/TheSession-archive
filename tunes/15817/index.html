Scandinavian

This tune is performed by the group Whistling Rufus. I have been unable to find the sheet music for it (perhaps it goes by another name) so I’ve undertaken a transcription myself. I believe I have about 95% of it. The performed piece as a nice cello counter melody as well. The intro D and B to E transitions are played as slides.

Re: Scandinavian

I’ve changed the name of this tune back to Scandinavian, because it’s listed that way on two albums -- “It’s About Time”, by Whistlin’ Rufus, and “Flora”, by “The Lilies of the Midwest” -- and that probably carries more weight than the video shown above. I also removed the link to “Fire & Grace”, by Alasdair Fraser & Natalie Haas, because the track on that album turned out to be an entirely different tune. (I couldn’t find much evidence that Terry Rasmussen is actually the composer, but none at all for anyone else, so I’m leaving Terry’s name up).

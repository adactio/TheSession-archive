“The Quare Fella” ~ C: Ted Furey

Bless ’em, bless the family, as they were with music and gave us a lot of pleasure too. I was surprised not to find this here already, but maybe it is, let me know and I’ll nudge all this over to the comments.

Back in 1975 Ted had two little A5 booklets of tunes printed, “The Rocky Road to Dublin 1 & 2”. This tune is at the end of volume 2. Hoping they won’t mind, I’ll add the gist of his transcription and a quote… It’s a toss up whether to notate triplets in straight time as (3, for example (3ABc, which makes for an easy read, or as they usually sound - A/B/c… I’ve gone for the later in the transcription I’ve given, while offering some alternative ways with it, but in honour of the original I’ll follow the way of the collection’s transcriptions.

Return Of The Swallows

This very nice slow piece was composed by Wexford musician Jim Boyce from Ambrosetown, near Duncormick in south Wexford. It features on an album released by the group The Model Banner which was made to raise funds for cancer support. You can hear sample clips from the album, including this piece here:

https://themodelbanner.ie/

I’ve collected and uploaded Jim’s tune as part of the Wexford Tune Collection project. Thanks to Nóra Byrne Kavanagh for notating this piece from the recording.

Interestingly, there’s a tune composed many years ago by Jim’s father, the fiddle player Michael Boyce, already uploaded a few months ago on to the database:

https://thesession.org/tunes/22679

Siar go hInis Bearachain

You can’t beat a Conamara man for waltzes, especially not this one: Johnny Óg is (in my opinion) one of the finest composers of waltzes around. I’m learning this, it wasn’t easy to pick out (and you have to “wrap around” the lower notes on many instruments), but it’s great, and I hope others will enjoy it.

Steren, X:2

I added setting of the first section of Steren (we could consider the whole tune as a “suite”) with a different distribution of measure barlines and, in consecuence, a redistribution of downbeats which I think is a bit more accurate to Gwendal’s composition.

↳ Posted by abecualann on January 14th, 2019.

Steren, X:3

X:3 is a transcription of the second section of Gwendal’s Steren (“Suite”). Between first and second section they play a modulation to D Mixolydian with just F# (though there are some accidental C#).

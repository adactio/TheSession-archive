The Young Prince Of Ulster

From Eoin Duignan’s album Pagan Irish, follows after a very similar tune, Dolar Durba (https://thesession.org/tunes/17862). The Young Prince is the simpler of the two in that set.

This setting is close to the basic melody. There’s a whole bunch of alternative notes and variations which suite the tune well.

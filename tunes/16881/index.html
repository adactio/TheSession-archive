Five comments

The Yellow Horse

Lovely jig that leads off the Alan Reid and Rachel Conlon album A Quare Yield. The liner notes say it’s from Joyce’s Old Irish Folk Music and Song (https://www.itma.ie/joyce/book/old-irish-folk-music-and-songs-part-1). But none of the three tunes called The Yellow Horse in that book really match up with it. The closest one melodically is the 2/4 Yellow Horse on p. 133 (#280). So here’s my impression of what they’re playing -- can’t get this tune out of my head!

↳ Posted by benhockenberry on March 27th, 2018.

Dinnibngton Rant

As the name suggests, this is a rant.

Dinnington is a little Northumbrian village to the NW of Newcastle where there are a couple of Victorian Reservoirs which supply Newcastles water. I believe that parsons had something to do with their construction as he installed hydraulic power on Newcastle’s Quayside.

The Angels play this a lot with “Come Dance and Sing” and a rant version of “Flowers of Edinburgh”.
Noel

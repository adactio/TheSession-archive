Two comments

Breton

This is one of the most beautiful waltz I’ve ever heard. Composed by Erwan Hamon, excellent Breton flute and bombard player. I never heard him playing it and I would love to find it in one of his album, I got it from the playing of Martha Guiney.
https://soundcloud.com/user-904976702/martha-guiney-marty-barry-breton-waltz

She starts the tune on the B part.
There is some third octave notes in the C part that requires cross fingering.
On my flute I use [c‘ = oxo xxx] & [e’ = xxo xxo], I personaly find de keys unhelpful in this case.

↳ Posted by Damien Rogeau on June 28th, 2020.

The Trip To O’Flaherty’s

Written by Liz Carroll (at the request of Ken Fleming) in honor of the O‘Flaherty Irish Music Retreat’s 20th anniversary. Performed by Liz on Saturday, October 21st, 2023 at the 2023 O’Flaherty Retreat in Midlothian, Texas, USA.

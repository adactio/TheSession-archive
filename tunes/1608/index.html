Roving Bachelor

I owe this one to contributer “slainte”. It’s the second of the two “Tommy Peoples’ ” reels on the pre - Altan recording by Frankie Kennedy and Mairead Mooney. I found it quite by accident under this title in one of the “Kerr’s ” collections of Scottish & Irish tunes. A nice flute tune, I think (as is the first one).

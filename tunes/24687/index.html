’Tis Better To Wear Out Than Rust Out

This unusual reel is a composition of the well-known County Wexford mouth organ player and All-ireland champion, Pip Murphy. He can be heard playing it on the wonderful TG4 programme, ‘Sé mo laoch’, which paid tribute to the Murphy family from Ballygow and their great tradition and unique sound. The tune uses a well-known local expression as it’s title.

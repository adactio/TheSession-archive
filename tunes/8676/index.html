“Our Last Night Together” ~ another lovely Seán Ryan hornpipe

Here follows the same tune simplified, minus the > & (3, with some other possibilities given and with the lead-ins as they appear in the excellent collection of his tunes “Seán Ryan: The Hidden Ireland”, where another transcription of this tune can be found on page 15….

X: 2
T: Our Last Night Together
C: Seán Ryan
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: hornpipe
K: Dmaj
|: a^ga |
fdAF GECE | DEFA dfga | bagf edcd | ecdB Aa^ga |
fdAF G2 CE | D2 FA d2 ga | bagf e2 cd | e2 dc d :|
|: BAG |
FDFA fedc | BGBd gfed | cAce agfe | dcde fdAG |
F2 DA f2 dc | B2 Gd g2 ed | c2 Ae aece | d2 dc d :|

Those lead-ins could also be handled as triplets ~

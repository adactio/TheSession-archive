Lad O’Beirne’s

From Mary Mac Namara’s 1991 recording “Traditional Music from East Clare”, the track originally linked to a Lad O‘Beirne hornpipe. Now the link is fixed. Jackie Small wrote in the sleeve notes that this is a Lad O’Beirne composition.

Mary played this on the recording in the low key of G - but that entails having an F below the bottom G on a fiddle; this version in D is more accessible.

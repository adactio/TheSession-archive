“Gan Ainm” ~ for the time being - A Major given but also goes nicely in G

Here’s another one dug up from a bunch of notes on notes, a Swedish mazurka, or at least it was among similar tunes, though there were mazurkas from elsewhere in the mix with it. Here’s a simpler and more repetitive take on it, showing the ‘agreement’ that carries through all three parts, bars 1 & 4 and 7 & 8 ~

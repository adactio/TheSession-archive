Re: The Tempest

The alternative title of this tune is a reference to the poet and outlaw Dafydd Ap Siencyn, sometimes known as the Welsh Robin Hood.

During the Wars of the Roses , Dafydd, who was a relative of Owain Tudur , was a supporter of the Lancastrian party . When the war turned against the Lancastrians, Dafydd became a challenger. He lived in Coed Carreg y Gwalch, near Llanrwst, for years. He is said to have held Harlech Castle with Robert ap Meredudd. With Ieuan ap Robert ap Maredudd, he held Nantconwy against the king until 1468, and ravaged parts of Denbighshire . In 1468 a Yorkist army under William Herbert, Earl of Pembroke came to occupy the area. He received a pardon in 1468, and was later appointed Constable of Conwy Castle , after he had killed his predecessor.

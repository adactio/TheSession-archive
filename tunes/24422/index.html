The Fiddle Bee

Slow-ish reel, composed by fiddle player Madeline Stewart. Thanks to “session.org” member “bigbean” for making me aware of this tune which was recorded by Scottish piper and whistle player Eddie Seaman on his CD “Cave Of Gold” [ Greentrax label, 2021 ].
Eddie’s sleeve notes : “Madeline Stewart is a fantastic fiddle player whom I met in Glasgow. This is just one of a number of great tunes by her and it refers to the time a bee that got trapped in her fiddle”.
A fine tune , which deserves to be played more.
PS - this is linking above to a recording by “Eriska”, of which Madeline herself was a member. I’ve just listened to the band playing it through the “Apple” link, and they play it more in a slightly swingy hornpipe style. Eddie’s rendition is more “straight”.

The German Hornpipe No.1

This excellent hornpipe was composed by the german guitar and whistle player Olaf Sickmann.. I just love the B part.. very astonishing! Olaf plays it on a High C Whistle..
It is on his new album that was released in 2014: WHISTLE MAN.

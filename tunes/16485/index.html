The Spotted Socks

I learned this reel from Natasha Sheehy from Abbeyfeale, Co. Limerick but now living in Boston. Natasha is a lovely player and a great teacher. I think she said she got this one off of Willie Larkin, himself a great teacher and musician, and that he might have composed it. I got the chance to ask Willie this past summer, but forgot how the tune went and the name brought nothing back. Natasha usually plays it with Kate From Castleisland, which shares the same history.

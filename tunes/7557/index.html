↳ Posted by Bryn on February 29th, 2008.

This is one of my all time favorite tunes. It is lovely and lends itself to drone and ornamentation well in first position, but I believe there is a deepened Irish “mood” to the piece when played in third position. It is one of those most memorable, even “haunting” waltzes that, each time you hear it, it takes you somewhere.

There is a beautiful orchestral version of this tune recorded by Eileen Ivers at Knock Basilica in Co. Mayo in which she tells how she wrote the tune for her father Seanie who had emigrated to the USA. The last time through the tune she repeats the last line.

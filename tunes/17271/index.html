Four comments

Seven Years

I got this from the album “Murmurs” by Andy Cutting, Nancy Kerr and Martin Simpson. I think Andy Cutting wrote the waltz. The original arrangement on this recording is: A-B-A-B-C, then repeat.

↳ Posted by Clare on September 13th, 2018.

Seven Years, X:2

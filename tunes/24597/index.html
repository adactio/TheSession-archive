Blathu

Blathu ( meaning Blooming /Blossoming) was commissioned for the soundtrack of a short film
“Connected to Each other” by Pat O Neill , and reflects the beauty and grace of the Rose of Tralee 2014 . Maria Walsh (Philadelphia) Rose of Tralee winner 2014 was featured in the film .
Aine Heslin from Kilmurry McMahon , Co Clare is the composer. She has composed many other tunes and you can connect with her on Facebook and hear more and buy her CD s.

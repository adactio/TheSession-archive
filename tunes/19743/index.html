Two comments

The Galway Dancer

My transscription of Colin Farrell’s lovely tune. Original version is in Emajor, this one here is easier to play on most instruments. Banjo and mandolin players can simply go up to E with help of a capo in the second fret :)
Here’s the link to Colin’s video:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vdnoneNH-Gc&pp=wgIECgIIAQ%3D%3D

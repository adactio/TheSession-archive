One comment

The Crying Mother

Composed by me a month ago. One of my favorites I could say. Maybe it sounds strange at the first time, but gets more lovely as you listen. I could do a simpler version, but as an example I’m still not sure about line 2 - bar 4 if “dAB BAB” is really a good choice to start the repetition (making the continuation for line 3 in this case), and couldn’t decide between line 5 and 7, bars 2 and 4 which one sounds better. Saying short, I think it still needs some polishment. Wish I could test the tune myself on a real instrument, but unfortunately I don’t have one.
Also, these days while I was checking the notes of the tune, I noticed that if you play the last two parts (4 lines) backward, it’s sound very similar with “The Creel Of Turf”. I don’t know how it’s possible, because I never intended to do that, nor was I inspired by it or something else. It came purely as a coincidence, even spend a few days writing everything from the beginning, but still very interesting how it ended to be like that.

Dry And Dusty, X:2

X:2 is a setting I picked up from session players in Sussex, UK, a few years ago. Quite similar to the first version, but with some simple double-stops, and dropping to the C in the second part. Personally, I don’t have the knack of playing old-time fiddle tunes convincingly, but I think this tune is a good ‘entry point’ to have a go at it.

↳ Posted by Bazza on January 1st, 2022.

The Wait!

…
This tune was written by Áine Heslin.. It’s on her fantastic album The Tunes Foundry..
Here what the sleeve notes says about this tune:
‘’This tune to me depicts our fun-filled wait here on Earth till we meet again the loved ones
gone before us. On the other hand it also depicts the wait in a waiting room, bus-stop, adjudication hall
etc. where our hopes rise and fall continually in the wait for a positive answer!‘’

Two comments

The Earl’s Tomb

This is another tune composed by banjo player John Cullen from Clonroche in Co. Wexford, collected and notated as part of The Wexford Tune Project. John was born and raised in Tominearly; his neighbour Tim Flood was an All-Ireland champion in hurling as well as a Senior Winner in banjo and duets at the 1965 All-Ireland Fleadh. John’s title for this tune is an English translation of the Tominearly townsland name - Tuaim an Iarla - the grave mound of the earl.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on October 13th, 2022.

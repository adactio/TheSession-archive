Re: Saw Na Ye My Peggie

Found a youtube of this, also on itunes, both under a slightly different title “Saw Ye Nae My Peggy” by Le Banquet d’Apollon & Francois Mutzenberg, the album “Aria di Camera: A Choice Collection of Scottish & Irish Songs”. I’ve seen that manuscript (Daniel Wright), but can’t see this tune in it, unless there’s another one. However, they play the 1st 6 lines of the above seemingly exactly, albeit incredibly quickly. the youtube is https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vCD3vj6vthY All the other dots out there seem to be different.

Re: Saw Na Ye My Peggie

A simpler two part version is in the Henry Atkinson Manuscript dated 1694 or 1695, called Saw Ye Not My Meggy, part of the Village Music Project http://www.cpartington.plus.com/Links/AtkinsoH/ATKINSON.pdf the tune is number 181 in the PDF section or 182 in the ABC: http://www.cpartington.plus.com/Links/AtkinsoH/ATKINSON.ABC

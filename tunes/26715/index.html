The Lake View

This tune came to my mind during our summer vacation at Fours (Burgundy/France) a few weeks ago. We stayed at a marvellous camipng site called ‘Vue du Lac’ which means Lake View. This lake was only a few yards away from our pitch, and it was a great help to stand the heat!

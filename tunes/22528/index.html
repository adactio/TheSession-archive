Re: Marcelle And Marcel

Hm. A bit more research uncovers this is a modern composition by French harmonica artist Jean-Jacques Milteau. He plays it solo here (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IFHtoToDoy8) and there’s a speedy backing track (!) here (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq1w1FG9BpI) in this key of C. His chords are a bit different from Connolly’s cover but oh well, they both work. Milteau calls it a valse musette rather than a mazurka, so I updated to reflect. Not Irish trad but sounds great on the box, as Connolly shows.

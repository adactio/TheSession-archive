Two comments

Cloudy Day

Composed by me some weeks ago. I’m not so confident in my works, have a few tunes composed and wish they could fit for any instrument as traditional music, but I’m aware some of these, probably, wouldn’t be as great as I thought. Still, I took the risk with this one, among the first tunes written by me. In both parts(A and B); the first two lines are the “core” of the tune, and the 3rd and 4th line, are variations I thought myself to see the potential it has.

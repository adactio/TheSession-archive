Seven comments

Peter’s Schottisch

A delightfully syncopated tune recorded by Dr. Faustus on their second album, “Wager.” Mainlynorfolk.info credits Tim van Eyken as the composer. It doesn’t seem to have much of a presence in the world (apart from me playing it at my local session a couple of times), but I think it’s too good a tune to languish in obscurity.

↳ Posted by Aelia Aeola on May 4th, 2025.

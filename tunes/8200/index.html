Oliver Jack

I first heard this fine Shetland tune on a fiddle weekend in the Yorkshire Dales by Jenna Reid - she follows it nicely with ‘Da Fashion O Da Delting Lasses’ and ‘Up and Doon da Harbour’ on her CD ‘Da Homin’ (see Recordings link)

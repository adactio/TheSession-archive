Gueirúa

Written by Asturian piper and whistle player José Manuel Tejedor.
From his 2018 album “Miraes”, first tune of the last track. Originally in C# minor.

Gueirúa / Gairúa is a beach in Cudillero, Asturias. It’s part of the Western Coastline Protected Landscape.
https://www.turismoasturias.es/en/descubre/costa/playas/playa-de-la-gueirua-gairua

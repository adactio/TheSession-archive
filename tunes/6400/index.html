Vincent McGrath Composition

This reel is played after my previous submission, “Sean McDonnell’s” on Vincent McGrath’s album.

It reminds me a little bit of the “Sally Gardens” and its one of the first tunes of Vincent’s that I really admired.

Notes from the Sleeve say:

“I named this reel after a fine accordion player and friend of mine from the village of Glengad, about then miles from where I live”.

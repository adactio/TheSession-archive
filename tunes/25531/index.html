Re: Doucet’s

A good tune, but the notation makes it tricky to read.

I may be wrong, but I think the flow of the tune means that the main bar lines should be shifted one beat to the left (making the ‘pickup’ note the first beat of bar one). Also, to be consistent with the 4/4 time signature - usual in reels - each bar should have eight quavers (eighth-notes) rather than the four shown here.

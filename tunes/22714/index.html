The Old Carnival

This reel was composed by Cian O Grady from Bannow in south Wexford. Cian is a member of the wonderful up and coming group ‘Tulua’ and has already composed a number of lovely tunes and pieces some of which form part of their repertoire. Thanks to Jon Reville for providing this tune in ABC form. Cian’s compositions are copyrighted so would need consent for their use.

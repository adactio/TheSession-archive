Two comments

Pibddawns Llewelyn Alaw

Based on a Tune taken from ‘Tro Llaw’ a Welsh tune book of Hornpipes, compiled and edited by Robin Huw Bowen for The Welsh Dance Society.

Llewelyn Williams was a 19 Century Harper tune writer. Alaw was the Bardic name given to him at the Eisteddfodau he took part in.

Pronounciation (roughly)

Peeb…thawns (Hornpipe)
Llew… elin
Allow.

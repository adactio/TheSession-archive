Two comments

Ainya’s

Opening tune on The Elusive Highland Beauty. Note that the B section is a repeating seven-bar pattern and the second half of the C section is identical to the second half of the A section.

↳ Posted by NielsJohnson on May 5th, 2021.

Re: Ainya’s

Written by Alasdair Taylor, in the second setting I’ve added in a few of the repeated notes. I prefer the E’s in the B section of the first setting compared to the F naturals in the second setting but this is closer to how it’s played Elephant Sessions’ recording.

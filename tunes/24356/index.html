Came across a video on the NPU site of Padraic introducing this tune;

“I heard Jesse Smith, the fiddle player, playing it a few years ago in Galway. I asked him then, where did he get that, and he made up some story. But then I asked him again a few years later, and turns out he actually wrote the tune.”

McCoy’s Measure

This lovely Scots Measure was populary played in the Leitrim and Longford region. It may have Scottish origins but I have been unable to identify it. This version here appears in the Alex Sutherland music manuscript collection submitted to the National Folklore Comission c1960. Sutherland (1873-1967), was a fiddle player from Toome, Carrigallen, Co. Leitrim.

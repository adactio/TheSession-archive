The Twisted Chimney

This is a fantastic tune written by Grace Broadhead, a young fiddler in Albuquerque, New Mexico. I particularly like the 3rd part, the unexpected turn through a G major chord in the second bar–I can’t think of any other tunes that do that in such a surprising way. Really spot on tune.

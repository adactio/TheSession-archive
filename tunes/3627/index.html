Patrick Maloney’s Favorite

This tune can be found on Micho Russell’s ‘Traditional Country Music of County Clare’ (1976) as “Captain Moloney”. I give his version here because it’s slightly different. Also, I think he’s playing an E or Eb whistle, but using G fingering.

X:1
T:Captain Moloney
S:Micho Russell, Trad Music Clare (1976)
Z:Nigel Gatherer
M:6/8
L:1/8
K:G

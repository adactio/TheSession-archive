Rince Philib A’Cheoil, X:3

A transcription of Duncan Davidson’s instrumental rendition of the tune on the flute.
It is listed as ‘gan ainm’ in his slip jig/jig/reel set on track 10, disk 1 on Wooden Flute Obsession vol. 2 2004.

Re: Rince Philib A’Cheoil

NB; the original song is in the rather rare 6th-less hexatonic ionian mode but the instrumental expansion is fully heptatonic.

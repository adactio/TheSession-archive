One comment

This is another great Will Harmon tune, written in honor of the Portal Irish Music Week festival in Portal, Arizona, USA. The inaugural festival was in October, 2011, and featured Brian Conway as the fiddle instructor. So the tune was written with the New York/Sligo style in mind.

I like the tune a lot!

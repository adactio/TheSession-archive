John Kelly’s

I transcribed this off a session recording taken in the Gaelic Club in Sydney a few years ago. The tune was played as transcribed by a couple of fiddlers, but their original source was Tola Custy and Cyril O’Donoghue’s album “Setting Free”. Everyone I’ve ever heard play this tune seems to have got it off this album. TC plays the A-part as transcribed the 1st time through but then swaps bars 1 and 5. This means that some people have picked up the tune this way, as shown by this transcription from Shelta’s website @ http://perso.orange.fr/shelta/Page2_.htm (I’ve taken out the chords).

Re: John Kelly’s

There are too many spaces in the abc of Michael McDonald’s setting: https://thesession.org/tunes/1808#setting39965 of https://thesession.org/tunes/15388.

The time endings don’t show if you leave a space between the bar line and the number so “| 1” should be “|1”.
Similarly, repeats won’t show with a space between the colon and the bar line so “: |” needs to be “:|”.
Second time endings work better with a double bar line at the end of the line so “||” rather than “|”.
Finally, including a blank line will exclude everything underneath it from appearing in resultant score.

The abc as below will result in a better presentation:

“A”DEBA B2GB | AGEF GABC | d2Bd gdd2 | egdB acba |
G3f GAB2 | AGEF GABC | d2Bd gedB |1 CAFA G3E :|2 CAFA GABd ||
egfg edBd | d2gd B3d | C3A B2dB | CBAG EDFE |
G3f GAB2 | AGEF GABC | d2Bd gedB |1 CAFA GABd :|2 CAFA G3E ||
“B”G3F GABG | AGFE GABC | d2Bd gd3 | egdB ACBA |
DEGA B2GB | AGFE GABC | d2Bd gedB |1 CAFA GDFE :|2 CAFA GABd ||
egfg edBd | d2gd B3d | C2CA B2dB | CBAG FDEF |

Peter And Midge’s Two Step

This is one of mine, I’m afraid, but I haven’t posted one in a long while.

Composed, this year, as a Silver Wedding present for a couple of fiddle players from Achiltibuie, whose acquaintance I was very pleased to have made in 2024.

Yes, I know, it’s got a little bit of Little Burnt Potato about it, but that comes with the two step territory.

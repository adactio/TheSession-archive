The Marquis of Huntley’s Fling

Found this bright little tune in ‘The Fiddler’s Tune-Book’. Here it is with swing:

X: 1
T: Marquis of Huntley’s, The
C: Trad.
S: The Fiddler’s Tune-Book; Reels & Rants, Flings & Fancies; p.29, No.121.
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
Q: 300
R: highland fling
K: Dmaj
|: A2 | d2 f>d g>e f>d | B2 e>d c<A A>c | d2 f>d g>e f>d | B>e d/c/B/A d2 :|
|: B2 | A>D (3FED A>B d>f | g>e f>d e>d B>d | A>D (3FED A>B d<b | a>f e>f d2 :|

The setting is given as follows:

|: D4 | Em2 A2 | D4 | Em A D2 :|
|: D4 | Em2 G2 | D2 G2 | D A D2 :|

Cheers!

Re: The Marquis Of Huntly’s

Nigel’s comments in the link above makes it clear he didn’t submit it as anything but The Marquis of Huntly(’s Highland fling / strathspey).
Only, he chose to submit it on the Denis Murphy’s slide page to emphasize the close melodic relationship between these 2 tunes.
Unfortunately, when you submit a tune under a specific genre like “slide” the slide word is automatically generated after the name if the name ends with ’s.
Hence the confusion.
Overzealous machine. It wasn’t fixed 20 years ago. Won’t be fixed now. :"/

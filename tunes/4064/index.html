Two comments

My most recent composition. I composed it a bit ago, inspired to write a reel because composing a reel for me is much way too hard for me compared to composing a jig!

The b part wasn’t intended until I started composing it, and that’s how it got its name. The end of the b part is where the Baroque-y-ness comes from.

Enjoy!

I ESPECIALLY love to pair this up with the Merry Blacksmith since it begins with d and the b part ends with a d, or is implied. Nice if you slow down a bit before the end, then speed back up into Merry Blacksmith.

Cheers and Final Fantasy I & II,
Armand

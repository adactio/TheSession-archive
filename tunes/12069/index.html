Hale’s Rag

Perhaps the best known tune played by Theron Hale, a banjo and fiddle player of the 20’s and 30’s. It is based on a composition by Charles Johnson. Like most rags, it is not meant to played very fast, and the tempo should have considerable swing to it. It likely isn’t a typical barn dance, but this is best category here I could find to submit it under.

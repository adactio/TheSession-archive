Two comments

Dave White’s

This is a Jerry Holland tune called “Dave White’s”, recorded as the 2nd tune on track 11 of his “Fiddler’s Choice” album (“Glenn and Karl’s Set”).

This name has often been erroneously applied to the Phil Cunningham tune “Harsh February” (https://thesession.org/tunes/1655), which appears as the last tune in the same set on Jerry’s album. They’re similar tunes, so the confusion is understandable. And in fact, several people (including the Mulcahy family on their album “The Reel Note”) have recorded Phil’s tune and labeled it as “Dave White’s”. But as far as I can tell this is the “real” Dave White’s.

↳ Posted by Ian Varley on December 6th, 2020.

David White’s, X:2

I learnt this from the playing of Jamie MacDonald, accompanied by Tara Rankin, at The Noust, Tiree for the Celtic Connections concert, Home on the Sea, first broadcast 27Jan2021.
I can’t now remember how much I altered his setting but there are various differences from the original, particularly the g in the seventh bar of the first part and the abbreviated part endings. The sixth bar of the second part is given as a variation in Jerry Holland’s first collection.

I like to return to the first part as a second time ending of the second part, as does Catriona Macdonald, on “Over The Moon”. Jerry has this for the final pass only.

I’ve changed the title to “David White’s” (actually “David White’s Reel”), as given by Jerry Holland in his book.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on June 20th, 2023.

Two comments

Lord Randall’s Bride

This was requested by fearuin a while back. It’s a Scottish single reel type tune, and this version was played by a fiddler from the county of Angus called Adam Rennie on a 78rpm record.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on January 5th, 2006.

Lord Randall’s Bride, X:2

This is a version we’ve been playing in “D”.

It’s very similar to Nigel’s albeit in a different key.

A nice tune although tricky to play at speed in parts. Lots of “fiddly” bits, if you pardon the expression…

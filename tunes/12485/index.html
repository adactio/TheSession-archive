Four comments

Father’s

Great old double from Rufus Guinchard. I think I remember hearing it was one of the first tunes he learned. I got it from Christina Smith, and it definitely was one of the first Newfoundland tunes I learned. Kind of dumbfounded that it’s not somewhere on youtube, I think of it as being a pretty common tune.

↳ Posted by Sol Foster on February 8th, 2013.

Father’s Jig

This mind-bending tune is taken from a recording by the Dardanelles: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCyxbk2Y0i8

I honestly wasn’t sure WHAT to classify it as, or really what meter to put it in. Feedback appreciated.

Father’s, X:2

This strikes me more as a modified slip-jig than a jig, despite the title listed by the Dardanelles of “Father’s Jig”.

Transcription based on the following recording: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCyxbk2Y0i8

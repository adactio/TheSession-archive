The Titanic

As played by Vincent Campbell, who attributes it to John Doherty:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K7rKrQhX_To

It has quite a few similarities to what is posted here as The Highland Reel (https://thesession.org/tunes/6263); I’ve posted it as a separate tune because of the different key (although there are no C#s in this basic setting, Mr. Campbell does play some C#s and G#s as part of variations, for example when he plays the penultimate bar like |gffe fdec|) and because of the last line which sets it somewhat apart IMO. But that’s probably a matter of argument.

Both may well be related to The Devil In The Kitchen (https://thesession.org/tunes/1746) anyway.

In The Deep Heart’s Core

Also known as Song of the Sea on Davy Spillane’s earlier album, Deep Blue Sea, this is another of his haunting “airs” for low whistle. Rising slides are indicated by J in the notation but they don’t show in music. I use > for indicating falling slides.

Three comments

Coir A’ Cheathaich

From the Patrick MacDonald Collection of 1784. It is sub-titled The Forest of Mamlorn and refers to Coire Cheathaich (Corry of the Mist) at the end of Glen Lochay in Perthshire. It is most likely connected to the well-known Gaelic song about the Corrie written by Duncan Ban MacIntyre.

↳ Posted by munro on March 10th, 2021.

Coir A’ Cheathaich, X:2

This version of the tune comes from The Angus Fraser Collection (published in 1996 by Taigh na Teud), but collected by Simon Fraser’s father in the mid to late 1700s.

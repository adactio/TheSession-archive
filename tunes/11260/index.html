Seven comments

This is one of Conal Ó Gráda’s tunes which he taught this year at Cruinniú na bhFliúit. I have tried as accurately as possible to include the ornamental cuts he puts into the tunes. This, along with his glottal stop, is what gives his playing such a great pulse. I should point out that these cuts are exactly as he wrote them down during the workshops. In order to illustrate his variations I have written the tune out twice, exactly as he played it in the class.

The tunes he taught in this class were some of the best illustrations of how to understand Irish music that I have ever seen. I will try to submit transcriptions of all of the tunes he taught his year.

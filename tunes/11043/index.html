Transcription based on Theresa Kavanagh’s recording *The Seventh Veil*. I believe it is an original composition. Her fiddle and Mike McGoldrick’s flute sound fantastic on the album.

Of course, flute and whistle players will want to transpose the unreachable notes in the B-part up an octave. :-)

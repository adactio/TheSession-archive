Tony

A setting of Brian Finnegan’s ‘Tony’ played alongside ‘Two Trees’ on his Album ‘Hunger of the Skin’. He plays it many times through with stunning variations, though I have only essentially transcribed the first time round.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zYI8t0dHPIA
Dedicated to his 90 year old neighbour, Tony.

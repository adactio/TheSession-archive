Gan Ainm

I got this from a private recording of Fermanagh’s Peter Flanagan who played it on tin whistle. The A-part is reminiscent of Jenny Picking Cockles and the B-part starts like Toss the Feathers. But, it still sits out on its own. Nameless. For now.

The structure is ABB.

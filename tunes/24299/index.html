My Reel #2

Hello @NfldWhistler,

Thank you for uploading my tunes and videos!

I took liberty to edit the titles, so they can be easily recognized.

Fun fact: my three reels share a melodic motif hidden in the first 4 bars of each tune.

If Québécois 1-row diatonic accordion players wish to play this particular tune, it is better to transpose a fourth below, in the key of D. But then, it will be too low for whistles and other similar instruments.

Cheers!

Marc-Antoine

Paddy Taylor’s fancy

I was listening to Paddy Taylor’s “Boy In The Gap” recording the other day, and wondered if the 2 unusual slides he plays had been posted. It seems not, so here’s the first, and the 2nd [ which is a real weirdo ] will follow soon. I got the LP direct from “Claddagh” a few years ago for £1.00. The catch was that they didn’t have any album sleeves, so I have no information on the 2 tunes other than the titles. They could be his own compositions, - the title “Taylor’s Fancy” might suggest so - but that’s pure speculation on my part. Any information gratefully received.

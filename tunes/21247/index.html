Re: Katie Jane Of Lewis

Composed by John Clark of Kylesku, Katie Jane’s husband. This originally a 2/4 march in A major, and the Wick Scottish Dnce Band played it with G sharps (and they repeated the parts); I think that Fergie might have as well. Although dance bands are terrible at playing A mix pipe tunes in A, I think this was probably written in A major.

Gan Ainm

This jig I’ve just annotated from a recording that a friend sent a while ago, wondering if I had a name for it. I hadn’t and neither Tunepal or Folkfriends seemed to find a match for it. Then today, on my Facebook links, comes a clip of accordionist, Noel Stapleton playing it, where it’s called Finbarr Dwyer’s. So I thought I’d pop it up here, wondering if anyone has any ideas about it? There are very few Finbarr jigs out there online and I’ve worked out that it’s not either a composition of John’s or Richard’s.

Someone asked me for this tune a while back and I completely forgot to post it - apologies.

The tune comes from ‘The Gunn Book’ and is reproduced in the ‘Hidden Fermanagh’ collection. The transcriber/compiler note that ‘We have not been able to find a reference to this unusually -titled tune in any other collection. It has a Scottish feel to it and may well have been the air to a popular song, presumably a comic song.’

Re: The Lamentation Of The Dead Perch

I agree that, although not strictly like Lannigan’s Ball, part one (in)tends to create a similar athmosphere than…

The formula in bar 2 is found in The Monaghan jig (bar one) but also in several Ulster songs in Irish.

Bars 3 & 4 are repeated, a fourth above, in bars 11 & 12.

The modulation from E to G in the first 3 bars of the turn is reminiscent of 18th c. harp music in the way it is played out.

Bars 13 & 14, in A, seem to have been patched into place (a borrowing from The 10 Penny Bit)

All and all, i find this tune more carelessly thrown together (or carefully mis-matched?) than expertly crafted; so that i find the reference to a comical rhyme, suggested by the title, spot on; Could we have here an unusual case of ironic music, whose “melodic dissonance” was intended to reinforce the satyre?

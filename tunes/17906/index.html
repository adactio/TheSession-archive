Moonlight

Composed by Shetland fiddler Tom Anderson in 1958. He said, “I wrote this tune while sitting outside at Eshaness in the light of a beautiful full moon which seemed to be illuminating a band of peerie trows dancing on a grassy knowe in front of me.” I’m not sure if this was the only time Dr Anderson took hallucinogens. :-)

Two comments

The Deeps Castle

A lovely reel, composed by the banjo player Fergal O‘Hanlon from Wexford. Fergal is a member of the popular folk group ’Green Road’ and is an excellent banjoist, guitarist and vocalist. The Deeps castle is a 14th century Norman towerhouse on the banks of the River Slaney on the Crossabeg side of the river near Killurin, close to Fergal’s home. The castle features in folklore, with the schools collection having stories of buried gold treasure and it is believed that King James II found refuge there on his infamous flight to France following the Battle of the Boyne in 1690.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on March 28th, 2023.

Violet Tulloch

This is a Shetland tune I was taught by Ross Couper.
It is on the Blazin’ Fiddle album The Key, on the track Harris Dance.
I’m not sure who wrote the tune.
Viollet Tulloch is an amazing piano player from Shetland.

The tune is in Bflat major but that didn’t seem to be an option.

Very good reel indeed, thanks.
It has been published in “Ringing Strings” (Shetland Times, 2004), p. 51, where this tune is dated 1972.
By the way, another great tune was written in honour of Violet Tulloch : “Violet”, composed by Ronald Jamieson. It is a beautiful slow air published in J. Murray Neil, The Scots Fiddle, vol. 3, Glasgow, 2004, p. 218. ; as there is a copyright, here are only the first 4 bars :

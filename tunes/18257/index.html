Rawlins Cross Reel

From the playing of Aaron Collis & Duane Andrews, composed by Séamus Creagh.

It’s a variant of a Newfoundland tune, Lake St. John:
https://thesession.org/tunes/18034

Séamus lived in Newfoundland for a while, has influenced the local music, and Rawlins Cross is a band from the island.

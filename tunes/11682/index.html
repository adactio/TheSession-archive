Composed by Josephine Keegan
Source: Quentin Cooper, Eoin O’Neill, Eimear Howley - The Fiddle Case (2009)

I only came across this reel quite recently, but I love it. The solo fiddle playing on “The Fiddle Case” is wonderful, and the Josephine Keegan recording I just found is equally wonderful. The jumps in the B part are tricky at first, but don’t take long to get used to.

http://tunearch.org/wiki/Coolderry_Bridge

COOLDERRY BRIDGE. Irish, Reel. D Major. Standard tuning (fiddle). AABB. Composed by Mullaghbawn, south County Antrim, fiddler and piano player Josephine Keegan (b. 1935). The tune is named for Coolderry, Cul Doire (Oak Wood), a tiny village and townland next to Keegan’s childhood home of Cornonagh. It is deserted now.

Printed source: Keegan (The Keegan Tunes), 2002; p. 27.

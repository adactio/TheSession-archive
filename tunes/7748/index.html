Three comments

This Waltz was written by English fiddle player, Chris Wood.

It first appeared on the Chris Wood/Andy Cutting album ‘Lusignac’, after the song ‘Hares On The Mountains’ and is loosely based on the song’s melody.

Don’t play it too fast - it really needs space to breath.

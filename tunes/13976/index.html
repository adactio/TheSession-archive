Sondheim’s Frolics

I wrote this tune and thought of my fiancé’s guinea pig, Sondheim. If you’ve ever seen a guinea pig “popcorn” -- a gleeful hop and twist when excited -- you’ll understand. For those that haven’t, here’s a YouTube video of someone else’s guinea pig “popcorning”:

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fwwmc5JoEVY

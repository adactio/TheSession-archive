Three comments

The Miramichi Fire

I learned this near pipe tune off of the playing of Michael Anthony MacLean of Cape Breton Island from his CD Good Boy M.A. On Paul Cranford’s website has has it attributed as an Acadian pipe tune. Michael allegedly learned this tune from some Acadian fisherman while they were in Cape Breton. I like the unique feel to the first half of the tune. Another name for it is Willie D.‘s Favourite. Cameron Chisholm learned like tune from his father’s lilting, and named it after his father, (who’s name was Wille D). Brenda Stubbert recorded a version quite different from this version, using the name Willie D.’s Favourite. It can be found in Brenda Stubbert: The Second Collection published by Paul Cranford.
http://www.cranfordpub.com/tunes/canada/MiramichiFire.htm

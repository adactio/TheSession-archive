Slán le Kennedy (jig)

Learnt from Marcas O’Murchu’s flute playing. He cites Brendan Tonra as the composer as well as the source. It’s followed by another jig penned by Tonra, “The Man in the Bog”: https://thesession.org/tunes/8685

The sleeve notes of the recording don’t mention who the title refers to. Possibly the assassinated President?

Setting from The Man Himself

The setting I’ve posted is from the playing of Brendan Tonra, for those interested. And yes, slainte, the title does refer to the slain American President.That event would’ve only been a handful of years after Brendan emigrated here in 1959. What a shock that must’ve been for a new U.S. citizen such as Brendan!

Re: Farewell To Kennedy

Composed 1962

“I wrote this tune the Sunday after Jack Kennedy was shot. I was sitting in the kitchen after mass, waiting for dinner to be ready. I was trying to entertain my first daughter, Barbara, so I wrote the tune for her, and for Kennedy.”
--Brendan Tonra, the Music of Brendan Tonra

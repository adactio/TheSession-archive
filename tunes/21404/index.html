Re: Sheik Billy

Nobody taking us on. See if this might make sense to you Stephen. Finbarr Dwyer recorded his early LPs in Belfast on the “Outlet” label, whose owner was one Billy McBurney. If I can put it this way, “King Billy” is an important historical figure for around half of “Northern Ireland”, so I think “Sheik Billy” might be gently poking fun at that, while giving Billy McBurney a mention at the same time.
This is just something that occurred to me when I first heard the tune and its’ curious title. It could be something else entirely, if anyone knows for certain, I’d love to know the true story.
In any case, once again, thanks for posting this transcription. It’s one of Finbarr’s best IMHO, and deserves to be better known and played more.
Regards, Kenny

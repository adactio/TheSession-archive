What Reel Is This

Transcription of the song ‘What Reel is This?’ from the album ‘Winter’s Day (2004)’ by the band ‘Mithril’.
My apologies for any errors.
Apparently, the song title is a joke referring to the Christmas song ‘What Child is This’.
It makes perfect sense in the context of the album, which begins with ‘Greensleeves’. Enjoy!

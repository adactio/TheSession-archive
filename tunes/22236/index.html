Sally’s Return

This is a tune of my own composition that I’ve notated and uploaded to thesession.org as part of the Wexford Tune Project. The Wexford Tune Project now has nearly 150 tunes, old and new, notated and uploaded to the site and accessible in one place via My Tunebook:
https://thesession.org/members/116353/tunebook.
I named this reel in remembrance of a great friend, fiddle player Sally Rattigan from Wexford, who lived almost all of her adult life in Argentina. In recent years, her return on her annual extended visit to Wexford - Sally’s Return - always seemed to mark the beginning of a summer of music and sessions here in Wexford. When she recently passed away in Argentina, her family made a nostalgic and wonderful trip back to her native place, to spend time with her musical friends (and come and listen to the sessions she loved ) and bring her home….Sally’s Return. I promised her daughter that one of my tunes would bear her name, a simple tribute.

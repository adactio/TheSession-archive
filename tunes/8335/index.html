“Kipeen Scanlon’s Hornpipe” ~ C: Ed Reavy

Here is a note and another take on this hornpipe found online, for comparison an offering other options….

Note: ( Kippen Scanlon ~ ) “Reputed to be one of the fiddlers who influenced Michael Coleman. Mike once told Ed that Kipeen was the best player in Ireland. A sad thing it is that none of his playing was ever recorded for the younger generation of players.” ~ Joe Reavy

Grötvalsen

Transcribed by me from the CD. “Beyond the Stacks” This was another gift from Simon Simonsson since our visit to Sweden 2 years ago.
Phrases A & B can be played as many times as you wish but the A phrase is played at the end, once, just to finish.

In translation it is known as “The Crying Waltz”

Re: Grötvalsen

I think I see what may have happened here. The YouTube clip of Aly Bain and Ale Möller gives the title as ‘Gråtvalsen’, where ‘Gråt’ (pronounced approx. ‘grort’) means ‘cry’ (c.f. the North British ‘greet’, as in ‘greetin’ fer yer ma‘). ’Gröt‘ (pronounced ’grert‘) on the other hand, means ’porridge’. Quite different, unless you find an oatmeal breakfast seriously upsetting.

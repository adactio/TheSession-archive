Re: Lango Lee

This is the tune found in O‘Neill’s Music of Ireland (1903) as “The New Langolee”. The tune before it, “The Old Langolee” is similar but different. The “Lango Lee” published by the Gows equates to “The Old Langolee” in O’Neill.

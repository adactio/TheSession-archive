Trip To Mullagh

Wrote this tune in the summer of - no, not 69 but 89 :)
Miltown Malbay (Willie Clancy Week) was packed and extremely noisy, so we fled to the next village (Mullagh) to have a wonderful session there, maybe the best ever. While we played nobody even whispered, the guests (including kids!) sat there listening with their eyes closed - we never had a night like this again.

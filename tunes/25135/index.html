Four comments

Island Elsk

I first heard this tune performed at the Orkney Folk Festival. The composer was kind enough to give me a copy when I visited a session in Shetland. It’s a sweet duo that deserves to be played and my fiddle students enjoy it as much as I do. It is a slow waltz around 70 bpm and very sentimental. I’m not aware of any recordings yet.

The Flaxdresser

This is number 98 in O’Neill’s 1001.

The title The Flaxdresser is sometimes given as an alternate title for Have A Drink With Me, and the two tunes are certainly very similar:
https://thesession.org/tunes/1922
But seeing as Have A Drink With Me has its own separate listing in O’Neill’s (number 20) I figured it deserved a separate listing here as well.

Ushag Veg Ruy

The title of this tune appears on the session already but, this is a different tune. I found it in 19th century manuscript collections of Manx traditional music as a musical fragment in the early 1990’s and only recently came across it my own notebooks. I’d never properly revised it so it could be played, so, after more than 30 years this is the result!

The full title is: ‘Ushag veg ruy ny moaney dhoo’, in Manx gaelic, or ‘Little red bird of the black peat’. There are several tunes with this title, so the song must have been widely known. As well as being a ballad it was apparently also sung by children as a skipping game. For all you musical nerds, here’s a link to the manuscript source. Open it and search for page 20, top of the page. You’ll see there’s not much to go on. https://manxmusic.com/media/History%20photos/CLAGUE%20COLLECTION%20BK%20A%201.pdf

There’s a possible link with the song to the North American song ‘Where did you sleep last night?’, or ‘In the pines’. I haven’t looked far into that connection so any comments welcome!

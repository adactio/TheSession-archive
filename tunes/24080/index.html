Gan Ainm

This is the hornpipe intro to the Dubliners song Oro Se Do Bheatha ’Bhaile, as played on track 7 (CD1) of their album Celebration (25 years).

↳ Posted by jeff_lindqvist on February 24th, 2024.

O-Ro Se Do Bheatha ’Bhaile

I’m not actually sure if this is a tune by another name. But this is the tune the precedes the Dubliners version of “O-Ro Se Do Bheatha ’Bhaile”. It’s a nice little swaggering tune. If someone knows it by another name please let me know.

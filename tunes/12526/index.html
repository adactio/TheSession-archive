Transcription not 100% Correct

The overwhelming majority of the initial transcription is correct, but there’s a few minor problems. There are only 15 bars in the first verse, and 31 in the second. Also, the first beat of the bar is skewed in the second verse so the real first beat actually occurs on what the transcription shows as the second beat.

I have made the amends, but as I created by Session account today (to enable me to upload the correction), it appears I can’t add it here.

For a working YouTube video of this tune, have a look at: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mRL8wqqkDZs which is where I took my corrections from.

If ever my account permits me to upload the corrected version, I’ll do so

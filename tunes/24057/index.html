Sunburn, Man-Flu, And The S#!+s

This is the first tune in Coig’s set “Bad Day at the Beach” and also performed as the first in Ross Couper and Tom Oakes’ “Sunburn/Harriet’s” set. It’s a very simple but catchy slip jig with easy but nice guitar chords.

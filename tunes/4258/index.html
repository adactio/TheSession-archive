Space Available

Written by Cheticamp fiddler, Marcel Doucet, and, according to Howie MacDonald ’s telling, the title has something to do with the business of driving HGVs in Labrador, bridges and space available. Great march and played regularly in CB recitals, concerts, sessions and wherever fiddlers gather. Glendale go deo!

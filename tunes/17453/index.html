Re: Reel Du Tricentenaire

The real name of this reel is: ‘The Belledune Breakdown’ composed by a Dalhousie , New-Brunswick, fiddler named Clayton Callahan. It’s called a Quebec reel here because every darn rells that Quebec’s musicians play becomes a ‘Quebec Reel’, Soon we might just see Tommy People’s reel, ‘The Oak Tree’ become a Quebec reel.

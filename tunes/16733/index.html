Farewell To Cape Helles

It’s not a waltz, it’s a Retreat March written during the First World War. Probably written for the Great Highland Bagpipe.

↳ Posted by Crudo dLa Masa on February 17th, 2018.

Re: Farewell To Cape Helles

Bagpipe tune indeed, written by PM Willie Ferguson, Cape Helles being in Turkey, part of the Gallipoli campaign in 1915. Bagpipe score here: https://ceolsean.net/content/Ferguss/Book01/Book01%2016.pdf

Two comments

Bushmills Fancy

Follows ‘Galway Bay’ on the recent, fabulous album from Peter Carberry, (and his Daughter Angelina on this & most of the other tracks). This one is composed by Paddy Mills of Belmullet, Co Mayo and the two tunes make lovely set. I’ve tried to transcribe it from Angelina’s Banjo playing and hope I have it about right.

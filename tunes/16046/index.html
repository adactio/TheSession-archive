Dhannsamaid Le Ailean

Simple wee port à beul in the form of a slip jig. From Dàimh’s album “The Hebridean Sessions”.

1st tune of the 2nd track. (The tune on the album is in Emin, so that’s the key I’ve transcribed).

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VWnmI6SRCRA

The lyrics are:

Dhannsadh, gun dannsadh,
Dhannsamaid le Ailean;
Dhannsadh, gun dannsadh,
Dhannsadh Ailean leinn.

Ruidhleadh Màiri Tàillear,
Dhannsadh Màiri Tàillear,
Ruidhleadh Màiri Tàillear,
Gus na dh’fhàs i tinn.

Ged bha Màiri crùbach
Agus car ’na glùinean,
Dhannsadh i gu sunndach
Air an ùrlar ghrinn.

Source: http://www.smo.uhi.ac.uk/gaidhlig/alltandubh/orain/Dhannsamaid.htm
I’m trusting that website to be correct, my gaelic is nearly inexistent.

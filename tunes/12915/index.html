Re: Dan Coakley’s

X:2 offers a good transcription of Na Filí’s rendition of the tune.

If you were looking for traditional Irish tunes in the lydian mode, this is an example of one.

Heptatonic lydian.

The tune is based on the major pentatonic mode “peppered” with the ionian 7th and the lydian 4th.
The raised 4th is not normally favoured in ITM except as a passing note and this is what it is used as here;
i.e.; it could easily be replaced by the 3rd, but placed between two 5ths it brings a bit of melodic nyah/bendy attitude to the tune without sounding too lydian and therefore out of place (or somewhat clumpy as the natural 4th might sound).

The Star, X:2

This tune appears in the Padraig O‘Keeffe manuscripts as an unnamed jig. Its here https://www.itma.ie/features/notated-collections/padraig-okeeffe-mac-aoidh-oconnor-manuscript-e on the ITMA website (complete with video of Andrea Palandri playing it). It also appears in O’Keeffe’s original handwritten tablature in Dan Herlihy’s book “Sliabh Luachra Music Masters” vol 1, page 46, # 14. The main difference between O’Keeffe’s version and the previously posted version is in bars 5 and 6.

The Star, X:3

This is a common tune in the Cape Breton repertoire, but it’s played quite differently from the other settings on this site. Over here people alternatively refer to it as the “Traditional G Jig.”

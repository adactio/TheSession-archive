The Sligo Lancers

A tune from Peter Horan and Fred Finn, the recording may be heard at comhaltasarchive.org, and was presumably an outtake from the LP they made. Various tunes for the Lancers quadrille are to be found in the Irish folk tradition and those all have their counterparts in printed sources, some of them very old; far as I know this is unique, though.

The title is my own invention, to distinguish it from other Lancers tunes on this site.

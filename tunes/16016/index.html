Transcribed from the playing of Steph Geremia from her “The Open Road” release.

↳ Posted by Rosie-123 on April 7th, 2017.

Linnane Terrace, X:2

Essentially X:1, Rosie’s version, except shifted one 1/8th note back in order to have it be accented like Steph’s recording.
The transcription is quite accurate and I changed almost nothing. I did however remove the couple 1/8 rests, as I find quarter notes to be an invitation to take a breath for us flute players anyway.

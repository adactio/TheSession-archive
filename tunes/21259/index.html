Re: Polly Put The Kettle On

This tune originated in Scotland or the north of England, often the same tunes were played in both areas.This is one and is played as a reel not a hornpipe. Reels were occasionally played in the south of England. The term “hornpipe” is vaguely applied to tunes in 4/4.

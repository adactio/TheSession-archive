The Millroad Devil, X:2

Please note that this tune was actually composed by James Barnett.
"James Barnett was born Kirkwall, Orkney in 1847 and died there in 1898. An instrument maker, fiddler and composer, he also held an official post, the Crown Chamberlain of Orkney.
Barnett produced a few decent violins and cellos based on classical patterns with distinctive elongated sound holes and covered in dark brown oil varnish, his works generally date from the 1880’s. Printed label: ‘James Barnett / Kirkwall / 1886’. Barnett’s many compositions include this well known strathspey ‘The Miller O’ Dervil’." David Rattray ( http://www.davidrattrayviolins.co.uk/ )

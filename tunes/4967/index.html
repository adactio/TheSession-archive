Thirteen comments

Lad O’Beirne’s Favourite

Sometimes you hear a tune and you think, “I’ve got to learn that - now”. This is one of them. This comes from Noel Hill’s recently released 2nd solo concertina recording, and is the 2nd reel on what I thought was the outstanding track on it, following after another fine reel, “McGettrick’s”.

↳ Posted by Kenny on September 16th, 2005.

It’s “The Geese” !!!!

I’ve just realised that this is “The Geese In The Bog”, the jig in “C”, shifted up to “D” and played as a reel.

↳ Posted by Kenny on October 2nd, 2005.

Three comments

The Bird Box

This tune is an eyewitness account of the battle of the bird box. There were three species contending. Each bird would swoop down to chase the other away then sit upon the nestbox and sing a song. For my part (a 4th species), scraping out the startings of the swallow and the wren were to help the blue.

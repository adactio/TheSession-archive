The Maid Of Hollywell

I’d always thought that this tune was a Clare tune, but I have my doubts.

It seems to be based on a tune called “Lady Johnstone’s Ree”, (Reel, possibly?) which appeared in “Alexander’s Fifty New Scotch and Irish Reels and Hornpipes arranged for the Violin or Flute by an Eminent Professor” and published circa 1825.

Here is a link to transcripts of the book in abc and pdf formats:

https://www.village-music-project.org.uk/?page_id=803

The tune is no. 12 in the short collection, or Alex50.12.

Here is a link to just the tune:

http://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=www.village-music-project.org.uk/%3Fpage_id=807.no-ext/0012

The order of the parts is reversed in this book.

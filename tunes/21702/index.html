Mazurka For Fernández

This tune was written by my friend Alan Jones, multi-instrumentalist and singer from East Kilbride, in Scotland.

The story of is quite simple. At the Islay Inn session we had a set of two mazurkas, Alan wanted three tunes. In January 2019 he wrote this one and named it after me, which I’m very gratful for. :)

The complete set is “The Munster Cloak (G)/ The Ass in the Graveyard (D)/ Mazurka por Fernandez (A)”

Here’s the full set, I believe it was recorded by Celtic Music Radio, when the Islay Inn session members were invited to play some tunes live.

https://soundcloud.com/david-fernandez-930314279/munster-cloak-mazurkas

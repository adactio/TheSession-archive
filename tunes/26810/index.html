Two comments

Belle Of The Stage

As recorded by Oisín McAuley in “Far from the hills of Donegal” (2007), track n°7, following “Souvenir of Venice”.
Obviously, the tune is inspired by 19th century virtuoso violin répertoire, and involves a lot of left-hand shifting and extension.
(Very) High E and A at the end of the arpeggio can both be played as harmonics overtones on the fiddle, in third position, with extended 4th finger on the E (1/2 string length), and first finger on the A (1/4 string length).

↳ Posted by petroblues on August 31st, 2026.

Belle Of The Stage, X:2

As notated in Ryan’s Mammoth collection, 1883 edition, p. 239.
It is defined as a Clog ; Edwin Christie is mentionned as composer ; the tune seems to originate from Lancashire.
The source is available at imslp.org and shows helpful fingerings.

Esme’s Dream

I wrote this tune for an 18 year old Scottish clarsach player, Esme MacIntyre. Esme was dying and had a wish that tunes be written for her so that a CD of clarsach music could be recorded to raise money for a teen’s cancer unit in Scotland. (The CD has now been released and has helped to fund the unit dear to Esme’s heart.) I give it here, not as arranged for clarsach but as it could more generally be played……….. hope it helps to keep Esme’s memory alive.
I can’t discover how to add slurs - but it’s supposed to be quite a slow gentle tune - so please adapt …….

The Five Pillars

This is a fantastic Pádraig Rynne tune from his latest release “Begin With The End In Mind”.

On the record, there’s a bunch of lovely variations he does with Jarlath Henderson on pipes (for example, in the first time through the A section, they play the first bar as “E2A BAG”, which is one of my favorite parts of the tune. But here, I just transcribed the “standard” version that’s played most consistently, and would be what you’d play in a session.

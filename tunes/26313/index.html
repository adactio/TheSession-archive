The Irish Waterman

23 years ago, there was an amusing post about a tune called “The Irish Waterman” - read it:
https://thesession.org/tunes/1312

This is a real tune of that name found in John Anderson’s First Selection (Edinburgh 1789).

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on January 29th, 2026.

The Irish Waterman, X:2

This setting is in O’Neill’s Music of Ireland (Chicago 1903), and has changed key and dropped the repeats. It is given the title “The Jolly Young Waterman.”

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on January 29th, 2026.

The Irish Waterman, X:3

Another setting from Alexander McGlashan’s Collection of 1781, this time simply called “The Waterman.”

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on January 30th, 2026.

Off Day

Cool reel composed by Olaf Sickmann. This tune is in ‘’New Irish Tin Whistle Tunes.‘’
About the tune:
‘’I wrote this one in the studio. After four days of recording I was completely exhausted and glad that we’d planned in a free day. It was great to have time to recuperate before going back to finish off the recordings.‘’

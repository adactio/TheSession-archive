Four comments

Pat and Al’s

This lovely jig in a is written by Liz Carroll, on her album ‘Lost in the Loop’. I’ve seen her play it live in Cork; she was delightfully natural and unaffected, and many of her tunes reflect this aspect of her personality, although this one here does have some tricky bits.

↳ Posted by petemay on June 25th, 2004.

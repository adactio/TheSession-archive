Two comments

Brudmarsch Efter Axel Inge

Learned from Josefina Paulson at a nyckelharpa workshop. I play it followed by Husby Brudmarsch - another tune from the same event. They don’t really go that well together, but they sort of work if you take this one fairly slow and free and pick up the tempo a bit for the next one. Josefina is a great advocate for tunes from Västmanland, as this one is.

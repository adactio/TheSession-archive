Second Victory

This jig was composed by Paddy O‘Brien shortly after the Ormond Street Ceili Band secured its second All-Ireland title at Fleadh Cheoil na hÉireann (Source ~ The Definitive Collection of the Music of Paddy O’Brien)

Great tune, found it here on this great file of a session in Gortahork, C. Donegal in 1984 featuring players such as Dermot Byrne, Mairead Ni Mhaoinaigh, Frankie Kennedy, Ciaran Curran & others. It is about 39m 30s in:

https://soundcloud.com/pashaone/session-of-the-century-1

Girls On The Line #1

This is from the track “Girls on the Line” on the album “Shamrock City” by Solas. The track has 2 tunes in it: this tune, then another I’ve also submitted as Girls On The Line #2 (then the track goes back to this tune). I cannot find any source for it, so I’m assuming Seamus Egan wrote it since it has that inimitable Solas touch to it.

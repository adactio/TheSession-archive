“Wee’l All Lye Together” ` the Browne Manuscripts? ~ G Major

“Furness Tradition Tune Book”
Mike Kermode, January 2001

There isn’t any ‘information’ in this book, but there are the dots for a number of tunes, including this as Major, and reportedly from something called the ‘Browne Manuscripts’? It also includes some melodies by more recently active musicians ~ Carolyn Francis, John Hazlehurst, Wal & Mike Willoghby, who are listed as having “dontated tunes to this collection.”

This is from the back ~

Furness Tradition Ulverston

Furness Tradition is a voluntary organization and aims to encourage creative participation in the social and working traditions of Low and High Furness, and associated boundaries. ~ etc.

For further information contact: emails with the @ removed and replaced with ~

trad ~ hartown.demon.co.uk
kmode ~ onetel.net.uk

I don’t know if this collection is still available. My G Mixolydian transcription above is not that far from theirs except they have it as G Major… An A4 collection of 67 tunes, there are no page numbers.

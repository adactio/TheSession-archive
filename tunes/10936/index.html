Five comments

Fine music from a lesser-known master..

I found this beauty on a wonderful fiddle & flute LP simply titled “McGreevy & Cooley” (John & Seamus, respectively). It’s sadly out of print, but is available for download here:

http://ceolalainn.blogspot.com/

Unfortunately, I was unable to surmise the tune’s authorship (I would assume it to be John McGreevy’s, but I can’t say for certain if that’s the case; If anybody knows, please post in this comments section!), but it’s a fine tune any way you slice it. They pair it on the recording with “Father Kelly’s” (it’s this one:

https://thesession.org/tunes/6899

I’ll be posting their excellent setting in that comments section if it hasn’t been done already). As always, enjoy!

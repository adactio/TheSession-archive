The Oystercatcher

Lovely air written by Dr John Holliday, Isle of Tiree.

↳ Posted by coppers and brass on February 10th, 2014.

“The Gaelic language is still quite strong among the older people and I have worked at becoming reasonably fluent in the language. My wife and children both speak Gaelic but don’t use it very much. I have a lot of interests outside medicine, with music being particularly important to me. I co-founded a traditional music teaching festival which now is in its tenth year and we have about a hundred children attending every year. They are taught a mixture of musical instruments, dancing and singing and it has helped a revival of traditional music and arts on the island.”

http://rosiedonovan.com/Holliday.htm

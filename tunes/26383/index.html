Three comments

Dancing In The Glen

The composer, Mick Denieffe, sent me through two versions of this lovely barndance yesterday. This is his “version 1” setting and I’ll post his alternative (and slightly more elaborate) “version 2” setting shortly. Incidentally, I’ve been playing the tune shifted up to G (with a few minor tweaks to some of the phrases) and I find it’s brighter in that key - but also considerably easier for my stubborn fingers to manage on mandolin. I’ve asked Mick if he has any objections to my posting my G Major take on his tune but until he replies I’ll sit tight.

↳ Posted by Aidan Crossey on February 27th, 2026.

Twelve comments

Mixed Blooms

This reel was composed/written down by my father, Martin Ruane, on 2 April 1964. It was recorded by Dad on a reel to reel tape at home, date unknown. I have submitted the written version. Will Harmon has been of great assistance in transcribing the recorded version which I hope to include here. I will also be making the recorded version soundclip available to members. If any member likes the tune and can record him/herself playing it, I would love to hear it. Regards, Mairtin

A big thank you to Jeremy for fixing the audio problem. He found a few notes that were entered in error and were preventing the tune from playing, removed them, and now you can listen to the tune, as written down and then as played by my father. He composed the tune when he was 70 and his self-recording on a home reel-to-reel recorder would have been made much later when he was in his 80s. Not being any good at transcribing myself, a member here at thesession.com, offered to do so. I believe it was Will Harmon and I thank him for that.
So, you first hear the reel as written, AABB, and then the way he played it.

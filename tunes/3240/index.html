The Holloway Road Fling

This tune comes from flute duet album “Double-Barrelled” by Kieran O’Hare and John Skelton. Obviously, it’s a reel setting of “The Cat that Ate the Candle” (not F. Kennedy tune) or “The Cow that Ate the Blanket”:
https://thesession.org/tunes/1430

Actually I haven’t got the recording yet, but the sample clip is generously long, so I could learn it. Both jig and reel settings are recorded in a set.

I’m hearing some small but not insignificant differences in what’s played the first go round on the album. E.g., the G in the Part A 3rd measure strikes my ear as odd, but I’ve listened closely, repeatedly, and am sure that’s the note played.

Re: The Cow That Ate The Blanket

The tune posted above by slainte is a reel version of the jig “The Cow That Ate The Blanket”, presumably created by John Skelton, so I’ve changed the name. It’s the middle tune in a track from John and Kieran O’Hare’s recording “Double-Barreled”, posted here on Youtube :
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VWBV7woQnEo
Opens with the jig, changes to the same tune played as a reel at around 1min [ posted by slainte ], and “The Holloway Road Fling” - posted recently by Bribak starts around 2m11s.
To avoid further confusion - nothing at all to do with the reel of the same name which I posted some years back.

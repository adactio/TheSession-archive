Dancing At Kilbrew

Composed by Catherine McEvoy, transcribed from her own playing:
https://youtu.be/irapfkHt8m4?t=63
I transcribed this in response to a request in Discussions, and glad I took the time to do so, as it’s a charming tune. This is the ‘skeleton’ of the tune, based more or less on the first time through – Catherine adds different variations each time round. The bottom Ds in bars 8, 12 & 16 could be played an octave up (and sounds good either way) – it is hard to tell from the recording which octave these notes are being played in.

Eight comments

Kilfenora reel

I got this from the Moher CD, “Over the Edge.” It’s a fun tune to launch into at a session because everyone will think you’re playing “Speed the Plough” at first. Then they realize something’s different and enjoy the quirkiness of the tune. It’s great craic to play and gives you a fun ride in the second part.

↳ Posted by Phantom Button on August 21st, 2004.

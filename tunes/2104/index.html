“Buddy’s Jig”

This is one you where it would have been wise to check titles before adding this, but, hey, there’s really no reason we can’t have more than one “Buddy’s Jig”. Howie MacDonald, who is well acquainted with the MacMasters has composed one for Buddy, with this very name, though also known as “The Buddy Jig”… There are a few, but the man is an inspiration…

This one, at least the first part, has more the feel of a song about it. Gian’s suggestions would improve it some…

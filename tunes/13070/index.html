Re: John Collin’s

Hello nmcshea, and I hope you’re enjoying your mandolin journey.

First, I think your tune is The New Roundabout, the first tune in the set, which is played before the tune on this page. The New Roundabout actually has its own page at https://thesession.org/tunes/2454

Next, you have a few accidentals (e.g. the d sharps, G sharps) which give the tune an almost Eastern European sound, and I don’t think that was what you were going for. If you were wishing to transcribe from the album you linked to, the first line would be something like this:

T: The Roundabout
R: polka
M: 2/4
L: 1/8
K: A
|:A>B cd|eA ce|de/d/ cB|Ac E2|
A>B cd|eA ce|de/d/ cB|1A2 A2:|2A2 Ac||
|:BA/B/ cB|Ac ec|Bc/d/ cB|AF FE|
BA/B/ cB|Ac ec|Bc/d/ cB|A2 A2:|

(I don’t hear it as A minor, by the way.)

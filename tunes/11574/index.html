Five comments

Lament for Frankie

I’ve submitted this effort in F although the original composition is in Bb.
It’s such a free moving Air that it’s impossible to reflect it in abc.
However, it’s a base to start from and express as you see fit.

Hope you enjoy this beautiful piece by the hugely talented Seamus Egan and Winifred Horan.

Feel free to transpose into Bb if preferred :-) (it does sound better lower down)

Finally the two play together

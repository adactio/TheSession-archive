Two comments

Granny Barnes’

A tune composed by accordion maestro John Whelan. John’s father Denis was a Wexford man from Blackwater, so some of John’s compositions pay homage to his Wexford roots, hence the title of this tune.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on March 11th, 2023.

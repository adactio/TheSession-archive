Re: The Lovat Scouts

There is a Dundee folksong called “The Back o’ Reres Hill” to its own melody. The entertainer Andy Stewart published his version of the tune in the 1950s with a very different tune - in fact “The Lovat Scouts.” That setting then entered the folk tradition and was collected from Dundee singer Stuartie Foy by Peter Shepheard.

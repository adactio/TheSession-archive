The Buffett Double

Newfoundland reel.

Great Big Sea play it on their album “The Hard and the Easy”, and have it listed as a Rufus Guinchard tune.

Might be one of Baxter Wareham’s. He’s from Buffett Harbour, which was abandoned during resettlement ( https://www.mun.ca/mha/resettlement/harbour_buffett_1.php ), and seen a few references to him for the tune.
https://www.discogs.com/Baxter-Wareham-Buffett-Double/release/13442375

There was a discussion about it here some time ago, might be a variant of The Laurel Tree ( https://thesession.org/tunes/883 ). See:
https://thesession.org/discussions/33201

Shanneyganock, first tune (Harbour Buffett was on the South Side of Long Island in Placentia Bay):
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v_GWQhVSofQ

Another:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ct0JwaDAJpg

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on May 24th, 2019.

The Buffett Double, X:3

From the playing of Great Big Sea.

Subtle differences from X2 (which has been updated to represent Shanneyganock’s playing), the most significant being how the A Part ends the first time through.

The tune is also related to The Blackthorn Stick Reel (https://thesession.org/tunes/18345 ).

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on September 10th, 2019.

Jim’s Jig, X:4

From the playing of Shoreline.

A few noticeable differences from the more typical version, though, definitely closer than the Buffet Double variant “Lloyd House’s Tune” (https://thesession.org/tunes/21242 ).

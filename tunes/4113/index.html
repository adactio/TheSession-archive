Kitty The Hare

From the repertoire of the esteemed Mr. McConnell. Also recorded at one time by Sean Keane[Galway] and others. A fine whistle tune.

Re: Kitty The Hare

Hi Daniel - the LP sleeve from Cathal’s “On Lough Erne’s Shore” says :
“Two* little-known reels from near my home in Co. Fermanagh, and still played today. I got them both from Big John McManus, who got them from his uncle, the late Hugh Gunn.”
Cathal was the source of many tunes recorded by “Altan”.
[ * - the first was “Big John’s” reel, which - without checking - I think was also recorded by “Altan” at some point.]

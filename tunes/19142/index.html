Penobscot Memory

I learned this at a Scottish session around 1981, it’s stayed with me ever since.

Just now looked it up, it’s said to have been written by Vince O Donnell in 1981. I didn’t know that when I heard it, it was a new tune.

My setting follows how I first heard it played. When I looked it up on YouTube just now I can hear an E for the first note in bar 27 rather than F#, something to be aware of, but I left my setting be both on the grounds of staying to the way I heard it, and because I think the F# sounds better.

Penobscot Memory, X:3

I’m Vince O’Donnell - The setting I’m submitting here is the tune as I wrote it in 1981. I’m delighted to see how it’s morphed into interesting other versions over time, but I wanted the original to be available here, too. The initial snap, the various ties across bars and the chords are the essence in my idea of this tune, but the other variations are interesting, too.

↳ Posted by Vince O’Donnell on July 17th, 2021.

Re: Penobscot Memory

Thank you so much Vince!

I’m sorry that my garbled version, and not the correct one, stands at the top here.

It’s surprising how soon after you wrote it that it was being played in sessions. It was 1981 or possibly 1982, in that window.

The session was here in Los Angeles. It might seem odd to some, but (at least at that time) a person could attend Scottish events of one kind or another several times a week. The Scottish Fiddlers Of Los Angeles is said to be the first Strathspey & Reel Society outwith Scotland, and the Los Angeles Branch of the Royal Scottish Country Dance Society was the largest in the world, with nearly 500 members.

The Meghan Jigs (from hawaii)

This tune was composed by Clark Ratliffe, a flute player from honolulu, in 2004 for is daughter Meghan, when she asked, “why aren’t there any Irish tunes with Meghan in the title?”

In hawaii we follow Meghan’s Fancy with Fancy Meghan. They were originally one double jig, but they work better as single jigs, with Meghan’s Fancy first.

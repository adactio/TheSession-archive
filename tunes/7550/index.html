A composition by Brendan Mulvihill (I hope he doesn’t mind me posting it). I transcribed it from his playing where he pairs it with Cock and the Hen:
https://thesession.org/tunes/93
(My version of it in the comments.)

Both tunes have a nice non-symmetrical rhythm to them; I think of them as anti-slip-jig slip jigs. Quite a good set!

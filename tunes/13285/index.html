One comment

Furnill’s Frolics

Track 1b from the recent recording “Forgotten Gems” by piper Padraig McGovern and Peter Carberry on button accordeon.
This tune appeared in the P. W. Joyce Collection. The lads have stuck quite stringent to the old text and added only few little extras in their playing.
The end note C appears low on the accordeon and an octave higher on the chanter of the pipes.

A Happy Day In June

Lovely slow air by Lauren MacColl. Transcribed from her recording at https://soundcloud.com/lauren-maccoll/a-happy-day-in-june.

↳ Posted by TomWyllie on February 7th, 2022.

A Happy Day In June, X:2

This is more of a slow jig, composed by Lauren MacColl for Jenna Reid and Iain Sandilands’ wedding day.
This transcription (X: 2) is taken from Rant’s 2016 recording (on Reverie) and is phrased slightly differently to Lauren MacColl’s original (see bandcamp link above), but it should also make more sense as to where the downbeats are. Rant play the first part repeated but not the second.
And, of course, the tempo is slower than PLAY AUDIO gives here.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on March 27th, 2022.

This is a reel I wrote a couple of years ago, in 2000 to precise.

I never really had a proper name to go with it (well, I had a few, but they all came and went), but given the fact that it was written in first year of the new millenium (what a weird summer that was, doh!), I thought it would make for a good name. I believe the tune is also known as ‘The Staggering Reel’ or ‘Phrase Dance’ to some players here in Germany.

There is a lot of syncopation going on here, and these syncopated notes should be played with a slight staccato feel to them.

When I back up the tune, I usually use a strong, funky Em7-based riff,

We play this in tandem with a modified version of Sporting Paddy, where I incorparetd some syncopation and chromatic passing tones. The whole thing goes under ‘Grooving Paddy’… ;)

This tune….

… is one of my own. It was originally put together when I was stuck in a static queue of traffic (not for the first time) on the way down to Cambridge. I often play it on a sopranino recorder, but using the same fingering as I would on a soprano instrument. Consequently, the key shifts up to Cmaj which, I think, adds a bit of extra variety.

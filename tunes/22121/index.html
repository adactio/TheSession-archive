Two comments

The Mill Of Roleen

Another old Wexford tune from The Feis Ceoil Collection of Irish Airs, published in 1914. The tune was collected, like the ‘98 March, from the lilting of Miss Ellen Newport from Rathangan in Co. Wexford in the late 19th century. The melody was I believe subsequently adapted to put an suitable air to an old hurling song in the Yola dialect of that area for the well-known traditional ballad singer and collector Paddy Berry, who can be heard singing ’A Yola Hurling Song’ here in this old RTE clip:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RFl9ptuxd8s&ab_channel=snadhghus.
Roleen is a place name, very close to the village of Rathangan in south Wexford.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 10th, 2022.

The Mill Of Roleen, X:2

A version of the tune adapted with a more standard double jig format and some triplets and rolls added for interest. As I’ve posted before these old archival tunes are effectively ‘frozen in time’ when they were collected in the mid to late 19th century and therefore have not evolved and had variations added etc since they disappeared from the local repertoire, most likely in the 1920’s or 30’s. I remember asking local fiddle maestro Gerry Forde, who was an All-Ireland fleadh winner in the 1960’s, about these tunes that had been collected and if he had heard or played them as a young man. He said that the tunes were still in the repertoire of the old mummer’s bands, but the tunes had limited appeal when the recordings of Coleman etc came along and with (then) younger musicians listening to tunes from elsewhere on radio etc. So these old tunes were consigned to the printed form that had been collected.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 10th, 2022.

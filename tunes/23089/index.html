Three comments

Jolly Polly Schottische

This Schottische is as notated in the ‘modern’ section of the M.F. Power manuscript from Kilmore in County Wexford. That part of the manuscript is c.1920 and is mainly re-transcribed tunes from printed sources although internet and tunearch.org searches haven’t matched this one. Perhaps someone else knows a bit more about it?

Re: Jolly Polly Schottische

Hi Dave, I’d absolutly agree. I’ve notated the tunes ‘as notated’ in the faded old manuscript. It’s hard to tell with some of the notation what the key signature was when it was written in the 1850’s or whatever. There’s also clearly ‘errors’ in the notation made by the original hand notating the material for some tunes. For example the previously unpublished jig from the manuscript Scotch Nancy is notated in D with two sharps but it clearly ‘sounds’ better with a C natural:
https://thesession.org/tunes/22009.
Once I uploaded the ‘original warts and all’ setting from the manuscript, I put a ‘second setting’ underneath with my own ‘take’ on the tune. I’ll have to get round to doing some reworking of some of the material I transcribed from the manuscript in the past weeks. Feel free to upload a second setting with the changes you’ve indicated if you’ve a mind to. It will be a while before I’ve time to go back on this old manuscript material as I’m snowed under with additional new Wexford compositions from various sources that need initial notation from recordings etc etc. You’d be doing me a service if you’ve time!!

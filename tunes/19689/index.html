Dad’s, X:3

This is the tune as I’m sure Shona wrote it and how it’s meant to be played. I think the versions above are more transcriptions of Talisk’s arrangement of the tune, including all the intros and extra bits they put in for effect.
I got this from the Box On album by Shona Kipling and Damien O’Kane, and I’m pretty sure it’s Shona who wrote this tune. It’s a banger.
I have vague memories of learning this tune many years ago, perhaps at Folkworks summer school some time in the late 00s? Couldn’t say when or from who though.

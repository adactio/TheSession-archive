Reel by Frank McArdle of Glasgow (thanks to Rosh for tracking down the title), from the playing of the St. Roch’s Ceili Band who play The Salamanca as the first tune. Posted on youtube.

When chording the tune, I note that St. Roch’s plays a C chord at the very end of the B part.

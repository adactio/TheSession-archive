A’ Sireadh Spors

This is the title track from the great first album by piper Dr. Angus MacDonald. The name translates as ‘Looking For Fun’. The tune is also known as Inverinate House. I’ve put the accents as Dr Angus plays them but the tune might by played straighter at a session.

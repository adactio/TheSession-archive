Background on Bridget Cruise

I learned that song today, and the teacher told this story:

Bridget Cruise met Carolan when he was still a boy (before his blindness which came at age 18). He fell in love with her, but as she came from a noble family they would never be allowed to get together. Anyway he could not forget Bridget and wrote 3 planxties for her.

Derek Bell set

The recording listed at https://thesession.org/recordings/3056 (Derek Bell. Carolan’s receipt - Shanachie, 1987) has an entry for Bridget Cruise which covers a set of three tunes selected (probably) from O’Sullivan. Carolan: life, times and music of an Irish harper vol.1 p.179-180.
As played they are: Bridget Cruise (third air) / Bridget Cruise (second air) / Bridget Cruise (fourth air) / Bridget Cruise (third air) (reprise).
The tune shown here is the third air.

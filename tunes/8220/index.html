Many tunes

Many tunes have similar characteristics like Levantne’s Barrel.
Posted here by “c.”

https://thesession.org/tunes/3493

At first I didn’t hear the similarities between this and Levantine’s, but now I do. The fiddlers Companion lists many possible variants/sources of the tune family… The Tullamore Piper, The Cows a Milking… Levantine’s. The Pennsylvania versions are more straight and reel like without the accidentals of Levantines and (like many mid-appalachian tunes of old) it has more of a Scottish/Irish way about it rather than a swingy barrel of beer way about it.

Jehile Kirkhuff with uilleann pipes

Unfortunately the JK recordings mentioned above can no longer be accessed, but I saved it all for myself years ago. One recording of the Bummer’s Reel on there was a duet with Tom Standeven playing the pipes; they made a tape together too, mostly Tom playing piano but on numbers like the Liverpool Hornpipe he’d play pipes or flute. Terrific music, Jehile had a ton of interesting tunes. He and Tom met at some festival in the 1960s. A piping friend from Scranton was startled that I knew about JK. Not exactly a household name even in Old Time circles.

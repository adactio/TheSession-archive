Three comments

This tune was written by Josephine Keegan. It’s interesting to see how her version varies from that which has been handed on. The following is a transcription from her recent publication, The Keegan Tunes. (Forgive the lack of headers; it’s in G Major.)

DB,D GBd|gfg dcB|ABc dcA|BGF G2:||

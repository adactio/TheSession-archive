Two comments

Shoogle’s Tune

I learned this from Shooglenifty’s The Arm’s Dealers’ Daughter album. On the album Michael McGoldrick plays Uilleann Pipes on this tune. This tune was composed by Shoogle’s Mandolin player Luke Plumb. It is not really a hornpipe but a cut time slow march (or 2/4, but I transposed it in cut).

↳ Posted by Avery on December 27th, 2006.

Tune for Bartley

The Bartley the tune refers to is Bartley O’Donnell who plays fiddle. Bartley is currently living in Adelaide South Australia, I had a gig with him a couple of weeks ago when it was about 40 degrees in the shade. My reed hasn’t been the same since. He is a great player and used to play in a band with Luke Plumb.

↳ Posted by JACKB on December 28th, 2006.

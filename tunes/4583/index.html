Re: Campbell’s

Wow, this is the tune that The Flanagan Brothers recorded as the bookends to a comic sketch entitled “The Flanagans Chase the Banshee” in 1926. They took it at almost a reel tempo, but it’s otherwise the same melody note for note. It’s apparently entered the old-time tradition as “The Flanagans’ Banshee” or “Chase the Banshee” as well. Funny how tunes surface in odd places.

Here’s a link to the recording:
https://wardirishmusicarchives.com/Exhibits/Irish-Music-on-78rpm-Discs/McGraw-78-00-985-Flanagan-Brothers-The-Flanagans-Chase-the-Banshee-comic-sketch-and-reel.htm

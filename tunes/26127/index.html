Yvon Dumont

A Métis fiddle tune, popular in Western Canada. Written by John Arcand and dedicated to Yvon Dumont, the first Métis lieutenant governor of Manitoba.
I transcribed this version from the playing of Patti Kusturok:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OF4mTM_8lKU

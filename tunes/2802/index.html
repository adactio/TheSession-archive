The Pandy Clock

The Pandy Clock is a clock in my hometown of Merthyr Tydfil in Wales.It was built by the Crawshay family who owned the ironworks in the town.It’s a tower,but the clock only has three faces,facing towards Cyfarthfa castle,where the Crawshay’s lived.The side facing the ironworker’s cottages in the town is blank,so that the riff-raff couldn’t see the time.

An Léimras

From the Brighde Chaimbeul set on her album “The Reeling”

↳ Posted by Gather on September 29th, 2022.

An Léimras, X:3

This is setting #1 transposed to the same key as the recording. Brighde plays it in Eb on her C small pipes, drones tuned to Bb and Eb (I think) and presumably using tape on her five finger and two finger holes to get Eb and Ab. Had to choose F dorian as the key as Eb wasn’t an option in the drop down.

↳ Posted by bagpie on June 10th, 2024.

An Léimras, X:4

And this is setting #1 transposed to C major for pipers who read or think in A. Some border pipes can cross finger the C natural and F natural, small pipers would need to half cover the five finger and two finger notes with tape to replicate what Brighde is doing. This would sound pretty horrible against A drones, you’d need to tune them to G/C if you can or just turn them off.

↳ Posted by bagpie on June 10th, 2024.

An Léimras, X:5

And finally setting #1 in D for pipers who have a high B. This has the benefit of no tape required or alternate drone tunings. You could also keep it in this key and replace the high B with another note to play it on any Scottish pipe with no funny business required. :)

↳ Posted by bagpie on June 10th, 2024.

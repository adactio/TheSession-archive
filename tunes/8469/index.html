You Rogue You Dar’n’t Meddle Me

A great A minor reel that drives really nicely and that deserves some serious meddling.

S:Dance Music of Ireland, 2nd Collection, 1873; No. 72, pg. 32.; O’Neill (Dance Music of Ireland: 1001 Gems), 1986; No. 632, pg. 114, (tune #632).

Cf. additional notes for this tune in its listing at http://www.ibiblio.org/fiddlers/YN_YZ.htm

From Dingle With Love

A ripper of a slide composed by Jamie Smith, transcribed off Barrule’s second album, *Manannan’s Cloak*. The B part reminds me of “The Kings of Kerry”. I’ve included transposed notation in Gmaj as a whistle-friendly option.

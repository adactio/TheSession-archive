Didn’t She Dance And Dance, X:2

Another nice variation of the slide, I’m not sure how I came upon it but I’m glad I did.
It’s commonly played with Denis Murphy’s less common slide ( https://thesession.org/tunes/7617 ).
I like it after Chase Me Charlie, particularly the Sliabh Luachra version, which starts the tune “gfe”. The transition is already there for you. ( https://thesession.org/tunes/1218#setting28387 )

Here’s Jimmy Doyle and others with a take on the set: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oGZY86fB0AQ
And Julia Clifford, after an air: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWDzITNz7Tc

↳ Posted by Thady Quill on April 15th, 2016.

Re: Didn’t She Dance And Dance

Padraig O’Keeffe gave a setting of this tune (without a name) to one his pupils:
https://www.itma.ie/digital-library/score/padraig_okeeffe_mcauliffe_ms_004
Intriguingly the turn seems to be the one associated with the Goulin Slide https://thesession.org/tunes/7617
These two slides are often played together (e.g. Johnny O’Leary The Trooper). Where did the exchange of partners occur?

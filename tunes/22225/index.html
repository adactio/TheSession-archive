Two comments

Paddy Hayes’

Another unnamed reel from Paddy Hayes‘ album, ’Return to Kilty‘. It’s the first of two reels on a track titled ’A Paddy Hayes Selection’. The album notes credits Paddy with their composition.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on September 5th, 2022.

Re: Paddy Hayes’

I recently had a good deal of contact with Paddy Hayes‘ family, while compiling ’The Wexford Tunebook‘, which includes all of Paddy’s known compositions. His daughter Ann Marie provided me a title from Paddy for one of his untitled reels. However they requested that this be titled (by them) as ’Lovely Lizzie, Leitrim Lass’ in tribute to Paddy’s wife.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 27th, 2024.

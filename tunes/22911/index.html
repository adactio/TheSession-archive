Two comments

Xuan de Mieres

This is traditional asturian march, also known as Marcha de Xuan de Mieres ou Xuanín de Mieres.
It is the first tune of the ‘Asturian Set’ of ‘The Reed Than Bends in The Storm’ by Mike McGroldrick, John McCusker and John Doyle. It plays it there in D thus the original key is C.

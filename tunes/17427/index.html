Marcha De Riosa

First tune of track 9 of Felpeyu’s album, Cerquina.

From Felpeyu’s website:
The march is a variation on one written by Llan de Cubel fiddler Simon Bradley and came out during a session between Lisardo and Dolfu R. Fernández. An example of distorsion due to playing by ear.

There’s a few variations here and there that I haven’t transcribed.

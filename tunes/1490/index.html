Re: The Westcoaster

Yes, Iain25, it’s always a toss up of whether to write hornpipes straight, for ease of reading, with the understanding that they are swung, or write them pointed (as in dot/cut), as you have done, which indicates swing (but doesn’t get it rhythmically right either). I’ve also often heard this played with some snaps (cut/dot).

I’m thinking perhaps your G flat in the fourth bar of the second part should be a G natural.

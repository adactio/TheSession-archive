Re: Miss Thompson’s

Miss Thompson (my great grand mother) was daughter of Errington Thompson who made the silver and ivory pipes that Joe Hutton had. Family tradition has it that Miss Thompson helped turn the (hand powered) lathe that made them. They lived at Sewingshields in Northumberland. That set of pipes was made for Pears John Liddell (of Liddell’s Hornpipe fame) who became her husband. I doubt that the tune that are attributed to Errington Thompson, above, were written by him (our family would be full of it). The local view would attribute these two at least to Whinham who was well know to them.
Christopher Wancke

The Queen’s Welcome To Invercauld

A Scottish March, composed by J. Scott Skinner & published in 1884.

I was watching a video last night of the Scottish band The Whistlebinkies, playing a concert in Haddo House, Aberdeenshire, with Stuart Eydmann on Concertinas & one of the tunes they played was this cracking wee 4 part March … which I just had to learn & record today, as you do.

Played on English Concertina, Fiddle, Tenor Banjo & Bodhran.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bIH1E79M87U

Cheers,
Dick

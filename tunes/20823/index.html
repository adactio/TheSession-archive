Two comments

In Flight

Written by Anders Lillebo and transcribed from a single he released in March 2020. There weren’t tune names listed besides calling the set “In Flight” so for now I’ve labeled both the jig and the reel with that name. He plays it on the piano accordion, but it also plays well flatpicking on DADGAD guitar (capo 2).
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0_l57ZBqC6U

↳ Posted by b.maloney on October 17th, 2013.

The Iron Man

He was William McHardy, Skinner’s benefactor and friend, for whom he also wrote The Laird O’ Drumblair. Tune not usually played with repeats: JSS himself didn’t play any on the early 20th C recording of him. ( Recordings made between 1905 and 1922 available on a CD from Temple Records). Flows better if played straight through, I think.

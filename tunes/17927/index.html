Lalabee

A quite different sound can be heard on Lalabee, a tender tune written by Austrian hurdy-gurdy player Matthias Loibner, who lent his disarmingly subtle playing to the track. The playing is exquisite, almost dreamlike. Sorry for the bars. Enjoy it!

It’s just, as you may guess, a lullaby. lalabee was - in my imagination - a bee who is a bit “lala” … so evoking funnny dreams. I’ve sung this song probably a thousand times for my boy, when he was little.

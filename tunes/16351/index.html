Wideford Hill

Wideford Hill’s a hill in mainland Orkney easily visible from Kirkwall. There is a chambered cairn on one side of it and it has excellent views.

I agree it’s a ramble of a tune (and sadly it is mine so you wouldn’t have heard it). It’s certainly not apt for an
Irish session music website. I was one of several people writing tunes for a composition class in 2002 or ’3
and a few of us had our tunes played by Hadirgaan (Douglas Montgomery’s K’wall Grammar School band
that you may have known). They made a great job of all our tunes, altering rhythms a bit and adding
accompaniments as they wished - all sounded attractive. Mine isn’t bad in C minor on piano. Hole in my
Pocket (posted here) is also mine. If truly objectionable, I can remove though I’m not sure how?

I enjoy posting tunes in general but seeing a tune of mine in notation v. occasionally is a bit too beguiling and
I’ve not learned to use other websites to produce scores. I’ll try more!!

Don’t know WHAT went wrong when I was trying to post this tune of mine, I seemed to put a jinx on it! BUT I think, with this edit, it is how I intended it - whether good, bad or middling. It did sound fine played by Hadhirgaan (as noted above… but I left out their middle “h”).

For interest, Hadhirgaan is a band formed from Kirkwall Grammar School pupils, so it changes personnel as musicians leave school and are replaced by younger ones on the way up (musically) - they’re encouraged to write their own tunes. I think Hadhirgaan translates as “Let’s have a go” or something similar!

Doyle’s Polka

This was recorded by the Boys of the Lough on their 1992 album ‘The Fair Hills of Ireland’ under the title “Maurice O‘Keeffe’s”, in the key of G. It can also be found in Johnny O’Leary’s Collection as “Jimmy Doyle’s No.2” in the key of D.

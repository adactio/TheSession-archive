John Egan’s Polka

Here’s the third and last tune published in the autumn 2002 edition of Treoir, identified as John Egan’s. This one is a polka, but a different tune from the Egan’s polka that’s already in the database.

Some information about the author can be found under this reel, in the comments section:
https://thesession.org/tunes/909

This tune is not the “John Egan’s” that appears in the Moneymusk album. That one is a hornpipe.

↳ Posted by glauber on August 22nd, 2002.

Scartaglen polka (S: Denis Murphy & Johnny O’Leary)

This version comes from Denis Murphy & Johnny O’Leary as they played it on track #16 of Music from Sliabh Luachra, RTÉ 183 CD, 1995.

I love how it sounds both lively and unhurried on this recording (unlike some of the more common versions today).

Denis and Johnny were recorded playing this polka in a set with the one that has become known as Denis Murphy’s. Here’s their intricate setting of it from the same CD:

https://thesession.org/tunes/357#setting49477

↳ Posted by Bregolas on April 3rd, 2024.

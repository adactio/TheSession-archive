One comment

The Christow Jig - comp. Gordon Francis

Christow is picturesque village to the west of Exeter on the eastern edge of Dartmoor.

It is a tranquil area and after visiting I wanted to write an English jig that had a wistful, melancholy feel. The Christow jig should be played steadily and gently but never hurried.

Re: Tricia Shannon

Added Ashe Road as an alternative name. The recording of it on Grapevine by Elvie Miller and Naomi Morse sounds like the same tune.

I searched Ashe Road on youtube for other recordings, and I didn’t find much, but what I did find was similar in tonality and melodic direction, but lacking ornamentation. David’s comment above makes me wonder if there are similar tunes getting mixed up in one way or another.

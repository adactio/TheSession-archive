Kelly Thompson’s #2

Tune composed by Frank Blaney of Belfast, for a girlfriend while attending U. of Illinois. Written about 1975. Tenor banjo was his main instrument: the ornaments are mostly staccato triplets. Tune printed in the “Irish Folk Club Tunebook, Vol.1”, (Champaign, Illinois, 1980).

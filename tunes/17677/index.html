↳ Posted by Beleragor on January 25th, 2019.

The Tin Wedding, X:2

I originally learned this setting from an old Josephine Keegan album. The version of this tune in O‘Neill’s is almost identical, except for the triplets in the 4th bar of the A part. It also appears in Luke O’Malley’s Collection of Irish Music. I’ve always heard the tune in the key of C major.

↳ Posted by Greg McLaughlin on November 1st, 2023.

Re: The Tin Wedding

Sorry. This is not in O’Neill’s Music of Ireland. It is an American tune that appears in Cole’s 1000 Fiddle Tunes. It was, however, associated with Irish born fiddler, Larry Redican of New York.

↳ Posted by Greg McLaughlin on November 2nd, 2023.

The Tin Wedding, X:3

A nice setting published by Luke O’Malley in his 1976 collection. His source was indeed Larry Redican.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on November 2nd, 2023.

Re: The Tin Wedding - duplication

Hi Greg
Ryan’s Mammoth Collection has two (essentially identical) entries for this tune as Tin Wedding (#822) and Peerless (#879). The Peerless is already on this site https://thesession.org/tunes/12587. Of course, Cole’s was derived from Ryan’s MC.
By the way, I see a lot of the Fisher’s Hornpipe https://thesession.org/tunes/872 in this tune. (And Miss Thompson’s https://thesession.org/tunes/2264 for that matter.)

Five comments

Halloween

Found on the Trad Tune Archive, adjusted to fit here. Needs a slower tempo.
Composed by James Sanderson, it’s published in William Campbell’s – “Book 16th of Strathspey Reels, Waltz’s & Irish Jigs for the Harp, Piano-Forte & Violin" (1801, p. 17).
Nothing like the other Halloween Strathspey here, but yet another strangely exuberant tune for Halloween.
I like this in several different minor keys for fiddle - suggest playing this in ANY minor key is much more fun for Autumn!

I did hear the bass rhythm lagging in the preview (thought about trying to adjust it, or remove the grace notes), but sounds fine when I play it in ABC Tools. Thought grace notes were not supposed to add any time(?)

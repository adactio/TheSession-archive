Tangerine Dream

This is a jig version of a Polka of the same name, as played on the album Naked and Bare.

It’s not quite a square tune, there is a rogue 9/8 bar, but also one line in the B part and another in the C part have 5 bars of 6/8.

The polka version is even weirder, but excellent: https://thesession.org/tunes/23368

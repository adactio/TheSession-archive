Curry Polka (c. Pádraig Kelleher)

Cork flute player Pádraig Kelleher wrote this tune after cooking and consuming a hearty portion of curry – according to Tony McCarthy, who I learned this quirky little polka from!

Tony recorded it on From Cork to Confort Berhet. I think he’s playing a B flute (and the bouzouki) on this track so I transposed it into Emin.

Three comments

Lutunn Noz

This is a Breton hanter dro; the time signature is actually 6/4. A very, very pretty tune, isn’t it? Bernard Benoit composed and recorded it, for guitar and mountain dulcimer, back in 1975. It has some characteristics that are more “artistic” than traditional so I’ll also add a somewhat simplified setting. On the recording, Benoit plays the parts in the order A B B C D B E B A B A B C D B. His tempo is about 1/4=200 (50 BPM).

↳ Posted by Vasha on February 10th, 2022.

Lutunn Noz, X:2

A version of “Lutunn Noz” with a shortened A part (if that is preferred for dancing) and less ornamentation in the B part. The parts could be played in the order A B C D B A B E D B (or, stick with C instead of E).

↳ Posted by Vasha on February 10th, 2022.

Three comments

Well Bill?

From an album by Goitse. This one can be danced quite easily as a waltz if you feel into it and don’t think too much, although if feels more like a Balkan circle dance despite being apparently Irish.

There are three phrases per bar but the phrasing is different in the A and B parts. I think I’ve put in beams that accurately reflect this, although the last couple of bars bleed into each other in the performance.

Darby Gallagher’s

John Doherty recorded this on “Bundle and Go” and called it Hudie Gallagher’s March.

↳ Posted by banjowalsh on July 16th, 2002.

Different tune

The tune posted here should not be confused with the totally different jig in D titled ‘Darby Gallagher’ appearing on page 110 of Josephine Keegan’s 2004 tune collection “A Drop in the Ocean”.

↳ Posted by rwwt on February 20th, 2015.

Darby Gallagher’s, X:2

From John’s playing. Randal Bays and Joel Bernstein called this after John. The original title was the Highlander’s Jig: http://tunearch.org/wiki/Annotation:Highlander%27s_Jig It was also entered here as the Widow’s Rant: https://thesession.org/tunes/13898

↳ Posted by Lucy Rietmann on May 16th, 2015.

Hudie Gallagher’s March, X:3

Caladh Nua recorded a nice version in D on their album “Next Stop”. They play it after “Dancing Eyes” and followed by “Three Deer And A Hare”.

↳ Posted by hone on October 11th, 2019.

The Santa Tracker

C: Peter Casey

a sweet little hornpipe song from Peter Casey who seem to have combined elements from the Belfast and the Derry hornpipes and formed something new, I think this is a good example of ‘organic’ composition, you have tunes diddlying in your head and suddenly a new tune is formed…

one can listen as well as buy the track here:

https://petercasey1.bandcamp.com/track/the-santa-tracker

here is more information on the tune from Peter in case the link stops working:

This song was written in December 2019 and was only sang a couple of times. as Christmas was nearly over before it had been completed. I had no intention of recording it but I got a request from a staff member in Ennistymon hospital to record a couple of songs for a Christmas party. I had decided that I would do a video recording this song but a few days before I was supposed to do it, AnneMarie’s aunt Aine passed away after having had Alzheimer’s for a about 6 years. At her funeral many stories were told about her love for young children, being a primary school teacher in Ballinkillen, Co. Carlow. She also had a great love of music.

After doing the video recording, we both said that it would be lovely to do a proper recording and release it as a fundraising single for the Alzheimer’s society in Aine’s memory. In a flash Kieran McDermott was practicing the air on guitar and we social distanced our way up to Fanore to John Queally’s stables where Rosaleen Marion arranged a recording. AnneMarie stood outside, Kieran and Rosaleen masked up and we got it done.

Gerry Howard from Fanore who has done years of fundraising for Alzheimer’s put me in contact with Fiona in ASI Ennis and she got me through to Jarlath in Dublin and we are delighted to be able to raise money for the Alzheimer’s Society of Ireland.

We really hope you enjoy the song. Please share it with children as we hope that it will add to the magic of Christmas while supporting a very good cause.

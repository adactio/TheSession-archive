Two comments

The Godmother

I got this tune from Goitse’s album Inspired By Chance. They’re playing it in E Major, so I’ve transcribed it that way, but I will also post it in D.

I don’t know the source of this tune. I only have that album through Spotify, so I can’t read the liner notes to see if they composed it, or listed their source.

It’s an interesting tune that meanders a bit, and has twists and turns that I don’t expect, which is something I like in a tune.

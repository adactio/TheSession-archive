The One-legged Chicken

Another ‘old-time’ waltz composed by Tommy Kinsella of Gorey and collected as part of the Wexford Tune Project. Thanks to Sinead Tobin, Tommy’s daughter for providing transcriptions of Tommy’s tunes.

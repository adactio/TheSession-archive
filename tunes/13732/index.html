Danse De Campagne

I transcribed this tune from the playing of Philippe Bruneau on the 1974 LP titled Danses Pour Veillées Canadiennes (Philo label, FI-2006). It is the third tune in a nine tune medley which fills the entire A side of the LP.

Bruneau plays this (and all the tunes in the medley) at 120 bpm. That is, if you tap your foot twice for each measure, you need to tap your foot 120 times in a minute.

Larry Lavin’s Choice

There is a mistake in Geoff’s transcription. Tim Collins plays through each part only once, without repeats.

This is also on Frankie Kennedy and Mairead Ni Mhaonaigh’s 2nd album together (and the 1st as “Altan”), track 6, entitled “Tommy Peoples’”. That setting is as follows:

X: 1
T: Tommy Peoples’
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Dmaj
A2FB ABdf|edfe fddB|A2FA ABdB|1 AFEG FDDB:|2 AFEG FDD2||
|:FAdA BAdA|(3Bcd ed cAAG|FAdA BAdB|1 AFEG FDDG:|2 AFEG FDD2||

It’s not a Tommy Peoples composition though - it just came from his playing. Proof of this is in the fact that it appears in O’Neill’s under the title “Larry Lavin’s Choice” (edited a bit by me for easy comparison):

X: 1
T: Larry Lavin’s Choice
S: O’Neill’s Dance Music of Ireland, 1551
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Dmaj
A2FA ABdf|edfd edBd|A2FA ABdB|1 AFEG FDDB:|2 AFEG FDD2||
|:FAdA BAdA|Beed cAA2|1 FAdA BAdF|GFEG FDD2:|2 FAAc BAdB|AFEG FDD2||

The Secret Of Roan Inish Theme

This is the theme from the Secret of Roan Inish film (definitely worth a watch).
Soundtrack composed by Mason Daring.
Rhythm isn’t my strongest suit so sorry for any errors there, but I think the melody sounds about right.

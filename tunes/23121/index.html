Celtic Dream

Adaptation of the Celtic Dream composed by Ronan Hardiman
The part A & B are repeated 3 times before moving on the C part which is played twice before coming back on the A & B part.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OhGhYYiypI0

↳ Posted by JohnMcTilly on May 7th, 2023.

Celtic Dream, X:2

You can play the tune with an F Whistle to be in the same tuning as the original Lord of the Dance but the C part will be tricky to play the G sharp

Nice tune off Afro Celt Sound System’s second album, *Release*. Features Ronan Browne and Michael McGoldrick on pipes and whistles.

The speed of the recording might be approximated by adding Q:C2=300 to the ABC notation. Whether the boys actually played this fast or they just sped up the recording electronically is up for debate :-)

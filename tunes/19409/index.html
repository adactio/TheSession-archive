Five comments

Glen Helen

I first learned the stormy night reel and searching for some nice version I found the tune in a set with this Glen Helen in Thunderhead album from Malcolm Dalglish and Grey Larsen. It is a lovely tune with a haunting feeling. It is great how f sharp and c sharp are placed there. I signed the tune as D dorian but it jumps between D dorian and D major. It seems a very recent tune but I don’t know who is the composer. Enjoy

Re: Glen Helen

I am very happy to find this tune transcribed here, it’s been haunting me for decades since I heard it on Malcolm Dalglish and Grey Larsen’s CD “Thunderhead.”

I’m sure Larsen, on Irish flute, plays F# on the third 8th note of the first measure (F E D F# E). I haven’t added a “corrected” score here as it seems to me this comment is enough -- the tune works either way. I haven’t been able to find a score on his website (greylarsen.com) so we’ll just have to trust my ears (or not!).

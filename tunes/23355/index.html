Re: You’re Welcome, Charlie Stewart

The earliest printing I have is 1757 (Bremner), where it’s called “Queensberry House”. In Gow’s Complete Repository it’s called “Charlie Stuart”. In Hogg’s Jacobite Relics the song is “You’re Welcome, Charlie Stuart” - Glen cites a date of 1748 for that one, and the air given as “Miss Stewart’s Reel”. The tune is found in the Scots Musical Museum for a song called “Lovely Polly Stewart”. Glen also mentions “an older set of the same air” under the title “The Confederacy” (c.1736) - I don’t have a copy of that.

When it comes to Burns, his song “You’re Welcome Willie Stewart” was simply based on the words of the older song, “You’re Welcome Charlie Stewart”.

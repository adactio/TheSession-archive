The Unfortunate Rake

Well it’s been a minute. I thought if I am ever going to post another tune it might as well be today of all days.
I found this in my copy of the estimable Capt. O’Neil’s 1850 melodies - #970, p.180, if you like - and started sharing it with local players. I love the well placed sharps in the melody. I tucked it into a set with The Killashandra Lasses. Perhaps they are the cause of the rake’s misfortune? We might never know.
I slapped some chords on top of the dots. It’s a habit I formed after having heard the question, “You got any chords for that?” too many times. Differences of harmonic opinion are always welcome.
I performed the usual due diligence checks for duplications, though if I missed something I have high confidence someone will let me know. Cheers! G5

Re: The Unfortunate Rake

Even though the two tunes, “The Unfortunate Rake” and the American cowboy tune “The Streets of Laredo” bear almost zero resemblance after almost three hundred years of separation, they do share the same DNA. Both tunes lyric’s detail the misfortunes and too-soon death of a young man who falls in with bad company and pays for the transgression with his life.

Tweedly Park

Nice little jig from James Hill which was published in Pete Loud’s Collection of Northumbrian, Tyneside & Other Traditional Fiddle Tunes.
I haven’t seen or heard this one before and it doesn’t seem to be available elsewhere in abc notation or with chords.
I’ve slightly chromaticized it to make it more concertina friendly.
Noel

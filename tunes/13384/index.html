Manioba’s Golden Boy

Another two-step composed by Manitoba fiddler Andy deJarlis (1914-1975), a Métis fiddler who composed some 200 melodies. Peter Yarensky says this tune has gained currency among New Hampshire fiddlers through its use for square dances and in jam sessions, and this New Hampshire variant has emerged.

↳ Posted by Alan Wilson on February 12th, 2014.

Two comments

The Bar Wench

One of my own compositions, written to a girl working behind the bar at our local session when it was held at the White Swan public house in St. Albans (some 15 years ago now).
Although written in 3/4, provisionally as a Waltz, I’m still unsure whether this best conveys the desired feel - the default playback speed here is certainly faster than intended.

Sabat’s Reel

This is the first Irish tune I wrote. A guitarist friend of mine actually suggested that the B section be put into major, as I originally kept it in D mixo. I like how the A and B sections differ in mood, a lot of my favorite tunes do that for sure. Enjoy!

I actually quite dig this tune… The A part, especially. Sounds a bit more as an Adorian feel to me.
For myself, I’d be inclined to in the tune on an A instead of a D, and I might agree with Smash about the B part, although to my ears it sounds a little bit like a contra tune. You could also join some of the notes like “a2aa- agf2|g2gg- gfe2|f2ff- fed2” to make it a wee bit more interesting.

Chase the Squirrel

This is the first Tune played in ‘The Squirrel Set’ played by ‘The Spailpíns’ in the 1960’s, taken from a reel to reel tape recording that I had. The other two tunes published earlier, are ‘Gathering Nuts’ & Squirrel’s Hornpipe’. I’m not to sure if these are the original names of these tunes, but it was what they named them when they introduced the set.

From “East of the Tracks” by Bridgetown (Johnny Connelly’s band, Portland Oregon) 1st tune in the set, followed by John Joe’s (or is it Joe John’s- I don’t have it in front of me), followed by a much faster reprise of Doggedness. One of those sets thatyou just have to call the day to screeching halt and learn the tunes on the spot…..

Three comments

Wilfred’s Fiddle

This jig was composed by Dan R. MacDonald, a Cape Breton fiddler. It is the third tune in a jig medley featured on the album Fiddle by the Cape Breton Symphony.

↳ Posted by Songcasserole on March 15th, 2015.

It is the first tune in the medley, and not the third.

↳ Posted by Songcasserole on March 15th, 2015.

Wilfred’s Fiddle

The tune was written for Wilfred Gillies, who was an early member of The Cape Breton Symphony. Other members have included Buddy McMaster (Natalie’s uncle) and the late Jerry Holland, among others.

↳ Posted by Bob Parsons on March 22nd, 2015.

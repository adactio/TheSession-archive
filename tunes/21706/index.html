Snow On The Tracks, X:3

It’s an interesting question as to where the bar lines fall in this tune, and I know some people who, because of this, struggle with the phrasing and can’t make sense of it. But you know, when you listen to the tune, you don’t hear the bar lines; you just hear the tune. So I don’t get too hung up about it and just enjoy playing the tune (over and over again - it’s hard to play it too many times).

In this third setting I’ve simplified the layout and added accompanying chords that I usually play.

Support From America

Relatively recent composition by Charlie Lennon, from his recording “A Terrible Beauty - A Suite In Honour Of The Heroes of 1916”. This is the first of 2 reels under the collective title of “Support From America”.
It was taught to a flute class at the Ballyvourney Flute “Cruinniu” in 2018 by that very fine musician Caoimhin
O’Fearghail.

Will You Come Home With Me?

From O’Neill’s 1001, #104. This is harmonically very close to Old Hag You Have Killed Me (https://thesession.org/tunes/1359), but melodically distinct enough to be listed separately. (It’s also different from the other tune of the same name (https://thesession.org/tunes/1220).

Re: Will You Come Home With Me?

Breandán Breathnach has a 4 part tune of this name in CRE2.8. He notes that the jig posted here as another setting. He also sees a connection, in its fourth part, with Ask My Father https://thesession.org/tunes/2288.

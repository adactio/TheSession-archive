Two comments

Dublin-Cork Train To Killarney

Actually a fling. Sean O’Dwyer composition, this is a very simplified transcription, since his playing is very swung and, of course, has plenty of double-stops and chords. Speaking of which, he seems to use (or suggest) Em, D, and G chords in the bass. Then again, I’ve never touched a concertina, so take that with a grain of salt.

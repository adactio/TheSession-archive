Donncha Lynch’s / Donncha Ó Loínsigh (Amaj)

We learned this jig from Méabh and Clíodhna Ní Bheaglaoich who call it after their uncle, the late box player Donncha Lynch (Donncha Ó Loínsigh). A native of Baile Bhúirne, Donncha was at one time a choice musician to play for the dancers and would often do so flanked by his sons Aogán and Donncha Jr. (the Donncha Lynch Trio). He would also accompany our late great friend, dance master Timmy “The Brit” McCarthy at some of his set dancing workshops.

You’ll be hard-pressed to find any video of Donncha Lynch playing so here’s the trio playing slides for the Baile Bhúirne Jig Set (also known as the Cúil Aodha Jig Set) some time in the early 1990s with Timmy and Rhona McCarthy dancing with fiery Ballyvourney set dancers. These are all slides, though – it would’ve been great to find a recording of Donncha himself playing this jig, too.

Giolcach shléibhe

http://www.irishwildflowers.ie/pages/22a.html
A live, musical bush!:
Walking through the besom in bloom on a very hot July day near Carnac, in Britanny, we heard all the already formed black pods crack open in the sun!

At the Eglantine community garden, in Belfast, we used the whole bush(!) as shakers during a hedge music workshop I organised a month later.

↳ Posted by birlibirdie on July 16th, 2012.

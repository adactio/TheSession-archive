Ten comments

“The Birds in the Tree” ~ another hornpipe 8-)

However, some of you may be familiar with this melody in another guise… The first transcription in A is from the playing of John Kimmel, and yes, he does swing it, and yes, he does do those 4 note runs as well. The second transcription is me taking that melody down to its more common key, in its other guise, but keeping the hornpipe going and having a bit of fun in the process. Considering the relatives, maybe this will particularly irritate the hornpipe hating member whose name, like Lord V of the Potter books, I will not name for fear of conjuring up their presence…

I’ll add simpler transcriptions, without those >s and (3s later…

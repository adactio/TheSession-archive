Re: Sweeney’s Dream / Brightest Eyes’

Setting X:3 was, as noted, taken from O‘Neill’s DMI (#745) as The Girl With The Laughing Eyes. An identical setting (barring one note, but including the slurs, and a switch from 2/4 to 4/4) is given in William Bradbury Ryan’s Mammoth Collection (#260) but called Brightest Eyes’. Now, Ryan’s collection was published around 1883, about 20 years before Captain O‘Neill’s first publication. It implies O’Neill’s setting came (by whatever route) from Ryan’s MC (unless it was from the same musician who had not changed his/her setting over 20 years…..) Sadly, according to Patrick Sky, in his preface to my 1995 reprint, O’Neill never acknowledged or mentioned Ryan in his publications. This is also true of later scholars (e.g. Breathnach)
As for John McGrath being the composer, he was born in 1900! (See this link https://irishtunecomposers.weebly.com/john-mcgrath.html)

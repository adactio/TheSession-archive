Three comments

Fiona’s Arrival

Composed c.2010 by Oisín McAuley and named after his daughter.

Originally recorded in C major on Danú’s Seanchas album.
The arrangement is exquisite, check it out.

Re: Fiona’s Arrival

Paradoxically enough, this tune sounds very “ionian” although it does not contain any 7th at all.
Try it in D on the flute,
G or A maj. on fiddle or, indeed, in C.

The first two beats seem directy inspired by the jig Tripping Down the Stairs

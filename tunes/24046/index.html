Gan Ainm, X:3

This is The Ball at the Hop. Jig. Number 29 in P.W. Joyce’s Old Irish Folk Music and Songs.
This seems to be the tune Derek Hickey, et.al. are playing, but in a higher key.
Appropriately, Joyce’s note says "Taken down about 1850 from John Hickey
of Ballyorgan Co. Limerick."

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on February 11th, 2024.

Re: Gan Ainm

Hickey et. al. seem to have improved the B part of the tune compared with
the Joyce setting, but it is clearly the same tune.

Red Island Tune

Newfoundland single/polka.

Fairly sure it’s a traditional tune. It bears resemblance to Don Freake’s:
https://thesession.org/tunes/17979

Red Island is an island of Placentia Bay, and the community which was once there was resettled.

For history, see:
http://www.redislandnf.com/redsitemap00.html
http://www.redislandnf.com/redindex00.html

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on June 10th, 2019.

Christmas Hornpipe

An American contra tune, first published in 1867.
Originally played in B flat, but played here in G.
I learned this version from the Melodeon playing of Anahata.

In this video of the tune, which I recorded on Christmas Eve 2022, I played it on English Concertina, Trump & Bodhran. ( My Lockdown Video #483 )

Cheers,

Dan Jeremiah O’Connor’s

A polka from the repertoire of Dan Jeremiah O‘Connor of Scartaglin, as played by Emma O’Leary.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OBTYvHZoIUs

This tune has at least two very close relatives in Cashman’s Favourite (aka the Low)
https://thesession.org/tunes/10906
and Mikey Duggan’s (aka Gullane)
https://thesession.org/tunes/2024

…and all three are lovely polkas! I hope I won’t start mixing them up after writing this…

P.S.: Of course, there’s no evidence Pádraig O’Keeffe “composed” this one, as someone boldly suggested in the “composer” field :-D Maybe they were confused by the phrase “O’Keeffe’s manuscript” (tunes which he wrote out to his students on any piece of paper he could find). Also, to avoid confusion, this polka is NOT on the Glountane Cross monument. The famously obscure tunes engraved on the monument are https://thesession.org/tunes/20646 and https://thesession.org/tunes/3115#setting31629 (see comments there).

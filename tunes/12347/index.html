Eight comments

Lori

Tune by Aidan O’Rourke dedicated to border fiddle player Lori Watson. It’s the opening track on KAN’s album Sleeper, played in Bb (no option for Bb tune here on The Session…) so play on Bb whistle:)

One of my (many) favourite tunes on the album…

In the B part, third bar, the second pair of “BA” is played with the B shorter and the A longer i.e with a “less than” symbol between them. For some reason any time I include a “less than” symbol (either in the abc or the text here), everything after it gets deleted….mysterious.

Anyway, enjoy.

Re: Juan Martino’s

That’s a great clip there, two absolute masters of their respective instruments playing together in a very atypical musical combination, but it certainly works. I don’t know how or why the composer’s title would appear to be Spanish, but I’m sure I heard somewhere that Mike Katz named it after Edinburgh fiddler John Martin. I am also told that he two of them can occasionally be heard together at the Sunday afternoon session in Edinburgh’s “Waverly” bar.
RIP Mike Whellans, one of Scotland’s best musicians ever.

About this tune…

Well, I’m sure this site is mostly for traditional tunes but I thought I may as well try my own so this is my own self-composed jig. Hopefully this time I got the ABC right! It was composed more for the fiddle although I’ve played it on my whistle. Feel free to use it and add some embellishment into it. As long as you don’t claim it as your own ;) p.s. If anybody DOES happen to like it and play it, could you record it and send it to me? I would love to heard people playing my tune :) if you do, send it to willywonka120@hotmail.com

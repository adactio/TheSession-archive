The King’s Bow

This reel was penned by Maurice Lennon, former Stockton’s Wings fiddler and still active composer of great tunes. I discovered this tune, while searching a version of an other reel : Maurice Lennon’s Tribute to Larry Reynold (https://thesession.org/tunes/12406). On this video we can hear Maurice play the King’s Bow before his Tribute to L.R. : https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=btZYEVM2JwM

The King’s Bow - or Bow?

I’ve recently discovered Maurice Lennon’s great tune ‘The King’s Bow’, which I love, but does anyone know whether the bow in the title is the type you play a fiddle with, or the forward-bending gesture of respect? Or, less likely but still conceivable, the front end of his watercraft, or the knot you tie with your shoelaces?

Re: The King’s Bow - or Bow?

“The first tune of this set was the first one ever written by Ciara in 1999, in the front room of her grandmother’s house. We found it worked well with two of Maurice’s more recent reels, The King’s Bow (Maurice dedicates this to his friend and fellow fiddler, Chicago native, Ian King) and The Last One Out, written whilst forming the trio and recording the album.”

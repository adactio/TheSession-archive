Four comments

Gan Ainm

Another old simple tune from a County Wexford source from The Feis Ceoil collection of Irish Airs (1914). The source for this tune was flute player John Ferguson.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 7th, 2022.

Gan Ainm, X:2

The untitled John Ferguson tune reworked as a single jig or slide.

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 7th, 2022.

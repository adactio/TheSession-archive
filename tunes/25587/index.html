Two comments

The Moyard Reel (c. Ciaran Kelly)

This lovely cheerful reel was penned by Ciaran Kelly, a Fermanagh box player, multi-instrumentalist, gentleman and an inexhaustible resource of tunes! Ciaran has christened this tune in honour of his dear departed friend Maurice Bradley’s home place, which is near Sixtowns, Draperstown, Co. Derry.

Maurice Bradley was a stalwart of traditional music in Northern Ireland and an all-round musician who played the fiddle, the pipes, the flute and the box. He very sadly passed away earlier this May and will be sorely missed by many.

You can hear Mr. Ciaran Kelly himself play The Moyard here:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oIWektGC_gA&t=116s

Maurice Bradley’s beautiful solo album that was released in January and features the backing of his close friends, Ciaran included, can be found on Bandcamp:

https://mauricebradleymusic.bandcamp.com/album/ran-na-heala

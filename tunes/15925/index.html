The Social Gallon

1 Social Gallon = 8 pints.

I was challenged (generally am), late one night, not so very long ago, during a music festival, to write a tune by this title. Finally getting round to it, and posting on here because the recalcitrant troglodyte who challenged me isnae on Social Meeja, and this is the only way to get it to him short of getting on a ferry. Also, he’s a bouzoukist, so I thought I’d write it in F to be awkward (though I suppose it would work ok in G, but I likes tunes in F ’cos I’m twisted). So there you have it.

Three comments

Old Toasty

Composed by Pipe Major Angus Lawrie of the City of Glasgow Police Pipe Band (later the Strathclyde Police band). His cousin Ronnie Lawrie was a fellow piper and member of the police band. “Old Toasty” was the nickname of Ronnie’s father, who apparently danced a lovely hornpipe.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on March 9th, 2017.

Re: Old Toasty

I should have said that I transcribed “Old Toasty” from the playing of Scottish folk group Black Donald, from their 1985 LP ‘Dancing Hazards’.

The Cas Jig

Another great tune from Hot Griselda. Written by Kaspar Laval. Recorded on F whistles with G “fingers” - the tune comes out in Bb, though I wouldn’t try this one on your fiddler friends. I love the off-beat section in the second part - for just a second it sounds like the Monahan Jig but then you’re off to other interesting places.

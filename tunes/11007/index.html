Kevin Ryan’s

This is a nameless tune from the playing of Michelle O’Brien on her album with Aogan Lynch and Gavin Ralston. I can’t find a name for it anywhere on the net and I’ve asked here on the Session without success, so I’ve named it after the source of a transcription I found elsewhere on the net (cuts edited out):

X: 1

M: 4/4
L: 1/8
S: Kevin Ryan, Ennis
R: Reel
N: Kevin says he heard on the radio played by flute & piano

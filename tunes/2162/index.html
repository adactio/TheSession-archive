Five comments

The Birnam Triangle

The tune was written by Hamish Moore, famous scottish piper and pipemaker. I stole it from a recording by Pipedown, “The First Measure”.

It’s a great tune, but also my first attempt on writing ABC code. Hope it’s OK, I will check in on it when the score is finished to see if it turned out right.

↳ Posted by eivind on November 16th, 2003.

The Birnam Triangle

Written by Hamish Moore as a 9/8 jig, not as a 6/8 previously posted. Published in his brilliant book of tunes “The Rumblin‘ Brig” and features on the ’Bees Knees’ Album.

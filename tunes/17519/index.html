Three comments

Savage Paddy

Another gem of a tune from the talented pen of fiddler / accordionist Damien Connolly , nephew to the great Seamus Connolly. The setting here is transcribed from a sound clip of Damien playing it on the fiddle (although the fiddle in question is NOT at concert pitch- it’s tuned down about one whole tone / whole step, but he IS playing the tune with A dorian fingerings). It comes once more from The Seamus Connolly Collection of Irish Music, an astounding collection of audio and printed material presented by the Boston College Libraries. Here is a link to the home page:

https://connollymusiccollection.bc.edu/

and a direct link to the tune page for Damien’s tune:

https://connollymusiccollection.bc.edu/document/631

Enjoy!

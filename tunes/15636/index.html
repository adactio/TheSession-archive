Big Ben

This tune was written for my uilleann piping friend, Ben, who is a big guy, both in size and in personality. I wrote it after Ben and I had a conversation about composing. I have had this posted in my profile since I wrote it, but Jeremy has asked that I get it into the tunes section since it is on my album, Giotár.

Welcome Home My Deary

An old Nothumbrian hornpipe as I heard Chris Norman played Melbourne in 2024.
According to https://tunearch.org it should appear in many sources:
William Vickers [Nothumbrian Collection]
Stewart’s A Collection of Reels, p. 9 [Johnson]
Johnson (Scottish Fiddle Music in the 18th Century), 1984; No. 77, p. 226.

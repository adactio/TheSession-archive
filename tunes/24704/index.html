The Castlebridge

This is the second of the compositions of the late Liam Gaul that was published in ‘The Wexford Tunebook’. Liam had a number of other traditional compositions which I’m seeking to get since his unfortunate passing last year. This tune was I believe composed as a simple reel to accompany an old figure dance - The Castlebridge Reel - that Liam had discovered had been collected in the area back in the early gaelic league days. Castlebridge is a large village just north of Wexford Town. Liam published an article in the local historical journal outlining the steps and figures for the dance and included this tune as a matching piece of music.

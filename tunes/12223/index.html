Three comments

Heartsease

This three-two was probably first published in Thomas Marsden’s Playford compilation of 1705, “Original Lancashire Hornpipes, Old and New”. These were mostly tunes with sets of variations - as this one amply demonstrates.

↳ Posted by fidicen on October 9th, 2012.

Heartsease

The modern publication of the original source is John Offord’s “John of the Green, the Cheshire Way”.

↳ Posted by fidicen on October 14th, 2012.

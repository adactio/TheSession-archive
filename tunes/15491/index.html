One comment

Kinnaird House

A belter by Ivan Drever from his Wolfstone days. To be swung some. Of the tune they write:
“Kinnaird House is the home of the Eaglesham brothers, Struan and Stuart. The tune was written primarily for their parents, Rena and Peter, whose hospitality knows no bounds.”

I remember this being played in music and several classmates being fairly gobsmacked by the guitar work. Being a young fan of the band, I tried to get them to join the “wolfpack”, but interest was superficial at best. To be fair, though, some of Wolfstone’s more dour or synthy material can be quite disenchanting.

So Long Liam

Phil Cunningham’s tribute to Liam O’Flynn, transcribed from his “No Rush” album with Aly Bain.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qi6HKJrZpB8&list=PLXtd9zo9rfLMNn7Hmo10cJOPlS9NiUI7G&index=11

This looks more complicated than it is: you can just play the G major parts with repeats, but the recording starts in D major without repeating the first part and then lifts both parts to the pipe-friendly key of G, finally repeating the last two bars as a coda.

Dusk Moon

From the album of the same name. Some articles discussing it note that the melody is “McClure’s” but I’m not aware of this melody being recorded anywhere else, so I’m going off what the recording says.

This isn’t a reel but I’ve entered it as a reel because that’s the closest fit. Clearly a pipe tune, but it’s a nice challenge for fiddlers.

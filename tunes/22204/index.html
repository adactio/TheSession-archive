The Badger

Written by Brìghde Chaimbeul for the Scottish smallpipes. Transcribed from LAS, the album she’s just released with Ross Ainslie and Steven Byrnes. It is played fairly slowly.

I’ve just added the basic version of the tune, there are some variations in there that I haven’t included.

https://rossainslie.bandcamp.com/track/the-badger-the-weasel

Re: The Badger

Jeff, you can keep the tune intact by putting tape over your two low G tone holes (which I think is what Brighde does). The first time you play it with that low F# is very gratifying. Experiment a bit, see how you go.

Killoran’s

This is another reel associated with the Sligo fiddler. It seems related to McFadden’s Handsome Daughter, but is different enough IMO. It was recorded with lots of variation by Sligo whistle virtuoso Carmel Gunning, and this is the very basic version of it. Has anybody ever heard it?

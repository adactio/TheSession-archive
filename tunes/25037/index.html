The Tourtise

I learned this tune from the wonderful Maura Shawn Scanlin when she taught at Alasdair Fraser’s Sierra Fiddle Camp. Apparently it is about a tortoise that she met while on tour with Conor Hearn promoting their new Rakish album. The tune can be found on the new Rakish album called Now O Now.

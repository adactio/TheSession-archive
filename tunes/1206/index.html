Three comments

This is from the New England Fiddler’s Repertoire. I haven’t been able to find it elsewhere, so I can’t say much about its origin except that the title has a nice Irish ring about it.

Here is the slightly ornamented version:

AFA fed|BGB g2B|AFA fed|BcB B2B|
AFA fed|BGB g2g|f/2g/2af gec|ded d3:|
|:a2a {b}afd|gab a2f|fga agf|gfg efg|
aba afd|gab a2g|f/2g/2af gec|ded d3:||

trevor

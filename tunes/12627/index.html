Six comments

“Charrada De Bercimuelle”

A lovely 5/8 Castillian dance tune from Salamanca, transcribed from the flute playing of Steph Geremia, recorded on her excellent album “The Open Road”, track 6, the second of two tunes… I like the album, her flute playing, and this ear worm of a tune. Now all I need do is find someone to teach me the dance, maybe a trip to Salamanca? ;-)

↳ Posted by ceolachan on March 25th, 2013.

Great album…

“Charrada De Bercimuelle” ~ the 3-step plan

Following the same original transcription I’ve added two more for C & D limited instruments, but both are easy enough on D limited winds. If your a wind player you can use the B minor transcription, D-fingering, with a Bb instrument and you’ll be playing the tune in G minor… But, the tune is hardly limited to or by that key, or any of these three. I love taking it in D minor myself. ;-)

↳ Posted by ceolachan on March 26th, 2013.

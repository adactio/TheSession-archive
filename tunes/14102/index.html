Three comments

The Gwanwitcha

This great tune, in C#m is by Colin Grant. Each part is played four times, with the third part being swung.
The A part has two identical lines played over alternating C#m and F#sus2 chords. Tempo is about 110 bpm (that’s half notes/minims).
Check out the Sprag Session version on YouTube at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JDwzdo-Mv78 .

↳ Posted by DonaldK on January 7th, 2015.

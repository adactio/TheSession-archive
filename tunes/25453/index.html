Two comments

Weeks Bay

This lovely tune was written by Colin Farrell for our local friend Walter Kirkland who passed away recently. Walter was a good friend to the music world and would host traveling musicians in his home. We play this tune at our session in Fairhope Alabama in C and jump up to D. I show it in C but wonder if it should be Gmix.

Six comments

Flook - Flatfish

This tune comes from Flook’s “Flatfish” album. The tune was written by Brian Finnegan, or perhaps Sarah Allen.

The scales in the second part makes it seem more like one of Sarah’s tunes to me, but all in all, it sounds more like one of Brian’s… I don’t have the liner notes, so I’ve not a clue.

On “Flatfish”, the set is played on an Ab whistle, putting this tune in Bbminor. Egad.

↳ Posted by Joseph Chordmichael on August 4th, 2009.

Re: The Sligo

Re. comment above but 1, Eamon McElholm was in a band with Brian called “Upstairs In A Tent”, and then in “Deanta” before joining “Solas”. Brian Finnegan recorded this tune on his first solo recording, “When The Party’s Over”.

The Plausible, X:4

I just discovered a cassette tape I made at Keaneally’s Irish Pub in Houston in 1986 when Paddy O‘Brien (from Offaly, living in St. Paul MN) and Sean O’Driscoll were playing there. Paddy and Sean used to come there for a three week gig, playing several nights each week.

The very first thing on the tape (which is probably what they opened with -- since I was probably there at the start) was this jig. This is the way they played it that night. On Sean’s record “Up the Airy Mountain” (which I also have on cassette), he says that he originally composed it as a two part jig, but that Paddy added the first part, so it is now a three part jig.

I really think that since Sean named the tune The Sorrowful Shilling, the official name should be changed to that and the Plausible (which I have never heard before) should be an a.k.a.

Re: Entertainer

First off… why are the ties sounding both notes separate, instead of one longer note in the setting? Plus, need a tie for the 1st two A’s in the 2nd bar. Hearing all the extra notes sound really messes it up.

I LOVE this tune from 1902 (love ragtime), so thought I’d mention I’ve seen easy play-along sheet music on YouTube in 2/4 and in 4/4, but the original was scored in 2/4. To me, there’s a difference in feel between 2/4 and 4/4, just like between a polka and a reel - and even though ragtime is syncopated, I think it still matters.
An Easy Violin Version in 2/4 can be viewed here (https://www.virtualsheetmusic.com/score/EnterVlEasy.html).
PDF of the original score here (https://soundreasonstudio.com/MuseScore-Library-msv3-041119/Repertoire/The%20Entertainer%20-%20Scott%20Joplin%20-%201902.pdf).

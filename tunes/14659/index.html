Two comments

Lament For Prophet

Transcription of a slow air (not a waltz) from Ashley MacIsaac’s album, Close to the Floor. Very moving tune that grabbed me the first time I heard it.

From Cranford’s, “The above air was composed (by Tom Anderson in 1981 and published in 1983 in Ringing Strings) to honour Prophet James Laurence Smith (1909-79) a union organizer for the National Union of Seamen in Lerwick. A man who worked for many causes, he was the first Labour candidate for the Orkney and Shetlands constituency and an advocate for the Scottish Agricultural Organization.”

↳ Posted by JoJofidhlear on August 16th, 2015.

Lament for Prophet

As JoJofidhle says it was written as a slow air, and was in 4/4 time which gives the player the opportunity to bring true feeling to this lovely tune. I never knew Prophet personally, but coincidentally I was given my middle name from Prophet’s sister who worked in the maternity unit where I was born, and who, I understand, did a lot to save both my and my mother’s lives at the time.

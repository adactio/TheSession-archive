↳ Posted by fiddlercjp on January 4th, 2013.

The Humours Of Tralibane, X:3

This version – just slightly different – appears in O’Neill’s Dance Music of Ireland - 1001 Gems (1907), p. 27, no. 66, as The Humors of Tralibane, Sugra Traig-Lia-Bain. According to the Fiddler’s Companion, this jig has no other title. To me, this jig sounds quite “peaceful”.

↳ Posted by Moxhe on October 31st, 2015.

Re: The Humours Of Tralibane

On An Ocean’s Breadth, Mick McAuley has this spelled as the Humours of Trailbane. I added that name here so it links to the recording. Tralibane, or Trawlebane, is in west Co. Cork, birthplace of Francis O’Neill, of the early 20th century tune books. As far as I know, there is no “Trailbane.”

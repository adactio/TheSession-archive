New Tommy Peoples Tune

I learnt this great tune from Tommy Peoples at a workshop he gave recently. It must be pretty new as it’s not on any of his recordings. Kinny Cally is the name of the place where he grew up.

It’s not an easy tune, lots of strange twists and turns, very distinctively a Tommy Peoples tune that goes through a few different modal changes.

Anyway hope you enjoy it!

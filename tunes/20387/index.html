The Breeches Buttoned On

This setting is taken from Ceolachan’s contribution X:3 to https://thesession.org/tunes/1426 Dálaigh’s. There are four other settings of this tune which is also sometimes called Downey’s. X:4, 11, 13, 19 are the further settings, which are quite different to the rest , which can reasonably called Dálaigh’s.

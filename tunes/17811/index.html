Hennessy & Kerr

Second tune in a set from Castor Bay track 5. Tune composed by Marie Fielding

↳ Posted by Donough on March 17th, 2019.

Hennessy And Kerr

From the playing of, and composed by, Marie Fielding.

Presumably named for two musicians, Donogh Hennessy and Laura Kerr, who played on her album “An Seisiún”.

Some alternatives for the last bar of the A Part (first repeat), and the B Part (last repeat):
- edB BAG
- edB A2e
- edB ~A3
- edB A2G

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on April 21st, 2020.

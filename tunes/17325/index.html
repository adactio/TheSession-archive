Salt Spring, X:3

This is the same code that Malcom Littlefield posted. I just added chords.
The 1st, 3rd & 5th measure of the B part suggests a bass run that I love.
It’s not played on most of the versions that I listen to before transcribing this tune.
The F# bass on the D chord is essential. The E chord with the G# bass is best played with subtle notes of the E chord. I usually play only an E note above the low G#. To me it sound great to just play the bass run and omit the chords altogether. Hope this helps you achieve the sound you love.

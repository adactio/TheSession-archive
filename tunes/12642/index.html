The Four Seasons

I learnt this tune back in the 1980s from a fiddler, the late Barry Morgan. I gather that his version of it was based on a tune in the Joshua Jackson MS.

I’ve transcribed it as D-Major albeit that the second sharp is superfluous. It’s actually D-Hexatonic - (no seventh).

Given the title of it, it seems a pity that it only has three parts, not four!

The Four Seasons, X:3

This is the version of the jig, called simply ‘The Seasons’ published in the Feis Ceoil collection of Irish Airs (1914). The tune as played was collected from the flute player John Ferguson of Rathangan in south Wexford. Most of the tunes collected from him would have been played for mumming. Many are tunes that today are still played in English folk music circles or Morris repertoire.

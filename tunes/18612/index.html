Two comments

The Cairn Stones

Yes, I wrote This Tune.

I came up with this tune one day, and decided that it was good enough to post to Facebook. So I did. And then I thought, “Well, it’s got a bit of attention on Facebook, may as well post it on The Session”. So I did.

The name isn’t too extravagant, I named it after an ancient tomb (that sort of collapsed in on itself) on a mountain near where I live called Mullyash.

The tune’s keys are a bit off as well. The first and last parts are solid Emix, but the second part goes from Emaj to Fmaj, back to Emaj and then Emin. I originally wrote it in D, and probably add it in D as well, however it simply sounds better in E so I transposed it. In D, the second part goes from Dmaj to D#maj, back to Dmaj and finally to Dmin, a bit awkward if you ask me.

So there’s my tune. Enjoy!

~Níall.

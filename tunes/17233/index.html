Parrez Lok Malo

Transcribed by JV from the Barzaz album. This is an air played at the end of the song. I imagine it was composed by JM Veillon. I think it´s on Fminor (four flats), which is not available here, but since the only d is natural Fdorian seems to work too.

↳ Posted by Javi V on September 5th, 2018.

Parrez Lok Malo, X:2

I cannot vouch for the original rhythm,
but here’s the tune in a more legible/decypherable setting, transcribed in the more congenial key of B (natural) minor.
Although fully heptatonic, the tune contains many pentatonic runs, which gives it its specific (peculiar, almost picturesque) colour.
The F phrygian feel stems less from the melody than the harmonic environment, it seems.

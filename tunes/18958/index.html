The Road To Ballydehob

First tune on the second track of Lily Gems and Eileen O’Driscoll’s “Early Reflections.” Similarities to a number of trad tune families (A part has a lot in common with Whiskey Before Breakfast for example), but it doesn’t seem to be a duplicate from my searches in a few keys.

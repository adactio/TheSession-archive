Three comments

The Tom Rosser

The jig dates back to around the 1850s where it appears in The Cambrian Newspaper, which periodically printed dance tunes, song words etc. Its anrecedents date back to John Thomas’ eighteenth century fiddler’s tune book, Walsh’s & others Dancing Masters etc. The Tom Rosser was a beautiful Pilot Cutter working the Severn Sea out of Swansea.

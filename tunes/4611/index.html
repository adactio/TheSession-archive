Edmonton Airbus

Written by Dougie McLean. I think this is on his “Craigie Dhu” album, and was a live track recorded at a Folk club in Canada - possibly Calgary - with my brother Martin playing guitar. A pretty good live recording. Works as a strathspey, as well.

↳ Posted by Kenny on June 20th, 2005.

The Doneraile

This jig was composed by Nóra Byrne Kavanagh. Nóra was born in Ferns in County Wexford but lives and lectures in Waterford. Viscount Doneraile was a landlord in Waterford, and the Doneraile walk in Tramore commemorates the family connection to the area.

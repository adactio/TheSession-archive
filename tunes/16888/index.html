The Knockatallon Rambler

I learned this tune from the accordion player Gerard Murray about 30 years ago. I know that he came up with the name for the tune at the time and it isn’t the original one. He played with a fiddle player form Knockatallon in Co. Monaghan at the time called Hugh McCague and I’m pretty sure that’s where the re-name came from.
I have passed this on many times to students over the years and never get tired playing it myself!!

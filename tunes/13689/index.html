Reel from Pascal Gemme

This exquisite and frantic tune is the second tune in a set of reels on Genticorum’s lastest recording “Enregistre Live!” as well as their first recording “Le Galarneau”. It would be hard to imagine getting very far on the third part without a recording to listen to. The first tune in the set, “Le Duleton” is not quite as explosive but is still moderately challenging and really fun. Good luck trying to find someone to play these with . . .

Note: the first part should be played a total of 3x through.

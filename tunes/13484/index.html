Thank you, G.

Probably my favourite tune of all time(At least this year).

I play the Blazing Fiddles version as transcribed in their Tune book Volume 2. It’s more or less the same with one or two different but subtle emphases on one or two notes.

Here’s them playing it here but I prefer their recorded version on the “Magnificant Seven” album. It’s slightly slower and sounds a little less “hashy”. They follow it with "the Eagle’s Whistle which is another grand tune.

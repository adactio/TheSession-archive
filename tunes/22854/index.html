Re: Y Fasged Wyau

Hi Moche,

Hope the transcription is useful, but please nobody take it as gospel/definitive, it’s just my rough attempt to put down what I’ve heard.
Y Fasged Wyau translates as The Egg Basket in English.
I know it as a welsh tune, but obviously it could be derived from an English/Irish tune or vice versa.

I heard originally heard it in a session in Newport, but it’s also been recorded a few times

Calan play it on their track Big D (1st tune), link here https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_KyWGOKD_I

Elfen also play it here (3rd tune) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VJ0PxYsFIqU

Crasdant play it here (2nd tune) https://youtu.be/FzaU8gcn98Y

Also here https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=3932524646874946

What I transcribed is a mix of those recordings and what I heard at the session and those recordings.
I have also heard of this tune sometimes being played in G

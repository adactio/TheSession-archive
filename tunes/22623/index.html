Four comments

La Valse Des Cerfs-volants, X:2

The composer, Mario Loiselle (1953-2017), was a highly regarded piano accompanist in Québec trad circles. This setting is based on his own transcription. All his compositions are available in a PDF at https://marioloiselle.wordpress.com/partitions/

To hear a piano recording, see: https://marioloiselle.wordpress.com/pieces-musicales/ (first tune on the page)

Or a band recording: https://siamsaceiliband.bandcamp.com/track/la-valse-des-cerfs-volants

Re: La Valse Des Cerfs-volants

There is another recording of this tune by fiddler Pascal Gemme and Mario Loiselle on the album “Musique du Monde / Music of the World” released about 10 years ago. According to the CD’s liner notes, this tune was Mario’s first composition and was recorded by him for the first time on this album.

Re: La Valse Des Cerf-volants

I recently learned that though the title of tune translates as Waltz of the Kites, if you translate it literally you get Waltz of the Flying Deer. I love that!

↳ Posted by jdicarlo on August 15th, 2026.

Re: La Valse Des Cerf-volants

Interesting word play and quirk of language! I think it relates to whether you run the translation for “des cerf-volants” (as in the title) versus “du cerf-volant”. Here’s an explanation via Google: “The name ”cerf-volant" (kite) comes from a phonetic evolution of the old expression serp-volant (flying snake), originating from the Occitan language where sèrp means snake. Over time, through a slip of the tongue and folk etymology, the word serp was confused with cerf (deer).
Anyway, it’s a lovely tune!

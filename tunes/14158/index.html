Re: Tadin-Tinaketa

This tune has been originally recorded by Skolvan*, a breton band, and takes its name from the onomatopoeias (called bols in indian music) sung by percussion player Dominique Mollard who plays tablas. It is a composition of Youenn Le Bihan and Gilles Le Bigot.
The rythm of this tune corresponds to the Hanter Dro (half tour) dance, and can’t be written in the way it’s presented here. The dance is based on 3 beats and thus the music written in 3/4. The tempo is not the one of a waltz, neither of a ridée, another breton dance whose pattern evolves on 6 beats.
* CD “Cheñchet n’eus an amzer”

Mi Na Meala

This one must have slipped under the radar. I’ve been really taken with this excellent reel from Aoife Granville’s CD “Sraid Eoin Shuffle”, posted here with next to no comment some 17 years ago.
Coincidentally, Aoife was up here in Aberdeen for an evening event last week. Slipped my mind to ask her about the origins of the tune, and I don’t have the recording any more. Maybe someone else can help.

↳ Posted by Kenny on October 28th, 2024.

Re: Mí Na Meala

Aoife mentions in her CD’s sleeve notes that this reel comes from Séamus Goodman’s (Canon James Goodman’s) collection.
https://www.discogs.com/release/13493705-Aoife-Granville-Sr%C3%A1id-Eoin-Shuffle

This tunes is no. 110 in Tunes of the Munster Pipers 1 (as published online by ITMA):
https://www.itma.ie/scores/goodman-vol-1-110/

Also known as “The Honey Moon” – not to be confused with The Honeymoon reel (Denis Murphy, Pádraig O’Keeffe et. al.) and “The New Honey Moon” reel (Tunes of the Munster Pipers 2, no. 486).

Re: Mí Na Meala / The Honey Moon

P.S.: I forgot to mention that a version of the “usual” Honeymoon reel can also be found in Tunes of the Munster Pipers 1 under the title of Lady O’Brien’s reel (no. 96) and has been recorded by this fine trio:

https://thesession.org/recordings/7266

(No. 96 is very close to how The Honeymoon is usually played – unlike Mí na Meala (no. 110) which is a different tune. Confusion prevented!)

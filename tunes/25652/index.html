Trip To Scourie

A reel composed by, I think, Orkney fiddler Fiona Driver. Scourie is a village in the North West of Scotland. I have transcribed it from Manus McGuire’s 2018 album The Copperplate Sessions where it is preceded by “The Buchan Barn Dance” https://thesession.org/tunes/25651

Road To Malvern

I heard and played this at old time jams. I was listening to Goitse play it and thought they did a bang up job.
Here is more info on the tune:
A modern composition by West Virginia fiddler Jim Childress, named after the Arkansas town where his wife was raised.
https://tunearch.org/wiki/Annotation:Road_to_Malvern
It is on the Goitse Transformed album listed as Dowd’s No 10.

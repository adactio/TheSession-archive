Baitheadh Bhrioclais, X:5

As played by Caitlín (2012).

“The Drowning at Bruckless is a tune named after the Bruckless Bay drowning in Co.Donegal in 1813. Crews of local fishermen were drowned in a storm. They had been fishing herring on this calm, moonlight night. They were out looking at their nets, the young boys singing and enjoying themselves and the old men smoking. All of a sudden, a great storm arose causing the boats to capsize. Most of the men were drowned in this storm, the greatest drowning that ever happened around the coast of Donegal.”
Source: Caitlín Nic Gabhan

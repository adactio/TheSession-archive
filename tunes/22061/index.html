Rorey

This tune “Rorey Waltz” is transcribed in the Leonard-Kernan MS (c1840s) of Abbeyshrule, Co Longford. It also appears in the Burchardt published music collection (c1900) as “Spanish Dance”. There is a second version in the local manuscripts with only two parts, the first and second parts of the original tune reversed.

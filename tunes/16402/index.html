Two comments

Michael O’ Connor

By Carolan, appears in Donal O‘Sullivan’s book as no. 120 alongside jig in same key for Michael O’Connor
(posted). One of my very favourites (as a pianist putting in chords), it must be lovely played by a harpist.
As it’s called ‘First Air’ it’s not strictly a reel but couldn’t resist adding it.

Re: Michael O’ Connor

NB Michael O‘Connor - Second Air - appears in Donal O’Sullivan’s book as No. 121, he says the subject is presumably the same as that of No. 120. But it has been recorded elsewhere as Michael Ward or Michael O’Connor and “Michael Ward” is how Second Air has been posted on The Session.

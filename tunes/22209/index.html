Here’s another unnamed hornpipe from the Power manuscript from Kilmore Co. Wexford. I haven’t found an exact match for it so far, although it has some similarities to an English air called ‘Pray Goody’
https://tunearch.org/wiki/Annotation:Pray_Goody
I have a vague memory of some of the older musicians in the Wexford area playing a variant of the tune as I recall in reel time.

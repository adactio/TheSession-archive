A tune of Liz Kane’s. Lots of rolls in the second part. Herself and Yvonne generally tie the last note of the second part to the first A as a double stop. A cracking tune.

Re: The Welcome Call

Susan—the tune can be played however you like. Shouldn’t be played too fast, ideally, the way Liz & her sister Yvonne play it. Their Connemara ‘style’ isn’t overly hurried. It’s more important that the melody’s clarity & phrasing is honored, with long, fluid, multi-note bow strokes & the delicate grace notes/rolls, rather than sped up. This is in contrast, of course, to other players brought up in the northern & southern counties, or places like Waterford, Mayo or Sligo, where music can be somewhat faster and the bowing is more staccato. You can slow it down, either here in the little audio sample, or with the Amazing Slow Downer app…

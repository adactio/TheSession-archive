Hughie McGinley’s

This neat little highland was taken off Donegal fiddler Vincent Campbell’s “The Purple Heather” album. That d-sharp found throughout the tune is pretty cool, as is the second part, where the “e<aa>g e<aa>f” is done with a drone on the open A-string.

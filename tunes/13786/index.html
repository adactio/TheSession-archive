Whistling Low

A jig written by Davy Spillane and Donal Lunny and appearing on the compilation CD, Common Ground. Interestingly, Davy also plays the tune as background to the air Inagh on The Sea of Dreams. Note the slides not appearing on the music are included as Js in the notation.

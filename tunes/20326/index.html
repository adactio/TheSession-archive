Two comments

Sir Ronald McDonald’s

From the playing of Scottish fiddle band RANT on their album “The Portage”. Track #2.1.
They add some variations so I’ve got bits and pieces from each time to make the barebones of the tune.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1p3wcI24wao

A different version is available on the 3rd Edition of “Gow’s Repository of the Dance Music of Scotland” (1817) and it’s listed as “Very Old”. I can only assume it is indeed quite an old tune :)

↳ Posted by DaveF on January 16th, 2021.

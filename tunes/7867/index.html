Two comments

A beautiful Reel written by fiddler Michael Moellers from Hannover, Germany, who recorded it with his band Dereelium.

Not your everyday choice of key: throughout the first part, the melody moves along in pure G harmonic minor, while the second part starts out in F mixolydian and then goes back to G harmonic minor for the turnaround.

Three comments

The Norwegian Gent

A great tune by Ian Stephenson
Ian was in dire need of some Northumbrian Pipes to learn on when a generous man with Norwegian roots lent him a beautiful set and got them fixed too. Thanks Stephen!
Kind permission given to post on The Session website, many thanks Ian.
This tune reminds me of a very good friend of mine from Oxford who upped sticks and moved to Norway a few months back to follow his dreams! This one’s for you Jed!
This tune is on Bandcamp in Eb:
https://andymaytrio.bandcamp.com/track/norwegian-gent
Go buy the album it’s a cracker:
https://andymaytrio.bandcamp.com/album/about-time

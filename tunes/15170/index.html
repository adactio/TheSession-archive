Gustavo

This tune was written by the fiddler Andrew Finn Magill.
http://www.andrewfinnmagill.com/
It’s a tribute for the members of the Brazilian Celtic Music Band: ORAN!
Here you can listen the full set ‘’Tunes for Gus’’ (DANNY/MILA/GUSTAVO):
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ol2K_f13WI

GUSTAVO Lobão plays the bodhrán. And I’ve never seen someone that does that with so much pleasure and passion!

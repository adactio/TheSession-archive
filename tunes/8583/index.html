Port an Puca

This is my current favourite tune, learnt off John Blake, Lamond Gillespie, and Mick Leahy’s second trio album. They inform that the jig appears on the Joyce Collection and that 19th century Galway piper Paddy Conneely is cited as the source there. A bit of research shows that it’s also on the O’Neill (no.319). A little different and less playable too.

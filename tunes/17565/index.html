Répertoire Gérard Lajoie

Second tune from (Raz-de-Maree aka Tidal Wave):
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n-3g7LzDcM4

Quebec reel, from the repertoire of accordionist Gérard Lajoie. Not sure whether he composed it or not, if he did, it might have an alternative name here: http://www.mustrad.udenap.org/bios/lajoie_gerard.html … too many to list, looks like he was a busy guy!

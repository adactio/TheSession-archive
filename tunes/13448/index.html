Harvest Fair Polka 1

This Charlie Lennon composed polka is the first of three on a set on track 4 of Flight from the Hungry Land. The third polka has also been transcribed on the session as Harvest Fair Polka 3. Another hat tip to Michael Black for finding these! Enjoy.

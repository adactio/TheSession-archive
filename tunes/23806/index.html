The Lark In Charlie’s Meadow, X:4

Paddy O’Brien (Offaly) plays this in D dorian. In his Tune Collection
(volume three, jig #64) he writes
"Liz Kane composed this tune in honor of her late friend Charlie O’Malley,
who was from Renvyle."

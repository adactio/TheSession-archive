Paudy Scully’s

Found this entry on Ceolas: TURNIP JIG, THE. AKA and see “Dan Patsy’s Slide,” “John Dore’s Favourite,” “Paddy Scully’s.” Irish, “Fast” Slide (12/8 time). G Major. Standard. AABB. Breathnach collected it from Cork fiddler Jack Connell and printed it in CRE II (“Gan ainm” No. 87, pg. 46). Source for notated version: Session musicians in Newmarket, County Cork [Sullivan]. Sullivan (Session Tunes), Vol. 3; No. 21, pg. 8.

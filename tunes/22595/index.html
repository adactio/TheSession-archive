Re: Thunder Bay

That’s not the standard way to notate a jig, TrevorS. Your colleague has transcribed the tune in a bouncy 4/4 (like 12/8) but, traditionally, jigs are in 6/8. Also, occasional line breaks help with the reading!

Like:
A2B|c2c cBA|E2C A,2A,|A,CE D2A|F3 FBc|
d2d dcB|EGB g2f|fec ecA|E3 A2B|
…. etc.

With the 16-bar sections it has a two-step feel to it.

According to this review (fourth down on the page) this tune is by Joe Harrison, who was a friend of Andy De Jarlis. According to the article, Joe is also a co-author of “The Sleeping Giant Two-Step”, which is a popular tune among old-time fiddle groups in western Canada.

Howard M. Lockhart

A nice Jimmy Shand polka written in honour of Howard Lockhart, a long standing broadcaster on the Scottish Home Service, BBC Home service, The Light Programme and, latterly, Radio Scotland when he presented the “request programme”.

He had quite a distinguished career and I suppose he could be described as a “thinking (wo)man’s Robbie Shepherd” :-)

However, on a lighter note, he was also the voice for “Larry The Lamb” ..not to be confused with Larry Lamb of Eastenders and Gavin and Stacey fame. ;-)

Read more about Howard here.

https://wiki.scotlandonair.com/wiki/Howard_M._Lockhart

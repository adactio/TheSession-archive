Padraig O’Keefe’s

Transcribed from the CD ‘Stone Walls and Street Lights’ by New Road.
The tune that was linked to this from the track listing (https://thesession.org/recordings/5679) is the The Gullane slide (https://thesession.org/tunes/1844). That is not the tune played on the album. I’ve updated the link.
New Road credit the tune to Kevin Burke who learned it from Padraig O’Keefe.

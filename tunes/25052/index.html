Mary MacArthur’s Wedding

A quick transcription from the playing of The Jura Ceilidh Band.

The B Part could be written more concisely as the following, though I couldn’t get the ABCs to display the sheet music correctly in this case:

|: c<e E<e | d<c B<A | A<B A>B | B<c B<A |
c<e E<e | d<c B<A | A<B E<E | E<F E<D |
[1 c<e E<e | d<c B<A | A<B A>B | B<c B>A |
A<B c<e | B<c B<A | F<F D<E | E4 :|
[2 E<E E<E | E<F E<D | D<E F<A | A<B A>B |
c>E c<e | d<c B<A | F<F D<E | E4 ||

Also note that after The B Part, JCB jump right into the next tune, with the last bar being “E2 e<f”, however, as a standalone tune, I believe it better to write as I have above, i.e., as “E4”.

There could be a few errors here and there, as I don’t typically play or listen to tunes of this time of Scottish character.

The tuner’s composer is currently unclear to me, see the discussion presently unfolding at the album page for more details:
https://thesession.org/recordings/6958

Mary MacArthur’s Wedding, X:2

From the first time through of Calum MacArthur’s version, on the album “Wooed And Married And AA”.

Transposed up 1 semitone with respect to the recording.

He played more (small) variations within the tune in comparison to JCB.

Off hand:
- bars 2 & 10 of The A Part could go as “=c<B d<F” instead of “=c<B A<F”
- bars 3 & 11 of The A Part could go as “A2 B<d” instead of “F<A B<d”
- bars 4 & 12 of The A Part could go as “e2 d<e” instead of “d>e d>e”
- bar 15 of both The A Part and The B Part could go as “B2 A<A” instead of “(3B=cB A2”, as well as bar 31 of The B Part
- bars 1 & 17 of The B Part could go as “a2 g<a” or “g<a a2”, instead of “g<a g<a”
- bars 3, 11, & 19 of The B Part could go as “e2 d>e” instead of “ d<e d>e”
- bars 4, 12, & 20 of The B Part could go as “(3fgf e<d” instead of “g<f e<d”

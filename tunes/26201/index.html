The Gillaroo

This is a lovely little reel composed by Maurice Lennon which he says he wrote in recollection of times spent fishing with his father on Lough Melvin for the not very pervasive Gillaroo trout.
The gillaroo (Salmo stomachicus; historically included in Salmo trutta) is a species of trout which eats primarily snails and is only proven to inhabit Lough Melvin in Ireland… Wiki!

↳ Posted by Ray Caldwell on December 20th, 2025.

The Gillaroo, X:2

This is a setting in G major for us pipers who don’t have the luxury of tunes south of bottom D. Jumping the octave can be jarring, especially when playing solo.

Two comments

The Sailor On The Rock

This reel titled as The Sailor on the Rock, appears in the Kilmore manuscript from County Wexford. It’s in the manuscript with the title written by possibly the second person to contribute sheets to the folder, so I reckon based on another tune featuring the same handwriting which is dated 1872, this tune was notate around then. It’s a completely different tune to The Sailor on the Rock as played today and I haven’t managed to locate a cognate tune through Tunepal or ABC searches etc. There are quite a few well known reels in the collection that feature older alternate titles to relatively well-known tunes (an example The Rose of Castletown is the title given for The Boys of The Lough)….perhaps someone has a different title for this tune??

↳ Posted by John E Roche on August 1st, 2022.

The Sailor On The Rock, X:2

This is the version of the above old tune that evolved from it being played again. Some of the longer notes have been tripleted as in the Wexford style (known to the real old guys as “doubling”??). In order to distinguish it from the better known ‘Sailor on the Rock’, it’s after getting the moniker, ‘Another sailor on the rock’. Well in fairness the rocks around Wexford are known as the graveyard of a thousand ships….

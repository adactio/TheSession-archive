Coming From The Wedding

Tune #854 in O’Neill’s 1001. I haven’t heard this one recorded, but it’s lovely! Its structure, particularly the ends of the parts, feels more like a reel. Transposed to G, there are some similarities to the common reel Miss Thornton’s.

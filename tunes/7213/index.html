James McGettrick’s

This is the McGettrick’s reel that is on O’Brien, Gans, and Sproule’s Snug in a Blanket record.
I first learned it from the Paddy O’Brien Tune Collection (vol I or vol II, I’m not sure which)
where Paddy called it James McGettrick’s, and it was one of my favorites.

Now playing through Snug in a Blanket, I find it there, and the McGettrick’s that got linked to
the track was not the correct one. I did a cursory search for this one but could not find it already here
so I added it as a new tune under this James McGetttrick’s title.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on December 10th, 2023.

The Three Wheeled Wagon

This jig is composed by a 15 years old freaking talented banjoplayer Morien McBurnie
from the Twin Cities in Minnesota, and he’s a mix of Welsh a bit of Irish and mainly Scottish blood.
The jig is a little tricky in a way, maybe not the most easy one to ‘fight’ with in the beginning, but I think it’s quite nice. Give it a try…. ;-)

Twenty-four comments

“Sleigh Ride” ~ for Bodhran Bliss, sleigh bells and coconuts in hand

This is a take on the Leroy Anderson number. I can’t remember exactly what we did with this at Christmas time, but we’d arranged it and played it during Christmas ceilis and a few times had sleigh bells, or Morris bells jangling and a wood block going, and some of us gave a fair imitation of a horse’s neigh. ( BB would probably want to say horse’s arse 8-) )

So, something for the season. It is a kick to play and to dance to, and in no way is it meant to be taken too seriously. As I said, I haven’t our old arrangement on hand to transcribe from, so this is trying to keep it in a reasonable range so most intruments can handle it, and who can’t manage an F natural on winds? ~ or eventually, with a little practice… ;-)

Tadie’s Wattle

Here is a nice little reel as it appears in Aird’s Selection of Scotch, English, Irish and Foreign Airs, vol. 2, no. 14 (my favourite source for the moment, as you have noticed).
Enjoy !

Tadie’s Wattle, X:2

O’Neill published this tune – as Tady’s Wattle – with only slight differences in “1850 Melodies” (1903/1979, no. 1416) and “1001 Gems” (1907, no. 653).

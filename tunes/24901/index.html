Gillis’ Favourite, X:2

I first learned this tune from Scots fiddler Clare McLaughlin as “Gillies Favourite.” She got it in Cape Breton. Setting X:2, however, is from a Cape Breton recording, Cynthia MacLeod’s album Crackerjack (2004). There are a few recordings of the tune, but they possibly all stem from a 1930s recording by The Inverness Serenaders, one of the fiddlers in which was Alex Gillis.

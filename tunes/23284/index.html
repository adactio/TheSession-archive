Wicklow Bookie

This is another ‘Fusion’ type tune, a 7/8 slip jig, composed by the Wexford Guitarist, banjo and bouzouki player Cian O‘Grady. Cian’s compositions are copyrighted and would require consent for republication or recording. He’s currently busy preparing the first full album of the Group ’Tulua’ of which Cian is a member, composing much of their instrumental material.

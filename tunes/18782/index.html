Mrs. James MacInnes - Edinburgh, X:2

From William Marshall’s Scottish Airs, Melodies, Strathspeys, Reels, 1822. This is a transcription of the tune as it appears in the publication, however I prefer the first note upbeat as an E. I also prefer the 6th quaver (8th note) of the 3rd bar as an A.

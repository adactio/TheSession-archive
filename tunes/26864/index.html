The Goose Sickle

The Goose Sickle was written for my friend after he decided he was going to hunt down one of the geese that were occupying his croft at the time with an old, rusty sickle from his shed. He would recount to me how he planned to pluck the bird and eat it for his tea one day. He ended up attempting it on several occasions; however, he returned fruitlessly every time. This tune is a tribute of sorts to his failed attempts to capture a goose with his trusty sickle.

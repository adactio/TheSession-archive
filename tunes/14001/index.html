Billy Pigg’s

This is a really great tune by the Northumbrian piper Billy Pigg. The A part is a bit odd, but it’s great fun to play.

↳ Posted by djfiddle on December 11th, 2014.

Re: Billy Pigg’s

Great addition to a border/small pipe repertoire this one. It has become a bit of an obsession of mine to pick up a few Northumberland and Border tunes for these instruments. A lot do not fit a single octave, mixolydian instrument particularly well but this one tweaks up nicely. I just replace the low E with a high E ( bars 2 & 5 ) and birl the low F# & E that follow the D as a low A. Works for me.

An Chúicín Bhinn ( Sweet Little Cuckoo)

An Chúicín Bhinn ( Sweet Little Cuckoo) is a lovely Traditional Irish tune sourced from Frank Roach (1866 – 1961),” Collection of Traditional Irish Music” (1927). This tune was arranged and played by Abhiann Tuath, on Màthair Uisge, A Collection of Lesser Known Traditional Celtic Tunes. https://abhainntuath.bandcamp.com/track/01-an-ch-ic-n-bhinn-sweet-little-cuckoo

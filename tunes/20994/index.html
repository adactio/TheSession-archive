“The ornamentation is optimised for piano accordion”

“this is because the triplets are played equally.”

Piano accordion or not, you notated them equally. What you’re after is often notated like DD/D/D, EF/A/c etc. (because that’s how a lot of musicians are playing). The first note is your eight note, the next two are shortened, and the final one is also an eight note. I think it would sound better with the Play audio feature, be easier to read (if that matters), and also reflect what people are doing.

This being said, I don’t recognize your tune.

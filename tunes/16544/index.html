Paddy Connelly Buys One Gets One Free 1 and 2

This set of jigs made by Lynn Tocker was intended to be played together as a set and are only differentiated by their number (1 or 2).

Lynn and I recorded this set in my house one afternoon shortly after the turn of the century;
https://soundcloud.com/holmesflute/lynne-tocker-jigs-paddy-connolly-buys-one-gets-one-free

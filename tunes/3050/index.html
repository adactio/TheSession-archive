Five comments

I’m Ready Now!

This gets played every week at our session. It’s dead easy to learn and very catchy. The 2nd part is very like the 1st part of The Monaghan Twig. This tune appears in O’Neills with the parts the other way round, but to be honest you’re probably more likely to hear it played as posted because most people will get it from Danu’s “Think Before You Think” reel set.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on May 21st, 2004.

Your Mother’s Fair Pet

This reel appears in O’Neill’s Dance Music of Ireland - 1001 Gems (1907), p. 111, no. 615, as Your Mother’s Fair Pet, Peata Geal Do Matar. It has nothing to do with “Your Mother’s little Pet” (https://thesession.org/tunes/1571).

↳ Posted by Moxhe on October 29th, 2015.

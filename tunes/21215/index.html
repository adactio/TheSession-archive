Three comments

Whittamore Road

Beautiful air by Donald Shaw from his album ‘Hebrides: Islands on the Edge Original Score’ (2013).

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on November 12th, 2021.

Whittamore Road, X:2

A whistle-friendly setting. The only Whittamore Road I can find is in Virginia, US.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on November 12th, 2021.

Whittamore Road, X:3

Here’s a version in E minor that could be played on an F whistle to match with another instrument playing it in G minor (i.e. the first setting posted here).

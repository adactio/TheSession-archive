Traveller’s Joy, X:2

This is the tune as written by the composer, Janet May, transcribed from an image of the dots sent to me by a friend of Janet’s. I have made a YouTube video based on this (link below) which she has heard and approved.
It’s slightly different from the usual version going round the sessions and we’d like to set the record straight!

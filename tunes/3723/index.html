Confusion between “Le reel de l’aveugle” and “La galerie pourrite”

I already have submitted a tune named “La galerie pourrite” but it was a mistake (confusion on the record’s cover) and the right name for the tune was “Le reel de l‘aveugle” (The blind man’s reel). I have corrected the informations in the comments of “Le reel de l’aveugle”.
So, I apologize and give the correct music for “La galerie pourrite”.
A really fine tune in B minor, played by the French-Canadian band “Le rêve du diable”.

Re: Violet Hill

From Carl Hession’s CD “Ceol Inne Ceol Inniu, I was told by the man himself that it was first recorded in 1995 for this CD. Kevin Carey, fiddle, was composing it at the time and asked Carl for help in finishing off the tune. Carl noted some people have accredited him for writing the tune but said it is Kevin that should get the acknowledgment. Carl posted the track of the tune from his CD on his YouTube channel.
https://youtu.be/V8FIE9g7uKA

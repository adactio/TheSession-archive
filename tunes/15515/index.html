Bob Johnson’s

A Scottish reel notated from a recording of Angus Chisholm from the early 1950s. It is played with a slight swing.

It was composed by A W Doig, who contributed six of his tunes to Kohler’s Violin Repository in 1881. It’s there under the title “Bob Johnstone’s Reel”.

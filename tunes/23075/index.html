The Black And Tan

Here’s another ‘ballroom’ type polka from the Power manuscript from Kilmore in County Wexford. This sheet from the manuscript is clearly by yet another hand and it’s the only sheet out of the entire folder with this particular handwriting and musical notation style. The other pieces on the page are an un-named quadrille and Blanchard’s Hornpipe.

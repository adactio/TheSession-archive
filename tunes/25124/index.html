John Mahony’s Jig (S: Denis Murphy)

Denis Murphy named this jig John Mahony’s referring to John Mahinney Barnard of Gneeveguilla, a musician from the older generation who was a friend and musical partner of Murphy’s father, Bill ‘The Weaver’. According to the in-depth booklet penned by Alan Ward, which accompanied Topic’s “Music From Sliabh Luachra” series, Bill Murphy and John Mahinney (Mahony) would frequently play together at house dances and in pubs. This would make John a likely early influence on the future star fiddle siblings Denis and Julia – indeed, Julia Murphy Clifford called several of her jigs after him.

I couldn’t find this particular tune anywhere. There is a jig titled Lucy McKenna / Luigseach Nic Cionnaith in Ceol Rince na hÉireann (CRE 1, # 25) that looks similar to me but it’s not a complete match (https://thesession.org/tunes/8438). Please let me know if you’ve come across this jig in some other collection, maybe I’m missing something!

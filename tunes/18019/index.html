Strut (Eddlestone Strut)

If my memory serves me correctly from the CD sleeve notes, this was a joint composition by Amy Thatcher of The Shee and Kieran Szifris of the Monster Ceilidh Band. In any case this is transcribed from the Shee recording on “Murmurations”. A very groovy tune!

Fiddler’s Heaven - the Key

I’ve transcribed this tune as “A Major” even though there is no G sharp anywhere in the tune.

But neither is the tune A Mixolydian, which it would be if it contained G Nats.

So strictly speaking, its key is A Hexatonic (i.e. with a missing seventh).

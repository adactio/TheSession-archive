Cuckoo in the Tree

I believe that this tune should be in cut time (2/4), so that’s how I submitted the abc. However, it would appear that (for some reason) Jeremy has modified the whole thing - so semiquavers have become quavers, quavers have become crotchets, and crotchets have become minims. This hasn’t of course changed the melody in any way.

For the record, here is my original abc submission:

Eight comments

Mc Carthy’s

This tune is not the McCarthy’s known more commonly as the ‘The man of the House’, but was collected from piper Tom Matthews, from Summerhill, Co Meath, and it appears in Breandan Breathnach’s ‘Ceol Rince na hEireann’ Book 4, No 181

It’s the opening track on an album by Angelina Carberry and Martin Quinn (2003 Reeltrad Records, RTR 001) from which this tune is transcribed.

↳ Posted by petemay on July 17th, 2004.

Mode and key

Ador and Dmix share the same scale, but the key is D and the mode is mixolydian.

Dmix is correct.

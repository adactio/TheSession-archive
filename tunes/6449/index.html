“Richard Robinson’s March” ~ not Jakob’s

I am doing a big meal today while dropping in and out of here for a bit of escapism. I had missed that Jakob was the composer of the seed for all this, and since he had dedicated the orginal to his grandfather, Richard, I will keep with that as well…

I was having some problems seeing how one goes from Jakob to Ricky, but I’m also feeling a bit thick today. I must be mad, another break was to run out and buy a Christmas tree, the earliest I’ve ever done that… I usually wait till the last week or even after Christmas and get some distorted left over for a few bob… Now dinner is near done, next it’s the decorations and mince pies and port… :-)

↳ Posted by ceolachan on December 2nd, 2006.

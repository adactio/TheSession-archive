The Shooting Star

This is a fine reel composed by fiddler/guitarist/luthier Paul Archer, based in Stockton-On-Tees (NE England). It has been recorded on his recent album “Waiting For Rain” (contact for details: www.archerguitars.co.uk) and on the album “Autumn” by local fellow-musician Martin Matthews, which is in the database here as: https://thesession.org/recordings/4178

There are low notes B, and A, as far as I can tell, in the second part of the tune, but these can be substituted for without prejudice to the tune by players whose instruments do not go below bottom D.

Paul, Martin and others regularly play a set consisting of The Steel Reel, The Shooting Star and The Gluepot; this last is a composition by Durham flute-player Norman Holmes.

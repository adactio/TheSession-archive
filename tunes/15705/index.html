Two comments

Miss Hog Of Newliston

A great old reel from The Athole Collection, included in a set played by Chris Norman and David Greenberg in their brilliant flute & fiddle solo album, Let Me In This Ae Night. Source: Fiddler’s Companion; http://trillian.mit.edu/

↳ Posted by Javi V on December 12th, 2016.

Miss Hog Of Newliston, X:2

Nice tune which would make a useful Strathspey, so here is. I’ll either play it in Gm or Em. Sounds as if Miss Hog was a slippery one…

Two comments

The third, and by no means the least, of the three Leslie Harris reels recorded on Trian I by Liz Carroll and Billy McComiskey, after whose son the tune is named. Great tune for either fiddle or box, but it’s also in Lesl Harker’s brand new book of tunes she got from Mike Rafferty, so not bad for the flute either.

↳ Posted by groovycelt on April 21st, 2009.

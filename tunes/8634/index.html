Paddy Fahey’s, X:2

X:2 Setting recorded by Liz and Yvonne Kane on opening track of “In Memory of Paddy Fahey” https://thesession.org/recordings/7638 . Marie Holohan included the same basic setting as Paddy Fahey Reel #13 Variation on p. 256 of her Ph.D. thesis. It has the same first part as X:1 with a different, some might say better, second part. The composer can be heard playing it around the 28:20 mark here: https://youtu.be/Gr8h3X_o2Dc?si=7_47-1G35n1R-E9p

Two comments

The Inside-Oot Fish Eater

This is a great tune composed by the notable (forgive the pun) Scott’s accordion player Peter Wood who plays with ‘The Shetland Dance Band’. I have searched the net for this tune and found a version on YouTube from which I learned and have now transcribed for the benefit of all. It is quite an energetic tune and I hope you have fun with it as well.
Mark

↳ Posted by Mark Percival on April 5th, 2017.

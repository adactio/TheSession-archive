Berceuse Pour Anna/Lullaby For Anna

A French Canadian tune written by Richard Forest and as played by Andre Brunet.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F3iVloKLJYs

↳ Posted by earle on November 12th, 2019.

Re: Berceuse Pour Hannah /Lullaby For Hannah

I’ve corrected the spelling of the name in the title for you. It may be pronounced “Anna” by Québécois musicians such as André Brunet, but the name of the young girl to whom the piece was dedicated is “Hannah”. And the tune was composed jointly by Réjean Brunet, Richard Forest and Sabin Jacques.

Re: Berceuse Pour Hannah

Sabin Jacques was my accordion teacher. His group Domino were performing at Summerfolk in Owen Sound Ontario years ago. The family they billeted with gave up their daughter Hannah’s room for them and in gratitude Domino wrote this tune for her.

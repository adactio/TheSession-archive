Myles The Slasher

A reel composed by Patrick O‘Leary in the late 1800s or early 1900s. The tune was contained in a music booklet given to me by Patrick’s Grandson Des O’Leary. I had misplaced this booklet but found it last week. Hope you enjoy the tunes.

Re: Skålarna Efter Byss Kalle!

A good tune (and nicely played!) but not, I think, a polska. It’s described as a ‘Skänklåt’ (which I think means a tune written as a gift) and while a gift-tune could well be a polska, this particular one is in 2/4 rather than the typical polska time sig. of 3/4, so perhaps the recipient of the gift was not a dancer.

According to Swedish wikipedia, the skänklåt was played primarily when collecting money for the player (spelman) from the audience. It was also used when wedding gifts were given to the couple being married.

The Pumphouse

This is a jig penned by Mick McAuley and as played by him on his cd An Ocean’s Breadth. He follows it with two slip jigs: Malbay Nights (https://thesession.org/tunes/18295) and the Fishnet Jig, both his own compositions as well.

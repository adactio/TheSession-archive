According to Pekaar, it’s in Robert Mathieson’s first book “Marking Time”, but they say the composer isn’t listed, whereas Robert Mathieson is listed as composer for some of the other tunes (Bagad Reel, The Opal, Commander Roger Reid (Victoria Police), etc).
Having said that, the only reference to The Hag’s Dance on Pekaar is for “Marking Time”.

Source

Another great Ross Ainslie tune. Transcription based on the *Partners in Crime* album with Jarlath Henderson. The tune is described as an “Air and Tune” on the album. I considered transcribing it as a hornpipe, but stuck with a reel for simplicity’s sake.

The boys play this tune on E whistles. For the whistle-impaired, here’s the tune transposed to Emaj:

K: Emaj
|: e3e dcBA | e2f/g/a g2fe | zABe ffBA | Befg a3a |
bgab c’2e/e/e | a3g/f/ e3z | BABf eeBA |1 Bde2 fzgf :|2 Bde2 f4 ||
K: Emin
|: Bedc BGGG | AEF/G/A B^c^de | fe^ga beee | a3f/e/ d4 |
Bedc BGGG | AEEE EFGA | BABf eeBA |1 B^de2 f4 :|2 B^de2 fz^gf ||

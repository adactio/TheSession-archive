The Hickleton Head

This tune by yours truly, came joint 3rd in the Northumbrian Pipers Society composition competition 2020.
Hickleton church porch (South Yorkshire, UK) has 3 skulls set behind bars with the inscription “Today for me, Tomorrow for thee”. One skull was stolen and is still at large despite the vicar announcing a skull amnesty. Hangmanstone Lane is not far away and they were reputed to be 3 robbers hung there. A headless horseman is reputed to frequent the church crossroads.
Is this tune the missing skull dancing with the rest of his skeleton?

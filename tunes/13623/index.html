Shoulder Dancing

Catchy whistle tune from Diarmaid Moynihan in G pentatonic. On his recording The Lights of Ranzanico.

He does some nice variations on the B-part:
egzd eGBd | eged B3d | gdeg BE~E2 | GA B/c/d ebag |
bzag ae~e2 | dged B2de | dBAG EGDE | GBAE ~G3A ||

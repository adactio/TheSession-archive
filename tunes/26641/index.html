The Tree Of Luck

Written by Hamish Napier with two native cherry trees in mind.
Originally in A flat, transposed it to A major for a more session-friendly version.
From the album “The Woods”:
https://hamishnapier.bandcamp.com/album/the-woods

“Both berries could be made into a devilish brandy that was forbidden in 18th century Strathspey due to the wild effect it had on the local forestry workers.”

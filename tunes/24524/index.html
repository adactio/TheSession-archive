On The Winds Of Chaos Born

Perhaps one of the coolest tunes I’ve ever learned, off of Duncan Chisholm’s album Black Cuillin. The B part’s pretty tricky to get a hang of, but in essence, the B part can be counted as 7/8, except for where it drops an eighth note in the 1st ending. Sorry about the weird formatting; the ABC notation was tweaking about connecting lines that had meter changes. I tried notating it like 10 different ways and eventually gave up on making it look aesthetic.

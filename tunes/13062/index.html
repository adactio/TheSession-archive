The Laird O’Brodie

I heard this tune on Music for Trust Vol.2 from Battlefield Band, played by Brian McNeill. I don’t really know if this is a Strathspey because Brian McNeill is playing it more like a Air, but with all these Scot Snaps I think it’s the right category.

Re: The Laird O’Brodie

It’s a slow strathspey a popular form with fiddlers in Scotland. I have a book called the Caledonian Companion which gives excellent instruction on how to play all the different tune forms from Scotland

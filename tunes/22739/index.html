Philosophy And The Free Elf

This is an original by 2 time All-Ireland Champ Jake James. It can be found on his album “Firewood” and is played last in the set called “For All That You Do”. In this set, Jake tunes up his fiddle to Eb making the Ab major. If you play the tune in standard tuning it is in G major with the same fingerings. This tune was written in honor of his dogs.

Gan Ainm

I found a transcription of this tune, attributed to Liz Carroll, in a bunch of sheet music yesterday. I think it came from a tape someone gave me years back of a live concert by “Trian” - Liz Carroll [ fiddle ], Billy McComiskey [ button-accordion ] and Daithi Sproule [ guitar / vocals ]. I don’t think it’s on either of the 2 “Trian” CDs, and have no other name for it, although Liz Carroll may have recorded it herself elsewhere, and some of you might recognise it.
Thanks to Aberdeen flute player Malcolm Reavell for the transcription.

Re: Aotea Rua

Thanks for posting this. I first heard it on “The Commonwealth Suite” by the band Rua in 1991. It later appeared on Rua’s “Homeland” collection. Great CD, perhaps still available via Greentrax.

Jimmy plays the tune on Northumbrian Smallpipes, and it’s gorgeous. He has the alternative title for the tune as “Land of the White Cloud”, and indicates that the “base” of the tune is a traditional Maori canoe-song.

↳ Posted by Tervs and Tunes on February 1st, 2024.

Re: Aotea Rua

Aotearoa (one word) is the Maori name for New Zealand, and translates as “Land of the Long White Cloud”. And Rua were a New Zealand band, based in Christchurch. (There have been other bands of the same name since). The piper in the band, Jimmy Young, played Northumbrian pipes. Their “Commonwealth Suite” won the NZ Folk Awards “Best Folk Album” for 1990 -91. I had it on cassette!

That’s Cat, Annie

A lovely reel composed by accordionist Ann Marie Hayes, daughter of the wonderful Paddy Hayes. This and another lovely jig of Ann Marie’s were included in ‘The Wexford Tunebook’, which I recently published. All of Paddy Hayes‘ children played while in London and Ann Marie continues to play the accordion, like her father. ’That’s cat’ was a favourite expression of Paddy’s apparently, usually in jest at someone’s musical efforts, while Annie was his pet name for his daughter. Thanks to Ann not only for agreeing to include her tunes, but for a lot of help with information about Paddy and his music.

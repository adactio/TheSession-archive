Minnie MacMaster’s

Tune transcribed from Natalie MacMaster album “cape breton girl”. I have put indicative chords that I currently use when playing it on accordion. I can not figure out if it is a composition from Natalie MacMaster or Jerry Holland, may be someone have an idea about that.

Re: Minnie MacMaster’s

Jerry Holland did write a tune called “Minnie MacMaster,” but it’s not this one. This one was indeed composed by Natalie MacMaster.

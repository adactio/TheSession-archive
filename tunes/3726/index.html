“Itsbynne Reel” - get it?

I put together an ABC of this to share the tune with my band mates, so I thought I’d submit it here as well. Trad form + jazz influence = great tune, taken to the nth degree by Mr. Brecker on his album, but also given a nice treatment by the Boston Pops.

LOL, that’s bad! My favorite B Pops track of “trad” music is when they tried to play Toss the Feathers and couldn’t (or wouldn’t) do any triplets or rolls on the repeated d’s in the B part. So to approximate (I’m being kind here) the timing, they inserted rests. The result (instead of |A~d3 A~d3|A~d3 edcd| we were treated to |Adz2 Adz2|Adz2 edcd|) was hysterical, a huge orchestral stutter. Of course, it also did a great disservice to the tune.

But that’s just my opinion, eh?

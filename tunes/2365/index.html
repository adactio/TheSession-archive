North Highland Country Dance

This tune is originally from the Patrick McDonald Collecton (1784). It is untitled, but as it’s in the section called ‘North Highland Reels or Country Dances’, it has been known as “North Highland” or “North Highland Country Dance”, possibly first by Bonnie Rideout.

Three comments

Central Point

Another lovely tune by Ian Stephenson from FRIGG. Not a march, but rather a slow tune in walking tempo.
The funny time signature changes hail from my personal conception of the tune and might be interpreted different by the composer or other trad. players.

The script here for some reason does not allow the reverse slash which needs to be filled in after the short irregular bars - if you do that after the abc download it will look right. Any tipps for the cure of that problem?

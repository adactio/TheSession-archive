Re: The Last Chance

@Kess, well, FWIW, it was recorded in 1977 by Kerry’s own Jimmy Doyle & Dan O‘Leary (O’Leary plays it solo on that track) and it sound similar to a couple of other Irish polkas like Joe Bane’s. My guess is that it might be popular at some Breton sessions due to its peculiar mode or else it might be an instance of a shared “continental” tune from the quadrille-craze era that stuck with both traditions (maybe not so much in Ireland – but thanks to Aidan Connolly’s recording it would now be more recognised!)

↳ Posted by Bregolas on March 27th, 2025.

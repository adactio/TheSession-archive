Bobby Brown’s

A very catchy Canadian jig composed by Jack Hayes which I picked up at Elgin.
Nice key change in Part 2 and then reverts to A for the repeat of the first part to finish.

Re: Bobby Brown’s

Further, Bobby Brown was a Scots/Canadian pianist and accordion player who was highly respected on the Scottish Dance Band scene in Ontario.

Jack Hayes, as you will maybe know, also composed The Lemonville Jig which was “popularised” by Bobby Brown’s band.

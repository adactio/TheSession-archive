The tune appears as a quickstep in David Glen’s Collection of Highland Bagpipe Music, Book 1 (c 1876).
It also appears as a 2/4 march in later pipe books. It is attributed to a “Donald Mackenzie”. This is unlikely to be the same “Pipe Corpl Donald McKenzie” (Seaforth Highlanders/ PM in Cameron Highlanders) who has a tune printed in Glen’s Book 15 , as he would have been about a year old when Book 1 was published!

Whether the tune has any connection with the ballad “The Cruel Mither”, which also has the “Greenwood Side” in other title variants is open to speculation.

Catherine And Thurlow’s

Here’s a nice jig from Cape Breton fiddler and composer, Donald Angus Beaton. I wrote out the abc for this using the recording (http://www.celticmusiccentre.com/mp3s/byDAB/cath_dab.mp3) of Donald Angus Beaton playing it. It goes nicely after the Pelee Island Jig I composed (https://thesession.org/tunes/12399).

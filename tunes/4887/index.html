Re: Cahal’s

…sometimes I play the B-part entirely in third position on fiddle; depending on my energy levels. But ending it on an open E string, the second time through, makes an easy transition to the repeat A-part.

↳ Posted by Erik-Fiddler on October 11th, 2022.

Cahal’s, X:2

The second setting are just some ideas, kind of what I do on the b-part sometimes. If I’m not playing it low, I’ll likely play the entire b-part in third position, on the A-string. Easy that way starting on “d”.

Alternatively, you could play the first two bars of the b-part in first position before shifting quickly to third position on bar B3 on the note “a3”. Either way, the open “e” string at the end of the repeat makes for an easy exit from the higher octave.

↳ Posted by Erik-Fiddler on April 18th, 2024.

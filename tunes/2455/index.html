Re: The Banks Of The Quay

The original title of this wren tune was ‘Back The Quay’. De Danann recorded it on a tour of Germany and Switzerland. When it came to putting down the liner notes, Charlie Piggott rang his uncle, Anton Flannery, who gladly passed on the names of the tunes. Unfortunately, because of a crackling international phone connection, Charlie misheard this one as ‘The Banks of the Quay’, and so it was made famous under that title.

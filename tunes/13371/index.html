Four comments

John Smeaton

Named after the baggage handler who helped to thwart a terrorist attack at Glasgow Airport in 2007, this tune was composed by Ewen Henderson and Gary Innes of the band Mànran on their 2013 album ‘The Test’. Requested by Stephen Drummond.

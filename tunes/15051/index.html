Southern Aristocracy

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on December 11th, 2015.

Origin: “Colored Aristocracy” ~ Mudcat discussion

http://mudcat.org/thread.cfm?threadid=24017

A popular tune for American contra dances, Big Circle, New England Squares/Quadrilles, The Grand March/Promenade, and the Cakewalk… We used to play this one quite a lot…

It’s earliest and most widely known name being “Colored Aristocracy”, with the supposedly P.C. version coming in the 20th Century: “Southern Aristocracy”…

Re: Colored Aristocracy

As I recall this was recorded by the New Lost City Ramblers who got from a West Virginia field recording by Charles Seeger. There is a 19th century cakewalk by this title but, as far as I can tell it has no melodic relationship to this tune.

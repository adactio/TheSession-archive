Soul Train

An unusual nicely syncopated slow reel from the guitarist and banjo player Cian O‘ Grady from south Wexford. Cian is a member of the excellent up-and-coming trad-fusion group ’Tulua’, all of whom have played music together since they were children with the Danecastle music group and Bannow CCE.
You can hear the band performing ‘Soul Train’ here:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMrQsQiKmc4&ab_channel=TULUAMusic

Thanks to Nóra Byrne-Kavanagh for transcribing the piece as part of the Wexford Tune Project. Cian’s compositions are copy righted, so would require consent to be re-used or re-recorded.

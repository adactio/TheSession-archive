Le Reel Du Nord

Quebec Reel. Composed by Montreal accordionist Alfred Montmarquette (1871-1944) in 1929. Seems he composed many tunes other tunes: http://www.mustrad.udenap.org/bios/montmarquette_alfred.html

There’s a key change in the B Part, Dmaj or Amix, in either case, the tune has two sharps throughout.

Here’s Andy Reiner playing it:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RkILAt85a1U

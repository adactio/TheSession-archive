The Cordal

From Brendan Breathnach’s Ceol Rince na hEireann Vol. 1, collected from fiddler Denis Cronin. A lovely simple tune with a surprisingly low internet profile; I could not find the dots anywhere - except in the book itself. So here it is, hoping it will get played more.

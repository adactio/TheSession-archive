Wasabi Heaven (not)

This tune by John McCusker and Ian Carr is called Wabazi (not Wasabi) Heaven. According to the Goodnight Ginger sleeve notes the name refers to ‘sweeties’ and not to the Japanese condiment, although this mistake may already have passed into folklore.

Late Night In Glasgow

Lovely, lovely tune by Sarah Markey. More of a slow reel, if anything. Played on an E♭ flute, so tranpose as necessary for concert pitch.

Here are the notes for the tune from Sarah herself: «The idea for the first tune in this set came to me during a late night of tunes with friends at Celtic Connections in Glasgow. I felt very inspired by all the amazing musicians I met in sessions and loved hearing all the different styles of music they played throughout the festival».

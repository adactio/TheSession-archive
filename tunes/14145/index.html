Keep The Country Bonnie Lassie (Na Caill a Chailag Ladhach)

Very Old tune. Featured as a song in Works of Robert Burns also in Gunn collection. Sits well with Lady Bighouses.

↳ Posted by jakethepeg on January 25th, 2015.

Keep The Country Bonnie Lassie, X:2

This is a modern Cape Breton version of the tune where it is played as a fast strathspey in A major, usually leading on into a reel proper. The Gaelic title suggests a better translation might be ‘Don’t lose the bonnie lassie’ or possibly ‘Don’t lose the bonnie country lassie’. It was recorded by the Rankin family, following on from ‘The Lime Hill’.

St Giles’ Cathedral

With apologies to John Sheehan…

After admiring “Christchurch Cathedral” (https://thesession.org/tunes/6249) and “St Patrick’s Cathedral” (https://thesession.org/tunes/11778), I thought Edinburgh’s 14th century St Giles’ Cathedral should have a similar tribute. I’ve tried to emulate the “feel” of Sheehan’s tunes with their nods to baroque and sacred music; whether I succeeded will be decided by others.

One comment

Gan Ainm

This is an unnamed hornpipe from the Power manuscript from Kilmore in County Wexford. This is from the section of the manuscript that has some notation dated in the 1870’s. I’ve found a similar tune notated in the Reynolds manuscript from Longford, titled as ‘The Dublin Hornpipe’.

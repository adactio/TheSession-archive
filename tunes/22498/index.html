One comment

Hey, Hey, Rise Up Ukraine

There was a wonderful programme about this tune and its importance for Ukrainian identity on Radio 4. I thought a dance version would be nice to incorporate into appropriate sets as a gesture of support and I thought other players might feel the same way.
I’ve added some seconds to thicken up its performance a bit.

Catriona’s

Composed by David Foley. Original key of Rura’s “Catriona’s.”

↳ Posted by Eli on May 2nd, 2021.

Catriona’s, X:2

Key of D Major transposition heard in “Takit” from David Foley&Jack Smedley’s Time to Fly album.

↳ Posted by Eli on May 2nd, 2021.

Catriona’s, X:3

Love this tune. This version works nicely on a whistle and would come out in D major on an A whistle.

↳ Posted by Aberfoyle Whistler on May 3rd, 2021.

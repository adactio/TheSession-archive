Star of Munster Trio

This (great) reel is the second tune on track 3 of the Star of Munster Trio.
They were Julia, John, and Billy Clifford.
I have heard that Paddy Cronin (RIP, March 15 2014) was a great man for
finding good tunes in O’Neill’s and other old tune books. So this might be Paddy’s
version of an old tune. Or, it could be a tune he learned from Padraig O’Keeffe.

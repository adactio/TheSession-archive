Bouncing Sam Jago

In response to a recent recording posted by by Threepenny Bit.
This is a popular tune played here in Cornwall written by Hillary Coleman some years ago from an idea she had when bouncing her son on her knee.
While not Irish, it is a good rollicking tune when played with a bit of push.
For easy playing or fitting in with other instruments, I often play this “up a string” in Am

Two comments

Old Peadar Carthy From Clonmore

I came across this - rather strange - hornpipe at Bill Black’s Cape Irish site (www.capeirish.com) - in his transcription of Darley & McCall’s 1914 publication “The Darley And McCall Collection Of Irish Music”. X:1 is a direct copy of Bill’s transcription. X:2 is how, after several runs-through, I found myself playing it. I found myself gravitating towards playing the “c” in measures 1, 3 and 5 of the second part as a sharp rather than natural, as in the original setting. If you’d like a GDAE tablature version of the X:2 tune, you can download it at https://crosseyirishmandolin.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/old-peadar-carthy-from-clonmore-hornpipe-gmaj-png.png And if, heaven forbid!, you’d like to hear a bare-bones solo version of X:2 on electric mandolin, then check it out at https://crosseyirishmandolin.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/old-peadar-carthy-from-clonmore-hornpipe-gmaj-belmuse.mp3

The Black Swan On The Turlough

This was requested by MacChristo72. It’s a Michael McGoldrick jig from the album “Arc”. McGoldrick plays this composition rather slowly and on a B-flat flute, so that it comes out in concert C dorian instead of E dorian. Pleasant to listen to, and I like the variations on some of the part repeats.

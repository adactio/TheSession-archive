Re: Rapide Du Joual Blanc

This tune is the composition of the late Jean Claude Mirandette, who passed away April 12, 2019. The tune appears on a 1995 CD called “Le Rapide du Joual Blanc” recorded by the group La Galvaude. Stephanie Lepine and Eric Beaudry were among the members of this group which was form in 1991.

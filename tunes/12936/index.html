Welcome to thesession.org, FaliqAuri, I hope this site is a great resource to you, as it has been to me.

However, please take a little time to read this, https://thesession.org/help , if you’re new here. Especially the part about:

"Can I submit my own compositions?
Well… The Session isn’t really intended for that. If you do post one of your own compositions, then you must do your “penance”, so to speak, by balancing each original composition with about five trad tune settings."

This isn’t to put you off writing tunes, oh no, in fact keep at it! Just instead of submitting them to the database,, which “isn’t really intended for that”, post them in your profile here: https://thesession.org/members/85708 as many other members have done.

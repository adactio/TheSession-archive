Bridget McBride

Basic transcription from the playing of Anne McAuliffe, Nicky McAuliffe and Michael O’Shea (second tune, starting at 00:55):

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_1zgPJN3dt0

This is tune 1254 in O‘Neill’s 1850. In his commentary to the O’Neill’s collections, with reference to tunes 1251-1254 Paul de Grae writes: “These four tunes are not found in any of the older collections in the Bibliography; it is thus at least possible that they were composed by Cronin.”

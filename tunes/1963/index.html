Cup of Tea

Cathal McConnell mentioned that he called this tune The Cup of Tea and that it “might” be a Charlie Lennon composition. But, to reiterate a previous comment, it does sound like an interesting reworking of the well-known, 3-part Cup of Tea. I’ve been playing this tune for many years and never actually made that connection until today. Did the McDonagh Brothers of Ballinafad record this tune at some stage?

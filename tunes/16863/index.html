Four comments

Going Home

This reel comes from boxplayer and fiddler Eamon Flynn of Mountcollins, Co. Limerick. Eamon played with the likes of Paddy Cronin, Brendan Tonra, Larry Reynolds, and Tommy Sheridan while in Boston before moving to Vermont, USA to influence another generation of musicians.

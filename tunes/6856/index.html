“Miss Linda MacFarlane” is very often played by another composition by Robin Brock called “MacRostie Park” (a Victorian park in my home town of Crieff in Perthshire, with an old-fashioned bandstand in it). I don’t know whether to reproduce “MacRostie Park” in these notes, or as a separate tune. Hmmm…

Robin Brock is a bass player who recorded with the likes of The Corries in the 1960s, and has played in several Scottish country dance bands.

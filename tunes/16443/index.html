Re: Terry Teahan’s

There’s not a missing half beat in X:1, but, as Nico says, it’s off by a half beat in that the first note (d) is a pick up note (anacrusis). The abcs should read: d|c {d}c AG|A/B/c de|…. etc.
It’s a common mistake on this site.

↳ Posted by DonaldK on April 5th, 2019.

Re: Terry Teahan’s

“It’s a common mistake on this site.”

Indeed, but it works the other way around as well. 3/4 retreat marches, where almost all pipe sources notate
“ | NN N2 N2 | ”
I can’t help changing it to
“ NN | N2 N2 ”
Who am I to say that pipers got it wrong?

And mazurkas on this site are almost always notated
“ NN | N2 N2 ”
But the emphasis in a mazurka is on the 2nd or 2nd and 3rd beats in the bar, so they should be notated
“ | NN N2 N2 | ”

Gotta stand and face it, life is so complicated.

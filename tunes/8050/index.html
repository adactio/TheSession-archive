Two comments

In memory of Johnny…

Must be one of the first tunes composed by Johnny Cunningham, who died 4 years ago yesterday. It’s a slowish jig, almost a 6/8 march, in fact, which he played on mandola on his first solo recording “Thoughts From Another World”. Not 100% convinced that it’s in E dor, but the 2 sharps fit. Someone may wish to advise.
I’m fairly sure “P.M.” stands for brother Phil, and not any Pipe Major.

↳ Posted by Kenny on December 17th, 2007.

P.M. Cunningham’s Odd Dance, X:2

I’ve come up with a slightly different transcription to Kenny’s, done direct from the source recording.
The dotted crotchets I have marked with rolls are mostly played as 3 quaver triplets in the album rendition, variably with 3 of the same note or with middle note raised or lowered one step ad lib. Also, the Bdd figures in the B music may sometimes be played Bc#d.

This could be considered to be in E Dorian (2 sharps throughout) since the A music is gapped on C/C# and any Cs in the B music are C#s. I’m probably wrong to put it in Em with accidental C#s.

Album info for the source recording can be found here: https://www.discogs.com/release/4971209-John-Cunningham-Thoughts-From-Another-World and I used this (unfortunately poor quality audio) transfer on YouTube to make my transcription: https://youtu.be/WCRi8GngtHw?si=yk4W9ZHMwIUdgukK&t=559

You can download my transcription of both tunes in the set here: https://app.box.com/s/lz6otxcxf9c6722x5vr3izro82cqu598

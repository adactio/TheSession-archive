Seven comments

The Sheffield Hornpipe

I put this tune in after seeing it on the Requests list. It’s the setting in the Charlton Memorial Tunebook, produced some time ago by the Northumbrian Pipers’ Society. I know nothing more about this tune, or how it connects with Sheffield.

↳ Posted by nicholas on November 19th, 2006.

The only recording I know of this tune is from the Boys of the Lough, “The West of Ireland.” Aly Bain and Brendan Begley do a very nice fiddle/accordion duet. Maybe somebody could look up what they say about this tune in their liner notes.

↳ Posted by smw on November 19th, 2006.

Thanks for this as I used to play it when I lived in Sheffield but had forgotten it.

Re: The Sheffield

The Traditional Tune Archive dates the first appearance of this hornpipe to The Charlton Memorial Tunebook of 1974, but this was a reprint of the 1956 first edition and, as far as I know, had no additional tunes in it.

↳ Posted by Gallopede on June 5th, 2023.

Re: The Sheffield

This tune is in the William Thomas Green manuscript from Morpeth, 1851, so that pushes it back another 100 years. It is a little bit different and is in G and very high pitched but it’s the same tune. There’s a transcription to ABC by Ruairidh Greig at http://www.cpartington.plus.com/Links/GreenWT/Green%2824-10-16%29.abc

Three comments

Johnny Ward’s

Another highland from Vincent Campbell from the album ‘The Brass Fiddle’. This is the first in the set, followed by
“Paddy Bartley’s” https://thesession.org/tunes/6606

From the sleeve notes: “Johnny Ward was a fiddler from Gageen Hill in the Croaghs, a mountainour region of great beauty between Glenties and Ballybofey”

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on January 8th, 2007.

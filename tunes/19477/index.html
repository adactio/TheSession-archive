Five comments

The Hungry Eye

This mad jig has been haunting me for years. The dots recently turned up in a draw I was cleaning out and as I can’t find it on here, I submit it as my first contribution to The Session. I have no info as to origin or composer, anything would be welcome. I can’t figure out how to represent triplets, but for me they play a large part in the tune and are very effective wherever the “bDb” structure can be found.
For variation the D arpeggio is lovely to replace the f# in bar 5. The only mention I can find is on the album Pure Irish Uilleann Pipes and is beautifully played by Neil Martin & Ray Gallen.

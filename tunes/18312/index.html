Re: Valse du Petit-Déjeuner = La Mal-Aimable

The original title of this lovely waltz is La Mal-Aimable
It was composed by Jean-Christophe Lequerré (1961-2006) a talented accordionist from France.
Here’s another score for it, with tabs;
https://www.diatonalpartitions.fr/partitions/compositions/mal-aimable-(la)-(j.c.-lequerr%C3%A9)

The original title means “The not very likeable”
or “The rude woman” .
I wonder how the tune acquired both its original name and its later monicker.

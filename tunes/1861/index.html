The Holm Band Jig

A lovely tune from Orkney isles, Scotland. It is on Wrigley Sister’s “Mither o’ the Sea” album.

I love to play it after Tobbin’s Favourite. Though the tune is in A major, it is relatively easy to get G# on the whistle.

It may be related to The Maid at the Well, but I’m not sure of the origin of the tune.

As an Orcadian, I’d like to make folk who want to play/discuss this tune aware that the pronunciation of Holm is “ham”. It is not known who composed this tune - presumably one of The Holm Band (which no longer exists)!

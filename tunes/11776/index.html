“The Old Torn Petticoat” ~ as a hornpipe

You’ll find another hornpipe version in the comments for the reel, here ~

“The Old Torn Petticoat”
Submitted on November 13th 2001 by Will Harmon.
https://thesession.org/tunes/368

Which reminded me of that general alternative way with it. After all, hornpipes are constantly ironed out into reels, and the same for all those lovely highland flings, so why not the other way round, as here. I’d love to see other hornpipe takes on this one if you or someone you know has given it that swing…

For button’s sake I usually add an ironed out version in the comments, but as the reel is already here that should suffice. ;-)

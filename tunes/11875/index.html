Two comments

“Martin McCabe’s Barndance/Hornpipe” ~ C: Jack Hayes of Toronto, Canada

I know nothing at all about Jack Hayes, so any information is welcome, but we have had the pleasure of the music and hospitality of the McCabe clan. I was reminded of this when rummaging through a shipment that came here unexpectedly, on the end of tragedy for others. In it I came across a name we knew, and a smile was raised, a book by Pat McCabe, his “Tutor and Tunes for the Button and Piano Accordion”, Dundalk, 1980 (ISBN: 0-906576-03-2).

As I was reminiscing and leafing through I saw this old number and remembered playing it and dancing to it. While in the book it is listed as “Martin McCabe’s Hornpipe”, it has that added definition of the dances that work well with it - barndances and schottisches, so I’ve put it under that category. My memory is different from the books, and the transcription given is along those lines. But , for those that would like to know the differences, I’ll also add the ABCs and chords from Pat’s book, which I believe is no longer in circulation?

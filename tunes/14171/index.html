The Mountain Dew

This is the tune listed as “The Mountain Dew” - second tune on “Weeds in the Garden” by the Lewis Family: https://thesession.org/recordings/1420

It’s a lovely little single reel, I’m not sure how commonly it’s played or if it’s known by another name. I only know it from this recording.

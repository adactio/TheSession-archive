Damp in the Attic…the bandname

Damp in the Attic is the name of a Trad. band based around Clonmel, Co Tipperary. The expression has the same meaning as “being a few pence short of a shilling” or “a bottle or two short of a six-pack”
Great band with some alternative tunes and instruments!!

Reign Of Love

This is a tune by Jeremiah McLane

↳ Posted by benschreiber on September 14th, 2015.

composer

This tune was written by Keith Murphy and published in his Black Isle Music II. All music copyrighted 2012 Keith Murphy/Black Isle Music, BMI. Keith says in his notes “Reign of Love (Rain of Love) - When our friends Eliot and Sarah Hiebert Burch were married in Philadelphia, we were invited to play for the event. I composed this tune somewhat in the Breton style as Sarah had requested. The event was showered in rain and love.”
Keith’s book is available by sending $15 plus $3 S & H to Keith Murphy 104 Washington St Brattleboro, VT 05301.

Jeremiah Mc Lane has written many wonderful tunes, has his own tune book and for nearly 20 years was bandmates with Keith and Becky Tracy.

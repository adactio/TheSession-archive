Reel Au Ralenti

Here is a nice reel written by Québec fiddler André Brunet and recorded by him and Eric Beaudry on their B+B recording. The title means “Idling reel” or “slow motion reel”, and André Brunet plays it at a moderate pace.
This is the first tune on the attached video.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoVCG5kRoms&ab_channel=JudyKavanagh
Here is the same setting in G major :
T:Reel au ralenti
C:André Brunet
M:4/4
L:1/8
K:Gmaj
g2dg edcA | dBBB cBAc | B3B DGBG | AGFG Adef |
g2dg edce | dBBB cBAc | B3d edcB |1 AGFG A/2G/2FGA :|2 AGFG A/2G/2FG^G |:

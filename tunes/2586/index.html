The sunnyside

Jig composed by Niall Vallely on the album Callan Bridge.
linernotes : “the sunnyside jig was written after a particularly enjoyable week in Sunnyside in New York.”

Sunnyside

Notation as heard on the Callan Bridge album by the Vallely Brothers. Played in a set with The High Jig and The Girl of the House. Listed on the album as Sunnyside. I like it because it cooks…so to speak. Will post the other two tunes above as well.

↳ Posted by Edward Ebel on January 9th, 2017.

Re: The Sunnyside

There is a lovely rendition of this on the piper Seánie Ó Loinsigh’s debut album Conghie Road. Some other lovely tracks there too, but this caught my attention when he played it on the Geantraí programme on TG4 after his own beautiful slow air “Achill”.

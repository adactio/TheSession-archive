The Wedding

The tune, properly titled “ The Wedding Reel” is by Edel Sullivan from Cork and is played with swing at a relaxed tempo.
I heard it played at a session a few months ago and then again last night. I’ve transcribed it from the recording Edel did for “In The Time Of” (released in 2006) which you can hear here:
https://edelsullivan.bandcamp.com/track/the-wedding-reel

The transcription illustrates some of the different ways with the parts, with the variations interchangeable.
Here it is in 2010: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B4m-rQpxb3w.

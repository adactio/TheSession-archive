“Up to Moville” - highland fling

The title comes from Ed Reavy, and a transcription for this straight, sans > & <, can be found here:

“The Music of Corktown: The Reavy Collection of Irish-American Tunes”
(Volume I, but no other volumes have been released.)
by Joseph M. Reavy, 1979
http://www.reavy.us/
http://www.reavy.us/corktown.html

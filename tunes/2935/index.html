Re: Ballykeale

Dave,
Commonality was name of group from Belfast. They issued album in 1989.
So Tamalin and Commonality were two different groups, but with some same members ( but not all!)
Commonality was formed before Tamalin.
Members were: Davy Maguire on flute, Paul Mc Sherry on gitar, his sister Joanne Mc Sherry on fiddle and Milo Crossan on mandolin.
Tamalin was group consist of members of Mc Sherry familly ( John on pipes, Joanne, Paul and Tiona on flutes) and lateKevin Dorris.

Five comments

Connie In The Pool

From Seamus Creagh album ‘Came the Dawn’, Ossian Publications
Composed by Seamus Creagh.
Booklet : ‘I wrote the first tune after an incident at a party at Don Walsh’s House’.
I don’t see what incident it could be : -)
It is followed by another polka ‘The Gortnatubrid’
Phil

Two comments

A’Chubhag (The Cuckoo)

A catchy Scottish tune from the beautiful harp-bouzouki duo of Gráinne Hambly & William Jackson, as they recorded it on their must-have album Music from Ireland and Scotland (2009).

https://hamblyjackson.bandcamp.com/album/music-from-ireland-and-scotland-2

It comes in a set with another colourful piping reel Eliza Ross’ (https://thesession.org/tunes/3473) and the CD notes say they learned both reels from the playing of piper Iain MacInnes. Both can be found on the piper’s 1999 album Tryst but in separate sets of tunes. https://thesession.org/recordings/2048

A’Chubhag is a form of ‘cuthag’, which is the name for the cuckoo in Gàidhlig. A fine wee tune for all the birdwatching musicians out there :-)

↳ Posted by Bregolas on February 26th, 2024.

I’m one of those with reservations about the posting of self-penned stuff on the site (alright; let’s not re-ignite the debate . . . ) but this one might make me reconsider. Had I read ‘A tune I wrote . . .’ before playing it I probably would have gone no further, but I did play it first and found it a lively and inventive little number: perhaps more of a music-hall flavour than Irish trad, but a delight anyway.

If particularly churlish online interactions are likely to produce more like this, darinkelly, feel free to send me a PM and I’ll reply with something really obstreperous to see if it provokes another good tune.

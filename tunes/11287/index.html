“Ripple Of The Teign” ~ C: Bob Cann

S: Bob Cann

A favourite English box player and character there are numerous different versions of this and I’m hoping others will offer theirs here, as I will. Bob plays this as given, 62 bars, but I’ve been more familiar with it as a 32 bar tune. Using the given transcription for a start, here’s a 32 bar version ~

X: 2
T: Ripple Of The Teign
C: Bob Cann
S: Bob Cann
M: 6/8
L: 1/8
R: jig
K: Dmaj

“Hardcore English: 300 cracking tunes from manuscript, recorded and aural sources”

Compiled and edited by Barry Callaghan
Tunes set by Pete Stewart
Published by The English Folk Dance & Song Society, 2007

Page 64: “Ripple of the Teign”

The version here is 32 bars in length and while the A-part is basically the same as the B-part here, the B-part is quite different… Here is another take based on the book, transcribed differently and for comparison:

X: 2
T: Ripple Of The Teign
S: “Hardcore English” - Barry Callaghan
C: Bob Cann
M: 6/8
L: 1/8
R: jig
K: GMaj
|: B |\
dcd BAB | GFG D3 | DEF GAB | c2 B A3 |
cBc AGA | FEF D3 |[1 DEF GAB | e2 d B3 :|
[2 ded cBA | G3- G2 ||
|: E |\
DCD GFG | BAB d3 | ded cBA |
[1 dcB c3 | FEF AGA | cBc e3 | fed cBA | edc B2 :|
[2 gfg e3 | fed cBA | edc BAG | cBA GFE | DEF G2 |]

Notes on this tune: “Composed by Bob Cann: given here in G, bugt it was Bob’s practice to alternate between G and D when playing it. Bob did not write this B music until some considerable time after the A, and in the meanwhile other B musics were written for it.” - Barry Callaghan

More on “Hardcore English” can be found here,
Including on the two accompanying CD recordings ~
https://thesession.org/recordings/3021

The complete set, book and two CDs, currently available direct from the EFDSS for £20, see the ‘comments’ for the CDs. A lot of heart and thought went into this collection…

Miss Caldwall’s Favorite

This lovely Scottish jig is to be found in John Pringle’s “Collection of Reels Strathspeys & Jigs” (1801); the indication is “Slowish”.
I do like it a lot because of its melody and harmony. I play this tune in D major – A and B parts several times and C part only once – to finish off a set (played at +/- 95 BMP) that I start with “Miss Erskine Wade”.
I wish you to enjoy this as much as I do.

Three comments

Kate Moloney’s

This reel is composed by Josephine Marsh for her friend and former student, accordeon player Kate Moloney from Quin, Co. Clare. I heard Josephine playing it over the years but notices that it hadn’t been posted yet. Josephine describes the tune as being in C, so that’s how I put it down here. But I suppose it could be written down as a Dmix tune as well.

Lady Down

From the Athole Collection. Also appears in the bass player for Niel Gow, Malcolm MacDonald’s collections (1788-1797) and the Skye (1887).

This tune is fairly simple but cool. The B part is basically the A part played up an octave, but there is something about it that sticks with you. Really tearing into the second part can accentuate the positive. I’ve shown how you might bow a few phrases to help give it some kick.

Here’s the tune without bowings and a variation on the first phrase of the the B part:

G|c2 cG ECCE|FEDC B,G,G,E|c2 cG ECCE|FDGE C/C/C C:|
e|gagf ecce|fdec BGGB|gagf ecce|fdge c/c/c ce|
gagf ecce|fedc BGGB|cGAF EFGf|ecde c/c/c c|]

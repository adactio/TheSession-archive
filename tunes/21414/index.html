Mary’s Roughty Refuge

Composed by Jackie Daly. Learned this fantastic polka from an ITMA recording of Jackie Daly and Mike Cranitch. Sounds to me that the A part is in Em and the B part is in G but seeing as their both the same scale theres plenty of room for backing interpretation.

Re: Reel 2 ¿?

This is a version of “The Red Fox”, a Robert Mathieson GHB tune, normally written in A. It’s classed as a hornpipe which means for everyone else it’s played like a reel. We play this at our local session in the Scottish Borders. There should, I think, be some variations second time through the B part.

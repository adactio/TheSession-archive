Re: Valse Des Bois

Played here by the composer (Richard Forest), along with André Brunet & Eric Beaudry.
https://youtu.be/rTQ-DFfGJrs

I’ve liked Richard’s tunes since I first heard him a few years back!
He’s a self-taught Fiddler & Composer - has played with the Réveillons (among other bands) for quite a few years & recorded a few albums with them. There’s a few other tunes of his on The Session, but also more over at the Traditional Tune Archive - https://tunearch.org/w/index.php?search=Richard+Forest&title=Special%3ASearch&wprov=acrw1_-1&ns0=1&ns844=1

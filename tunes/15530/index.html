Peter Flanagan’s

Similar to Down the Broom (https://thesession.org/tunes/514) but different enough for Alan Ng to identify separately at https://www.irishtune.info/tune/1572/. My transcription doesn’t really do justice to the source recording, but does sketch the tune shape as recorded by Gary Connolly, James Carty, Reg Hall & Francis Gaffney on the album It was great altogether (disc 3) (The voice of the people). - London: Topic, 2015. (https://thesession.org/recordings/5383)

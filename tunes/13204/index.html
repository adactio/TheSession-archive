Re: Liam O’Flynn’s

PLEASE NOTE: this tune has been wrongly accredited to Liam O‘Flynn (common convention when giving a tune “from the playing of…” is to call it their “favourite”, naming the tune directly after the player implies that they wrote it). This is a Tommy Peoples’ composition and is already listed:

https://thesession.org/tunes/1100

Not sure what the need is for this tune to be on twice, or for unnecessary “variations” to be appended, as this implies they are not legitimate “versions” in their own right.

One comment

Patricia’s

Here’s a beautiful continental-style waltz composed by the wonderful Paddy Hayes. Paddy was originally from Piercestown just outside of Wexford town, spent many years in London before retirement to Co. Leitrim where he made two fine accordion albums. This waltz was composed for his daughter’s wedding and featured on his first album, Return to Kilty. Huge thanks to Nóra Byrne Kavanagh who notated this and other waltzes and slow pieces for me as the Wexford Tune Project collection nears completion.

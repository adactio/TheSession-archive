Crosskeys

A jig composed by Stevie Dunne from his album Live at Crosskeys.
It’s fairly easy to play on tenor banjo and has a nice groove.
The grace notes in the B part are to be played as slides up the neck
on banjo or fiddle (and somehow on other instruments).
Here is a video of Stevie playing it:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nErMi1w0qA
Note that Stevie plays with a capo on the first fret, so , in that video ,
it sounds up a semitone from what I posted.

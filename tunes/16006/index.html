Re: Mogh Roith

Well, this is a bit of an odd one. I was playing around on the whistle, and then I accidentally played the first bar of this tune. I played it again and again, sure that I had heard it somewhere before. I couldn’t remember anything about this ghost tune except what it sounded like, and soon, memory failed there too, so most of the second part is made up. I hope someone who knows more than me can recognize it!
All the best!

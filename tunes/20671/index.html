The Reign O‘Trump Is O’er, X:2

Since this is your tune, and not traditional (albeit in the style of), I’d like to offer this setting which seems to convey my feelings on the interminable past 4 or 5 years of misery that much, or most of the population has had to endure.
We are still shackled with the corruption and misdeeds of trump, so therefore I offer this to convey some of the despair/dread/disappointment we continue to experience.

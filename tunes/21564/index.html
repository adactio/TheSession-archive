Three comments

Eveleen’s Bower

No. 642 in O’Neill’s 1850. He credits it to Carolan, but apparently that is considered dubious by Carolan scholar O’Sullivan.

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 13th, 2022.

Eveleen’s Bower, X:2

Taking it up to D major to make it more whistle-friendly. This has got a surprisingly small range and so can be played on instruments that span just an octave

↳ Posted by NylonFlute on February 13th, 2022.

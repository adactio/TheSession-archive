Re: Transformed

Thanks for taking the time to put this together! I believe that, in the A section, the rhythm in the second half of each measure is incorrect. This changes to the first half of each measure in the B and C sections, but flips back in the last line. The way that it is written would make the time signature 7/8. A sort-of informed guess would be that some of the 4-note groups of 8th notes should have two 16th notes instead, so a group of two 8ths and two 16ths to form a total of three beats instead of four. In the measures containing two quarter notes, one of the two should be an 8th note. These corrections would put it back to 6/8 time. I hope this is helpful, and do appreciate that you put the time and effort into making this.

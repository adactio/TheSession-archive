Re: Ales Engelska

I don’t know the background of this specific tune but, in general, an Engelska is a tune from Sweden derived from an English tune (‘Engelska’ simply means ‘English’. You sometimes see them with ‘Anglais’ in the title). They seem to occur in the traditions of the south-western part of the country - which would make sense I suppose, as it would be traders and fishermen from that area that would have had most contact with their English opposite numbers and could have picked up some of their tunes. I’m not aware of a particular dance form associated with them, though I’m sure others would have more information about this.

I wonder if the ‘Ale’ of the title here might refer to Ale Möller - eminent multi-instrumental maestro of the Swedish folk world. Wouldn’t surprise me if Dreamer’s Circus had the occasional tune from him.

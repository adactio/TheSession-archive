Stephen’s

From the formidable concertina player Jack Talty’s debut solo album, released November 11, 2016 on Raelach Records. https://raelachrecords.bandcamp.com/album/in-flow
Make sure to listen to his recording to get the timing and feel of the tune! All eigth-notes are “swung”/“triplet-feel”…

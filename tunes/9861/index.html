Two comments

Mick McGarry’s

This is my first ABC tune that I have learnt by ear and tried to transpose to ABC…it is taken from a recording by Australian group ‘Trouble In The Kitchen’ the first part of the tune on track 12 of their album “The Next Turn” - a brilliant CD and must have collection for any Irish music lover.

To quote Trouble In The Kitchen’s cd notes this tune “comes from Mick McGarry, a fiddle player from Backwater, in the New England district of NSW”. This is a wonderful tune.

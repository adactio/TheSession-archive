Le Batteux

Le Batteux is a French Canadian tune made for dance at the end of the summer, during the harvest. A "Batteux‘’ is a Quebecois word for thresher. This tune is usually played on a fiddle tuned in AEae. Louis Boudreault and La Bottine Souriante made good versions of this tune.

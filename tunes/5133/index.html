“The Novelty” by F. A. Morrison

McKillop lifted this tune from his idol Sean McGuire, who recorded it on the LP “Sean Maguire Plays.” McGuire probably got the tune from Ryan’s Mammoth Collection (1000 Fiddle Tunes) but he mixed up the name with that of another tune in the book. There is an “Eclipse” hornpipe in Ryan’s, but this tune is really “The Novelty,” a Lancashire clog composed by F.A. Morrison, who also is credited as the composer of “The Avalanche” clog in Ryan’s. Just to confuse things further, there is an entirely different Eclipse hornpipe in Kerr’s Merry Melodies.

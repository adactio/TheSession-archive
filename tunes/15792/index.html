Re: Colclough’s

This tune was originally published in one of the first tutor books for the Uilleann pipes, written by Dudley Colclough and published in the early 1800’s. Although it doesn’t say so in the printed tutor, this tune is probably a composition of Dudley Colcough. The Colclough’s were a family of benevolent landlords in County Wexford in the 1700s/1800s. There were many ‘gentleman’ pipers in Wexford in that era including Larry Grogan of Johnstown Castle who also composed some tunes still played today. A number of years ago, to mark the reopening of the wonderful beautifully restored walled garden of Tintern Abbey in south Wexford (The ancestral pile of the Colcloughs) , local piper Ned Wall I believe played this tune on the occasion.

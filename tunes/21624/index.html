Bleaton Gardens

Another tune I transcribed many years ago from the playing of Ron Gonnella. I remember discussing the tune with fiddler Duncan Wood, who later recorded it with Cathall McConnell. Duncan had actually met Ron Gonnella, and he told me some stories. There are some slight differences in his settings, so I might post it.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on February 21st, 2022.

Two comments

DGaF

This tune was written by Shannon Heaton, and is part of the first track on their album Whirring Wings. Shannon has a companion book for the album (https://shannonheatonmusic.com/product/cd-and-companion-book-mail-order/) which has her transcription of it, along with the stories about the tunes, including how this one got named (other than the fact that the first 4 notes of both parts are dGAF, but many people use it as an acronym that means something else… ;-))

You can also check out the whole set from Matt & Shannon Heaton here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uVDxAts-e24

Three comments

Info

This is yet another great tune by Liz Carroll. Seems like that’s all I’m posting lately. It’s from her CD with John Doyle “In Play.” The liner notes say, “Liz’s home among the cornfields of Illinois.” The B part really stands out on this one.
Enjoy.

Fremont Center, X:2

As taught by Catriona Macdonald about five years ago.
Some differences at the end of the A part and in the repetitive figure (without syncopation) in the B part.
The tune works well at a slightly gentler pace with a bit of swing.

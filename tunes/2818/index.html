Ward’s Bow

I’m afraid this is a composition of mine… It was written in honour of a friend of mine, Mike Ward, who is a good fiddler who has given me lots of tips and tricks and who remains an inspiration.

He plays with a very strong lilt to his tunes, not disimilar to Kevin Burke, but with a heavier bow action. This tune was written with this in mind, I hope you enjoy it.

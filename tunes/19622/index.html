Three comments

Cnoc na bPoll

Cnoc na bPoll. This is from Ceol Rince na hÉireann II. 253. It is similar (but not identical) to the version that Paudie O‘Connor and John O’Brien play on the whistles on Wind and Reeds. Except they play it in Bdor (Cdor using sharp whistles I guess). This setting (plus decoration) was collected from the great Denis Murphy.

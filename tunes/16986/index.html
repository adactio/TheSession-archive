Two comments

Teddy’s Polka

The second in what will become a series of tunes named after my grandson, Teddy (check out Teddy’s Delight).

I love the Gmajor jolliness to Gminor darkness switch in the B part, and then the lift back to G major when the A part comes round again.

Enjoy….

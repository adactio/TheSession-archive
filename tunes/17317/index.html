Añada Pa Gael

Beautiful tune written by Asturian piper José Manuel Tejedor for baby Gael.

Añada, in Asturian, means lullaby, so it’s a very gentle and slow tune.

Here’s a video of Tejedor and Duncan Chisholm many moons ago.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LmI7U8d95Bo

The last line comes from a version they played at the Gordon Duncan Memorial concert in 2007.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AypzlIu-yqk

Re: Añada Pa Gael

Lovely tune: I play it in G - less squeaky on the higher notes than in D!
First heard Tejedor as background music to our breakfast in a hotel in Asturias in the early 2000s - not your usual breakfast music at all: went to ask at reception what it was: had to wait another few years before they appeared at Glasgow’s Celtic Connections before I managed to get one of their CDs.
Btw Asturian gaitas usually use a C chanter, so C and F are favourite keys.

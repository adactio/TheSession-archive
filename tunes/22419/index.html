The Wexford Minstrel

This reel was composed by the very prolific tune composer John Noctor. Born in Dublin but of Wexford parents, John has composed many nice tunes, many with titles connecting them to his parent’s (and his wife’s) home county. This little reel has echoes of some other tunes such as the second part of ‘Tom Moylan’s ’, or parts of the reel, ‘I have no money’. However I think it is a nice catchy little tune as composed. Given it’s Wexford title and the family connections, it merits inclusion in The Wexford tunes database that is held in ‘My Tunebook’.

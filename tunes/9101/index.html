A future discussion! ~ Sadly ~ :-( ~ we have NO Galician music amongst our recordings, aside from what is in our heads, hearts and feet from experiencing the music and dance live. Well, no Galician music by Galicians. We have it by others, including several Irish takes on it.

We used to perform with a group that did exchanges, and through that we fell in love with it. That includes some great session exchanges, gaita and tambourine, lovely and spirited and full of smiles and laughter. This tune seemed to be an old standard, and it surprised me it wasn’t already here. It was something you recently contributed to, Swiss, that dragged this out, after also visiting the above linked to resource and then doing a search for any recordings before submitting these takes on it… It makes me smile and want to dance…

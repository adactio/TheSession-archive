Vals Efter Lasse i Lyby

This is an old Swedish waltz, written by Lasse i Lyby, and appears on the Nordic Fiddlers Bloc CD.The abc nomenclature was found on a Swedish website, shared by Patrick Mödsson in 2008, and he wrote that the tune was composed in Sköna, Sweden in 1373. The transcription was in D minor, so I changed it to A minor, as played by Nordic Fiddlers Bloc.

↳ Posted by Fiddlebuck on May 17th, 2015.

Re: The Nightingale

I learned this tune from Ryan’s a long time ago, and it’s always been a favorite.

Ryan’s has a section of hornpipes (straight up 4/4) and clogs (dot and flag), this is a clog. Strictly speaking.

The melody is much like this little number from the Music Man: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tyNnbFx6T64

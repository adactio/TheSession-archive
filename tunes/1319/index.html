Don’t be bashful, Kenn. This is good. It has a slight melancholy feel to it despite being in a major key maybe due to the unusual minor 6th interval in the first part. The last part is in really in D major. I like the title also!

↳ Posted by milesnagopaleen on January 12th, 2003.

Wrote this tune following a meeting and outdoor session with David Wilkie and Alberta band “Cowboy Celtic”, in Princes street Gardens, Edinburgh. David and a CBC cameraman were filming for a possible documentary on the celtic roots of music in the Wild West.

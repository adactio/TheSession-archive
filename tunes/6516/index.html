Three comments

Storm Cloud

A new jig (October/November 2006) which seems to be the product of mixing a pentantonic blues scale and a phrase from ‘Banish Misfortune’ on an octave mandola (where the fingering just seems to suggest itself).
It has a certain ‘rock’ quality to it. I wasn’t entirely sure about the key signature, especially with mixing of B flats and B naturals in the second half - but I’ve stopped worrying about it.

A G drone (or G5 drone) seems to go quite well in the first half with maybe Gmajors and Fs added to the second

Dedicated to my partner Dawn.

↳ Posted by Edgar Bolton on December 17th, 2006.

Re: Storm Cloud

I’ve updated the ABC for the D version. The site had picked it up as being in D mixolydian and was displaying some of the notes wrong. The key shifts from D dorian to D mixolydian into the scond part but we cannot add K: statements into the ABC on the site. So I have edited the ABC to make it D dorian then put explicit sharos in the second part.

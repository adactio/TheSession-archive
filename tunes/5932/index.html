Some Notes…

St Michael’s Mount was written by Andy Cutting and appears on a number of his CDs including “One Roof Under” with Karen Tweed. There is some good ornamentation on the recording that, for simplicity, I did not add here. Take a listen to the CD for a better idea.

The 2nd time bar at the end of the 2nd phase is intended as a link back to the beginning of the tune.

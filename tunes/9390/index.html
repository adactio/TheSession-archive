Six comments

This is a lovely 3-part reel composed by Eamon Flynn, from Mountcollins Co. Limerick, and recently recorded by Billy McComiskey on his wonderful new (2008) CD “Outside the Box.” I’m more of a fiddle player and only a recent convert to the art of the B/C, so I was interested in expanding my horizons and learning how to manage the section of the 2nd part built around the Fmaj chord, which turned out to be surprisingly easy to play.

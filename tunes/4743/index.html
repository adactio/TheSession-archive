Three comments

Letterkenny Blacksmith

Composed by Ed Reavy. Recorded by “Fisherstreet”, Tony McManus and Ed himself on the LP of his compositions on the “Rounder” label.

↳ Posted by Kenny on July 17th, 2005.

Great tune

This is a great tune that I wish was better known, hopefully it will be picked up by more people now. I have my own little variation in the second half which I played to Tony MacManus once, he seemed to like it. It’s just something I thought I heard in the autoharp harmonies of his recording but he hadn’t heard it himself! I don’t know how to read or write ABC so unfortunately I can’t put it in here :-( I do plan on recording the tune on my first album if I ever get around to making it though!

↳ Posted by Dave Flynn on July 19th, 2005.

Re: The Letterkenny Blacksmith

The c# in part 2 gives an unusual and ambigious modal turn to the melody.
It can be heard as “G maj. followed by A maj.” -as would be the intention in Victorian and Georgian parlour / Classical music-
…but also as a lydian modulation (G ion. => G lyd.) , which is quasi inexistant in ITM.

In Quebec, however, a significant number of (Scottish inspired) reels use this sort of trick with the c#.

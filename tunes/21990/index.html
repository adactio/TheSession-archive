Enniscorthy

Unusual jig from John Whelan. John, London born but living in the U.S. for many years has composed a considerable amount of lovely and beautiful tunes. His father Denis, from Blackwater in County Wexford, was a proud Wexfordman and John was a regular visitor back to sessions in County Wexford through the 1970s and for years afterwards. I first heard him, playing in the famous ‘Wavecrest’ bar on Wexford quays, not long after he had recorded his first record for Outlet. I remember thinking at that time (he was a couple of years older than me) how incredible it was to have a fantastic album of traditional music made at such a young age…who’d have thought that there would be numerous albums after and a career as a professional musician in store for John. I’m including his Wexford-titled compositions in the ‘Wexford tune project’ that I’m working on.

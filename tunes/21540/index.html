Farewell To The Catskills

I heard this improbably beautiful waltz that Joannie Madden has composed. I searched for the sheet music here, but NO, the only thing that was here was the title of the page with their CD ‘Heart of the Home’ which had been added by ‘Connor Hickey’ two years ago. Then I started listening to it and writing it down - and it was certainly a heavy job for me, it took a lot of time, but now it’s here! Likewise the video with the Ladies <3

All The Way To Galway

Nothing whatsover to do with the other tunes called “All the Way to Galway” already posted to session.org.

This tune was popular with ceilidh bands in my area (Bristol UK) in the 1960s/1970s. Less commonly played now - although we still play it (but not at sessions).

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on April 17th, 2013.

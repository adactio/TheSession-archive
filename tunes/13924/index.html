Merry Nicht Under The Tummel Brig

The earliest printing of this tune that I can find is in Daniel McLaren’s Collection of 1794, where it’s called “Mr Lindsay’s Reel”. McLaren was a Perthshire fiddler and composer, “Mr Linday’s Reel” being one of his own. John Glen reprinted it as “Mr Lindsay” in his Collections (1891/95), but it had appeared in Ryan’s Mammoth Collection (1883) as “Merry Night at Tumble Brig”. That name (actually “The Mirry Night at Tumble Bridge”) is attached to an earlier, different reel in Malcolm McDonald’s 1st Collection, and I wonder if the names have either been mixed up or recycled (this happens a few times in Ryan’s).

I may be wrong, but I’ve always thought that the subject of the title is Tummel Bridge at the West end of Loch Tummel, Perthshire, built by General Wade in 1730.

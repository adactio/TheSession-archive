Brenda’s Favorite

This lovely reel is composed by Séan Nugent and was the leader of ‘The Pride of Erin Céilí Band’
The video clip below is not the reel Brenda’s Favourite.

https://www.rte.ie/archives/2020/0424/1134663-the-pride-of-erin-ceili-band/

The Pride of Erin Céilí Band perform a selection of jigs on ‘The Mountain Lark’.
Acclaimed in both céilí and traditional music circles, the Pride of Erin Céilí Band from Fermanagh are led by renowned fiddler Seán Nugent. The winners of many awards in Ulster, they also competed at All-Ireland level, winning that title at Fleadhanna Ceoil in 1983 and 1985.
This episode of ‘The Mountain Lark’ was broadcast on 13 May 1985. The presenter is Seosaimhín Ní Bheaglaioch.

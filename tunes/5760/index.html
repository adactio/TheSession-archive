Spailpin Fanach

This is a really beautiful setdance danced in Irish dancing. It also goes by the name of “The Whistling Gypsy”, and is often played with a swung, dotted feel. Hope you like it!

Re: Spailpin Fanach

When learning this tune a few months back, I went looking for recordings on the streaming services. I found only versions of it with lyrics and the big dramatic pause before the final couplet is delivered in the B part - with the exception of the recording that made it onto Nathan Gourley and Laura Feddersen’s album Brightly or Darkly, which is thus far the only instrumental/hornpipe version I’ve encountered.

Undeterred, I launched into it at a session last night and very quickly found I was greeted with a wall of unrecognizing faces - so it was twice through and then onto another tune. Hopefully next time I’ll have better luck.

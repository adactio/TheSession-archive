Re: Reel Around The Sun

This tune has long been one of my favorites on fiddle. It’s technically from Riverdance, of course, and is called “The Chronos Reel”, part of the live show’s opening set which is called ‘Reel Around the Sun’.

The number/track is arranged as:
Corona Air (low whistle slow air solo)
The Chronos Reel (reel)
Reel Around the Sun (faster reel, male lead dancer’s entrance)

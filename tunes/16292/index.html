Josie’s Tune

This is an “air” that could easily have come from one of Davy Spillane’s solo albums . . . but it doesn’t. It is a tune written by guitarist Chris Rea for his album “Dancing With Strangers,” and while Davy contributes pipes and low whistle to several other tracks, this is an almost solo piece for low whistle. Interestingly, this is not an unusual pattern and Davy plays solo tracks on a number of other artists albums that he contributes to. You can listen to Josie’s Tune on YouTube.

About Shetland Pony

Written by Dallas fiddler Michelle Feldman. I first learned this in 2004 in the previous band Michelle and I played in together (Gallus). We’ve shared it in local sessions, and our new band finally got around to recording it.

We always play it as a reel, although it can be slowed down and played in hornpipe style as well.

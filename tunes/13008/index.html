One comment

Lucania

A very jolly J Scott Skinner tune, named after a steam liner of the late 19th century, and dedicated to its Captain. The title page of the original sheet music may be seen here: http://www.abdn.ac.uk/scottskinner/display.php?ID=JSS0715 JSS recorded it in 1910, a year after the ship caught fire while in dock, and was subsequently scrapped. The composer’s recording may be heard on the Strathspey King album; Alasdair Frazier fancies that perhaps JSS was a passenger on his first voyage to America in 1893, the same year the ship made its maiden voyage.

Again I’ve thrown in D.S., segno, and Coda markings; these come from the transcription in Mark Weeg’s very fun little book “The Violinist’s Guide to Fiddling,” which is chock full of entertaining fingerbusters of all sorts.

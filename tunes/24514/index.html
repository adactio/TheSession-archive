Four comments

Bainis

A hornpipe composed by Josephine Marsh for Clare banjo player Kevin Griffin’s wedding.
It’s from her first solo CD. You can find it in Josephine’s new book
111 Original Irish Music Compositions, where there are many more great hornpipes
(as well as jigs, reels, planxties, etc.).

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on July 20th, 2024.

Bainis, X:2

On her record, I Can Hear You Smiling, Josephine has a hornpipe called Springtime
but it is just Bainis transposed up to Cmajor (with only very minor changes).
I wonder if she recomposed the tune in C without realizing
that it was the same as Bainis.
Both tunes appear in her book of compositions and the notes say nothing about
them being the same tune.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on July 20th, 2024.

Re: Bainis

Josephine emailed me that the hornpipe Springtime is a completely different tune and the notes for it in the book are an error! I was the first to bring it to her attention. It’s a bummer because now she probably has to include a loose sheet with the correct notes with each copy of the book.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on July 21st, 2024.

Re: Bainis

Jo has sent correct notes for Springtime to everyone who ordered her book. And she has updated the sound files that accompany the book. I hadn’t even known about those! Her friends consoled her with the observation that 110 out of 111 tunes in the book were correct.

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on July 22nd, 2024.

Autumn Cobblestones

I transcribed this from Cullen Vance’s album Faol: https://thesession.org/recordings/8126

I’m unsure if it was written by him, but I couldn’t find the tune: https://thesession.tradtuneexplorer.com/melody-fragment-search.html?abc=EAc+e2c+%7C+B2c+B2A+%7C+EAc+e2g+%7C+fgf+e3&rhythm_mode=soft&type=jig&meter=6%2F8&limit=50

He speeds up quite a bit throughout the track--but in a musical, interesting way, not sloppily or by accident. There are numerous flourishes and ornaments, the 1/16th notes shown being only the most obvious and consistent of them.

I would advise listening to the track if at all possible; it was impossible to transcribe every grace note and slide, and doing so would have meant a lot of clutter anyway for those just wanting the core tune--though I imagine that’s true for most tunes.

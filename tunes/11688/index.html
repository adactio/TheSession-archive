High Bill

This was composed by the Derry whistle player Padraig O‘Mianain (Paddy O’Meenan) for President Bill Clinton and the positive roll he played in the peace process in Northern Ireland.Paddy is a teacher and musician of many years, so I will say that this ABC is very similar to what he plays the first time through.

My instrument is Fiddle and I think it translates well enough from whistle. The pick up notes around the repeats are the only place I think it gets a bit sketchy, but you can always adjust accordingly.

I learned this from a recording I made at a weekly session I attended while studying abroad at the University of Ulster in Derry. The pub is a place called Tinney’s and the session is from 9:30ish-12:00am every Tuesday, if you ever find yourself rambling through that part of Ireland.

-Dan

Maurice O’Connor’s

This tune “Maurice O’Connor’s Hornpipe” was submitted by the fiddler Alex Sutherland (1874-1969) of Drumreilly, Co Leitrim to the National Folklore Commission in 1957. I have not been able to match it thus far. The first part resembles the first part of “Soldier’s Joy” while the second part resembles the first part of “The Duke’s Retreat”

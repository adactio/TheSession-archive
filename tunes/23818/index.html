Four comments

The Sunny Side Of The Latch

This pretty jig composed by Jason O’Rourke is from his latest album, also called The Sunny Side of the Latch. In the sleeve notes for the album he tells the story behind the title but I can’t fully remember the details (don’t have it to hand today) and prefer not to miss-tell the story here. Buy the album and find out for yourself!

It’s a lovely album with lots of great compositions on it, all very well played.

My transcription aims to give a feel of how Jason plays it, without going into too much detail.

Looking forward to playing this in a session myself one of these days.

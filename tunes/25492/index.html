Re: Smoke On The Air

Composed b y Sandy Brechin (accordion) and Gavin Marwick (fiddle). It’s a companion piece to Brechin’s “Hangin’ Out the Windows” which was about how to smoke a cigarette when you live in a non-smoking appartment. As Gavin Marwick also employed this method, they collaborated on this march “…watching our smoke drift over the Edinburgh rooftops on that fine summer’s day.”

Re: Wassalou River

6/4!!! Wow!! I love this tune!! First time I see a tune in that time signature, very nice!

I was just looking for something in that rhythm the other day. And yes, I can see now that the feeling of this rhythm has nothing to do with a reel, therefore I don’t agree much with the music scores that the French Canadians do with the reels that have irregular metrics when they use single bars in 6/4. They do that just to leave in there the leftovers that don’t fit in the 4/4 bars. They could just use 2/4 in those cases and that would also fit with the foot tapping they always do.

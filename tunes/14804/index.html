Mykines Valsurin

A waltz for Mykines, Føroyar (Myggenæs, Færøerne if you’re Danish) written by Faroese violinist Angelika Neilsen. “Mykines Valsurin” just means “The Mykines Waltz” in English. Pronounce it “MEETCH-en-ehs”.

9. Mykines Waltz (Máire Breatnach) — Rock Pools CD

Written by Máire Breatnach

Mykines is the most Westerly of the Faroe Islands, a bird paradise halfway between Scotland and Iceland. Faroese fiddle player Angelika Nielsen joins for an Irish / Faroese fiddle duet – with strong guitar support from Scotland by Ian.
Feat. Angelika Nielsen fiddle; Ian Melrose g.

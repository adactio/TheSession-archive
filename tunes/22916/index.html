(From Wikipedia) The “Uist Tramping Song”, “Come Along”, “Null do dh’Uidhist” (Over to Uist) or “Tiugainn Leam” (Come With Me) is a traditional Scottish folk song, Gaelic lyrics by Archibald MacDonald, music by John R. Bannerman (1939), arranged and English words by Hugh S. Roberton.

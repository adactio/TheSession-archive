Rosaleen’s Children

From the playing of, and composed by, Mick Kinsella.

Transposed from Fdor.

Based on the liner notes of his album Harmonica, Rosaleen’s Childreen seems to refer to the children of Rosaleen and Geoffrey (Geoff) Perrin.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on April 12th, 2020.

Re: The Laird Of St. Johnston

If it had been Scottish, I might have thought of it as being a strathspey, especially with the quadruplets (although they’re played more as a triplet on the first two notes of a triplet).
However, being Irish, I thought maybe March was more appropriate, and Jeremy has altered the beaming of the notes to make it look like a 2/2 march (like our 2/4s, except with all the notes twice as long).

The Laird Of St. Johnston, X:2

This is the Cathal Ó Curráin setting, which has a few minor differences from Caroline Regan’s.
In particular, the snap in the first bar is more obvious and less like a grace note. I’ve added that snap to Caroline’s setting as well.
The run back to the first part is also played as (3BAG, as well as (3GFE - they are played simultaneously.

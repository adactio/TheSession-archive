Y Cofi

Found in ‘Tro Llaw’ 200 Welsh Hornpipes collated by R.H.Bowen. I’ve changed a couple of note just to make it play better. Much better played swung than played straight, really can dig in, on the fiddle.
Pronounced
Uh! Co.- vee
Translates as The Coker’s (Hornpipe)

Two comments

The Athens Taxi Ride

From our recent trip to Athens, we were met at the Ferry terminal by a Greek taxi driver who asked me, “You like Greek Music” I said I loved it so it was on with his DVD player, playing very loud Greek music all the way to our Motel, at a very fast rate of speed. So this tune is to be played with great energy and speed.

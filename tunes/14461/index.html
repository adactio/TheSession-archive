Three comments

Hop, Skip, Jump!

This is the third slide from a set called “Hop, Skip, Jump!” on Máire Breatnach’s album “Angels Candles”.

The A part sounds like “I Saw Three Ships Come Sailing In”.

Máire Breatnach says they’re just called “Hop”, “Skip” & “Jump”.
But I was wondering if any of you would have other (traditional?) names for them…

You can listen to the whole set here:

http://chomikuj.pl/rakuyou/muzyka/celtic*2c+folk/Maire+Breatnach/%281999%29+Angels*27+Candles/Maire+Breatnach+-+14+-+Hop*2c+Skip*2c+Jump!,1689708670.mp3%28audio%29

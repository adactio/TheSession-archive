Three comments

Scottisch 1

Music for eight bells from the Colnago tradition, taken from a manuscript of Giuseppe “Piles” Pewrani (1933-1988), bells player in Colnago.
Colnbago is in north Italy, not far from Milano.

Thanks to Walter Biella , music researcher and “baghèt” maker
( the baghèt is the the bagpipe of the Bergamo valleys ).

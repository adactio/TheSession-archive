Re: Fante Vals

‘Fante’ is the Norwegian word for a ‘traveler’, and ‘vals’ is of course ‘waltz’. In Scandinavia, only the first letter and proper nouns are capitalized in titles, so it should be ‘Fante vals’.

I recognize this waltz from the dancing I’ve done both in the US and in Norway. I ‘hear’ it in my mind on a button box.

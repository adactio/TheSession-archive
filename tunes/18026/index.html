Three comments

The Boys In The Bunker

Newfoundland polka/single, composed by fiddler Colin Carrigan.

This is the basic melody.

Both Graham Wells and Fred Jorgenson of The Navigators play it in E minor, and given that Colin plays on The Navigators’ album, would assume this is the original key.

Fred plays it transposed up an octave from this setting, and throws in a bunch of variations. Some of the holding-notes variety, and some of the alternative note variety.

With that said, I prefer it in A minor, using a C-whistle. Sounds good in that key, and avoids the second-octave D-note (if playing the Emin transposed-up setting).

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on June 11th, 2019.

The Boys In The Bunker, X:2

A simple setting in A minor.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on June 11th, 2019.

The Boys In The Bunker, X:3 & X:4

Variations from Fred’s playing, transposed down an octave, and a few of my own thrown in.

A few others include:
- holding the first G-note in the 3rd bar of the A Part for the full bar
- hold the first G-note at the end of each part for half of the bar, and/or the whole bar
- holding the first A-note in the 2nd bar of the B Part for the full bar

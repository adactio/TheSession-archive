The Butchers Of Bristol, X:2

Re: William Irwin’s Modal or XYZ

The tune is in Gm in the 1850 William Irwin manuscript and there is an accidental in the second part. Changing it to Em (and perhaps also losing the awkward accidental) makes it into a nice, stable D/G melodeon tune - https://youtu.be/8cE2PgFLXjE - and John Spiers is a brilliant player - but it’s much more exciting on a fiddle in Gm and with accidental(s) - https://youtu.be/ECFTEWVfp3U. It is the nature of melodeons to resolve keys and be powerful and I love them. It is the nature of fiddles to do whatever wild thing a crazy player decides to do and I love them even more.

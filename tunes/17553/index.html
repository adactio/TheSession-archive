Two comments

Garey Ford

I found this tune on a recording from the band Barrule (look at recordings), but there was no link or anything to the transcription. This tune is (btw) awesome! You can play this one (if you want) very fast, and there is still the jig feeling.

↳ Posted by Ralf Brockhausen on December 19th, 2018.

Garey Ford, X:2

From Ímar’s 2018 album “Avalanche”. They play it in C#min. I’ve transposed to it Emin to make it a bit more whistle friendly. Tried Bmin too, but I like how it sounds in Emin better.

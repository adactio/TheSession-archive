Six comments

Dancing Sarah

This lovely 4 part reel was written by Charles “Wolf” Jevons, flute and whistle player from Toronto.
I heard it a few times in Toronto sessions, but finally learned it properly from Michael Ayles of Montreal. You see, Wolf is just too modest to play his own tunes unless strongly encouraged.

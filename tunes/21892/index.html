Martin With The Long Ears

From the Cherish the Ladies ‘At Home’ album. Siobhan Egan wrote it and can be heard playing it here on an archive recording made in Philadelphia at a Newly Composed Tunes Workshops from way back in 1989 (with a hornpipe of her own making too): https://library.villanova.edu/Find/Record/vudl:445862 (The tune is named for her family’s pet basset hound).

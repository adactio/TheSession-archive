“Jackson’s Dear Whiskey”

Looking into tunes ascribed to the 18th century Irish piper Walter Jackson I came across a few here:

“Handed Down: Country Fiddling and Dancing in East and Central Down” by Nigel Boullier
- a wonderful book which also includes transcriptions for many tunes…

I did some searches and while this one sounded familiar, and I found some similarities in other tunes, I didn’t manage to find an entry specific to this, so here it is, with a few simple variations from the book in repeats that came on my as I enjoyed playing it (A-part: bars 5 & 6 / B-part: bars 5-7).

Here also is where the dots for this are in the above printed source: Page 292: Jig - “Jackson’s Dear Whiskey"
The book’s source - handwritten music manuscripts penned by James Ward of Drumaknockan, Central County Down, 1865-1944…

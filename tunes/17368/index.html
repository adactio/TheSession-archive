Re: Reel Des Bananes D’une Autre Saveur

I know… I saw the mistake too late, and couldn’t change it (or I simply don’t know how) :D Obviously French isn’t my first language (or even my second). The traditional original is from Quebec and it’s called “Reel du Gâteaux aux bananes” (you’ll find it here http://montrealsession.mine.nu/lookup.php?id=92&tb=quebec.abc ) and it contained time changes that didn’t appeal to me so I rearranged it, and so it got a different “flavour”, hence the name.

Re: Reel Des Bananes D’une Autre Saveur

Hello. I thought I might be able to help here. This tune actually sounds like Dans les bras de la barouette, a composition of Michel Bordeleau. It starts at 2:17 on this video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aD-JY8wl4Ys Its a really great tune! Michel Bordeleau writes lots of great tunes, which is why the name here actually led me to this tune. He’s also written a great tune called Gateau de banane. Both of these tunes are recorded on Les Monocles, “L’Album Blanche.”

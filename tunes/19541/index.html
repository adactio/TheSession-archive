Two comments

Frank Cassidy’s

I could have sworn I saw this here previously, but I cannot find it for the life of me now. This is a great hornpipe from the playing of the Donegal fiddler Jimmy Lyons off the album “Tuning the Radio”. He also played it on “The Donegal Fiddle”, I believe in the key of A. O’Neill also has this as “Gillespie’s Hornpipe” according to Alan Ng.

↳ Posted by Daniel Parker on July 12th, 2020.

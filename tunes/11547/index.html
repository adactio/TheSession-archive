Use of & In The ABC Notation Posted

“&” is standard ABC notation that adds a second note or phrase underneath something else. I am using in in the B part to get the long high D that goes over the top. Sorry for any confusion. My ABC editor (ernie for MAC) reads the “&” notation fine. I play from music personally so I just use the ABC as a way of writing the music…

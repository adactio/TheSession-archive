Dunstanburgh Castle

From the playing of 422, on the album “Community Dances”.

Northumbrian tune. Composed by Willy Miller.

Stewart Hardy & Ali Say, The Hills of Lorne/Sherlock’s Hornpipe/Dunstanburgh Castle:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKYlEfdxvsM

The Geckoes, Jock Wilson of Fenton/Dunstanburgh Castle:

Dunstanburgh Castle, X:4

This ais a transcription of the way that the Cheviot Ranters played it. Very fast, as a rant rather than a hornpipe.
None of the other settings have an anacrusis which crucial for playing rants which need the off-beat drive.

↳ Posted by Noel Jackson on July 7th, 2024.

Dunstanburgh Castle, X:5

Feels more like a reel than a hornpipe to me, especially when played after Great North Run and Soldier’s Joy. Great tune either way.

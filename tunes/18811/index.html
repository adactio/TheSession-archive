Riptide

This driving reel in F is the last tune in a set I learned from Barbara McOwen.
Composed by Neil Davey, of the Orkney band Anam, this transcription is from the playing of Halali on their debut CD. They have a free mp3 of the tune to download from their website:
https://www.halalifiddle.com/sounds/riptide.mp3

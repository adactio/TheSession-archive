Owen’s

Hiya, good people.

This is a great wee tune which I’ve just “learned from the dots”. :-P

It doesn’t appear to be related to the other tune “Owen’s” which was previously submitted by Ceolachan some time ago. However, it is also Welsh.

I learned this while tackling some of the tunes in a Harp tutor book by Delyth Jenkins and it can also be found on her album “Aros”. She states that the tune is traditional although she has added her own arrangement for the harp.
However, I’ve just transcribed the basic melody line here which I am assuming was already in circulation and not the left hand part which would be Delyth’s own work. So, fingers crossed, that’s OK.

One comment

Kwallen

By Bert Aalbers.
Bert was a key figure in Dutch folk music since the mid 1960s. As composer and multi instrumentalist he played in groups like Crackerhash, Windkracht Acht and Foo Foo Band.
He must have written hundreds of tunes. It is said that he particulary loved to compose while lying in front of his tent or traveling by train. Many of his tune titles reflect Bert’s two main interests: traveling and maritime life: Kwallen actually means Jelly Fish.
Bert Aalbers died February 20 2007, only 63 years old…

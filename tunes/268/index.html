N: Leitrim fiddlers.

N: this in 9/8 would be wrong here, IMO.
N: You could perhaps write that as “𝅘𝅥𝅮𝅘𝅥𝅮 ≠ 𝅘𝅥𝅘𝅥𝅮”.

N: Top The Candle.

N: were tuned one whole tone down from the standard tuning. Here

N: fiddlers nowadays.

Re: Do It Fair

I firmly disagree that writing it in 9/8 would be wrong. It’s a typical hop jig, which are better written in 9/8 - I hear the feel in this recording (and in Brian McNamara’s) as having a triplet feel under each pair AND the ending of the B part definitely has triplets (not 16ths as you’ve written). Brian got them from this recording.

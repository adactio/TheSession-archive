Re: The Thornton

The second setting is not quite right and sounds odd to my ears.
On Mar a Tha Mo Chridhe, Julie Fowlis plays Es (and not F#s) in the descending triplets in the second and sixth bars of the second part, so aed and aec, rather than afd and afc.

Paddy The Bat, Mind Where You’re At

Roughly from the playing of Dorman Ralph.

Composed by Dorman Ralph (1923-1999). He plays the tune with a few more held notes than indicated here.

Dorman was a Newfoundland harmonica & accordion player, who became blind shortly after birth.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on January 3rd, 2021.

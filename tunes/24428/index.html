JD’s Rag

This is the bare bones of the tune as he repeats the parts with slightly different endings throughout the tune in both parts. There is also a break half way through for a jazz solo for pianno sax or clarinet. It is believed to be a John Doherty tune from late 60s -early 70’

JD’s Rag, X:3

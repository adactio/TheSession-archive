The Slopes Of Sliabh Luachra, X:2

This is obviously connected to the song. This 3 part setting is based on Connie O‘Connell’s playing https://thesession.org/recordings/1461. This setting is close to one on the County Bounds recording. It is usually played as a slide, see Johnny O’Leary’s setting for instance. I’d enter it as a slide but the song version has that spot, https://thesession.org/tunes/19223.

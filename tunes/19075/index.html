“Wedding Waltz”

Beautiful waltz composed by Newfoundland fiddler Emile Benoit. I transcribed it from the album by Canadian singer Suzie Leblanc & Co, La Mer Jolie (Chants d´Acadie). It originally appeared in Emile´s album, Vive La Rose.

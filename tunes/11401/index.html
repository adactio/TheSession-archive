Re: Paddy’s Perambulation

A beautifully mellow piping tune in G ionian with a great lift til it. The lift comes partly from the mode being ambiguously poised between G maj. and E min.
It is the type of atmospheric tune the dots can never do justice to and it will only come alive under the fingers of the player.
Eoin Dillon’s touch influenced the way this waltz is written here (X:1); the easy melodic swing have been included but not his contrasting playful cracking riplets (first down then up) in the 3rd part especially.

Some pipers will use the drones, some won’t for they could feel the ambiguous key (G/E) attracts a tonal rather than modal accompaniment.

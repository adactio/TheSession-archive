Re: Hounds On The Heath

Ali Hutton composed this as a reel in 6/4 time, and it took me some time to get the timing and ornamentation down. In the way I play it, it sounds like a reel, but with some characteristics of a jig.

Re: Hounds On The Heath

The ”three beat” feel is obvious. One could call it a”slip reel”, so 6/4 makes sense (or 3/2 for those ”reel = 2/2” people). Of course, it’ll look the same on the screen.

↳ Posted by jeff_lindqvist on April 11th, 2025.

Re: Hounds On The Heath

“6/4 makes sense”

…except, strictly speaking, 6/4 is two lots of 3/4 tagged together rather than three lots of 2/4.
6/4 is compound and 3/2 is not.
However, it seems that nowadays, in the trad world anyway and going against accepted theory, 6/4 is taken to mean 2/4 + 2/4 + 2/4.

I would much rather, though, that 3/2 and 6/4 were not interchangeable.

Re: Shootin’ The Bull

This tune is hexatonic.
The 3rd is “missing”; so it is arguably neither dorian nor mixolydian
or can be both
depending on how you hear it, how it is accompanied on strings (accordion, etc) or what passage note you use.

A good choice on the Scottish pipes.

Killy Burn Brae

In my quest for melodeon tunes I found this YouTube of Dermot Byrne
(who has a recording called Donegal Melodeon). In the notes it says
“Donegal box player Dermot Byrne, here on the melodeon with Danny Meehan’s …”,
but as the video plays a title comes up naming the first tune as Killy Burn Brae.
Dermot plays lots of variations but this is close to his first time through the tune.
(I have transcribed it for D melodeon. Dermot’s melodeon is in A, five semitones lower).
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EL1eBWJ5PG8

Re: O’Rahilly’s Grave

This air is based on the 3rd & 6th less minor mode.
Form: ABA‘ (aa’bb‘a’‘a’‘’).

A different slow air, “Liam Ó Raghallaigh”, with a similar feeling, possibly more beautiful still, can be heard on Mary Bergin’s 1979 album Feadóga Stáin-1, on track 5.

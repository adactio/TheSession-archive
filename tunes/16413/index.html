St. Gilbert’s

Found this tune in my head the last couple days and noticed it hadn’t been transcribed on this site. Seemed like a good excuse to learn it. The setting is based on the playing of Brendan Bulger, Marty Fahy, and Kathleen Bulger, recorded on their trio album “Music in the House”.

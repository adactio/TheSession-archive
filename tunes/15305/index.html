Three comments

Brisk Bob

Traditional scottish strathspey from the playing on Johnny Cunningham album “Thoughts From Another World” and live “Celtic Fiddle Festival”. A very little known tune. He made a very cool reel version of the tune on both recordings.

↳ Posted by Mr G. Cunningham on March 25th, 2016.

From Celtic Fiddle Festival’s album.

Brisk Bob, X:3

Reel version. From the same album, they go from strahspey to reel and then to the next tune, another strathspey.

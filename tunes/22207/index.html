Dans Loudieg #1

Dans Loudieg#1 is the first tune played on the set of Dans Loudieg on Kornog 1st Album

Dans Loudieg means Round of Loudéac.

We use to write it in 2/4 notation

The round of Loudéac (or round of Penthièvre) is a Breton dance originating from a large region around the country of Loudéac. It’s a set of 4 danses :
-Ronde
-Bal
-Ronde double (the B part of the tune add 4 mesures)
-Passepied

Kornog doesn’t play the Passepied

Ballybunion Beach Days

Composed by Máire O‘Tiarnaigh from Enniscorthy in County Wexford. It was published in ’The Wexford Tunebook’ recently. The book included compositions of Máire’s, along with her sister Aedín, and her father, fiddle player Colm. The tune was composed for her grandad’s 80th birthday, reminding of many happy days spent on the beach at Ballybunion.

Re: Maura McGlynn’s

This does indeed appear to be a two part version of the Tommy Peoples’ tune “Gráinne’s Jig” (as pointed out by Dr. Dow 6 years ago - thanks!) :-
https://thesession.org/tunes/488

The name “Maura McGlynn’s” (and pairing with #1) is attributed to the Billy McComiskey recording “Outside The Box” https://thesession.org/recordings/3071
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xGRM6QknQA

A discussion of the tune source can be found at The Traditional Tune Archive :-
https://tunearch.org/wiki/Annotation:Gráinne%27s_Jig

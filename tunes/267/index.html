Launching the Boat

Setting #2 comes from the flute and fiddle duet playing of P.J. Crotty and James Cullinan. It’s not very different from other settings except in the first two bars of the second part.

Here’s yet another interpretation of the tune by Dermy and Tara Diamond: http://source.pipers.ie/Media.aspx?mediaId=23570

↳ Posted by slainte on July 7th, 2013.

Launching The Boat, X:4

I heard this great reel when I was playing in sessions in Tigh Ruartaí in Glencolmcille this summer.

I asked fiddlers Stephen and Claire Gallagher if I could record the tune, and they graciously agreed. Here’s the setting more or less as they play it (somewhat simplified).

Claire played it in a set followed by Old Cuffe Street.

Stephen played it after The Scolding Wife.

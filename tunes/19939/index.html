Four comments

Three Men On A White Horse

From the playing of Don Messer.

Composed by Manitoba fiddler, Bill Delorme.

Don Messer:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p9q_PNVSkVw

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on October 31st, 2020.

Three Men On A White Horse, X:2

From the playing of Patti Kusturok.

She ends the tune with “c2e2 cBAB | c2e2 A2ef/g/ | a2(3fgf edcB | AFE2 A4” (in place of the last four bars of the C Part).

Patti Kusturok, starting at ~1 min 5s:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5jJqasXJddU

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on October 31st, 2020.

Three Men On A White Horse, X:3

From the playing of Jamie Snider.

He omitted the B Part, and wrote two additional parts.

Jamie Snider:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oCqBx1e1Aw0

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on November 5th, 2020.

Three Men On A White Horse, X:4

A few variations, mostly from the playing of Jamie Snider.

He learned the tune from a recording by Clarence Strongarm.

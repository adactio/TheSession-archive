Four comments

This is an old, VERY OLD, wedding-march I’ve learned by a friend of me, Paul Sveinall.
It’s a hardinger fiddle tune, but I think it works at regular fiddle, and since I think it’s a very good tune too, I submitted it here between lots of Irish music.
I hope someone’ll learn it, it’s a good tune!

Gard :)

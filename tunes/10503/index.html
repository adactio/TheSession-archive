Séamus Mór McKenna’s

I got this great reel from the new Teada recording “Ceol & Cuimhne / Music & Memory”. Wasn’t sure the best key to submit it in as they play it in C but thought in the end it would be more likely to be played if I submitted it in D. It’s a cracker!

Re: Over The Hills And Far Away

X:4 is indeed a different tune from X:1-3, and is based on the tune to the old British song ‘Over the Hills . . ’ which I understand has its origins in the C17th. and which I’ve only ever heard played/sung in 4/4 time. The verses that are currently well known were written relatively recently by John Tams.
See: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Over_the_Hills_and_Far_Away_(traditional_song).
Personally, I can’t see much merit in adapting the melody to a 6/8 rhythm but, you know . . . there’s probably no rule against it . . .

↳ Posted by Bazza on June 29th, 2024.

Five comments

“Gan Ainm” ~ but sure this one has a name…

I’m hoping that Hetty or Kenny or ‘Toxic Rag’ might know this one. I’d been holding back on it since it came to memory, but I haven’t yet found it at home or elsewhere. I was also sure it was already here on site, but after playing through all the barndances several times, while there are some that have a measure or two in common, I didn’t find this one. I hate putting in tunes without a name, and I’m sure I had one for this but I haven’t been able to remember it or find it in my notes. So, here it is after a long wait, and hopefully someone has a name or associations with it…

↳ Posted by ceolachan on July 29th, 2006.

G & D Major ~

Sometimes it was played this way ~ as if it were a four part tune, a kick taking the fifth, and the octave jumps between duh D - d’s… But, it is the same 32 bar tune in two keys, the second take given a bit more variation than the first. I particularly like the first take and that lower key of G…

Colin Burns

A nice Shetland reel which I learned as part of a set.

It has been recorded by Trevor Hunter and also Sandy Legget & the Carseloch Ceilidh Band among others. I’m not sure who composed the tune but I’ll update the setting when I find out.

Just seen that it is on the Dab Hand album “High Rock And Low Glen” which I actually have in the house although I don’t know where. :-)

Three comments

The Gifted Father T

A composition of Luton, England’s own Seven-Time All-Ireland Senior Button Accordion Champion John Whelan, from his CD Celtic Fire. Actually, in Bb Major, but that is not available as a selection; regardless of the key, this tune really swings and flows.
Please enjoy this selection from my dear friend, and musical inspiration, Mr. John Whelan.
In the future, I hope to bring more of John’s compositions to the masses and into the sessioneer’s repertoire.
Until the next ..

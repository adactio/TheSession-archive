Bouncing Bobby

Just in case anyone doesn’t know, this is a two-step, used for dances such as the Military/Brittannia/Boston Two Step. Other popular tunes for these dances include “Looking for a Partner” (https://thesession.org/tunes/6941), “The 6-20 Two Step”, “The Dancing Dustman” (https://thesession.org/tunes/7238), “The Ornithologist” and so on.

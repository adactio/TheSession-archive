Kajsa

In my neck of the woods, waltzes never get combined into “sets”. but this sounds good-- for example-- followed by Chris Romaine’s “Nancy’s Waltz” (1980). The waltz is the romantic time in a dance evening-- why not stretch things out and play two in a row? Many people come to dances as couples.

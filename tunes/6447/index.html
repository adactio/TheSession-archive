A common session tune - the Norbeck version (not the one on this site) is the more common, in my experience. The “Paddy in London” listed on this site is basically a version of *this* tune, ie The Four Courts. No question, the two tunes are related, but I think they’re sufficiently different to be classed as different tunes.

Here’s the link - look for “Paddy in London”:

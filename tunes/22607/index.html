The Shore Road

Another tune by Scottish flautist Geoffrey Kelly; first recorded on his solo album as the second tune in his Galloway Set, and later by The Paperboys under the title String of Horses. “The Shore Road” is a reference to a landmark in his hometown of Creetown, Scotland.

I’ve written the tune out in straight eighths, but ideally it should be played swung.

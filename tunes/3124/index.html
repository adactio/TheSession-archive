Ten comments

in response to recent requests for a)flatpicking tunes and b)original tunes, here’s a thing I play what I made up. With any luck the title will become dated, a footnoted tune with all sorts of apocryphal explanations as our current regime fades into the dim reaches of obscurity.

It does a bit of a leap into the major in the second part - don’t sprain anything. The B flats are intentional, as is the B natural on the way down at the end. I like to follow out of it with Sporting Paddy (another mean flatpicking tune) or Last Night’s Fun.

If you don’t like the political connotations of the title, call it something else - perhaps John’s Favorite Curry or something, I don’t mind.
Enjoy.

↳ Posted by Jon Kiparsky on June 6th, 2004.

Ornamentation

The only ornamentation that I really use on guitar are picked triplets, which would go in as follows. Left them out for clarity on the posted version. There are lots of ways to vary the A part, most of which I think are obvious.

Ganley’s Reel

A fine reel composed by Josie McDermott. He plays it after Pat McGrath’s on “Darby’s Farewell”. https://thesession.org/tunes/4003

However, I transcribed this setting from the flute playing of Steve Ducke, which is accessible to your ears here: http://www.tradschool.com/en/tunes/ganleys-reel

Maybe someone having the album at hand can submit Josie’s original setting… :)

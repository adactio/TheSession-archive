“Lady Madelina Sinclair’s Birthday”

This is notated in the Athole and Skye collections as a reel, but in keeping with that common cross over, a melody going from reel to strathspey, I’m fond of it with swing and snap, as a strathspey. It is also notated in those collections in Bb, but loving the lower realms I’ve given it here in G. I’ll add and take or two of the Bb reel later, and link the two, and add transcriptions of the melody from earlier sources.

There are also the unavoidable influences of highland flings and Cape Breton fiddling (a favourite fiddler Winston Scotty Fitzgerald played this as a Bb reel) on me that are probably good reasons why this melody comes to me this way.

LADY MADELINA SINCLAIR‘S BIRTHDAY. AKA and see “Lady Elgin’s Courtship.” Scottish, Reel. B Flat Major. Standard tuning. AB (Surenne): AAB (Athole, Cranford, Gow): AABB’ (Kerr). Lady Madelina Sinclair (1772-1847) was the second daughter of Alexander, 4th Duke of Gordon, and William Marshall’s employer and patron. Her second husband was named Palmer (See note for “Lady Madelina Sinclair” for more). ~

Carlin (The Gow Collection), 1986; No. 266. Cranford (Winston Fitzgerald), 1997; No. 110, pg. 45. Kerr (Merry Melodies), vol. 2; No. 203, pg. 23. MacDonald (The Skye Collection), 1887; pg. 135. Ryan’s Mammoth Collection, 1883; pg. 60. Stewart-Robertson (The Athole Collection), 1884; pg. 284. Surenne (Dance Music of Scotland); pg. 34. WMT002, Wendy MacIsaac – “That’s What You Get” (1998?).

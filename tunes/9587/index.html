Two comments

Play it wit’ SOUL!

I found this beauty near the end of the “Double Jigs” section of O‘Neill’s 1,001. I did some minor tweaks to the first and second endings of each section (for instance, the tune as it appears in the book has the exact same phrase as ending 1 & ending 2 of the third part!) let me know how it sounds to y’all. I found it sits really nicely alongside “The walls of Liscarroll” (the less common version on Frankie Gavin’s “Frankie Goes To Town” CD) & another jig (actually in the database here) “Joe Liddy’s”. Play ’em (with SOUL, as instructed above!) & let me know what you think. And as always, enjoy!

↳ Posted by jaychoons on May 4th, 2009.

The Humours Of Clare, X:2

This basic version appears in P.W. Joyce’s Old Irish Music and Songs (1909), p. 351, no. 701, as The Cat’s Bagpipes.
According to the Fiddler’s Companion, it is also known as The Humours of Clare.

Miss Montgomery

I got the hand written score from the awesome Irish pipes player Michael O’Reilly of Geraldine Aotearoa. I touched it up by using the version here: https://tunearch.org/wiki/Miss_Montgomery_(1).

Nice little piece.

Re: Miss Montgomery

This tune can be found in the Skye Collection (1887) as a four-part pipe tune, “Lady MacKenzie of Seaforth” and Jerry Holland learned it as “MacBeth’s Strathspey” from “a 78rpm recording of Cape Breton fiddler Little Jack MacDonald.”

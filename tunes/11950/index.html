Salthill Prom

Composed by Carl Hession, this setting appears as a slow reel on his album Stepping Stones. The following version appears on Mick Conneely’s album Selkie:

X: 1
T: Salthill Prom
C: Carl Hession
S: Mick Conneely “Selkie”
M: 4/4
L: 1/8
R: reel
K: Gmaj
DG~G2 B^cde|f2ge fdce|dgfd (3cde dc|ABcA dcAF|
DG~G2 B^cde|~f3g fdcA|d2 (3ABc defg|adcA dGG2:|
|:~g3d cdBG|ADFG AB^ce|d2Bd ~g3a|abag fdef|
~g2d^c c/c/dBG|ADFG Adef|~g2ag fdde|1 fdgd fdef:|2 fdgd fdcA||

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on June 1st, 2012.

Iris, X:2

Hardly trad Irish, but a beautiful waltz. I first heard this on a brilliant album from Ensemble Galilei “From Whence We Came” (https://youtu.be/PdsfdVP4jUI). My flute doesn’t have a low C, so I had to tinker with it a bit.

This composition is from Guido Piccard, longtime session player, composer from Belgium, and frequent collaborator with Philip Masure of “Comas”. Piccard is the founder of the group “Floes” (find their album “Belovodia” at Bandcamp https://homerecordsbe.bandcamp.com/album/belovodia)

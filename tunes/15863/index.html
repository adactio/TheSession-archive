Alborada D’Allunquía

This is not really a reel, but an spanish tune called “alborada” tipical from some regions of Spain like Asturias or Galicia. This one is asturian and it is recorded by the group Felpeyu among others.

↳ Posted by Guilleflute on February 13th, 2017.

Alborada D’A Allunquía, X:2

From Felpeyu’s album “Ya!” 2nd tune of the 1st track.

From their website:

We learnt this tune from Rubén Arias and Antón Barquero, whom in turn learnt it from bagpiper Sergio Carbajal, from Folgosa and living in Avilés. He heard it played by a bagpiper in “A Alloquiña (A Fonsagrada, Galicia, near the Asturian border) . The name that village on the other side of the border is ”A Allunquía".

This is Felpeyu’s own version as they’ve added two “mudanzas”* they wrote.

In a dance a “mudanza” is movement which will change and could be unique in the dance.

You can find info about this on their website (in Asturian and Spanish).
http://felpeyu.com/project/ya/

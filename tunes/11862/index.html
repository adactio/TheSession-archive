I only know of one case where he came down on someone and actually deleted the last couple of contributions in a run of compositions that happened over a few days. I think it was one-a-day. :-D In general he’s not unreasonable, on the contrary… There are some folks that wish he ruled with an iron fist. I’m not one of them. ;-)

Highland Flings and Hornpipes - amongst the ‘Irish crowd’, you must be kidding. The relationship between straths and flings is obvious, and generally accepted. As it is ‘dance music’ it is about the dance too, meaning the dances that go with these. Most Irish musicians that are familiar with the form, including recordings, will be well aware of the norms of that form. Those familiar as well with the dances associated with it will have no doubt.

But you wouldn’t be the first to take it where they wanted, detached from the ‘tradition’. That seems to be quite common amongst those with an eagerness to compose…

As to proofs, do a search on this site using just ‘fling’ or ‘highland’. There are even some dance descriptions scattered about as well for the different forms. Also, check any recording where the musicians are likely to have a direct connection to this particular part of tradition, such as the fiddlers from Donegal - John Doherty, Con Cassidy, etc., but the form was hardly confined to the Northwest, check out some of the early American 78s, and not forgetting Altan. But, as said, the highland fling and the dances and music attached to this category were all over Ireland, and are still danced in parts of the Southwest, and in the U.S.A. and Canada too, as well as England, Scotland, Wales, Cornwall and the Isle of Mann, though those connections are in some cases tenuous, of which you would be proof of that.

It’s not like you can’t take things your own way, as you have, in your excitement to ‘make new’. But, that doesn’t mean there isn’t a clear history for the form. Some choose to know, some choose to ignore… Do as you will… You can connect and honour those connections, roots, or you can go off on your own tangent. You wouldn’t be the first or the last… :-/

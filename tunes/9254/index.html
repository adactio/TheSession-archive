The Midlothian Pipe Band

My abc submission is based on a recording of the tune made by the Richard Fitzgeral Ceili Band in 1967. Transcribed from memory - hopefully reasonably accurate, but please forgive any errors.

Member nigelg has advised that this setting is an adaptation from the original pipe march. Here is the original abc, as transcribed by Nigel:

X: 1
T: Midlothian Pipe Band, The
C: ------ Beaton
S: Scots Guards, Standard Settings Vol.1
R: March
M: 6/8
K: D
fe | d2A d>ef | ABA A2A | f2f f>ed | e>fe A>Bc |
d2A d>ef | B<GB B2g | f>ga A>Bc | d3 d2 :|
a/g/ | f2f f>ed | e2e e2g | a2a f2e | d>BB B>ag |
f2f fed | e2e e2g | a<fd A>Bc | d3 d2
a/g/ | f2f f>ed | e2e e2g | a2a f2e | d>BB B2g |
f>ga e>fg | f>ed B2g | f>ga A>Bc | d3 d2 |]

↳ Posted by Mix O'Lydian on January 13th, 2009.

This jig was popular for Ceili Dancing in the Inishowen area. Played by local fiddler Seamus Grant https://seamusgrantweekend.com/ We never had a name for it. Thanks to Mix O’Lydian for posting.

Twelve comments

“Hats off to Hatao” ~ 8-) ~ ;-) ~ 8-)

With respect for his passion and talents, one made man to another, and in answer to the OTT arrangement he did of “O’Carolan’s Draught” for flute and violin and someone laying down chords. It was a hoot and had me laughing for a good spell. You’re a gem Hatao and the door is always open…

This too might be considered O.T.T., over the top, and definitely has some of the fairground about it, or the musical “Music Man”, or at least those things came to mind as I was waltzing around the kitchen bumping in to things and getting dizzy.

I’ve given this in a key for D limited instruments, but that’s not the key it came to me in, which was lower. I’ll add that in the comments…

“Hats off to Hatao” / “The Ferris Wheel Waltz” ~ & the slow of it ~

This arrived slow and relaxed, at say 1/8 = 145 - 150 bpm, roughly half the speed of the fast waltz tempos listed above, that was before I started bouncing into the furniture with it.

If I a devilish humour came upon me, and there were dancers on the floor, and there were other musicians in agreement, I’d consider taking it this nice and relaxed way to start and then maybe shifting up to the fast waltz way of it… Some of the older musicians used to do similar things with the likes of “Shoe the Donkey”, a bit of the endurance test about it, and some fun…

↳ Posted by ceolachan on May 10th, 2009.

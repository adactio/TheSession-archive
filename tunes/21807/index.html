Three comments

Diu Regnare

I was in two minds whether or not to post this but I think it’s a really good tune and very “catchy” too. So, I hope most of you will enjoy the tune for its own sake regardless of whatever other views you might have.

It was composed by Pipe Major Stuart Liddell as a tribute to Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II, on the occasion of her Platinum Jubilee, June 2022.
Although it’s a new tune, it’s also freely available online and I don’t envisage any issues re copyright.
There is also a setting for Northumbrian pipes in G

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwYsdPtRH_s

but I can post this later or allow someone else to do so.

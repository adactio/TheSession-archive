Jackson’s Over The Water

Published in 1774. ABC found here: http://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=tunearch.org/wiki/Jackson%27s_Over_the_Water_(1).no-ext/0002

A note on key: I’ve played this in Fmaj (as originally set; see http://digital.nls.uk/special-collections-of-printed-music/pageturner.cfm?id=87704967), Gmaj, and Dmaj and all three sound equally good to my ears.

Six comments

McDonnell’s Rant

I started a discussion about this cool tune and Noxious Blanket found its name and the ABC on this website:

http://www.ibiblio.org/fiddlers/Mc_MEN.htm#McDONNELL’S_(SLIP_JIG

It says it’s Irish:
“McDonnell?s Rant.? Irish, Slip Jig. G Major. Standard. AABB?. The dates for the O?Farrell collection, printed by John Gow in London, England, vary: O?Neill gives 1797, while Pat Sky gives 1804, based on an entry in the Stationer?s Hall Registers. Source for notated version: copied from O?Farrell?s National Irish Music (1797)”
And old tune as you can see.

I knew it from some mp3 played by Northumbrian pipes (I think) so probably that’s why I didn’t know the second endings, because those pipes can’t play those high notes.
I can’t get used to the second endings and I think I like it better without them.
Here’s my transcription of that mp3:

X: 1
T: Gan Ainm
M: 9/8
L: 1/8
R: slip jig
K: Gmaj
BAG GAG GDG|BAG GDG ABc|BAG GAG GDG|cAF FAF ABc:|
d2d BGG BGG|d2d BGG ABc|d2d BGG BGG|cAF FAF ABc:|

Re: McDonnell’s Rant

Having played music in Ballycastle’s famous session pub ‘The House of McDonnell’ ( Wee Tom’s ) for nearly 30 years, this tune was an obvious choice for me. 🙂

The word Rant apparently has its origins in the Netherlands & means boisterous behaviour, which also seems rather appropriate. ;-)

Played on Hammered Dulcimer, Trump, English Concertina, Fiddle, Tenor Banjo & Bodhran.

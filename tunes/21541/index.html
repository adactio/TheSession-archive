Gan Ainm

This unusual tune is the second tune in set entitled “Three Characteristic Reels” submitted by the fiddler Alex Sutherland (1874-1969) of Drumreilly, Co Leitrim to the National Folklore Commission in 1957. I have not been able to match it thus far. The other two tunes in the set are “Wake up Nancy” (aka Wake up, Susan) and Smash the Windows. These two reels appear to be assimilated from printed music collections so it’s possible this one is too.

Seven comments

Da Galleywatch

I learnt this tune from great Shetland fiddler Jenna Reid at a weekend workshop in the Yorkshire Dales last year.

The way she taught it was to start on an upbow, slurring the e into a shortened f thus

e-|:fz df etc

↳ Posted by domhnall. on August 19th, 2008.

Da Galley Watch, X:4

X:4 is a tune called “Mr Stewart Dowally’s Reel” published in Robert Petrie’s first collection (1790) which has so much similarity to “Da Galley Watch” that I think it may very well be its ancestor.

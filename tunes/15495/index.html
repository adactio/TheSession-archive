Two comments

Ben’s Brogues

A tune I learned it from Oisín Mac Diarmada at a workshop a few weeks ago, composed by Charlie Lennon (titled after his brother). I love how the three parts are both compact melodically speaking and very different from each other, with the switch to Edorian in the 2nd part.

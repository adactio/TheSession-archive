Re: The Model T

Ashley MacIsaac and I wrote this tune in the recording studio. I produced Mary Jane Lamond’s first CD in 1994. The English translation of one of the Gaelic songs was Song For An Old Ford Car, I believe either written or collected by her grandfather. The song is in 6/8 time in the key of E minor. We wrote the tune as an interlude between some of the verses. The title refers to the name of one of the first cars the Ford Motor Company produced.

The Stonewaller Polka

Great tune. Sounds as if could be played as a reel. It’s got the drive of a reel.

↳ Posted by gemtrimmer on June 13th, 2010.

Stonewaller polka

In fact if you look at it as a 4/4 tune, a reel, you get 8 bars each in the A and B parts instead of the 16 bars in each part as a 2/4 tune, so you end up with a standard 32 bar tune. I would play it like a reel.

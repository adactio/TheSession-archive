Four comments

A great tune from the Skye Collection (and in an almost identical form in The Atholl Collection). It has similarities to a Scott Skinner tune, “The Auld Wheel”, the first part of which appears as the 3rd part of “Flood on The Holm”; I trust that’s enough information for tune buffs

Two Birds On a Tree

The renowned Sliabh Luachra fiddler Padraig O‘Keeffe played this tune, and wrote it down in one of his manuscript books, in a form of fiddle tablature. This is my transcription from a reproduction of O’Keeffe’s book:

X:341
T:Two Birds On a Tree
S:Padraig O‘Keeffe MS, via ’Music from Sliabh Luachra’
R:Hornpipe
Z:Nigel Gatherer
L:1/8
M:4/4
K:Amix

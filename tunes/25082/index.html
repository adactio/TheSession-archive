Re: This Flower I Pick For You

“…I composed it in late October ’04, after a visit with my dad … Shortly after the initial recording of this piece, my father … peacefully passed away…. He left behind a legacy that he was truly proud of and loved with all of his heart; his family, his music, as well as his love for the sea. This piece was inspired by, and written for my hero who is my dad, and is dedicated in his beautiful memory to the Parkinson’s Society of Canada for which I am honored to be the musical ambassador and spokesperson. The official flower of Parkinson’s is the tulip. The flower in the title is represented by the melody of this tune that was inspired by my fathers love for music and his influence in my life. He is always in my heart and continuously drives my passion and devotion towards music and life.” - Jennifer Roland

Five comments

The Haggis

This is an old Scottish tune attributed to Captain Simon Fraser. My setting is based on the one on Capercaillie’s Crosswinds. The Gaelic title I think means something like The Haggis With The Pin In It. The pin is the large skewer which Burns refers to in his poem about the haggis, and the idea of the tune title is that the bag and chanter of the pipes resemble a haggis skewered with one of these pins.

↳ Posted by Dr. Dow on May 26th, 2005.

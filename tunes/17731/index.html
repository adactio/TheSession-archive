Between, X:3

Jig composed by Natalie Haas rather than Alasdair Fraser ! They recorded it together on their duet album “In the Moment”. She also plays it with guitarist Yann Falquet : Between is credited as her composition, watch this video :

Re: Between

There’s an explanation of the title of the jig in the booklet of Alasdair and Natalie’s album : Natalie wrote this tune on the plane flying from Madrid back to her home in New York. Musicians tend to spend a lot of their time in a suspended state between places, reflecting on all the wonderful experiences they’ve just had while simultaneously trying to prepare themselves for the ones they’re about to have.

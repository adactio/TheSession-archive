Attingham Waltz

This was written by Helena Torpy and I learnt this a couple of years ago. It is one of the first folk tunes I learnt on fiddle - much thanks to a nice girl called Sarah who took the time and trouble to teach it to someone who was very very nervous having not played fiddle in front of anyone except teachers and examiners!

Re: Attingham

Trish - I’ve had the pleasure of playing this tune with Helena herself.

If I’ve remembered the story right, she was conducting a workshop at Attingham and taking a walk in the grounds when the tune popped into her head some distance from the house.

She had nothing with her to record it, so she said that she hummed it obsessively to herself till she could get back and note it down!

I’ve led this at festival sessions remote from Devon and been surprised when people knew it - it seems to be on the way to becoming a session standard. And well deserved too - it really is a gorgeous tune, and in our Totnes session we often close the evening with it.

The second version here is closest to my memory of Helen’s original.

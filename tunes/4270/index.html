Ten comments

This is my effort at transcribing the tune as it appears on the Teada recording. They vary the phrases slightly each time around but I tired to catch the essence here. I found it interesting that they play it in F - not a very common key in Irish Traditional music but a good excuse to get out of the “comfort zone”.

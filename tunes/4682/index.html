Learning this one

We were struck by this tune and its oddly sinister sound and like it very much.

We were trying to learn it from Peter Loud by the river at Newcastleton on the Sunday night when we were approached by a man who said he was “The Water Gillie”. He passed round a strange substance in a hip flask whcih he claimed he had brewed from potatoes, damsons and otters’ p*ss and then demanded £13 each for the free camping. At this moment the midges emerged in a frenzy from the waters and pounced. We fled.

↳ Posted by Gallopede on July 11th, 2005.

Another Great Tune by Chris Ormston

At Newcastleton I spent most of my time sat by the river learning another great tune by Chris, “A Dog’s Life”, getting it up to speed for Rothbury two weeks later.
The story goes that it was written after the death of Ken’s dog Nipper. (Ken is a strange Yorkshire box player who drinks in the Tap & Spile at Morpeth every Sunday afternoon).
I’m a great fan of Chris Ormston’s tunes, he’s written a few more too.

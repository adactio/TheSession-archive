Santa’s Tartan Trews

This is a nice tune for Christmas.
I’ve used the “hornpipe” time signature although it’s not really a hornpipe. It gets played at a nice steady and not too fast pace.

The tune was composed a few years ago by the extremely talented young clarsach player Elinor Evans from Edinburgh.

It’s a tune we used to play regularly at the annual “Harps of Gold” concert(I imagine they still do although I haven’t joined them in recent years). However, I always “dig it out” at this time of year to get into the spirit of things.

This came to me as a slow reel, fussing over a chicken and wild garlic soup in our kitchen… ’Tis the season, for wild garlic, and it has very much naturalized where I’ve established it in our little strip of a garden. I get carried away this time of year and put it in pretty much everything. I have now eased up on Tuesdays, before our weekly get together, after a comment from one of our dancers ~ “You’ve been eating garlic!” Followed by, “I don’t mind the smell of garlic.” Thank goodness for that little grace.

Latter I ripped through this with a bit of attitude mixed with sadness and anger…

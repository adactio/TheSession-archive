Re: Hiding Daylight In Dark Corners

A lovely traditional tune misattributed to John Carty. Ben is correct, #19498 is a duplicate of #7781 (I’ll suggest the merge).

As suggested in the comments to #7781, this is a version of a traditional Cape Breton / Scottish reel known as MacKinnon’s Rant. The author may eventually be found in some old music collection but until then I’ve cleared the composer field – sorry, John :-)

James Carty’s sleeve notes to his fine solo album, titled after this tune, tell the story behind this wondrous title:

“My father heard the first reel played by a highland piper on a tape given to him by one of my mother’s relations. We had no name for it so have christened it after a saying of a neighbour at home Christy Healy, who passed away whilst I was recording the album. Christy was a real character, full of sharp wit and devilment and had some great, sometimes nonsensical sayings. He was a regular visitor to our home and “Hiding daylight in Dark Corners” was always his reply if you asked him what he had been doing all day.”

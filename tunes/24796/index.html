Polonaise In F

While this tune is outside the Celtic diaspora, there appears a lovely version of it on whistle, harp and fiddle by the Irish group Dordan on their 1992 recording on the Shannachie label. The setting of the melody comes from the Notebook for Anna Magdelena Bach 1725, it was Johann Sebastian Bach’s personal gift to his second wife, The young Anna Magdelena, an accomplished singer, whom he married in 1721.

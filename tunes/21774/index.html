Chris Stout’s Compliments To The Bon Accord Ale House

Catchy tune with some complex rhythm by Chris Stout. Played by Fiddler’s Bid. Taught to me by a harpist who knew it from Maeve Gilchirst, but Frigg also play on their album “Frost on Fiddles”.

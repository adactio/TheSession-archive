Two comments

Miss May Hay

Great little tune from the Athole Collection. The notes say that it was originally published in 4 collections (1 of the 4, I reckon) by one Daniel Dow of Perthshire c1773. The book states that Dow lived from 1732-1783, making this one an old one even if he composed it himself. It has that extra layer of mold where newer tunes that sound creepy fall short me thinks.

The Fretless Flute Song, X:3

For those who want to exercise their keyed flutes, here is the tune as the composer plays it on YouTube, in G minor. I give a full abc below to make it easy to cut and paste into EasyABC:
X:1
T:Fretless Flute Song, The
C:Steve Kujala
Z:cac (adapted from gian marco)
S:Steve Kujala (Y/T)
R:waltz
M:3/4
L:1/8
K:Gmin
|:e2b2a2|a4g2|B2g3B|f6|
e2f2e2|d2-dc d2|B2G2F2|G6|
e2b2a2|a4g2|B2g3B|f6|
e2b3e|d4d2|B2E2B2|G6:|
b2c‘3b|a2-ag f2|a2c’3b|a4g2|
a4f2|f4e2|g6-|g6|
b2d‘2c’2|a3gf2|a2c’3b|g3fe2|
d4c2|A3GF2|G6-|G6:|

On a simple system wooden flute it is not possible to bend all the notes the way Kujala does using a Boehm flute, but our slides work pretty well. The tune is very good practice for using the Bb key. Here is the link to Kujala’s playing: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mBMm9ximzE

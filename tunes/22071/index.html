Three comments

Bannow Bay

A hornpipe composed many years ago by mouth organ legend Phil Murphy of Ballygow in Wexford. The 30th Phil Murphy festival has just been held in nearby Carrig-on-Bannow. It has I’ve always thoughtthis tune had the ‘feel’ of an old tune. It was recorded by Phil and his two sons, John and Pip, on their seminal ’Trip to Cullenstown" album.

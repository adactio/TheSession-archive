Two comments

Oldtown

When I first heard Ciarán Tourish’s solo album, “Down the Line” (2005), I remarked this original tune that has a different flavor from the rest of the album. The tempo is rather quiet for a reel (?) but it still gives the envy to dance, maybe because of the slightly jumpy rhythmic backing by Arty McGlynn on the guitar. I wanted to learn it, then the time went by, and I forgot it until I recently rediscovered it. A composition by former Altan’s fiddler, C. Tourish from Buncrana / Inishowen.

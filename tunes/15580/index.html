Six comments

Metcalfe

Over the summer during the John Arcand Fiddle Fest, I heard Kyle Burhout practicing this reel that he wrote. He was planning to play it in the competition, and I thought it sounded lovely. He told me the name was Metcalfe, but did not give any explanation as to why that is. The tune is in F# Major.

Metcalfe is a village located in the south-end of the city of Ottawa, Ontario, Canada.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Edwgf8szsyc

Metcalfe Fair, X:3

Here is the composer’s original setting (very similar to X:2 - well done, Nigel).
Kyle Burghout says that he “named this tune “Metcalfe Fair” after the Metcalfe Fair which is held every fall just outside of Ottawa, Canada where he lives. They have a fiddle & stepdance contest as part of the fair, and he wrote this tune while he was as the contest a few years ago”.

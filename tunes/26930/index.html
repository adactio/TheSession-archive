Two comments

Grey Owl

Grey Owl is a contemporary(depending on when you’re reading this) Metis fiddle tune that was composed on July 2, 1989, by legendary Canadian fiddler John Arcand, who is widely known as the "Master of the Métis Fiddle

Arcand wrote the tune near the Grey Owl Estates housing development in Saskatchewan, naming it in honor of the famous British-born naturalist Archibald Belaney (who adopted the name “Grey Owl”)

I’ve first heard this tune in bluegrass circles, but I think it fits and translates well to traditional and old-time fiddle playing syles.

Three comments

A Potato For Emile

From the playing of Zav (Jamie) RT.

The tune is presumably named after fiddler Émile Benoît, and, is a reference to one of his tunes, “Gerald Thomas’ Burnt Potato” (https://thesession.org/tunes/6743 ).

The first two tunes in Zav’s set, Les Patates Brulees (Gerald Thomas‘ Burnt Potato)/Reel De La Pistroli/A Potato For Emile, were composed by Emile, and, this tune, has a similar sound as Gerald Thomas’ Burnt Potato.

↳ Posted by NfldWhistler on July 7th, 2020.

Re: The Balintore Fisherman

I didn’t mean to suggest that you were wrong, Lucy; just adding to the knowledge. I think I’ve got a copy of the original sheet music for “The Balintore Fishermen” somewhere.

Jimmy Blue played in Ian Powrie’s band, Powrie on fiddle, Blue and Mickie Ainsworth on accordions. Jimmy is long gone, but I’ve seen his wife Joan many times accompanying local musicians on piano. They used to live in a house which they called “Tigh na Gorm” - Gaelic for “house of blue”.

The Ian Powrie band (from Perthshire) was popular in Shetland, resulting in one of Ronnie Cooper’s great tunes being named for Mickie Ainsworth: https://thesession.org/tunes/7242

First Post…

Hello all,

This is my first post to “The Session”, my new favorite website! Please feel free to let me know of any mistakes on my part; I’m new to “abc” format and ITM, so…

I heard Solas do this tune, and fell in love with it. I don’t have a Bozouki, so I play it on my mandola (CGDA) with a capo on the second fret. Or, if I only have my mandolin, I just play it in the key of D. Hope you like it.

The Mayo Fling, X:3

This tune (Fling Mhaigh Eo) was composed by Laoise Kelly for Mayo Day 2021. I learned my setting from John Carty, who picked up the tune from the playing of Laoise Kelly and Matt Molloy, as in the video above. John Carty played it before the reel Caught in the Surf (https://thesession.org/tunes/21157).

It bounces along in a really lovely way, and there’s plenty of room for putting in extra flourishes--or indeed taking out some of the flourishes to keep the rhythm!

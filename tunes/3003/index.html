Paddy Ryan’s Nightmare/Paddy Kenny’s

The X:2 setting above is from Teada’s recent album ‘Ainneoin na Stoirme’ (In Spite of the Storm, 2013). They call it “Paddy Kenny’s” and they got it “…from fiddler Paddy Ryan during a Scoil Eisge workshop in Sligo in 1990, with Paddy himself learning the tune from Ballinameen, Co Roscommon fiddler, Paddy Kenny…”

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on March 4th, 2014.

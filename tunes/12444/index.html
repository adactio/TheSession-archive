The Ross Memorial Hospital (not A Barndance!)

Hi there, this is a lovely tune by Phil Cunningham.

The notes are right in this ABC just the duration of some is not what it should be.
There are some notes that are played shorter, but if you look up the tune on youtube
or somewhere else you can figure out how to really play it.

If you play it exactly the way it’s written down here, it’ll be boring so don’t :-)

Have fun!

Re: The Ross Memorial Hospital

I can really recommend this beautiful tune by Phil Cunningham. I used to have his tune-book and really fell for this. Best played as a slow air - not too fast. Took me a while getting this on fiddle, but Tom McConville’s cd - posted here - has helped me a lot. He puts in the harmony in this recording. “Back to Scotswood” - he’s a Geordie, grew up in a pub… I met him once at the stramash - finale - when he came up to Orkney to perform and teach. He can sing too. Guess he’s in his late 60s now?

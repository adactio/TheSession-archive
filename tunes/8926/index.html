Mrs. Crotty’s Hornpipe

I’ve posted the basic tune, in C, as taught by Gearoid O hAllmhurain at the Catskills Irish Arts Week in 2008.

Here’s a way to flesh it out a little, working from my memory of what Gearoid taught, two months later.

X:2
T:Mrs. Crotty’s
R:Hornpipe
L:1/8
M:C
K:C
ef|gG eg fA df|eG ce dA Bd|(3ccc Bc de fd|eG ce dd ef|
gG eg fA df|eG ce dA Bd|(3ccc Bc de fd|e2 c2 c2:|
ed|cA AG Ad cA|GF EF GA BG|c2 Bc de fd|eG ce d2 ed|
cA AG Ad cA|GF EF GA BG|c2 Bc de fd|e2 c2 c2:|

And here it is in D for them that prefers it that way.

X:3
T:Mrs. Crotty’s
R:Hornpipe
L:1/8
M:C
K:D %Transposed from C
fg|aA fa gB eg|fA df eB ce|(3ddd cd ef ge|fA df ee fg|
aA fa gB eg|fA df eB ce|(3ddd cd ef ge|f2 d2 d2:|
fe|dB BA Be dB|AG FG AB cA|d2 cd ef ge|fA df e2 fe|
dB BA Be dB|AG FG AB cA|d2 cd ef ge|f2 d2 d2:|

The Pooka

Sean Maguire had this as “The Pooka” in “Irish Tunes by the 100,” a collection he published with Josephine Keegan in 1975.

It has a resemblance to “The Arkansas Traveller.”

↳ Posted by blarneystar on September 20th, 2008.

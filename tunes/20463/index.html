Shuffle And Cut

Simplified setting of tune #997 in O’Neill’s 1001. It’s in several English dance collections in 9/4. This is in the Miscellaneous section, so without listening examples (I’m coming up dry in searching for clips of it as a dance) I’m not sure whether it would be played with a slip jig rhythm or something else entirely. I’ll post a second setting with a direct transcription (just a few ornaments and bow markings).

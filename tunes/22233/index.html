The Blind Piper

Another reel of my own composition, notated and uploaded as part of The Wexford Tune Project. I composed the second strain of this tune as a possible third part to Nick Sheridan’s Farewell
https://thesession.org/tunes/22167.
However, I ended up putting a first part to it instead and this is the result.
The title refers to the part played by my classmate (and musical friend of over 50 years), Mick Lawler, who played the part of a ‘blind piper’ in a great Comhaltas Scoraiocht show that featured at the Fleadh Nua All-Ireland finals in the 1980’s. Mick had a little too much liquid refreshment prior to the show, so The Blind Piper was rather the worse for wear and only located at the last possible moment from his spot (chosen for tuning his pipes, beneath the stage) in rather hilarious circumstances. However the performance I can assure you was worth the wait. Pipes?? Send for a plumber is my advice!

Tim The Turncoat

As played by the Gallowglass Ceili Band, although their repertoire sounds like it’s played directly from O"Neill’s Music of Ireland (1903). The first phrase is similar to other tunes (e.g. The Steamboat Hornpipe and The Goodnatured Man), but they are not the same tunes.

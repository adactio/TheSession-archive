Three comments

Guiseppe’s

This Damien O’Kane tune is featured in the album “Banjophony.” This is my best ear-transcription of the tune. It was written for his father, whose name is not Guiseppe, but whom Damien and his siblings affectionately refer to as such in reference to a movie character.

↳ Posted by Charleville on March 14th, 2022.

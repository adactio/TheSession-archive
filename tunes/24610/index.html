Re: Mary’s Return

Very similar to a tune called “The Ball, a New Country Dance” which was published in Edinburgh in about 1790 on a two-page sheet called “Five Favorite New Country Dances as Danced at the Assembly.” The Assembly Rooms in George Street, Edinburgh, opened in 1787, was a favourite venue where one could hear Nathaniel Gow and colleagues playing for dances.

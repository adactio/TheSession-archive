Two comments

Sean Ryan, Master Composer

It never fails to amaze me just how many of this man’s compositions I learned as traditional before I knew they were his. This one comes from the playing of Kathleen Collins & is only slightly different than the printed version in “The Hidden Ireland”. If you don’t have this wonderful book of Sean Ryan’s compositions, get it. Get it now!

Three comments

H.R.H. The Duke Of Rothesay

This march (not a jig) was composed by “J. Abercromby” and published for the first time in 1869 in the [William] Ross’s Collection (of) Pipe Music (no. 320, p. 160).
I post this tune in response to the posting of the Bachelor (https://thesession.org/tunes/20608), a jig which “derives” from this one, according to Paul Cranford. I don’t really agree with Mr Cranford…
I didn’t include the numerous grace notes in my ABC transcription. W. Ross wrote “It may be stated that all the airs may be played on the Pianoforte or Violin by simply omitting the Grace Notes.”
By the way, the current Duke of Rothesay is H.R.H. Prince Charles.

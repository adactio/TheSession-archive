The Sea Stallion / Havhingsten

A beautiful tune (absolutely not a polka) composed by Poul Bjerager Christensen in 2004 for the launch of the Sea Stallion from Glendalough (a viking ship), and played by a lot of fiddlers - John Sheahan and many others, before it’s journey to Dublin.

Read more here: http://www.vikingtoday.com/ships/denmark/seastallion-from-glendalough.htm

Listen here: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XrVfgTLlEGw

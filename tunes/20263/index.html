Strings Attached

12/8 written by Cullen fiddler Jack Smedley, member of RURA. Transcribed from his album with David Foley “Time to Fly”. Track #3.2.

The tune goes AAB AB and then they repeat the last two bars of the B part a few times. Mind the time signature change in the last bar.

The tune starts around 2:20.

The Curse Of The Molcolbhin Line

"Though the history of Salem, Massachusetts County, England is rife with stories and folk tales that are anything from mundane, to strange, and to nearly impossible to explain, the story of the Kaxby-Molcolbhin feud is among the more interesting.

In 1887, Mr. Grünewald Kaxby, a Yenish warwolf hailing from somewhere in Germany, found his way into Salem with his wife and 7 children. Defeating their purpose, they settled down. In 1888, Grünewald met with Mr. Ciaran Molcolbhin, a human immigrant to Salem via Waterford, County Waterford, Ireland. The Kaxby family, who had quickly become notorious for their shifty nature, owned a convenience store, ‘Kaxby’s’, on North Haddings St. One day, Ciaran was buying groceries at the store, where Grünewald reportedly tried to scam him into overpaying for a bottle of milk, citing its magical properties. The two got into a heated debate, and Grünewald cursed the entire Molcolbhin line to misfortune, before stealing Ciaran’s heirloom pocketwatch when he wasn’t looking.

Four generations passed, and the feud was forgotten or ignored. Tyler Egger, a teen warwolf-werespitz mix, is a football player for the minor-league Salem Owls. He is an average kid who achieves average grades in school, listens to average radio music, and drinks Averageade Sports Drinks. He is also stuck as being the unfortunate wearer of the team’s dusty spare jersey, #19, ‘Molcolbhin’. The curse is still alive, and its latch on Tyler has made him the worst player on the team, him having scored 0 goals and inadvertently preventing several for his team. Let’s hope he finds a way to lift the curse before the big game. Or just get a new jersey, maybe one named ‘Egger’.

Wish You Were Here,

-Jon"

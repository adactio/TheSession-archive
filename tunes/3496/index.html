One comment

Charley, Frances and Ivan

The boredom of Frances got the best of me Sunday evening, and I composed this hornpipe on on the penny whistle. At least I think I did, I won’t discount the possibility that my subconscious dredged it out of the dusty archives of my mind.
At any rate play it like a hornpipe - sassy, with a lot of bounce and swing.
ralph

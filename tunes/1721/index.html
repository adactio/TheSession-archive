Godfather’s Reel

Composed this for my Godfather on his birthday. Me and his daughter Gael Cassidy, a great flute player who has spent alot of time in Clare, played its first performance, but he declared that he prefered “tripping up stairs”… …oh well!

A slower reel which has hints of toss the feathers, but I hadn’t heard or learnt that tune then. Hopefully you lot might like it more than my godfather seemed to!

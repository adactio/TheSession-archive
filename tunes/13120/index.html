The Hunstsman, X:3

2 small changes that ive heard that take this tune from Epic Status to Legendary status.
The e in bar 4 in the first part is replaced with an f. This really rings out the up beat major end of the mixolydian key
And the c’s in bars 4, 5 and 6 of the second part have been flattened to bring in slightly darker minor edge before returning to the major on repeat.

↳ Posted by Paul F on November 29th, 2024.

Final edit.
After listening to these guys playing it (link below, last tune, starts about half way through) Ive taken out the c naturals (i think the minor is a nice touch for an arrangement but the f# sound livelier and more natural) and jiggled around a few other notes. so now it seems it has essentially become almost identical to the original version posted here but with that all important F# in the 4rth bar.

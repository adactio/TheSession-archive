Re: Pasucáis De Xuan Martín

Composed by Elias Garcia [RIP] of “Llan de Cubel”.
Sleeve notes : “The following tune possesses the typical characteristics of the Asturian ”pasucais“ - street marches - although with an infrequent rhythm ( 9/8 instead of 6/8 ). As we composed it when spending some days in the house of John Martin, the famous fiddler from Edinburgh, we dedicate it to him in appreciation of his hospitality. ( ”Xuan Martin“ is John Martin re-named in the Asturian language )”.

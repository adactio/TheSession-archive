Five comments

Four Courts

This is the second “Four Courts” in O’Neill’s 1001, #641. It’s a bit of a hybrid of Keel Row/Jenny’s Frolics (https://thesession.org/tunes/1375) and Jenny’s Gone to/High Road to Linton. The closest similarity is to Jenny’s Frolics, but there are a lot of differences (bars 2 and 4 of each part, and the four parts). This is a direct transcription with bowings, though I reduced duplication by adding repeats. I’ll add a second setting without the fiddle markup (which will consolidate it further due to elimination of bowing differences on repeat of part 4).

Early version

“The Hen and Her Clutch” appears in the Larry Smyth MS of c.1890-1920. Smyth (d.1930) was from Abbeylara, Co. Longford. Keegan sourced this tune through a Longford contact, who in turn sourced it from Fr. John Quinn of Gortletteragh, Co. Leitrim who has a copy of the MS since it was rediscovered in c.1969. He is a proponent of local Longford and Leitrim music and used this tune from the Smyth MS in one of his Ceili Bands in the 1970s.

Risipiti

↳ Posted by jardineromi on August 7th, 2021.

Re: Risipiti

The mode is D dorian-with-a-lydian-4th (those G# are more than an accidental presence).
The Bb at the end of the turn and the sprinkling of C# in the lower reach are harmonic minor borrowings. A lovely mix of notes!!

Does one know where the name / the tune originate exactly? Or through what channels it came into the repertoire of The House Band?

Eileen’s Request

Tune composed by Paddy O’Brien (1922 - 1991).

This setting is the one I whistle, I transposed the tune to G Major. In this key I don’t need to do any change of octave.

https://youtu.be/oBuA_EcoucQ

↳ Posted by Fernando Durbán Galnares on December 31st, 2020.

Setting in the original key, D Major.

Eileen’s Request, X:3

Setting in the original key (D Major) but with the changes of octave I have chosen for whistling.

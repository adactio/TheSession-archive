↳ Posted by J. A. Cerro on October 17th, 2021.

Re: To An Old Rose

I am glad to see this beautiful waltz spreading into the Irish context. I love Seamus Egan’s take on it. Joe Pancerzewski (Pan-zer-es-ki) composed this and named it for an old rose which grew in Vivian and Phil Williams’ garden in Seattle, WA. He was born in 1905 and grew up near Minot, North Dakota, later moving to Saskatchewan and Washington State. He was a great dance fiddler and prolific composer. To an Old Rose was published in 151 Brand New Old Time Fiddle Tunes by Voyager Publishing, recorded by Joe and also by Frank Ferrel. You can read more at www.voyagerrecords.com and www.wotfa.org. Enjoy.

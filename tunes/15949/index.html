The Tammienorrie

This is the first tune of the medley on Shooglenifty’s Venus in Tweeds. Hard to find any other versions of this, so I am not sure whether or not this is a traditional tune. Anyway, I hope this transcription comes close.

↳ Posted by Cyril Johnson on March 9th, 2017.

Re: The Tammienorrie

It is also on a CD by Shetland fiddler Maggie Adamson and guitarist Brian Nicholson as the title track, spelled slightly differently as Tammy Norie. A tammie norrie is of course a puffin! Not sure myself if it is Shetland traditional or written by one of Shooglenifty. Maggie taught us the tune at a workshop in Edinburgh, and I have the dots somewhere, so will compare with your transcription, Cyril. Great tune, as were many of the “Shoogles”!

↳ Posted by trish santer on March 9th, 2017.

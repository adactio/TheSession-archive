Two comments

7x8 Alexis

Composed by Stolen Notes piper Alejo Parra. Second bar in third part is 9/8 (2+2+3+2) and the last bar is also 9/8 (3+2+2+2). Whole third part can be played like binary or (4/4) because combination of 9 and 7 is 16/8.

↳ Posted by Juan Almaraz on April 14th, 2017.

“Princess Florence’s Jig” ~ C: Donald Angus Beaton - by the book, and not by the book

X: 2
T: Princess Florence’s Jig
C: Donald Angus Beaton, 1912 - 1982
B: “Donald Angus Beaton’s Cape Breton Scottish Violin Music”, tune #32
M: 6/8
L: 1/8
R: jig
K: CMaj
|: G |\
c2 G cec | d3 def | edc GAB | cGE CEG |
c2 G cec | d3 def | edc GAB | c3 c2 :|
|: e |\
f2 f fag | e2 c Gec | d2 B GAB | cBc edc |
f2 f fag | e2 c Gec | d2 B GAB | c3 c2 :|

But, while Donald himself plays it pretty much this way, he has a slightly different, and interesting, tale on the first and fifth bar of the B-part ~

| f2 f faf | ~ rather than ~ | f2 f fag |

↳ Posted by ceolachan on September 4th, 2012.

| f2 f faf |

The Beatons of Mabou also play it so. But, other than this one part, the basic melody of this tune is played as transcribed in the book…

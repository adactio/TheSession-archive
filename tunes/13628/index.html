Two comments

The Referendum

Written by Alasdair Fraser in 2012 “for the occasion of Scotland’s First Minister Alex Salmond’s visit to Alasdair’s fiddle course at Sabhal Mor Ostaig on the Isle of Skye, and to celebrate the upcoming referendum.”

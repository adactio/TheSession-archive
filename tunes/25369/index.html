Two comments

Salt Creek

The fiddle tune “Salt Creek,” popularized by Bill Monroe, is a bluegrass staple that originated as “Salt River,” an old Irish-American tune, and was recorded by the Kessinger Brothers in 1929/1931. Monroe renamed it “Salt Creek” in honour of the creek near his Bean Blossom Festival in Indiana.

This tune is played by every known old time and bluegrass artist. It even pops up on a Byrds album, no doubt suggested by Clarence White.

↳ Posted by Jim Sime on March 12th, 2025.

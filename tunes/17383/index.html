Re: The Rollicking Skipper

Looks like Sol’s comments on this page and a related tune page were lost with the outage + back-up.

The gist was Wilf Doyle played this tune, and titled it as “Slippin’ The Jig”.

I noticed that a while ago as well, but forgot to come here and make note of it.

This left Sol wondering if the McNulty’s family recording titled “Slippin’ The Jig” was of this tune.

Sol had also acknowledged the following, and I lean more towards the following possibility, that McNulty’s “Slippin’ The Jig” could be one of these tunes:
https://thesession.org/tunes/23090
https://thesession.org/tunes/23091

As I understood it, Sol made a good case for why it would have more likely been one of those two rather than the other, specifically because Frank had improvized the B Part of one of them (i.e., tune 23090 or 23091) using the melodic structure of the other (i.e., tune 23091 or 23090).

I’m missing a few details that are probably important (e.g., some of Sol’s source, the tune the A Part was derived from for the above tune that had the B Part improvized), and Sol could better re-state those than I could.

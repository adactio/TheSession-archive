The Lakeland Fiddlers

This is just to draw a little attention to one of our gems here in the Northwest of England, The Lakeland Fiddlers, who now have two albums to their name. This tune, aptly titled, opens their second recording “One Big Fiddle”, a steal at a tenner. You can get an earful of this lovely little march and more on this bit of web realty they have:

http://www.lakelandfiddlers.co.uk/

& their two recordings ~

“Fiddles in the Cowshed”
https://thesession.org/recordings/2685

“One Big Fiddle”
https://thesession.org/recordings/3678

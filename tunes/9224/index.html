The Priesthaugh Hornpipe

©Matt Seattle
Priesthaugh is usually pronounced Priestoff. The tune came wafting down the Teviot valley to me while I was immersed in the story and tune of WIllie Henderson (Rattlin Roarin Willie) who lived there.

I hardly ever play hornpipes these days (except the 3/2 variety) having previously overdosed on James Hill, but this one came knocking at the door, and…

DonaldK has some nice chords to this one, maybe he’ll post them?

Happy New Year!

Four comments

This tune…

… was composed by my wife Birgit Dalmer in January 2009 on a beautiful holiday in a charming hut on the island of Fehmarn, Germany. It was in remembering the evening before, after we bought some good bottles of Single Malt Whisky in a tax-free shop and tasted (yes, only tasted!) them :-))

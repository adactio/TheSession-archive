Re: Donald Ian Rankine

Ian Rankin is a novelist. The composer of this tune is Andrew Rankine (1930-1985).

“Donald Ian Rankine” was indeed recorded by Dundonian fiddler Angus Fitchet (1910-1998).

I can’t hear much difference between mkenneth’s setting (no.3 above) and geoffwright’s setting (no.1).

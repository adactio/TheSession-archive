Aird Uí Chuain

Famous sean-nós classic (actually a song rather than a march) in Irish (Gaeilge) about a migrant missing his native Aird Uí Chuain (or Aird Uí Chumhaing in the album Portland by Burke/Ó Domhnaill), in English Ardicoan (County Antrim, Northern Ireland), although I couldn’t find it on googlemaps!

I originally heard it from the likes of Lillis Ó Laoire (check his album Celtic Tales and Tongues) who dropped by at a uni session thing, but this is more like the modern version by Ciara McCrickard on the video below:

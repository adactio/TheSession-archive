An Seachrán Sí (The Stray Sod)

This jig by Conal O Grada is the last tune in the second track of his 2025 album, Anu Abu. It is in a jig set of three, following Nota Stota and Budgies Ball. It starts in DM, and then chromatically shifts to GM for the second half.

On Birkin Tree album “A Cheap Present”

I asked the band If I can submit this tune. They agreed. The composer is Simone Sisani.

↳ Posted by Pierre Commes on April 19th, 2008.

Not e Reel but a Highland

Michel Balatti of Birkin Tree sent me an email to tell me:
“Actually it’s meant to be an highland rather than a slow reel”

↳ Posted by Pierre Commes on April 21st, 2008.

Re: Skies Through Leaves

I confirm that the song was written by Simone Sisani, perhaps around 1994, I remember that we played it together when we were both in Bogside, the Irish Trad formation in Rome at that time. I only changed a few notes and added the harmony.

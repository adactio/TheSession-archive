Re: The Lamb On The Lawn

Sean Ryan won the newly composed tunes competition at the All Ireland in 1961 with this tune together with “The Castle Jig” and “The Winding Boreen”(hornpipe). Fr John Quinn, who christened a lot of Ryan’s tunes for him, notes how he came up with the title: “This tune suggested to me the frolicking of a little lamb in Paddy Farrell’s great big front field, known as “the lawn”, across the road from our own house at home in Longford, in January when I was home for the Christmas holidays from Maynooth [seminary at St Patrick’s College, Maynooth].”

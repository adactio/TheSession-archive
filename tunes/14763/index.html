Willie Murray’s, C:?

Here’s a great reel I found on the Old Blind Dogs‘ “Play Live” album. Part 3 is an E minor modulation of part 1, but since no “C”s are involved I’m pretending otherwise. On the album, part 4 is just a repeat of part 2, but this might not be the case elsewhere e.g. Blazin’ Fiddles, who play this in a piping style - it’s less straight, anyway. I’ll probably add that setting soon.

OBD, third tune: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UBeg4_DZEmQ
BF, second tune: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXV2x8bbmYY

↳ Posted by Poll-eisg on September 30th, 2015.

Willie Murray’s Reel

On both “Play Live” and “Ther Gab o’ May” the Old Blind Dogs have this as traditional. It was previously recorded by Ossian, and if I remember rightly it’s attributed to “MacDonald”.

Didn’t Make It

We didn’t make it. The Dutch team just lost the final of the world cup.
Here’s a tribute for all those soccer fans who took a flight to South Africa, last minute, without having a chance to obtain the last available vuvuzela, and who spent all 120 minutes loudly singing their adaptations of traditional tunes, and of course a hit by the Bonzo Dog Doodah Band.

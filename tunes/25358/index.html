Three comments

O’Brien’s Trip To America

Taken from Treoir volume 2 number 1, page 11, 1970

“This tune, along with ”Let’s Dance“, ”Lough Key (2)“, and ”Forget Me Not (1)“ were composed by Larry Redican and presented to Paddy O’Brien on the occasion of his recent visit to America.”

↳ Posted by Liam Slivka on March 7th, 2025.

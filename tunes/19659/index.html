Surely Wee Och Aye

Key: Bb (not G minor)
C: Clodagh Boylan

composed by: Clodagh Boylan

From Laoise Kelly’s playing on her album “Just Harp”,

Laoise says:“For a long time on tour in 1991, this tune had no name. While on tour we spent much of our time slagging Clodagh’s sister Sheila, about her lovely Derry accent. It was always ”surely“ this Ö and ”wee“ that Ö or ”och aye“ the other Ö In the end the tune had to be christened ”Surely Wee Och Aye".

↳ Posted by Yooval on August 23rd, 2020.

Dr Flora MacAulay Of Carradale

Cracking tune written by Allan MacDonald, more information about who Dr Flora MacAulay was can be found here - https://www.thepipingcentre.co.uk/visit/other-visit/dr-flora-macaulay-s-photographs . Good version can be found on Skipinnish’s track ‘20th Anniversary Jigs’, it is the final tune played in the set.

↳ Posted by DuncanDFC on January 27th, 2020.

Re: Dr Flora MacAulay Of Carradale

The piping version of this tune with ornamentation is included in the second volume of Allan MacDonald’s tunes ‘The Moidart Collection II - An Dara Ceud (The Second Hundred)’: https://allanmacdonald.com/purchase/
Incidentally the correct title is Dr Flora MacAuley, Carradale.

↳ Posted by Andrew Newland on March 28th, 2021.

Re: Dr Flora MacAulay Of Carradale

Cracking tune indeed. It took a while to spot the difference between the first and second time endings for the last part.
I like to play it with Dr Iain MacAonghais (Dr John MacInnes), another of Allan’s four parters.

Ged Foley’s

I learned this reel from one of Paddy O’Brien’s tune collections.
He says that Ged Foley played the reel to him, but I don’t know if Ged Foley is the composer.
Paddy plays it on B/C accordion, but I’ve been playing it on tenor banjo tuned CGDA
(where it is perfect because it does not go below C).

It might also be nice (nicer on some instruments) in Edor.
In that key it bears some resemblance to this Paddy Kelly reel
https://thesession.org/tunes/7095#setting18664

↳ Posted by ithaca-markb on November 10th, 2019.

Re: Ged Foley’s

In the notes to Paddy’s tune collection he writes:
"Ged put this on a cassette for me, but he had no name for it.
It’s an old fiddle reel, usually played in the key of E minor.
I play it here in D minor, which is a little easier on a B/C accordion.
The melody of it puts me in mind of the Scots/Northern Ireland fiddle tradition."

Ged Foley’s, X:2

As Paddy says, it is usually in E minor (dorian), and, indeed, this is how I play it
on a C#/D accordion. I see that my previous setting was a bit too “banjo-ey”
(too many triplets written out). So this is also a better setting of the tune.

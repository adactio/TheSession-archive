The McDonnell-Garvey

This tune, composed by Josephine Keegan, is in The Séamus Connolly Collection of Irish Music.
Josephine Keegan dedicated it to “her friend, the late Máire McDonnell-Garvey, who was a fiddle player and the author of a number of books about her native province of Connacht.”
It’s a great hornpipe.
Enjoy !

The Harvest Knot

A handsome 16 bar reel in the major pentatonic mode learned from a recording by Gatehouse.
It sounds totally traditional yet was penned in the early 21st century, at a time when the skill of Harvest knot weaving was all but forgotten, like the peculiar sound of the corncrake in the wheat and barley fields, both confined today, for the most part, to the folk and natural history museums.
With excellent tunesmiths such as Roscommon’s own John McEvoy and an ever growing posse of trad musicians in Ireland and abroad to carry on the tradition, there is no fear that this particular sound and skill will die out soon.

On their album The Harvest Knot is followed by another of John McEvoy’s lovely traditional sounding tune, The Healing Stone, a name which also alludes to customs of the past.

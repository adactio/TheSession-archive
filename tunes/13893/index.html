Doyle’s Fancy

T:Doyle’s Fancy
C:20th century Cape Breton
C:Dougie MacDonald
R:Clog

Cranford’s Pub had a few images of Dougie MacDonald tunes but not the abc versions. Some of the tunes were already here but three of them were not. I’ve gone ahead and created the abc versions. The original key was Eb so I’ve transposed it to Dmaj. Clog as a tune type isn’t supported here so I’ve used reel instead. I hope I haven’t messed it up too badly.

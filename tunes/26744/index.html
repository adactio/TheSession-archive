Válsa Na Rógairí

This is a tune by Jack Talty from his new album Coire an Cheoil where it is performed by At the Racket. The original is in Bb major, presumably to suit the saxophone, but I’ve transposed it to G here which I think is the natural key for traditional music instruments to play it in.

It has been an earworm of mine since I got the album so I had to get the notes down. I’ve tried to keep this to the basic melody without ornamentation and I hope I have it about right.

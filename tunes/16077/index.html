Four comments

Clog De Pariseau

From the repertoire of Alfred Montmarquette, this piece - a reel, despite its name - is dedicated to one of his great friends, accordionist Louis-Philippe Pariseau. Source: http://www.mustrad.udenap.org/tounes/TQ283_clog_de_pariseau.html

↳ Posted by Javi V on May 6th, 2017.

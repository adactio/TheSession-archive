Sputnik Breakdown

Composed by Marguerite “June” Eikhard, née Cameron (Canada’s First Lady of the Fiddle). She was born in Moncton, New Brunswick, on December 1, 1932, and died in Oshawa, Ontario February 19, 2023. Mother of songwriter Shirley Eikhard.

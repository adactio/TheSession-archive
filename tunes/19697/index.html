Smiler, X:2

↳ Posted by TomWyllie on September 2nd, 2020.

Re: Smiler

Cracking and catchy tune by Ross Ainslie / Ali Hutton, here it at https://youtu.be/OBxd5briGi4?t=220 (included 10 seconds of the previous tune because the transition is too good to not hear). I’m not sure I’d describe it as a slip jig but the tempo is easiest to count in 9/8, and it’s originally in C#major (!!).

It’s certainly not in Fdorian but that’s the closest mode I can get on here - Fdorian is same key sharp signature as D#major which has F and C double sharps in place of C#major’s F# and C# (single sharps). Or if you want to think of it from the other direction, add A#, E#, B# to Emajor. It’s not the friendliest key so I was taught it in A major - I’ve added the original as well as an A major version.

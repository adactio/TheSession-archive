O’Connor’s Fancy

Tune number 876 in The Dance Music of Ireland (1907) by Francis O’Neill.

Setting inspired by the playing of Kyle Borley in the video he uploaded to YouTube (see down below).

I included in the setting some of his variations and as ornamentation I picked up a few silent notes where he breathes in and a cran in D he plays twice in the first part.

Kyle Borley is doing a great job playing O’Neill’s tunes and uploading all the videos to YouTube. He has the project of playing them all, a long process that is going to take a few years.

He says he is doing his own arrangements to the tunes and they very much depend on the instrument he plays each time (he plays flute, banjo and tin whistle).

I didn’t find this tune in this website, if it’s duplicated please let me know and I will add this ABC as one more setting.

This is the video where Kyle plays the tune:

Re: Da Forfeit O’ Da Ship

Title should read Forfit o da Ship. The forefoot is that part of a ship’s bow where it enters the water. Forfeit means something else!

↳ Posted by Len Wilson on May 31st, 2026.

Re: Da Forefit O’ Da Ship

“Da Mirrie Dancers - A Book Of Shetland Fiddle Tunes” - edited by Tom Anderson and Tom Georgeson.
Published by the Shetland Folk Society 1970.
Index of titles and pg 24 : “Da Forefit O Da Ship”. Traditional. As played by J.C. Smith".

↳ Posted by Kenny on May 31st, 2026.

Re: Da Forefit O’ Da Ship

“Hand Me Doon Da Fiddle” by Tom Anderson and Pam Swing also titles the tune as “Da Forefit O Da Ship”. It’s tune 26 in the book and has a set of notes , in Shetland dialect, that explain the basis of the tune.

Roaring Hornpipe, X:3

Setting #3 could be regarded as a different tune, perhaps, but is certainly closely related, as well as having a rather similar title. This version is widely known in Wales, although according to https://tunearch.org/wiki/Annotation:Roaring_Hornpipe_(The) it is also played in England (and I have never heard it referrred to by a Welsh title). The aforementioned link has it notated in dotted rhythm, but I have never heard it played that way.

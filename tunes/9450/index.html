Re: Kilteery

Just a comment on how tunes sometimes live their own lives.

I recently heard a very idiosynchratic version which made me wonder what tune it really was (and whether I knew or, or “should” know it) - I had this Kilteery in mind, though (and some phrases in the B part reminded me of setting#2 of this one - https://thesession.org/tunes/1062).

The musicians had learned it from a session recording (from Ennis, I think), but also mentioned Yvonne Casey’s album, but her setting is pretty similar to the one Danú once recorded (and that’s the one I play).

Whatever happened with this fairly straightforward tune, I don’t know… So similar, yet so different.

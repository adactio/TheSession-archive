“Kip’s Jig” ~ from the playing of Hugh Gillespie

The parts for this one have always rang bells of familiarity with other tunes, the most obvious being the A-part shared with ~

“The Bride’s Favourite”
Key signature: G Major
Submitted on October 23rd 2004 by Kenny.
https://thesession.org/tunes/3746

But there is another tune on site here that it is obviously related to on both parts, and close enough that I did consider just adding this to the ‘comments’ for that ~

“The Munster Jig”
Key signature: G Major
Submitted on November 14th 2001 by Jdharv.
https://thesession.org/tunes/372

I thought on this and before submitting it queried a couple of members, Dow and slainte, and it was slainte that, with just the B-part, found “The Munster Jig” for me to link to. I did make several attempts myself to chase up the B-part, but I’m not in much of a fit state at the moment for anything. I just hope giving this its own entry was a reasonable decision…

La Reel De La Baie Sainte-Marie

Transcribed from Saltfishforty - Netherbow album, “Howie MacDonald’s / Rhena’s 80th / La Reel De La Baie Sainte-Marie” (3rd tune in set)
I recommend leaving the bow constantly in a double-stop position, it will give the tune a nice ringing effect

Re: La Reel De La Baie Sainte-Marie

Named after the thriving French speaking settlement of Baie Sainte Marie in Wagweiik/ Clare (St Mary’s Bay), Enmiwgtaqamu’g/Alba Nuadh/Nouvelle-Écosse (Nova-Scotia).

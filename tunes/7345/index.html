Two comments

Our House At Home

I learnt this off of Danu’s “The Road Less Traveled” album. They do it in Emaj with a flat set of Pipes (the Pipes being a B set). It is a good tune and easy to learn, I am surprised that it hasn’t been posted yet.

↳ Posted by Avery on June 12th, 2007.

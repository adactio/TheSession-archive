Two comments

The Barking Dog

This is my humble, personal transcription of this lovely Reel by the Leblanc family.

I transcribed it from their very professional video on YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmqbCarTsgU&t. I hope it’s not too far from the original ! Please let me know if you spot any mistakes.
Many thanks to Robin for giving me permission to do so.

I made a small adaptation so that it can be played on a C whistle. If you have a low G whistle, you should play the first B roll an octave lower.

↳ Posted by fran6ois on August 26th, 2026.

Re: The Barking Dog

Composer : Robin LeBlanc

The original tune is in A minor. I transcribed it one whole tone higher, in B Aeolian, so that the fingering on a C whistle follows the familiar D-whistle fingering. Thus, the fingering for D on a D whistle produces C on a C whistle.

Reel Setting

This tune is also played as a reel. There are several versions of it. Micho Russell recorded a much different version and Jackie Daly recorded one similar to this but also different in some phrases. The Rising Sun reel doesn’t seem to be posted here but a close variant on it is. That is, The Old Blackthorn or The Blackthorn Stick (not to be confused with the jig of the same title.)

I could post the reel if someone wanted but I would need time to iron out it out because there are so many versions. Should I post it here or as a new tune?

Re: The Rising Of The Sun

Version 2 above is identical to the setting of this hornpipe from the Power manuscript from Kilmore in County Wexford. The notation is from the later 1920’s part of the manuscript. That section of the book appears to me to have several tunes re-transcribed from printed sources.

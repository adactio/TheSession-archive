H.G.Abel’s Fancy

Another piece that I didn’t know from the pile of manuscripts I am sorting. I have no idea who H.G.Abel was nor the composer of this tune. Its on one of Bobby Macleod’s dance albums and on a RSCDS 90th Anniversary record too, so I guess its one of those tunes that was fairly popular in the past but which has now been forgotten. I hope Nigel Gatherer can shed some light on this one.

↳ Posted by Noel Jackson on March 27th, 2018.

Re: H.G.Abel’s Fancy

It was composed by Jim MacLeod, whose band played at Dunblane Hydro as well as appearing on television and radio. They recorded it on their 1976 LP ’By Special Request" and it was also published in The Jim MacLeod Collection (Glasgow, c1976). I don’t know who H G Abel is/was, however.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on March 28th, 2018.

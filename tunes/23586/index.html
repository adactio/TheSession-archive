One comment

Páidí’s

This is another composition of the Wexford All-Ireland winning pianist Nóra Byrne Kavanagh. The tune was named to mark the arrival of a grandson to the well-known Sinnott musical family of Boolavogue. Nóra has kindly agreed for the inclusion of many of her traditional compositions, which are copyrighted, in the wexford Tunes Collection project online here at thesession.org.

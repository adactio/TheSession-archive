Five comments

This reel was composed by J. Scott Skinner. It can be hard at first, but it just requires a bit of position work. For bars A8 and B4, I start by playing the A with my first finger, and then sliding up to the E really quickly after my 4th finger has played the D#.

↳ Posted by Erik-Fiddler on February 15th, 2004.

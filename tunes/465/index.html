The plant is called “Lonicera periclymenum”, in German its Waldgeissblatt.
I jave it in my garden. It is very common in Swiss Woods and as kids we used to smoke dried pieces of its “trunk”. We got sick when smoking too much :-)
But no impact on you as other plants, but we felt like grown up!

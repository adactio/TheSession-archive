Submitting own compositions

I get slightly tired of this complaint when my webABC site - which anyone is invited to visit - has over 4000 tunes, including recent additions from “Northern Fiddler” and Sean Ryan’s “Hidden Ireland”.

Access to this site - as Mr C is well aware - is open to any musician willing to take the trouble to visit it (http:www.capeirish.com/webabc/collections/coll-index.html). It represents a hell of a lot of work so my patience with snide comments is probably less than it should be.

I’m figuring that everyone can put up with one original tune from me now and then but evidently there are folks out there more interested in counting than actually doing anything with the material that I have provded and will continue to provide on the webABC site.

If the owner of this fine site has a problem with my submissions, he’s welcome to contact me. But for now I figure Mr C might be the only one who has trouble dealing with a 4000 to 7 ratio.

There were numerous postings to this site after Larry’s passing, all well-deserved tributes to a guy who did more for Irish trad in the Boston area than anyone else. He welcomed me to the Boston scene after we moved to the area from NY in 1979. The tune was written early on, and yes, Larry heard it and liked it. I’m not sure he ever learned it but I’m betting he would have if it hadn’t been named after him!

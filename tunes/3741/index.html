Re: Molly On The Shore

Grainger’s arrangement as played by the violinist above is 2 reels stuck together. I’m looking right now at a photocopy of the page from the Joyce collection which was the source. I copied it from an edition of Joyce’s book held in Aberdeen University library in the early 1970s - I think it may still be there.
“Molly On The Shore” is #902, and is a reel with 3 parts.
The tune above it on the same page - #901 - is a 2-part reel listed as “Temple Hill”.
Oddly, these days I hear “Temple Hill” often played with 3 parts, taking and adding on what is the 3rd part from “Molly On The Shore”.
Both are listed in Joyce as “Cork reels from P.Carew’s MSS”.
Setting #4 above is closest to the music published by Joyce for “Molly On The Shore”.

The Stig Jig

Composed by guitarist John McCartin from Leitrim, presumably after a character who appeared on the BBC car magazine programme ‘Top Gear’.

↳ Posted by Nigel Gatherer on June 6th, 2013.

The name dates from Jeremy Clarkson and Andy Wilman’s school days at Repton, I believe? They called new boys “the Stig”, I think.

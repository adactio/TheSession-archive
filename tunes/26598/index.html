My Love Is But A Lassie Yet

A very widely-known polka which (unless I’ve missed something) doesn’t seem to have been posted here yet. My old band used to play it going into ‘Buffalo Girls’ (aka ‘Old Johnny Walker’) and ‘The Rose Tree’. A good set for dancers. (Chords given here are mine so use, change, or ignore as you will).

This title is also shown as alternative name for Niel Gow’s ‘Farewell to Whisky’. Is that really true?

Re: My Love Is But A Lassie Yet

“as [an] alternative name for Niel Gow’s ‘Farewell to Whisky’. Is that really true?”

I’ve heard that somewhere, Bazza, but I can’t remember where. The tune is part of a standard set for the Dashing White Sergeant alongside “The Rose Tree” and others. Alternative names for it include “Miss Farquharson’s Reel” (Bremner 1757), “Lady Badinscoth’s Reel” and “The Gordons Hae the Girding O’t;” (Robert Petrie’s 3rd collection 1790).

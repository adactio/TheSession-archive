Charming Mary Kelly

A catchy single reel, that fidldler Monika Braaksam pointed out to us! It first appeared as No. 222 in Capt. Francis O’Neill last collection “Waifs & Strays of Gaelic Melody” (Lyon & Healy, Chicago 1922) in which at the age of 73 he published - in his own words: “Comprising Forgotten Favorites, Worthy Variants, and Tunes not Previously Printed.” A comparison between the recording at hand and the manuscript reveals nearly 100% congruence, Liz & Kieran have stuck very closely to the original notation.
On one occasion a variation for the opening bar is introduced:
~F3A d2ed | cAGF …. etc
Unfortunatlely the digital tanscription of Paul Kinder does not tell anything about the origin of the tune, maybe there were no clues available in the original book?
